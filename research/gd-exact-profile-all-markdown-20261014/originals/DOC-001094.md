## 1. Original quantities and what a negative energy disproves

For any nonnegative length-n word h, normalize smoothness to1 and write

 s=sum_(i<n)h_i, A=1+s,

Every actual smooth convex trajectory satisfies Q_ij<=0, including all
remote, terminal, and optimum comparisons, by1252b8823bb5a290. We take
the optimum jet to be x_star=g_star=f_star=0.

For a full original multiplier array Lambda, let e be the all-ones
column, L_flow=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda, and

 T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, T_it=0 for t>i,
 D_prim=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(n-1)(2-h_(n-1)),-s(s+2)),

i!=j, and L_flow^T e=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(n-1),2s)^T. Accepted

## 2. Static projection functions and the complete normal check

For a compact convex set K containing0, define

 F_K(x)=max_(g in K){<x,g>-||g||^2/2}.                    (2)

The unique maximizer is the Euclidean projection P_K(x), by completing
the square. It is characterized by

 <x-P_K(x),v-P_K(x)><=0 for every v in K.

Adding this inequality at two points yields
||P_K(x)-P_K(y)||^2<=<P_K(x)-P_K(y),x-y>, so P_K is1-Lipschitz.
The two maximizing inequalities imply

 0<=F_K(x+z)-F_K(x)-<P_K(x),z>
      <=<P_K(x+z)-P_K(x),z><=||z||^2.

Thus F_K is convex, differentiable, and1-smooth, with gradient P_K.
If0 is interior to K, it is the unique minimizer: F_K(0)=0, and for
x!=0 substituting a sufficiently small positive multiple of x in(2)
gives F_K(x)>0.

At a proposed query whose gradient is g_i=P_K(x_i), put

 y_i=x_i-g_i, f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2.

Direct substitution in the definition of Q gives

 Q_ij=<y_j,g_i-g_j>.                                    (3)

Hence checking the entire normal cone at EACH original query proves
ALL ordered inequalities at once. Comparisons to the optimum are also
included since0 belongs to K; in particular Q_(i,star)=0.

The actual GD norm telescope is

 ||x_i||^2-||x_(i+1)||^2
      =2h_i f_i+h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2.

Using the actual terminal value in V_h therefore gives

 V_h=||x_0||^2-||x_n+s g_n||^2.                         (4)

This identity also applies to the scalar capped function below, since
its values have the same expression x*g-g^2/2 at every query.

## 3. The original global cuts come first

For any index j with h_j>1 define

 P=1+sum_(i<j)h_i, D=1+sum_(i>j)h_i, eta=(h_j-1)/D.

Take K=[-eta,1] and x_0=P. Every query through j has gradient1,
query j equals1, and the step h_j lands at -D*eta. Every remaining
gradient is -eta: the nonnegative later steps move monotonically on
the negative plateau, ending at x_n=-eta. These are actual projection
queries for the single static function(2), whose unique minimum is0.
The true initial distance is P>0. Formula(4) gives

 V_h=P^2-A^2(h_j-1)^2/D^2.

Consequently every original certificate must satisfy, for EVERY j,

 A(h_j-1)_+ <= (1+sum_(i<j)h_i)(1+sum_(i>j)h_i).         (5)

A failed cut already supplies an actual negative-energy witness. When
we analyze a word below, all cuts are first imposed; we need no further
test on a word already excluded by one of them.

For the first and last physical steps a,d of a length5 word, (5) gives

 a,d <= 2A/(A+1) <2,                                  (6)

provided A>1. If an endpoint is<=1, (6) is automatic; if it is>1,
its cut rearranges to (A+1)a<=2A or (A+1)d<=2A.

## 4. An adjacent-pair witness with every exterior query retained

Fix any two adjacent steps

 h_j=1+B, h_(j+1)=1+C, B,C>0,

All other physical steps are arbitrary and nonnegative. Define the
normalized defect and its unnormalized version by

 Delta=P^2 H=BC[P^2+B(P+A)]-DP^2.                       (7)

We prove that D<=BC always excludes the word, and that H>0 excludes it
when D>BC. This is a necessary test, not a completeness assertion.

### 4.1 The capped scalar case D<=BC

Use the static function

Its derivative min(x,1) is continuous, nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz;
the function is convex, differentiable,1-smooth, with unique minimum0.

to1. The next two positions and gradients are

All suffix queries are>=1, because their total movement is D-1 and
BC-(D-1)>=1. Their gradients are1 and x_n=BC-D+1. Every original

Here s=P+B+C+D was used. In fact V_h<-3 because P>=1 and
(1+B)(1+C)>1. All original interpolation inequalities hold by the
actual smooth-convex function, including the optimum and terminal.

### 4.2 The planar triangle case D>BC

Define

positive relation B u+(1+C)v+D w=0 show that K is a nondegenerate
triangle containing0 in its interior.

Start at x_0=P u. Prescribe gradient u at every original query through

The sum over an empty range is zero. Formula(9) makes the middle update
x_(j+2)=D w, so every GD equality in(10) holds.

We check all normal cones, including every exterior query. The basic
inner products are

Every prefix normal is a nonnegative multiple of u. Its comparisons
with w-u and v-u are respectively multiples of t-1<0 and
-(B+D t)/(1+C)-1<0.

At query j+1 the normal is y=-B u-v. Its comparisons are

 <w-v,y>=(-B^2 C+kappa*t)/(1+C)^2=0,

Subtracting -BC(1-t)/(1+C) from the second expression leaves exactly
(-B^2 C+kappa*t)/(1+C)^2, hence zero.

Every suffix normal is a nonnegative multiple of w. Its comparisons
with u-w and v-w are respectively zero and multiples of
-t[1+(B+D)/(1+C)]<0. This proves every cone in(10). In particular
the prescribed gradients are actual projections onto ONE static K;

The true initial distance is P, and x_n=g_n=w. Equations(4),(9) give
