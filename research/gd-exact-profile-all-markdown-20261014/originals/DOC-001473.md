## 2. Every original row and the formal matrix

Normalize L=1 and xstar=gstar=fstar=0. The sampled labels are
I={0,...,n,*}; indices 0,...,n-1 are queried by the updates, while n is
the original unqueried terminal. The formal vectors are independent
coordinates (x0,g0,...,gn), of size d=n+2. Put

    p_i=e_0-sum_(t<i) h_t e_(t+1), u_i=e_(i+1)  (0<=i<=n),
    p_*=u_*=0, x_i=p_i^T z, g_i=u_i^T z.

For every ordered pair, including optimum, terminal, and diagonal, use

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 <=0.
    A_ij=Sym(u_i (p_j-p_i)^T)+(u_i-u_j)(u_i-u_j)^T/2,
    Sym(B)=(B+B^T)/2.

Thus the gradient part is z^T A_ij z. Diagonal rows are identically zero.
The finite value-flow matrix has column (i,j) equal to e_i-e_j, omitting
the fixed optimum coordinate. In the full polynomial

    q||x0||^2-2f_n+sum_(i,j in I) lambda_ij Q_ij,

the coefficient of each f_i, 0<=i<=n, is
out_i-in_i-2*1_(i=n). Exact function cancellation therefore requires

    out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n), 0<=i<=n.

There is no independent optimum-value variable; summing the finite flows
gives out_*-in_*=-2. After cancellation the complete original matrix is

    M(q,lambda)=q e_0 e_0^T+sum_(i,j in I) lambda_ij A_ij.

All multipliers are nonnegative and M must be PSD on the entire formal
space. For an actual normalized trajectory, nonpositive Q rows give
0<=z^T M z<=q||x0||^2-2f_n. Hence exact feasibility is sufficient for
W_n(h)<=q. Numerical feasibility is not that exact assertion.

## 3. Declared restrictions and complete free-terminal interface

This batch fixes lambda_ii=0, lambda_(i,n)=0 for i!=n, and
lambda_(*,i)=0 for all i. The last restriction is an additional search
restriction, not a theorem about all duals. No other ordered prefix row
is removed. These fixed entries are represented by literal zero constants;
every remaining original multiplier is an independent nonnegative variable.

Terminal flow gives sum_(i<n)lambda_(n,i)+lambda_(n,*)=2. Define

    pi=lambda_(n,*)/2, alpha_i=lambda_(n,i)/2,
    pi>=0, alpha_i>=0, pi+sum_(i<n)alpha_i=1.

Writing mu for the prefix multipliers gives the full remaining flows

    sum_(j in {0,...,n-1,*})mu_ij
       -sum_(j<n)mu_ji=2alpha_i, i<n,
    sum_(i<n)mu_(i,*)=2(1-pi).

The diagonal mu entries and optimum-source entries are fixed zero. Let
y_i=x_i-g_i, delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2, and

    w_i=pi+sum_(j<=i)alpha_j,
    r_i=h_i*w_i-alpha_i,
    V_alpha=x_n-sum_(i<n)alpha_i*y_i=pi*x0-sum_(i<n)r_i*g_i.

Direct expansion of the terminal rows gives

    2sum_(i<n)alpha_i Q_(n,i)+2pi Q_(n,*)
      =2f_n-2sum_(i<n)alpha_i delta_i
         +||g_n-V_alpha||^2-||V_alpha||^2.

Consequently M=||g_n-V_alpha||^2+R, where the complete prefix polynomial is

    R=q||x0||^2-||V_alpha||^2
        +sum_(i<n)alpha_i||g_i||^2
        +sum_(i,j in prefix)mu_ij (gradient part of Q_ij).

The value terms cancel by the displayed prefix flows. The entire prefix
matrix has blocks [[q-pi^2,b^T],[b,K]], with

    b_i=pi*r_i-mu_(i,*)/2,
    K=diag(alpha)-r*r^T+sum mu_ij (A_ij)_(gradient,gradient).

This is equivalent to full PSD because the free formal gn may be set
equal to V_alpha. In particular q>=pi^2 is necessary. If q>pi^2 the
exact Schur condition is K-b*b^T/(q-pi^2)>=0. If q=pi^2, PSD instead
requires b=0 and K>=0. Thus imposing pi=sqrt(q) forces
mu_(i,*)=2pi*r_i; the free-pi problem does not impose those equalities.
No sign of r is imposed separately. The numerical model uses the full
original matrix M and original flow, not this eliminated Schur formulation.

For the requested diagnostic let prefix_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, S=sum h_i,
D=1+2S, and B=sum alpha_i*(1+prefix_i). Then sum r_i=S-B=:V.
On a common-gradient formal prefix the complete R becomes the 2 by 2 form

    [[q-pi^2, pi*(V+1)-1],
     [pi*(V+1)-1, D-(V+1)^2]].

Indeed sum b=pi*V-(1-pi), and the weighted prefix flows give the gradient
coefficient sum_i alpha_i+sum mu_ij A_ij(g,g)=2B. This is a necessary
restriction of full PSD, not a sufficient test. B, r, and this small matrix
will be exported as diagnostics inside each timed child; no such scalar
screen replaces the full original eigenvalue checks.

## 4. New exact feasible calibration and independent elimination

For h=(1), x1=x0-g0 and q=3/8, the allowed original rows are exactly
Q_10,Q_(1,*),Q_(0,*). All other multipliers are fixed zero by the declared
restrictions. Direct original-row expansion, before any elimination, gives

    A_10: (||g1||^2+||g0||^2)/2,
    A_(1,*): -<g1,x0>+<g1,g0>+||g1||^2/2,
    A_(0,*): -<g0,x0>+||g0||^2/2.

The flow at0 is lambda_(0,*)-lambda_10=0 and at1 is
lambda_10+lambda_(1,*)=2. Consequently every legal multiplier vector is

    lambda_(1,*)=2pi,
    lambda_10=lambda_(0,*)=2(1-pi),  0<=pi<=1.

The optimum flow is then exactly-2. In the original order (x0,g0,g1)
the full matrix derived from these THREE original rows is

    M(q,pi)=[[q, -(1-pi), -pi],
             [-(1-pi), 2(1-pi), pi],
             [-pi, pi, 1]].

Completing only the actual terminal square gives

    M(q,pi)=||g1-pi*x0+pi*g0||^2+R(q,pi),
    R(q,pi)=[[q-pi^2, pi^2+pi-1],
             [pi^2+pi-1, 2-2pi-pi^2]].

Here the first equality denotes equality of quadratic forms. The change
from g1 to g1-pi*x0+pi*g0 is invertible and has determinant1. Thus
M is PSD exactly when R is PSD, and their determinants agree. Expanding
the actual two-by-two determinant independently yields

    det M=det R
      =(q-pi^2)(2-2pi-pi^2)-(pi^2+pi-1)^2
      =2q-1+2(1-q)pi-(1+q)pi^2.

At q=3/8 this becomes

    (-2+10pi-11pi^2)/8,
    pi_-=(5-sqrt(3))/11, pi_+=(5+sqrt(3))/11.

It is nonnegative exactly on [pi_-,pi_+]. There is no lost sign or
principal-minor condition. Write s=sqrt(3), so1<s<2. The endpoints
satisfy0<pi_-<=pi_+<1 and pi_->1/4, since4s<9. Throughout the interval,

    q-pi^2 >= q-pi_+^2=(139-80s)/968>0,
    2-2pi-pi^2 >= (104-32s)/121>0.

The first strict sign follows from139^2-(80s)^2=121>0 and139,80s>0;
the second follows from s<2. These verify both diagonal entries of R.
For completeness the original one-by-one principal minors are
q,2(1-pi),1, all positive; its two-by-two principal minors are

    (1-pi)(2q-1+pi)=(1-pi)(pi-1/4),
    q-pi^2,
    2-2pi-pi^2,

all positive on this interval. Its full determinant is nonnegative.
Conversely, PSD requires this full determinant nonnegative, giving exactly
the same interval; multiplier nonnegativity imposes no additional cut.
Therefore the exact free-pi optimum in this restricted original-row model
is pi_-=(5-sqrt(3))/11. The endpoint singularity of an optimized matrix
does not affect the existence of the strictly feasible point next.
