## 2. Finite Euclidean realization of the new dependence law

For any finite nonnegative list t_i, sort its distinct positive values as
0<tau_1<...<tau_d and put tau_0=0. With orthonormal vectors e_1,...,e_d,
define

    v_i=sum_(ell:tau_ell<=t_i) sqrt(tau_ell-tau_(ell-1))*e_ell.

Then the common coordinates of v_i and v_j are precisely those with
tau_ell<=min(t_i,t_j). Their inner product telescopes to min(t_i,t_j),
and ||v_i||^2=t_i. Thus this is an explicit finite-dimensional generating
rule, not a PSD oracle. Equal t_i give identical vectors; increasing and
decreasing t_i turn coordinates on and off according to scale. Nothing
here establishes smooth interpolation of a prescribed GD transcript.

## 4. Local transition calculation and first explicit law

Consider one cell after a bridge of excess B>0. Write p=v_(j-1),v=v_j,
P=||p||^2>0,T=||v||^2, and use <p,v>=min(P,T). At the central query,

    u=-(B*p+A*v)/D,
    y=(-bB*p+A^2*v)/D.

The retained row with source gradient v and target gradient u is

    D^2*Q_(v,u)=K*T+E*B*min(P,T)-bB^2*P.       (4)

To derive(4), multiply
(v-u)=[B*p+(2A+b)*v]/D by y. The mixed coefficient is
B[A^2-b(2A+b)]=B*E, and the other two coefficients are-bB^2 and K.

For a shrinking endpoint T<=P, this row requires

    T/P<=alpha(B):=bB^2/(K+EB),                (5)

whenever the denominator is positive. For a growing endpoint T>=P it
requires

    T/P<=gamma(B):=B(bB-E)/K.                  (6)

The factorization

    K*(gamma(B)-1)=(bB-A^2)(B+2A+b)           (7)

shows where the two regimes meet. At B=A^2/b both bounds equal1.
The first explicit extrapolation law selected in plan6e9c5cb11fc77d32
was to take equality in(5) below this threshold and equality in(6)
above it, using the exact B_j and fixed enclosing-m parameters in every
cell. This produces nonmonotone reset norms and persistent state.

The following obstruction is STRONGER: it allows arbitrary reset norms
subject only to the local inequalities, not this maximal-transition law.
Thus lowering a selected factor or independently retuning all endpoint
norms cannot repair the entire declared min-kernel family.
