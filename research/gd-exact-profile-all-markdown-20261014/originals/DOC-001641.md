# Source methods revisited, 2026-10-08

These are primary-source method leads, not Danus facts or imported proofs of the target. No posted solution to the exact benchmark was sought.

## Dense interpolation multipliers

[Chikhi, arXiv2609.13765v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13765v2), Section4, constructs nonnegative interpolation multipliers from two monotone scalar sequences for a specially balanced constant schedule. The source also distinguishes terminal smooth-gradient performance from the proximal objective evaluated at a different point. It was already inspected on October1; there is no literature-newness claim.

The new root work allowed varying steps and nonnegative diagonal slack, and derived a sufficient17/16 terminal penalty. The independently checked extension is in dense_history_search_20261008_r2/REPORT.md. Its entire specified monotone A/D family fails the actual hierarchy's central bridge. Thus merely varying those two scalar sequences cannot supply the desired full-class upper. Non-diagonal remainders remain outside the exclusion.

## Laplacian sum-of-squares structure

[Bok and Altschuler, arXiv2412.05497](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.05497), Section3.2 and Lemma14, prove positivity of a recursive slack matrix using a Laplacian and its Schur complement. This is a method lead for retaining structured cross terms.

The source analyzes proximal gradient updates and the original Silver schedule. This project's projection-gradient dynamics and relaxed proximal interpretation do not automatically match those updates. No theorem or coefficient table was transplanted. Any future use needs an exact update/criterion translation and a new identity for the altered same-m word.
