# Signed terminal credit: two exact failures of direct repair

Status: complete candidate calculations, NOT verifier-accepted. No numerical
PEP or fact_submit call. These results do not decide equality of the signed
and ordinary primitive cones. In particular, preservation of the entire PSD
remainder is stronger than preservation of positive semidefiniteness.

## 1. Exact space of multiplier changes preserving the full remainder

Fix a nonnegative list h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), with n>=1, and formal independent
gradients g_0,...,g_n. Put s=sum h_i, x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, and

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                               -s(s+1)||g_n||^2.

An intrinsic certificate is a multiplier array lambda on distinct finite
pairs such that V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij cancels the values and has a PSD
quadratic remainder on ALL independent formal gradients. A signed certificate
may have negative coefficients only on i->n. A certificate for
E_theta=V_h-theta sum_(i<n)h_i Q_(i,n) has an equivalent signed array with
lambda_(i,n)>=-theta h_i and all other coefficients nonnegative.

Define the (n+1)-by-(n+1) lower triangular matrix A by

 A_ii=1, A_it=h_t if t<i, A_it=0 if t>i.

Let e be the all-one vector and r=A^(-1)e. Direct forward substitution gives
r_0=1 and r_(i+1)=(1-h_i)r_i. For a change Delta lambda, with zero diagonal,
put L=diag(Delta lambda e)-Delta lambda. The following parameterization is
exact, without any sign assumption:

 Delta lambda preserves both value cancellation and the ENTIRE quadratic
 remainder if and only if

   L=B A^(-1), B^T=-B, B e=0, B r=0.                 (1)

To prove this, preserving value coefficients is exactly equality of incoming
and outgoing Delta flow at each node, equivalently e^T L=0. The row identity
L e=0 holds by definition. Write H=A-I. The position terms in sum Delta
lambda Q are g^T L H g. Because the flow is balanced, its squared-difference
terms are g^T L g. Thus the full quadratic change is g^T L A g, which is
identically zero exactly when B=L A is skew-symmetric. The two flow identities
become B r=0 and e^T B=0; skew-symmetry turns the latter into B e=0.
Conversely, these conditions imply both flow identities and a zero quadratic
change, and off-diagonal entries Delta lambda_ij=-L_ij recover the array.

If some step is positive, e and r are independent. The possible B are precisely
skew maps on the orthogonal complement of span{e,r}; hence the dimension of
the space in (1) is (n-1)(n-2)/2. This dimension is about algebraic changes
preserving a supplied remainder, not about sign-feasible certificates.

If h_0=1, then r=(1,0,...,0). Therefore B r=0 forces its first column and,
by skew-symmetry, first row to vanish. The first row of L also vanishes.
Consequently EVERY outgoing coefficient lambda_(0,j) is invariant under
every change preserving the complete remainder and value cancellation.
In particular, a negative lambda_(0,n) cannot be repaired in this way.

## 2. An arbitrarily long, strictly PSD example with a negative invariant

Take any integer n>=2, h_i=1 for all i<n, epsilon=theta=1/n, and
d_k=g_k-g_(k+1). Begin with the nonnegative adjacent array

 lambda^0_(k,k+1)=2(k+1)^2,
 lambda^0_(k+1,k)=2(k+1)(k+2), 0<=k<n,

all other entries zero. Direct expansion of the two adjacent Q terms at each
step, and summation of the value coefficients, gives

 V_h+sum lambda^0_ij Q_ij=sum_(k=0)^(n-1)(k+1)^2||d_k||^2.       (2)

Modify the four coefficients by adding

 Delta lambda_01=n epsilon,
 Delta lambda_10=(n-1)epsilon,
 Delta lambda_0n=-epsilon,
 Delta lambda_1n=epsilon.                             (3)

These are distinct ordered pairs for n>=2. When n=2, the 1n change is simply
added to the existing adjacent coefficient. The flow of (3) is zero at every
node. Its full quadratic is exactly

 epsilon[n Q_01+(n-1)Q_10-Q_0n+Q_1n]
   =-epsilon <d_0,sum_(k=1)^(n-1)(n-k)d_k>.           (4)

For a direct check, the value terms cancel. The quadratic left side divided
by epsilon is (g_0-g_1) dot [sum_(j=1)^n g_j-n g_1]. Writing
g_j-g_1=-sum_(k=1)^(j-1)d_k yields the right side.

The resulting signed remainder M is therefore the following sum of squares:

 M=sum_(k=1)^(n-1)(k+1)^2
       ||d_k-epsilon(n-k)d_0/[2(k+1)^2]||^2
   +[1-epsilon^2/4 sum_(k=1)^(n-1)(n-k)^2/(k+1)^2]||d_0||^2.   (5)

The last coefficient is strictly greater than 3/4. Indeed,
epsilon=1/n, (n-k)^2<n^2, and
sum_(j=2)^n 1/j^2 < sum_(j=2)^n 1/[j(j-1)] <1.
Thus M is positive definite in all the gradient differences and PSD on the
complete original formal gradient space, with exactly the constant-gradient
kernel. The only negative signed multiplier is lambda_0n=-1/n.

For the ACTUAL E_theta certificate, add theta h_i=1/n to every incoming
terminal multiplier. The 0n entry becomes zero, and every other entry is
nonnegative. These additions cancel the -theta J_h in E_theta, so the
remainder remains exactly (5). This is a full certificate, including the
actual function-value cancellation, for 0<theta<1.

Nevertheless no ordinary nonnegative certificate for V_h can have this SAME
remainder M: by Section 1 its 0n multiplier would still be -1/n. The ordinary
certificate (2) does exist and has a different PSD remainder. Thus this is
NOT a strict enlargement example. It disproves a general repair that keeps
the given matrix fixed, even for strictly positive difference matrices and
arbitrarily many positive steps. Any successful general repair must genuinely
change that matrix or use a different primal argument.
