## 3. Approach 1: an explicit unequal-cap critical construction fails

### 3.1 Fixed actual function and the proposed recursion

Fix A>0 independent of m. Consider the one-dimensional function

    f_A(x)=x^2/2,             -A<=x<=1,
           x-1/2,             x>=1,
           -A x-A^2/2,        x<=-A.

Its derivative is clip(x,[-A,1]), which is continuous, nondecreasing, and 1-Lipschitz. The function is convex, differentiable, and 1-smooth, with unique original minimizer 0. At every x,

    -2 f_A(x)+2x f_A'(x)-f_A'(x)^2=0.               (3.1)

Thus a correctly realized trajectory would have Q_star,i=0 at every query. The construction below never changes this function or its minimizer.

We tried to generalize the old complete critical trajectory by defining positive and negative starting magnitudes

    X_1^+=beta, X_1^-=A beta,
    L_j=s_(j-1)+1+k_(j-1),
    X_j^+=L_j-X_(j-1)^-,
    X_j^-=A L_j-X_(j-1)^+,           j>=2.           (3.2)

The proposed positive task H_j at X_j^+ would execute its entire left child and bridge with gradient +1 and land at -X_(j-1)^-. The proposed negative task H_j at -X_j^- would execute its entire left child and bridge with gradient -A and land at +X_(j-1)^+. The right child would recurse with opposite orientation. At the base, the starts lie inside the quadratic interval and the actual one-step outputs are -a beta and +a A beta, respectively. There is no declaration that the higher-level saturated execution is valid.

The REQUIRED saturation inequalities follow at the bridge query, because the left-child total step mass is s_(j-1):

    positive orientation: X_j^+>=s_(j-1)+1
                          iff X_(j-1)^-<=k_(j-1),
    negative orientation: X_j^->=A(s_(j-1)+1)
                          iff X_(j-1)^+<=A k_(j-1). (3.3)

If these inequalities hold, the same inequalities at earlier left-child queries follow since their physical prefix mass is at most s_(j-1). If they fail, the proposed bridge query lies outside the region for the claimed saturated gradient, so the proposed entire saturated itinerary cannot be actual. This does not determine the true bridge query after an earlier departure. This is an actual projection constraint, not an interpolation table supplied without execution.

### 3.2 Exact solution and the incompatible top-level conditions

Adding and subtracting (3.2) gives

    X_j^++X_j^-=(1+A)X_j,
    X_j^+-X_j^-=(1-A)(s_j+beta-1-a).               (3.4)

For the second identity, its base value is (1-A)beta and

    sum_(l=2)^j L_l
      =alpha(c^j-c)+(1-alpha)(2^j-2),

which together with (2.1) yields (3.4). Hence these are exact formulas for the proposed starts, even when they are not feasible starts for the intended itinerary.

Uniformly in the literal enclosing parameters, (2.3)--(3.4) yield, at j=m-1 and j=m-2,

    X_j^+/s_j -> p=(c0-A)/(c0+1),
    X_j^-/s_j -> q=(A c0-1)/(c0+1),
    k_j/s_j -> c0-2=r,       r=sqrt(2)-1.           (3.5)

For a proposed positive root at depth m, the root's left-child/bridge requires X_(m-1)^-<=k_(m-1), so its limiting requirement is q<=r. Its negative right child at depth m-1 requires X_(m-2)^+<=A k_(m-2), so its limiting requirement is p<=A r. Since c0=2+r and c0 r=1, these two requirements simplify exactly to

    q<=r  iff A<=1,
    p<=A r iff A>=1.                               (3.6)

For every fixed A>1, the first difference in (3.6) is strictly positive in the wrong direction, so the positive root bridge is infeasible for every sufficiently large enclosing m. For every fixed 0<A<1, the second difference is strictly positive in the wrong direction, so the negative right-child bridge is infeasible for every sufficiently large enclosing m. This uses uniform errors at the two actual top depths and does not assume that a fixed internal-depth limit describes the enclosing word.

Thus no fixed unequal cap A permits this particular complete alternating saturated recursion for all large m. If the proposed starts become nonpositive in some parameter range, that is an additional failure and cannot repair (3.6). At A=1 the proposed recursion reduces to the old symmetric critical trajectory, which the supplied Profile C addendum already calibrates positively at both parities. We do not count replaying it as a Profile C obstruction.

## 4. Approach 2: actual dense cyclic coefficients and their full-matrix failure

### 4.1 A diagonal identity and why causal flows cannot work

For ANY admissible C and a physical query i, define its backward and forward outgoing masses

    P_i=sum_(j<i) C_ij, F_i=sum_(j>i) C_ij.

Zero row sums and the actual Bmat give

    (C Bmat)_ii=-P_i+(h_i-1)F_i.

Since K_ii=u_i(h_i-u_i), the COMPLETE remainder diagonal is

    R_ii=u_i(2-u_i)+2P_i-2(h_i-1)F_i.              (4.1)

The covariance is already included in (4.1). In particular, a necessary condition for full PSD at a large physical bridge is

    P_i-(h_i-1)F_i >= u_i(u_i-2)/2.                 (4.2)

No sign is imposed separately on R-K.

Here is a checked obstruction to the causal subcase, supplied as a diagnostic within this coefficient approach. Regard C_ij, i!=j, as directed flow from i to j. Its net incoming minus outgoing mass is b_i. If its positive-edge graph is acyclic, choose a topological ordering. At the cut just after a node i, no positive edge enters the prefix. The total outgoing flow from the prefix is minus its sum of b-values, which is at most U because n is the only node with negative b and b_n=-U. Every outgoing edge of i crosses that cut. Therefore

    P_i+F_i<=U.                                    (4.3)

This also follows from source-to-sink path decomposition; (4.3) does not assume that the topological order agrees with chronological query order. Since every physical h_i>1, equations (4.1)--(4.3) imply

    R_ii<=u_i(2-u_i)+2U.                           (4.4)

At the actual middle query k=2^(m-1)-1, put s=s_(m-1) and hmid=1+k_(m-1). Then S=2s+hmid, T_k=s. Formula (2.1) gives, with the original enclosing parameters,

    s/S -> r,
    hmid/S -> r^2,
    u_k/S -> (1-r/8)r^2>0,
    U<=S.                                         (4.5)

Consequently the right side of (4.4), divided by S^2, has strictly negative limit superior. Every admissible acyclic C fails full PSD for all sufficiently large enclosing m. This includes dense directed acyclic graphs, not just adjacent or lower-triangular flows. It does not include graphs with circulations. The parent provided the acyclic-flow guard, and the topological-cut proof above checks it.

### 4.2 An explicit law with circulations and exact coefficients

To test a construction outside that excluded class, use the prescribed ell and b to set

    E=sum_(i=0)^n ell_i=S+ell_n,
    q_i=ell_i/E,
    p_i=ell_i-b_i,
    C=p q^T-diag(p).                               (4.6)

These are ACTUAL closed-form coefficients, without a solver or unproved recurrence. In particular,

    p_i=(1-rho_i)h_i>=0,          i<n,
    p_n=ell_n+U>0.

All q_i are strictly positive, sum q_i=1, and sum p_i=E because sum b_i=0. Thus every row of C sums to zero, all off-diagonal C_ij=p_i q_j are nonnegative, and the i-th column sum is

    (sum_j p_j)q_i-p_i=ell_i-p_i=b_i.               (4.7)

For all i>=1, p_i>0, so the subgraph on indices 1,...,n has genuine two-way cycles. This construction is not rejected merely by the acyclic result above.

### 4.3 The zero covariance row at the original query

Since rho_0=1, K_0j=0 for every j. Here p_0=0, so the entire initial row of C vanishes. Because h_0=1+a<2 and u_0=h_0,

    R_00=h_0(2-h_0)>0.                             (4.8)

The zero row of K does NOT imply a zero row of R. For j>0, row sums and Bmat give

    (C Bmat)_j0=(1-h_0)C_j0,
    R_0j=(h_0-1)C_j0=(h_0-1)p_j h_0/E.             (4.9)

Thus the degenerate covariance boundary is included explicitly, without an invalid inverse of K or an assumption that the entire residual row vanishes.

### 4.4 A strictly negative central diagonal of the FULL R

For physical i, set Q_before=sum_(j<i)q_j and Q_after=sum_(j>i)q_j. From (4.6),

    H_ii=(C Bmat+Bmat^T C^T)_ii
         =2p_i[(h_i-1)Q_after-Q_before].            (4.10)

At the middle query k, the palindromic physical word and the terminal algebraic weight yield

    Q_before=s/E, Q_after=(s+ell_n)/E,
    p_k=s hmid/(8S)>0,
    H_kk=(2p_k/E)[(hmid-1)(s+ell_n)-s].             (4.11)

For all task depths, hmid>3: m-2>=1 and

    k_(m-1)=beta c^(m-2)>=beta c
            =c0(1-epsilon^2)>2.

For the last strict inequality, c0>12/5 and epsilon^2<1/6 imply c0(1-epsilon^2)>2. Therefore the bracket in (4.11) is

    (hmid-2)s+(hmid-1)ell_n>0,

so H_kk>0. Moreover s/S<1/2, hence rho_k>15/16 and

    u_k>(15/16)hmid>45/16>2.

Including the whole covariance diagonal exactly,

    R_kk=K_kk+u_k(1-hmid)+b_k-H_kk
         =u_k(2-u_k)-H_kk<0.                       (4.12)

Thus the explicitly constructed cyclic C in (4.6) fails full PSD for EVERY m>=1536. This is a decisive test of the FULL matrix: its quadratic form on the coordinate vector e_k is negative. It neither discards covariance terms nor requires separately nonnegative physical diagonal slack. It excludes this concrete law only. The coordinate vector is not asserted to be an actual smooth-convex trajectory, so (4.12) is not a counterexample to D_u>=0 and not an exclusion of every admissible C.
