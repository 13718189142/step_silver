The evaluator retains every off-diagonal Lambda entry as a free variable,
with its original nonnegative sign; diagonal entries are zero by definition.
The original full flow is retained without dropping its final row.
All matrices T, L, D and M are exactly those in the assignment.

Under the original flow, e^T M e=0: the D term is
-sum(h_i^2)-s^2. The remaining scalar is omega^T T e, which equals
-2 sum_i h_i(1+sum_(t<i)h_t)+2s(1+s)=sum(h_i^2)+s^2.
For a symmetric positive semidefinite M, e^T M e=0 implies M e=0:
positivity of (e+t v)^T M(e+t v) for both signs of arbitrarily small t
forces v^T M e=0 for every v. Conversely, if M e=0, the invertible
matrix P=[e_0,...,e_(r-1),e] gives P^T M P=diag(M[0:r,0:r],0).
Thus the exact original PSD constraint is equivalent to M e=0 together
with PSD of its leading r-square principal block. This does not remove
any original formal gradient or identify any pair. Full M is read back.
