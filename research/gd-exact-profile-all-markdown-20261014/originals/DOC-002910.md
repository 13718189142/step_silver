## 1. Definitions and the three scoped conclusions

Work with smoothness 1 on a finite-dimensional real Euclidean space.
For a supplied finite word h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), actual GD means

    X_(i+1)=X_i-h_i g_i,  g_i=grad F(X_i),
    f_i=F(X_i)-min F,  V=2f_n/dist(X_0,argmin F)^2.

The optimum is denoted star, with X_star=g_star=f_star=0. Define the
original residual, for every distinct ordered pair including star, by

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,X_j-X_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2.       (1)

Define the positive affine value reserve

    delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2.

Here are the conclusions, each limited to its stated class.

A. Every nonempty supplied word with min_i h_i>1 has the explicit
two-state, time-dependent Markov construction in Sections 2--5. All
sampled value reserves are positive, the original gradients have dense
signed product correlations and real independent innovations, and every
ordered row (1) is proved. One fixed convex 1-smooth function realizes
the entire run and has unique minimizer 0 and exact initial distance eta.
The normalized payoff and a paid append rule are explicit.

B. For the original recursive diagnostic words H_m, the ENTIRE broader
class of queried Markov product correlations with an aligned initial
point and tight queried optimum upper rows has negligible original-B
payoff, under the varying support-ratio conditions of Section 7. This
allows arbitrary varying positive support ratios, arbitrary correlations,
arbitrary amplitudes, and a completely free terminal gradient and value.
It is not only a bound on the displayed safe rule.

C. The exact small example in Section 9 refutes replacing the distant
rows by adjacent rows, optimum rows, positive affine values, real PSD,
and even all reverse chronological rows. A remote forward row fails.

Neither A nor B nor C is a theorem about unrestricted W_n(h). In
particular min h>1 is already a restriction for A; the additional
geometric hypotheses for B are essential and are identified explicitly.

## 2. The bounded rule, genuine joint law, and real coordinates

Fix n>=1 and h_i>1. Choose two scalar parameters

    1<eta<min_i h_i,  0<theta<1,

and set a bookkeeping value h_n=eta. This h_n is NOT a physical step.
For example, the completely specified choice

    eta=(1+min_i h_i)/2, theta=(n+1)/(n+2)

depends only on the supplied word. Keeping the two scalar parameters
visible makes the continuation law and its bounds clearer. Define

    b_i=(h_i-eta)/eta,
    t_i=theta*min{1,(h_i-eta)/h_(i+1)},
    r_i=-sqrt(t_i/b_i),                         0<=i<n,
    A_0=1, A_(i+1)=sqrt(t_i*b_i)*A_i.                (2)

No Gram entry is a free parameter. The fixed rule reads only successive
physical steps and the two common scalars. Because h_(i+1)>=eta>1,

    0<t_i<=theta<1,
    t_i/b_i<=theta*eta/h_(i+1)<=theta<1,
    h_i-t_i*h_(i+1)>=eta,
    h_i-t_i>=eta.                                   (3)

The third inequality follows from t_i<=(h_i-eta)/h_(i+1).
The fourth follows because h_(i+1)>1. Thus -1<r_i<0.

Let S_0 be a fair sign and let B_0,...,B_(n-1) be independent signs,
independent of S_0, with P(B_i=1)=(1+r_i)/2. Put S_(i+1)=S_i B_i.
The complete finite joint law on sign strings s=(s_0,...,s_n) is

    p(s)=2^(-n-1) product_(i<n)(1+r_i*s_i*s_(i+1)).   (4)

Every atom is positive, and summing successively over the last sign
proves that the probabilities sum to 1. Independent bond signs give

    E[S_i S_j]=product_(k=i)^(j-1)r_k, i<j.          (5)

For example, literal real coordinates u_i(s)=sqrt(p(s))*s_i realize
(5). A smaller explicit realization uses the orthonormal basis
e_0,...,e_n of R^(n+1):

    u_0=e_0,
    u_(i+1)=r_i*u_i+sqrt(1-r_i^2)*e_(i+1).           (6)

Induction gives ||u_i||=1 and (5) as its actual real inner products.
Thus all PSD requirements are proved by coordinates. Every new
direction has a nonzero innovation, and all distinct inner products
are nonzero with sign (-1)^(j-i). The transition law changes when its
successive-step formula changes; it is not a fixed one-initial-sign
constant-correlation law. Section 6 gives a concrete changing example.

Set, in these SAME coordinates,

    g_i=A_i*u_i,
    X_0=eta*u_0,
    X_i=X_0-sum_(t<i)h_t*g_t,
    q_i=A_i^2,
    f_i=(eta-1/2)*q_i, delta_i=(eta-1)*q_i.           (7)

Every q_i and delta_i is strictly positive. These are data at all the
original samples, including the terminal. No subword is reset.

For later use define, for i<=j,

    P_ij=product_(k=i)^(j-1)(-t_k),
    E_ij=product_(k=i)^(j-1)(t_k*b_k)=q_j/q_i,        (8)

with empty products 1. Since A_(k+1)*r_k/A_k=-t_k,

    <g_i,g_j>=q_i*P_ij, i<j.                        (9)

## 3. Actual support and every original directed inequality

First prove the actual support identity

    <X_i,g_i>=eta*q_i, 0<=i<=n.                     (10)

It holds at i=0. The new basis vector e_(i+1) is orthogonal to X_i
and g_i, since both are in span(e_0,...,e_i). Hence

    <X_(i+1),u_(i+1)>=r_i*(eta-h_i)*A_i
                           =eta*A_(i+1),

where (2) was used. This proves (10) by induction. It proves support
along the actual GD displacement; it is not an independently assigned
inner product.

The optimum rows are now exactly

    Q_(i,star)=0,
    Q_(star,i)=-(eta-1)*q_i<0.                       (11)

For every i<j, all future innovations in u_j are orthogonal to X_i,
so <X_i,g_j>=eta*<g_i,g_j>. Expanding (1), (7), and (10) gives

    Q_(j,i)=(eta-1)*q_i*(P_ij-1).                   (12)

If j-i is odd then P_ij<0. If j-i is even, 0<P_ij<1 by (3).
Thus every reverse chronological row is strictly negative.

For the other orientation keep the whole physical displacement:

    Q_(i,j)/q_i
      =eta-sum_(k=i)^(j-1)h_k*P_ik-P_ij
                                      -(eta-1)*E_ij. (13)

No old step is omitted from this expression. We show

    sum_(k=i)^(j-1)h_k*P_ik+P_ij>=eta.              (14)

For even d=j-i, pair the physical terms consecutively:

    sum_(k=i)^(j-1)h_k*P_ik+P_ij
      =sum_(r=0)^(d/2-1)P_(i,i+2r)
          [h_(i+2r)-t_(i+2r)*h_(i+2r+1)]+P_ij.

Each even-prefix product is positive. Every bracket is at least eta
by (3), and the first product is 1. The last term P_ij is positive,
which proves (14).

For odd d=2v+1, the analogous identity is

    sum_(r=0)^(v-1)P_(i,i+2r)
          [h_(i+2r)-t_(i+2r)*h_(i+2r+1)]
        +P_(i,j-1)*[h_(j-1)-t_(j-1)].

If v=0, the only bracket is at least eta by (3). If v>=1,
the first paired bracket is at least eta and every remaining term
is positive. This proves (14) in every case. Equation (13) is
therefore at most -(eta-1)*E_ij<0.

Equations (11)--(14) prove ALL ordered original rows, including every
old/new cross row and both directions at the unqueried terminal.

## 4. One static function and the true radius

Define y_i=X_i-g_i and form, once for the complete supplied word,

    H(v)=||v||^2/2+max{0,max_(0<=i<=n)(<y_i,v>-delta_i)},
    F(X)=sup_v(<X,v>-H(v)).                          (15)

This is the explicit full-data conjugate of accepted
`1252b8823bb5a290`. To match its notation, expand

    <X_i,g_i>-f_i+<X_i,v-g_i>+||v-g_i||^2/2
       =||v||^2/2+<X_i-g_i,v>-delta_i.

The established all-pair inequalities therefore imply that F is
everywhere finite, convex, differentiable and 1-smooth, and that
F(X_i)=f_i and grad F(X_i)=g_i for EVERY i. Consequently its actual
GD visits precisely the prescribed samples. The function is fixed
before the first update.

Every delta_i is strictly positive. The maximum in (15) therefore
equals 0 in a neighborhood of v=0. Since H(v)>=||v||^2/2, F(0)=0.
For any X!=0 choose a sufficiently small s in (0,1) with sX in that
neighborhood. Then

    F(X)>=(s-s^2/2)||X||^2>0.

Thus argmin F={0}, and the true initial squared distance is exactly
||X_0||^2=eta^2. It has not been replaced by a projected or reset radius.
The exact payoff is

    V=(2eta-1)/eta^2 * product_(i<n)(t_i*b_i).        (16)

Every queried i>=1 produces actual paid transverse action. The point
X_i has zero e_i coordinate, whereas the new coordinate of g_i is
A_i*sqrt(1-r_(i-1)^2)>0. Thus the e_i component of X_(i+1)-X_i is

    -h_i*A_i*sqrt(1-r_(i-1)^2),                     (17)

which is nonzero. In particular a two-step segment already has a
nonzero displacement transverse to its first gradient. All of it is
inside (7), (10), (13), and the distance calculation above.

For arbitrary smoothness L>0 use L*F and physical steps h_i/L; the
same normalized W=2R payoff (16) follows.

## 5. The paid continuation rule and its limit

For a jointly planned chronological segment [r,s), the amplitude and
value transfer is exactly

    q_s=q_r*E_rs, f_s=f_r*E_rs,
    X_s=X_r-sum_(i=r)^(s-1)h_i*A_i*u_i.              (18)

The direction is propagated by (6), keeping the old coordinates.
The state consists of the actual X_r,u_r,A_r, the common eta, and the
last physical-step guard; no independent Gram block or free mode is
chosen. The vector dimension grows by the prescribed orthogonal
innovation. Its coefficient is fixed by (2).

For a literal append to already fixed jets 0,...,k, the sufficient
join condition inherited from (3) is

    t_(k-1)*h_k<=h_(k-1)-eta,                       (19)

when k>=1. Here h_k is the first NEW physical step. Retain all old
jets and transition coefficients, require h_k>eta, and choose every
new transition by (2) using the supplied future word. All old guards
then remain true and (19) supplies the missing boundary guard.
The proofs of (10)--(14) apply to every pair straddling the boundary.

This proves existence of a single interpolant for the extended full
history. It does NOT mean changing the running objective at a join,
nor that the old interpolant (15) on a shorter data set already has
the required behavior outside its samples. In the intended use the
entire word, all guards and (15) are fixed before GD starts.

The old artificial value h_k=eta used to terminate a shorter word
cannot simply be replaced for free by a larger physical h_k. If (19)
fails, this sufficient append rule gives no extension claim. The
exact all-pair formula (13) remains available, but adjacent constraints
alone cannot repair it, as Section 9 demonstrates.

For perspective, even the safe family itself is extremely weak on
the original H_m of Section 8. Put ell=1+a. Every even physical index
has h_i=ell; there are N/2 such steps and the last step is even.
Let b_low=(ell-eta)/eta<a. Pair each low step except the last with
the following step H>ell. Equations (2) give

    (t_low*b_low)*(t_high*b_high)
      <=theta^2*b_low^2*(H-eta)/H<=b_low^2.

The unpaired last step has t_last=theta*b_low and contributes
theta*b_low^2<=b_low^2, since b_low<a<1. The prefactor in (16) is
at most 1 because eta^2-(2eta-1)=(eta-1)^2. Thus

    V<=b_low^N<a^N.                                 (20)

This is only the displayed guard rule. The next result treats a much
larger family, without imposing that guard or a constant eta.

## 7. A whole-class obstruction with varying positive affine values

The following hypothesis class is substantially broader than (2).
Let an ACTUAL convex 1-smooth function with minimum 0 at 0 generate
a nonzero queried trajectory g_i=A_i*u_i, A_i>0, ||u_i||=1,
for 0<=i<n. Suppose:

  (i) The initial point is X_0=eta_0*g_0.
 (ii) For every queried i there is 1<eta_i<h_i such that

          <X_i,g_i>=eta_i*q_i,
          f_i=(eta_i-1/2)*q_i, q_i=A_i^2.           (21)

(iii) There are real r_0,...,r_(n-2) in [-1,1] with

          <u_i,u_j>=product_(k=i)^(j-1)r_k, i<j<n. (22)

There is NO constraint on the terminal gradient or value beyond
being generated by the same legal function. No sufficient guards,
stationarity, common amplitude ratio, common eta, or full-support
probability assumption is imposed on this class.

First, the true initial distance is exactly eta_0*A_0. Indeed (21)
at i=0 implies f_0+q_0/2=eta_0*q_0. For any minimizer z, the
necessary smooth interpolation row gives

    f_0+q_0/2<=<g_0,X_0-z>=eta_0*q_0-<g_0,z>.

Hence <g_0,z><=0 and
||X_0-z||^2=eta_0^2*q_0-2eta_0<g_0,z>+||z||^2
>=eta_0^2*q_0. The minimizer 0 attains this distance. Thus the
denominator is proved even when the minimizer is not unique.

The Markov product identity and X_i in span(g_0,...,g_i) give

    <X_(i+1),u_(i+1)>=r_i*(eta_i-h_i)*A_i.

Combining with (21) at i+1 forces r_i<0 and gives

    b_i=(h_i-eta_i)/eta_(i+1)>0,
    A_(i+1)=-r_i*b_i*A_i,
    t_i=r_i^2*b_i>0,
    q_(i+1)=t_i*b_i*q_i,       0<=i<n-1.            (23)

The b_i in this section are the varying-ratio generalization of (2),
not an assumption that eta_i is constant. For i<j<n, (22) also gives
<X_i,g_j>=eta_i<g_i,g_j>. Full expansion of the ORIGINAL row yields

    Q_(j,i)=(eta_i-1)*q_i
             [product_(k=i)^(j-1)(-t_k)-1].         (24)

All occurrences of eta_j cancel in this identity. Thus for any two
queried indices of the same parity,

    product_(k=i)^(j-1)t_k<=1.                      (25)

This is a necessary remote-row restriction, irrespective of how the
correlations and support ratios are selected from the full word.
It is NOT asserted sufficient for interpolation.

If n is odd, its last queried index n-1 is even. Taking (i,j)=(0,n-1)
in (25), or using the empty product for n=1, gives

    q_(n-1)/q_0<=product_(i=0)^(n-2)b_i
                        <=product_(i=0)^(n-2)(h_i-1). (26)

The last inequality follows individually from eta_i>1 and
eta_(i+1)>1. Thus no fixed positive floor eta_i-1 is needed; the
bound remains uniform when every reserve ratio tends to zero.

For a final physical step ell=h_(n-1) in (1,2), smoothness gives

    2f_n<=2f_(n-1)-ell*(2-ell)*q_(n-1)
          =[2(eta_(n-1)-1)+(ell-1)^2]*q_(n-1)
          <[(ell-1)^2+2(ell-1)]*q_(n-1).           (27)

This uses the actual terminal value, without specifying its gradient
or requiring any terminal analogue of (21). Equations (26)--(27)
are the complete-class obstruction used next.

## 8. Exact comparison on the ORIGINAL enclosing-m words

For m>=4 define

    N=2^m, n=N-1, sigma=1+sqrt(2), beta=1-1/m,
    c=sigma*(1+1/m), a=beta/c,
    H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a*c^j,H_j), 0<=j<m.     (28)

The word is exactly h=H_m, with the SAME enclosing m in every child.
Its smallest and last step is ell=1+a, and 0<a<1/2, so ell<2.
The offset product is

    D_m:=product_(h_i in H_m)(h_i-1)
        =a^(N-1)*c^(N-m-1)
        =beta^(N-1)*c^(-m).                         (29)

One direct proof is induction from D_0=1 and
D_(j+1)=D_j^2*a*c^j. The displayed expression at general j is
a^(2^j-1)c^(2^j-j-1). Thus the product through the last queried
transition, omitting the final physical step, is D_m/a.

For EVERY legal instance in Section 7, divide (27) by the true
eta_0^2*q_0 and apply (26) and (29). It follows that

    V <= (a+2)*beta^(N-1)*c^(-m).                   (30)

The strict version follows when n>=1 and the ratios in (21) are
strict, but the weak inequality is the convenient uniform statement.
It holds uniformly over arbitrary horizon-dependent correlations,
amplitudes, functions, dimensions and eta_i satisfying the class
hypotheses. The terminal is entirely free.

Let B_n and U_n^ZJ=1/B_n be the ORIGINAL all-split denominator and
benchmark in accepted `b98bb807d514e80d`. That fact gives
B_n<=1+4n^(log_2 3)<1+4*3^m. Since beta^(N-1)<=exp(-(N-1)/m),

    B_n*V <= (a+2)*(1+4*3^m)*c^(-m)*exp(-(2^m-1)/m)
             ->0.                                  (31)

For completeness, c>2 and a+2<5/2 bound the logarithm of all factors
except the final exponential by a constant plus m*log(3/2).
The quantity 2^m/m divided by m tends to infinity. This proves the
limit without a numerical evaluation or a benchmark replacement.

Equation (31) closes this precise class as a source of a matching
lower witness on the original diagnostic words. It does NOT show
small unrestricted W_n(H_m), and it does NOT decide the original

## 9. Exact failure of local-only interpolation checks

Take the physical word h=(3,10) and eta=2, but now choose the legal
Markov correlations r_0=-1/sqrt(2), r_1=-1/2. Their corresponding
parameters in (23) are b=(1/2,4), t=(1/4,1). They have t_0*t_1=1/4,
so even the same-parity reverse-row condition holds. Use

    g_0=e_0,
    g_1=(-e_0+e_1)/4,
    g_2=(e_0-e_1)/4+sqrt(3/8)*e_2,
    X_0=2e_0, X_1=-e_0, X_2=(3e_0-5e_1)/2,
    q=(1,1/8,1/2), f=(3/2,3/16,3/4).

These are literal real coordinates, with every innovation nonzero,
and every sample has <X_i,g_i>=2q_i and delta_i=q_i>0.
All optimum rows pass. The adjacent rows are

    Q_01=-7/8, Q_10=-5/4,
    Q_12=-11/8, Q_21=-1/4.

The remote reverse row also passes: Q_20=-3/4. But

    Q_02=3/4>0.                                    (32)

For a direct check, f_0-f_2=3/4,
<g_0,X_2-X_0>=-1/2, and ||g_0-g_2||^2=1.
Thus (32) follows from (1). There is no static convex 1-smooth
interpolant for these jets. This is a counterexample ONLY to the
local-only sufficiency claim, not a legal GD counterexample to any
theorem about actual functions.

## 10. The remaining interface and a priced escape identity

Changing eta_i alone does not escape (30) while the other hypotheses
of Section 7 hold. The crucial conditions are aligned initial support,
the Markov product relation, h_i>eta_i>1, and tight optimum upper rows.
Actual transverse displacement, positive affine values and varying
dependence have all been included in the excluded class.

One precise escape is to retain nonzero optimum slack. Define

    z_i=<X_i,g_i>-f_i-q_i/2>=0,
    eta_i=<X_i,g_i>/q_i,
    f_i=(eta_i-1/2)*q_i-z_i.                         (33)

Under the same aligned-anchor Markov support calculation, the actual
reverse row becomes, by expanding its value difference,

    Q_(j,i)=(eta_i-1)*q_i
             [product_(k=i)^(j-1)(-t_k)-1]+z_i-z_j. (34)

Positive reserves require z_i<(eta_i-1)*q_i. Thus it is invalid to
keep the product amplification while dropping its cost z_j in the
terminal value. Another possible escape introduces an initial anchor
with correlations to future innovations, in which case the support
recursion (23) and (24) acquire additional terms. Allowing support
ratios to cross physical step sizes also changes the sign/parity
argument. None of these broader interfaces is closed by this draft.

The next round should preserve the actual law, every old/new row,
the true distance and the slack cost in (33)--(34). A generic free
Gram matrix or a finite prescribed-word success would not close the
arbitrary-h quantifier gap. No new computational or verifier allowance
is inferred from this candidate.
