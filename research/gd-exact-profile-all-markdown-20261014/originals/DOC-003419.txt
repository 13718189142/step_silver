# Exact Constant Matching Lower Bound for Predetermined-Step Gradient Descent

Resolve the following minimax problem completely.

The objective is not merely to recover the silver exponent. The goal is to determine whether the best possible predetermined nonnegative stepsize schedule for gradient descent matches, asymptotically and with the correct leading constant/profile, the objective-value schedule constructed by Zehao Zhang and Rujun Jiang.

Throughout, “complexity” means the convergence/iteration-complexity rate as a function of the number of gradient steps \(n\), not the computational complexity of constructing a stepsize schedule.

---

## 1. Optimization model

Let \(L>0\), and let \(\mathcal C^{1,1}_L\) denote the class of all finite-dimensional convex differentiable functions

\[
f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R
\]

whose gradient is \(L\)-Lipschitz and which possess at least one minimizer \(x_\star\).

For a predetermined nonnegative stepsize schedule

\[
h=(h_0,\ldots,h_{n-1})\in[0,\infty)^n,
\]

gradient descent is

\[
x_{t+1}
=
x_t-\frac{h_t}{L}\nabla f(x_t),
\qquad
t=0,\ldots,n-1.
\]

The schedule may depend on the horizon \(n\), but it must be chosen before seeing \(f\), \(x_0\), gradients, function values, or any other oracle information.

Define the normalized worst-case objective error

\[
\mathcal W_n(h)
:=
\sup
\frac{2\bigl(f(x_n)-f_\star\bigr)}
{L\|x_0-x_\star\|^2},
\]

where the supremum is over

- every finite dimension \(d\);
- every \(f\in\mathcal C^{1,1}_L\);
- every minimizer \(x_\star\in\arg\min f\);
- every \(x_0\neq x_\star\).

Define the minimax value

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_n^\star
:=
\inf_{h\in[0,\infty)^n}\mathcal W_n(h).
}
\]

The normalization is chosen so that it agrees directly with the constants in Zhang–Jiang.

If instead one uses the normalization

\[
\mathcal R_n(h)
=
\sup
\frac{f(x_n)-f_\star}{L\|x_0-x_\star\|^2},
\]

then

\[
\mathcal W_n(h)=2\mathcal R_n(h).
\]

Do not mix the two normalizations.

---

## 2. The Zhang–Jiang benchmark

Write

\[
\rho
:=
\log_2(1+\sqrt2)
=
1.271553303\ldots.
\]

Define

\[
\varphi(x,y)
=
\frac{
-x-y+\sqrt{(x+y+2)^2+4(x+1)(y+1)}
}{2},
\]

and

\[
\psi(x,y)
=
\frac{
3-2y+\sqrt{(2y+1)(2y+8x+9)}
}{4}.
\]

Define two scalar sequences recursively.

First,

\[
S_\circ(0)=0,
\]

and for every \(n\ge1\),

\[
\boxed{
S_\circ(n)
=
\max_{0\le k<n}
\Bigl[
S_\circ(k)
+
\varphi\!\bigl(S_\circ(k),S_\circ(n-k-1)\bigr)
+
S_\circ(n-k-1)
\Bigr].
}
\]

Second,

\[
S_\bullet(0)=0,
\]

and for every \(n\ge1\),

\[
\boxed{
S_\bullet(n)
=
\max_{0\le k<n}
\Bigl[
S_\circ(k)
+
\psi\!\bigl(S_\circ(k),S_\bullet(n-k-1)\bigr)
+
S_\bullet(n-k-1)
\Bigr].
}
\]

These are the sums of the primitive and dominant schedules generated by the Zhang–Jiang concatenation dynamic program.

Let \(h_\bullet^{(n)}\) be any Zhang–Jiang dominant schedule attaining \(S_\bullet(n)\).

Their analysis gives the exact worst-case objective profile

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_n\!\left(h_\bullet^{(n)}\right)
=
\frac{1}{2S_\bullet(n)+1}.
}
\]

The equality is important: the associated Huber instance attains the bound.

Define

\[
\boxed{
U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}
:=
\frac{1}{2S_\bullet(n)+1}.
}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star
\le
U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}
\qquad
\text{for every }n.
\]

This exact sequence, rather than the decimal \(0.4239\), is the benchmark that must be matched.

---

## 3. Exact problem to resolve

Determine whether

\[
\boxed{
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\mathcal W_n^\star}
{U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}}
=
1.
}
\tag{P}
\]

Equivalently, determine whether

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_n^\star
\ge
\frac{1-o(1)}
{2S_\bullet(n)+1}.
}
\tag{LB}
\]

Since the reverse inequality

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star
\le
\frac{1}{2S_\bullet(n)+1}
\]

is already supplied by the Zhang–Jiang schedule, proving (LB) establishes asymptotic minimax optimality of their schedule horizon by horizon.

The quantifiers in (LB) are strict:

for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there must exist \(N_\varepsilon<\infty\) such that for every \(n\ge N_\varepsilon\) and every predetermined schedule \(h\in[0,\infty)^n\),

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_n(h)
\ge
\frac{1-\varepsilon}
{2S_\bullet(n)+1}.
}
\tag{Q}
\]

The threshold \(N_\varepsilon\) must be independent of the particular schedule \(h\).

The adversarial function and its dimension may depend on \(n\) and on \(h\).

---

## 4. What “matching the complexity and constant” means

A complete affirmative solution must prove (P), not merely the silver exponent.

In particular, since

\[
S_\bullet(n)=\Theta(n^\rho),
\]

(P) automatically implies the optimal polynomial convergence exponent

\[
\rho=\log_2(1+\sqrt2).
\]

It also matches the complete asymptotic constant profile, including any genuine oscillation of \(S_\bullet(n)/n^\rho\).

For reference, define

\[
\bar\nu_\bullet
:=
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
\frac{S_\bullet(n)}{n^\rho}
\]

and

\[
C_{\mathrm{ZJ}}
:=
\frac{1}{2\bar\nu_\bullet}.
\]

Then

\[
\limsup_{n\to\infty}
n^\rho U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}
=
C_{\mathrm{ZJ}}.
\]

Therefore (P) implies

\[
\boxed{
\limsup_{n\to\infty}
n^\rho\mathcal W_n^\star
=
C_{\mathrm{ZJ}}.
}
\]

However, proving only this limsup identity is weaker than (P) and does **not** count as a complete affirmative solution.

Likewise, proving merely

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star=\Theta(n^{-\rho})
\]

does not count.

---

## 5. Important warning about the number \(0.4239\)

Do not formulate the theorem as

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star
\sim
0.4239\,n^{-\rho}.
\]

That is not the precise Zhang–Jiang theorem.

Their rigorous asymptotic statement is expressed through

\[
\bar\nu_\bullet
=
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
S_\bullet(n)/n^\rho
\]

and the coefficient

\[
(2\bar\nu_\bullet)^{-1}.
\]

The frequently quoted number \(0.4239\) comes from an additional numerical estimate for \(\bar\nu_\bullet\), and is not itself the exact rigorously identified asymptotic constant.

The paper also notes numerical evidence for still smaller constants.

Thus:

- \(0.4239\) may be used as a numerical sanity check;
- it may not be assumed as an exact constant;
- a proof that only matches \(0.4239\) numerically is not sufficient;
- the rigorous benchmark is \(U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}=(2S_\bullet(n)+1)^{-1}\).

---

## 6. Current lower-bound benchmark

A recent lower bound of Ye and Liu considers, after setting \(L=1\),

\[
\mathcal R_n(h)
=
\sup_{f,x_0}
\frac{f(x_n)-f_\star}
{\|x_0-x_\star\|^2},
\qquad
r_n^\star
=
\inf_{h\ge0}\mathcal R_n(h),
\]

and proves that for some absolute \(C>0\),

\[
r_n^\star
\ge
n^{-\left(
\rho+
C\sqrt{\frac{\log\log n}{\log n}}
\right)}
\]

for all sufficiently large \(n\).

Equivalently, in the normalization of this problem,

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star
\ge
2
n^{-\left(
\rho+
C\sqrt{\frac{\log\log n}{\log n}}
\right)}.
\]

This establishes the correct polynomial exponent only up to a subpolynomial loss.

It does **not** establish (P).

Removing the exponent loss alone is also not sufficient: the leading asymptotic constant/profile must be matched.

---

## 7. Existing hard-function tool

The Ye–Liu lower bound may be used as a starting point.

For checkpoints

\[
1\le t_1<\cdots<t_k\le n,
\]

write

\[
b_i=h_{t_i},
\qquad
s_i
=
\sum_{j=t_{i-1}+1}^{t_i-1}h_j,
\]

with \(t_0=0\), and let \(s_{k+1}\) denote the sum of the steps after the last checkpoint.

For suitable parameters \(\epsilon,\gamma>0\), putting

\[
c=\sqrt{1+2\gamma+\epsilon^2},
\]

their construction yields constants \(a=a(\epsilon,\gamma)\) and \(B_0=B_0(\epsilon)\) such that, when every checkpoint obeys \(b_i\ge B_0\),

\[
\mathcal R_n(h)
\ge
\frac{1}{4(1+s_{k+1})}
\prod_{i=1}^k
\left[
\frac{b_i}
{c(a+s_i)+\epsilon b_i}
\right]^2.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal W_n(h)
\ge
\frac{1}{2(1+s_{k+1})}
\prod_{i=1}^k
\left[
\frac{b_i}
{c(a+s_i)+\epsilon b_i}
\right]^2.
}
\tag{Y--L}
\]

You may sharpen, replace, or completely abandon this construction.

Do not assume that merely optimizing its existing parameters is sufficient.

A central obstruction in the current proof is that the auxiliary constant \(a(\epsilon,\gamma)\) deteriorates rapidly when parameters are sent toward the regime needed to recover the exact silver exponent. Any proposed constant-level proof must track such losses quantitatively and cannot hide them inside \(o(1)\) unless that \(o(1)\) is genuinely negligible relative to \(U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}\).

---

## 8. Acceptable complete resolutions

Assume for purposes of this task that the question admits a rigorous resolution, but do not assume in advance that the answer to (P) is affirmative.

### Affirmative resolution

Prove

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\mathcal W_n^\star}
{U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}}
=
1.
\]

Because the upper bound is known, the substantive requirement is the uniform lower bound (Q).

A complete proof must apply to every sufficiently large horizon \(n\) and every predetermined nonnegative schedule of length \(n\).

### Negative resolution

Prove rigorously that (P) is false.

For example, it is sufficient to establish that there exist

\[
\delta>0,
\qquad
n_j\to\infty,
\qquad
h^{(j)}\in[0,\infty)^{n_j},
\]

such that

\[
\mathcal W_{n_j}\!\left(h^{(j)}\right)
\le
(1-\delta)
U_{n_j}^{\mathrm{ZJ}}
\]

for every \(j\), with a rigorous worst-case upper-bound certificate.

Equivalently, it is sufficient to prove

\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\mathcal W_n^\star}
{U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}}
<1.
\]

Numerical evidence is not a negative resolution.

Failure to prove the lower bound is not evidence that it is false.

---

## 9. Results that do not count as a solution

Do **not** stop for any of the following:

- reproducing the known \(n^{-\rho-o(1)}\) lower bound;
- proving \(n^{-\rho-\varepsilon}\) for every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\);
- proving only that \(\rho\) is the optimal polynomial exponent;
- proving \(\mathcal W_n^\star=\Theta(n^{-\rho})\);
- obtaining an unspecified positive constant times \(n^{-\rho}\);
- obtaining a constant strictly smaller than the Zhang–Jiang benchmark;
- proving the desired constant only along \(n=2^m-1\);
- proving it along any other subsequence;
- proving only a limsup or liminf lower bound;
- proving only
  \[
  \limsup n^\rho\mathcal W_n^\star=C_{\mathrm{ZJ}};
  \]
- showing that a candidate hard instance works for the silver schedule but not for every schedule;
- restricting stepsizes to a bounded interval;
- assuming stepsizes are monotone, ordered, separated, or bounded away from zero;
- assuming the schedule has a prescribed recursive/composable form;
- assuming all large steps can be selected as checkpoints without proving this;
- proving a lower bound only for Zhang–Jiang's class of concatenated schedules;
- proving a lower bound only for “basic”, “composable”, periodic, symmetric, or silver-like schedules;
- replacing nonnegative schedules by positive schedules without treating zero steps;
- permitting the adversary to choose the schedule after seeing the function;
- allowing adaptive stepsizes, momentum, averaging, restarting, or additional state;
- proving a result for gradient norm rather than last-iterate objective gap;
- solving the anytime version instead of the finite-horizon non-anytime problem;
- changing the performance normalization;
- dropping the factor \(1/2\) in the Zhang–Jiang normalization;
- treating \(0.4239\) as an exact proven constant;
- using floating-point calculations as proof of an asymptotic constant;
- producing a stronger lower bound than the Zhang–Jiang upper bound without noticing the resulting contradiction;
- hiding a subpolynomial loss inside a claimed constant;
- obtaining a bound of the form
  \[
  n^{-\rho}\exp[-o(\log n)]
  \]
  rather than
  \[
  (1-o(1))U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}};
  \]
- proving finite-\(n\) inequalities for finitely many computed horizons;
- providing only a conjectured extremal schedule;
- providing only a conjectured hard function;
- reducing the problem to an unproved statement of essentially the same minimax strength;
- reporting a promising lemma, numerical pattern, or “substantial progress”.

Intermediate positive progress is not a stopping condition.

---

## 10. Proof standards

Every invoked theorem must be stated with the hypotheses needed here.

In particular, carefully verify:

- convexity;
- \(L\)-smoothness;
- existence of a minimizer;
- the exact GD recursion;
- nonnegativity of all stepsizes;
- the distinction between a schedule chosen for a fixed horizon and an infinite anytime schedule;
- the normalization by \(L\|x_0-x_\star\|^2/2\);
- uniformity over every schedule \(h\in[0,\infty)^n\);
- whether constants are absolute or parameter dependent;
- every use of \(o(1)\), including its sign and uniformity;
- every limit interchange;
- every passage between \(\mathcal R_n\) and \(\mathcal W_n\);
- every use of finite-dimensional interpolation;
- every claim that a constructed collection of values and gradients is realizable by a convex \(L\)-smooth function.

A lower-bound construction may depend on \(n\) and on the entire predetermined schedule \(h\), because the worst-case function is chosen after the schedule.

Its dimension may grow with \(n\).

---

## 11. Promising proof families to investigate

Use genuinely different approaches rather than repeatedly varying one incomplete argument.

Relevant families include:

- exact smooth-convex interpolation and primal PEP constructions;
- dual PEP certificates and complementary slackness;
- deriving a dual dynamic program to the Zhang–Jiang concatenation recursion;
- minimax/Bellman formulations in which \(\varphi\) and \(\psi\) arise from primal-dual optimality;
- hard functions whose recursive composition exactly mirrors ConPP/ConPD;
- sharper checkpoint constructions without the \(\epsilon\)-dependent constant explosion;
- curved-gradient or Moreau-envelope constructions with exact rather than approximate transfer factors;
- piecewise quadratic or Huber-type interpolation constructions;
- finite-dimensional resisting-oracle constructions;
- composition/duality of extremal first-order methods;
- exact characterization of the PEP value for arbitrary nonnegative stepsizes;
- identification of a scalar functional \(V(h)\) satisfying both a universal lower bound and the same concatenation recursion as \(S_\bullet\);
- structural reductions proving that an arbitrary schedule can be decomposed at essentially no constant loss;
- induction on \(n\) with a sharp split inequality;
- continuous scaling limits of the recursion, but only when converted back to rigorous discrete estimates with the correct constant;
- exact algebraic or semialgebraic certificates for small horizons that reveal the correct general recursion.

Do not assume one of these approaches must succeed.

---

## 12. Multiagent search protocol

Use multiagent v2 aggressively and dynamically, with up to four concurrent agents if available.

Begin with a genuinely diverse portfolio.

Do not tell most early agents what the currently favored proof route is.

Maintain an explicit registry of mathematical approach families, organized by actual mechanism rather than wording.

If multiple agents converge to the same checkpoint construction, PEP dual, or recurrence argument, redirect some agents to genuinely different formulations.

Keep both possibilities alive:

1. the Zhang–Jiang schedule is asymptotically minimax optimal;
2. another schedule beats its constant/profile.

Do not discard the second possibility merely because the exponents match.

When an approach reaches a missing lemma whose strength is essentially equivalent to (Q), mark the route as blocked. Do not call that lemma “technical” or “routine”.

Only reopen such a route if a new mechanism appears.

---

## 13. Computational agents

Use computation as an adversarial and discovery tool, not as proof.

For moderate \(n\), agents should attempt to compute or tightly bound:

\[
\mathcal W_n^\star,
\qquad
U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}},
\qquad
\frac{\mathcal W_n^\star}{U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}},
\]

using exact or high-precision PEP/SDP/branch-and-bound methods where possible.

They should also:

- compute \(S_\circ(n)\) and \(S_\bullet(n)\);
- inspect the maximizing split indices;
- search for schedules beating \(h_\bullet^{(n)}\);
- test proposed universal hard-function inequalities;
- search for counterexamples to intermediate lemmas;
- inspect primal and dual PEP optimizers;
- identify rank patterns and active interpolation constraints;
- attempt rational/algebraic reconstruction of numerical certificates;
- verify normalization factors independently.

A numerical counterexample to a proposed lemma is useful and should kill that lemma.

A numerical confirmation of the main conjecture is not a proof.

---

## 14. Adversarial audit

Every proposed complete affirmative proof must be independently attacked for at least the following failure modes:

- it actually proves only the exponent;
- a fixed \(\varepsilon>0\) remains in the exponent;
- \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_n\to0\) creates a hidden constant blowup;
- it proves only infinitely many horizons;
- it proves only dyadic horizons;
- its \(o(1)\) depends on the schedule;
- its hard function is not globally convex and smooth;
- the interpolation data are inconsistent;
- the minimizer used in the normalization is not valid;
- the dimension is implicitly infinite without justification;
- very large stepsizes are excluded;
- zero stepsizes break the argument;
- a checkpoint-selection lemma silently loses a constant;
- a product inequality loses a nonvanishing factor;
- a factor \(2\) is lost when translating between normalizations;
- the Zhang–Jiang constant is replaced by \(0.4239\);
- a limsup statement is mistaken for horizon-by-horizon asymptotics;
- a numerical observation is used inside a supposedly analytic proof;
- the proof establishes optimality only within a restricted schedule family;
- a PEP dual certificate is feasible only numerically rather than exactly;
- strong duality is invoked without verifying its hypotheses;
- an asymptotic recurrence is substituted for the exact discrete one;
- the final lower bound exceeds a known rigorous upper bound because of a normalization error.

A proof survives only if all such issues are resolved explicitly.

---

# 1. 完整命题与假设

本项目研究：预先确定的非负步长梯度下降，是否在最后一次迭代的目标函数误差上，渐近精确匹配 Zhang–Jiang 构造的最佳常数曲线。

完整原始任务逐字保存在 [PROBLEM.md](PROBLEM.md)。其 SHA-256 为 `3ecc3159a696e8528ebc727776d42f657cfbc6842e9212b28c870fcd05edb89d`，与最初用户附件及导入副本一致。下文是方便新系统阅读的数学整理，不修改原任务。文献归一化的校正见第 3 份文件。

## 原始模型

固定 $L>0$。函数 $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$ 为凸、可微，梯度 Lipschitz 常数至多为 $L$，并且存在最小点。维数 $d$ 可以是任意有限正整数。

给定迭代长度 $n$，先选择有限非负步长序列

\[
h=(h_0,\ldots,h_{n-1})\in[0,\infty)^n.
\]

它可以依赖 $n$，不能依赖随后选择的函数、初始点或算法运行中看到的梯度、函数值。允许零步长，也允许任意大的有限步长。算法只有

\[
x_{t+1}=x_t-\frac{h_t}{L}\nabla f(x_t),\qquad 0\le t<n.
\]

这里没有动量、重启、平均、附加状态或自适应步长。评价的是实际最后一点 $x_n$，不是最好的一步或迭代平均。

对每个原始选择的最小点 $x_\star\in\arg\min f$ 和不同的初始点 $x_0\ne x_\star$，定义

\[
\mathcal W_n(h)=\sup_{d,f,x_\star,x_0}
\frac{2(f(x_n)-f_\star)}{L\|x_0-x_\star\|^2},
\qquad
\mathcal W_n^\star=\inf_{h\in[0,\infty)^n}\mathcal W_n(h).
\]

上确界覆盖全部上述对象。对手可以在看完整个 $h$ 后选择函数、维数和初始点；选择步长的人不能反过来看到函数后再改步长。若使用无分子因子 2 的 $\mathcal R_n$，本项目固定 $\mathcal W_n=2\mathcal R_n$。存在多个最小点时，不能随意改变原始分母；使用最近最小点必须说明相应的最优化关系。

## 精确基准

记 $\lambda=1+\sqrt2$、$\rho=\log_2\lambda$。为避免只比较一个小数常数，保留完整递推：

\[
\Omega(x,y)=\frac{x+y+\sqrt{x^2+6xy+y^2}}2,
\qquad
G(x,y)=2x+\frac y2+\frac{\sqrt{y^2+8xy}}2.
\]

令 $C_0=B_0=1$，对于每个整数 $n\ge1$，

\[
C_n=\max_{0\le k<n}\Omega(C_k,C_{n-k-1}),
\qquad
B_n=\max_{0\le k<n}G(C_k,B_{n-k-1}).
\]

最大值中的 $k$ 遍历全部整数切分。它们与原文记号满足 $C_n=1+S_\circ(n)$、$B_n=1+2S_\bullet(n)$。定义

\[
U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}=\frac1{B_n}.
\]

Zhang–Jiang 的 dominant schedule 达到精确最坏值 $U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}$，从而 $\mathcal W_n^\star\le U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}$。本项目要判断

\[
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\mathcal W_n^\star}{U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}}=1.}
\]

这要求匹配完整常数曲线，包括可能存在的随尺度变化的振荡。不能把 $U_n^{\mathrm{ZJ}}$ 替换成 $0.4239n^{-\rho}$，也不能把全部整数切分自行替换为平衡切分。

## 量词必须保留

肯定方向的缺失内容等价于：对每个 $\eta>0$，存在仅依赖 $\eta$ 的 $N_\eta$，使每个 $n\ge N_\eta$ 和每个 $h\in[0,\infty)^n$ 都满足

\[
\mathcal W_n(h)\ge\frac{1-\eta}{B_n}.
\]

一个硬函数可以依赖 $n,h$，维数可以增长；阈值 $N_\eta$ 不能依赖具体的 $h$。仅在某些步数、某类递归步长或某类函数上证明结论，不能满足这个量词。

# 2. 目标版本与成功标准

截至本包整理，原始命题 OPEN。已有 18 份旧 Danus 接受事实均为有明确适用范围的支撑结论；没有完整肯定证明或完整否定证明。

## 可接受的完整结果

**肯定结果**：证明第 1 份文件中的统一下界，对所有充分大的整数步数和全部非负步长序列成立；与已有 Zhang–Jiang 上界合并得到比值趋于 1。

**否定结果**：例如构造固定 $\delta>0$、$n_j\to\infty$ 及预定非负序列 $h^{(j)}$，并对全部原始函数类证明

\[
\mathcal W_{n_j}(h^{(j)})\le(1-\delta)U_{n_j}^{\mathrm{ZJ}}.
\]

否定方向的子序列足够；肯定方向的子序列下界不够。上式需要真实的最坏情形上界证书，不能由一批跑得较快的函数样本推出。

## 目前最具体的否定接口，仍为条件式

对每个 enclosing depth $m\ge1536$，令

\[
\epsilon_m=\frac1{4m},\quad \beta=1-\epsilon_m,\quad
c=(1+\sqrt2)(1+\epsilon_m),\quad a=\beta/c,
\]
\[
H_0^{(m)}=(),\qquad H_{j+1}^{(m)}=(H_j^{(m)},1+ac^j,H_j^{(m)}).
\]

所有子块继承同一 $m$ 的参数；记 $S_j=\sum H_j^{(m)}$、$n_m=2^m-1$、$D_m=1+2S_m$。注意这个 $S_j$ 是候选族的质量，不是基准最优序列的 $S_\circ(j)$。

旧 Danus 接受事实 `931b9653ce0f7201` 给出

\[
C_n\le(n+1)^\rho,\qquad B_n\le\frac{12}{5}(n+1)^\rho,
\qquad \frac{B_{n_m}}{D_m}<\frac{47}{50}.
\]

因此若能对所有充分大的 $m$ 证明完整函数类上界

\[
\mathcal W_{n_m}(H_m^{(m)})\le\frac{17}{16D_m},
\]

便得到比值子序列小于 $799/800<1$，从而否定原命题。**上述全函数类上界尚未证明**。接受事实原文将全 $m\ge1536$ 写入条件；只要求充分大的 $m$ 也能通过同一逐点比较得到所需渐近结论。

## 目前最具体的上界证明输入，仍未闭合

用 $\widehat C_j=1+S_j$ 避免与基准 $C_n$ 混淆，需对每个充分大的 $m$、每个 $1\le j<m$ 及每条实际光滑凸轨迹证明

\[
E_j:=2\sum_{i<N_j}h_i(f_i-f_{N_j})
-\sum_{i<N_j}h_i(h_i-1)\|g_i\|^2
-\left(\frac7{10}\widehat C_j^2-\widehat C_j\right)\|g_{N_j}\|^2\ge0,
\quad N_j=2^j-1,
\]

此处归一化 $L=1$。已经写成完整草稿的 PD 拼接能从此输入推出总权重 $1+(32/17)S_m$ 的完整优势证书；其标量余项行列式有统一正下界。但此输入的全深度成立性没有证明，它是路线的真实缺口。

## 不能据此宣布完成

- 只得到 Silver 指数、$\Theta(n^{-\rho})$、未知正常数或只有 limsup 的结果。
- 只覆盖递归、basic、composable、正步长、有限范围步长或某个特殊函数族。
- 有限 PEP 数值、有限步数精确算例、局部最优性或未经证明的 SDP 可行性。
- 仅把原题换成另一个同等难的全称命题。
- 将“每个固定 $j$，足够大 $m$”交换成“足够大 $m$，所有 $j<m$”。
- 把一个证书模板失败说成原命题失败，或把某类硬函数太弱说成全函数类上界。

完整候选需要给出假设、实际对象、全量词、原始分母、所有常数和依赖链，并经过独立审读。旧系统接受或新系统 verifier 接受均不等同于证明助手形式化；准备对外使用的数学结果仍需人类核查。

## R13 最值得保留的三个结果

**第一，真实轨迹排除了保留全部弱左余量的归纳。** 令子块质量 $s$、$\widehat C=1+s$、桥步超额 $k$、$r=k/\widehat C$。在静态函数 $g(x)=\operatorname{clamp}(x,-r,1)$ 上，从原始点 $\widehat C$ 出发，左块全梯度为 1，右块全梯度为 $-r$，最后点为 $-r$。对 $\theta=7/10$，

\[
E_L=(1-\theta)\widehat C^2,quad
E_R=(1-\theta)\widehat C^2r^2,quad
E_P=\widehat C^2\{1-\theta[r(2+r)]^2\}.
\]

实际 $j\ge3$ 时 $r>\sqrt2-1$，所以 $E_P<E_L$。因此系数 $\alpha\ge1$ 的规则 (E_P\ge\alpha E_L+\beta E_R+\text{非负项}) 不成立。与此同时 $E_P>0.29\widehat C^2$，所以这不是弱 history 本身的反例。

**第二，每个固定深度有严格余量。** 在 $\epsilon=0$ 的精确 Silver word 上，标准 history 等号加终端梯度为零，递归迫使所有梯度为零。弱化后的 history 因而在归一化有限 Gram 可行集上有正最小值。紧致性与连续性给出：对每个固定 $j$，存在 $\eta_j>0,M_j<\infty$，使所有 $m\ge M_j$ 都满足 $E_j\ge(\eta_j/2)\sum_i\|g_i\|^2$。缺失的是随 $j$ 增长的定量控制；不可以交换量词。

**第三，递增权重的简单证书锥仍不够。** 对 $u_i=(1+T_i/(2S))h_i$，整个指定正区间模态锥的完整对角条件在极限强迫 $1+r^3\le1+r/6$，与 $6r^2>1$ 矛盾。一般系数矩阵未被排除。另一份真实 Huber 分析允许增长深度的内层相位，仍得到该指定残差的统一正值；它不能证明全函数类成立。
