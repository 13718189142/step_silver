## 1. The intrinsic property and every original vector

Normalize L=1. For a nonnegative word h of length n let s=sum h_i,
H_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, and

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                      -s(s+1)||g_n||^2.

Call h primitive if V_h>=0 on every actual smooth convex run. This is exactly
Zhang--Jiang's primitive property: expansion of their difference of squared
positions cancels x_0 and all mixed gradient terms and leaves V_h. No norm
of x_0 is being substituted for the original objective distance.

Let e denote the n+1 column of ones; define A_ii=1, A_it=h_t for t<i,
and A_it=0 for t>i. The formal gradients g_0,...,g_n are independent.
Choose ALL n(n+1) ordered finite multipliers lambda_ij>=0, i!=j; their
zero diagonal is conventional. Define L=diag(lambda e)-lambda and

 omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(n-1),2s)^T,
 D=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(n-1)(2-h_(n-1)),-s(s+2)).

Then the full sufficient cone is exactly

 L^T e=omega,                 M=D+(LA+A^T L^T)/2 >=0.            (1)

Indeed value cancellation in V_h+sum lambda Q is the displayed flow.
The position terms have matrix sym(L(A-I)); the squared-difference terms
have matrix sym(L)-diag(omega)/2. Adding V's diagonal gives (1), with

                  V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g)>.          (2)

Thus (1) implies V_h>=0 because Q_ij<=0. All incoming terminal edges are
present. There is no rank, symmetry, child, sparsity, or fixed-remainder
restriction on lambda or M. In particular accepted terminal reduction
1252b8823bb5a290 is NOT used to delete primitive constraints.

Adding optimum indices to this certificate cannot enlarge its feasible
cone. With x_star=g_star=f_star=0, the x_0 squared coefficient remains
zero. Its mixed coefficient with g_i is -lambda_(i,star). A PSD matrix with
a zero diagonal entry has that entire row zero (test the corresponding
2x2 principal minors). Therefore all lambda_(i,star)=0. The sum of finite
value-flow equations is then -sum lambda_(star,i)=0, since the V value
coefficients sum to zero. Nonnegativity forces all lambda_(star,i)=0.
Consequently the full optimum/position column is forced to be identically
zero; (1) preserves the entire remaining cone, not a guessed reduction.

A unit-slope Huber run started beyond 1+s has g_i=1, Q_ij=0, and V_h=0.
For any certificate (1) this gives e^T M e=0 and hence M e=0. This is an
EXACT equality kernel, never a positive full-space eigenvalue margin. The
numerical formulation below uses the full matrix without a kernel quotient.

## 2. Lossless intrinsic primal: explicitly restore a minimizer

For arbitrary finite sample jets obeying every Q_ij<=0 and the GD recursion,
there is a legal smooth convex function with a minimizer and the SAME V_h.
This justifies the intrinsic model at the level of actual functions as well
as the preceding full-certificate column argument.

Fix epsilon>0 and append one coordinate. Put

 g'_i=(g_i,epsilon), x'_i=(x_i,T-epsilon H_i),
 f'_i=f_i+c-epsilon^2 H_i.

The GD recursion remains exact, and direct substitution shows Q'_ij=Q_ij:
the extra function difference -epsilon^2(H_i-H_j) cancels the extra
position term epsilon^2(H_i-H_j). Choose the finite constants

 c>=max_i{-f_i+epsilon^2 H_i+(||g_i||^2+epsilon^2)/2},
 epsilon T>=max_i{f_i+c-<g_i,x_i>+(||g_i||^2+epsilon^2)/2}.

After adding the optimum jet (0,0,0), these are exactly Q'_(star,i)<=0
and Q'_(i,star)<=0. The full conjugate interpolation construction in
accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290 supplies an everywhere finite convex
1-smooth interpolant with this optimum and every displayed jet. Thus it
is a genuine same-function GD run with an actual minimizer.

The additional V contribution is epsilon^2 times

 2sum_i h_i(s-H_i)+sum_i h_i(1-h_i)-s(s+1)=0,

because 2sum_i h_i(s-H_i)=s^2+sum_i h_i^2. Hence V'_h=V_h exactly.
This lift does NOT preserve the original distance or normalized objective,
and is used ONLY for intrinsic primitive validity. Zero steps and n=0
cause no exception; identical original queries stay identical after a zero
step. All formal original gradients remain in the final cone model.

It follows that minimizing V_h over the full Gram G>=0, trace(G)=1, all
ordered Q_ij<=0, and a harmless value gauge f_n=0 detects primitive failure
exactly at the sign level. Every negative feasible V yields a legal failure
by the lift. Conversely every nonzero-gradient legal failure can be divided
by its positive total squared gradient to meet trace(G)=1. Zero-gradient
jets cannot have negative V, since all values then agree. A common-gradient
Huber transcript has V=0, so this minimum is always <=0.

For positive steps the primal also has strict feasibility: use a diagonal
quadratic with n+1 distinct curvatures in (0,1) and nonzero initial
coordinates, then scale to trace(G)=1. The gradient polynomial columns
z product_(t<i)(1-h_t z) have distinct degrees 1,...,n+1 and are independent
at n+1 nonzero distinct nodes (divide by z). Every finite distinct-query
residual is strictly negative. This is the same Vandermonde argument as in
accepted1252b8823bb5a290. Values are bounded after fixing f_n because the
two ordered residuals bound every f_i-f_n and trace(G)=1 bounds every Gram
entry. Thus no unbounded objective is concealed by the gauge.

## 3. Composition identities, including arbitrary PSD child remainders

These identities are rederived from the Q definition and coincide with the
proofs of Zhang--Jiang, arXiv:2410.12395v2, Theorems3.1/3.2. Their exact
local paper, definitions, and both proofs were reread. No external result
with unexamined hypotheses is needed for the algebra below.

Primitive join. Let a,b be primitive, A=1+sum a, C=1+sum b. Let d>0 solve

 d(A+C+d)=AC; T=A+C+d, kappa=(T/C)^2.

Form h=(a,1+d,b). Let p be the bridge query and z the terminal query, and
let j range over the nonterminal queries in b. Direct expansion gives

 V_h=V_a+kappa V_b-(kappa-1)[sum_j b_j Q_(j,p)+Q_(z,p)
                                  +d Q_(p,z)+sum_j b_j Q_(j,z)]. (3)

One way to audit all coefficients is to call the bracket C_aux; its value
part is 2sum_j b_j f_j+(d-C)f_p+(2-C-d)f_z, and its gradient part is

 sum_j b_j(1-b_j)||g_j||^2+[(C-d)/2-d^2]||g_p||^2
                                          +(C+d)||g_z||^2/2.

Insert these in (3), and use (kappa-1)(C-d)=2(A+d), kappa d^2=A^2,
kappa C^2=T^2. The mixed terms cancel. Since C>d and kappa>1, every
bracket coefficient is nonnegative. Child certificates with matrices M_a,
M_b produce the FULL parent remainder M_a+kappa M_b on disjoint original
child-gradient sets. They may have arbitrary PSD rank. Its mass is

 T=Omega(A,C)=(A+C+sqrt((A+C)^2+4AC))/2.

Dominant join. Define, for weights u_i>=0 of mass D=2sum h+1,

 J_(h,u)=||x_0||^2+2sum_i u_i Q_(i,star)-2D f_z
                              -||x_0-sum_i u_i g_i||^2.

Dominance means J>=0 on every legal run; it implies W(h)<=1/D by Q_i*<=0.
The empty word has u=1 and J=0. A primitive word is dominant with
u=(h,s+1), because expanding the last square gives J=V_h exactly.
For a primitive left child of mass A, and a dominant right child of mass D
and weights v, put

 mu=2A/D, r=(1+sqrt(1+8A/D))/2, gamma=A+1, beta=1+A/r.

The parent word is (a,beta,b). Its weights are u=(a,gamma,r v), all
nonnegative, of total G(A,D)=2A+D/2+sqrt(D^2+8AD)/2=Dr^2. Its identity is

 J_parent=V_a+r^2 J_right-2mu sum_(i>p) v_i Q_(i,p).             (4)

To verify the equality expand both sides. The value flow uses
mu D=(sum a)+gamma=2A. The gradient coefficients reduce to
(sum a)(sum a+1)+mu D+gamma=gamma^2,
r^2=r+mu, and r^2 beta=mu+gamma r; these cancel every mixed term and
leave the defining parent square. This proves (4), including empty
children. It proves exactly the ConPD rule in accepted benchmark notation.

## 4. A finite seed threshold that compares the ORIGINAL exact B_n

Let lambda=1+sqrt(2), rho=log_2(lambda), q=1/rho. Suppose an actual
nonnegative primitive word a has length r_seed and A_seed=1+sum a. Put
m_seed=r_seed+1. The sufficient target condition is

                       A_seed>m_seed^rho.                      (5)

A gain merely above C_(r_seed) does not imply (5).
Here are the full amplification argument and the parameter losses.
First Omega(x,y)^q<=x^q+y^q for x,y>=0. For positive arguments use
x=exp(u-t), y=exp(u+t), t>=0. It becomes J(t)>=0 where

 J(t)=q^(-1)log(2cosh(qt))-arsinh(cosh t).

J(0)=0 and J(t) tends to zero at infinity. Its derivative has the sign
of qt-alpha(t), where alpha=artanh(sinh(t)/sqrt(1+cosh(t)^2)). Direct
differentiation gives alpha'=cosh(t)/sqrt(1+cosh(t)^2), strictly increasing
from 1/sqrt(2) to1. Also 1/sqrt(2)<q<1: lambda>2 and lambda^3<16 imply
1>q>3/4>1/sqrt(2). Thus alpha-qt starts by decreasing, has strictly
increasing derivative, and tends to infinity; it crosses zero once on
t>0. Therefore J first increases and then decreases to zero, proving the
inequality. Zero arguments follow directly from Omega(x,0)=x.

Induction on each primitive binary tree now gives C_k<= (k+1)^rho.
Unrolling a maximizing dominant tree, its primitive side branches have
leaf counts l_1,...,l_t summing to n. Since G(A,D)<=D+4A,

 B_n<=1+4sum l_i^rho<=1+4n^rho.

Balanced primitive trees give C_(2^k-1)=lambda^k, by the preceding upper
bound and Omega(A,A)=lambda A. Hence B_(2^k)>=G(lambda^k,1)>=2lambda^k.
The number K=limsup B_n/n^rho thus satisfies 2<=K<=4. No phase limit is
assumed and this is the ORIGINAL exact DP sequence.

Take a fully expanded optimizing dominant tree at length n, with smallest
maximizing split as deterministic tie rule. It has exactly n primitive
empty leaves and one final dominant empty leaf: induction on the unique
right dominant chain proves this count. Replace every primitive empty
leaf by a and recompute all physical bridges by (3)-(4). Leave the final
dominant empty leaf unchanged. The actual resulting word H_n has length
m_seed n and is dominant. Homogeneity of Omega makes each primitive mass
exactly A_seed times its former mass.

Let D_n=2sum H_n+1. In an auxiliary scalar evaluation also change the
terminal dominant mass from1 to A_seed. Homogeneity of G makes that
auxiliary root A_seed B_n. Accepted0be8b54e0a33381c proves
sqrt(G(A,d))=(sqrt(d)+sqrt(d+8A))/2 and that this is increasing and
1-Lipschitz as a function of sqrt(d). Propagating the unchanged terminal
leaf discrepancy therefore gives

 sqrt(A_seed B_n)-(sqrt(A_seed)-1)<=sqrt(D_n)<=sqrt(A_seed B_n). (6)

Choose n_j with B_(n_j)/n_j^rho tending to K. Equation(6) gives
D_(n_j)/n_j^rho tending to A_seed K, whereas by the SAME global limsup
limsup B_(m_seed n_j)/(m_seed n_j)^rho<=K. Consequently

 limsup_j B_(m_seed n_j) W_(m_seed n_j)(H_(n_j))
                           <= m_seed^rho/A_seed <1.            (7)

For delta=(1-m_seed^rho/A_seed)/2>0, every sufficiently large output
horizon in this subsequence has ratio <=1-delta. This is a fixed relative
all-function upper separation and suffices to refute the original uniform
asymptotic matching claim, conditional on the actual certified seed.
The unused dominant leaf and its O(sqrt(B_n)) mass loss are explicit.
