# The exact DP already dominates every reset-doubling candidate

Status: self-contained unverified route rejection. Uses only the universal Huber lower bound in accepted fact 0be8b54e0a33381c and elementary algebra of the original DP. No new verification allocation is requested.

For every k,j>=0, the reset-doubling schedule q^(k,j) from research/max4_reset_seed_boundary_certificate.md satisfies
W_(len q^(k,j))(q^(k,j)) > U_(len q^(k,j))^ZJ.
Thus the entire stated family fails as a direct negative construction, including the smaller seed depths left open by the asymmetric-Huber test. No assertion about other richer joins or a modified family is made.

Notation: lambda=1+sqrt(2), A=lambda^k, B=beta_k-1, R=A+B+2, A=2B^2+B. The length n and shifted sum of the candidate are
n+1=2^j(2^k+2), 1+sum q^(k,j)=lambda^j R.
Write P_n=S_circle(n)+1 and D_n=2S_bullet(n)+1 (D_n is the benchmark B_n, renamed here only to avoid conflict with the seed parameter B). The shifted joins are
F(X,Y)=(X+Y+sqrt(X^2+6XY+Y^2))/2,
G(X,D)=2X+D/2+sqrt(D^2+8XD)/2.
Both are increasing in their arguments. F(X,X)=lambda X.

First, D_n>=2P_n-1 for all n. To check the needed comparison, take X,Y>=1 and C=F(X,Y), so C^2-(X+Y)C-XY=0. The polynomial whose larger root is G(X,2Y-1) is
q(z)=z^2-(4X+2Y-1)z+4X^2.
Direct substitution gives
q(2C-1)=2[X-(2X+1)(C-X-Y)].
Here C-X-Y=XY/C. Moreover C<X+2Y<=Y(2X+1), since 2XY-X-Y>=0 for X,Y>=1. Hence q(2C-1)<0. Therefore G(X,2Y-1)>2F(X,Y)-1. Induct on n along a maximizing primitive split and use the increasing dominant join. The case n=0 is equality. This proves the claim without assuming that the dominant DP literally lists every primitive schedule.

Balanced primitive splits also give P_(2^r-1)>=lambda^r for each r>=0. For our n, use the legal dominant split
m=2^(k+j)-1, ell=2^(j+1)-1, n=m+ell+1.
It follows that
D_n >= G(lambda^(k+j),2lambda^(j+1)-1)
     = lambda^j G(A,2lambda-u), where u=lambda^(-j) in (0,1].

It remains to compare this with the candidate's Huber denominator 2lambda^j R-1. Set d=2lambda-u and c=8-2lambda-u. We have d>1, c>0, d-c=4lambda-8>0, and d+c=8-2u>0. Using A=2B^2+B gives the exact identity

d^2+8Ad-(4B+c)^2
=16(d-1)B^2+8(d-c)B+(d-c)(d+c)>0.

All quantities under comparison are positive. Taking square roots and substituting into G gives
G(A,2lambda-u)>2A+2B+4-u=2R-u.
Consequently D_n>2lambda^j R-1.

Finally the Huber lower bound from fact_id 0be8b54e0a33381c yields
W_n(q^(k,j)) >=1/(2lambda^j R-1)>1/D_n=U_n^ZJ.
This is a pointwise rejection for every member of the concrete family, so it is stronger than merely ruling out a fixed asymptotic improvement. The same sum argument applies to a permutation of any such schedule, but does not establish how to modify its steps successfully.
