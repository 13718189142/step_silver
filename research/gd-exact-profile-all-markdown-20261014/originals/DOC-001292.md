## 5. Product envelope for all high-level factors

This is an algebraic estimate inside the same joint proof. Suppose a
nonnegative sequence of complete block gains has initial value J at level t
and for subsequent levels is squared and multiplied by factors at most
z*c^ell, ell=0,...,k-1. Expanding the finite product, its final value is at most

    J^(2^k)*z^(2^k-1)*c^(2^k-k-1)

no division by J or z and remains valid when either vanishes (0^0=1 only in
the empty-product cases). It is an upper bound for actual products, never
