# Two-cut inner returns and an exact rejection of the paired four-copy seed

Status: complete explicit candidate proof, not verifier-accepted. No additional
verification allocation is assumed. This refutes a proposed primitive schedule;
it is not a negative or positive resolution of the unrestricted minimax target.

## 1. Exact legal inner-return construction

Partition any nonnegative schedule chronologically into

    (block_0, b_0, block_1, b_1, block_2),

with ordinary block sums s_0,s_1,s_2. Choose numbers 0<w<=1 and R>=w.
Put

    r_outer=R+1-w,
    A=b_0-r_outer=b_0-1-R+w,
    v=A/(1+s_1),
    z=(b_1-1)v.

Require A>0 and

    (1+s_2)w <= z <= R.                                    (1)

The following SINGLE derivative is fixed before running the schedule:

    g(x)=-v                     for x<=-v,
    g(x)=x                      for -v<=x<=w,
    g(x)=w                      for w<=x<=R,
    g(x)=x-R+w                  for R<=x<=r_outer,
    g(x)=1                      for x>=r_outer.             (2)

It is continuous, nondecreasing, and 1-Lipschitz: the successive slopes are
0,1,0,1,0 and endpoint values agree. Define f(x)=integral_0^x g(t)dt.
Then f is convex, differentiable, 1-smooth, and minimized at zero with f(0)=0.
Explicitly,

    f(x)=-v x-v^2/2                            for x<=-v,
    f(x)=x^2/2                                 for -v<=x<=w,
    f(x)=w x-w^2/2                             for w<=x<=R,
    f(x)=(x-R+w)^2/2+wR-w^2                    for R<=x<=r_outer,
    f(x)=x-r_outer+1/2+wR-w^2                   for x>=r_outer.

Start at x_0=s_0+r_outer. The entire first block has gradient1 and reaches
r_outer. The first checkpoint reaches -A. The middle block has gradient -v:
every partial sum is at most s_1, so it stays in [-A,-v] and ends at -v.
The second checkpoint reaches z. By (1), the final block stays on the inner
positive plateau [w,R], with gradient w, and ends at

    x_n=z-s_2 w >= w.

All individual steps, including zero steps, obey the supplied GD recursion.
The exact normalized terminal objective is

    2 f(x_n)/x_0^2
       = [2wz-(2s_2+1)w^2]/(s_0+R+1-w)^2.                 (3)

This differs from the verified outward-ray C6 construction: the second return
can have gradient w strictly smaller than the old positive gradient1. The old
positive plateau lies farther out. The connecting ramp and its area cannot be
discarded in a primitive-energy calculation.

## 2. Exact primitive energy, retaining the remote area

Let s=s_0+b_0+s_1+b_1+s_2. The saturated intrinsic primitive energy is

    V_h=2 sum_(i<n) h_i(f_i-f_n)
          +sum_(i<n) h_i(1-h_i)g_i^2-s(s+1)g_n^2.          (4)

A primitive schedule must make (4) nonnegative for every legal smooth convex
trajectory. Set Q_i=f_i-x_i g_i+g_i^2/2. Expanding
x_(i+1)^2=x_i^2-2h_i x_i g_i+h_i^2g_i^2 and summing gives the exact identity

    V_h=x_0^2-x_n^2-2s f_n-s(s+1)g_n^2+2sum_(i<n)h_i Q_i.

For all first-block samples and the first checkpoint query, (2) gives

    Q_i=-(1-w)(R-w).

For every remaining sample, including the terminal sample, Q_i=0, since its
ray is a constant extension of the unit quadratic core. Also g_n=w and
f_n=w x_n-w^2/2. Therefore

    V_h=(s_0+R+1-w)^2
          -[z+(s_0+b_0+s_1+b_1)w]^2
          -2(s_0+b_0)(1-w)(R-w).                          (5)

The last term is the contribution of the old positive area. It is negative
and is required by the exact function values. Formula (5), with constraints
(1), is a new necessary scalar test for any claimed primitive schedule.
It is not a sufficient certificate.

Form

    s_0=s_1=A-1,  s_2=(11/5)A-1,
    b_0=1+(7/10)A,  b_1=1+A.

    w=7/44,       R=7A/20+7/88,       v=R/A,       z=R.
