## 2. One globally fixed function and the actual GD trajectory

On R^2 define, before running GD,

    J(z)=max(0,<g_A,z>,<g_B,z>,<g_C,z>),
    f(x)=min_z[J(z)+||x-z||^2/2].                       (6)

This is one finite convex polyhedral function and its unit Moreau
envelope. The quadratic proximal objective is coercive and strictly
convex, so its minimizer is unique. Subgradient monotonicity gives
||grad f(x)-grad f(y)||^2<=<grad f(x)-grad f(y),x-y>;
the minimizing inequalities identify grad f=x-prox_J(x). Thus f is
globally convex, differentiable, and 1-smooth. It is nonnegative and
minimized at origin. The two gradient directions g_A and g_B are not
collinear because v>0.

Start at x_0=(1,0)=4g_A. The displayed queries and gradients are

    x_0=(1,0),                         grad f(x_0)=g_A,
    x_1=(5/8,0),                       grad f(x_1)=g_A,

## 3. Exact negative primitive energy

The proposed saturated primitive inequality, with physical capacity
T=1+sum_(i=0)^7 h_i and S=T-1, requires nonnegativity of

    V_h=2 sum_(i=0)^7 h_i(f_i-f_8)
       +sum_(i=0)^7 h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
       -S T ||g_8||^2,                                (10)

for every convex 1-smooth GD trajectory. This is the precise primitive
energy being tested; no nonsaturated objective certificate is rejected
by merely giving it this name.

Every sampled gradient here is one of the active homogeneous plane
normals, so its actual value satisfies

    f_i=<g_i,x_i>-||g_i||^2/2, including i=8.             (11)

In particular every optimum interpolation residual is zero. Substituting
(11) into (10), and using x_(i+1)=x_i-h_i g_i, gives the complete telescope

    2sum h_i f_i+sum h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
      =sum[2h_i<g_i,x_i>-h_i^2||g_i||^2]
      =||x_0||^2-||x_8||^2,

    V_h=||x_0||^2-||x_8+Sg_8||^2.                      (12)

By (8), g_8=g_C, x_8=Z+g_C and <Z,g_C>=0. A direct calculation gives

    ||Z||^2=r(5r-8)/80,
    T^2||g_C||^2=T^2 r s^2/(160 C^2)
                 =941192/(295245 r),                  (13)

where (2) was used in the second equality. Hence the EXACT energy is

    V_h=1-r(5r-8)/80-941192/(295245 r).                  (14)

This is strictly negative with a rational margin. Since r<5/2,

    T^2||g_C||^2 >1882384/1476225>1,
    V_h < -406159/1476225 <0.                           (15)

The additional term ||Z||^2 is positive and strengthens the rejection.
No numerical residual, optimizer output, or limiting argument is needed.

## 4. Scope

Equations (6)--(15) give a legal two-dimensional counterexample to the
exact eight-step schedule (1) being primitive at its proposed physical
capacity T. It therefore cannot be used as a primitive seed for the
conditional amplification route in that form. Passing all original
single-cut tests and full-curvature equality does not save that seed.

This does not rule out a weaker objective certificate for the same
schedule, different bridge parameters, or any unrestricted minimax
