## 5. Complete exact decision of the two-energy optimization

beta,tau,zeta are those in (19).

    Psi(Earg,Garg)=beta*tau*Earg.                               (21)

If zeta^2>beta*tau, set

                         when Garg<=tau^2*Earg/zeta^2;
          2*beta*zeta*sqrt(Earg*Garg)-beta^2*Garg
                         when tau^2*Earg/zeta^2<=Garg
                                      <=zeta^2*Earg/beta^2;
          zeta^2*Earg       when Garg>=zeta^2*Earg/beta^2.       (22)

At the shared endpoints the expressions agree. These formulas include
Earg=0 or Garg=0 without division by either energy. In particular Psi(0,Garg)=0
and Psi(Earg,0)=beta*tau*Earg.

Here is a direct proof that Psi is exactly the maximum in (20). For
fixed y, the unconstrained optimal x is zeta*y/beta, truncated at
sqrt(Garg). Completing the square in x gives

 -beta^2*(x-zeta*y/beta)^2+(zeta^2-beta*tau)*y^2.

If the last coefficient is nonpositive, the maximum is zero, attained
at x=y=0, which proves (21). Otherwise this expression increases along
the unconstrained x optimum until y reaches sqrt(Earg) or x reaches
sqrt(Garg). On the latter boundary the concave quadratic in y is maximized
at min(zeta*sqrt(Garg)/tau,sqrt(Earg)); this value is at least the entry point
beta*sqrt(Garg)/zeta because zeta^2>beta*tau. These alternatives give the
three cases in (22), respectively. They also prove attainment in the
closed rectangle, rather than only an upper bound.

### 1. Statement and notation

Write a positive-excess four-step word as

    h=(1+a,1+b,1+c,1+d),    a,b,c,d>0,
    C=1+sum h=5+a+b+c+d.

    u=1+a,             delta=u^2-2,

    u*delta <= Krow*Erow/nu + 2*sqrt(Qenergy*Erow/nu).          (A)

    Erow > nu*delta^2/u^2,                                 (D)
