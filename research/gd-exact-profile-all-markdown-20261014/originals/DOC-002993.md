# Exact initial-chord coefficient family and its capacity obstruction

Status: **UNVERIFIED joint proof candidate**, max4, cumulative round24.
The current TASK authorizes symbolic research but no new fact_submit,
numerical discovery, replay, installation, or publication. No new fact is
claimed. The accepted input is04219554e41d9750, Sections10--11, for the
original full primitive matrix and ConPP identities. Every new assertion
used below is derived in this document. Earlier drafts are not premises.

The result concerns a specified family of full coefficient arrays. It is
not a characterization of the full four-step cone. Within this family the
tail's entire symmetric PSD remainder and every admissible skew completion
are free; neither its rank nor its incoming-terminal signs are frozen.
The initial-to-terminal coefficient is also free. The family has an exact
nonzero-chord rational example, but its four-step capacity cannot exceed
the exact non-dyadic ConPP baseline, which is below193/25.

## 1. Original formal identity and the kernel used here

For a nonnegative word h of length n, put s=sum h_i, C=1+s,
P_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, and retain every independent formal gradient g_0,...,g_n.
Use the original convention

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                             -s(s+1)||g_n||^2.

For any REAL off-diagonal array lambda, not necessarily nonnegative, let
L=diag(lambda*e)-lambda, where e is the all-ones column. Let T_ii=1,
T_it=h_t for t<i, and T_it=0 for t>i. Define

 omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(n-1),2s)^T,
 D_prim=diag(h_i(2-h_i) for i<n,-s(s+2)).

The value cancellation and full gradient remainder are exactly

 L^T e=omega,
 M=D_prim+sym(LT),
 V_h+sum_(i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g)>.                 (1)

Here sym(B)=(B+B^T)/2. These formulas are the accepted full identity,
which is linear in the array and hence also valid for real signed arrays.
The position contribution is sym(L(T-I)), and the gradient-difference
contribution is sym(L)-diag(omega)/2; this also checks (1) directly.

Flow alone gives e^T M e=0. Indeed

 e^T D_prim e=-s^2-sum h_i^2,
 e^T LT e=omega^T Te=s^2+sum h_i^2,

using Te=(1+P_i)_i and 2sum h_i P_i=s^2-sum h_i^2.
If M is PSD, then Me=0: nonnegativity of (e+t v)^T M(e+t v)
for both signs of sufficiently small t forces v^T Me=0 for every v.
The individual nonterminal rows are therefore

 sum_j lambda_ij(P_j-P_i)=2h_i(1+P_i),  i<n.              (2)

The terminal row is sum_(j<n)lambda_nj(s-P_j)=2s(s+1).
No second kernel is assumed. Once Me=0, the quadratic is invariant under
subtracting the same vector from every formal gradient.

For a nonnegative array and h_0=a>0, (2) also proves a<2 whenever s>0.
The first outgoing mass is positive, because sum_j lambda_0j P_j=2a.
The first diagonal is a(2-a)+(1-a)sum_j lambda_0j. It is strictly negative
if a>=2, contradicting PSD. We will need only this weak endpoint bound.

## 2. A freely variable signed tail and the full coefficient construction

Let a>0, x>=0, and let b=(b_0,...,b_(m-1)) be any nonnegative tail.
Set

 A=1+a, D=1+sum b_i, C=A+D+x,
 R=D+x, H=A+x, delta=AD-Cx,
 k=C^2/D^2, t=2CH/(DR), gamma=2k-2-t,
 sigma=H*delta/(D^2 R).                                  (3)

Thus the actual parent word is h=(a,1+x,b), its capacity is C, and
C-1=a+R. Indices0,1 are the initial query and bridge query. Tail local
indices i=0,...,m correspond to original indices i+2; write z=m+2 for
the original terminal index. Define weights w_i=b_i for i<m and w_m=1,
so sum_i w_i=D.

Take ANY real tail array ell that has value cancellation for V_b and a
FULL PSD tail remainder M_b. Require ell_ij>=0 on every edge other than
the incoming-terminal edges i<m,j=m. Those incoming entries can be
negative, subject only to the actual parent signs below. All formal tail
gradients, including at zero steps, remain independent. By Section1,
M_b has the constant-gradient kernel.

Choose a parameter Z>=0 and set

 mu=2-a^2+(a-1)R Z/a.

Define the following COMPLETE parent array; all entries not listed are0:

 lambda_01 = 2-(C-1)Z/a,
 lambda_10 = 2A-R Z/a,
 lambda_0z = Z,
 lambda_1z = 2AH/R-Z,
 lambda_(i+2,1) = t w_i,                     0<=i<=m,
 lambda_(i+2,j+2) = k ell_ij
                   +gamma*b_i*1_(i<m,j=m),  i!=j.        (4)

All indices in (4) are original indices. If m=0, the sole tail gradient
is the terminal one, and there are no within-tail entries. In the actual
four-step application m=2 and there are five formal gradients.

The exact sign conditions are

 2-(C-1)Z/a>=0,  2A-R Z/a>=0,  2AH/R-Z>=0,
 k ell_(i,m)+gamma b_i>=0 for every i<m,                 (5)

together with Z>=0 and the nonnegative nonterminal tail entries already
specified. Every remaining listed coefficient is automatically
nonnegative, because k,t>0 and

 gamma=2H[D^2+(C+D)x]/(D^2 R)>=0.

In particular (5) retains the exact lower bound on each signed incoming
tail coefficient, instead of replacing it by ordinary tail primitivity.

Write

 U=g_0-g_1,
 J=sum_(i<m)b_i g_(i+2)+g_z-Dg_1.

The full formal identity for (4) is

 V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij
   = k<M_b,Gram(g_2,...,g_z)>
       +mu||U||^2-Z<U,J>+sigma||J||^2.                  (6)

Consequently, under the stated tail hypotheses and (5), (4) is a full
nonnegative primitive certificate IF AND ONLY IF

             [ mu,   -Z/2 ]
             [ -Z/2, sigma ] >=0.                     (7)

This is equivalent to mu>=0, sigma>=0 and Z^2<=4mu*sigma.
The equivalence is on the whole original formal gradient space.

### Complete algebraic check of (6)

The relations needed for flow and row moments are

 2k=2+t+gamma,
 gamma D-tx=2H,
 tD=2CH/R,
 C=A+R=D+H.                                            (8)

At node0, outgoing minus incoming is
2-(C-1)Z/a+Z-(2A-RZ/a)=-2a.
At node1 it is 2a+Z+(2AH/R-Z)-tD=-2(1+x).
At a nonterminal tail node it is
(-2k+gamma+t)b_i=-2b_i.
The terminal flow then follows by summing all flows.

For the kernel, node0 has moment
a lambda_01+(C-1)lambda_0z=2a.
Node1 has moment -a lambda_10+R lambda_1z=2A(1+x).
For a tail index with local prefix q_i=sum_(j<i)b_j, its within-tail
moment plus its edge to node1 is

 2k b_i(1+q_i)+gamma b_i(D-1-q_i)
                       -t b_i(1+x+q_i)
   =2b_i(1+H+q_i),

by (8). This is (2) at its original prefix P_(i+2)=H+q_i.
Thus every nonterminal row of Me vanishes. Flow gives e^TMe=0, so the
terminal row vanishes too. This calculation does not assume (7).

It now suffices to compute after setting g_1=0. Let
G=sum_(i<m)b_i g_(i+2) and q=g_z. The coefficient of ||g_0||^2 is

 a(2-a)+(1-a)(lambda_01+Z)=mu,

and the only cross term involving g_0 and tail gradients is
-Z<g_0,G+q>. For the tail-only part, subtract its supplied identity
k M_b. The gamma sum of incoming-tail Q and the t sum of tail-to-bridge
Q cancel all coefficients of sum b_i(1-b_i)||g_(i+2)||^2, by
1-k+(gamma+t)/2=0. Their remaining coefficients of ||G||^2 and
<G,q> are (t-gamma)/2 and t-gamma. The remaining ||q||^2 coefficient is

 -(C-1)C+k(D-1)D
      +[t+(2AH/R-Z)+Z+gamma(D-1)]/2
   =(t-gamma)/2.

For this equality, use kD^2=C^2 and 2AH/R=tD-2H.
Finally

 (t-gamma)/2=t-(k-1)=H(AD-Cx)/(D^2R)=sigma.

This proves (6) with g_1=0. The already checked constant kernel restores
the displayed U,J form for arbitrary g_1.

Sufficiency of (7) follows from (6) and M_b PSD. For necessity, set every
tail gradient equal to one freely chosen vector v. Its tail quadratic is
zero. Then J=D(v-g_1) and U=g_0-g_1 can be varied independently, so full
PSD forces precisely (7). This argument does not delete a formal
coordinate or prescribe equality in the statement of the certificate.

## 3. A nonzero-chord rational four-step certificate

For a=1, x=0 and b=(1,1), take the tail array

 ell_01=2, ell_10=4, ell_12=8, ell_21=12,

with other entries0. Expanding (1) gives

mu=1 and sigma=4/9. The complete nonzero parent array is

 10:5/2, 04:1/2, 14:13/6,
 21:20/9, 31:20/9, 41:20/9,
 23:50/9, 32:100/9, 24:4/3, 34:212/9, 43:100/3.         (9)

Its word is (1,1,1,1), and its full remainder is exactly

 (25/9)[||g_2-g_3||^2+4||g_3-g_4||^2]
       +||U-J/4||^2+(55/144)||J||^2,
 U=g_0-g_1, J=g_2+g_3+g_4-3g_1.                        (10)

Thus the family does allow a nonzero new chord and a changed positive
remainder. Equations(9)--(10) follow symbolically from (3)--(6); no model

## 4. Two-step signed tails cannot acquire extra capacity

This section rederives the capacity consequence of the older short-tail
re-selection draft f3d96c901566e96b. It is NOT a new claim of priority and
does not use that unverified draft as a premise.

Put r=sqrt(2), lambda=1+r, and

                 D2=Omega(1,lambda).

Suppose a tail consists of u,v>=0 and has a real value-canceling full PSD
identity, with every coefficient nonnegative except possibly ell_02 and
ell_12. There is no lower bound on these two signed entries in this
section. Then

                       1+u+v<=D2.                      (11)

First let u,v>0, S=u+v and D=1+S. Let p=ell_01>=0 and q=ell_10>=0.
The two nonterminal kernel rows from (2) force

 ell_02=u(2-p)/S,
 ell_12=2(1+u)+q u/v>0.                                (12)

The first diagonal is

 M_00=u(2-u)+(1-u)(v p+2u)/S.                           (13)

If u>r, then u>1 and (13) is decreasing in p. At p=2 it is2-u^2<0.
PSD therefore forces p<2, so (12) makes ell_02>0 also. The entire
supplied tail array is then nonnegative.

Irrespective of the signs in (12), terminal outgoing coefficients are
nonnegative. Its kernel moment and diagonal give

 S ell_20+v ell_21=2S(S+1),
 ell_20+ell_21>=S(S+2).

Since S>=v, these imply v<=2D/(D+1). If u<=r, substitute
v=D-(1+u) to obtain

 D^2-(2+u)D-(1+u)<=0,
 D<=Omega(1,1+u)<=Omega(1,lambda)=D2.

If u>r, the array has already been proved nonnegative. Put
O=ell_01+ell_02. Its first moment gives 2u<=S O, and its first diagonal
gives (u-1)O<=u(2-u). Therefore 2(u-1)<=S(2-u), equivalently
u<=2D/(D+1). If v<=r, that bound
gives D<=Omega(1,1+v)<=D2.

It remains to treat u>r and v>r. On f(x)=x^2/2 with initial point1,
the three gradients are1,1-u,(1-u)(1-v), and all finite Q vanish.
The identity sum h_i(2-h_i)g_i^2=1-g_2^2 gives
V_b=1-D^2(1-u)^2(1-v)^2. Thus (1) and PSD give

                 D (u-1)(v-1)<=1.                     (14)

On the other hand (u-r)(v-r)>0 implies

 (u-1)(v-1)>(r-1)(D-2-r).

The positive root D2 satisfies D2^2-(2+r)D2-(1+r)=0, hence
(r-1)D2(D2-2-r)=1. The function (r-1)D(D-2-r) is strictly increasing
for D>=D2. Thus D>=D2 contradicts (14). This proves (11) for positive
steps, without assuming the tail is already primitive.

For u=0,v>0, the zero-step row forces ell_02=0. The terminal moment
gives ell_20+ell_21=2(v+1), so its diagonal is2-v^2 and v<=r.
For v=0,u>0, the row at index1 forces ell_10=0, and the row at index0
forces ell_01+ell_02=2. The first diagonal is2-u^2, so u<=r.
For u=v=0 the capacity is1. Thus all zero-step boundaries are included
without identifying or deleting formal gradients.

## 5. An exact obstruction for the ENTIRE parameterized family

Set

             C0=Omega(lambda,D2),
             x0=C0-lambda-D2>0.                        (15)

Every four-step certificate from (3)--(7), with ANY two-step signed tail
permitted there, has

                         C<=C0.                        (16)

This is global in the family's parameters a,x,Z and the entire tail
array. It includes curved or distant variations; it is not a first-order
normal or a fixed-remainder assertion.

By (11), D<=D2. By the original nonnegative parent signs and PSD, a<2.
Suppose first a<=r. Since H,D,R>0, sigma>=0 implies delta>=0, that is
Cx<=AD. As x=C-A-D>=0, this is equivalent to

 C<=Omega(A,D)<=Omega(lambda,D2)=C0.

Now suppose r<a<2 and C>=C0. Put eps=a^2-2>0 and K=(a-1)R/a>0,
so mu=-eps+K Z. Condition (7) has sigma>0; if sigma=0, it would force
Z=0 and mu=-eps<0. Its determinant inequality gives

 (Z-2sigma K)^2<=4sigma(sigma K^2-eps),
                         sigma K^2>=eps.               (17)

The exact simplification is

 HR=AD+Cx,
 sigma K^2=(a-1)^2[(AD)^2-(Cx)^2]/(a^2D^2),
 (a-1)^2 A^2-a^2(a^2-2)=1.

Consequently (17) forces the especially small algebraic obstruction

                        C(a-1)x<=D.                    (18)

For fixed a and x=C-A-D>=0, the expression
C(a-1)(C-A-D)/D increases with C and decreases with D. The former follows
from derivative (a-1)(2C-A-D)/D>0, and the latter follows by writing it
as (a-1)[C(C-A)/D-C]. Therefore C>=C0,D<=D2 imply

 C(a-1)x/D >= (C0/D2)(a-1)(r+x0-a).

The source relation C0 x0=lambda D2 and lambda(r-1)=1 gives

 (C0/D2)(a-1)(r+x0-a)-1
       =(C0/D2)(a-r)(1+x0-a).                          (19)

We have x0>1. Indeed D2>3, since its defining polynomial at3 is2-4r<0;
thus (lambda-1)(D2-1)=r(D2-1)>2. The polynomial
x(lambda+D2+x)-lambda D2 is strictly increasing for x>=0 and negative
at1, proving x0>1. Since r<a<2<1+x0, (19) is strictly positive.
This contradicts (18). It excludes even equality C=C0 in the a>r case
and completes the proof of (16).

The contradiction in (18)--(19) already ignores the additional upper
bounds on Z in (5). Thus no adjustment of those nonnegative coefficients,
no tail skew re-selection, and no increase of the tail PSD rank can repair
this family above C0.

For an explicit rational comparison, r<99/70 gives lambda<169/70.
At D=201/50, the polynomial D^2-(1+lambda)D-lambda is strictly greater
than362/17500>0, hence D2<201/50. At C=77/10,

 C^2-(169/70+201/50)C-(169/70)(201/50)=71/1750>0.

Monotonicity of Omega yields

                       C0<77/10<193/25.                 (20)

This proves the family cannot meet the required s>=168/25.

## 6. Exact length-four ConPP calibration at the cap

Let y=D2-1-lambda, so y>0 and y D2=lambda. Take the actual word

                    h0=(r,1+x0,1+y,r).                 (21)

It has capacity C0. Put k2=(D2/lambda)^2, t2=k2-1,
k4=(C0/D2)^2, and t4=k4-1. Its complete nonzero array is

 01:2, 10:2lambda,
 21:t4(1+y), 31:t4 r, 41:t4, 14:t4 x0,
 32:k4 t2 r, 42:k4 t2,
 24:k4 t2 y+t4(1+y),
 34:k4(2k2+t2 r)+t4 r,
 43:2lambda k4 k2.                                     (22)

Every entry is nonnegative. The inner word (1+y,r) is the ConPP join
of the empty word and the one-step r word; the outer word joins (r)
to that inner word. The accepted identity04219554e41d9750, Section11,
gives (22) with zero FULL remainder: the empty remainder and the

It also calibrates the new formulas directly: take a=r, x=x0,

No quadratic kernel was imposed during the construction or obstruction.

## 7. Restriction, counterexample to a global reading, and next interface

The declared family omits all tail-to-initial edges, all initial-to-tail
edges except the terminal chord, and all bridge-to-tail edges except its
terminal edge. It also forces the tail-to-bridge entries to be proportional
to (b_0,...,b_(m-1),1). These are construction restrictions, NOT consequences
of the full interface in04219554e41d9750.

For a concrete counterexample to the claim that every full certificate
has the proportionality restriction, consider the same unit word
(1,1,1,1). Set, for i=0,1,2,3,

 lambda_(i,i+1)=2(i+1)^2,
 lambda_(i+1,i)=2(i+1)(i+2),

with all other entries0. Direct flow gives (-2,-2,-2,-2,8), and expansion
of (1) gives sum_(i=0)^3 (i+1)^2||g_i-g_(i+1)||^2. In its cut after
index1, the outgoing tail-to-bridge entries are (12,0,0), not proportional
to (1,1,1). This array lies outside the family even though the same
word also has the different inside-family array(9). Thus this example
refutes an array-level claim only; it does not prove that the restricted
family fails to represent the existence of a certificate for some word.

The smallest obstruction to the attempted capacity gain is(18), together
with the short-tail cap and exact source factorization(19). Increasing
the PSD rank within this family cannot change it. A subsequent construction
must release an actual restriction in Section7, for example independent
tail-to-bridge coefficients, initial chords to nonterminal tail samples,
or tail-to-initial coefficients. Such a construction must again check
every actual sign and the entire original matrix. No useful member of
that enlarged family, and no separator for the unrestricted full cone,
is supplied here.
