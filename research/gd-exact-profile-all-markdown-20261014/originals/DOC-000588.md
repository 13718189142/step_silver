# Finite equal-angle obstruction to polynomial activation allowances
Status: UNVERIFIED companion to the joint spiral checkpoint candidate cdd7f057606aeb18.
No fact_submit or mathematical computation was used.

Targeted claim: for the specified spiral support geometry with a required zero
minimum at the old reset point, an inverse scale c_sp S+A(kappa), or
c_sp S+kappa b+A(kappa), can work for every ordinary word with a fixed
polynomially bounded nonnegative A(kappa) as kappa tends to0.
This claim is refuted by the following exact finite family, at draft level.

Fix 0<kappa<=1/2 and set epsilon=kappa,
Theta=arctan(1/kappa), c_sp=sqrt(1+kappa^2)exp(kappa Theta),
d_sp=1-kappa^2, T_sp=[exp(d_sp Theta/kappa)-1]/d_sp.
Put e(theta)=(cos theta,-sin theta),
e_t(theta)=(-sin theta,-cos theta), R_sp(theta)=c_sp exp(-kappa theta),
v(theta)=R_sp(theta)e(theta), and K=conv({0} union {v(theta):0<=theta<=Theta}).
The vector n_sp(theta)=e(theta)+kappa e_t(theta) supports K at v(theta):
its inner product with v(phi), divided by R_sp(theta), is
exp(-kappa Delta)(cos Delta+kappa sin Delta), Delta=phi-theta.
Its derivative is -(1+kappa^2)exp(-kappa Delta)sin Delta, so its maximum
on [-Theta,Theta] is1, at0. It is also strictly larger at v(theta) than at0.

For any integer M>=1 set Delta=Theta/M, theta_l=Theta-l Delta, and
A_D=exp(-kappa Delta)(cos Delta+sin Delta/kappa),
B_D=exp(-kappa Delta)sin Delta/kappa,
t_0=0, t_l=B_D+A_D t_(l-1),
r_l=R_sp(theta_l)[(1+t_l)e(theta_l)+kappa t_l e_t(theta_l)],
alpha_l=1-exp(-kappa Delta)cos Delta+B_D+(1+kappa^2)B_D t_(l-1).

All t_l>=0 and alpha_l>=0. Resolving radial and tangential components
gives r_l-r_(l-1)=alpha_l v(theta_l). The supporting-normal calculation
gives Pi_K(r_l)=v(theta_l). Hence the ACTUAL finite chronological
ordinary word (alpha_M,...,alpha_1) follows these projection gradients
from r_M to r_0=(kappa,-1). Every step is finite; this family is defined
analytically, without executing any sweep.

Let S_M=sum alpha_l, P_M=(r_M)_1-kappa, Q_M=-(r_M)_2-1.
Then P_M=c_sp(1+t_M)-kappa and 1+Q_M=c_sp kappa t_M.
For sufficiently large M, A_D>1. Exact summation gives
t_M=B_D(A_D^M-1)/(A_D-1),
S_M=M a_D+(1+kappa^2)B_D(t_M-M B_D)/(A_D-1),
where a_D=1-exp(-kappa Delta)cos Delta+B_D.
The derivatives at Delta0 imply
t_M -> T_sp,
S_M -> (1+kappa^2)T_sp/d_sp-kappa(1+kappa^2)Theta/d_sp.

Now center and scale the finite support envelope to start at (1,0):
z_circ=(1,0)-sigma(kappa+P_M,-1-Q_M),
phi(z)=sigma max_(v in V_fin)<v,z-z_circ>,
where V_fin contains0, both endpoint vectors and every gradient just constructed.
The terminal local jet is
x_end=z_circ+sigma(kappa,-1),
g_end=sigma(kappa,-1),
f_end=sigma^2(1+kappa^2)/2.
It is an actual envelope jet, because (kappa,-1) belongs to K.

If the old reset point0 is to remain a minimizer with f_star=0,g_star=0,
the ORIGINAL directed interpolation row must satisfy
Q_(end,star)=f_end-<g_end,x_end>+||g_end||^2/2
 =sigma[-kappa+sigma(kappa(kappa+P_M)+1+Q_M)]<=0.
This is exactly the endpoint-plane condition phi(0)=0. It forces

    sigma^(-1)>=kappa+P_M+(1+Q_M)/kappa.

Thus the excess after paying the FULL available horizontal budget is
E_M=kappa+P_M+(1+Q_M)/kappa-c_sp S_M, and its exact limit is

    E_sp=c_sp[1+(1-3kappa^2)T_sp/d_sp
                       +kappa(1+kappa^2)Theta/d_sp]>0,
    lim_(kappa->0) kappa log E_sp=pi/2.

Therefore a universal inverse scale c_sp S+A(kappa) requires A(kappa)>=E_sp.
For the form c_sp S+kappa b+A(kappa), take the legal fixed local
checkpoint b=1+kappa^(-2); it requires A(kappa)>=E_sp-kappa b.
If A(kappa)<=C kappa^(-p) for any fixed finite C,p, choose kappa small
enough to violate this strict limiting inequality, then choose a finite
M large enough. The displayed ORIGINAL row Q_(end,star) is strictly positive.
The failed conclusion is the promised preserved minimum/interpolation
condition, not smoothness of the support envelope at a different minimum.

This obstruction keeps the actual finite chronological states and pays
c_sp S_M-P_M before declaring a deficit. It is stronger than merely
observing Q_M is large. It applies only to this support curve and the
specified zero-height reset architecture. Positive-height gluings,
other endpoints/curves, and unrestricted W_n remain open.
