# Exact radial square reserve for two actual resolvents

SELF-AUTHORED analytical lead, JOINT UNVERIFIED. No mathematical program or formal submission. This note supplies a local reserve only, not an all-depth payment or an upper bound for the original problem.

Let D be one fixed compact convex set containing zero. Fix z, d>1 and s>=0, and put g=P_D(z), q=P_D(z/d), delta=g-q. Both projections are actual points of the same body. Their two original projection normals give

N1=<z-g,g-q>>=0,
N2=<z-dq,q-g>>=0.

Direct expansion gives N1+N2=(d-1)<q,delta>-||delta||^2. Therefore

<q,delta>=[||delta||^2+N1+N2]/(d-1).

For the radial term already present in the accepted finite-block identity,

Lrad(s,z,d)=2s<z,g-q>+s^2(||g||^2-||q||^2),

substituting <z,delta>=||delta||^2+<q,delta>+N1 yields the exact identity

Lrad(s,z,d)
= [2sd+s^2(d+1)]/(d-1) ||g-q||^2
  + [2s(d+s)]/(d-1) N1
  + [2s(1+s)]/(d-1) N2.

Every coefficient is nonnegative. In the original augmented-row orientation Q_ij=<X_j-G_j,G_i-G_j><=0, N1=-Q_(q,g) and N2=-d Q_(g,q), where the auxiliary input for q is z/d. Thus the identity does not import a new legality assumption or replace the physical trajectory. If d=1 then q=g and Lrad=0; the singular displayed coefficients are not used at that endpoint.

A possible use is to pay differences along a growing tree of actual radial auxiliary edges, in combination with bridge squares and full original remainders. Such a tree payment would still need its exact inequality, original-row coefficients, parameter dependence and target constant. Nothing here proves that it exists or that the earlier bounded-feature obstructions are bypassed.
