Let C be an (n+1)-square real matrix with C1=0; its off-diagonal
entries may have either sign. Put b=C^T1. Define T(h) by T_ii=1,
T_ij=h_j when j<i, and T_ij=0 when j>i, and set
H(C,h)=CT(h)+T(h)^T C^T.

The exact sign dictionary is

  =-2 sum_i b_i f_i - sum_(i,j)H_ij<g_i,g_j>

vanish. The position term is 2sum C_ij<g_i,x_j-x_i>

x_j=x_0+g_j-(Tg)_j and again using C1=0 gives
2sum C_ij<g_i,g_j>-2sum(CT)_ij<g_i,g_j>.
The squared-gradient difference contributes
b^T(||g_i||^2)-2sum C_ij<g_i,g_j>. Combining proves (1).

## 2. Simultaneously changing the schedule

Let h'=h+a>=0. Define the full cut vector

z_t(lambda)=sum_(i,j)lambda_ij
 (1_(t<i)-1_(t<j))g_i,

where either indicator is zero at the star. Define the quadratic form

K(a;lambda)=sum_(t<n)a_t<g_t,z_t(lambda)>.

Then (1)--(3), now applied at h', give the exact identity

M(q',lambda',h') =
M(q,lambda,h)+(q'-q)||x_0||^2+K(a;lambda)-H(C,h').    (5)

This includes all remote forward-cut terms. For i<j, the matrix entries
of K in the gradient block are

2K_ij=a_i[mu_j+sum_(l<=i)lambda_(j,l)]
       -a_j sum_(l>j)lambda_(i,l),
K_ii=-a_i sum_(j>i)lambda_(i,j).                     (6)

The expression in (6) follows directly by collecting the two cut
contributions to <g_i,g_j>.

Consequently a diagonal H(C,h') does NOT diagonalize the full
joint perturbation: K still has the mixed entries (6). The exact
zero-mixed-entry target is H_ij=K_ij for i!=j, not H_ij=0.

For example, if every finite forward edge is zero, (6) becomes
2K_ij=a_i c_(i,j), where
c_(i,j)=mu_j+sum_(l<=i)lambda_(j,l).
Summing flow over the suffix shows sum_(j>i)c_(i,j)
=2+sum_(j>i)nu_j>0. Thus if H is diagonal AND K-H is
required to have no mixed entries, each a_i must be zero.
This is a precise incompatibility of those two simultaneous
requirements. It does not rule out allowing a non-diagonal PSD
remainder in (5).

## 4. The triangular elimination can be made inhomogeneous

Assume h'_i>1 for i<n. Specify the strict upper entries U_ij=C_ij.
For j>i define L_(j,i)=sum_(l<=i)C_(j,l), L_(j,-1)=0.
The desired off-diagonal matrix H has the exact equation

(h'_i-1)L_(j,i)+L_(j,i-1)
 =U_ij+h'_j sum_(l>j)U_(i,l)-H_ij.                 (9)

At j=n the displayed sum is empty and the auxiliary h'_n is irrelevant.
Indeed (CT)_ij=U_ij+h'_j sum_(l>j)U_(i,l), while
(CT)_ji=-(h'_i-1)L_(j,i)-L_(j,i-1), proving (9).

Taking H_ij=K_ij makes (9) a scalar elimination for the mixed
cancellation in (5). After it, one STILL has to check every sign in
(4), all diagonal entries or the full remaining PSD matrix, and
strict output improvement. Nothing in the elimination proves those
conditions automatically.
