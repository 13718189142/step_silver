## Primitive energy and the full cone

Normalize smoothness to1. For a nonnegative word h of length n, put
s=sum h_i, A=s+1, P_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, T_i=sum_(t>i)h_t and
x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t. Define

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                      -s(s+1)||g_n||^2.

Let Lambda be the full nonnegative array on distinct finite indices0..n,
with zero diagonal. Set e=(1,...,1)^T, L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, T_it=0 for t>i, and

 omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(n-1),2s)^T,
 D=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(n-1)(2-h_(n-1)),-s(s+2)).

Expansion of the value coefficients gives L^T e=omega, and expansion of
the gradient coefficients gives

 V_h+sum Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g)>,
 M=D+(LT+T^T L^T)/2.                                      (1)

For example the position part of sum Lambda Q has matrix
sym(L(T-I)); the squared-gradient differences contribute
sym(L)-diag(omega)/2. Adding the displayed diagonal of V gives (1).
Consequently the original full interface is exactly flow, Lambda>=0 and
M>=0. It is sufficient for V>=0 on every actual run, by Q_ij<=0 from
accepted1252b8823bb5a290. Every original finite gradient is retained.

Flow alone forces e^T M e=0: Te has entries1+P_i (P_n=s), and
e^T LTe=omega^T Te=s^2+sum h_i^2, whereas
e^T De=-s^2-sum h_i^2. PSD therefore forces Me=0. Indeed for any v,
positivity of (e+t v)^T M(e+t v) for both signs of small t forces
v^T Me=0. Thus the exact dual formulation may impose Me=0 and maximize
t subject to M-t(I-ee^T/(n+1))>=0. Its t>=0 feasibility is precisely the
original cone. This removes only the proved constant-gradient kernel;
no quadratic equality, rank, ordering, symmetry or sparsity is assumed.

The companion full primal minimizes V subject to Gram(g)>=0,
trace(Gram(g))=1, f_n=0 and every original ordered Q<=0. Its value is
always <=0: a constant-gradient Huber run gives V=0. Every negative
feasible transcript can be lifted to a legal function with a minimizer.
To see this, append the coordinate
g'_i=(g_i,epsilon), x'_i=(x_i,R-epsilon P_i),
f'_i=f_i+c-epsilon^2 P_i, with epsilon>0. Then Q'_ij=Q_ij.
Choose finite c and R so

 c>=max_i(-f_i+epsilon^2 P_i+(||g_i||^2+epsilon^2)/2),
 epsilon R>=max_i(f_i+c-<g_i,x_i>+(||g_i||^2+epsilon^2)/2).

These inequalities add the zero optimum jet in both orientations. The
conjugate construction of accepted1252b8823bb5a290 supplies the actual
convex1-smooth function. The additional V is epsilon^2 times
2sum h_i(s-P_i)+sum h_i(1-h_i)-s(s+1)=0, since
2sum h_i P_i=s^2-sum h_i^2. No distance-preservation claim is made.

## Zero steps: exact two-way cone correspondence

Partition the query indices into maximal consecutive classes separated
by positive steps. In each class except the last, choose the index of
the positive outgoing step as representative; choose n in the last.
Let hhat be the ordered list of positive steps. Embedding any compressed
certificate on these representatives gives the original V and Q on
their independent formal gradients. All omitted coefficients are zero,
so its full matrix is the coordinate-selection congruence of the
compressed PSD matrix. This proves the forward embedding.

Conversely, restrict an original certificate to formal gradients and
values that are equal within each class. Its V becomes V_hhat; each
cross-class Q becomes the corresponding compressed Q; each within-class
Q is zero. Sum all original nonnegative multipliers on each ordered
pair of distinct classes. This yields compressed nonnegative multipliers,
the compressed flow, and a PSD matrix by congruence. Thus certificate
existence is equivalent in both directions, including all-zero schedules
(V=0, Lambda=0). No numerical model below uses this compression: all
five original gradients are still saved and optimized.

## Necessary filters proved from legal functions

For j with b=h_j>1, write P=P_j, R=T_j and a=(b-1)/(R+1)>0.
Use the one-dimensional convex1-smooth function whose derivative is
clip(x,-a,1), with minimum0, and start at x_0=P+1. Every query through
j is on the upper plateau, the bridge lands at -(R+1)a, and all later
queries remain on the lower plateau until x_n=-a. At every query
f_i=x_i g_i-g_i^2/2, equivalently Q_(i,star)=0. Summing the GD norm
identity and substituting this equality into V yields

 V_h=(P+1)^2-A^2 a^2.

Thus a necessary condition for the cone is

 A(h_j-1)_+ <= (P_j+1)(T_j+1), for every j.                (2)

For h_j<=1 this is automatic. At j=0 or n-1, (2) gives
h_j<=2(s+1)/(s+2)<2 when h_j>1. Also, for h_j>1,
h_j-1 <=(P_j+1)(T_j+1)/A<=min(P_j+1,T_j+1), because
A=P_j+T_j+h_j+1 >=max(P_j+1,T_j+1).
At n4 this bounds h_1,h_2<4 using the adjacent endpoint bound.
So [0,2]x[0,4]x[0,4]x[0,2] is a lossless closed outer box.
It does not imply all points of the box, or all points satisfying (2),
are primitive. In particular no upper sum below12 is claimed.

For curvature z in(0,1], use f(x)=z x^2/2, x_0=1, and
p_0(z)=1, p_(i+1)(z)=(1-z h_i)p_i(z). Direct substitution gives

 V_h(z)=sum_i z h_i(1+z-z h_i)p_i(z)^2
                      -s z[1+(s+1)z]p_n(z)^2 >=0.        (3)

At z=1 this becomes 1-A^2 product_i(1-h_i)^2>=0 by telescoping.
Each fixed z gives a necessary test only. The implementation records
z=1,1/2,1/4; it performs no hidden curvature or word scan.
