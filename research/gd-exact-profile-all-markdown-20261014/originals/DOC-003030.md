# Growing Newton temporal spaces on the actual critical word

Status: complete draft candidates, UNVERIFIED. No solver, numerical evaluation,
installation or fact_submit. Accepted facts 1252b8823bb5a290 (interpolation and
normalization) and b98bb807d514e80d (the exact benchmark and its elementary
envelope) are the only correctness inputs. Every new estimate is proved here.
These are obstructions for specified growing temporal spaces, not an upper
bound for the unrestricted worst-case objective and not the original target.

## 1. Actual word, queried prefix and a genuinely restricted growing space

Fix m>=128 and keep the following parameters fixed within that horizon:

    lambda=1+sqrt(2), c=lambda(1+1/m), a=(1-1/m)/c,
    d_j=1+a c^j, H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,d_j,H_j),
    n=2^m-1, q=n-1.

The prescribed queried-prefix steps h_0,...,h_(q-1) are H_m with its last
entry d_0 removed. Queries are indexed 0,...,q; the last free step is b in
[0,2], including the original b=d_0. Write

    P_i(z)=product_(t<i)(1-h_t z), 0<=i<=q.

Choose a REAL monic polynomial chi of degree r, with 1<=r<=q. For a
polynomial p let [p] denote its remainder modulo chi. This is an r-dimensional
real vector space; use coefficients in 1,z,...,z^(r-1) as its real coordinates.
Let T be ANY real linear map from this space into any finite-dimensional
Euclidean space, with ||T[1]||=1. Put

    x_i=T[P_i],       g_i=T[z P_i],     0<=i<=q.          (1)

The map, ambient dimension, metric, coefficients and polynomial can all depend
on m. Singular maps are allowed. Initial correlations are unrestricted. The
identity P_(i+1)=P_i-h_i zP_i proves the ACTUAL GD recursion for (1).

All queried values f_i remain joint variables. Assume that the data (1), those
values and the optimum jet (0,0,0) belong to ONE convex differentiable function
F with 1-Lipschitz gradient. Thus the initial distance to this designated
minimizer is exactly one. All conclusions below hold for EVERY interpolant of
these jets; no terminal gradient identity is assumed after the last step b.

This is a fixed r-dimensional temporal space, even when the physical image
dimension is larger. For r=m it retains m independent Newton coefficients.
More generally the theorem below permits a degree much larger than m but
strictly smaller than the full n+1-node representation of arbitrary data.

## 2. The full computable all-pair law and function realization

Let J be multiplication by z modulo chi, let v_0 be the coefficient vector of
1, and define

    v_(i+1)=(I-h_i J)v_i,    w_i=Jv_i,    M=T^T T.

Then x_i=T v_i, g_i=T w_i, M is a REAL PSD matrix, and v_0^T M v_0=1.
Conversely every real PSD M has a real Euclidean factor T. With
v_star=w_star=0 and f_star=0, the necessary and sufficient full interpolation
law is, for EVERY distinct ordered pair i,j in {star,0,...,q},

 f_i-f_j+w_i^T M(v_j-v_i)
                   +(w_i-w_j)^T M(w_i-w_j)/2 <=0.       (2)

This is exactly Q_ij<=0 in fact 1252b8823bb5a290, evaluated on the stated
vectors. No pair or common value is replaced by a componentwise condition.
For feasible data that fact constructs an actual function explicitly:

 cjet_j=<g_j,x_j>-f_j,
 H(v)=max_j {cjet_j+<x_j,v-g_j>+||v-g_j||^2/2},
 F_max(x)=sup_v {<x,v>-H(v)}.

The maximum includes the optimum jet. The function is finite, convex and
1-smooth, interpolates every queried value and gradient, and has minimum zero
at zero. This provides a function realization, not just a Gram representation.

For a Newton basis with roots theta_1,...,theta_r, repetitions allowed,
the basis polynomials are N_k(z)=product_(ell=1)^k(z-theta_ell), 0<=k<r.
Multiplication obeys zN_k=theta_(k+1)N_k+N_(k+1), with N_r=0 in the quotient.
Thus J is a bidiagonal Newton chain in that basis. For nonreal conjugate
roots the original real monomial coordinates, or real and imaginary parts of
the Newton coordinates, give the same REAL law (2); a complex matrix is not
being called a real PSD metric.

## 3. Exact level resonance: a complete growing-span annihilation obstruction

The level d_j occurs 2^(m-j-1) times in H_m. In the retained prefix the
counts are

    N_0=2^(m-1)-1, N_j=2^(m-j-1), 1<=j<m.              (3)

Let mu_j=1/d_j. Suppose

    chi(z)=product_(j=0)^(m-1)(z-mu_j)^(r_j),
    0<=r_j<=N_j,     1<=sum r_j<=q.                    (4)

Since P_q(z)=product_j(1-d_j z)^(N_j), chi divides P_q. Thus x_q=g_q=0
in (1), for EVERY map T and every common metric, regardless of its condition
number or initial correlations. Every legal interpolant has

                    2F(x_q-b g_q)=0                    (5)

for every real b. This includes one independent Newton direction at EVERY
recursive large-step level (r=m), as well as every growing multiplicity
permitted by the actual counts (3). It also handles confluent derivatives:
vanishing to order r_j, not merely vanishing once, is the exact requirement.

One can add a SINGLE zero root, chi=z product_j(z-mu_j)^(r_j), provided its
degree is <=q. Then chi divides zP_q, so g_q=0. The position need not be zero,
but legality says it is a minimizer of F; again (5) holds. Adding a second
zero root is not covered: a nonzero constant terminal gradient may survive.

For the simple positive roots r_j=1, all factors have occurred by the first
central top-level step (index 2^(m-1)-1). The model already reaches zero after
that step. This uses the actual chronology; (3) is the more general terminal
criterion for growing confluent blocks.

## 4. Near-unit roots: quantitative obstruction with growing Jordan depth

Suppose EVERY complex root theta of the real polynomial chi satisfies

                         |theta-1|<=1/(32m).            (6)

Roots may be arbitrarily close, repeated, nonreal, or depend on the horizon.
There is no bound on T or on a Jordan-chain condition number. For every legal
prefix (1) and every b in [0,2],

 2F(x_q-b g_q)
 <=3600 c^(-2m)(384m r^(3/2))^(2r)
                              exp[-3n/(2m)].           (7)

Consequently the ratio to the EXACT benchmark U_n^ZJ=1/B_n tends uniformly
to zero whenever

             r log(384m r^(3/2))=o(n/m).                (8)

In particular r=m is covered. There is also an explicit larger range:

 1<=r<=floor(n/(32m^2))  implies
 2F(x_q-b g_q)<=3600 exp[-11n/(8m)].                    (9)

This closes an entire growing polynomial/Jordan space, not just a direct sum
of individually legal modes. It does not cover a fixed-width disk about one,
slow roots near zero, or arbitrary spectra.

### 4.1 Elementary parameter and product estimates

The rational inequalities 12/5<lambda<17/7 and m>=128 give

    12/5<c<5/2, 3/8<a<1/2, 11/8<d_0<3/2.             (10)

For example c<2193/896<5/2 and
a>(127/128)/(5/2)=127/320>3/8. Also ac=1-1/m.

Let mathcal_P_m(z) be the product for the COMPLETE word H_m. Its multiplicity
formula gives

 mathcal_P_m(z)=product_(j=0)^(m-1)(1-d_j z)^(2^(m-j-1)),
 |mathcal_P_m(1)|=(ac)^n c^(-m).                       (11)

Indeed sum_j 2^(m-j-1)=n and sum_j j2^(m-j-1)=n-m, each proved by induction
using H_(j+1)=(H_j,d_j,H_j). For z=1+w with |w|=R=1/(16m),

 |1-d_j z| <= a c^j (1+4|w|),

because d_j/(a c^j)=1+1/(a c^j)<11/3<4. Therefore 1+t<=exp(t) and
1-1/m<=exp(-1/m) imply

 |mathcal_P_m(z)|<=c^(-m) exp[-3n/(4m)].               (12)

On that circle, |1-d_0 z|>=a-d_0R>1/3 by (10). Since the retained product
is P_q=mathcal_P_m/(1-d_0z), put

 E_m=3 c^(-m) exp[-3n/(4m)].

Then

             |P_q(z)|<=E_m,  |zP_q(z)|<=2E_m          (13)

on the whole circle. The damping estimate is for complex z, not a real-axis
estimate incorrectly differentiated across a growing Jordan block.

### 4.2 Legal early queries bound every polynomial image, without conditioning

Write D_r=product_(t<r)(1+h_t). Fact 1252b8823bb5a290 gives
||x_i||<=product_(t<i)(1+h_t)<=D_r for all 0<=i<r: this follows directly from
||grad F(x)||<=||x|| and the actual recursion. All these queries are retained.

The polynomials P_0,...,P_(r-1) have successive degrees and nonzero leading
coefficients, so they form a basis of degree-<r polynomials. Moreover

                zP_i=(P_i-P_(i+1))/h_i.                (14)

Every h_i>1. Thus multiplying a polynomial of degree <=r-2 by z increases
the l1 norm of its coefficients in the P-basis by at most a factor two.
Multiplying instead by z-theta increases that norm by at most 2+|theta|<=4
for the roots in (6). Starting with P_0=1 proves

                     ||T[N_k]||<=4^k D_r, k<r.        (15)

If N_k has complex coefficients, extend T complex linearly and use the usual
Hermitian norm. Its expansion in the REAL x_i still proves (15) by the
triangle inequality. No inverse Vandermonde or inverse root-separation factor
occurs, and no bound on the common metric was imposed.

In the first r chronological steps, a level j occurs only if 2^j<=r. Since
c<5/2<2^(3/2), h_t<=1+(1/2)r^(3/2) there. Hence

                     D_r<=(3r^(3/2))^r.               (16)

This is deliberately a loose legal-trajectory bound. Its exponential cost
depends on the temporal degree r, not on the full horizon n.

### 4.3 A separation-free Newton remainder bound, including repeated roots

For completeness the required contour identity is proved here. For any
ordered roots theta_1,...,theta_r inside the circle |z-1|=R, put
N_k(t)=product_(ell<=k)(t-theta_ell). Direct telescoping gives

 1/(z-t)=sum_(k=0)^(r-1) N_k(t)/N_(k+1)(z)
                         +N_r(t)/[(z-t)N_r(z)].        (17)

For a polynomial p define

 C_k=(1/(2pi i)) integral_(|z-1|=R) p(z)/N_(k+1)(z) dz.

Multiplying (17) by p(z) and integrating shows
p(t)-sum C_k N_k(t) is divisible by N_r(t)=chi(t): for t inside the circle,
the remainder term is chi(t) times an analytic function. Thus it and its
required derivatives vanish at every root, with the specified multiplicities.
A polynomial vanishes with those multiplicities iff it is divisible by chi,
as follows by successive division by each linear factor. The polynomial sum
has degree<r, so it is exactly [p]. The integral identity for polynomials is
also obtainable by expanding in powers about the circle's center and
integrating each finite/geometric series; no node-separation hypothesis occurs.

For (6), each |z-theta_ell|>=1/(32m) on the circle. If |p(z)|<=E there,

                       |C_k|<=2(32m)^k E.              (18)

Combining (15), (18) and the geometric sum with 128m>=2 gives

 ||T[p]||<=2ED_r sum_(k<r)(128m)^k
                        <=4ED_r(128m)^r.              (19)

This proof covers collisions of roots directly. The explicit root ordering
need not preserve real coefficients term by term, since the final remainder
of a real polynomial by real chi is real.

Apply (19) to p=P_q and p=zP_q using (13) and (16). With

 K_mr=12 c^(-m)(384m r^(3/2))^r exp[-3n/(4m)],

we get ||x_q||<=K_mr and ||g_q||<=2K_mr. For 0<=b<=2 the actual final
position y=x_q-b g_q satisfies ||y||<=5K_mr. Since F has minimum zero at zero
and is 1-smooth, 2F(y)<=||y||^2. This proves (7), including every possible
terminal interpolation and the original last step d_0.

### 4.4 Exact benchmark comparison and the explicit degree range

We retain the actual B_n from b98bb807d514e80d, rather than replacing the

ratio is B_n times the right side of (7). The accepted elementary estimate

             B_n<=1+4n^p, p=log_2(3),                 (20)

is sufficient to show this exact ratio tends to zero under (8): its logarithm
contributes O(m), whereas n/m grows exponentially in m. No unverified C54

For r<=n/(32m^2), use r<n<2^m. The inequalities log384<6, logm<=m/16 for

## 5. Actual nonquadratic, full-rank realizations in the growing class

The class in Section4 is not empty and is not restricted to quadratic data.
Here is a finite, symbolic, quantitative construction for every m>=128 and
2<=r<=q. This is a nonvacuity calibration, not a new strong lower witness.

Choose a rational alpha in

              (1-1/(64m), 1-1/(128m))                 (21)

such that all P_i(alpha), 0<=i<=q, are nonzero and pairwise distinct. Such a
choice exists: each P_i is a nonzero polynomial, and P_i-P_j is nonzero for
i!=j because their degrees differ. Only finitely many real roots are
forbidden, whereas the interval contains infinitely many rational numbers.
This is a finite algebraic specification, not a request for numerical search.

Let e_0,...,e_(r-1) be the orthonormal basis of R^r and let N_shift e_k=e_(k+1)
for k<r-1, N_shift e_(r-1)=0. For epsilon>0 set

 A_epsilon=alpha I+epsilon N_shift,
 X_i=P_i(A_epsilon)e_0, G_i=A_epsilon X_i,
 V_i=(alpha/2)||X_i||^2.                               (22)

Take optimum data zero, and put nu=1/4. Define transformed jets

 G'_i=(G_i-nu X_i)/(1-nu),
 V'_i=(V_i-nu||X_i||^2/2)/(1-nu).                      (23)

At epsilon=0 these are the jets of the scalar quadratic of curvature
alpha'=(alpha-nu)/(1-nu), lying strictly in (0,1), at points
P_i(alpha)e_0. All points, including the optimum, are distinct. Thus EVERY
ordered residual is strictly negative:

 Q'_ij(0)=-alpha'(1-alpha')||X_i(0)-X_j(0)||^2/2<0.

For epsilon variable each Q'_ij(epsilon) is a real polynomial, since all
quantities in (22)--(23) are polynomial. Define the exact finite constants

 delta=min_(i!=j)(-Q'_ij(0))>0,
 D=1+sum_(i!=j) sum_(k>=1) |coefficient_(epsilon^k) Q'_ij|,
 epsilon=min(1/2,delta/(2D)).                           (24)

All indices here include star and every query. The sums are finite and
provide a computable algebraic law, not a solver oracle. Since epsilon<=1,
|Q'_ij(epsilon)-Q'_ij(0)|<=epsilon D<=delta/2. Thus all transformed ordered
inequalities are strictly satisfied. Fact1252 constructs a convex 1-smooth
interpolant K of them. The single function

                F(x)=(1-nu)K(x)+nu||x||^2/2           (25)

is convex, 1-smooth and nu-strongly convex, has its UNIQUE minimizer at zero,
and interpolates EVERY original (X_i,G_i,V_i). Its actual GD follows (22)
with the prescribed steps. X_0=e_0 has TRUE initial distance exactly one.

These data have the representation (1) with chi=(z-alpha)^r and
T[p]=p(A_epsilon)e_0. The r vectors e_0,A_epsilon e_0,...,
A_epsilon^(r-1)e_0 are linearly independent: in the standard basis their
coefficient matrix is triangular with nonzero diagonal epsilon^k. Thus T is
injective and its REAL common metric is positive definite. The first r
positions X_0,...,X_(r-1) also form a basis, since their polynomials have
successive degrees and nonzero leading coefficients.

No quadratic function with a minimizer at zero can interpolate these
gradients. Its Hessian would be symmetric and would have to equal
A_epsilon on that basis, whereas A_epsilon is nonsymmetric for r>=2 and
epsilon>0. Thus (25) is genuinely nonquadratic on the required jet data.

There is an exact positive terminal payoff, even uniformly over all real b.
Write p0=P_q(alpha)!=0 and p1=P'_q(alpha). The first two coordinates of X_q
and G_q are respectively

            (p0,epsilon p1),
            (alpha p0,epsilon(alpha p1+p0)).

Their determinant is epsilon p0^2. Minimizing the squared distance from the
first vector to the line spanned by the second gives, for every real b,

 2F(X_q-b G_q)
 >=nu||X_q-b G_q||^2
 >=nu epsilon^2 p0^4 /
                 [alpha^2 p0^2+epsilon^2(alpha p1+p0)^2] >0.  (26)

The line-distance identity follows by completing the scalar square in b;
the denominator is nonzero since alpha p0!=0. All quantities are fixed by
(21)--(24) and the actual word. For degrees covered by Section4, (7) also
applies, so this positive payoff is nevertheless asymptotically negligible
against the exact B_n profile. It is not presented as an improved adversary.

with the Hermite remainder; Proposition25 gives the bidiagonal multiplication

These drafts close two SPECIFIED growing-span classes. Exact reciprocal roots
at every scale annihilate, while a near-unit cluster with growing Jordan depth
is controlled uniformly over all common metrics by legal early positions.
Neither proof extends by spectral radius alone to arbitrary metrics or to
slow nodes of order1/S_m. In particular, adding a zero root of multiplicity
>=2 to the resonant class leaves polynomial/constant-gradient suffix data and
is genuinely outside the annihilation proof. A changed generator at each
block is also outside this common-quotient class.

The all-pair law (2) for unrestricted chi of degree n+1 would simply reproduce
the original finite interpolation problem. Nothing here treats that
theorem-strength interface as a solved mechanism. The exact all-schedule
profile target remains unresolved, and there is no new verified fact.
