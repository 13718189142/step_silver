## Appendix A. Independent proof that cd<1 for any full seed

The following proof covers arbitrary a,b>=0 and positive c,d. Put
Q=3+a+b and C=Q+2+c+d. It first proves cd<1 without assuming a
suffix trajectory stays within a clipping threshold. It then derives
the full last-triangle inequality.

For 0<r<1 set u=(1,0), w=(r,sqrt(r(1-r))), and
v=-(c*u+w)/(1+d). The origin belongs to conv{u,v,w}. Use

    x=(Q*u,(2+b)*u,u,-c*u,w), g=(u,u,u,v,w).

The GD identities hold. Set n=-c*u-v. The required triangle normal
comparisons at target3 are

    <w-v,n>=[-c^2*d+Ktail*r]/(1+d)^2,
    <u-v,n>=[-c*d*(c+d+1)+(c+d+2-c*d)*r]/(1+d)^2,

where Ktail=c^2*d+(c+d+2)(1-cd).
At targets0,1 the normals are positive multiples of u, and all
comparisons are nonpositive because <u,v><0 and <u,w>=r<1.
At target2 the normal is zero, as is the terminal normal. To check
the target3 comparisons when cd>=1, take r sufficiently close to1
from below. At r=1 the first numerator is
(c+d+2)(1-cd)<=0, and the second numerator simplifies to
(c+d+2)(1-cd)<=0. If cd>1 both are strictly negative and continuity
gives legal r<1 arbitrarily near1. If cd=1, Ktail=c^2*d>0 and the
first numerator is Ktail(r-1)<0; the second coefficient
c+d+2-cd=c+d+1>0 and its numerator is that coefficient times(r-1),
also negative. All optimum comparisons follow because0 is in the
triangle. Hence the data are legal for r arbitrarily close to1.

Their energy is Q^2-C^2*r by (14). Since C>Q, choosing r sufficiently
near1 makes it negative, contradicting (13). Thus cd<1 is necessary.

For cd<1, Ktail>c^2*d>0 and the choice r=c^2*d/Ktail belongs to(0,1).
The first target3 normal vanishes. Substitution into the second gives
a negative quantity (equivalently -c*d*(1-r)/(1+d) in the usual
triangle normalization). The same checks give a legal function and

    C^2*c^2*d<=Q^2*Ktail.

Since C-Q=c+d+2, expanding the difference gives

    C^2*c^2*d-Q^2*Ktail
      =(c+d+2)*{cd[Q^2+c(Q+C)]-Q^2}.

Therefore every full seed satisfies

    cd[1+c(Q+C)/Q^2]<=1,

including the strict consequence cd<1 already established without
assuming it in a trajectory. This appendix completes the prerequisite
chain used in Sections 4--6.
