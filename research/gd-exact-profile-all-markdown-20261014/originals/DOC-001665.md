# 0. Mathematical verdict

**Not solved.** Neither the uniform exact-profile lower bound nor a certified asymptotic improvement has been established.

The fixed goal is unchanged: with `W_n(h)=sup 2(f(x_n)-f_*)/(L||x_0-x_*||^2)`, `W_n^*=inf_{h in [0,infinity)^n} W_n(h)`, and the exact supplied Zhang--Jiang dynamic-programming sequence `B_n=2S_bullet(n)+1`, prove `W_n^* B_n -> 1`, or prove a fixed relative improvement on unbounded horizons with legal full-class upper certificates. A positive resolution requires a threshold independent of the entire nonnegative schedule. The exponent `rho=log_2(1+sqrt(2))` alone is not the goal.

The signed-closed components are five scoped tools or obstructions, listed below. None closes a target-level sub-task. A viable conditional negative architecture is now explicit: a primitive seed of length r with `A=1+sum(h)>(r+1)^rho` would amplify into an asymptotic separation. The seed is unknown, and the amplification argument itself remains a shared, unverified derivation. On the positive side, full interpolation certificates and nonseparable adversaries are available as frameworks, but the quantitative comparison remains unmatched.

The main blocker is not a source-normalization issue or a missing routine interpolation citation. It is the absence of either a legal witness uniformly strong enough for all schedules, or an actual improved infinite schedule family. The highest-priority unresolved interfaces are full-matrix descent without triangular restrictions and residual-preserving composition without a fixed constant loss. Existing exclusions are **method failure**, not **proposition failure**. Do not interpret PARTIAL or SUBSTANTIAL as almost solved.

Central missing lemma for this skeleton: there exist an integer r>=1 and h in `[0,infinity)^r`, with s=sum(h), such that `1+s>(r+1)^rho` and, for every finite-dimensional convex 1-smooth function with a minimizer and every initial point, its GD trajectory satisfies

# 2. Interface contract table

### Full interpolation to joint descent
**Input required.** A complete tight certificate `(q,lambda,h)` with nonnegative interpolation weights, exact objective flow, and the full quadratic form `M=q||x_0||^2-2f_n+sum lambda_ij Q_ij` positive semidefinite after substituting the actual GD recursion. Here `Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0`.

**Output claimed.** The shared suffix-deformation calculation gives strict improvement when finite-index flow is triangular and its later cut profiles have a stated proportionality. The proportionality is automatic with two remaining steps. Earlier results cover certain final-gradient Schur directions and transferable star-outgoing flow.

**Available facts.** No accepted fact asserts this new deformation or those structural hypotheses. Fact `dfde2fa326c908bf` is a different realization tool and does not fill this gap.

**Missing verification on the actual model.** (1) Remove or prove the triangular restriction. (2) Handle incompatible later cut profiles. (3) Connect any resulting global Huber-tightness statement to the exact DP mass cap; tightness alone is not the target.

**Failure mode if ignored.** Forward edges create mixed matrix entries under step changes. Dropping them or checking only a small principal block gives a false full certificate. Local stationary tuples cannot be promoted to global minimizers.

**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Correlated geometric data to a uniform legal witness
**Input required.** One finite-dimensional smooth convex function, all cross-time interpolation inequalities and an actual minimizer. In the Moreau realization, all old and future supporting planes must be dominated at each proposed proximal point, with retained offsets and function values.

**Output claimed.** Shared constructions realize nonseparable Markov and arc relays for explicit schedule families. Positive checkpoint values repair one genuine signed-history failure; closest-minimizer normalization improves some witnesses.

**Available facts.** `0e7e78056dc6258c` excludes orthogonal moments only. `b98bb807d514e80d` and `1fca3e5dd555cb4a` give legal scalar baseline witnesses.

**Missing verification on the actual model.** (1) Quantitatively choose the data for general nonperiodic schedules. (2) Retain every history constraint through recursive scales. (3) Obtain a uniform relative loss tending to zero against B_n.

**Failure mode if ignored.** An explicit large-error periodic example can remain far from the difficult near-critical schedules. Positive offsets, PSD Gram matrices, or local correlation inequalities alone do not establish all-plane legality.

**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Scalar interval reachability to a strong fixed function
**Input required.** A single nondecreasing 1-Lipschitz g on `[0,infinity)` with g(0)=0, its odd extension, and the fixed even function `f(x)=integral_0^{|x|} g(r)dr`.

**Available facts.** No accepted fact yet covers this new interval package. `1fca3e5dd555cb4a` covers a different scalar construction.

**Missing verification on the actual model.** Choose one globally compatible multi-knot derivative that produces the required area. No full scalar-class profile lower bound has been derived.

**Failure mode if ignored.** Optimizing the derivative independently at each time silently changes the function. Exact interval dynamics do not justify a minimax exchange between the fixed function and timewise controls.

**Status.** PARTIAL.

# 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- **DANGEROUS:** "The remaining generalization is just removal of triangularity." It changes the mixed entries of the full PSD form and is an unmatched structural hypothesis, not notation.
- **DANGEROUS:** "The interval state removes all history." It removes trajectory reachability history only after one entire legal scalar function is fixed; monotonicity, slopes and accumulated area remain global constraints.
- **DANGEROUS:** "A primitive seed better than the recursive value disproves the conjecture." The actual amplification condition is stronger, or requires a different exact phase comparison.
- **DANGEROUS:** "The new periodic relay proves sharpness of the Silver constant." It proves a restricted-family witness. Its parameters have not been selected uniformly for arbitrary schedules.
- **DANGEROUS:** "No improved seed appeared, so the benchmark is optimal." A finite numerical search cannot establish an all-length, all-certificate assertion.
- Do not reuse orthogonal moments or the universal complex lens without a mechanism escaping their accepted obstructions.
- Do not replace full interpolation by adjacent cocoercivity or variable curvature matrices chosen independently in time.
- Do not discard signed-history costs or the terminal function-value area in a recursive construction.
- Do not identify recursive composability with the ambient class of all primitive or all schedules.
- Do not replace the exact B_n sequence by a decimal constant or assume convergence of its normalized phase.
- Do not interpret a fixed loss in one upper-certificate rule as a lower bound on the schedules it analyzes.

# 4. Missing bridge lemmas

### Bridge 1 -- an actual above-power primitive seed
**Target statement.** The r,h,s and universal potential inequality in section 0, with `1+s>(r+1)^rho` and nonnegative h.

**Prerequisites.** The actual primitive definition, full smooth-convex interpolation, exact multiplier signs and PSD verification, and the same objective normalization.

**Existing facts to use.** `0be8b54e0a33381c` for the terminal-leaf transport in the subsequent amplification, not for existence of the seed.

**Missing checks.** A concrete seed; an exact certificate; the complete external closure chain. Finite numerical non-discovery supplies none of these.

**Closure criterion.** A specific finite schedule with an algebraic/rational certificate proving the universal potential and the strict power inequality. This bridge is independent of the affirmative route; the amplification step depends on it. A scoped nonexistence theorem would close only its stated seed class, not the original goal.

### Bridge 2 -- full feasible descent above the Huber value
**Target statement.** For any positive globally minimizing schedule h with optimal tight value `q>tau(h)=1/(1+2sum h)` and a complete matching primal-dual certificate, construct a same-length nonnegative h' and a full-class certificate with `q'<q`, or identify an exact obstruction that invalidates this proposed descent theorem. No triangular-flow or cut-proportionality hypothesis may be assumed without proof on the actual minimizing tuple.

**Prerequisites.** Attainment and complete primal-dual matching, with all interpolation, full PSD, complementarity and inactive-edge constraints retained. The existing stationarity derivation is shared candidate material, not a substitute for these checks.

**Existing facts to use.** `0be8b54e0a33381c` supplies the universal Huber baseline tau. No accepted fact supplies the unrestricted descent.

**Missing checks.** Forward-edge terms, cut-profile incompatibility, kernel behavior, and the possible difference between stationary and globally minimizing schedules. Even successful descent still leaves the exact DP mass bound for the remaining tight class.

**Closure criterion.** An actual feasible strict descent formula under the full hypotheses, not a definition of a desired direction; alternatively, an exact matching tuple disproving a proposed local implication with its scope carefully stated. This bridge is independent of the negative seed route. It is a substantial unresolved mathematical statement, not a routine perturbation exercise.
