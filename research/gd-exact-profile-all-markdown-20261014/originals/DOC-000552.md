The following elementary identity is useful for subsequent geometric search.

Let D=conv{v_0,...,v_k} be any fixed finite convex hull and h>1. Given a
future position y, choose a vertex index s maximizing the finite scores

    score_s=<y,v_s>+(h-1)||v_s||^2/2.

Put x=y+h v_s. For every vertex v_u,

    <x-v_s,v_u-v_s>
      =score_u-score_s-(h-1)||v_u-v_s||^2/2 <=0.

Therefore P_D(x)=v_s and x-h P_D(x)=y. This is an exact finite vertex
preimage selector without the equal-norm hypothesis. It applies to the
actual projection gradient of one static F_D and may be iterated backward
in the original word when every h_i>1. The zero vertex, if present, is
one ordinary candidate in the score maximum. Ties cause no legality issue.
