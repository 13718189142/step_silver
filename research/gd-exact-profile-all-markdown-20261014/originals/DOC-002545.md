and proves equivalence of the original full PSD requirement to first-r flow
and null equations plus the entire leading-block lower-Cholesky identity.
The lifting basis B=[I_r;-ones(r)^T] gives M=B K K^T B^T. Zero factor
diagonals and every possible PSD rank are allowed. No original gradient
coordinate or ordered multiplier is deleted, including at zero steps.

The search moves every original h and every ordered Lambda jointly with
all lower-factor variables. The only extra restrictions are the explicitly
registered finite search boxes: h in[0,6], Lambda in[0,400], and each lower
factor entry in[-100,100] with nonnegative diagonal. These are not global
bounds. No symmetry, support, unit-step, repeated-gradient, zero-remainder,
or positive-rank restriction is imposed on a joint run.

## What the bounded outcome supports

This pilot supplies no actual sufficient-capacity certificate and no seed
proof. The accepted physical lift at18*2^j-6 or21*2^j-7 is therefore not
triggered. No conclusion about the unrestricted cone, global optimality,
nonexistence, or the original asymptotic minimax problem follows.

The concrete issue exposed here is feasibility convergence of the local
joint factor model: both nonlinear runs exhausted their iteration allowance
while the full matrix equalities remained too inaccurate, and both warm SDPs
also had sign/residual failures. A justified future direction, only with a
new allocation, is to repair that interface before further discovery—for
example, test an exact parametrization of the linear-in-Lambda flow/null
equations with all degeneracies retained, followed by a fully metered PSD
factor optimization. This is a proposal, not an established lossless
parametrization or an inference that the threshold can be reached. No further
run, analysis of subthreshold families, or formal gate is launched here.
