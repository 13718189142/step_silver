## Strict finite matching and smoothing

del Barrio, Cuesta-Albertos, Hallin and Matran,
[Smooth Cyclically Monotone Interpolation, explicit v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.01238v1).
Proposition2.1 relates finite cyclic monotonicity to supporting weights; its
strict version corresponds to uniqueness of the matching. Theorem2.2 and
Corollary2.4 retain Assumption(A). For bounded output vectors, the latter
constructs an interpolating Moreau envelope with gradient Lipschitz bound
1/epsilon_0, where epsilon_0 is half the minimum strict supporting gap.
Equations2.11-2.12 formulate gap selection and a circulation dual.

Potential use: test whether a concrete proposed legal repair has a uniform
supporting-gap bound, rather than fixing one violated pair. This is only a
possible construction tool. Relabeling gradients by an optimal matching
does not preserve a prescribed GD trajectory automatically. A small gap may
cause an unacceptable smoothness/normalization loss. The optimum jet, actual
terminal value and true initial distance still need explicit checks. Use the
strict stated theorem with its hypotheses; no unqualified extension statement
is imported here, and no minimum-cycle algorithm or optimal Lipschitz theorem
is asserted beyond the displayed source statements.

## Value-free interpolation and the metric it actually bounds

Lee and Seiler,
[Finite Step Performance of First-order Methods Using Interpolation Conditions Without Function Evaluations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.01825).
Lemma4 states the transformed cyclic-monotonicity criterion for the stated
strongly convex regime0<mu<L. Lemma8 identifies the cone generated by I-P,
with P a permutation matrix, with zero-excess doubly hyperdominant matrices.
Theorem1's displayed performance metric is iterate distance, not last-iterate
objective gap; the paper's trajectory reconstruction also has explicit
feasibility/nondegeneracy conditions.

Possible use is a combinatorial organization of ALL cycle constraints, not
a free import of its performance theorem. For the present convex objective
problem, derive the exact needed specialization from accepted1252b8823bb5a290
and keep function-value completion. An exact value-free reformulation alone
is already known and is not a target-specific lower bound. No finite distance
bound, strongly convex rate or lossless-S-procedure slogan closes this goal.
