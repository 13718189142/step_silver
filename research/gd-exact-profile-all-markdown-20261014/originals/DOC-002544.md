## Full original model and the exact reduction

For a physical length r, keep every h_i>=0, 0<=i<r, and every ordered
Lambda_ij>=0, 0<=i,j<=r, i!=j; put Lambda_ii=0. Define s=sum h_i,
A=1+s, e=ones(r+1), T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, and zero otherwise.
Write L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda, omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(r-1),2s)^T,
D=diag(h_i(2-h_i),-s(s+2)), and M=D+(LT+T^T L^T)/2.
The original constraints are L^T e=omega and M PSD on all R^(r+1).

The accepted original identity is V_h+sum Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g)>.
Here Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0 by
1252b8823bb5a290, x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, and
V_h=2sum h_i(f_i-f_r)+sum h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_r||^2.
In a direct coefficient audit the value coefficient is canceled by the flow;
the position terms are sym(L(T-I)); the gradient-difference terms are
sym(L)-diag(omega)/2. Addition of V_h's diagonal gives exactly M.
This is the full original coordinate certificate, not an actual-gradient
quotient. Every zero-step coordinate and every ordered multiplier survives.

Here is the joint algebra allowing a smaller factor. Since Le=0, the sum
of the flow residuals is zero: e^T(L^T e-omega)=0. Thus the first r flow
equations imply the terminal equation. Put H_i=sum_(t<i)h_t. Then
(Te)_i=1+H_i, including H_r=s, and under the flow equations

  e^T D e=-sum h_i^2-s^2,
  e^T L T e=omega^T T e=2s^2-2sum h_i H_i
             =s^2+sum h_i^2,

because 2sum h_i H_i=s^2-sum h_i^2. Consequently e^T M e=0.
For a PSD symmetric M this forces Me=0: for any vector z, positivity of
(e+t z)^T M(e+t z) for both signs of real t makes the linear coefficient
2 z^T Me vanish. Conversely, if the first r entries of Me vanish,
e^T M e=0 forces its last entry to vanish as well.

Let P denote the leading r-by-r principal block of M, let u=ones(r),
and let B be the (r+1)-by-r matrix formed by stacking I_r above -u^T.
For symmetric M with Me=0, its last column is (-Pu,u^T P u)^T,
so M=B P B^T. In particular M PSD iff P PSD: one implication is
principal restriction, and the other follows from this identity.

Every real PSD P has P=K K^T for a lower triangular real K with
nonnegative diagonal, including singular P. A proof by induction:
if P_00=0 then positivity of its two-coordinate restrictions forces
P_0j=0 for all j; put the first column of K equal to zero and factor
the remaining PSD principal block. If P_00>0, set K_00=sqrt(P_00)
and K_i0=P_i0/K_00. The Schur complement is PSD, since its quadratic
form is that of P on (-P_00^(-1)P_0,rest z,z); factor it inductively.
This argument imposes neither strict diagonal positivity nor a rank cap.

Hence the joint polynomial model is exactly

  (L^T e-omega)_i=0                      for i<r,
  (Me)_i=0                              for i<r,
  M_ij-(K K^T)_ij=0                     for 0<=i<=j<r,
  h_i>=0, Lambda_ij>=0, K_ii>=0.

It has r+r(r+1)+r(r+1)/2 variables and 2r+r(r+1)/2 equalities.
Full M=B K K^T B^T is read back from the original model; the dropped
terminal equations are explicitly reported. All retained expressions have

  Lambda_(i,i+1)=2(i+1)^2,
  Lambda_(i+1,i)=2(i+1)(i+2),  0<=i<r,
  all other ordered entries=0.

All entries are nonnegative. At each nonterminal node, out-in=-2;
at the terminal node it is 2r. With T the lower triangular all-ones
matrix, direct row multiplication gives (LT)_ii=2i(i+1) for i<r,
(LT)_rr=2r(r+1), (LT)_(i,i+1)=-2(i+1)^2, and every other off-diagonal

  M=sum_(i=0)^(r-1)(i+1)^2 (e_i-e_(i+1))(e_i-e_(i+1))^T.

Here e_i are the original coordinate unit columns. This PSD matrix
has kernel precisely span(e), since zero quadratic form means all
successive coordinates agree. Its leading principal block is positive
definite. Thus the original h=(1,...,1) word has a genuinely feasible
full original certificate and a nonzero remainder of maximal possible

the unit-step restrictions: all original h and Lambda become free.
