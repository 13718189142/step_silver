## 1. Definitions and the exact core being decided

Write

    h=(1+a,1+b,1+c,1+d), C=5+a+b+c+d,
    P=2+a, Q=3+a+b, D=2+d, H=3+c+d,
    k=max((1+a)(a^2+2a-1)/a,0), R=P^2-k,
    eta_mid=b/H, J=R-k*eta_mid.

The precise endpoint core is the following set of algebraic inequalities:

    193/25 <= C < 87/10,
    0<a,d<77/97, 0<b,c<1552559/1081453,
    bc<1,
    Ca<=C-1-a, Cb<=P*H, Cc<=Q*D, Cd<=C-1-d,
    ab[1+a(C+1)]<=H,
    bc[R+b(P+C)]<=D*J,
    C^2*eta_mid^2<=J,
    Ccd<=Q, Cabcd<=1, Cbcd<=P,
    Cbd<=P(2+c),
    cd[1+c(Q+C)/Q^2]<=1,
    C^2*b^2*d^2<=R(2+c)^2-k*b*(2+c).                 (1)

Here Cbcd denotes C*b*c*d, and similarly for other concatenated factors.
On bc<1 these are exactly the complete lower-clipping inequalities,
all four cuts, the stated first and middle triangles, mixed first cut,
suffix and unit-quadratic tests, last triangle, and lifted return.
No claim of sufficiency for the full primitive certificate is made.

For comparison with the endpoint notation, put

    Ksum=C-5-a-b=c+d, y=Cb/P, m=max(1,aP),
    Bprod=min(1/(m*y),Q/C), r_end=(C-1)/(C+1),
    A=b*(R+b*(P+C))/J,
    Lcut=(C*Ksum-2Q)/(C+Q),
    Ltri=(A*Ksum-2)/(A+1).

The exact admissible endpoint interval has

    d>=max(0,Ksum-1/b,Lcut,Ltri),
    d<=min(Ksum,r_end,Ulift),
    0<d<Ksum, d>Ksum-1/b, d(Ksum-d)<=Bprod.            (2)

The last triangle is additionally retained. No endpoint belonging only
to the closure of one of the three strict inequalities is admitted.
When k=0, as will be proved on our survivor region, the actual stronger
return endpoint is exactly

    Ulift=(Ksum+2)/(1+y).                              (3)

Thus using (3) below is a proved inactive-lift branch, not deletion of
an active term. The original lifted inequality in (1) is checked too.

### 2.1. The cuts and their independent first-cut conditions

For either u=a or u=d, u<1/2 and C>3 give

    (C+1)u < (C+1)/2 < C-1.

This proves both endpoint cuts strictly. Since C<8, b<1, P>2 and H>4,
the second cut has Cb<8<PH. The third cut follows from

    Cc < (31/4)(121/100)=3751/400,
    QD > (419/100)(231/100)=96789/10000,
    96789-25*3751=3014>0.

Since k=0 and eta_mid=b/H, the mixed inequality C^2*eta_mid^2<J is
the square of the strict second cut. The first adjacent triangle obeys

    ab[1+a(C+1)] < (1/2)[1+(1/2)*9]=11/4<H.

The independent endpoint condition a<r_end is the first endpoint cut.

### 2.2. The middle triangle, including its lift

With k=0 the lifted middle triangle is exactly

    bc[1+b(P+C)/P^2]<=D.

The bounds in (7) give

    bc<=962001/1000000,
    b(P+C)<=8110125/1000000,
    P^2>=5755201/1000000,
    D>=2319/1000.

Therefore its left side is at most

    (962001/1000000)*(13865326/5755201)<2319/1000.

The exact integer difference after clearing positive denominators is

    2319000*5755201 - 962001*13865326
        =7853641674>0.                                (8)

This proves strict survival uniformly, rather than relying on a sampled
value of the middle inequality.

### 2.3. Products, both return tests, and the last triangle

The bc boundary is strict because bc<=962001/1000000<1.
Using bc<963/1000, d<=321/1000 and C<31/4 gives

    Cbcd < (31*963*321)/4000000
          =9582813/4000000
          <2399/1000<=P.                              (9)

The last strict comparison has numerator difference 13187>0.
Because aP<1, (9) also gives Cabcd<aP<1. The suffix inequality follows
from C<8, c<5/4, d<1/3 and Q>4:

    Ccd<10/3<4<Q.

For the return inequality, C<8, b<1, d<1/2 give Cbd<4, whereas
P>2 and c>1 give P(2+c)>6. Since k=0 this proves the stronger lifted
return in (1) strictly as well, and therefore d<Ulift.

Finally Q>4 and C<8 give (Q+C)/Q^2<3/4. Thus

    cd[1+c(Q+C)/Q^2]
       <(5/12)[1+(5/4)(3/4)]=155/192<1.               (10)

This proves every inequality in (1), all strictly except the allowed
capacity boundary C=193/25.

### 2.4. Both endpoint components and all their open boundaries

For the projected values C,a,b arising in (4),

    Ksum>151/100, Ksum<153/100,
    Bprod<=P/(Cb)<2/5.

For the second inequality use P<=2401/1000 and
Cb> (193/25)(799/1000); these imply P/(Cb)<2/5 by direct multiplication.
Consequently Delta=Ksum^2-4Bprod>9/4-8/5=13/20>0. Both product roots

    d_minus=(Ksum-sqrt(Delta))/2,
    d_plus=(Ksum+sqrt(Delta))/2

are therefore real and distinct. Since sqrt(Delta)>4/5, d_plus>23/20.
Every admissible endpoint has d<=r_end<1, so the WHOLE upper product
component is empty for these projected values. Our actual d<1/3 lies
below Ksum/2 and satisfies d(Ksum-d)<Bprod by (9) and the suffix test.
It therefore lies strictly below d_minus, in the surviving lower
component. Its positivity, c>0 and strict bc<1 already checked in
(7)--(9) give every open boundary in (2), including d>Ksum-1/b.
The strict third cut and middle triangle give d>Lcut and d>Ltri.

The last-triangle polynomial does not introduce a hidden second
branch on this particular projected region. Writing v=(Q+C)/Q^2<3/4,
for every 0<=d<=Ksum and c=Ksum-d one has

    d*c*(1+v*c)<=Ksum^2/4 + (3/4)*(4*Ksum^3/27)
               <(153/100)^2/4+(153/100)^3/9
               =491589/500000<1.                    (11)

The product maxima used here follow from
dc<=Ksum^2/4 and d*c^2<=4*Ksum^3/27; the latter follows by
differentiating c^2(Ksum-c) on [0,Ksum]. Thus both (15) and (16) of
the preceding endpoint draft are actually decided on this region:
the upper product component is empty, the lower one has genuine strict
survivors, and the last-triangle exclusion is inactive. No closure
point or inadmissible endpoint was substituted for a survivor.
