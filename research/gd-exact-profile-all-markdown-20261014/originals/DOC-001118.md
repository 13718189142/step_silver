analytical round35. No numerical, solver, machine-algebra, replay,

Nothing here is a new fact. The original arbitrary-word asymptotic

The new constructive object is a positive-parallel, genuinely transverse
cell, followed by a later reversal. One static two-dimensional function
retains every old query, pays its actual transverse minimizer displacement,
and returns its full outgoing affine-history/sublevel-distance state.
This is a nonempty two-transition rule on the explicitly stated family.

accepted alternating-ray witness on the SAME word.

specified family of supplied words, EVERY smooth convex extension of the
displayed old jets, in every dimension, with any changed minimum and any
future gradients, has a normalized payoff below the same-word accepted
reference. Thus merely changing the final chronology cannot make this
particular old history competitive there. The adversary may instead choose
its prefix using the whole word; that freedom is not excluded.

## 2. Every changed minimum pays a radius cost

    Q_(B,star)=t_star-mu <=0,
    Q_(C,star)=-t_star-mu <=0,

    0<=mu<=1,       |t_star|<=mu.                     (2.1)

In particular, for the TRUE initial distance

    R=dist(3e,argmin F),       R>=3-mu>=2.             (2.2)

These constraints hold for every minimizer, including minimizers outside

## 3. Arbitrary subsequent gradients, with an explicit large-step payment

For any current point x, current gradient g, and h>=0, smoothness gives

    F(x-hg) <= F(x)-h(1-h/2)||g||^2.                  (3.1)

Also F(x)-mu>=||g||^2/2: apply the same inequality with h=1 and use
mu<=F(x-g). Thus each later step has the gap bound

    F(x-hg)-mu <= a(h)[F(x)-mu],
    a(h)=1 if 0<=h<=2, and a(h)=(h-1)^2 if h>2.       (3.2)

Let A_tail be the product of a(h) over the ACTUAL supplied tail, with
empty product1. Iterating(3.2) along its actual GD run gives

    2(F(x_end)-mu)/R^2
       <= A_tail * 2(C_delta-mu)/(3-mu)^2.            (3.3)

    2(2C_delta-3-mu)/(3-mu)^3 <=0,

    V_F:=2(F(x_end)-mu)/R^2

## 5. A genuine transverse escape with one static function

    q=(1/2)e+(2/5)v,       r_q=||q||^2=41/100,
    P(z)=1/2+<q,z>=1/2+t/2+2s/5,
    J(z)=max(0,t,-t,P(z),-2P(z)),
    f(x)=min_z [J(z)+||x-z||^2/2].                   (5.1)

Here is a direct justification of the static envelope construction.
The defining minimum exists, since J>=0 and the quadratic is coercive,
and it is unique by strict convexity. If a plane of normal g is active
at z and x=z+g, its supporting inequality shows that z minimizes the
objective: substituting any w gives an excess at least||w-z||^2/2.
Hence f(x)=J(z)+||g||^2/2. For two proximal points z(x),z(y), summing
their subgradient inequalities gives

    ||g(x)-g(y)||^2 <= <g(x)-g(y),x-y>,
    g(x)=x-z(x).

The supporting inequality and evaluation at z(x) bound the value
remainder between0 and||x-y||^2/2, proving differentiability with
gradient g(x). The displayed inequality proves its Lipschitz constant
is at most1. Convexity follows by joint convexity of the proximal
objective. Thus f is an everywhere finite convex1-smooth function.

In proximal coordinates the original residual(1.3) is equivalent to

    H_j >= H_i+<g_i,z_j-z_i>.

## 9. Zero steps, limiting cases, scaling, and exact scope

All ordinary blocks above can be split into arbitrary nonnegative lists.
Zero steps duplicate an actual jet; no proof divides by a step. Empty
blocks are included when their mass is0. The empty tail gives A_tail=1
and all bounds remain valid. In the explicit tilted family sigma=0 or
T=0 leaves strict positive heights by(5.4)--(5.5), even at the endpoints.
The boundary sigma=1/10,T=1/20 is attained, not merely a limit.

The bound(3.4) permits mu=0 and mu=1. At mu=1 some retained proximal
heights recentered by the minimum would be zero; allowing this boundary
only strengthens the obstruction. Strict-height extensions are included
as a smaller class. Delta=0, S=0, and S=1/2 are allowed whenever the
stated b condition is adjusted to b>=1 at its boundary. The uniform
performance bound extends continuously there. No positive gap or strict
height is claimed solely from a limiting zero-height construction.

Adding zero steps anywhere to either displayed actual word preserves
the actual trajectory, score, and accepted cut scores. It changes the
length n, and B_n>=3n+1 makes the benchmark comparisons still valid.
This creates arbitrarily long easy words, not a uniform result for all
words. The arbitrary-large-step suffix bound has its entire explicit
product A_tail; it is not a useful uniform-depth invariant by itself.

For any positive spatial scaling c, replace the objective by
f_c(x)=c^2 f(x/c), and start at3c e. Gradients and displacements scale
by c, heights and squared distances by c^2, and every normalized score
