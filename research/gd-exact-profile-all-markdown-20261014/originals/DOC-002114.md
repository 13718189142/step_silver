  **Conflated with a stronger claim.**a23356adcbb96c7e has a specified
  neighborhood and coefficient class;it does not optimize original W_n.
- "The new smaller activation constant removes the profile loss."
  **Conditional only.**It remains exponential in the matched small parameter,
  while transfer and threshold losses accumulate over the checkpoint count.
- "Use epsilon<kappa in the already proved formula."
  **Not automatic.**Coordinate extrema,normals,global planes and the true
  radius must be re-established with any changed constants.
- "A good record-level selection optimizes every checkpoint selection."
  **Method shortcut,not a proof step.**A selected pattern gives a lower
  certificate only;the maximum over all patterns needs an additional argument.
- "A weak mass-only certificate upper-bounds the constructed function."
  **Mathematically incorrect as stated.**The certificate is a lower estimate;
  its failure need not refute the actual true-distance payoff.
- "A killed reward is controlled by its killing vertex or pathwise budget."
  **Mathematically incorrect as stated.**20e54647c50a5a7f gives exact original
  arrays with missing signed compensation.
