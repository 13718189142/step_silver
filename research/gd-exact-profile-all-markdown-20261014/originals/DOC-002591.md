# Two-step suffix: a full joint move and the remote-flow obstruction

Status: UNVERIFIED. All statements are conditional on a complete certificate
or on a complete matching stationary primal, as explicitly specified below.
This is a hostile test and extension of J1, not a target theorem.

## 1. Full notation

Normalize L=1 and x_star=g_star=f_star=0. Let h>=0 and q>0.
Set Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2.
A full certificate means lambda_ij>=0, out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n),
and M=q||x_0||^2-2f_n+sum lambda_ij Q_ij PSD after the GD
substitution. Write mu_j=lambda_(j,star), nu_j=lambda_(star,j).

Let n>=2, k=n-2, a=h_k, b=h_(k+1). Define

c_1=mu_(k+1)+sum_(l<=k)lambda_(k+1,l),
c_2=mu_n+sum_(l<=k)lambda_(n,l),
c_3=c_2+lambda_(n,k+1)>0,
r_01=lambda_(k,k+1), r_02=lambda_(k,n), r_12=lambda_(k+1,n).

The last assertion follows from terminal flow:
c_3=2+nu_n+sum_(l<n)lambda_(l,n).

For the explicit deformation in Section2 only, assume

lambda_(i,j)=0 whenever 0<=i<k<j<=n.                 (R)

Thus no forward edge from an EARLIER prefix vertex crosses into the
two final gradient vertices. All three forward edges within
{k,k+1,n} remain allowed. The condition is vacuous at n=2.

## 2. Explicit finite deformation allowing local forward edges

Assume (R), a>1, and nu_k>0. First suppose c_2>0. Choose v>0 and define

u=(v/c_2)[c_1 - b r_02(c_2+v)/((a-1)(c_3+v))].       (1)

Require u>=0 and u+v<=nu_k. Change

nu'_k=nu_k-u-v,   nu'_(k+1)=nu_(k+1)+u,
nu'_n=nu_n+v,
lambda'_(k+1,k)=lambda_(k+1,k)+u,
lambda'_(n,k)=lambda_(n,k)+v,                         (2)

with all other multipliers unchanged, and set

a'=(a c_2+v)/(c_2+v),   b'=b c_3/(c_3+v).            (3)

Keep all earlier steps fixed. All new coefficients are nonnegative,
flow is preserved, a'>1, and 0<=b'<=b.

The complete matrix identity is

M(q,lambda',h')-M(q,lambda,h)
 =(a-a')(r_01+r_02)||g_k||^2
  +[u+(b-b')r_12]||g_(k+1)||^2+v||g_n||^2.           (4)

Every coefficient is nonnegative and v>0.

Here are all mixed-entry checks. For the pair (k,n), the coefficient
change is (a'-a)c_2+v(a'-1)=0. For (k+1,n), it is
(b'-b)c_3+b'v=0. For (k,k+1), it is
(a'-a)c_1+u(a'-1)-(b'-b)r_02=0 by (1).
Every pair involving an earlier prefix index is unchanged by (R):
a change of a or b could only couple to such an index through one
of the forward edges excluded by (R). The only changed outgoing
finite masses are +u at k+1 and +v at n; the diagonal dependence
on the changed steps contributes the remaining terms in (4).
The x_0 row is unchanged because every mu_j is unchanged.

The condition in (1) is explicit. In particular, if

(a-1)c_1 c_3 > b r_02 c_2,                            (5)

then all sufficiently small v>0 have u>=0, and they also satisfy
u+v<=nu_k. If r_02=0, (1) reduces to u=v c_1/c_2>=0
without needing a strict inequality in (5). Thus adjacent forward
edges do not obstruct this move; the skip edge k->n is different.

If c_2=0, then c_1>0 by the cut flow. Choose 0<u<=nu_k,
take v=0, and instead set

a'=(a c_1+u)/(c_1+u),   b'=b.

The same changes (2) give
M_new-M_old=(a-a')(r_01+r_02)||g_k||^2+u||g_(k+1)||^2>=0.
No division by c_2 is used in this case.

These identities imply an actual strict worst-case improvement,
not just feasibility at level q. For c_2>0 let
B=product_(t<n)(1+h'_t). On every new legal trajectory with
R=||x_0||>0 and E=2f_n/R^2, one has ||x_n||<=BR and
f_n<=||g_n||||x_n||. Therefore (4) implies

q-E>=v E^2/(4B^2),

hence
W_n(h')<=2q/[1+sqrt(1+v q/B^2)]<q.                   (6)

For c_2=0 use ||g_n||<=(1+b')||g_(k+1)|| to obtain the
same conclusion with v replaced by u/(1+b')^2.
These elementary bounds use the full old PSD matrix evaluated on
the new formal vectors. They do not pretend that the old matching
primal follows the new schedule.

## 3. A stronger necessary condition at a full stationary tuple

Now DROP (R). Suppose the certificate matches a SINGLE legal primal
transcript with ||x_0||=1, 2f_n=q>0, M zero on its data, and
lambda_ij Q_ij=0. Assume positive last steps and all cut stationarity
equalities <g_t,z_t(lambda)>=0, where

z_t(lambda)=sum_(j>t)(mu_j+sum_(l<=t)lambda_(j,l))g_j
            -sum_(i<=t)(sum_(j>t)lambda_(i,j))g_i.

First, <g_(n-1),g_n>=0. This follows from the complete system,
without differentiability of W: vary only the last step by s in
both signs on the same legal function. Its new final gradient
changes by O(|s|), and f_n(s)=f_n-s<g_(n-1),g_n>+O(s^2).
The old PSD form has value O(s^2) on the changed formal vectors
because it annihilates the old data. The first-order change of the
matrix itself has zero pairing by the last cut equality, so its
new value is also O(s^2). Since all new Q_ij<=0 and the same lambda
is nonnegative, the certificate identity implies
2f_n(s)<=q+O(s^2). Taking s in both signs gives the orthogonality.

Suppose nu_k>0. Complementarity gives

f_k=||g_k||^2/2.

The descent inequality then shows y=x_k-g_k is a minimizer. If
0<a<1, Lipschitz continuity of the gradient at the two endpoints
y and x_k forces g_(k+1)=(1-a)g_k: the two Lipschitz inequalities
have lengths summing to ||g_k||, so equality in the triangle
inequality fixes the vector. At a=1 the next point is a minimizer.
For 0<a<1, the last orthogonality makes g_n perpendicular to g_k,
while x_n-y=(1-a)(1-b)g_k; cocoercivity against y then forces
g_n=0. Both cases contradict q>0. Therefore

a>1.                                                 (7)

Put d=||g_k||^2, A=||g_(k+1)||^2, Bn=||g_n||^2,
u0=<g_k,g_(k+1)>, v0=<g_k,g_n>, and
F_i=lambda_(i,k+1)+lambda_(i,n) for i<=k.
All d,A,Bn are positive when q>0.

Interpolation Q_(k+1,k)<=0 and Q_(n,k)<=0, using f_k=d/2
and last-gradient orthogonality, gives

-(a-1)u0>=f_(k+1)+A/2,
-(a-1)v0>=f_n+Bn/2.                                  (8)

The kth cut stationarity equality is

c_1 u0+c_2 v0-F_k d-sum_(i<k)F_i<g_k,g_i>=0.

Combining with (8) yields the quantitative REMOTE-flow obligation

-sum_(i<k)F_i<g_k,g_i>
 >=F_k d+
   [c_1(f_(k+1)+A/2)+c_2(f_n+Bn/2)]/(a-1)
 >0.                                                  (9)

For strict positivity, summing the cut flow gives
c_1+c_2=2+nu_(k+1)+nu_n+sum_(i<=k)F_i>0,
and f_(k+1)+A/2 and f_n+Bn/2 are strictly positive.

Thus some earlier index i<k must satisfy BOTH F_i>0 and
<g_i,g_k><0. If there is no such remote forward edge, nu_k
cannot be positive at a full stationary tuple. In particular:

- At n=2, the sum in (9) is empty, so every full positive-step
  stationary tuple with q>0 has nu_0=0. No triangular-multiplier
  hypothesis is needed.
- At arbitrary n, a positive nu_(n-2) requires a genuine earlier
  forward edge into the last two samples and an oppositely directed
  earlier gradient. Local forward edges alone cannot support it.
- This gives the exact failure point for extending a suffix-only
  multiplier argument: the missing term is the signed remote sum
  in (9), not an unspecified PSD or regularity issue.

The conclusion does not eliminate terminal nu_n, does not force
q=1/(2sum h+1), and does not resolve the exact benchmark profile.
It only narrows the full-feasible obstruction.

All elementary inequalities used above follow from convexity and
1-smoothness. In particular, f(x-grad f(x))<=f(x)-||grad f(x)||^2/2;
and <grad f(x),x-y>>=||grad f(x)||^2 at any minimizer y follows by
adding the two smooth-convex interpolation inequalities with y.
