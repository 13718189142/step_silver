If W=UV, splitting the finite sums at the block boundary gives

## 7. An explicit paid boundary bound for the growing state

This subsection gives a concrete sufficient local payment, rather than

All factors are positive and at most1, and (3) gives the exponent bound.
The exact jump is

Cauchy--Schwarz in the finite j sum, retaining arbitrary common vector
cross terms, gives

                                      *sum_(j>=K)||b_j||^2.

Furthermore c_i=(h_i-1)s_(i+1)<=h_i*s_(i+1), and

 <=d_(K-1)^2 sum_i s_(i+1)h_i

Dropping only the nonnegative first sum in (9) proves the actual bound

The price is explicit, applies to the complete correlated high-mode

Keeping the full state therefore avoids an algebraic information loss,
