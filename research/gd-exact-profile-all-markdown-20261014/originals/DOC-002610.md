# C80: an exact two-dimensional four-step primitive obstruction

Status: UNVERIFIED joint candidate, no verifier allocation. This tests a
specific full primitive interface, not the original asymptotic theorem.
The defining construction and every required normal are given below.

Let h=(a,b,c,d), with a,d>=0, b>1, c>1. Define

 P=a+1, B=b-1, C=c-1, D=d+1, U=b+c+d, A=1+a+b+c+d.

Assume D>BC and put

 Kappa=B^2 C+U(D-BC), t=B^2 C/Kappa.

Then Kappa>0 and0<t<1. In the Euclidean plane define

 u=(1,0), w=(t,sqrt(t(1-t))), v=-(B u+D w)/c,
 K=conv(u,v,w), F(x)=max_(g in K)(<x,g>-||g||^2/2).

Here K is a nondegenerate triangle. Since B u+c v+D w=0 and B,c,D>0,
zero is in its interior. Completing the square shows grad F=P_K,
the Euclidean projection onto K. For completeness, the projection normal
inequalities at two points imply
||P_K(x)-P_K(y)||^2<=<P_K(x)-P_K(y),x-y>, so the projection is1-Lipschitz.
The unique-maximizer formula gives
0<=F(x+z)-F(x)-<P_K(x),z><=<P_K(x+z)-P_K(x),z><=||z||^2,
proving differentiability. F is convex as a supremum of affine functions.
Since0 is interior to K, for every x!=0 sufficiently small epsilon>0
has epsilon x in K, whence F(x)>=epsilon(1-epsilon/2)||x||^2>0.
Thus0 is its unique minimizer and F(0)=0.

The actual iterates and gradients are

 x=(P u,u,-B u,D w,w), g=(u,u,v,w,w).                    (1)

The four GD updates follow from c v=-B u-D w and D=d+1.
It remains to check that each listed gradient is the actual projection.
The inner products are

 ||u||^2=1, <u,w>=||w||^2=t,
 <u,v>=-(B+D t)/c, <v,w>=-(B+D)t/c.

At x0 the residual x0-u=a u has nonpositive pairing with v-u and w-u;
at x1 the residual is zero. At x2 the residual y2=-B u-v satisfies

 <w-v,y2>=[-B^2(c-1)+Kappa t]/c^2=0,
 <u-v,y2>=-B(1-t)(c-1)/c<0.

The first equality uses the expansion
Kappa=D(2B+D)+c(D-BD+B)-B c^2
       =B^2(c-1)+(b+c+d)(D-B(c-1)).
At x3 the residual d w pairs to zero with u-w and to
-d t[1+(B+D)/c]<=0 with v-w. At x4 the residual is zero.
These are all vertex comparisons and establish every projection normal.

Put f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2. Then for every original ordered pair

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2
      =<g_i-g_j,x_j-g_j><=0.

The optimum comparisons hold because0 belongs to K. In fact Q_(i,star)=0
for all i. There is one explicit globally legal function, with true initial
distance P to its unique minimizer, and no missing terminal comparison.

For the intrinsic energy

 V_h=2sum_(i=0)^3 h_i(f_i-f_4)+sum_(i=0)^3 h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                            -s(s+1)||g_4||^2,

where s=A-1, summing the GD norm identities and using
f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2 gives

 V_h=||x0||^2-||x4||^2-2s f4-s(s+1)||g4||^2
     =P^2-A^2 t.                                       (2)

Any original full nonnegative interpolation/PSD certificate must therefore
obey the following additional necessary inequality in this regime:

Indeed A=P+U and

is equivalent to (3). This is only a necessary condition for b,c>1 and
D>BC; it is not a full characterization or a global capacity obstruction.

## Closing the complementary adjacent-pair regime

If b,c>1 but D<=BC, use instead the one-dimensional function with
derivative min(x,1) and minimum value0 at its unique minimizer0.
Its formula is F(x)=x^2/2 for x<=1 and F(x)=x-1/2 for x>=1.
The iterates from x0=P are

 x1=1, x2=-B, x3=BC, x4=BC-D+1>=1,
 g=(1,1,-B,1,1).

All four physical updates hold. Again Q_(i,star)=0, while
f4=x4-1/2, so the exact primitive energy is

 V_h=P^2-(x4+s)^2=P^2-(P+bc)^2<0.                      (10)

Thus D>BC is itself necessary for a primitive word when b,c>1.
Combining (10) with the triangle regime proves that (3), equivalently
(9), is a necessary condition for EVERY adjacent b,c>1, without a
separate assumption on D-BC. Its positive right side also implies
D>BC directly. This is still not a condition on pairs having a step<=1.

## Additional adjacent-pair filter

The same construction applies to any nonnegative word with two adjacent
steps b>1,c>1. Let a be the sum of all preceding steps and d the sum of
all following steps. Use initial point(a+1)u, retain gradient u throughout
the preceding steps, and retain w throughout the following steps.
All intermediate residuals on those blocks are nonnegative multiples of
u or w and satisfy the already displayed normals. Thus (3) is a valid
necessary condition at each adjacent pair with b,c>1, without asserting
that the prefix or suffix is primitive: the complementary one-dimensional
construction also retains constant gradients throughout those blocks.
In particular it may
be applied separately at j=0,1,2 of the original four-step word.

There is also an exact quantitative relation to the single-cut slack.
Put E=c+d+1=C+D+1 and delta=P E-A B, which is the single-cut
slack at b. Since A=P+B+E, direct expansion gives

 P^2 D-BC[P^2+2BP+BU]
        =P^2(D-CE)+C(P+B)delta.                         (7)

Thus the same actual triangle yields the exact energy

 V_h=U[P^2(D-CE)+C(P+B)delta]/Kappa.                     (8)

In particular primitive feasibility in the stated regime forces

 CE-D <= C(P+B)delta/P^2.                               (9)

At a saturated cut(delta=0), (9) forces the two-step suffix cut
condition CE<=D. For positive cut slack it gives its explicit allowable
defect, without assuming a primitive prefix or suffix. This is a
quantitative local interface, not an assertion that all capacity-optimal
words have a saturated cut or that the remaining global problem closes.
