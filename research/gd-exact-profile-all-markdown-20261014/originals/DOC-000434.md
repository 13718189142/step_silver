Uniform enclosure of the original Zhang--Jiang denominator rays and a conditional full-certificate four-step seed lift.

For a nonnegative integer n and a finite nonnegative schedule h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), let W_n(h) be the supremum of 2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L||x_0-x_star||^2), over all finite dimensions, all convex differentiable functions f with L-Lipschitz gradient and at least one minimizer x_star, and all x_0!=x_star, with x_(i+1)=x_i-(h_i/L)grad f(x_i). Here L>0 is arbitrary and fixed. The empty schedule is included. This is the W=2R normalization.

For a,d>=0 define Omega(a,d)=(a+d+sqrt((a+d)^2+4ad))/2 and G(a,d)=2a+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8ad)/2. Set C_0=B_0=1 and, for every integer n>=1, C_n=max_(0<=k<n)Omega(C_k,C_(n-k-1)) and B_n=max_(0<=k<n)G(C_k,B_(n-k-1)). These are the original all-split benchmark recurrences, with C_n=1+S_circle(n), B_n=1+2S_bullet(n), and U_n^ZJ=1/B_n. Put lambda=1+sqrt(2).

For every positive integer b, the positive finite limit Z_b=lim_(j->infinity)lambda^(-j)B_(b*2^j-1) exists. For every integer j>=0,

    lambda^(-j)B_(b*2^j-1) <= Z_b <= lambda^(-j)(sqrt(B_(b*2^j))+3/100)^2.

The strict oriented comparison is Z_15/Z_3<19299/2500<193/25. For every fixed nonnegative integer d, lambda^(-j)B_(b*2^j-1-d) also converges to Z_b, over indices where it is defined.

The following further assertions are conditional. Suppose that a=(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3) consists of four nonnegative real steps with s=sum_(i=0)^3 a_i>=168/25, and put A=1+s. Suppose there exist twenty nonnegative real multipliers Lambda_ij, for distinct i,j in {0,1,2,3,4}, with Lambda_ii=0, satisfying the following FULL five-gradient certificate. Let e=(1,1,1,1,1)^T, let T_ii=1, T_it=a_t for t<i, and T_it=0 for t>i, and define

    L_flow=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    omega=(-2a_0,-2a_1,-2a_2,-2a_3,2s)^T,
    D_prim=diag(a_0(2-a_0),a_1(2-a_1),a_2(2-a_2),a_3(2-a_3),-s(s+2)).

Assume L_flow^T e=omega and M_prim=D_prim+(L_flow T+T^T L_flow^T)/2 is positive semidefinite on all of R^5. In particular no equality between formal gradients is imposed at zero steps, and no coordinate or interpolation-pair support is deleted.

For each n>=0 choose any fully expanded maximizing tree of the ORIGINAL B_n recurrence, with every C child expanded by the original C recurrence. Such a tree has n primitive empty leaves and one terminal dominant empty leaf. Replace each primitive empty leaf by the actual four-step word a, retain every internal node, and leave the terminal dominant leaf empty. Recompute each actual bridge as follows: for a primitive--primitive join with child capacities A_left=1+sum(left) and A_right=1+sum(right), choose d_pp>0 solving d_pp(A_left+A_right+d_pp)=A_left*A_right and concatenate (left,1+d_pp,right); for a primitive--dominant join with left capacity A_left and right mass D_right=1+2sum(right), put zeta=(1+sqrt(1+8A_left/D_right))/2 and concatenate (left,1+A_left/zeta,right). Denote the resulting actual schedule by Hsub_n and its mass by Dsub_n=1+2sum Hsub_n.

Then length(Hsub_n)=5n, its full all-function objective certificate gives W_(5n)(Hsub_n)=1/Dsub_n, and

    (sqrt(A B_n)-(sqrt(A)-1))^2 <= Dsub_n <= A B_n.

The equality in W is attained by a one-dimensional Huber function whose initial distance to its unique minimizer is1. For every positive integer b,

    lim_(j->infinity) B_(5(b*2^j-1)) W_(5(b*2^j-1))(Hsub_(b*2^j-1)) = Z_(5b)/(A Z_b).

In particular, for EVERY integer j>=25, with n_j=3*2^j-1 and N_j=5n_j=15*2^j-5,

    W_(N_j)(Hsub_(n_j)) < (1-1/50000) U_(N_j)^ZJ.

This theorem does not assert existence of the four-step word and multipliers in its hypothesis, does not assert their impossibility, and does not resolve the original unrestricted asymptotic minimax question.
