## 4. Exact signed interval interface

## 9. What remains, precisely

use that f is nonincreasing on the negative ray and nondecreasing

Equation (9.1) is an unresolved interface, not a theorem or a closed
profile claimed for the unrestricted family. Section7 evaluates it
uniformly on the entire ratio band(T); Section8 evaluates a concrete

bound endpoint objective energy after a large-side-to-small-side
overshoot and possible later returns, when the negative and positive
endpoints occupy different affine/quadratic pieces. Bounding their
absolute radii by a common plateau loses the small-side objective

For completeness, the exact general-curvature one-step interval

when the relevant curvature is positive, and set the corresponding

    p'=max(C_+(q), p-h*min(kappa_-*p,b_minus), 0),
