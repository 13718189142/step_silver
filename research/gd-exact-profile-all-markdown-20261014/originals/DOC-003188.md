## 2. Full original-space seed condition

Normalize L=1, translate a selected minimizer to zero, and subtract f_star
so that its value is also zero. For a finite
nonnegative word h of length r, let s=sum h_i, A=1+s, and retain every
formal gradient g_0,...,g_r, including gradients at zero steps. Put

    x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t,
    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    V_h=2sum_(i<r)h_i(f_i-f_r)
          +sum_(i<r)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_r||^2.

Every actual smooth convex trajectory has Q_ij<=0. We call the seed
primitive when V_h>=0 on every such trajectory in every finite dimension.
A concrete sufficient certificate retains ALL ordered finite multipliers
Lambda_ij>=0, i!=j, and Lambda_ii=0. Let e be the all-ones column, and set

    T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, T_it=0 for t>i,
    L_flow=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(r-1),2s)^T,
    D_prim=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(r-1)(2-h_(r-1)),-s(s+2)).

The feasibility conditions are

    L_flow^T e=omega,
    M_prim=D_prim+(L_flow T+T^T L_flow^T)/2 >=0.            (4)

Indeed substituting the GD equations and collecting function coefficients
and gradient inner products gives the exact identity

    V_h+sum_(i!=j)Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M_prim,Gram(g_0,...,g_r)>.

To audit the matrix formula, the position terms of sum Lambda Q contribute
sym(L_flow(T-I)); the squared-difference terms contribute
sym(L_flow)-diag(omega)/2. Adding V_h's diagonal yields (4).
Thus (4) proves the required actual primitive inequality. It has no support,
rank, symmetry, quotient, or terminal-edge restriction.

Adding optimum multipliers cannot supply a missing positive x_0 diagonal:
that diagonal is zero. Its mixed coefficient with g_i is
-Lambda_(i,star), so PSD forces each Lambda_(i,star)=0. Summing the finite
function-flow equations then forces sum_i Lambda_(star,i)=0, and their
nonnegativity makes all these multipliers zero. Therefore the original
formal space (x_0,g_0,...,g_r) is retained, with its x_0 row zero.
No equality between formal gradients at duplicated query positions is
imposed. For the implication used here, sufficiency of (4) is enough;
no unproved dual attainment is invoked.

## 3. Complete primitive and dominant composition identities

Primitive join: let a,b be primitive, with capacities A=1+sum a and
C=1+sum b. Choose d>0 by d(A+C+d)=AC, put T_mass=A+C+d and
kappa=(T_mass/C)^2, and use the physical word h=(a,1+d,b).
Let p be its bridge query, z its terminal query, and j range over the
nonterminal queries of b. Direct expansion gives

    V_h=V_a+kappa V_b-(kappa-1) E_PP,
    E_PP=sum_j b_j Q_(j,p)+Q_(z,p)+d Q_(p,z)
                                      +sum_j b_j Q_(j,z).       (5)

For a coefficient audit, the value part of E_PP is
2sum b_j f_j+(d-C)f_p+(2-C-d)f_z. Its gradient part is

    sum_j b_j(1-b_j)||g_j||^2
       +[(C-d)/2-d^2]||g_p||^2+(C+d)||g_z||^2/2.

The remaining coefficient identities are
(kappa-1)(C-d)=2(A+d), kappa d^2=A^2, and kappa C^2=T_mass^2.
They follow from d T_mass=AC. Also 0<d<C and kappa>1, so all
added multipliers are nonnegative. If the child certificates have arbitrary
PSD remainders, the full parent remainder is their original-coordinate sum
M_a+kappa M_b, embedded on the actual child-gradient indices. The resulting
capacity is exactly Omega(A,C).
The physical bridge obeys 1+d=phi(A-1,C-1), as required by (1).

Dominant join: for a word h with terminal z, put D=2sum h+1. For
nonnegative weights u_i summing to D define

    J_(h,u)=||x_0||^2+2sum_i u_i Q_(i,star)-2D f_z
                                  -||x_0-sum_i u_i g_i||^2.    (6)

The empty word has u=1 and J=0. A primitive word is dominant with
u=(h,s+1), since direct expansion gives J=V_h. In general J>=0 implies
2f_z<=||x_0||^2/D on every actual trajectory.

Let a be a primitive left child of capacity A and b a dominant right child
with mass D and weights v. Define

    mu=2A/D,  zeta=(1+sqrt(1+8A/D))/2,
    gamma=A+1,  beta=1+A/zeta.

Use the physical parent word (a,beta,b), and weights u=(a,gamma,zeta v).
Their mass is G(A,D)=D zeta^2=2A+zeta D=2sum(parent)+1. With p the
bridge query, all right-child queries i>p, including its terminal, give

    J_parent=V_a+zeta^2 J_right
                         -2mu sum_(i>p)v_i Q_(i,p).             (7)

The physical bridge is exactly beta=psi(A-1,(D-1)/2); multiply
zeta^2-zeta=2A/D by D/zeta to check this against the radical in psi.

This is an equality on the full original formal space. Function
coefficients use mu D=2A. Expanding all squares and the GD bridge uses

    (A-1)A+mu D+gamma=gamma^2,
    zeta^2=zeta+mu,  zeta^2 beta=mu+gamma zeta;

these cancel the mixed terms and give (7). The child remainder is pulled
back from the actual right-child starting position x_p-beta g_p; hence the
original x_0 and all inherited gradients remain in the certificate.
All extra interpolation coefficients are nonnegative. Induction on the
actual tree supplies a full PSD objective certificate: divide (6), after
inserting its nonnegative interpolation representation, by D. Its PSD
remainder is the retained final square plus the embedded child remainders,
all divided by D. This is an all-function objective bound, not a bound on
a quadratic, projection or temporal subclass.

## 4. The exact physical substitution and the untouched dominant leaf

Fix an ACTUAL seed a satisfying section 2, of length r=m-1 and capacity
A=1+sum a>=1. For each n choose a fully expanded maximizing dominant tree
for B_n in (1), for example the smallest maximizing split at each node.
The unique dominant right spine ends at one dominant empty leaf. Every
other leaf is primitive: there are exactly n of them, by induction on (1).

Replace each primitive empty leaf by the actual word a, retaining every
original node, and recompute each physical bridge by (5) or (7). Leave the
last dominant empty leaf empty, with mass 1. Call the resulting word H_n
and its true mass D_n=2sum H_n+1. There are n old bridge steps and nr
inserted seed steps. Thus

    length(H_n)=mn,  W_(mn)(H_n)<=1/D_n.                       (8)

Every primitive branch capacity is multiplied by A, by homogeneity of
Omega. If the last dominant leaf were ALSO changed from mass 1 to A in
an auxiliary scalar evaluation, homogeneity of G would make the root
A B_n. This last change is scalar only, not an additional physical block.
The square-root join is

    sqrt(G(a,d))=(sqrt(d)+sqrt(d+8a))/2.

Fact 0be8b54e0a33381c proves that it is increasing and 1-Lipschitz in
sqrt(d). Propagating the final-leaf difference along the whole spine gives

    sqrt(A B_n)-(sqrt(A)-1)<=sqrt(D_n)<=sqrt(A B_n).            (9)

Writing c_A=sqrt(A)-1 and ell_n=c_A/sqrt(A B_n), this implies

    (1-ell_n)^2<=D_n/(A B_n)<=1,
    0<=A B_n-D_n<=2c_A sqrt(A B_n)-c_A^2.                    (10)

The lower square-root bound is positive because B_n>=1. The accepted
bound B_n>=2n+1 already makes ell_n tend to zero; the phase theorem gives
the sharper ell_n=O(n^(-rho/2)). The bound is independent of tree depth,
split ties and multiplier choices.

In fact (8) is equality. The actual nonnegative word H_n has sum
(D_n-1)/2. The one-dimensional Huber function in fact 0be8b54e0a33381c,
started at x_0=1 with threshold 1/D_n and minimizer 0, attains normalized
error 1/D_n on that entire word. Its initial distance is genuinely 1.
Together with the full upper certificate this proves, within the joint
candidate,

    W_(mn)(H_n)=1/D_n.                                      (11)

Restoring any L>0 by multiplying the normalized objective by L leaves
the specified h_i/L GD updates and the ratio 2gap/(L distance^2) unchanged.

## 5. Exact phase comparison, errors, and attaining sequences

Let X=n+1 and Y=mn+1=mX-(m-1). Source matching and the definition of eps
give the exact scalar ratio

    B_(mn)/B_n=(Y/X)^rho
        [Psi_F(log_2 X)+eps_X]/[Psi_F(log_2 Y)+eps_Y].         (12)

Thus the normalized actual objective satisfies

    B_(mn) W_(mn)(H_n)
      =(1/A)(Y/X)^rho
        [Psi_F(log_2 X)+eps_X]/[Psi_F(log_2 Y)+eps_Y]
        *[A B_n/D_n].                                      (13)

The last factor lies in [1,(1-ell_n)^(-2)]. Formula (13) retains all
three errors: finite-index phase errors eps, Y=mX-(m-1) rather than mX,
and the untouched terminal leaf. If L_Psi is a Lipschitz constant, then

    dist(log_2 Y,log_2 X+log_2 m)
        <=(m-1)/(Y log 2),

using -log(1-u)<=u/(1-u). Since min Psi_F>0, the denominator in (12)
stays bounded away from zero for large n. Uniform eps convergence,
this phase displacement, and (10) therefore make (13) converge uniformly
in its moving input phase to

    R_m,A(t)=(m^rho/A) Psi_F(t)/Psi_F(t+log_2 m).              (14)

More explicitly, fix ANY t in [0,1) and set

    X_j=floor(2^(j+t)),  n_j=X_j-1,  output horizon=m n_j.

Then dist(log_2 X_j,t)<=1/(X_j log 2), and (13) tends to R_m,A(t).
This constructs the required inputs, including at a minimizer of (14).
It does not assume that a convenient dyadic ray happens to minimize it.

Define the now-identified scalar threshold

    tau_m=m^rho min_(t in R/Z) Psi_F(t)/Psi_F(t+log_2 m).      (15)

The minimum is attained. Compactness gives the reverse inequality for
any sequence n->infinity, and the displayed n_j attains it. Therefore

    liminf_(n->infinity) B_(mn) W_(mn)(H_n)=tau_m/A.           (16)

Consequently A>tau_m is NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT for a fixed relative
improvement along some infinite subsequence of THIS actual substitution.
For sufficiency take delta=(1-tau_m/A)/2 and sufficiently large j in the
constructed minimizing-phase sequence. For necessity, a fixed improvement
would force the left side of (16) below 1. This is stronger than merely a
sufficient comparison of certificates because of the Huber equality (11).
It does not characterize other possible uses of the same seed.

## 6. What nonconstancy does and does not prove

For m=2^k, the phase shift is zero, so tau_m=m^rho. For every integer
m>=2 that is not a power of two, log_2 m is irrational: if it equalled
a/b with integers a,b>0, m^b=2^a would force m to be a power of two.
If Psi_F(t)>=Psi_F(t+log_2 m) for all t, integrate the continuous
nonnegative difference around the circle. Its integral is zero, so equality
holds everywhere. Iterating the irrational rotation and using continuity
would then make Psi_F constant. This contradicts Theorem 6.5. Thus

    tau_m<m^rho for every non-dyadic integer m.              (17)

This is a qualitative lowering, not the quantified threshold requested
as TASK output (1). It supplies neither a rational interval inside the
gap nor the sign of a comparison at a specified pair of rays.

A concrete caution: fix a nonzero irrational shift alpha and any
0<a_0<1. Let psi_e(t)=K(1+e cos(2pi(t-t_0))), with K>0 and

    0<e<(1-a_0)/(1+a_0).

It is positive, Lipschitz, periodic and nonconstant, but every ratio
psi_e(t)/psi_e(t+alpha)>a_0. Moreover at t=t_0 that ratio is strictly
greater than 1. Hence no prescribed quantitative lowering or specified
improving input phase follows from those qualitative properties alone.
If desired, this example also meets the numerical-free enclosure in (3):
take K=c_star*(1+1/phi_min)/2 and make e smaller than
min{1-c_star/K,c_star/(phi_min K)-1}. Those bounds are positive.
This is NOT a counterexample to the Bellman recurrence or to the real
source phase. It identifies exactly the missing Bellman information.

## 7. Comparison with the primitive baseline

There is an unconditional lower comparison within this same scalar model:

    tau_m>=P_(m-1).                                        (18)

To prove it, use a recursively generated maximizing primitive word of
length m-1 as the seed. Such a word is produced from empty leaves using
(5), and has capacity P_(m-1) by (1). Its substituted word is itself an
ordinary recursive dominant word of length mn, so D_n<=B_(mn) by (1).
Equation (9) with A=P_(m-1), followed by a sequence for any phase t, gives
m^rho Psi_F(t)/Psi_F(t+log_2 m)>=P_(m-1). Minimize over t.
Thus a seed capacity strictly above the primitive baseline is necessary;
strictly above the power envelope is sufficient; their difference cannot
be silently discarded.

One can further prove the existence of nondyadic lengths with a strictly
positive intervening gap, although these source inputs do not identify
such a length quantitatively. Let

    Gamma=(min Psi_F)/(max Psi_F).

## 9. Direct attempts at (21), and the precise remaining obstacle

First attempt: use the source's reciprocal sandwich. On fixed rays it
gives 2P_(b-1)<=Z_b<=lambda P_(b-1). These separate upper/lower bounds
do not orient the two objective phases and retain the factor lambda/2>1.
The stronger enclosure (3) still gives only
Z_15/Z_3<5^rho/phi_min, which exceeds the desired power threshold. It
therefore cannot prove (21).

Second attempt: use the homogeneous support barrier at the first pivot.
Define the symmetric continuum capacity
P_cont(x)=x^rho Phi(log_2 x), and d_star=1/c_star. A prospective
one-pivot upper bound based only on the global continuation cap is

    sup_(0<=a<=x) G(P_cont(a),d_star*(x-a)^rho),
    P_cont(0)=0.

It equals d_star*x^rho: the support inequality bounds it above by this
quantity, but a=0 attains it because G(0,d)=d. Thus this relaxation
cannot detect any strict phase penalty. Deleting a=0 does not repair it,
since the same value is approached as a tends to zero. One must charge
the total cost of a string of small pivots before the first macroscopic
one, as the source's concentration proof does qualitatively, or retain a
sharper phase-dependent continuation bound. A finite prefix of dyadic
ray values supplies lower approximations; without a certified remainder
it supplies no upper enclosure for the numerator Z_15.

The cosine family in section 6 explicitly falsifies a proposed shortcut
from nonconstancy/continuity/enclosure to a predetermined gap or favorable
ray. It does not reject a phase-refinement program that uses more of (1).
