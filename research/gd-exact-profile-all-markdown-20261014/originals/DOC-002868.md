## 2. Why the variable-step factorization cancels all mixed terms

For i<j the two telescoping sums are

 sum_(a<=i)mu_(a,j)=U_i(D_j+t_j D_(j+1)),
 sum_(a>j)mu_(a,i)=(U_(i-1)+t_i U_i)D_(j+1).

The coefficient of <g_i,g_j> in sum mu Qbar is exactly

 2(mu_ij+mu_ji)
 -2h_i sum_(a<=i)mu_(a,j)+2h_j sum_(a>j)mu_(a,i).           (5)

Expansion of(4) gives

 mu_ij+mu_ji
 =h_i U_i(D_j+t_j D_(j+1))
       -h_j(U_(i-1)+t_i U_i)D_(j+1),

so(5) is zero for every pair. This calculation allows unequal steps;
it is not an appeal to the constant-step source theorem.

Let d_i=sum_(j!=i)(mu_ij-mu_ji), so that the coefficient of f_i is
2d_i. For i<r put p_(i+1)=U_i D_(i+1), with p_0=0. The weights
entering i from smaller and larger indices are, respectively,

I_i=U_(i-1)(D_i+t_i D_(i+1)) for 1<=i<r,
 J_i=(U_(i-1)+t_i U_i)D_(i+1),

with I_0=0. Therefore

              t_i J_i-I_i=t_i^2 p_(i+1)-p_i.                (6)

If d_i=h_i, the coefficient of ||g_i||^2 is

 -d_i-2I_i-2J_i+2h_i J_i
                   =-h_i+2(t_i^2 p_(i+1)-p_i).             (7)

At the terminal, d_r=-s, the incoming weight is p_r, and its squared
gradient coefficient is s-2p_r. These formulas also directly check the
terminal orientation of the original array.

## 3. Complete variable-step construction with explicit sign tests

For given t_i>0 define

 P_k=1+sum_(i<k)(1+t_i),
 R_k=product_(i<k)t_i^(-1),

   = (P_k+R_k)(1+R_(k+1))/((1+R_k)(P_k-R_k)),

All these factors are positive. Their successive D ratios are

   = (P_k-R_k)(R_(k+1)-1)/((R_k-1)(P_k+R_k)).              (10)

For the exact sign check, U_k>=U_(k-1) and D_k>=D_(k+1) are equivalent
to the following two scalar inequalities for each 1<=k<r:

 -R_k(2+R_k+R_(k+1))
       <=P_k(R_(k+1)-R_k)
       <=R_k(R_(k+1)+R_k-2).                               (11)

No matrix PSD test is being replaced by these inequalities: the FULL
matrix will be proved identically zero. Inequalities(11) concern all
of the actual coefficient signs of this particular factorization.

Here is the complete value-flow verification. Define

 u_k=(sum_(i<k)h_i U_i)/U_k,
 v_k=(sum_(j=k+2)^r h_(j-1)D_j)/D_(k+1).

The exact identities are

 v_k=(P_(k+1)-R_(k+1))/(R_(k+1)-1).                        (12)

For u, start from u_0=0 and use
u_k=(U_(k-1)/U_k)(u_(k-1)+h_(k-1)). Inserting the preceding
formula for u_(k-1) gives
u_(k-1)+h_(k-1)=(P_k+R_k)/(1+R_k), and then(9) proves(12).
For v, start from v_(r-1)=0, which agrees with R_r=P_r, and use
v_(k-1)=(D_(k+1)/D_k)(h_k+v_k). The factor h_k+v_k equals
(P_k+R_k)/(R_(k+1)-1). Substitution in(10) proves the asserted
formula for v_(k-1). Thus both identities hold, including the endpoints.

Differences of(13) give d_i=h_i for i<r, and total flow zero gives

The definition of R gives
