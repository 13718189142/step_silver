\[
\limsup_m B_{2^{m+1}-1} E_m\le2(\sqrt2-1)<1.

The polar-cone check is included: for a nonzero negative clock gradient the minimizer is unique, and at zero negative clock gradient 0 remains the closest minimizer. The ceiling is therefore not an artifact of selecting a distant minimizer.

Scope is crucial: this ceiling concerns the displayed pasted witnesses. It is not a bound on \(W_n\), and it does not exclude a larger proof strategy that selects a global Huber or quadratic witness on these same words. Do not record it as an obstruction to the original conjecture or to every conceivable static gluing.
