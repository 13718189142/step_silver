## 2. One triangle made of the last three actual queries

For a scalar parameter z=alpha+i beta set sigma=|z|^2 and
kappa=alpha-sigma. Starting locally at1, the last two steps have positions

    v_0=1, v_1=1-d_0 z, v_2=(1-d_0 z)(1-d_1 z).

The forward triangle0->1->2->0 has signed doubled area

    Im(conj(v_0)v_1+conj(v_1)v_2+conj(v_2)v_0)
                          =-d_0^2 d_1 sigma beta.      (6)

To check it, the first two path-edge areas are -d_0 beta and
-d_1 beta|1-d_0 z|^2, while the return edge contributes
beta(d_0+d_1-2d_0d_1 alpha). Their sum is(6).
The triangle's squared-chord energy is sigma E(z), where

    E(z)=d_0^2+d_1^2|1-d_0 z|^2
                       +|d_0+d_1-d_0d_1 z|^2.         (7)

Hence the REVERSE triangle, by the exact cycle identity in accepted
dfde2fa326c908bf, has weight

    K(z)=sigma[d_0^2 d_1 beta^2-(kappa/2)E(z)].        (8)

Put A(z)=P_(q-2)(z). The same three ACTUAL indices q-2,q-1,q, with
the same reverse orientation, give the JOINT necessary condition

    sum_z w_z |A(z)|^2 K(z)<=0.                        (9)

There is no optimum vertex in this cycle. Nevertheless the optimum
remains imposed elsewhere on the joint prefix. All function values
cancel in(9); no separate component values are chosen in using it.

## 3. Positive fast defects and cubic slow credit

For the parameters in(1), elementary bounds valid for m>=4096 are

    1-6/m<=alpha<=1, |beta|<=1/20,
    sigma>=49/50, 0<kappa<=1/m, beta^2>=4/m.           (10)

sigma>=alpha^2>49/50. The step bounds follow directly from a,c, and

    |1-d_0z|<=1/2+(3/2)(1/20)<3/5,
    |1-d_1z|<=1+2/20=11/10.

    |d_0+d_1-d_0d_1z|
       <=3/4+3(6/m+|beta|)<1.

    |(1-d_0 z)(1-d_1 z)|^2<1/2.                       (11)

Thus each fast mode contributes at least(20/m)|P_q(z)|^2 to the
common cycle(9), regardless of its own illegal prefix.

For a slow mode, all prefix polynomials have modulus at most exp(R):
use |1-hz|<=1+h|z| and product(1+h|z|)<=exp(|z|S). In particular
|A(z)|^2<=exp(2R). Since |z|<=1/8, (7) gives

    E(z)<=9/4+4(19/16)^2+(31/8)^2<25.

The negative part of K(z) is at most

    (sigma/2) max(kappa,0) E(z)<= (25/2)|z|^3.

Here max(kappa,0)<=max(alpha,0)<=|z|; no sign assumption on a slow
mode's real part is needed. Its total possible negative contribution
to(9), after summing all slow probabilities, is therefore at most

    (25/2)exp(2R) R^3/S^3.                             (12)

Combining(9),(11),(12) yields the genuinely JOINT endpoint bound

    sum_(fast z) w_z |P_q(z)|^2
                         <=(5/8)m exp(2R)R^3/S^3.    (13)

This is the cubic credit constraint missed by an optimum cycle alone.
It allows positive weights on illegal fast components, but those weights
times their terminal amplification are uniformly controlled.

For any legal interpolant, optimum interpolation at q and final-step
smoothness give, for b in[0,2],

    2F(x_q-b g_q)
       <=sum_z w_z[2alpha-(1+b(2-b))sigma]|P_q(z)|^2.

On fast modes alpha<=1, so(13) bounds their contribution by twice its
right side. On slow modes alpha<=|z|<=R/S and |P_q|<=exp(R), so their
contribution is at most2R exp(2R)/S. This proves(3), with every queried

## 4. Benchmark estimate, and why the fast modes are relevant

The exact recursions from acceptedb98bb807d514e80d have mass joins

    Omega(A,B)=(A+B+sqrt((A+B)^2+4AB))/2,
    G(A,B)=2A+B/2+sqrt(B^2+8AB)/2.

    d^2/ds^2 log V(exp(s))
      =rho t(1+t)^(rho-2)[(1+t)^rho-1-rho t]/V(t)^2>0.

Thus V(t)V(1/t)>=V(1)^2=2. Substituting t=(A/B)^(1/rho) shows
Omega(A,B)<=(A^(1/rho)+B^(1/rho))^rho. Continuity covers zeros.
Induction gives C_k=S_circle(k)+1<=(k+1)^rho. Since G(A,B)<=4A+B,
induction and superadditivity of t^rho give B_k<=1+4k^rho.

Here a/(c-2)->1, c^m/lambda^m->e and (2/lambda)^m->0; hence
S/lambda^m->e. In particular m/S^2->0. Multiply(3) by B_n to obtain

## 7. Exact scope left open

The donor condition |z|<=R/S is essential to the cubic credit estimate.
Mixtures containing intermediate frequencies, including illegal modes
at many larger physical scales, are outside this theorem. Such modes
may supply more triangle credit and must be checked against other common
cycles; a union of individual-mode exclusions would not settle them.
Arbitrary normal mixtures, nonnormal cross-mode models, and the original
all-horizon/all-schedule exact target remain unresolved.
