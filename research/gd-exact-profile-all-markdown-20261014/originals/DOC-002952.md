## 1. Actual core data and the common projection function

The input is a nonnegative finite word p=(p_0,...,p_(ell-1)) and ALL vectors
x_i,g_i,0<=i<=ell, including the endpoint and every repeated/zero-step sample.
The optimum sample is0. Require

    x_(i+1)=x_i-p_i g_i, y_i=x_i-g_i,
    <g_j-g_i,y_i><=0 for every i,j, <g_i,y_i>>=0,
    x_ell=g_ell=w !=0.                                    (1)

Set K=conv(0,g_0,...,g_ell) and

    F_K(x)=max_(g in K)(<x,g>-||g||^2/2),
    f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2, f_*=0,
    A=1+sum p_i, R_i=sum_(t=i)^(ell-1) p_t, R^2=||x_0||^2,
    Delta=A^2||w||^2-R^2, kappa=A^2||w||^2/R^2.            (2)

Completing the square identifies the maximizer with the projection onto K;
(1) is exactly its normal condition. Adding the projection conditions for
two points gives ||P_K(x)-P_K(x')||^2<=<x-x',P_K(x)-P_K(x')>.
It follows that P_K is 1-Lipschitz. Evaluation of the two maxima at their
opposite maximizers bounds the differentiability remainder by ||x-x'||^2,
so F_K is convex differentiable and 1-smooth with gradient P_K and values (2).

Its minimizers are K^polar={z:<z,g><=0 for every g in K}. To prove the
nontrivial implication F_K(z)=0 => z in K^polar, test epsilon*g in the
definition and let epsilon decrease to0. The GD identity gives

    x_0=w+sum_(i<ell) p_i g_i.                             (3)

Thus <x_0,z><=0 for every minimizer z and
||x_0-z||^2>=||x_0||^2, with equality at0. R is the TRUE closest-minimizer
distance. Also direct expansion of the original interpolation residual gives

    Q_ij=<g_i-g_j,y_j>, Q_i*=0, Q_*i=-<g_i,y_i><=0.        (4)

Every subsequent normal check therefore covers all original ordered-pair,
bridge, old-sample and optimum interpolation inequalities, with the indicated
values. No child translation or common value shift is a free operation.

## 2. A finite explicit lift of every actual child

Call a child admissible for the joint operation if, in addition to (1),

    <g_i,w>>=0, <y_i,w>>=0,
    <g_i,x_0>>=0, <y_i,x_0>>=0 for every i.                 (5)

These are four lists of ACTUAL boundary pairings, not a condition on Delta
alone. Any input satisfying (1) can be converted into such a child. Define

    beta_min = max(0,
       max_i (-<g_i,w>),
       max_(i:R_i>0) (-<w,y_i>/R_i),
       max_i (-<x_0,g_i>/A),
       max_(i:R_i>0) (-<x_0,y_i>/(A R_i))).                (6)

An empty maximum is omitted. Choose any finite beta>=beta_min and set

    ghat_i=(g_i,sqrt(beta)),
    xhat_i=(x_i,(1+R_i)sqrt(beta)),
    what=(w,sqrt(beta)), Khat=conv(0,ghat_0,...,ghat_ell). (7)

The physical GD updates hold. For old vertices, the added gradient
coordinates cancel, so every old/old normal is exactly the original one.
The zero-vertex normal is -<g_i,y_i>-beta R_i<=0. The four pairings in
(5) become, respectively,

    <g_i,w>+beta,             <w,y_i>+beta R_i,
    <x_0,g_i>+A beta,         <x_0,y_i>+A beta R_i.         (8)

They are nonnegative by (6). If R_i=0, all remaining physical steps vanish,
so x_i=x_ell=w; uniqueness of projection gives g_i=w and y_i=0. Thus no
uncontrolled numerator was omitted in (6). Formula (6) is the exact minimum
beta within THIS constant-coordinate lift for all four signs (5).

The new values, minimizers and true distance are explicit:

    fhat_i=f_i+(R_i+1/2)beta,
    argmin F_Khat=Khat^polar,
    Rhat^2=R^2+A^2 beta,
    Deltahat=Delta,
    kappahat=1+Delta/(R^2+A^2 beta).                       (9)

The distance follows from (3) for the lifted samples. In particular all
positive defects remain strictly positive at every finite lift; the cost is
real and is included in (9). All nonzero original residuals remain present.

## 3. The joint construction with both complete children

Take any two inputs satisfying (1) and (5), with capacities A,C, initial
distances R_L,R_R, and endpoints w_L,w_R. Let r>0 be the positive solution

    P=A+C+r, rP=AC.                                      (10)

This is exactly the source primitive bridge1+r. Define unit vectors
e=w_L/||w_L||, d=x_R,0/R_R, and real isometries

    J_L u=(<u,e>,u-<u,e>e,0),
    J_R v=(-<v,d>,0,v-<v,d>d), a_scale=r||w_L||/R_R.      (11)

The common ambient space is R direct-sum e^perp direct-sum d^perp. Keep the
entire left transcript under J_L and the entire right one under a_scale J_R.
At the bridge, J_L w_L-(1+r)J_L w_L=a_scale J_R x_R,0, so the actual word
is (p_L,1+r,p_R). The parent endpoint W=a_scale J_R w_R is a core endpoint.
The cross Gram block, for all child vectors, is

    <J_L u,a_scale J_R v>
        =-r<u,w_L><v,x_R,0>/R_R^2.                        (12)

Equation (11) proves real PSD. The parent body is the convex hull of0 and
ALL displayed gradients. Left values are f_L,i; right values are
a_scale^2 f_R,j. Every within-child and optimum normal is inherited.
For every left sample i and right sample j, the two remaining normals are

    -r<g_R,j,x_R,0><w_L,y_L,i>/R_R^2-<g_L,i,y_L,i>,
    -r<g_L,i,w_L><x_R,0,y_R,j>/R_R^2
        -a_scale^2<g_R,j,y_R,j>.                          (13)

They are nonpositive because of (1) and (5). This checks EVERY cross pair,
including the left endpoint/bridge gradient and any nonzero right residual.
By (2)-(4) there is one actual smooth convex function realizing all data.
Its true initial distance is R_L, since the initial point is still the
positive combination of retained left gradients in (3).

Writing kappa_L,kappa_R for the full input ratios, exact norms give

    kappa_raw=P^2||W||^2/R_L^2=kappa_L kappa_R.            (14)

For future iteration apply (6)-(7) to this ALREADY LEGAL parent. Let
b=beta/||W||^2, where beta is its chosen reset buffer, and let

    sigma_L=<x_L,0,w_L>/(A||w_L||^2),
    sigma_R=<x_R,0,w_R>/(C||w_R||^2).

Using (12) and rP=AC gives the raw correlation
<X_0,W>/(P||W||^2)=-sigma_L sigma_R. Consequently the complete cost law is

    kappa_new=kappa_L kappa_R(1+b)/(1+b kappa_L kappa_R),
    sigma_new=(b-sigma_L sigma_R)/(1+b),
    R_new^2=R_L^2(1+b kappa_L kappa_R),
    Delta_new=R_L^2(kappa_L kappa_R-1).                  (15)

Here beta is the explicit finite maximum (6) for the actual raw parent.
Thus (11)-(15) are a concrete iterable operation with BOTH child defects.
It is not a scalar proxy or a bare matrix feasibility restatement. The
positive product term in Delta_new is present, and so is its normalization
cost. A reset cannot repair illegal cross pairs, because its old/old normals
are unchanged; that is why (5) is imposed BEFORE constructing the raw parent.

One old vertex forces a useful part of this cost. The raw parent retains
G_bridge=J_L w_L. To satisfy the first list in (5) after the reset requires

    beta >= -<G_bridge,W>,
    b >= C sigma_R/r=P sigma_R/A.                        (16)

Indeed (12) gives <G_bridge,W>=-r||w_L||^2
<x_R,0,w_R>/R_R^2 and ||W||^2=r^2||w_L||^2||w_R||^2/R_R^2.
This is a necessary inequality for the stated reset class. Other entries
in (6) may demand larger beta; none can remove (16).

## 4. The exact balanced identical-child iteration loses its relative surplus

Fix one actual input satisfying (1),(5), with kappa_0>1. At each level use
two identical copies of the current complete child and the balanced source
bridge, then ANY finite reset beta satisfying (5). This includes every
choice beta>=the exact maximum (6), with unrestricted retuning at each level.
Set lambda=1+sqrt2, theta=sqrt2-1. Then

    A_(j+1)=lambda A_j, r_j=theta A_j,
    u_j=1/kappa_j=R_j^2/(A_j^2||w_j||^2),
    sigma_j=<x_j,0,w_j>/(A_j||w_j||^2),
    b_j=beta_j/||W_j||^2,

where W_j is the raw endpoint before the jth reset. Equations (15)-(16) give

    u_(j+1)=(u_j^2+b_j)/(1+b_j),
    sigma_(j+1)=(b_j-sigma_j^2)/(1+b_j),
    b_j>=lambda sigma_j.                                (17)

These recurrences were derived from the actual vectors, not substituted for
their legality. The full construction in sections1-3 proves existence for
every finite depth, and every buffer selected by (6) is finite.

For every level, 0<u_j<1 follows from kappa_j>1 and (17). Also
0<sigma_j<1: (3) and the first sign list in (5) give
<x_j,0,w_j>>=||w_j||^2>0; Cauchy--Schwarz gives sigma_j^2<=u_j<1.
In particular a zero starting correlation is not an omitted boundary case
for a positive-defect input of this class.

Define

    F(s)=(lambda s-s^2)/(1+lambda s),
    s_min=min(sigma_0,theta)>0,
    eps_0=(theta-sigma_0)_+,
    c=(1-s_min)/(1+lambda s_min) in[0,1),
    mu=lambda eps_0/2 in[0,1/2).

The map (b-s^2)/(1+b) increases in b, so sigma_(j+1)>=F(sigma_j).
For 0<s<=theta,

    F(s)-s=(lambda+1)s(theta-s)/(1+lambda s)>=0,
    theta-F(s)=(theta-s)(1-s)/(1+lambda s).

For theta<=s<=1,

    F(s)-theta=(s-theta)(1-s)/(1+lambda s)>=0.

These identities prove by induction

    sigma_j>=s_min,
    (theta-sigma_j)_+<=eps_0 c^j,
    b_j>=1-lambda eps_0 c^j.                             (18)

Now set d_j=1-u_j in(0,1), so kappa_j-1=d_j/(1-d_j). From (17)-(18),

    1/d_(j+1)=(1+b_j)/[d_j(2-d_j)]
       >=(1-mu c^j)(1/d_j+1/2).                         (19)

The last step uses 1/[d(1-d/2)]>=1/d+1/2 for 0<d<1.
For 0<=t<=mu<1, log(1-t)>=-t/(1-mu), by integration of -1/(1-t).
Therefore every finite product of the factors in (19) is at least

    P_min=exp[-mu/((1-mu)(1-c))]>0.                      (20)

Iteration of (19), including every inhomogeneous1/2 term, gives

    1/d_j>=P_min(1/d_0+j/2),
    kappa_j-1<=1/[P_min(1/d_0+j/2)-1]                    (21)

whenever the last denominator is positive. Thus kappa_j-1=O(1/j) and
in particular tends to0, uniformly over EVERY sequence of allowed finite
buffers in this class for the fixed seed. If sigma_0>=theta, then P_min=1
and the last upper bound is2/[j+2/d_0-2]. No assumption of minimal buffers
or a favorable interaction sign has been made.

The absolute defect does retain both children:

    Delta_(j+1)=2Delta_j+Delta_j^2/R_j^2,
    R_(j+1)^2=R_j^2[1+b_j(1+Delta_j/R_j^2)^2].            (22)

Nevertheless the necessary distance charge (16) makes the normalized
surplus vanish. This is weaker loss than the previous serial exponential
loss, but it still precludes a fixed positive relative surplus from this
specified identical-child reset operation.

## 5. Actual defective seeds and a leading-order mass change

    x_0=(H+z)U+HV, g_0=U;
    x_1=bU+HV,     g_1=U;
    x_2=HV,        g_2=V;

The updates hold. At sample0 the normal against V is -a; at sample1 it
is0. The zero normals at these samples are -(a+b-1) and -(b-1). At

The value and exact-distance formulas of section1 apply. Direct expansion

    A=H(2+xi), R^2=H^2 D,

Thus every xi>theta is an actual positive-defect seed for the ALL-DEPTH

For a concrete continuation of the detected small word, put

Every buffer modifies the witness, not the physical word. Hence the step

    A_j(t)/A_j(0)=1+(10s-14)t/lambda^2>1.                 (26)

This is a genuine leading-order mass perturbation and not the previously

## 6. Matched objective-tail conversion, without another hidden buffer

Take ANY final child satisfying (1),(5), of capacity A, endpoint w and
true distance R. Let the actual finite tail z have nonnegative entries,
total T>=0, and D=1+2T. Choose any d>0, set k=-(d/D)w, and append
bridge1+d followed by exactly that tail. Enlarge the same body to include k.

At every old sample, the k normal is

    <k-g_i,y_i>=-(d/D)<w,y_i>-<g_i,y_i><=0.

At any point v k with v>=1, the normal against an old vertex g_i is

    (v-1)<g_i-k,k>
      =-(v-1)[(d/D)<g_i,w>+(d/D)^2||w||^2]<=0.

The normals against0 and k also hold. Thus every old sample stays legal
and k is the gradient on the entire tail. After the bridge the iterate is
Dk; at a tail partial sum t it is (D-t)k, with D-t>=T+1>=1. At the final
point (T+1)k the exact value is

    2F_final=D||k||^2=d^2||w||^2/D.                       (27)

The true initial distance remains R by (3), because the old positive-cone
representation is retained. For the source dominant bridge choose

    d=(sqrt(D^2+8AD)-D)/4,
    2d^2+Dd=AD,
    B_concat=2A+2d+D=(D+2d)^2/D.                         (28)

The identity d(D+2d)=AD proves d^2/(DA^2)=1/B_concat. Hence the attained
normalized objective for the ACTUAL schedule is exactly

    E_tail=kappa/B_concat.                              (29)

This is the full conversion used here; it needs no C61/C62 premise and no
unrecorded final lift. It allows arbitrary ordering, arbitrary finite
nonnegative tail entries, zero mass, and the empty tail. Both L-scaling and
normalizing the true initial distance follow directly from (2).

For every resulting horizon n=ell+1+length(z), its exact comparison is

    B_n W_n(p,1+d,z)>=kappa B_n/B_concat.                (30)

Nothing replaces B_n by B_concat or by a dyadic asymptotic proxy. For the
iteration in section4, the EXTRA factor kappa tends to1 by (21).
The construction therefore supplies no persistent relative surplus over
its own actual reciprocal-mass witness. The right side of (30) must still
be compared with the exact B_n before drawing any schedule-level conclusion.

For example, with an empty objective tail, B_concat(A)=2A+1/2+sqrt(1+8A)/2.
Equation (25) gives B_concat(A_j(t))/B_concat(A_j(0)) tending to the fixed
ratio (26). This identifies the leading mass increase; it does NOT assert
that the unmodified split attains the optimizing B_n at those horizons.

## 8. What this closes and what is still missing

This package gives a full legal, real, arbitrary-depth two-child construction,
an exact interaction/normalization law, and a necessary old-vertex cost
that makes its relative surplus O(1/depth). It retains a growing collection
of modified blocks, nonzero post-step residuals, values and the global
closest-minimizer distance. It is a substantive improvement over a serial
lift's geometric dilution, but it still has no fixed asymptotic gap.

Leaving this obstruction requires retaining signed boundary data rather than
forcing all four lists (5), changing the cross-correlation operation, using
nonidentical/unequal children outside the class of section4, or changing the
core interface itself. A transverse correlation change alone cannot repair
the particular planar bridge normal recorded in the separate round18 draft.
None of these remaining mechanisms is proved here. A failed lower gluing
rule gives neither an all-h lower theorem nor an improving full upper
certificate. The original target, with every horizon and every nonnegative
schedule compared to the exact B_n, remains open.
