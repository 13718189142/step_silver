## 1. Original full certificate and the physical word

For a nonnegative word w of length n, W_n(w) is the supremum of
2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L||x_0-x_star||^2), over all finite Euclidean
dimensions, all convex differentiable functions with L-Lipschitz
gradient and a minimizer x_star, and x_0!=x_star, under
x_(t+1)=x_t-(w_t/L)grad f(x_t). Here L>0 and the empty word is allowed.
This is the project's W=2R normalization. The letter q below follows
TASK.md and means the child WORD, not a scalar derivative from another
fact. Other locally defined letters carry the scopes explicitly given.

Normalize the smoothness to1. For a word w=(w_0,...,w_(n-1)), put
S_w=sum w_t, K_w=1+S_w, x_(t+1)=x_t-w_t g_t, and

 Q^w_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_w=2 sum_(t<n) w_t(f_t-f_n)
       +sum_(t<n)w_t(1-w_t)||g_t||^2-S_w(S_w+1)||g_n||^2.

All finite ordered interpolation residuals Q^w_ij are nonpositive on an
actual convex differentiable1-smooth function. Let Lambda^w have zero
diagonal and nonnegative entries on every ordered distinct pair. With
e the all-ones column, T^w_tt=1, T^w_tu=w_u for u<t, zero for u>t, set

 L^w=diag(Lambda^w e)-Lambda^w,
 omega^w=(-2w_0,...,-2w_(n-1),2S_w)^T,
 D^w=diag(w_0(2-w_0),...,w_(n-1)(2-w_(n-1)),-S_w(S_w+2)),
 M^w=D^w+(L^w T^w+(T^w)^T(L^w)^T)/2.

The assumed input conditions are (L^w)^T e=omega^w and M^w PSD on the
whole R^(n+1). Accepted04219554e41d9750 gives the polynomial identity

 V_w+sum_(i!=j)Lambda^w_ij Q^w_ij=<M^w,Gram(g_0,...,g_n)>.       (1)

There is no quotient at a zero step, no restriction to a gradient rank,
and no support, product, reflection, or multiplier symmetry assumption.

Let h have length r, h_i>=1, capacity A=1+sum h_i, and a full input
certificate as above. Let q have length s, nonnegative steps, capacity
C=1+sum q_a, and an arbitrary full input certificate. Define

 beta_i=1+C(h_i-1),
 H=(q,beta_0,q,beta_1,...,q,beta_(r-1),q).                     (2)

There are r+1 child copies, r bridge steps, and exactly

 N=(r+1)s+r=(r+1)(s+1)-1

physical steps. There are N+1=(r+1)(s+1) formal gradients. Index a node
as (i,a), where 0<=i<=r, 0<=a<=s, at physical index i(s+1)+a. These
disjoint child node sets partition the entire formal space. The endpoints
p_i=i(s+1)+s are the bridge queries for i<r and the final query for i=r.
The full array has N(N+1) ordered off-diagonal slots. In particular, all
gradients at zero child steps are retained.

Every physical step is nonnegative because beta_i>=1. Direct summation gives

 1+sum H=1+(r+1)(C-1)+r+C(sum h_i-r)=AC.                     (3)

If r=0 the output is q; if s=0 it is h. An all-zero but nonempty q
still contributes all its formal gradients and zero physical steps.

## 2. Exact virtual data and energy decomposition

All child copies are segments of ONE actual GD trajectory for ONE static
function. For block i write z_i for its starting position, y_i=x_(p_i)
for its endpoint, e_i=g_(p_i), f_i^e=f(x_(p_i)), and

 P_i=sum_(a<s)q_a g_(i,a),
 dinf_i=P_i-(C-1)e_i,
 Dinf_0=0,  Dinf_i=sum_(k=1)^i dinf_k for i>=1.

Thus y_i=z_i-P_i and z_(i+1)=y_i-beta_i e_i. Define formal virtual jets

 X_i=[y_i+(C-1)e_i+Dinf_i]/C,
 F_i=f_i^e/C+(C-1)||e_i||^2/(2C).                            (4)

Their positions obey EXACTLY X_(i+1)=X_i-h_i e_i: indeed the change in
the numerator is

 -beta_i e_i-P_(i+1)+(C-1)(e_(i+1)-e_i)+dinf_(i+1)
 =-[beta_i+C-1]e_i=-C h_i e_i.

This is a formal algebraic GD relation; no interpolating virtual function
is asserted. Define the virtual residual

 Qhat_ij=F_i-F_j+<e_i,X_j-X_i>+||e_i-e_j||^2/2.

Expanding (4) cancels every extra norm and endpoint inner-product term:

 C Qhat_ij=Q^H_(p_i,p_j)+<e_i,Dinf_j-Dinf_i>.                     (5)

Let V_i denote the ORIGINAL primitive energy V_q of block i, relative to
its own actual endpoint. The following identity is exact on every formal
gradient and function-value coordinate:

 V_H=sum_(i=0)^r V_i+C^2 V_h(F_0,...,F_r;e_0,...,e_r).       (6)

Here the final energy uses the original steps h and the virtual data (4).
To check (6), subtract the child energies. The coefficient of f_i^e-f_r^e
for i<r is 2[(C-1)+beta_i]=2Ch_i. The coefficient of ||e_i||^2 is

 beta_i(1-beta_i)+C(C-1)
 =C^2h_i(1-h_i)+C(C-1)h_i.

The final coefficient is C(C-1)-(AC-1)AC. Since sum h_i=A-1, this equals

 -C(C-1)(A-1)-C^2 A(A-1),

which is precisely the final coefficient obtained from C^2 V_h and (4).
All internal child coefficients agree without alteration. These checks
also apply at zero child steps.

## 3. A full positive-flow candidate, and the exact missing payment

Let E_i be the coordinate restriction from all physical gradients to
the s+1 gradients of block i. Let E extract the r+1 endpoint gradients.
Embed child arrays in their original coordinate sets and put

 Lambda^0=sum_i E_i^T Lambda^q E_i+C E^T Lambda^h E.           (7)

All off-diagonal entries are nonnegative; coinciding contributions add.
The flow is exactly the required physical flow. Internal child nodes
have flow -2q_a. At a nonterminal block endpoint, its flow is

 2(C-1)-2C h_i=-2 beta_i.

At the final endpoint it is 2(C-1)+2C(A-1)=2(AC-1).

Let G be the full physical gradient Gram matrix, and define B as the
quadratic form

 B=C sum_(i!=j)Lambda^h_ij <e_i,Dinf_j-Dinf_i>.                    (8)

Combining (1), (5), and (6), the full remainder of (7) is

 V_H+sum Lambda^0_ab Q^H_ab
 =sum_i <E_i^T M^q E_i,G>+C^2<E^T M^h E,G>-B.              (9)

The first two terms are full-coordinate PSD congruences. The last term
has no established sign. Dropping it is an error. Thus (7) is an explicit
positive-flow lift, but NOT a proved PSD lift.

It actually fails for the M=0 inputs (13). In their full physical space
R^4 choose scalar formal gradients (g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3)=(0,1,1,0).
Then e_0=1, e_1=0, dinf_1=a, and (9) equals -2Ca<0. These are formal
coordinates used to disprove PSD of (7); they are NOT asserted to be
an actual function trajectory. A different array certifies the physical
word, as Section4 shows.

Here is a precise additional sufficient collective payment condition.
Put K=C^2 sum Lambda^h_ij Qhat_ij. Suppose there are real numbers
0<=theta_i<=1, an actual ordered array Gamma_ab>=0 with zero diagonal,
and a matrix P PSD on all physical gradients, such that the following
POLYNOMIAL identity holds after the original physical GD substitutions:

 K=sum_i theta_i V_i+sum_(a!=b)Gamma_ab Q^H_ab-<P,G>.         (10)

Then a full certificate is explicitly

 Lambda^out=sum_i(1-theta_i)E_i^T Lambda^q E_i+Gamma,
 M^out=sum_i(1-theta_i)E_i^T M^q E_i+C^2 E^T M^h E+P.        (11)

Indeed substitute (10) into (6) and use both input identities (1).
Every array coefficient in (11) is nonnegative and every matrix summand
is PSD on the unchanged physical coordinates. Cancellation of all formal
function values implies the exact original flow; the matrix equals the
original D+sym(LT) by (1). This is an EXTRA sufficient matching condition,
not a consequence proved from the stated input primitive conditions.

For an exact formulation with no sufficient-cone narrowing, any array
with the physical flow equals Lambda^0+Xi, where Xi is a real zero-
diagonal array with zero divergence at every node. Full feasibility is
equivalent to existence of such Xi with Lambda^0+Xi entrywise nonnegative

 sum_i E_i^T M^q E_i+C^2E^T M^h E-B+sum Xi_ab Q^H_ab          (12)

PSD. The last sum is purely quadratic because Xi has zero divergence.
In (12), the embedded matrices are evaluated against G; B and the last
sum denote scalar quadratic forms on the same full gradient coordinates.
This is a useful exact residual interface, not a solution of that
feasibility problem. It does not discard any original array.

## 4. The virtual-oracle shortcut is false even for two M=0 inputs

Put a=sqrt(2), C=1+a, d=a-1=1/C, and take h=q=(a). Both words have
the ORIGINAL full certificate

 Lambda_01=2, Lambda_10=2C, Lambda_00=Lambda_11=0, M=0.       (13)

For completeness, the flow is (2-2C,2C-2)=(-2a,2a).
With T=[[1,0],[a,1]], the first diagonal of D+sym(LT) is
a(2-a)+2(1-a)=2-a^2=0; the second is
-a(a+2)+2C=0; and twice the off-diagonal is -2+2C(a-1)=0.
Thus (13) verifies the entire two-gradient matrix and both ordered signs.

The physical output is H=(a,2,a), with length3 and capacity C^2.
Use the single static convex1-smooth function f(x)=x^2/2, minimizer0,
and initial point x_0=-C. Its whole actual run is

 x_0=-C,  x_1=1,  x_2=-1,  x_3=d,
 g_t=x_t,  f_t=x_t^2/2.

Block0 ends at e_0=y_0=1. Block1 starts at -1, ends at e_1=y_1=d,
and has P_1=-a, dinf_1=-a-a d=-2. Consequently the virtual data (4) are

 (X_0,F_0,e_0)=(1,1/2,1),
 (X_1,F_1,e_1)=(-d,d^2/2,d).

They obey X_1=X_0-a e_0, but

 Qhat_01=-2d<0,      Qhat_10=2d^2>0.                       (14)

For the second equality, expansion gives
Qhat_10=(d^2-1)/2+a d+(d-1)^2/2=d(d+a-1)=2d^2.
For the first, Qhat_01=(1-d^2)/2-a+(1-d)^2/2=1-d-a=-2d.

Thus these virtual jets are NOT smooth-convex interpolable. Both input
certificates are M=0 and satisfy the balanced product relation. Neither
of these additional restrictions rescues this virtual-oracle assertion.

Both actual child energies are zero: on the unit quadratic the one-step
energy is x_start^2-C^2 x_end^2, and C d=1. Every actual Q is zero.
Therefore even allowing nonnegative payments by child energies cannot
make each virtual interpolation residual nonpositive separately: the
positive Qhat_10 in (14) has zero child payment available. By contrast,
the template-weighted sum is

 2 Qhat_01+2C Qhat_10=-4d+4C d^2=0.

This demonstrates why a potentially viable theorem must control the
COLLECTIVE template-weighted expression, not certify a fictitious
smooth-convex oracle pair by pair.

This example is explicitly NOT a counterexample to the physical lift.
H=(a,2,a) is the accepted binary primitive join of q with itself: the
join surplus is1, since 1(2C+1)=C^2. Its full lifted certificate exists
by accepted04219554e41d9750 Section11. Its actual V_H is zero on this run.

## 5. A sufficient closure in the proved, proper template subclass

Call a template BASIC here if it is obtained by primitive--primitive
binary joins starting from empty primitive leaves, with surplus d_v>0
at every node satisfying d_v(A_left+A_right+d_v)=A_left A_right.
This is precisely the primitive tree construction of the accepted fact,
not a definition imposed on arbitrary primitive inputs.

For every such basic template h and every arbitrary full-certificate
nonnegative child q, the word (2) DOES have a full certificate. Replace
each of the r+1 empty leaves of its tree by q. At each original node,
its two child capacities and its surplus are all multiplied by C:

 (A_left,A_right,d_v) -> (C A_left,C A_right,C d_v).

The defining surplus equation and its positive root are preserved.
The bridge becomes exactly 1+C(h_i-1), in its original inorder position.
The resulting inorder word is (2), and its length and capacity are (3).

Here is the full array transformation, including the sign proof. At
each transformed node set kappa=((A_left+A_right+d_v)/A_right)^2,
which is unchanged under the common scaling. Embed the complete left
array with factor1 and right array with factor kappa. Write p for the
bridge query, z for the node's final query, and b_j for each physical
right-child step at its nonterminal node j. Add the actual ordered edges

 (j,p): (kappa-1)b_j,    (z,p): kappa-1,
 (p,z): (kappa-1)C d_v,  (j,z): (kappa-1)b_j.                (15)

There are no omitted finite coordinates. All coefficients are
nonnegative since kappa>1, d_v>0, and the physical b_j>=0. The accepted
identity gives exactly

 M_parent=E_left^T M_left E_left
                 +kappa E_right^T M_right E_right,         (16)

with the original flow and D+sym(LT). Induction proves the full matrix
PSD and every original ordered sign. This uses one same function on
one actual GD word throughout; there is no oracle reset.

The template subclass is proper EVEN INSIDE the full M=0 class. For
h=(3/2,3/2), a full M=0 array is

 Lambda=[[0,1,1/2],[7/2,0,17/2],[1,14,0]].                 (17)

Its row-minus-column flow is (-3,-3,6). For
T=[[1,0,0],[3/2,1,0],[3/2,3/2,1]] and D=diag(3/4,3/4,-15),
the diagonal entries of LT are (-3/4,-3/4,15), and the paired

 (-1-3/4)+(7/4)=0,
 (-1/2)+(1/2)=0,

Thus D+sym(LT)=0 on all three formal gradients; all six ordered entries

about using (17), or every other M=0 word, as an arbitrary template.

## 6. Repetition and the distinction between seed and template

For a FIXED basic template h of length r>=1 and capacity A, start with
any fixed nonnegative full primitive child q^(0) of length s and capacity
C. Recursively use (2) with q=q^(k). Equations(15)--(16) give actual full
certificates at every depth; all physical steps remain predetermined
and nonnegative. The exact counts and capacities are

 length(q^(k))+1=(r+1)^k(s+1),
 capacity(q^(k))=A^k C.                                    (18)

Accepted04219554e41d9750 Section11 makes a primitive word dominant with
weights (w,sum w+1). Its full objective certificate and the accepted Huber
witness therefore give, at every depth and in the original W=2R units,

 W_(length(q^(k)))(q^(k))=1/(2A^k C-1).                     (19)

The upper bound is over every finite dimension and every admissible
smooth convex objective. It has no multiplicative loss depending on k.
Rescaling f by L restores arbitrary L>0 and leaves W unchanged.

The exponent of this family depends on A and r, NOT on the arbitrary
leaf capacity C. Thus an exponent lower bound applied to (18)--(19)
can restrict A as a TEMPLATE capacity in a recursively closed class.
It cannot restrict C as an arbitrary fixed SEED capacity. To put C in
the exponent one would have to prove the missing theorem allowing that
seed itself to serve as the template, with all the requisite hypotheses.

Moreover the accepted basic recurrence already gives
A<=C_r<=(r+1)^rho, rho=log_2(1+sqrt(2)), for basic h. No external exponent
bound is needed for that existing basic-template restriction. In
particular, (18)--(19) place no new ceiling on an arbitrary length5/6
primitive seed or on the full M=0 subclass.

## 7. Source-dependent exponent check, after the sufficient basic closure

This section assumes an EXTERNAL premise; it is not a Danus fact import.
Source: Yuhan Ye and Kaizhao Liu, *Silver Rate Is (Almost) Optimal for
Gradient Descent*, arXiv:2609.09152v2 (2026), Theorem1.1 and definition(1),
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09152v2 . Its applicable statement is:
there are absolute K_YL>0 and n_YL such that every integer n>=n_YL
and every predetermined h in[0,infinity)^n satisfy

 W_n(h)>=n^(-rho-K_YL sqrt(log log n/log n)).                 (20)

Its R_n divides the objective gap by (L/2)||x_0-x_star||^2,
so R_n there is EXACTLY this project's W=2R. The supremum includes
all finite dimensions and all minimizers. At L=1 its GD convention
coincides with ours. Zero and arbitrarily large nonnegative steps are
allowed; the theorem is uniform over horizon-dependent words. This is
the non-anytime theorem, not the infinitely-many-horizons anytime result.
The primary definition, statement, Section3.2 proof and its uniform
constant passage were read, including the finite-dimensional construction
and its distance normalization in Section3.1. Exact HTML/PDF/text copies
are in downloads/ye_liu_2609.09152v2.*. No formal acceptance is asserted.

Here is the deduction, applied only when actual recursive feasibility
and (19) have been proved. Put m=r+1>1 and n_k=m^k(s+1)-1. The lower
bound (20) and (19) would imply

 log(2CA^k-1)/log(n_k)
 <=rho+K_YL sqrt(log log(n_k)/log(n_k)).                      (21)

For fixed finite s,C and A>1, the left side tends to log(A)/log(m),
while the additional term on the right tends to zero. Therefore such
a recursively certified template necessarily obeys

 A<=m^rho.                                                  (22)

This implication assumes (20). It uses every depth of the ACTUAL
finite-horizon family, not an anytime embedding or a formal scalar
capacity recursion. Fixed prefactors, including C and s+1, disappear
only after division by log(n_k); no depth-dependent loss is discarded.
For the basic templates proved here, (22) is already contained in the
accepted scalar bound, so it is not a new restriction. Applying (22)
to an arbitrary length5/6 seed, or to all M=0 inputs such as (17), would
assume the very template-closure theorem this pilot has not established.

The primary background source on composition was also downloaded and

## 8. A second nonnegative-flow formula fails even with M=0 inputs

The first candidate (7) is not the only tempting generalization of the
binary join. The following chronological fan formula has exact physical
flow and nonnegative entries, and agrees with the accepted lift for the
one-step saturated template. For a general input array Lambda^h put

 b_i^back=sum_(j<i)Lambda^h_ij,    mu_i=1+b_i^back.

Scale the full child array in block i by mu_i. For each i<j add
C Lambda^h_ij on the endpoint pair (p_i,p_j). For each i>j add
Lambda^h_ij times each of the two fans

 sum_(a<s)q_a Q^H_((i,a),p_j)+Q^H_(p_i,p_j),
 sum_(a<s)q_a Q^H_((i,a),p_i).                              (23)

All coefficients are nonnegative. The nonterminal child flow is
-2mu_i q_a+b_i^back q_a+b_i^back q_a=-2q_a. The total flow of
block i is C omega_i^h, so the endpoint flow is precisely that of (7).
This verifies every required flow equation and sign, without a PSD claim.

Take the M=0 template (17) and the M=0 child q=(sqrt(2)) from (13).
Their physical word is

 H=(a,(3+a)/2,a,(3+a)/2,a),  a=sqrt(2).

Set all scalar formal endpoint gradients e_0,e_1,e_2 to zero, and let
the child starting gradients be u_0,u_1,u_2. Set every formal value to
zero. Thus dinf_i=a u_i and V_i=a(1-a)u_i^2. Because bridge gradients
vanish, z_1=y_0 and z_2=y_1=y_0-a u_1.

For the backward edge1->0, the sum of the two fans in (23) equals V_1:
the residual from the block1 start to y_0 is u_1^2/2, and that to its
own end is (1/2-a)u_1^2, both multiplied by a. The same calculation
gives V_2 for edge2->1. For edge2->0, the earlier endpoint is
y_0=z_2+a u_1, so the fan sum is instead

 V_2+a^2 u_1u_2=V_2+dinf_1dinf_2.

All forward endpoint residuals vanish on this formal assignment. The
child M matrices and the template M matrix are zero. Identity(6) gives
V_H=sum V_i. Therefore the complete remainder of (23), after the
child scalings cancel, is exactly

 Lambda^h_20 dinf_1dinf_2=dinf_1dinf_2.

Choosing (u_0,u_1,u_2)=(0,1/a,-1/a) makes this remainder -1. Hence the
second formula fails full PSD even for the explicitly positive M=0
template (17) and saturated M=0 child (13). This is another array-local
failure, NOT a valid static negative-energy witness for the physical H.
It does not establish nonexistence of a different full array.

## 9. Remaining question

The arithmetic, full energy decomposition, positive-flow candidate,
proper basic-template closure, and virtual-oracle counterexample above
are complete joint analytical drafts. They do not prove that every
full primitive h is a valid template, and they do not supply an actual
negative-energy witness for a lifted word with valid inputs. The exact
unpaid term is (8); (10) is one fully specified sufficient collective
payment hypothesis, and (12) retains the entire unrestricted array
problem. A failure of (7), or of pairwise virtual interpolation, cannot
be promoted to failure of every array for the physical word.

No new fact has been submitted or accepted in this pilot. Sections3 and8
are two distinct failed full-array constructions; Section4 is a genuine
static counterexample to their tempting oracle premise, but not to the
unrestricted inflation claim. The latter remains unresolved, including
for arbitrary full M=0 templates.
