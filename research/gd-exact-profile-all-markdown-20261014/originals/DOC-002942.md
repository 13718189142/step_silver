# Variable-matrix terminal-credit repair along saturated right tails

Status: complete self-contained candidate, NOT verifier-accepted. No numerical
solver and no fact_submit call. This is a certificate theorem in a specified
unbounded-length class, not the unrestricted primitive-cone theorem and not an
objective-profile bound. The only accepted background used is the actual
interpolation inequality from fact 1252b8823bb5a290. All additional algebra and
the short-leaf repair are proved here rather than imported from findings.

## 1. The exact certificate class and result

For a positive list h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), write s=sum h_i, C=1+s,
x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, and

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                                   -sC||g_n||^2,
 J_h=sum_(i<n)h_i Q_(i,n),   E_theta(h)=V_h-theta J_h.

All n+1 formal gradients are independent. A signed terminal certificate is
an array lambda_ij on distinct finite pairs such that:

 (a) V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij cancels EVERY formal f_i coefficient;
 (b) the resulting FULL quadratic form M_h is positive semidefinite;
 (c) lambda_ij>=0 whenever j!=n, while lambda_(i,n) may be any real number.

An ordinary certificate additionally has lambda_(i,n)>=0. A nonnegative
certificate for E_theta is exactly a signed certificate with
lambda_(i,n)>=-theta h_i: to pass from the signed to the actual E_theta
array, add theta h_i on i->n. No interpolation inequality is replaced.

Define a class R of positive schedules recursively. Every list of length at
most two belongs to R. A longer list belongs to R if it has a decomposition

 h=(a,1+r,b),   A=1+sum a,   D=1+sum b,
 T=1+sum h=A+D+r,   r>0,   rT=AD,                 (1)

where a is ANY positive list (possibly empty) and b belongs to R. No
primitivity assumption is imposed on a. Fix one such finite decomposition
chain when a schedule has several choices. The word "right" only indicates
that the recursion continues inside b. The earlier lists a can be arbitrarily
long and need not themselves have a decomposition.

Candidate theorem. Every signed terminal certificate for any h in R can be
converted explicitly into an ordinary certificate for that SAME h. The
construction is allowed to change the full PSD remainder. More precisely,
at each cut (1), a supplied certificate forces an ordinary certificate for a
and a signed certificate for b. If the input has the E_theta lower bounds,
0<=theta<1, then the inherited tail bounds are exactly those of E_theta_b,
where

 kappa=(T/D)^2=(A/r)^2,
 theta_b=1-(1-theta)/kappa.                        (2)

The construction uses every actual multiplier, value flow, and full matrix.
It remains valid for nonnegative schedules whose positive compression is in
R, with a final certificate on all original independent gradients.

The assumptions include existence of the stated finite certificate. No dual
attainment theorem identifying arbitrary universal validity with this
certificate class is asserted.

## 2. A saturated cut has two explicit equality trajectories

Fix (1). Let L be the bridge query, so a occupies steps 0,...,L-1.
Call queries 0,...,L the prefix group P. The tail group B comprises the query
immediately after the bridge, all subsequent tail queries, and final query z.
Use local tail indices j=0,...,m, with z=m, and always embed them into the
original list when they occur in Q. Put p=g_L, q=g_z,
H_b=sum_(j<m)b_j g_j, and eta=r/D=A/T.

First use a scalar unit-threshold Huber function, starting sufficiently far
out on its positive affine ray that every query has gradient 1. It is convex,
differentiable, and 1-smooth. Every finite Q is zero and

 V_h=2sum_i h_i sum_(t=i)^(n-1)h_t
          +sum_i h_i(1-h_i)-s(s+1)=0.

Here 2sum_i h_i sum_(t=i)^(n-1)h_t=s^2+sum_i h_i^2.

The second scalar function is obtained by integrating

 f'(x)=min(1,max(x,-eta)),   f(0)=0,

and starts at x_0=A. Its derivative is continuous, nondecreasing, and
1-Lipschitz, so it is a legal convex 1-smooth function with minimizer 0.
If R_i^a is the remaining prefix step sum at query i in P, its prefix data are

 x_i=1+R_i^a,   g_i=1,   f_i=R_i^a+1/2.

At L, R_L^a=0. The bridge lands at -r. At a tail query with remaining tail
sum R_j^b, the data are

 x_j=-eta(1+R_j^b),   g_j=-eta,
 f_j=eta^2(R_j^b+1/2).

In particular x_z=g_z=-eta. All same-group Q are zero. Expanding the displayed
data gives every cross residual:

 Q_(i,j)=-(1+eta)eta R_j^b,   i in P, j in B,
 Q_(j,i)=-(1+eta)R_i^a,       i in P, j in B.        (3)

For example the first expression is
R_i^a+1/2-eta^2(R_j^b+1/2)-eta(1+R_j^b)
 -(1+R_i^a)+(1+eta)^2/2. The second expression follows by swapping the
roles in the definition of Q and equals -(1+eta)R_i^a.

Every incoming-terminal residual is zero: j=z has R_z^b=0 and same-group
tail residuals are zero. At every query one also has
f_i=<g_i,x_i>-||g_i||^2/2. Telescoping squared positions therefore gives

 2sum_(i<n)h_i f_i+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
     =||x_0||^2-||x_z||^2,

and consequently V_h=A^2-T^2 eta^2=0 by eta=A/T.

## 3. Complementarity forces the full mixed block, even with signed credit

Let lambda be any supplied signed certificate from Section 1. On either
equality trajectory, V_h=0 and every term having a potentially negative
coefficient has Q_(i,z)=0. All other coefficients are nonnegative and their
actual Q are nonpositive, by (3) or fact 1252b8823bb5a290. Hence

 0<=M_h=V_h+sum lambda Q<=0.

Every term with a strictly negative Q must therefore have coefficient zero.
Because the steps inside each list are positive, (3) leaves only these cross
edges:

 nu_i=lambda_(i,z), i in P;     mu_j=lambda_(j,L), j in B.     (4)

The nu_i are initially allowed either sign; all mu_j are nonnegative.
The two equality gradient vectors are e_P+e_B and e_P-eta e_B. A PSD matrix
annihilates a vector on which its quadratic form is zero (diagonalize the
real symmetric matrix, whose eigenvalues are nonnegative). Since eta>0,
both block indicators e_P and e_B lie in the kernel of the FULL M_h.

The only mixed prefix-tail terms remaining after (4) are

 -sum_(i in P)nu_i <g_i,H_b+q>
       +r sum_(j in B)mu_j <p,g_j>.                (5)

To check (5), the prefix-to-terminal position term has tail contribution
-<g_i,H_b>, and its squared difference contributes -<g_i,q>.
For j->L, the position contribution involving p is (1+r)<g_j,p>, while the
squared difference contributes -<g_j,p>, leaving r<g_j,p>.
All within-group Q and V_h have no mixed term.

For i<L, row i of M_h e_B is -D nu_i/2. Thus
nu_i=0 for every i<L. Rows j in B of M_h e_P then give

 mu_j=(nu_L/r)b_j for j<m,    mu_z=nu_L/r.          (6)

Value cancellation in V_h+sum lambda Q means that outgoing minus incoming
flow at every nonterminal index i is -2h_i. Summing through P yields
sum_j mu_j-nu_L=2(A+r). Using (6) gives

 nu_L=2r(A+r)/(D-r)=r(kappa-1),
 mu_j=(kappa-1)b_j (j<m),     mu_z=kappa-1.         (7)

Here D>r, kappa>1, and
(kappa-1)(D-r)=2(A+r), obtained by substituting rT=AD and T=A+D+r.
All mixed coefficients in (5) now vanish individually. Thus the full
prefix-tail matrix is zero, not just its value on the equality trajectories.

This proof permits arbitrary negative incoming-terminal coefficients. Their
signs never enter the complementarity step because the corresponding Q all
vanish on both equality runs.

## 4. Full energy identity and the exact inherited credit

Let

 J_b=sum_(j<m)b_j Q_(j,z),
 C_cross=sum_(j<m)b_j Q_(j,L)+Q_(z,L)+r Q_(L,z).

The identity needed for restriction and rebuilding is

 V_h=V_a+kappa V_b-(kappa-1)(C_cross+J_b).          (8)

Here V_a has terminal query L, and V_b uses the original tail queries.
For a full coefficient check put C_aux=C_cross+J_b. Its value and gradient
parts respectively are

 C_aux,f=2sum_(j<m)b_j f_j+(r-D)f_L+(2-D-r)f_z,
 C_aux,g=sum_(j<m)b_j(1-b_j)||g_j||^2
          +[(D-r)/2-r^2]||p||^2+(D+r)||q||^2/2.    (9)

The identity
2sum_j b_j<g_j,sum_(i<j)b_i g_i>
 =||H_b||^2-sum_j b_j^2||g_j||^2
gives (9) by expanding the four kinds of Q in C_aux; the mixed products
in p,H_b,q cancel. Inserting (9) into (8) matches the prefix coefficients
unchanged. It matches the bridge value and squared-gradient coefficients
2(1+r) and -r(1+r), and terminal coefficients -2(T-1) and -T(T-1).
These matches use

 (kappa-1)(D-r)=2(A+r),  kappa r^2=A^2,  kappa D^2=T^2.

Let lambda^a, lambda^b be the supplied within-group arrays. Define

 lambda_hat^b_ij=lambda^b_ij/kappa
                   -(1-1/kappa)b_i 1_(i<m,j=z).    (10)

Equations (7)-(8) give the exact formal identity

 M_h=M_a+kappa M_b,
 M_a=V_a+sum lambda^a_ij Q_ij,
 M_b=V_b+sum lambda_hat^b_ij Q_ij.                 (11)

The prefix and tail values are disjoint, so cancellation of all original
values implies separate cancellation in (11). Their formal gradients are
disjoint, and the mixed block is zero, so PSD of the full M_h implies PSD of
BOTH full blocks. The prefix coefficients are nonnegative because their
targets differ from z. The tail coefficients are nonnegative except possibly
those into z. Thus (11) is an ordinary certificate for a and a signed one
for b, as claimed.

If lambda_(i,z)>=-theta h_i, then (10) gives

 lambda_hat^b_(i,z)>=-[1-(1-theta)/kappa]b_i.

Every other tail sign remains nonnegative. This proves (2), with
0<=theta_b<1 when 0<=theta<1. No lower bound on the signed terminal entries
is needed for the unrestricted signed-certificate statement.

For reference, in the actual nonnegative E_theta multiplier array Lambda,
the forced cross entries are exactly

 Lambda_(i,z)=theta a_i                         (i<L),
 Lambda_(L,z)=r(kappa-1)+theta(1+r),
 Lambda_(j,L)=(kappa-1)b_j                      (j<m),
 Lambda_(z,L)=kappa-1,

with all other cross entries zero. This lists the ACTUAL interpolation
coefficients rather than a formal enlargement of their allowed signs.

## 5. Repair of the terminal leaf, with all value-flow equations retained

An empty schedule has V=0 and needs no multiplier. For one positive step u,
let c=lambda_01. Value cancellation gives lambda_10=c+2u. The constant
gradient equality puts (1,1) in the PSD kernel. Its second row equals
u(c-2)/2, so c=2. The full form is

 (2-u^2)||g_0-g_1||^2.

Thus PSD forces u<=sqrt(2), and both coefficients are already nonnegative.

For two positive steps (u,v), put s=u+v and
c=lambda_02, d=lambda_20, a=lambda_01, b=lambda_12.
Here c,b can be signed, whereas a,d,lambda_10,lambda_21 are nonnegative.
Value cancellation and the constant-gradient PSD kernel give respectively

 lambda_10=a+c-d+2u,       lambda_21=b+c-d+2s,
 u a+s c=2u,              v(b+c)+u d=2s(u+1).

For example these kernel equations follow by expanding the first and last
rows in the following six residuals, where F_ij=f_i-f_j:

 Q_01=F_01+(1/2-u)||g_0||^2+||g_1||^2/2-<g_0,g_1>,
 Q_10=F_10+(||g_0||^2+||g_1||^2)/2+(u-1)<g_0,g_1>,
 Q_12=F_12+(1/2-v)||g_1||^2+||g_2||^2/2-<g_1,g_2>,
 Q_21=F_21+(||g_1||^2+||g_2||^2)/2+(v-1)<g_1,g_2>,
 Q_02=F_02+(1/2-u)||g_0||^2+||g_2||^2/2
                                     -v<g_0,g_1>-<g_0,g_2>,
 Q_20=F_20+(||g_0||^2+||g_2||^2)/2
                                   +(u-1)<g_0,g_2>+v<g_1,g_2>.

Solving the four scalar equations gives

 a=2-(s/u)c,
 b=2s(u+1)/v-c-(u/v)d,
 lambda_10=2(u+1)-(v/u)c-d,
 b=2(u+1)+(u/v)lambda_10>0.                       (12)

Write U=g_0-g_1, Z=g_1-g_2. Since the constant gradient is in the kernel,
the entire full form is obtained by setting g_2=0 and expanding the six
displayed residuals. The result is

 [2-u^2+v(u-1)c/u]||U||^2
  +[c-(u-1)d]<U,Z>+[C_0-(u/v)d]||Z||^2,
 C_0=s[2(u+1)/v-s].                               (13)

If c>=0, all supplied coefficients are nonnegative, by (12), so retain them
and their full PSD matrix. If c<0, PSD in (13) forces C_0>=0 because d>=0.
It also forces u<=sqrt(2): this holds directly when u<1, and when u>=1 the
c contribution to the first diagonal is nonpositive, so 2-u^2>=0.
Replace the supplied array by

 lambda'_01=2, lambda'_10=2+2u,
 lambda'_12=2s(u+1)/v, lambda'_21=2s(u+1)/v+2s,
 lambda'_02=lambda'_20=0.                         (14)

Every coefficient is nonnegative, all value-flow equations remain valid,
and the full remainder, by (13) with c=d=0, is

 (2-u^2)||U||^2+C_0||Z||^2>=0.                    (15)

This deliberately changes the whole leaf matrix; no fixed-matrix repair is
claimed.

## 6. Explicit bottom-up reconstruction on arbitrarily long schedules

Repeatedly apply Sections 2-4 to the fixed decomposition chain. This extracts
ordinary certificates (lambda^a,M_a) for every earlier prefix block, and one
signed certificate for the final leaf. Apply Section 5 to that leaf.

At a cut, suppose sigma^b is the newly obtained ordinary tail array and
N_b its full PSD matrix. Define the rebuilt parent array by:

 within P: use lambda^a;
 within B: use kappa sigma^b_ij+(kappa-1)b_i 1_(i<m,j=z);
 cross: use (kappa-1)b_j on j->L for j<m,
        kappa-1 on z->L, and r(kappa-1) on L->z;
 every other cross entry: zero.                   (16)

All actual coefficients in (16) are nonnegative. Equation (8) gives, with
every value term retained,

 V_h+sum lambda_new,ij Q_ij=M_a+kappa N_b>=0.       (17)

The right side is a block-diagonal full PSD matrix on all original gradient
coordinates at this level. Repeat (16) until the root is rebuilt. The
recursion is finite because each tail is strictly shorter. This proves the
candidate theorem without a solver, a removed interpolation edge, or an
assumed tail coefficient floor.

Along multiple cuts the retained credit also has the exact telescoping law

 1-theta_leaf=(1-theta_root)(C_leaf/C_root)^2,       (18)

since each factor 1/kappa is the squared capacity ratio. This states credit
transport only; validity and repair come from the full coefficient proof.

## 7. Original-index zero steps

For a nonnegative schedule, partition query indices into classes at identical
positions caused by consecutive zero steps. Restrict the supplied full
certificate to the subspace where each such class has equal f and g. This is
a linear congruence of its PSD matrix. Between members of one class Q=0.
For different classes, aggregate all coefficients between the two classes.
Every nonterminal-target coefficient remains nonnegative. Only the terminal
class can have signed incoming coefficients. For an E_theta certificate its
aggregated lower bound is -theta times the sum of h_i in the source class,
which is the corresponding positive compressed step.

The restricted energy is exactly the positive-compressed V. Apply the theorem
to this compressed signed certificate. To lift the ordinary output, choose
the original positive-step query as representative of each nonterminal
class, and original n as terminal representative. Put each compressed
multiplier on the corresponding original pair, and all others zero. The
selected positions agree as formal identities because omitted steps have
zero coefficient. Thus the output energy and interpolation sum give the
compressed PSD quadratic on the selected independent gradients; every unused
original gradient has a zero row and column. No equality between distinct
formal gradients is imposed in the final certificate. The empty compression
has V=0 and the all-zero certificate.

## 8. An explicit unbounded family where this move changes the matrix

For any integer k>=0 take a to be k unit steps and b=(1,1). Put A=k+1,
D=3, and choose the positive root r of r(A+3+r)=3A. Let T=A+3+r and
kappa=(T/3)^2. This gives an (k+3)-positive-step schedule in R.
The prefix has the explicit ordinary adjacent certificate

 lambda^a_(i,i+1)=2(i+1)^2,
 lambda^a_(i+1,i)=2(i+1)(i+2), 0<=i<k,
 M_a=sum_(i<k)(i+1)^2||g_i-g_(i+1)||^2.

Substitution of the unit-step residuals verifies cancellation and this
identity; at query i the net flow is -2, and at query k it is 2k.

Set c=-1+1/(2kappa), so -1<c<0. On the two-unit-step tail use

 lambda^b_01=2-2c, lambda^b_10=4-c,
 lambda^b_12=8-c,  lambda^b_21=12,
 lambda^b_02=c,   lambda^b_20=0.

Its full remainder is ||U||^2+c<U,Z>+4||Z||^2, equal to
||U+(c/2)Z||^2+(4-c^2/4)||Z||^2, so it is PSD. Build a signed parent by
the formulas (16), using this signed tail instead of an ordinary tail.
The parent's only negative coefficient is

 lambda_(tail 0,z)=kappa c+(kappa-1)=-1/2.

All other nonterminal-target coefficients are nonnegative, and all other
terminal coefficients are nonnegative. It is therefore an E_(1/2)
certificate: adding h_i/2 to every incoming-terminal coefficient leaves
every actual multiplier nonnegative (the displayed negative entry becomes
exactly zero). Its full matrix is

 M_a+kappa[||U||^2+c<U,Z>+4||Z||^2].

The repair (14)-(16) yields the ordinary full matrix

 M_a+kappa[||U||^2+4||Z||^2].

The difference is -kappa c<U,Z>, an indefinite quadratic when c!=0.
Thus this is a genuine change of the PSD matrix, not the addition of an
automatically PSD term or a claim that all old coefficients already met the
ordinary floor. No claim that every possible fixed-matrix repair fails on
this example is made. No capacity gain or asymptotic improvement is claimed.

A rational calibration is k=1, giving h=(1,2,1,1), r=1, T=6, kappa=4,
c=-7/8. The ACTUAL nonnegative E_(1/2) coefficients are

 Lambda_01=2, Lambda_10=4,
 Lambda_21=Lambda_31=Lambda_41=3, Lambda_14=4,
 Lambda_23=15, Lambda_32=39/2,
 Lambda_34=39, Lambda_43=48, Lambda_04=1/2,

with every unlisted entry zero. Its full remainder is

 ||g_0-g_1||^2+4||g_2-g_3||^2
 -(7/2)<g_2-g_3,g_3-g_4>+16||g_3-g_4||^2.

The ordinary repaired coefficients are

 lambda'_01=2, lambda'_10=4,
 lambda'_21=lambda'_31=lambda'_41=lambda'_14=3,
 lambda'_23=8, lambda'_32=16,
 lambda'_34=35, lambda'_43=48, lambda'_24=3,

with every unlisted entry zero. Its full remainder is
||g_0-g_1||^2+4||g_2-g_3||^2+16||g_3-g_4||^2.
Expanding the ten possible scalar Gram monomials and five independent
function values verifies both identities exactly. A trivial rational
coefficient check reproduced these expansions; it is not the proof, an SDP
solve, or a verification-service verdict.

## 9. Exact scope and the unresolved interface

The local rigidity requires rT=AD. Without this equality, the second Huber
trajectory has energy A^2-T^2r^2/D^2 instead of zero, and it supplies neither
the two kernel vectors nor (7). The theorem therefore cannot be used to
repair a schedule with no such saturated decomposition.

Even for a saturated decomposition ending in a longer irreducible tail, the
restriction (10) only yields another signed certificate, with precisely the
increased credit (2). It is not an ordinary certificate until that remaining
tail is separately repaired. In particular, this proof does not settle a
general four-step leaf, general terminal-saturation of primal witnesses, or
the unrestricted exact B_N objective comparison. Its useful output is a
constructive, full-matrix repair on the specified class, and a narrower
remaining location for any strict cone separation.
