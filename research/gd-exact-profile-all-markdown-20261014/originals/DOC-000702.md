# Complete-certificate obstruction to the unchanged remote-edge extension

Status: UNVERIFIED exact counterexample. This refutes one specified extension
of the suffix transport, not the existence of some descent and not Huber
tightness at global minimizers. It directly addresses TASK.md's unrestricted
two-step-suffix test.

## 1. A complete tight certificate

Take n=3, L=1, h=(2,9/4,3), q=25/4, and zeta=sqrt(78)/5.
Use Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2, with
x_star=g_star=f_star=0. All unspecified multipliers below are zero:

mu_0=lambda_(0,star)=25/2+5sqrt(78)/8,
mu_1=lambda_(1,star)=5sqrt(78)/8,
nu_0=lambda_(star,0)=5sqrt(78)/4+1,
nu_1=lambda_(star,1)=9/2,
nu_2=lambda_(star,2)=5,

lambda_10=25/2-5sqrt(78)/8,
lambda_21=8, lambda_32=2,
lambda_02=5, lambda_20=4.                            (1)

All are nonnegative; lambda_10>0 because sqrt(78)<9.
The finite flow equations can be checked explicitly:
at0, out=in=35/2+5sqrt(78)/8;
at1, out=in=25/2;
at2, out=in=12;
at3, out-in=2.

For independent formal vectors (x_0,g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3), define

R0=x_0-g_0,
U=g_0+g_1,
V=g_2+(5/4)g_1,
Z=g_3+2g_2.

The FULL matrix has the exact sum-of-squares representation

M=q||x_0||^2-2f_3+sum lambda_ij Q_ij
 =(25/4)||R0-(sqrt(78)/20)U||^2
   +(1/32)||U-16V||^2+||Z||^2.                     (2)

Here GD substitution and the displayed flows cancel every f coefficient.
Thus this is a full PSD certificate, with no omitted prefix or remote entry.

A short derivation of (2) also checks every coefficient. At the first step2,
the multiplier set
mu_0=2+zeta/2, mu_1=zeta/2, nu_0=zeta,
lambda_10=2-zeta/2
gives the identity
||x_0||^2-2f_1+sum lambda Q
 =||R0||^2-(zeta/2)<R0,U>+||U||^2.
Multiply it by25/4. For a step of length1+r, r>=1, direct expansion gives

2r^2 f_i-2f_(i+1)+2Q_(i+1,i)+2(r^2-1)Q_(star,i)
 =||r g_i+g_(i+1)||^2.

Add four times this identity with i=1,r=5/4 and once with i=2,r=2.
This gives a base certificate with the same q and the same multipliers
as (1), except lambda_02=lambda_20=0, nu_0=5sqrt(78)/4,
and nu_2=6. Its slack is

M_base=(25/4)[||R0||^2-(zeta/2)<R0,U>+||U||^2]
        +4||V||^2+||Z||^2.                         (3)

Finally add the exact zero-flow multiplier change
5Q_02+4Q_20+Q_(star,0)-Q_(star,2).
Its quadratic form is
-5||U||^2-<U,V>+4||V||^2.
Combining with (3), and using
(25/4)(1-zeta^2/16)=161/32, gives (2).

The certificate is TIGHT on the globally legal one-dimensional function
f(x)=x^2/2, with x_0=1. Its GD data are

(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3)=(1,-1,5/4,-5/2),

Every Q_ij, including star pairs, is zero on these data, (2) vanishes,
and 2f_3=q. Thus W_3(h)=25/4. This is a complete legal matching

The selected star multiplier is nu_(n-2)=nu_1=9/2>0.
There is a genuine remote prefix-to-suffix edge lambda_02=5.

## 2. The precise extension being refuted

Apply the previously valid c_2=0 two-step transport to k=1 while
leaving the remote edge unchanged. In this certificate,

c_1=mu_2+lambda_20+lambda_21=12,
c_2=mu_3+lambda_30+lambda_31=0,
c_3=lambda_32=2.

For any 0<eta<=9/2, the proposed extension changes

h'_1=(27+eta)/(12+eta),    h'_0=2, h'_2=3,
lambda'_21=8+eta,
nu'_1=9/2-eta, nu'_2=5+eta,                          (4)

and leaves every other multiplier and q unchanged. All steps and
multipliers remain nonnegative, and every flow equation remains exact.
Thus the only issue is the full PSD condition.

Direct expansion gives

M_new-M
 =eta||g_2||^2
   +[25 eta/(4(12+eta))]<g_0,g_1>.                  (5)

The second term is the previously absent remote cut contribution
(h_1-h'_1)lambda_02<g_0,g_1>. It cannot be discarded.

## 4. Scope and a different successful action on this example

This is NOT a stationary tuple and NOT a globally optimal schedule.
It does not refute some other simultaneous descent. Indeed one exists
with a different certificate re-selection.

First continuously undo the last zero-flow adjustment in Section1,
moving from (1) to its base certificate (3) at the SAME h,q.
Every intermediate actual multiplier is nonnegative, and every
intermediate slack is a convex combination of the PSD matrices (2)
and (3). The same legal quadratic remains matching throughout.
The base slack (3) has no extra kernel beyond that quadratic direction,
because zeta^2/16=39/200<1.

Now use the schedule h'=(2,28/13,3), corresponding to eta=1 in (4).
An explicit full certificate with value q'=900/169<25/4 is obtained
by keeping the first-step multiplier pattern
(mu_0,mu_1,nu_0,lambda_10)
=(2+zeta/2,zeta/2,zeta,2-zeta/2),
multiplying it by q'=4(15/13)^2, and then adding the two
function-growth identities from Section1:
four times the i=1 identity with r=15/13, and once the i=2
identity with r=2.

Its slack is

q'[||R0||^2-(zeta/2)<R0,U>+||U||^2]
 +4||(15/13)g_1+g_2||^2+||2g_2+g_3||^2 >=0,

all multiplier signs hold since15/13>1 and zeta<4, and its
function-value flow is exactly2e_3. The quadratic again attains q'.
This is a real fixed-length improvement for this example.

The obstruction therefore distinguishes two mechanisms:
the same sparse transport can fail at an unnecessarily degenerate
optimal dual certificate, whereas reselecting the dual certificate
inside the fixed-h optimum face can make a different descent available.
No theorem is claimed that such re-selection removes remote edges
for every schedule, nor that the maximal-rank dual face always has
the needed kernel structure.

## 5. A continuous repair that RETAINS the remote edges

The same obstruction can be repaired by a different, explicit simultaneous
reweighting, without first setting the remote multipliers to zero.
This supplies a concrete alternative mechanism on this example only.

For 1<=r<=5/4, take h(r)=(2,1+r,3), q(r)=4r^2, retain
zeta=sqrt(78)/5, and set z(r)=64r^2/25. Choose

mu_0=4r^2(2+zeta/2),  mu_1=2r^2 zeta,
nu_0=4r^2 zeta+z(r)(r-1),
nu_1=8(r^2-1),        nu_2=6-z(r)(r-1),

lambda_10=4r^2(2-zeta/2),  lambda_21=8, lambda_32=2,
lambda_02=r z(r),          lambda_20=z(r).            (8)

All unlisted multipliers are zero. They are nonnegative throughout the
interval: z<=4, r-1<=1/4, so nu_2>=5; and zeta<4.
Direct substitution gives out-in=0 at0,1,2 and2 at3. At r=5/4
these are exactly (1), and both remote edges stay positive for
every r in the interval.

Use R0=x_0-g_0, U=g_0+g_1, V_r=g_2+r g_1, Z=g_3+2g_2.
The full slack is

M(r)=4r^2||R0-(zeta/4)U||^2+||Z||^2
     +A(r)||U||^2-(r-1)z(r)<U,V_r>+(4+z(r))||V_r||^2,

A(r)=r^2(161-128r)/50.                              (9)

To derive (9), scale the first-step identity by4r^2, use the two
function-growth identities with factors r and2, and add the
finite/star flow change corresponding to
C_02=r z/2, C_20=z/2.
Its quadratic contribution is
-z[r||U||^2+(r-1)<U,V_r>-||V_r||^2].
Completing the same first square yields (9).

The remaining two-by-two matrix in (U,V_r) has positive A(r), and
its determinant is exactly

r^2(5-4r)(512r^3+1664r^2+16r+3220)/1250 >=0.          (10)

Thus the FULL M(r) is PSD. It has a positive determinant in this
remaining block when r<5/4, so the extraneous kernel responsible
for (7) is removed. The legal quadratic f(x)=x^2/2 follows
(1,-1,r,-2r), satisfies every active interpolation equality, and
attains q(r)=4r^2. Consequently (8)--(10) give the exact identity

W_3(2,1+r,3)=4r^2  for 1<=r<=5/4.

In particular moving r downward from5/4 is a continuous strict
same-length descent starting from the original degenerate certificate,
with nonnegative flow and both remote finite edges retained.
The failed sparse update and this successful update start from the
SAME full certificate. Their difference is the simultaneous scaling
of the prefix-source coefficients and the remote weights (quadratic
and cubic in r), which changes the full kernel correctly.

This does not assert that a comparable homogeneous reweighting exists
for an arbitrary near-optimal schedule. It identifies a genuinely
different concrete mechanism from keeping prefix multipliers fixed.
