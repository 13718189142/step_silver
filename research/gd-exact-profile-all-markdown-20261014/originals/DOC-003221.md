# max66: a complete-prefix objective multiplier law and its exact scoped failure

Status: COMPLETE JOINT ANALYTICAL DRAFT, UNVERIFIED. No formal submission,
no new fact, and no unrestricted objective upper bound are asserted.
The original exact-profile minimax problem remains OPEN.

Outcome of this draft: on the unchanged tau=1/4 words, the symmetric
kernel fails the full PSD test for every m>=3. Its sole backward-tilt
repair, including every nonnegative parameter choice in the displayed
family, fails at the Huber level for every m>=4. In fact any PSD level
within this same law would have to exceed 1/100, so this law cannot even
provide a vanishing objective bound on that sequence. These are scoped,
unverified statements about a proposed certificate family, not W itself.

One mechanism is tested: a product kernel on every directed queried-prefix
pair, completed by explicit minimizer and terminal multipliers. The base
kernel is symmetric. The ONLY repair increases every backward multiplier
by a second product kernel. Both the concrete positive rule and the whole
nonnegative two-parameter family are addressed. This is a whole-word
objective calculation, not a primitive-capacity assumption or a modular
boundary inequality.

## 3. Complete flow cancellation and the exact Huber-null equations

 mu66_i+sum_j K66_ij-sum_j K66_ji-ell66_i=0.             (9)

The finite-prefix drift of the proposed kernel is exactly

 sum_(j<n)K66_ij*(p66_i-p66_j)
  =u66*h_i*(S66*p66_i-T66)+v66*h_i*d66_i.                (11)

Equations (3) and (11) give

 (2S66-p66_i)*mu66_i
      =2h_i+sum_(j<n)K66_ij*(p66_i-p66_j), i<n.         (12)

r66_i by p66_i and sum to obtain

 sum_i p66_i*r66_i=sum_(i,j<n)K66_ij*(p66_i-p66_j).

 sum_i(2S66-p66_i)*mu66_i=2S66+sum_i p66_i*r66_i.

## 4. Full original matrix, including all cross-block entries

Let C66 be the symmetric (n+1)-gradient coefficient matrix of (1). The
complete full matrix is

              M66(q_cert)=[[q_cert,-mu66^T/2],
                           [-mu66/2,C66]],             (14)

where mu66=(mu66_0,...,mu66_n). This is a matrix on the original n+2
coordinates; it is not a matrix restricted to realizable trajectories.

For clarity we derive its entries from ALL original rows. A term
lambda_ij*||g_i-g_j||^2/2 contributes half the total incident mass to
the diagonal and minus lambda_ij to the corresponding cross coefficient.
Only a forward edge out of i contributes -h_i*lambda_ij to ||g_i||^2
through <g_i,x_j-x_i>. By (9)--(10) the half incident mass is
out_i-1_(i=n). Thus, with h_n=0,

 C66_ii=out_i-1_(i=n)-h_i*sum_(j>i)lambda_ij.            (15)

For 0<=i<j<=n, the difference-square terms contribute
-(lambda_ij+lambda_ji). A term based at i with destination after j
contributes -h_j*lambda_ik. A term based at j with destination at or
before i, or at star, contributes h_i times its multiplier. Therefore

 2C66_ij=-(lambda_ij+lambda_ji)
          -h_j*sum_(k>j)lambda_ik
          +h_i*(mu66_j+sum_(k<=i)lambda_jk).             (16)

All sums in (15)--(16) except the explicitly separated star term are
over finite sample indices. Substituting (3)--(4) gives every entry:

 C66_ii=mu66_i+h_i*[u66*(S66-h_i)+v66*p66_i]
                 -u66*h_i^2*(S66-p66_i-h_i), i<n;
 C66_nn=1;                                            (17)

 2C66_ij=h_i*mu66_j+h_i*h_j*[-(2u66+v66)
                 -u66*(S66-p66_j-h_j)
                 +(u66+v66)*(p66_i+h_i)], i<j<n;       (18)

 2C66_(i,n)=-ell66_i
             +h_i*(mu66_n+sum_(t<=i)ell66_t), i<n.     (19)

These expressions include every left/right cross-block coefficient and
every terminal gradient coordinate. No product of child remainders or
independent-gradient assumption has replaced them. The candidate is formed
on the whole parent word; no child slack was assumed zero or discarded.

Here is also a coordinate-free expansion checking the same whole matrix.
Write z_i=-sum_(t<i)h_t*g_t, so x_i=x_0+z_i and z_star=0. The value-free
polynomial is exactly

 q_cert||x_0||^2-sum_(i<=n)mu66_i*<g_i,x_0>
 -sum_(i<=n)mu66_i*<g_i,z_i>+sum_(i<=n)mu66_i*||g_i||^2/2
 +sum_(i<n)ell66_i*[<g_n,z_i-z_n>+||g_n-g_i||^2/2]

In particular, both the entries and their linking to the full matrix are
exact polynomial identities; no numerical matrix is substituted.

Let e66 be the all-ones vector of length n+1. Multiplying the gradient
part of (20) by e66 gives the row identity

 2C66*e66=2h+mu66+drift66,
 drift66_i=p66_i*mu66_i
                +sum_(j finite)lambda_ij*(p66_i-p66_j). (21)

For a direct verification, the squared differences contribute mu66/2
to C66*e66. The bilinear position terms contribute half their row drift
and half the vector sum of their displacements. The latter sum is
-2z_n=2sum h_t*g_t, because full finite flow is 2 at n and all star
outgoing multipliers vanish. This gives (21) on every coordinate.
Equations (12)--(13) now imply

              C66*e66=D66*mu66/2.                     (22)

Consequently M66(1/D66)*(1,e66/D66)=0 exactly. More generally, the
Schur completion in the initial-point coordinate is

 M_cert=q_cert*||x_0-sum_i mu66_i*g_i/(2q_cert)||^2

when q_cert>0. At q_cert=1/D66 this Schur remainder annihilates e66,

The accepted terminal-square parameterization is respected as well:

## 5. Exact geometry of the unchanged hierarchy

For m>=3, let

 k66=2^(m-1)-1, r66= h_(k66)=1+a*c^(m-1),
 A66=sum H_(m-1), Jleft66=sum_(i<k66)h_i^2,
 E66=A66*r66-Jleft66, Kroot66=3A66+2r66.                (24)

r66 is the physical root step, not r66_i of (3). The whole word is
palindromic, with left/right sums A66, so

 S66=2A66+r66, p66_(k66)=A66,
 T66=2A66^2+2A66*r66-Jleft66,
 d66_(k66)=(A66^2+Jleft66)/2,
 S66*A66-T66=-E66,
 T66-E66=A66*S66.                                     (25)

The identity for d66_(k66) follows by writing
sum_(i<k66)h_i*p66_i=(A66^2-Jleft66)/2 within the actual left block.
Every child step is strictly smaller than r66, because c>1 and it has a
strictly smaller level. Thus 0<Jleft66<A66*r66 and E66>0.

We will need A66>=r66. This is proved inside each enclosing m. Let
s_j=sum H_j and t_j=1+a*c^j. Then

 s_(j+1)=2s_j+t_j, t_(j+1)=c*t_j-(c-1).

For m>=3, 2<c<3 and 0<beta_word<1. Since

 s_2=3+2a+beta_word, t_2=1+beta_word*c,
 s_2-t_2=2+beta_word*(1+2/c-c)>0,                       (26)

the base s_2>t_2 holds. To see the sign without a decimal, c<3 gives
1+2/c-c>-2; multiplication by beta_word in (0,1) keeps the product
strictly above -2, whether that parenthesis is positive or negative.
If s_j>=t_j, then s_(j+1)>=3t_j>c*t_j-(c-1)=t_(j+1).
Induction through j=m-1 gives A66=s_(m-1)>t_(m-1)=r66.

Here c<3 follows from c<=13lambda_s/12 and lambda_s<5/2; the latter is
sqrt(2)<3/2. Also beta_word>=11/12 and lambda_s>12/5, so r66>3 for
m>=3. For m>=4 the simpler estimate

 r66=1+beta_word*c^(m-2)
       >=1+(3/4)*lambda_s^(m-2)>4                     (27)

holds. These are symbolic inductions/inequalities, not executed recurrences.
The first step h_0=1+a is a smallest positive step and is at most A66.

## 6. First-query nonnegativity constrains the entire repaired family

We now allow ANY u66,v66>=0 in (3), not just the explicit choice (5).
Suppose all of (4) are nonnegative. Only one necessary sign, ell66_0>=0,
already imposes a strong condition. Put b=h_0>0 for this section. Since
p66_0=d66_0=0,

 ell66_0=b*[(2-u66*T66)/(2S66)-v66*(S66-b)]>=0.

Therefore

 u66*T66+2S66*v66*(S66-b)<=2,
 u66<=2/T66, v66<=1/[S66(S66-b)].                      (28)

If (28) is violated, an original terminal multiplier is negative, which
already disqualifies that parameter choice. The remaining argument grants
every other sign and still excludes PSD in the stated range.

At the root, (3), (17), and (25) give exact scalar coefficients

 mu66_(k66)=r66*[2-u66*E66+v66*d66_(k66)]/Kroot66,
 C66_(k66,k66)=mu66_(k66)
                   +r66*A66*[u66*(2-r66)+v66].        (29)

Let mu_r denote mu66_(k66) only in the rest of this proof. Because E66>0,
d66_(k66)>0, and (28) holds, (25) gives

 mu_r>=2r66*(1-E66/T66)/Kroot66
      =2r66*A66*S66/(Kroot66*T66)
      >=r66*S66/[Kroot66*(A66+r66)]>0.                (30)

The last inequality uses T66<=2A66*(A66+r66), since Jleft66>=0.

For r66>2, the u66 term in the root diagonal of (29) is nonpositive.
Using (28), (30), and b<=A66 gives

 [v66*r66*A66]/mu_r
 <= A66*Kroot66*(A66+r66)/[S66^2*(S66-b)]
 <= A66*Kroot66/S66^2
 < 3/4.                                               (31)

The last strict sign is exactly

Thus the COMPLETE original root gradient diagonal satisfies

                 C66_(k66,k66)<(7/4)*mu_r.             (32)

On the other hand, A66>=r66 implies

 Kroot66*(A66+r66)<=(10/9)*S66^2,                       (33)

because 10S66^2-9Kroot66*(A66+r66)
=(A66-r66)*(13A66+8r66)>=0. From D66>2S66, (30), and (33),

                     D66*mu_r>9r66/5.                (34)

No step in (28)--(34) assumes PSD or a certificate on an isolated child.
These are necessary constraints for the explicitly defined WHOLE-WORD

## 8. Zero physical steps and the small degenerate cases

The target words themselves have every h_i>1. No physical bridge vanishes
at any claimed m. Nevertheless the coefficient definitions have a complete
zero-step interpretation, which is useful for detecting hidden coordinate
identifications.

If S66>0 and T66>0, formulas (2)--(5) apply even with zero steps. Every
zero-step index i<n has h_i=mu66_i=ell66_i=0 and no incident K66 edge.
Equations (17)--(19) make its entire gradient row and column zero.
For nonzero indices, the same formulas depend only on their actual
chronological prefix masses. Thus, after selecting the original indices
of positive steps and the ORIGINAL terminal n, the full matrix is exactly
T_pick^T*Mhat66*T_pick, where T_pick is the coordinate selection map and
Mhat66 uses the compressed positive word. This is a full-space congruence
with zero omitted columns. No equality between formal duplicate gradients
is imposed. This is also the explicit lifting convention in accepted1252.

If S66=0, including n=0, set only lambda_(n,star)=2 and q_cert=1.
Then M_cert=||x_0-g_n||^2 on the entire original space. Every unused
gradient coordinate is a zero column.

If S66>0 but T66=0, exactly one physical step is positive, say h_i=b.
Use u66=v66=0 in (3). The only nonzero multipliers are
lambda_(i,star)=lambda_(n,star)=lambda_(n,i)=1. With D66=2b+1,
the full completion is

 M_cert=(1/D66)||x_0-(D66/2)(g_i+g_n)||^2
                     +(3-2b)||g_i-g_n||^2/4.          (40)

All other original coordinates are free zero columns. Formula (40) is
PSD at the Huber level exactly when 0<b<=3/2; for b>3/2 its difference
direction is negative. This finite calibration is not the claimed
unbounded bridge. For m=1 the target b=1+3/(5lambda_s)<3/2, so the
one-step calibration succeeds. The formula (5) is never divided by zero
in these degenerate cases.
