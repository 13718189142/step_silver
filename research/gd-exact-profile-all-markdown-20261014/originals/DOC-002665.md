## 3. Actual growing dimension and nonseparability
The visited block-start points are the m+1 vectors e_0,...,e_m. The vectors w_0,...,w_m are linearly independent: their coordinate matrix is triangular with nonzero diagonal. Their Gram matrix is explicitly real PSD because they are the displayed Euclidean vectors. Its only off-diagonal entries at distinct stages are
<w_i,w_(i+1)>=-rho_i rho_(i+1) for i<m-1,
<w_(m-1),w_m>=-rho_(m-1)/6.
Each w_i is queried twice, so the temporal Gram matrix also has positive repeated-gradient correlations. Energies are2rho_i^2 on the relay stages and1/36 at the terminal point. They vary with the chosen b_i. In particular the accepted orthogonal-moment obstruction0e7e78056dc6258c does not apply.

The function cannot be written as a sum of univariate functions in any fixed orthonormal coordinates. Indeed linear independence of the w_i lets us choose, for any i, a point z with <w_i,z>=eta and <w_j,z><eta for every j!=i. In a neighborhood of z, J is exactly max{0,<w_i,z>-eta}. At x=z+theta w_i with0<theta<1 and sufficiently small surrounding perturbations, its envelope has Hessian
P_i=w_i w_i^T/||w_i||^2.
For two neighboring, nonparallel w_i,w_(i+1), their inner product is nonzero, so the rank-one projectors P_i and P_(i+1) do not commute. All Hessians of a function separable in a fixed orthonormal basis would be diagonal in that basis and would commute wherever they exist. This contradiction proves genuine nonseparability, not merely a rotated scalar witness or an orthogonal direct sum.

## 4. Why this schedule test is not just a large quadratic product
For any scalar quadratic f_lambda(x)=lambda x^2/2 with0<=lambda<=1, one block's multiplier is

For0<=lambda<=1/6 and5<=b<=11/2, it is nonnegative and at most1-lambda<=exp(-lambda).
For1/6<=lambda<=1, its largest positive value is at most5/36, and its negative minimum has magnitude
(b-1)^2/(4b)<=81/88.
The latter expression increases with b>1, and its endpoint value at b=11/2 is81/88. These statements also follow by checking the vertex and endpoints of the quadratic p_b on the given interval.

Consequently the worst normalized objective over every scalar quadratic satisfies
max_(0<=lambda<=1) lambda product_i p_(b_i)(lambda)^2

The first term comes from maximizing lambda exp(-2m lambda). A general finite-dimensional convex quadratic diagonalizes orthogonally, and its normalized error is a convex combination of these scalar errors; zero-curvature directions contribute no objective. This does not assert that scalar nonlinear witnesses fail on these schedules. The point is to give a concrete globally consistent variable-energy high-dimensional trajectory surviving mixed short/long steps even when every quadratic channel converges.
