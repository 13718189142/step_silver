# R5: joint histories, retained heights, and a static simplex replacement

2026-10-08. UNVERIFIED analytical draft. Original target OPEN.

This was one new analytical invocation with two substantive lines. No mathematical program, numerical array, solver, additional model, or formal submission was used. The constructions below are actual functions with complete trajectories. No proof that their portfolio attains the required uniform lower bound was obtained, and no full-class negative resolution was obtained.

## 1. Exact target and the baseline pool

Set \(L=1\). For the prescribed nonnegative word \(h\), write \(S=\sum h_i\) and

\[
W_n(h)=\sup_{f,x_0,x_\star}
\frac{2(f(x_n)-f_\star)}{\|x_0-x_\star\|^2},
\qquad x_{i+1}=x_i-h_i\nabla f(x_i).
\]

The supremum has the full finite-dimensional smooth-convex scope in PROBLEM.md. Define \(C_0=B_0=1\) and, over every integer split \(k+l=n-1\),

\[
C_n=\max\Omega(C_k,C_l),\qquad B_n=\max G(C_k,B_l),
\]

\[
\Omega(x,y)=\frac{x+y+\sqrt{x^2+6xy+y^2}}2,\qquad
G(x,y)=2x+\frac y2+\frac{\sqrt{y^2+8xy}}2.
\]

The missing conclusion is

\[
\forall\epsilon>0\ \exists N_\epsilon\
\forall n\ge N_\epsilon\ \forall h\in[0,\infty)^n:
\quad W_n(h)\ge(1-\epsilon)/B_n,
\]

with a threshold independent of the word.

Two actual baseline witnesses are retained throughout this attempt:

\[
W_n(h)\ge \frac1{2S+1},
\qquad
W_n(h)\ge \lambda\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(1-h_i\lambda)^2
\quad(0\le\lambda\le1).
\tag{1}
\]

The first is the Huber function with derivative \(\operatorname{clip}(x,-1,1)\), started at \(2S+1\). Every query remains on its positive affine ray. The second is \(f(x)=\lambda x^2/2\), started at 1. Both use the original last iterate and radius. Thus a word needing further work must fail both selected lower bounds at the proposed target level. The R4 ceiling on a particular pasted quadratic-Silver family was not used to exclude this larger pool.

The source motivating the joint boundary quantities is Zhang--Jiang, [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.12395v2), Sections 2.2, 3.1 and 3.2. Its sufficient composition theorems were not reversed or treated as a classification of arbitrary words.

## 2. Line 1: exact support bookkeeping and the retained child heights

### 2.1 Static realization convention

For a compact finite gradient hull \(K\) containing 0, use

\[
F_K(x)=\max_{g\in K}\{\langle x,g\rangle-\tfrac12\|g\|^2\}.
\tag{2}
\]

Its gradient is \(P_Kx\), so it is convex and 1-smooth, with \(F_K(0)=0=\min F_K\). A proposed gradient \(g_i\in K\) at \(x_i\) is realized precisely when

\[
\langle x_i-g_i,g-g_i\rangle\le0\quad(g\in K).
\tag{3}
\]

Checking the generating vertices suffices. Values are then fixed as
\(\langle x_i,g_i\rangle-\|g_i\|^2/2\). Every use of (2) below selects one body before the run; no function is changed at a checkpoint.

### 2.2 Independent check of SUPPORT_CLOCK_RECURRENCE_20261008_R5

Take an actual suffix projection history \((v_j,b_j)\) for its prescribed word of mass \(\tau\). Let

\[
M=\max_j\langle v_0,b_j\rangle>0,\quad
R_B^2=\|v_0\|^2,\quad
a_B=R_B^2/M,\quad z_B=2F_B(v_{\rm end})/M.
\]

For the preceding prefix mass \(\sigma\), set \(C=1+\sigma\), and write the bridge as \(1+d>1\). Choose

\[
\theta>0,\quad r\ge0,\quad
\theta+(\tau+1)r\le d,\qquad
p^2=\frac{M}{\theta(1+r)},\quad T=(C+\theta)p.
\]

Use \(X_0=(v_0,T)\), prefix gradients \((0,p)\), and suffix gradients \((b_j,-rp)\). During the prefix its horizontal coordinate is \(v_0\); during the suffix its clock coordinate is \((\theta-d+t_jr)p\).

The prefix-to-suffix normal rows are bounded above by
\(M-\theta(1+r)p^2=0\). The reverse rows are

\[
-\langle v_j-b_j,b_j\rangle
-[d-\theta-(t_j+1)r](1+r)p^2\le0.
\]

The within-child and minimizer rows also hold. Thus the convex hull of 0 and these displayed gradients realizes the complete trajectory.

The initial support of the full body is exactly

\[
M_{\rm new}=Tp=JM,\qquad
J=\frac{C+\theta}{\theta(1+r)}.
\]

Indeed every suffix support is at most \(M-rTp<Tp\). Its actual state is therefore

\[
a_{\rm new}=C+\theta+\frac{a_B}{J},
\]

\[
z_{\rm new}=\frac{z_B}{J}
+\frac{2r(d-\theta)-(2\tau+1)r^2}{C+\theta}.
\tag{4}
\]

The selected-origin payoff is \(z_{\rm new}/a_{\rm new}\). The recurrence is correct, but each input is a property of one and the same realized child. It is not legitimate to optimize \(a_B,z_B,M\) separately and feed the resulting values into (4).

### 2.3 A pasting paid by actual heights, without an auxiliary clock

The full R4 normal rows contain a useful quantity that (4) does not retain. For the same actual child define

\[
H=\min_j\langle v_j-b_j,b_j\rangle\ge0,\qquad \eta=H/M.
\tag{5}
\]

For a nontrivial child, \(0\le\eta<1\): its initial row has
\(\langle v_0-b_0,b_0\rangle\le M-\|b_0\|^2<M\).

Choose \(v>0\) and \(a>0\) satisfying

\[
a^2=M/v,\qquad H\ge(1-d/v)M.
\tag{6}
\]

Use one new scalar coordinate for a constant prefix gradient, with original start

\[
X_0=((C+v)a,v_0).
\]

For prefix partial mass \(s_i\), prescribe

\[
X_i=((C+v-s_i)a,v_0),\qquad g_i=(a,0).
\]

The bridge lands at \(((v-d)a,v_0)\). For the entire suffix prescribe

\[
Y_j=((v-d)a,v_j),\qquad g_j=(0,b_j).
\]

Take the single body

\[
K=\operatorname{conv}(\{0,(a,0)\}\cup\{(0,b_j)\}).
\tag{7}
\]

Every prefix-to-suffix row is at most

\[
\langle v_0,b_j\rangle-(\sigma-s_i+v)a^2
\le M-va^2=0.
\]

Every reverse row is

\[
(v-d)a^2-\langle v_j-b_j,b_j\rangle\le0
\]

by (6). The child rows and all rows against 0 hold. Consequently (2),(7) realize exactly the displayed GD trajectory, including all old values and gradients of the suffix. The prefix values are explicitly \((\sigma-s_i+v+1/2)a^2\). Its terminal value is precisely the child terminal value.

The original radius is

\[
\|X_0\|^2=R_B^2+\frac{(C+v)^2}{v}M.
\tag{8}
\]

This is a valid selected-minimizer lower witness; no claim that 0 is always the closest minimizer is needed for this lower bound.

The particularly useful choice \(v=C\) is legal when

\[
\eta\ge1-d/C.
\tag{9}
\]

Its added squared radius is exactly \(4CM\), giving

\[
W(h_{\rm prefix},1+d,h_{\rm suffix})
\ge\frac{z_B}{a_B+4C}.
\tag{10}
\]

The state after this actual pasting is also explicit. Its initial support is
\(M_{\rm new}=JM\), \(J=1+C/v\). Its minimum height is \(H\), since the minimum prefix height is \(M\). Thus

\[
a_{\rm new}=C+v+a_B/J,\quad
z_{\rm new}=z_B/J,\quad
\eta_{\rm new}=\eta/J.
\tag{11}
\]

Unlike the clock specialization with \(r=0\), this operation permits \(v>d\) when the child's genuine positive height pays the reverse rows. But (11) also shows why the height condition cannot silently be inherited unchanged.

### 2.4 The Zhang--Jiang bridge is locally tight for this actual construction

This is a check of the construction, not a global converse. Put \(T_0=2\tau+1\), let \(d>0\) solve

\[
2d^2+T_0d=CT_0,\qquad
B=2C+2d+T_0=G(C,T_0).
\]

Then \(A=T_0+2d=\sqrt{BT_0}\). Take an actual constant-gradient Huber suffix with gradient \(b\), start \(v_0=Ab\), and its prescribed nonnegative suffix steps. Its whole trajectory stays on the ray, because \(A\ge\tau+1\). Direct calculation gives

\[
a_B=A,\quad z_B=2-T_0/A,\quad
\eta=1-(\tau+1)/A.
\]

These jointly attained values satisfy

\[
Bz_B-a_B=4C,\qquad
d/C=T_0/A\ge(\tau+1)/A=1-\eta.
\]

Thus (9) holds and (10) attains \(1/B\) exactly. No favorable support or height was postulated independently of the child.

This tight local match does not settle the hard words. For an arbitrary suffix, the Huber state depends on its actual mass, while the desired comparison uses the length-based exact \(B_l\). If a different adverse child is selected to improve its objective, (9) still requires its own minimum height. Equation (11) can decrease that height ratio. I did not prove a jointly attainable family that resolves these constraints at every required split of every surviving word.

## 3. Line 2: a static simplex with negative cross inner products

The second line attacked a concrete cost in the first: independent positive coordinate gradients retain positive old supports that every future history must dominate. The following construction instead uses one centered simplex. It preserves all history with explicit negative cross inner products and pays a centered initial variance, not a sum of independent coordinate radii.

### 3.1 Construction for any chosen phase partition

Partition the whole prescribed word into \(m\ge2\) phases. For \(i<m\), phase \(i\) consists of any prefix subword of mass \(\sigma_i\), followed by a chosen checkpoint \(b_i>1\). The last phase has mass \(\tau\). Define

\[
C_i=1+\sigma_i,\quad d_i=b_i-1\quad(i<m),
\qquad C_m=1+\tau.
\]

The total mass is

\[
S=\sum_{i=1}^m C_i+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}d_i-1.
\]

Choose positive numbers \(A_i\) such that

\[
\sum_{i=1}^m A_i^{-1}=1,\qquad
P_i:=\sum_{j=i}^m A_jC_j,\qquad
A_i d_i\ge P_{i+1}\quad(i<m).
\tag{12}
\]

Such numbers exist for every selected partition: first set a positive temporary \(A_m\), choose earlier \(A_i\) backwards sufficiently large to meet the displayed inequalities, and finally multiply all \(A_i\) by their temporary reciprocal sum. The inequalities are homogeneous and normalization then holds. This is an existence construction, not a numerical evaluation.

In \(\mathbb R^m\), put

\[
u=(A_1^{-1/2},\ldots,A_m^{-1/2}),\qquad
w_i=\sqrt{A_i}e_i-u.
\]

Then \(\|u\|=1\), all \(w_i\) lie in \(V=u^\perp\), and

\[
\langle w_i,w_j\rangle=-1\ (i\ne j),\qquad
\|w_i\|^2=A_i-1.
\tag{13}
\]

Moreover \(\sum_i A_i^{-1}w_i=0\), with strictly positive coefficients. These vectors span \(V\), and 0 is in the relative interior of their simplex.

Take the actual function (2) on the finite-dimensional space \(V\), with

\[
K=\operatorname{conv}\{w_1,\ldots,w_m\}.
\]

Its minimizer is uniquely 0 in \(V\). Define

\[
\bar P=\sum_i P_i/A_i,\qquad
X_0=\sum_i \frac{P_i}{\sqrt{A_i}}e_i-\bar P\,u.
\tag{14}
\]

This point lies in \(V\), and

\[
\langle X_0,w_i\rangle=P_i-\bar P,\qquad
R^2:=\|X_0\|^2=\sum_i P_i^2/A_i-\bar P^2>0.
\tag{15}
\]

The strict positivity follows because the \(P_i\) strictly decrease and (15) is their variance under the positive weights \(1/A_i\).

### 3.2 Every phase and cross-history row

Prescribe gradient \(w_i\) throughout phase \(i\), including its checkpoint query when \(i<m\). Let \(q_j\) be the accumulated step mass already spent with gradient \(w_j\), and let \(s=\sum_jq_j\) be the global accumulated mass. The physical GD recursion gives

\[
\langle X,w_j\rangle=P_j-\bar P+s-A_jq_j.
\]

Write \(z_j=P_j-A_jq_j\). For a query intended to use \(w_i\), its projection row against any other vertex is exactly

\[
\langle X-w_i,w_j-w_i\rangle=z_j-z_i+A_i.
\tag{16}
\]

For a future phase \(j>i\), \(z_j=P_j\le P_{i+1}\). During the prefix portion of phase \(i\),

\[
z_i-A_i\ge P_i-A_iC_i=P_{i+1}.
\]

For every finished earlier phase \(j<i\), the checkpoint has left

\[
z_j=P_j-A_j(C_j+d_j)=P_{j+1}-A_jd_j\le0.
\]

These inequalities prove (16) is nonpositive throughout the entire run. In the last phase the analogous terminal bound is \(z_m-A_m\ge0\). Since 0 is already a convex combination of the vertices, its row follows as well. Thus the prescribed gradients, values, switches and chronology all come from the one static function. No unvisited coordinate is assumed to remain inactive without this check.

### 3.3 Exact last-iterate payoff and original radius

At the final point, the gradient is \(w_m\) and its support is \(A_m-\bar P+S\). Consequently

\[
2F_K(X_n)=2S+1+A_m-2\bar P.
\]

This gives the actual full-word witness

\[
W_n(h)\ge
\frac{2S+1+A_m-2\bar P}
{\sum_i P_i^2/A_i-\bar P^2}.
\tag{17}
\]

The numerator is positive: (12) implies
\(\bar P\le S+1\), hence it is at least \(A_m-1>0\). The minimum is unique, so the radius in (15) has no hidden minimum-cone correction.

This construction applies to any chosen checkpoints exceeding 1, uses every step of the original word, allows zero steps within phases, and allows arbitrarily large finite steps. The dimension is \(m-1\). Parameters and the partition can depend on the entire word before the single function is selected.

### 3.4 What this replacement did and did not resolve

The negative cross products in (13) are realized by explicit vectors. The reduction of radius in (15) is a true orthogonal centering, with a unique valid minimizer. This removes the need to postulate an independent cheap support state or to add a separate positive clock coordinate in this family.

The decisive closure inequality is still unproved. I did not establish that, for every word for which both baselines in (1) are below \((1-\epsilon)/B_n\), there is a partition and parameters satisfying (12) for which (17) reaches that target with a word-independent loss. Backward existence of parameters in (12) alone does not control (15) sharply enough. Nor was a counterfamily of surviving words proved to exclude all such simplex choices together with the baselines. Therefore this family has not been declared insufficient on the basis of an already easy Silver word.

No interpolation or construction lemma is missing inside (12)--(17). The missing statement is the original-scale all-word selection and quantitative comparison with the exact discrete \(C_n/B_n\) recurrences. Presenting its optimization as a new Bellman or feasibility problem would not prove it.

## 4. Final audit and status

Both substantive lines use actual finite-dimensional static functions, original GD chronology and explicit initial radii. Multiplying any constructed function by a prescribed \(L>0\) restores \(L\)-smoothness and leaves the normalized performance unchanged. The target normalization throughout is \(W=2R\).

The support recurrence was independently checked. The retained-height operation has a genuine improvement over discarding those heights and is locally tight at the Zhang--Jiang bridge with an explicitly realized child. It does not establish recursively available joint states on arbitrary words. The simplex line has explicit full-history interpolation and a unique minimizer, but lacks the required all-word payoff estimate.

There is no complete affirmative candidate, no rigorous full-class asymptotic separation, and no uniform \(o(1)\) to report. The original target remains OPEN. This one-call/two-line allocation ends here; no automatic renewal, verification submission, truth-store change or Goal change is made.
