## One exact static-function construction for both early screens

  p.q=-u, ||q||^2=N,
  w=-(B p+C q)/D.                                     (5)

the triangle T=conv{p,q,w}. Define one static function for this whole word

  F(x)=max_(v in T)(<x,v>-||v||^2/2).                  (6)

Completing the square gives its unique maximizing v=Proj_T(x). Projection
normal inequalities imply
||Proj_T(x)-Proj_T(y)||^2<=<Proj_T(x)-Proj_T(y),x-y>, so projection is
1-Lipschitz. The two maximizing inequalities for F(x+z) and F(x) bound
F(x+z)-F(x)-<Proj_T(x),z> between0 and||z||^2. Therefore F is convex,
differentiable and1-smooth, with gradient Proj_T. It has unique minimizer0:
Proj_T(x)=0 implies <x,v><=0 for all v in T, and0 is interior.

Start x_0=Kp. The intended gradients are p,p, then q on every middle
query through the second long step, then w,w. The actual positions are

  x_1=p, x_2=-B p,
  x_j=-B p-(sum of preceding middle steps)q,
  x_(r-1)=D w, x_r=w.
