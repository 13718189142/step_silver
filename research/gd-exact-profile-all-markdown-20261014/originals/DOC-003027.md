# Variable-matrix repair of a terminal-chord certificate class

Status: complete candidate proof, NOT verifier-accepted. No new solver or
fact_submit call. All identities below are proved directly. The theorem has
arbitrary positive length and does not require a saturated composition cut.
It is a support-and-cumulative-sign theorem, not general credit re-selection.

## 1. Definitions and exact statement

Let n>=1 and let h_0,...,h_(n-1)>0. Put

 P_i=sum_(t<i) h_t, p_i=1+P_i, s=P_n, C=1+s,
 R_i=sum_(t>=i) h_t=C-p_i, with R_n=0.

Use the actual GD substitutions x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t and

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                            -s(s+1)||g_n||^2.

Suppose a real signed array lambda cancels every value coefficient in
V_h+sum_(i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ij and its FULL remainder M is PSD on independent
g_0,...,g_n. Suppose its only possibly nonzero entries are

 lambda_(i,i+1)=a_i, lambda_(i+1,i)=b_i       (0<=i<n),
 lambda_(i,n)=c_i                           (0<=i<n-1).

The latter are the nonadjacent terminal chords; the adjacent last incoming
edge is a_(n-1), not another c entry. Set c_(n-1)=0 for summation and

 zeta_k=sum_(i<=k)c_i,     0<=k<n.

Assume the following cumulative condition at each long nonfinal step:

           zeta_k<=0 whenever k<n-1 and h_k>1.             (1)

Then the following array is an ordinary nonnegative certificate for V_h:

 a_i^0=2P_(i+1)p_i/h_i,
 b_i^0=2P_(i+1)p_(i+1)/h_i,                              (2)

on the two adjacent directions, with all other entries zero. Its complete
formal remainder is

          M^0=sum_(i<n) w_i ||g_i-g_(i+1)||^2,            (3)
 w_i=(P_(i+1)/h_i)[p_i-(h_i-1)p_(i+1)]>=0.

In particular, if the input is an actual E_theta certificate represented
by lambda, with lambda_(i,n)>=-theta h_i, 0<=theta<1, and all other entries
nonnegative, it can be repaired to the ordinary certificate (2). The proof
does not need the numerical bound theta once the displayed support, full
PSD, flow and cumulative condition are assumed. The incoming chords c_i
can have both signs; only their indicated cumulative sums are restricted.

No property is asserted for a certificate with extra remote prefix edges,
extra outgoing terminal edges, or positive zeta_k at a step h_k>1. All these
are genuine remaining cases for the general cone question.

## 2. Full affine-kernel identity and weighted circulation

This section holds for any full-support real multiplier array. Let e be
the all-one column, L=diag(lambda e)-lambda the row Laplacian, and let A
have A_ii=1, A_it=h_t for t<i and zero entries for t>i. Put

 omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(n-1),2s)^T,
 D_quad=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(n-1)(2-h_(n-1)),-s(s+2)).

Value cancellation is exactly L^T e=omega, and expansion gives

                M=D_quad+sym(L A),   sym(B)=(B+B^T)/2.         (4)

For completeness, the position terms in sum lambda Q are sym(L(A-I)).
The squared-gradient differences contribute sym(L)-diag(omega)/2.
Adding the diagonal gradient terms of V gives (4).

Evaluating this value-canceling quadratic on a constant-gradient legal
affine segment gives e^T M e=0: all Q vanish, and V=0. Such a segment can
be realized by a scalar Huber function started sufficiently far along an
affine ray. Since M is PSD, M e=0. Equivalently this follows by considering
the quadratic on e+t v for arbitrary v and both signs of t.

As A e=p, equation M e=0 yields

                 Lp=-2D_quad e-A^T omega=diag(p)omega.         (5)

Here, for i<n,

 (A^T omega)_i=-2h_i+2h_i P_(i+1),
 -2h_i(2-h_i)-(A^T omega)_i=-2h_i p_i;

and the terminal component is 2s(s+1). These explicitly verify (5).
Combining (5) with L^T e=omega shows that

                 mu_ij=lambda_ij p_j                    (6)

has equal incoming and outgoing sums at every node. Indeed (5) says
sum_j lambda_ij p_j=p_i sum_j lambda_ji. Thus mu is a real circulation,
though its terminal edges can still be negative.

The separate value-flow condition must be retained: with u_i=1/p_i,

          sum_j mu_ij(u_j-u_i)=omega_i.                  (7)

Circulation alone does not imply (7), positivity, or PSD.

## 3. The input adjacent coefficients are forced by the chords

Sum the exact value flows over vertices 0,...,k. The only crossing edges
are the adjacent pair k,k+1 and the terminal chords from this prefix, so

               a_k-b_k+zeta_k=-2P_(k+1).                 (8)

Sum the circulation balances (6) over the same prefix:

               a_k p_(k+1)-b_k p_k+C zeta_k=0.           (9)

For k=n-1 the formula counts the last adjacent incoming edge only in a_k;
c_(n-1)=0 prevents double counting. Solving (8)-(9) gives

 a_k=a_k^0-(R_k/h_k)zeta_k,
 b_k=b_k^0-(R_(k+1)/h_k)zeta_k.                         (10)

This retains all actual coefficients rather than selecting an assumed
matrix. It follows from the full input PSD through its affine kernel.

## 4. A diagonal test in the full gradient-difference coordinates

For each k<n let z^(k) be the formal gradient column equal to1 on indices
0,...,k and0 thereafter. This is a legitimate direction in the FULL
original gradient space, regardless of whether it is itself a legal run.
For ANY real array satisfying value cancellation and (5), a direct
calculation yields

 (z^(k))^T M z^(k)
     =-P_(k+1)^2
          +sum_(i<=k<j)lambda_ij[1+p_j-p_(k+1)].         (11)

Here are all terms of that calculation. Set every value to zero. In a Q
with both indices in the prefix, the gradient coefficient is p_i-p_j.
For a prefix-to-suffix pair it is p_i-p_(k+1)+1/2. For a suffix-to-prefix
pair it is1/2, and it is0 inside the suffix. Sum (5) over prefix rows to
replace the first group and the p_i-p_j part of the forward crossing group
by -2sum_(i<=k)h_i p_i. The remaining forward coefficient is
p_j-p_(k+1)+1/2. By the value cut flow, the total reverse crossing mass is
the forward mass plus2P_(k+1); this adds another1/2 to each forward weight
and contributes P_(k+1). Finally

 sum_(i<=k)h_i(1-h_i)-2sum_(i<=k)h_i p_i+P_(k+1)
       =-sum_(i<=k)h_i^2-2sum_(i<=k)h_i P_i
       =-P_(k+1)^2.

This proves (11) without suppressing any matrix direction.

For the stated adjacent-plus-terminal support, (11) becomes

 (z^(k))^T M z^(k)
       =-P_(k+1)^2+a_k+(1+R_(k+1))zeta_k
       =w_k+[(h_k-1)R_(k+1)/h_k]zeta_k.               (12)

The second equality follows from (10), R_k=h_k+R_(k+1), and
a_k^0-P_(k+1)^2=w_k. Because M is PSD, the left side is nonnegative.

If h_k>1 and k<n-1, (1) makes the last term in (12) nonpositive, whence
w_k>=0. If k=n-1 it vanishes because R_n=0, with the same conclusion.
If 0<h_k<=1, the formula

 w_k=(P_(k+1)/h_k)[(2-h_k)p_k+h_k(1-h_k)]

is strictly positive directly. Thus every w_k in (3) is nonnegative.

## 5. Complete repaired certificate

The coefficients (2) are nonnegative. Across edge k their net forward
flow is a_k^0-b_k^0=-2P_(k+1), so the difference between adjacent cuts
is -2h_k, with the terminal flow2s. Hence every original value coefficient
cancels. Direct expansion of the adjacent Q terms gives a tridiagonal
gradient matrix. Its affine kernel vanishes, as can be checked from (2)
or (9) with zeta=0. Its off-diagonal entry on edge k is

 [ -a_k^0+(h_k-1)b_k^0 ]/2=-w_k.

Therefore the full matrix is precisely the weighted path Laplacian (3):
the diagonal entries are w_(k-1)+w_k, with endpoint conventions. Since
all w_k>=0, (3) proves PSD on ALL independent original gradients. No equality
between two formal gradients, no rank-one test, and no primal-witness-only
PSD inference is used.

The construction actually changes the matrix. It does not assert that
M^0-M is PSD, and the next family shows that difference can be indefinite.

## 6. A nonsaturated signed input family at every positive length n>=2

Take h_i=t=4/3 for all i<n, let d_i=g_i-g_(i+1), and put

 w_k=(k+1)[2-t^2+k t(2-t)],
 epsilon=(2-t^2)/(4t n^2)=1/(24n^2),
 theta=epsilon/t=1/(32n^2).

Start with (2) and add these four signed changes:

 Delta lambda_01=n epsilon,
 Delta lambda_10=(n-1)epsilon,
 Delta lambda_0n=-epsilon,
 Delta lambda_1n=epsilon.                              (13)

For n=2 the last change is added to the existing adjacent coefficient.
Every actual E_theta multiplier is nonnegative: add theta h_i=epsilon
to every incoming terminal edge. In particular the only negative signed
entry, lambda_0n=-epsilon, becomes0. Value flow is unchanged at every node.
For n>=3 the chord cumulative sums are zeta_0=-epsilon and zeta_k=0 for
k>=1. For n=2 the sole relevant cumulative sum is zeta_0=-epsilon.
Thus (1) holds.

For a complete independent check of input PSD, expand (13):

 Delta M=epsilon[nQ_01+(n-1)Q_10-Q_0n+Q_1n]
   =-epsilon(n-1)(t-1)||d_0||^2
      -epsilon sum_(k=1)^(n-1)[1+t(n-1-k)]<d_0,d_k>.    (14)

This follows by collecting g_0^2, g_1^2 and g_0g_1 and then using
g_j-g_1=-sum_(k=1)^(j-1)d_k. All value coefficients in (14) are zero.
Write beta_k=1+t(n-1-k). Completing squares gives

 M=sum_(k=1)^(n-1) w_k ||d_k-epsilon beta_k d_0/(2w_k)||^2
       +[w_0-epsilon(n-1)(t-1)
                   -epsilon^2/4 sum_(k=1)^(n-1)beta_k^2/w_k]||d_0||^2.
                                                                  (15)

Here w_0=2-t^2=2/9, w_k>=(k+1)w_0>0, beta_k<tn, and
sum_(k=1)^(n-1)beta_k^2/w_k<t^2 n^3/w_0. Consequently the two deductions
from w_0 in the last coefficient of (15) are respectively less than
w_0/4 and w_0/(64n). Their sum is strictly less than w_0, so the last
coefficient is positive. This proves full input PSD (strict positivity
in the difference coordinates) for every n>=2.

The repair (2) removes (13), producing sum w_k||d_k||^2. By (14), M^0-M
has a nonzero d_0 d_1 coefficient but zero d_1^2 coefficient. Restricting
to (d_0,d_1) gives determinant -epsilon^2 beta_1^2/4<0. Thus the matrix
change is indefinite, even though both complete endpoint matrices are PSD.

There is no saturated cut in any nonempty constant-t block. For a cut at
step i, let A_cut=1+it, D_tail=1+(n-i-1)t and r=t-1=1/3. Since A_cut,D_tail>=1 and
A_cut+D_tail=2+(n-1)t, their product is minimized at an endpoint and obeys

 A_cut D_tail>=1+(n-1)t=(4n-1)/3,
 rC=(4n+3)/9,
 A_cut D_tail-rC>=(8n-6)/9>0.

Thus lengths n>=3 in this family are outside the recursive class requiring
an exactly saturated first cut and a <=2-step eventual continuation leaf.
The new support-based repair is not merely that saturated-cut argument.

## 7. Scope and remaining interface

Condition w_k>=0 is equivalent to

 h_k<=[1-p_k+sqrt(p_k^2+6p_k+1)]/2<2.

Thus every schedule admitted by this repair theorem has C<1+2n. This is
an unbounded-length re-selection class, not an improving primitive seed
family or an asymptotic separation from the exact objective profile.

The circulation equations (6)-(7) isolate why deleting negative chords
arbitrarily is invalid: both conservation and reciprocal-p drift must hold.
The argument succeeds here because the support forces (10) and the full
PSD then forces each coefficient of the independent repaired path squares.
Section 8 below allows arbitrary backward edges and also gives an explicit
weighted-cut criterion for full-support arrays. No result here is claimed
as a new fact until verifier acceptance; no verifier allocation was used.

## 8. General full-support cut identity and a stronger repair theorem

Return to a real array with FULL support, exact value cancellation, full
PSD, and nonnegative entries except possibly into the terminal. Define,
for each k<n, the following weighted actual forward and backward cut sums:

 F_k=sum_(i<=k<j)lambda_ij[p_j-p_(k+1)],
 B_k=sum_(j<=k<i)lambda_ij[p_k-p_j].                       (16)

The adjacent edge contributes zero to either sum. Every term in B_k is
nonnegative; F_k can be negative only through signed incoming-terminal
edges. There is no omitted interpolation coefficient in these definitions.

The exact FULL matrix identity on the cut direction is

 (z^(k))^T M z^(k)=w_k+[(h_k-1)F_k-B_k]/h_k.             (17)

To derive it, let O_k and I_k be the total forward and backward crossing
masses without weights. The value-flow cut says O_k-I_k=-2P_(k+1).
The weighted circulation cut says

 sum_(i<=k<j)lambda_ij p_j
                   =sum_(j<=k<i)lambda_ij p_j.

The left side is p_(k+1)O_k+F_k and the right side is p_k I_k-B_k.
Using p_(k+1)-p_k=h_k therefore gives

 O_k=[2p_k P_(k+1)-F_k-B_k]/h_k.

Formula (11) equals -P_(k+1)^2+O_k+F_k, which becomes (17) because
2p_k P_(k+1)/h_k-P_(k+1)^2=w_k. This proves (17) for the complete
multiplier array and full original matrix.

**Full-support repair criterion.** If at every k with h_k>1 the input
array satisfies the explicit inequalities

                       (h_k-1)F_k<=B_k,                 (18)

then the ordinary array (2) and full PSD remainder (3) repair it.
Indeed (17) and full input PSD force w_k>=0 at such k; at h_k<=1 the
direct positive formula in Section 4 does so. Section 5 checks every
actual coefficient, value flow and formal PSD direction of the output.
Condition (18) is a checkable sufficient condition on a supplied full
certificate, not a proposed characterization of all credit certificates.

**Arbitrary backward edges.** In particular, the original repair remains
valid if ANY additional nonnegative backward edges i->j with i>j are
allowed. Assume every forward edge with j<n is adjacent, while arbitrary
signed incoming-terminal chords obey (1). Then

 F_k=R_(k+1) zeta_k, B_k>=0.

At a long nonfinal step (1) gives F_k<=0; at the final step F_(n-1)=0.
Thus (18) holds. Extra terminal-to-prefix edges and long backward prefix
edges are all covered, with no assumption that their coefficients vanish.
The signed constant-4/3 family in Section 6 supplies nonordinary inputs at
arbitrarily large lengths in this class, outside the saturated-cut class.

**Quantitative constraint on genuinely long steps.** Conversely, if
w_k<0, then (17) forces

                    (h_k-1)F_k-B_k>=-h_k w_k>0.          (19)

Such a step necessarily has h_k>1. Hence some positive forward multiplier
crosses the cut and lands strictly beyond k+1; adjacent-only forward mass
cannot supply (19). If the input is an actual E_theta certificate with
nonnegative multipliers Lambda, so that

 lambda_(i,n)=Lambda_(i,n)-theta h_i,

and other lambda entries equal their actual Lambda entries, define
F_k^actual by (16) with Lambda. Then the exact conversion is

             F_k=F_k^actual-theta P_(k+1)R_(k+1),

and (19) becomes

 (h_k-1)F_k^actual-B_k
      >=theta(h_k-1)P_(k+1)R_(k+1)-h_k w_k.              (20)

Thus terminal credit does not eliminate the positive forward-cut weight
obligation for these long steps. It increases its required actual weight
by the displayed amount. Equation (20) is a necessary coefficient bound,
not a bound on what those weights can achieve and not a universal repair.
No conclusion about asymptotic objective minimax follows from it alone.
