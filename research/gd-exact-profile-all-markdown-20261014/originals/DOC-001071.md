## 1. Data and statement of the necessary condition

Let h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)) be any finite nonnegative word, let
s=sum h_i,A=1+s, and select an index j with h_j>1. Define

 P=sum_(i<j)h_i, R=sum_(i>j)h_i, H=P+h_j=s-R,
 eta=(h_j-1)/(1+R)>0, C0=eta(1+eta),
 V0=(1+P)^2-A^2 eta^2.

For kappa in(0,1] let

 p_0=1, p_i=product_(t<i)(1-kappa h_t) for0<=i<=j+1,
 E=1+kappa R, q=kappa p_(j+1)/E,
 t_kappa=(1-kappa)/(2kappa).

If R>0 put

 beta=max{0, max_(0<=i<=j)
       [q(kappa p_i-q)-t_kappa(kappa p_i-q)^2/R]}.

If R=0 put beta=0. Finally define

 V_kappa=
   kappa sum_(i=0)^j h_i[1+kappa(1-h_i)]p_i^2
   -H[s+R+1+1/kappa]q^2.                               (1)

Every full primitive certificate for the ORIGINAL word necessarily
satisfies, for every such j and every0<kappa<=1,

             beta V0+C0 V_kappa >=0.                    (2)

For beta=0 the stronger explicit interpretation is V_kappa>=0,
which is equivalent to(2) because C0>0. No other step is assumed
positive, above1, symmetric, ordered or part of a primitive prefix.

## 5. Exact energy calculation

For the auxiliary suffix, f_i^1-f_n^1=q^2 R_i and
f_n^1=q^2/(2kappa). The elementary finite identity

 2sum_(i>j) h_i R_i = R^2+sum_(i>j)h_i^2

follows by expanding R^2. Thus the combined suffix contribution of
the weighted value differences and gradient diagonals is R(R+1)q^2.
The prefix contribution is

 kappa sum_(i<=j)h_i[1+kappa(1-h_i)]p_i^2-H q^2/kappa.

Subtracting the terminal s(s+1)q^2 gives(1), because
s(s+1)-R(R+1)=H(s+R+1).

The primitive energy is linear in all values and Gram entries.
The repaired planar trajectory therefore has energy

          V0+(C0/beta)V_kappa

when beta>0, and the legal auxiliary trajectory has energy V_kappa
when beta=0. Accepted04219554e41d9750, Section10, says an original
full nonnegative flow/PSD certificate forces nonnegative energy on

## 6. The unit-curvature slice and exact low-degree polynomial interface

At kappa=1, q=p_(j+1)/(1+R)=-eta p_j, t_kappa=0, and

 beta=max{0,max_(i<=j)q(p_i-q)}, V_1=1-A^2 q^2.

For the last identity, the prefix sum in(1) telescopes to
1-p_(j+1)^2, using p_(i+1)^2=(1-h_i)^2 p_i^2. Then
(1+R)^2+H(s+R+2)=A^2. This rederives the unit-curvature
repair without treating the shared max2 draft as a correctness premise.

For R>0 the entire curvature interval can be expressed through
finitely many POLYNOMIAL branches. Define

 d_i(kappa)=[E p_i-p_(j+1)]/kappa,                     0<=i<=j,

The numerator defining d_i vanishes at kappa=0, so d_i is a
