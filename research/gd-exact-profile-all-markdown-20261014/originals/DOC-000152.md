# Source lead: coefficient signs and dual arc diagrams

Primary sources checked September27,2026. This note is awareness/strategy,
not a verifier-accepted theorem for the project.

## General certificate congruence still needs signs

Shu--Wang, [A Unified Theory of H-Duality in First-Order Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03281v1), Sections1.1--1.2,4.3--4.7 and AppendixC.

The paper transforms certificate matrices so that the associated full slack
forms are congruent. The remaining coefficient-sign conditions are explicit;
PSD preservation alone is insufficient. Proposition2 supplies sign preservation
for invertible reduced tridiagonal certificates in the listed D/G performance
pairings. The main text also points to a combinatorial characterization in the
source below. This does not establish reversal of an arbitrary intrinsic V_h
certificate: its performance setup, actual signs and any certificate selection
must still be matched. The main agent separately confirmed the scope audit
already published as4ad2db6604a59a2c.

## A precise combinatorial sign criterion in another setting

Yoon--Grimmer, [A Theory of Composition and Duality of Extremal Optimal Fixed-Point Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02231v2), v2 August6,2026, Sections3--4, especially Theorem4 and its proof in4.2.2.

The setting is minimax-optimal vertex algorithms for nonexpansive fixed-point
residuals. Their interpolation-multiplier supports are encoded by arc diagrams.
Theorem4 says the antitransposed vertex method remains optimal exactly when
its arc diagram is noncrossing; the resulting method is again a vertex. The
proof relates signs of finite differences of an inverse certificate matrix to
weighted graph/forest structure. Crossing examples in this class show why a
generic appeal to time reversal is insufficient. The theorem is not about
arbitrary predetermined GD schedules or the intrinsic primitive energy.

## Concrete applicability test, not an imported conclusion

For max4's proposed general reversal interface, the possible lead is to seek
a sign-controlling structure in the ACTUAL intrinsic certificate array, or an
exact obstruction to a proposed matrix transform. First match the performance
functional, row/column constraints, reduction/invertibility and the distinction
between a given certificate and all certificates for the schedule. Failure of
one transformed certificate does not disprove existence of another certificate.
An optimal-vertex assumption cannot be imposed on the current problem without
proof. Likewise, no noncrossing or decomposition hypothesis may be presumed.

These are DUAL multiplier graphs. C35/C39's primal coordinate-flow graph
obstructions do not automatically exclude this proof architecture. Conversely,
the source's vertex classification does not validate those primal constructions.
This is an optional mechanism lead inside the current task, with no additional
numerical, verification, model or round allocation.
