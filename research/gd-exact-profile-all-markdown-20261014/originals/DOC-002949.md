## 1. Full objective identity and a relaxed proof-weight choice

Normalize L=1 and x_star=g_star=f_star=0. Let

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 <=0,
    C_ij=-2Q_ji
        =2(f_i-f_j)+2<g_j,x_j-x_i>-||g_i-g_j||^2 >=0,

and put C_ii=0. All positions use the exact physical GD recursion.
Every formal vector x_0,g_0,...,g_n is independent.

At a block h of length n put S=sum h_i, M=S+1, P=sum h_i g_i, and fix

    w_i=h_i for i<n, w_n=1, D=sum w_i=M,
    W=P+g_n,
    J_rel=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)
                   +sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2.    (1)

The same quantity can be written

    J_rel=2sum_i w_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_i w_i||g_i||^2
       +2sum_i w_i<g_i,x_0-x_i>-||W||^2.

To check this, expand ||sum h_i g_i||^2 and use
2sum_i h_i<g_i,sum_(t<i)h_t g_t>
=||sum h_i g_i||^2-sum_i h_i^2||g_i||^2.

Suppose finite nonnegative coefficients lambda_ij give the exact identity

    J_rel=sum_(finite i!=j)lambda_ij C_ij+R.          (2)

All function coefficients cancel if and only if

    out_i-in_i=w_i-M 1_(i=n).                       (3)

For EVERY real q the corresponding FULL objective polynomial is

    q||x_0||^2-2f_n
       +2/M sum_i w_i Q_(i,star)
       +2/M sum_(finite i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ji
      =(q-1/M)||x_0||^2+[||x_0-W||^2+R]/M.          (4)

This follows by expanding the optimum interpolation terms. All actual
multipliers are nonnegative. The array fixes Q_(star,i) multipliers to
zero; general relaxed certificates need not have that property.
If R were PSD, (4) at q=1/M would be the requested weaker objective
upper bound. This is genuinely above the Huber value when S>0.

## 2. The actual four-fan recursion, with signed corrections allowed

Use two copies of the same block. Let p be the left endpoint and q_end
the parent endpoint. Insert bridge b=1+d with d>0, and write

    g=g_p, e=g_(q_end), M'=2M+d,
    P_L=x_start-x_p, P_R=x_(p+1)-x_(q_end),
    W_L=P_L+g, W_R=P_R+e,
    V=d g+W_R, W_parent=W_L+V.

Choose level-dependent A,E>=0 and real B,C,p0,q0. Set

    gamma_L=1+C+E, gamma_R=1+A+B,
    p0-q0=(1-A+E)M+d.                              (5)

Require gamma_L,gamma_R>=0. Scale the COMPLETE left and right child
finite arrays by gamma_L and gamma_R. Add the fans

    C w_i on (p,i),       E w_i on (q_end,i),   i in left samples,
    A w_i on (p,i),       B w_i on (q_end,i),   i in right samples,

omitting self edges, and add p0 on (p,q_end) and q0 on (q_end,p).
Require EVERY FINAL finite multiplier nonnegative. In particular B,C,
p0,q0 can be signed when their actual resulting entries are nonnegative.
The new proof weights are the old physical weights in each queried
copy, with the left terminal weight changed from 1 to b, and the
right terminal weight retained as 1. Thus D_parent=M', exactly as in (1).

No earlier interpolation constraints are deleted. The final array is
the explicit sum just given. Its value flow is (3); alternatively the
following expansion checks every value coefficient directly.

The four weighted fan identities, derived by expanding C, are

    sum_L w_i C_(p,i)
       =-J_L-||W_L||^2+2<W_L,P_L+g>-M||g||^2,
    sum_L w_i C_(q_end,i)
       =-J_L-||W_L||^2+2<W_L,P_L+b g+P_R+e>
                                     -M||e||^2-2M(f_p-f_(q_end)),
    sum_R w_i C_(p,i)
       =2M(f_p-f_(q_end))-J_R-||W_R||^2
                                     -2d<g,W_R>-M||g||^2,
    sum_R w_i C_(q_end,i)
       =-J_R-||W_R||^2+2<W_R,P_R+e>-M||e||^2.       (6)

After adding the child identities and these fans, the only remaining
function coefficient is
2[(1-A+E)M+d-p0+q0](f_p-f_(q_end)), which is zero by (5).
Substitute P_L=W_L-g and P_R=W_R-e in the quadratic terms. The exact
FULL gradient remainder is

    R_parent=gamma_L R_L+gamma_R R_R+F_rel,

    F_rel=-(C+E)||W_L||^2+(A-B)||W_R||^2
       -2E<W_L,W_R+d g>
       +2(Ad-q0)<g,W_R>+2p0<W_R,e>
       +(gamma_L M-d^2-2dq0)||g||^2
       +(gamma_R M-2M-d)||e||^2+2p0d<g,e>.          (7)

This retains both physical displacements through the displayed W_L,W_R
and retains every child matrix. It is not a local-PSD assumption.

## 3. Exact undeformed calibration

Let lambda=1+sqrt(2). For the undeformed Silver word, M=lambda^j and
d=lambda^(j-1) at level j. Choose

    A=B=lambda, C=E=0, p0=0, q0=lambda d,
    gamma_L=1, gamma_R=lambda^2.                    (8)

Start with the one-step finite array lambda_(0,1)=lambda,
lambda_(1,0)=1, physical step sqrt(2). Direct expansion gives
R=M(M-1)||g_end||^2 at this seed. Equation (5) holds in (8) because
(1-lambda)M+d=-lambda d. All final coefficients are nonnegative.

Assuming the same remainder formula for both children, (7) proves it
for the parent. The g coefficient from the left child cancels
M-lambda^2 d^2=M-M^2. The e coefficient is
lambda^2 M(M-1)+lambda^2 M-M'=M'^2-M'. All mixed coefficients are
zero. Thus the relaxed identity passes the FULL undeformed benchmark
at every depth, with its true initial distance and all values canceled.

## 4. A formal template for EVERY allowed choice of the new fans

For the deformed word H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+d_j,H_j), let
M_j=1+sum H_j. Define signed numbers

    kappa_1=M_1=2+a,
    kappa_(j+1)=-kappa_j^2/d_j.                     (9)

Every array produced by (5)--(7) has a recursively specified scalar
formal-gradient template with terminal gradient e such that

    W_j=kappa_j e,
    R_j=(kappa_j^2-M_j)e^2.                         (10)

At the one-step seed, take both formal gradients equal to e. The two
finite C residuals are zero after setting f_0-f_1=h_0 e^2. Equation
(1) gives R_1=M_1(M_1-1)e^2=(kappa_1^2-M_1)e^2 for ANY seed array
satisfying (3). PSD of the seed is not needed for this calculation.

For the induction use a right template with terminal e and a left
template with terminal g=-kappa_j e/d_j. Then

    V=d_j g+W_R=0,
    W_parent=W_L+V=kappa_j g=kappa_(j+1)e.

On V=0, the COMPLETE local expression (7) becomes

    F_rel=-(gamma_L-1)||W_L||^2
          +(gamma_L M_j-gamma_R d_j^2)||g||^2
          +(gamma_R M_j-M_(j+1))||e||^2.            (11)

Indeed W_R=-d_j g cancels both p0 mixed terms and the E term. Adding
gamma_L R_L+gamma_R R_R and W_L=kappa_j g cancels gamma_L, gamma_R
and M_j, leaving (kappa_j^4/d_j^2-M_(j+1))e^2. This proves (10).
No child PSD, attained zero, greedy storage selection, or saturation
assumption occurs anywhere in this induction.

Now insert (10) into the FULL initial-distance identity (4). After
multiplication by M_j its restriction to independent scalar x_0,e is

             [ M_j q       -kappa_j     ]
             [ -kappa_j  2kappa_j^2-M_j ].           (12)

If 2kappa_j^2<M_j this matrix is negative at x_0=0,e=1 for EVERY q.
If the lower diagonal is positive, PSD would force

    q>=kappa_j^2/[M_j(2kappa_j^2-M_j)]>=1/kappa_j^2.

The last comparison is exactly (kappa_j^2-M_j)^2>=0. Thus merely
increasing q cannot repair the late negative diagonal.

## 5. Explicit failure at the actual critical horizon

Put m>=128, epsilon=1/m, c=lambda(1+epsilon), a=(1-epsilon)/c and
d_j=a c^j=(1-epsilon)c^(j-1). All parameters are fixed within this
whole word. Solving (9) in absolute value gives

    |kappa_m|=(1-epsilon)c^m Rcrit^(2^(m-1)),
    Rcrit=(2+a)/[(1-epsilon)c].                     (13)

The formula holds at j=1 by definition, and squaring and dividing by
d_j proves the next term. Direct subtraction gives

    1-Rcrit=epsilon[2-lambda^2 epsilon(1+epsilon)]
                    /[lambda^2(1-epsilon)(1+epsilon)^2]
             >=epsilon/4.                         (14)

For the inequality, lambda^2<6, the numerator bracket exceeds 7/4,
and the denominator is less than 6(129/128)^2<25/4. Their ratio is
therefore greater than 7/25>1/4. Also 0<Rcrit<1.
Since 1-t<=exp(-t) and c<3,

    |kappa_m|<3^m exp[-2^(m-3)/m]
               <=(3/exp(2))^m<2^(-m).              (15)

Here 2^(m-4)>=m^2 holds at m=128 and propagates by induction because
2m^2>=(m+1)^2 for m>=3. Also exp(2)>6 follows from the four positive
series terms 1+2+2+4/3. Since M_m>=1, (15) implies
2kappa_m^2<M_m. Equation (12) is therefore a concrete negative FULL
matrix restriction at the actual target horizon, for every real q.
