## 8. Exact nonlinear remainder and strict local capacity loss

For arbitrary finite displacements p=h-hbar, Y=Lambda-Lambdabar, the full
polynomial expansion is EXACT:

    M(h,Lambda)=W(p,Y)+R2(p,Y),
    R2(p,Y)=diag(-p0^2,...,-p6^2,-S^2)+sym(L(Y)Tp).          (31)

Flow is exactly f(p,Y)=0. Every original sign is retained:
Y_ij>=0 on Z0 and Lambdabar_ij+Y_ij>=0 on E58. There are no omitted terms in
(31). In particular the mixed coefficient-step term is retained, and higher
orders arising from a nonlinear choice of h,Lambda are already covered by
this exact displacement formula.

Put ||(p,Y)||_* = sqrt(||p||_2^2+||Y||_F^2). Cauchy--Schwarz gives

    ||L(Y)||_F <=(sqrt(7)+1)||Y||_F,
    ||Tp||_F <=sqrt(7)||p||_2,
    ||diag(-p_i^2,-S^2)||_F <=||p||_2^2+S^2<=8||p||_2^2.

The first bound uses seven off-diagonal entries in each row. The second uses
||Tp||_F^2=sum_(j=0)^6(7-j)p_j^2. Symmetrization does not increase Frobenius
norm, and the Frobenius norm of a product is bounded by the product of norms.
Consequently

    ||R2(p,Y)||_F <=8||p||_2^2+(7+sqrt(7))||p||_2||Y||_F
                   <=13||(p,Y)||_*^2.                     (32)

Define the compact set

    K58={(p,Y) in C58: ||(p,Y)||_*=1}.

It is closed because flow and W are linear and the sign and PSD cones are
closed; it lies on a finite-dimensional unit sphere. It is nonempty, as the
following actual feasible path confirms without a new computation. For
0<=t<q58 let x=q58-t. The singleton word x with multipliers2 and2(1+x) has the
full2-by2 remainder(2-x^2)[[1,-1],[-1,1]], which is PSD. This follows by
substituting L=[[2,-2],[-2(1+x),2(1+x)]] and T=[[1,0],[x,1]] into D+sym(LT).
Join two copies at bridge
1+u(1+x), then two resulting children at bridge2+x. The accepted join has
positive coefficients, fixed right scaling lambda^2 at both levels, and
actual word

    (x,1+u(1+x),x,2+x,x,1+u(1+x),x).

Its base array is Lambdabar; all coefficients are differentiable in t.
Its tangent has p=(-1,-u,-1,-1,-1,-u,-1) and S=-lambda^2, so is nonzero
and belongs to C58. Normalize it to obtain a point of K58.

Equations(7),(26) show -S>0 everywhere on K58. Hence

    m58=min_((p,Y) in K58)(-sum_i p_i)>0, c=m58/2>0.        (33)

The minimum is a mathematical definition, not a solver output or a new
computation. Its positivity follows from compactness: a sequence of unit
cone points with -S->0 would have a unit limit in the equality set(26).

We now prove that all sufficiently small FEASIBLE joint displacements obey

    -S >=c||(p,Y)||_*.                                    (34)

If this failed, choose nonzero feasible displacements (p_k,Y_k) with
t_k=||(p_k,Y_k)||_*->0 and -S_k<c*t_k. Normalize
(p_k,Y_k)=t_k*(p_hat_k,Y_hat_k). A subsequence of the unit normalized pairs
converges to a pair(p_hat,Y_hat) of norm1. Exact flow and all36 active signs
pass to the limit. Homogeneity of(31) gives

    M(hbar+p_k,Lambdabar+Y_k)/t_k
       =W(p_hat_k,Y_hat_k)+t_k*R2(p_hat_k,Y_hat_k)>=0.

By(32), the second term tends to0 in norm. Thus W(p_hat,Y_hat)>=0 on the
FULL R8, so the unit limit lies in K58. The assumed failure of(34) gives
-sum_i p_hat_i<=c=m58/2, contradicting(33). This proves the existence of
a joint radius r58>0 on which(34) holds for every feasible pair.

Finally use the uniform convergence(30) and h->hbar to choose epsilon>0
such that EVERY feasible array above a word with ||h-hbar||_2<epsilon
satisfies ||(h-hbar,Lambda-Lambdabar)||_*<r58. The cube condition can be
ensured by making epsilon smaller, since hbar is in its interior. Equation
(34) is then exactly the asserted capacity bound(3). At the base it holds
with both sides0. Cauchy--Schwarz also gives

    c||Lambda-Lambdabar||_F <=Abar-A(h)
                          =-sum_i(h_i-hbar_i)
                          <=sqrt(7)||h-hbar||_2,

which proves(4).

This normalization uses the JOINT displacement, so it never assumes bounded
