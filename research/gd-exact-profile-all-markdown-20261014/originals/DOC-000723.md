## 2. One global legal function, every pair, and true distance

In R2 define

  F_par(X)=max_(g in K_par)(<X,g>-||g||^2/2).

convex set: complete the square, use coercivity for existence and strict
convexity for uniqueness. The normal inequalities at two projection points
give firm nonexpansiveness. Comparing the defining maxima shows that F_par
is differentiable with gradient equal to the projection; it is globally
convex and1-smooth. In detail, for the projection p(X),
||p(X)-p(Y)||^2<=<p(X)-p(Y),X-Y>, and
0<=F_par(Y)-F_par(X)-<p(X),Y-X>

  G(r)=(r,r^2/sqrt(2)).

  Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<G_i,X_j-X_i>+||G_i-G_j||^2/2

The identity follows by writing f_i=<X_i,G_i>-||G_i||^2/2 and expanding.
There is no adjacent-only interpolation condition in this construction.

The normal variable u_i=v_i+r_i^2 is nondecreasing for this entire physical
word, because(7) gives the exact identity

  u_(i+1)-u_i=(h_i-1)r_i^2+r_(i+1)^2>=0.                  (21)

Every h_i>1 here. Before the first turn, any previous step h_t preserved
positivity, so h_t<1+u_t. A later step h_i no larger than that previous
h_t then satisfies h_i<=h_t<1+u_t<=1+u_i and CANNOT turn. Consequently
the first turn occurs at a NEW RECORD step, necessarily the first occurrence
of some level j. In the actual recursive word that step is b_j=1+a c^j,
its prefix is exactly H_j with mass P=S_j, and its suffix mass is

  T=S_m-S_j-b_j>=S_(m-1).                                 (22)

The last inequality follows because S_j+b_j is increasing in j and the
top-level decomposition is S_m=2S_(m-1)+b_(m-1).

Let r>0,u be the state just before this first turn. The positive prefix
has decreasing r-values. Therefore its initial horizontal magnitude R
and the new magnitude R_after obey

  R=r(1+u)+sum_(t<i)h_t r_t>=r(P+1+u),
  R_after=r(h_i-1-u).

The numerator is positive precisely because this step turns. Hence

  R_after/R <=(h_i-1-u)/(P+1+u)
             <=(h_i-1)/(P+1)<c-2.                         (23)

The strict final inequality is(14)--(15), with the actual H_j prefix.
There is no restart of v: the tail starts at the ACTUAL state produced
by the turning step. By hypothesis there is no further turn, so Section5
applies to this suffix with its true initial distance R_after and mass T.
The function and its minimum0 remain identical. Thus

  2F_par(X_n)/R^2
    <=E_par(T)(R_after/R)^2
    <(c-2)^2 E_par(S_(m-1)).                               (24)

For monotonicity used here, logarithmic differentiation gives
E_par'(T)/E_par(T)=4/(2T+3)-2/(T+1)-2/(2T+1)<0; the first term is already
strictly smaller than2/(T+1). The suffix mass in(22) is at least1/2.

Since S_(m-1)/lambda^(m-1)->exp(1), the exact benchmark bound(19) gives

  limsup B_(2^m-1) sup_(all exactly-one-turn initial states) P_par
      <=lambda^2(lambda-2)^2/[4 exp(1)]
       =1/[4 exp(1)]<1/4.                                 (25)

The equality uses lambda(lambda-2)=1. Combining with the zero-turn estimate
(4), every initial state whose actual trajectory has at most one turn has
uniform benchmark limsup at most lambda/[4 exp(1)]<1/4. No initial norm,
vertical-state, amplitude, or smallness restriction was imposed on the
one-turn class. This is a proof about the full actual trajectory and its
retained normal state, not about a constant-gradient single-cut ansatz.

## 9. Remaining interface

Every parabolic start whose trajectory stays on one horizontal side is now
below even the global Huber lower witness by(17). Thus a useful parabolic
mechanism on this ruler must actually TURN. Condition(1) also quantifies
which initial horizontal scales cannot turn. The turning domain
0<R<S sqrt(c-2), with arbitrary coupled r0,v0, is not excluded here.

The k=1/4 finite starts had one turn and small observed M*payoff. Section8
now excludes the ENTIRE one-turn class, independently of those samples.
The k=1/16 starts had several turns; two or more turns remain open. A next
proof must retain how every turn transports BOTH the remaining horizontal
magnitude and the accumulated v into the actual suffix, with its unchanged
physical parameters. After the first turn, a later turn need not be a new
global record; applying the first-record argument afresh would be invalid.
Resetting v or treating the two H_j copies independently is also invalid.

This is a concrete uniform regime result with exact nonlinear block losses,
not a solution of the full parabolic family, the critical diagnostic, or the
original all-schedule exact-profile goal. All new statements remain drafts
until separately authorized independent verification.
