## 1. The actual interface and notation

For an integer m>=128, put

 lambda=1+sqrt(2), c=lambda(1+1/m), a=(1-1/m)/c,
 d_j=1+a c^j, H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,d_j,H_j).

Set n=2^m-1 and q=n-1. The queried steps h_0,...,h_(q-1) are the word
H_m with its final d_0 deleted. The actual final step is b in [0,2]. Define

 s_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, S=s_q,
 p_ij=product_(t<i)(1-h_t/d_j),
 R_i=sum_(j<m)p_ij w_j, Z_i=sum_(j<m)d_j p_ij w_j,
 x_i=u-s_i v+Z_i, g_i=v+R_i, 0<=i<=q.

All these vectors use ONE arbitrary real PSD Gram matrix. The values f_i
form ONE array; the optimum jet is x_star=g_star=f_star=0; ||x_0||=1 is
the distance normalization relative to the chosen actual minimizer. Every
original queried inequality is retained:

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0.       (1)

The first d_j occurs at step 2^j-1. Thus p_ij is positive before query
2^j and zero thereafter, and R_q=Z_q=0. The recursion gives

 Z_i-Z_(i+1)=h_i R_i, sum_i h_i p_ij=d_j.             (2)

Here and below unqualified time sums range over 0<=i<q.

For clarity about the original value budget, define

 C_min=f_q-<v,x_q>, eta=-C_min-||v||^2/2,
 delta_i=f_i-<v,x_i>-C_min-||R_i||^2/2.

On an actual legal function, tilting by -<v,x> makes x_q a minimizer;
the smooth optimum inequalities give delta_i>=0. Also eta>=0 follows
from Q_(q,star). Expanding Q_(0,star) gives the exact common budget

 eta>=delta_0+||R_0||^2+<v,R_0>-<R_0,x_0>.          (3)

No height is set to zero and neither eta nor the norm of x_0 is discarded.

## 2. A lossless stochastic change of coordinates

Define polynomials

 phi_k(z)=product_(r<k)(1-d_r z), 0<=k<=m.

Work modulo z phi_m(z), which has the m+1 distinct nodes
0,1/d_0,...,1/d_(m-1). The double zero in the position quotient leaves
exactly this simple-zero gradient quotient. Put

 V_k=v+sum_(j<m)phi_k(1/d_j) w_j, 0<=k<=m.

Then V_0=g_0 and V_m=v. This change from (w_0,...,w_(m-1),v) to
(V_0,...,V_m) is invertible: the evaluation matrix
phi_k(1/d_j), with columns ordered j=0,...,m-1, then the node0, is
upper triangular with strictly positive diagonal.

There are coefficients B_ik determined by B_00=1, B_0k=0 for k>0 and

 B_(i+1),0=(1-h_i/d_0)B_i0,
 B_(i+1),k=(1-h_i/d_k)B_ik+(h_i/d_(k-1))B_i,(k-1), 0<k<m,
 B_(i+1),m=B_im+(h_i/d_(m-1))B_i,(m-1).             (4)

Although an unused factor 1-h_i/d_k can be negative for a dead state,
it always multiplies zero. More precisely, if r_i distinct ruler levels
have occurred before query i, induction gives B_ik=0 for k<r_i.
The next step has level at most r_i, so h_i<=d_k on every occupied
state k<m. If it is the first d_(r_i), the mass at r_i moves entirely
to r_i+1. Consequently all B_ik are nonnegative, every row sums to one,
and after the first d_(m-1) the row is the point mass at state m.

The polynomial identity

 (1-hz)phi_k=(1-h/d_k)phi_k+(h/d_k)phi_(k+1)

for k<m, together with (1-hz)phi_m=phi_m modulo z phi_m, proves

 g_i=sum_(k=0)^m B_ik V_k.                           (5)

Thus B is exactly the marginal law of a time-inhomogeneous pure-birth
chain: from occupied state k<m it stays with probability 1-h_i/d_k
and advances one state with probability h_i/d_k; m is absorbing.
This probability space is auxiliary. No state vector V_k is assumed
to be a gradient at an additional sample of the legal function.

The positions are equally explicit. Let U_k=V_k-v and

 Y_k=sum_(r=k)^(m-1)d_r U_r, Y_m=0.

The conditional expected change of Y at step i is -h_i U_k when the
state is k. Hence sum_k B_ik Y_k changes by -h_i R_i. Initially

 Y_0=sum_k d_k U_k=sum_j d_j w_j=Z_0,

where the middle equality follows by telescoping
d_k phi_k(z)=(phi_k(z)-phi_(k+1)(z))/z at z=1/d_j.
Therefore

 Z_i=sum_k B_ik Y_k, x_i=u-s_i v+sum_k B_ik Y_k.      (6)

In particular the troublesome coupling remains, explicitly,

 <v,Z_0>=sum_(k<m)d_k<v,U_k>.                        (7)

Equations (5)-(6) preserve the original vectors, not just their norms.
Substitution in (1) gives EXACTLY

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j
   +<sum_k B_ik V_k, (s_i-s_j)v+sum_k(B_jk-B_ik)Y_k>
   +(1/2)||sum_k(B_ik-B_jk)V_k||^2.                  (8)

All earlier/earlier, suffix, and optimum conditions remain in this one
array. In particular (5) does not authorize independent state heights,
orthogonality of state vectors, or projected legality.

Two occupation identities retain quantitative physical costs:

 sum_i h_i B_ik=d_k,                                (9)
 sum_i s_(i+1)h_i B_ik=d_k sum_(r<=k)d_r, 0<=k<m.    (10)

To prove (9), sum the flow equation (4). State0 has initial mass1 and
final mass0, so its total outflow is1. Successive transient states have
equal total inflow and outflow, so their total outflows are also1.
For (10), write M_k=sum_i s_(i+1)h_i B_ik and M_(-1)=0.
Summation by parts gives

 sum_i s_(i+1)(B_(i+1),k-B_ik)=-sum_i h_i B_ik=-d_k.

Using (4), M_k/d_k-M_(k-1)/d_(k-1)=d_k, with the second term zero
at k=0. Induction proves (10). The terminal occupation is
sum_i h_i B_im=S-sum_(k<m)d_k; it is nonnegative and keeps the full
constant-gradient suffix in the budget.

## 3. Total nonnegativity for the ACTUAL chronological evaluations

This is a theorem about temporal coefficient matrices, not yet the
weighted energy matrix. It holds for arbitrary strictly increasing positive
levels d_0,...,d_(m-1) with the same ordered first-occurrence rule.

Let P be the matrix with chronological rows i=0,...,q and columns
p_i0,...,p_i,(m-1),1, in that order. Then P is totally nonnegative:
every square minor, with its inherited row and column order, is >=0.
The same is true of B, and of the jump matrix

 A_ij=(h_i/d_j)p_ij, 0<=i<q, 0<=j<m.                (11)

Every column of A sums to1 by (2).

External theorem used, with its local definitions made explicit:
David Galvin and Adrian Pacurar, *Total non-negativity of some combinatorial
matrices*, arXiv:1807.08658v3 (2019), Theorem1.2; published JCTA172 (2020),
105179. For a nondecreasing real sequence a_l, define C_ik by
product_(t<=i)(X-e_t)=sum_k C_ik product_(l<=k)(X-a_l).
Then C is totally nonnegative iff e is restricted growth relative to a:
the cap initially is a_1, an e_t below the cap leaves it unchanged,
an equality advances it to the next a_l, and exceeding it is forbidden.
The proof constructs a planar path matrix; zero pivots at cap hits move
all negative edge weights out of the network without changing its path
matrix. Disjoint-path determinant cancellation then proves the minor signs.

The exact PDF and extracted text were read, including Definition1.1,
Theorem1.2, the pivot lemmas3.7/3.9, and the complete proof of1.2.
Files: downloads/1807.08658v3.pdf and downloads/1807.08658.txt.

return the paper; the primary arXiv text, not a fabricated Matlas hit,
grounds this citation. No verifier acceptance is claimed for this application.

Here is the complete applicability check. Set

 a_1=-1/d_0<...<a_m=-1/d_(m-1)<a_(m+1)=0,

and continue the a sequence with zeros as far as necessary. Set
e_(i+1)=-1/h_i. A first occurrence of d_j is exactly a hit of the
current cap a_(j+1). Between first occurrences, every level is smaller
and hence its e is strictly below the next cap. After all levels have
appeared, every e is negative and the cap remains0. Thus the theorem's
restricted-growth condition is satisfied, with actual repetitions and
the actual deleted last d_0, not an ordered surrogate schedule.

Let N_k(X)=product_(r<k)(X+1/d_r), so
phi_k(z)=(product_(r<k)d_r)N_k(-z). The exact Newton expansion gives

 B_ik=(product_(t<i)h_t) C_ik/(product_(r<k)d_r),
 0<=k<=m.                                          (12)

The coefficients with k>m vanish upon evaluation at the m+1 nodes
-1/d_0,...,-1/d_(m-1),0. Positive row and column scaling and truncation
preserve all minor signs, proving that B is totally nonnegative.

For completeness the node-evaluation matrix V_kj=phi_k(1/d_j), with
the node0 last, is also totally nonnegative. After positive row scaling,
its transpose is L_jk=product_(r<k)(a_(j+1)-a_(r+1)). It is lower
triangular. In Neville elimination its pivot in row i,column k is

 product_(r=i-k)^(i-1)(a_(i+1)-a_(r+1))>0, i>=k.

Indeed the pivot is the ratio of determinants on consecutive rows
i-k,...,i and initial columns0,...,k, and on rows i-k,...,i-1 and
columns0,...,k-1. These determinants are Vandermonde determinants:
the monic Newton polynomials differ from monomials by a triangular
column change with diagonal1. Cancelling gives the displayed pivot.
All elimination multipliers are positive. Reversing the elimination
expresses L as a product of elementary lower bidiagonal matrices with
nonnegative off-diagonal entries and a positive diagonal matrix.
Each factor is totally nonnegative, as follows directly by expanding
its minors, and products preserve this property by Cauchy--Binet.
Thus V is totally nonnegative. Now P=BV, proving the assertion for P.
Finally A is a row and column positive scaling of P's residual submatrix,
so A has the asserted minor signs and stochastic column sums.

The chronological matrix has full column rank: the queries immediately
before first d_j, followed by any query after first d_(m-1), give a square
upper triangular evaluation submatrix with strictly positive diagonal.
Thus none of these representations has silently removed a modal direction.

## 4. Exact cancellation of ALL shared heights

Define the elapsed-time quadratic form

 J=sum_i [2s_(i+1)h_i<g_(i+1),g_i>
          +s_(i+1)||g_(i+1)-g_i||^2+h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2].

 ||x_0||^2-2S f_q+2sum_i h_i Q_(i,star)

Also Q_(i+1,i)=f_(i+1)-f_i+h_i<g_(i+1),g_i>
 +||g_(i+1)-g_i||^2/2. Summation by parts with s_0=0 and s_q=S
cancels the displayed common values and proves (13). All multipliers
are nonnegative. This is a sufficient certificate combination and does

## 5. A Brownian-bridge covariance with the full diagonal cost

Put D_i=g_i-g_(i+1)=sum_j A_ij w_j, where A is (11). Define increasing
clock knots and nonnegative penalties

 beta_i=s_(i+1)(s_i+1), 0<=i<q,
 beta_q=S(S+1), beta_(-1)=0,
 c_i=(h_i-1)s_(i+1).

All h_i>1, so c_i>=0, and beta_0<...<beta_q. Expanding the squared
differences in J gives

 J=sum_(i=0)^q(beta_i-beta_(i-1))||g_i||^2
                          -sum_(i<q)c_i||D_i||^2. (14)

For example the coefficient of ||g_i||^2 at an interior node is
s_i(h_(i-1)+h_i)+h_i; that of ||g_q||^2 is S h_(q-1).
These are exactly the differences of beta in (14).

Since g_i=v+sum_(r>=i)D_r, changing the order of finite sums yields

 J=beta_q||v||^2+2<v,sum_i beta_i D_i>
        +sum_(i,l<q)beta_min(i,l)<D_i,D_l>
        -sum_i c_i||D_i||^2.

Define

 Kbr_(i,l)=beta_min(i,l)-beta_i beta_l/beta_q.

Completing the ONE constant-mode square proves the exact joint identity

 J=beta_q||v+(sum_i beta_i D_i)/beta_q||^2
       +sum_(i,l<q)Kbr_(i,l)<D_i,D_l>
       -sum_i c_i||D_i||^2.                         (15)

The kernel Kbr is a real covariance kernel. For a direct elementary
construction, take independent centered random variables xi_r of variances
beta_r-beta_(r-1), 0<=r<=q, let Z_i=sum_(r<=i)xi_r, and set
Zbr_i=Z_i-(beta_i/beta_q)Z_q. Direct expansion gives
E[Zbr_i Zbr_l]=Kbr_(i,l). Consequently Kbr is PSD. This use of
probability creates no randomness in the legal instance or its shared Gram.

The remaining sufficient claim is the EXPLICIT known-matrix domination

 A^T Kbr A >= A^T diag(c_i) A                        (16)

in the real PSD order, for the actual nearcritical ruler words. It is
equivalent to J>=0 on this temporal quotient, because the constant square
in (15) can be minimized by a freely chosen formal vector v. Every cost
is present, including all repeated small steps, the large-step penalties,
and the final constant-gradient suffix through beta_q.

Thus (16), IF PROVED, combines with (13) and ||x_0||=1 to give
2f_q<=1/S, and the ordinary smooth descent inequality then gives

## 6. Why total nonnegativity does NOT settle the energy sign

Here is an exact counterexample to that GENERIC implication. It is not a
counterexample to (16) for the actual m-dependent parameters.

Take levels t=3/2 and u=4 and the ruler word (t,u,t). On its first two
steps, choose the single residual column at rate1/u, namely

 g_0=1, g_1=5/8, g_2=0, v=0.

The post-annihilation third step contributes zero. The ordered Newton
matrix and birth matrix are totally nonnegative by the same construction
as above. Nevertheless the first step contributes

 2t^2(5/8)+t(3/8)^2+t(1-t)=291/128,

and the second contributes

 (t+u)(5/8)^2+u(1-u)(5/8)^2=-325/128.

Therefore J=-17/64<0. The hypotheses used to prove the temporal minor
signs all hold, but weighted-energy positivity fails. These scalar data
are a formal certificate test, not an asserted legal interpolation array.

Accordingly, a planar network for A cannot by itself be called a proof of
(16). A successful path argument must ALSO absorb the explicit costs c_i
using the actual relation between ruler levels and chronological mass.
