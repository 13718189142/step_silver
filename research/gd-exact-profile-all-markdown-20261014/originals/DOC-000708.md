## Statement

Exact unqueried-terminal reduction and full-certificate re-selection for arbitrary nonnegative gradient-descent schedules.

For every integer n>=0, every L>0, and every finite h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)) in [0,infinity)^n, let W_n(h) be the supremum of 2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L||x_0-x_star||^2) over all finite-dimensional Euclidean spaces, convex differentiable functions f with L-Lipschitz gradient and a minimizer x_star, and x_0!=x_star, with x_(t+1)=x_t-(h_t/L)grad f(x_t). The empty schedule n=0 is allowed.

For the following finite formulations use normalized smoothness1, x_star=g_star=f_star=0, normalized sampled values f_i, formal gradients g_i, and x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t. Put I_n={star,0,...,n}, J_pre={star,0,...,n-1}, and Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 for distinct i,j in I_n. When n=0, J_pre={star}.

(a) The finite Gram maximization of 2f_n subject to G_pr>=0, G_pr_(x0,x0)<=1, all Q_ij<=0 with distinct i,j in J_pre, and all Q_(n,j)<=0 with j in J_pre, has attained optimal value W_n(h). Here G_pr is the full Gram matrix of the n+2 formal vectors (x_0,g_0,...,g_n), with no rank constraint. No incoming terminal inequality Q_(j,n)<=0 is imposed. For any feasible prefix jets in a finite-dimensional Euclidean space, define cjet_j=<g_j,x_j>-f_j, H_j(v)=cjet_j+<x_j,v-g_j>+||v-g_j||^2/2, H(v)=max_(j in J_pre)H_j(v), and F(x)=sup_v(<x,v>-H(v)). Then F is an everywhere finite convex differentiable 1-smooth function interpolating all prefix jets, F(0)=0=min F, and it is the pointwise maximal such prefix interpolant. For every reduced-feasible terminal pair (g_n,f_n), F(x_n)>=f_n. At an optimal reduced transcript, F(x_n)=f_n and grad F(x_n)=g_n; hence every optimal reduced transcript is fully interpolable and obeys the deleted incoming terminal inequalities.

(b) There exist nonnegative multipliers lambda_ij on all distinct pairs in I_n, with lambda_(j,n)=0 for every j in J_pre, finite-node flow sum_j lambda_ij-sum_j lambda_ji=2*1_(i=n), and a FULL positive semidefinite quadratic certificate
M_cert=W_n(h)||x_0||^2-2f_n+sum_(i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ij
after the GD substitution. This certificate is PSD on the original formal space (x_0,g_0,...,g_n), including every gradient at a zero step, without imposing equality between duplicated formal gradients. Thus some tight full certificate has no incoming edge at the unqueried terminal sample for every nonnegative schedule. For schedules with positive steps this follows from an explicit strict primal and a separation argument. If there are zero steps, choose the original index of each positive step as the representative of its compressed query and the original index n as the terminal representative; embedding a certificate for the positive-step compression gives the asserted full-space certificate. The all-zero and empty schedules have W_n(h)=1 and the direct certificate lambda_(n,star)=2, all other lambda zero, with M_cert=||x_0-g_n||^2.

(c) For any real certificate level q_cert and any h as above, certificates with no incoming terminal multipliers are parameterized exactly as follows. Choose alpha_j>=0 for j in J_pre, with sum_j alpha_j=1, and nonnegative prefix multipliers lambda^pre_ij for distinct i,j in J_pre whose finite prefix flow is out_i^pre-in_i^pre=2alpha_i for 0<=i<n, where out_i^pre=sum_(j in J_pre,j!=i)lambda^pre_ij and in_i^pre=sum_(j in J_pre,j!=i)lambda^pre_ji. Define y_j=x_j-g_j, delta_j=f_j-||g_j||^2/2, V_alpha=x_n-sum_(j in J_pre)alpha_j y_j, with y_star=delta_star=0, and
R_alpha=q_cert||x_0||^2-2sum_(j in J_pre)alpha_j delta_j-||V_alpha||^2+sum_(i!=j in J_pre)lambda^pre_ij Q_ij.
The prefix flow cancels all f_i in R_alpha. Set lambda_(n,j)=2alpha_j, all incoming terminal multipliers zero, and retain lambda^pre. The complete identity is
M_cert=||g_n-V_alpha||^2+R_alpha.
Consequently full PSD of M_cert is equivalent to PSD of the entire prefix quadratic R_alpha in (x_0,g_0,...,g_(n-1)). For n>=1, with w_(-1)=alpha_star and w_t=alpha_star+sum_(j=0)^t alpha_j, one has 0<=w_(-1)<=w_0<=...<=w_(n-1)=1 and
V_alpha=alpha_star x_0-sum_(t<n)[h_t w_t-(w_t-w_(t-1))]g_t.
For n=0, alpha_star=1, V_alpha=x_0, and R_alpha=(q_cert-1)||x_0||^2. Any actual normalized trajectory attaining a feasible q_cert has g_n=V_alpha, R_alpha=0, and multiplier complementarity.

This result deletes no interpolation inequalities inside the queried prefix. It asserts existence of a selected optimal certificate, not that every optimal certificate has the selected support. It neither establishes Huber tightness nor removes earlier remote forward edges, transfers earlier-cut stationarity under arbitrary re-selection, or proves the project's asymptotic exact-profile conjecture.

## Proof

1. Normalization, interpolation necessity, and elementary bounds.

For an original instance let R=||x_0-x_star||>0. The normalized function
F_0(z)=[f(x_star+R z)-f(x_star)]/(L R^2)
is convex differentiable with gradient Lipschitz constant1, a minimizer0,
and initial norm1. Its GD uses the same h and its normalized objective
is 2F_0(z_n). This also proves the converse scaling correspondence.
We may work with smoothness1 and the optimum sample zero.

For a differentiable function K with 1-Lipschitz gradient, integration
of its gradient along a segment gives
K(x+d)<=K(x)+<grad K(x),d>+||d||^2/2.
For convex K, apply this to K(z)-<grad K(x_i),z>, whose minimum is
at x_i, taking a unit negative-gradient step from x_j. Its minimum
value is no larger than the stepped value, so
K(x_j)-K(x_i)-<grad K(x_i),x_j-x_i>
    >=||grad K(x_j)-grad K(x_i)||^2/2.
This is exactly Q_ij<=0 for the actual samples, with our stated
orientation of Q.

For a normalized legal instance, put D_i=product_(t<i)(1+h_t), D_0=1.
Since grad K(0)=0, ||g_i||<=||x_i|| and ||x_(i+1)||<=(1+h_i)||x_i||.
Thus ||x_i||,||g_i||<=D_i, and
0<=K(x_i)<=||x_i||^2/2.
In particular W_n(h)<=D_n^2<infinity.
It is strictly positive: choose the scalar quadratic K(x)=kappa*x^2/2
with kappa=1/[2(1+sum_t h_t)] in (0,1], starting at1.
Every factor 1-kappa h_t is positive, and its normalized error is
kappa*product_t(1-kappa h_t)^2>0.

2. Explicit interpolation and the maximal prefix extension.

Let any finite collection of prefix jets (x_j,g_j,f_j), j in J_pre,
satisfy every ordered prefix inequality and include the zero optimum jet.
Define cjet_j,H_j,H,F as in the statement, on the same finite-dimensional
Euclidean space. The function H(v)-||v||^2/2 is the maximum of finitely
many affine functions and hence is convex. H is continuous, finite,
1-strongly convex, and has quadratic growth. For each x, the function
<v,x>-H(v) therefore has a unique maximizer, denoted v(x).

The maximizer condition is x in partial H(v(x)). For any x,x',
the two strong subgradient inequalities for H, added together, give
<x-x',v(x)-v(x')>>=||v(x)-v(x')||^2.
Consequently ||v(x)-v(x')||<=||x-x'||.
The definition of F, evaluated at v(x) and v(x'), gives
0<=F(x')-F(x)-<v(x),x'-x>
    <=<v(x')-v(x),x'-x><=||x'-x||^2.
Thus F is differentiable with grad F(x)=v(x), and its gradient is
1-Lipschitz. It is convex as a supremum of affine functions and finite
by the stated quadratic growth.

For i,j in J_pre direct expansion gives H_j(g_i)-a_i=Q_ij.
The term with j=i equals zero and every other term is nonpositive;
hence H(g_i)=a_i. The active quadratic H_i has gradient x_i at g_i,
and H>=H_i with equality there, so x_i is a subgradient of H at g_i.
The unique maximizer defining F(x_i) is consequently g_i, and
F(x_i)=<x_i,g_i>-a_i=f_i. This proves interpolation of all prefix jets.
In particular F(0)=0 and grad F(0)=0, so zero is a global minimizer.
The same construction, for any finite index set including the zero
jet, proves sufficiency of ALL pairwise Q inequalities for a legal
convex 1-smooth interpolant; repeated positions cause no exception.

If K is any other legal function interpolating the prefix, apply the
necessity calculation in paragraph1 between its sample at an arbitrary
x and each prefix jet j. Writing g=grad K(x), those inequalities give
H_j(g)<=<x,g>-K(x) for every j. Thus
K(x)<=<x,g>-H(g)<=F(x).
This proves the claimed pointwise maximality without importing an
interpolation or conjugacy theorem from a paper.

3. Exact value of the reduced Gram problem and primal attainment.

Realize any feasible Gram matrix by vectors in dimension at most n+2;
if its rank is zero, one may embed its zero vectors in R.
The complete prefix inequalities give the function F of paragraph2.
Its gradients at every queried prefix point equal the supplied g_i,
so the GD recursion reaches the same supplied x_n. No terminal gradient
is used to determine x_n, even when some steps vanish.

For the reduced terminal pair, the retained inequalities give
H_j(g_n)<=<x_n,g_n>-f_n for all j in J_pre. Therefore
f_n<=<x_n,g_n>-H(g_n)<=F(x_n).
If ||x_0||>0, normalization of this actual function yields
2F(x_n)<=W_n(h)||x_0||^2<=W_n(h).
If ||x_0||=0, all actual prefix iterates and x_n are the minimizer0,
so F(x_n)=0 and 2f_n<=0<W_n(h).
Thus the reduced supremum is at most W_n(h).
Every actual legal normalized trajectory is feasible for the reduced
Gram problem, so the reverse inequality holds.

For clarity the supremum is attained. Consider first the FULL Gram
problem, including every incoming terminal inequality. Its feasible
set is closed. Paragraph2 applied to the entire finite index set
I_n realizes every feasible point as a legal function; hence paragraph1
bounds all sampled gradients, all Gram entries by Cauchy--Schwarz,
and all values 0<=f_i<=D_i^2/2. The feasible set is therefore compact
in finite-dimensional matrix/value space. It is nonempty and has an
objective maximizer. Necessity and the interpolation construction
show its value is exactly W_n(h). The same full optimizer is reduced
feasible, so it also attains the reduced value.

At any reduced optimizer, the chain
W_n(h)=2f_n<=2F(x_n)<=W_n(h)||x_0||^2<=W_n(h)
has equal endpoints and W_n(h)>0. Thus ||x_0||=1,
f_n=F(x_n), and <x_n,g_n>-H(g_n)=F(x_n).
The maximizer of that conjugate expression is unique, so
g_n=grad F(x_n). The supplied terminal jet therefore belongs to the
same legal F as the prefix and satisfies every deleted incoming
inequality. This also proves the assertion for every reduced optimizer,
not just one optimizer chosen from the full feasible set.

4. Dual attainment for positive schedules, by separation.

Assume n>=1 and h_t>0 for every t<n. Let E_red be the retained
ordered pair set:
{(i,j): i,j in J_pre, i!=j} union {(n,j):j in J_pre}.
Use the matrix/value variables of the reduced primal.

There is a strictly feasible point with G_pr positive definite,
G_pr_(x0,x0)<1, and every retained Q strictly negative.
Choose n+2 distinct real theta_l in (0,1), let A_quad be the diagonal
matrix with these entries, let K(x)=<x,A_quad x>/2, and choose x_0
with every coordinate nonzero and norm less than1.
Write Poly_i(z)=product_(t<i)(1-h_t z), including Poly_0=1.
The columns (x_0,g_0,...,g_n) are evaluations at the theta_l of the
n+2 polynomials 1,z Poly_0,...,z Poly_n, times nonzero initial
coordinate factors. These polynomials have distinct degrees
0,1,...,n+1 and nonzero leading coefficients. A polynomial of degree
at most n+1 vanishing at n+2 distinct nodes is zero, so the evaluation
matrix is invertible and G_pr is positive definite.
For i!=j finite, Poly_i-Poly_j is a nonzero polynomial of degree
at most n; it cannot vanish at all chosen nodes. Poly_i cannot vanish
at all nodes either. Thus all finite sample positions are distinct
from each other and from star. Direct substitution for this quadratic
gives, with I the identity matrix,
Q_ij=-<x_i-x_j,A_quad(I-A_quad)(x_i-x_j)>/2<0.
This proves the full stated strictness.

Let w_opt=W_n(h), which is finite by paragraph1. Consider the open
convex set O of tuples (s,t,c), with s in R^(E_red) and t,c real,
for which there exist G_pr>=0 and real
sample values f with
s_ij>Q_ij(f,G_pr) for ij in E_red,
t>G_pr_(x0,x0)-1, and c<2f_n.
It is open since it is a union of open orthants; it is convex by
linearity of every Q in the Gram/value variables and convexity
of the PSD cone. The point (0,0,w_opt) is not in O.
Finite-dimensional separation of a point from a nonempty open convex
set gives a nonzero linear functional, chosen nonnegative on O
relative to that point. Independent upward changes of each s_ij
and of t, and downward changes of c, force its coefficients to be
(lambda_ij,rho,-eta) with lambda_ij,rho,eta>=0.
Taking limits to the boundary of each strict inequality yields,
for ALL G_pr>=0 and ALL real sample values f,
sum_(E_red)lambda_ij Q_ij(f,G_pr)
    +rho(G_pr_(x0,x0)-1)-eta*2f_n >= -eta*w_opt.       (S)

If eta=0, some lambda_ij or rho is positive, since the functional is
nonzero. Substitution of the strict primal point constructed above
makes the left side of (S) strictly negative, a contradiction.
Hence eta>0 and we divide the coefficients by eta to set eta=1.

The f_i are unrestricted real variables in (S). Their coefficients
must vanish:
out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n), for 0<=i<=n.
After their cancellation, (S) says
trace(M_cert(rho,lambda,h) G_pr)-rho>=-w_opt for every G_pr>=0.
If this matrix had a negative quadratic direction, scaling the
corresponding rank-one PSD G_pr would contradict this inequality.
It is therefore PSD. Taking G_pr=0 gives rho<=w_opt.
Conversely the flow, lambda>=0, and full PSD give, for every reduced
feasible point,
2f_n<=rho G_pr_(x0,x0)<=rho
because rho>=0. Thus w_opt<=rho. Equality follows.
Setting lambda_(j,n)=0 on the omitted pairs gives the required
tight full certificate on the original positive-step formal space.

5. Zero-step compression and lifting in the ORIGINAL formal space.

Suppose n>=1 and exactly m>=1 of the original steps are positive.
List their original indices as
0<=i_0<i_1<...<i_(m-1)<=n-1,
and define the positive compressed schedule hhat_s=h_(i_s),
0<=s<m. Deleting zero steps changes no actual iterate; therefore
W_m(hhat)=W_n(h) by the same function/initial-point trajectories in
both directions.

Apply paragraph4 to hhat, obtaining a tight incoming-terminal-free
certificate lambdahat on {star,0,...,m}.
Define the injective index map
iota(star)=star, iota(s)=i_s for 0<=s<m, and iota(m)=n.
On the original formal space define
xhat_0=x_0, ghat_s=g_(i_s) for s<m, ghat_m=g_n.
Its compressed formal positions satisfy
xhat_s=x_0-sum_(r<s)h_(i_r)g_(i_r)=x_(i_s) for s<m,
and
xhat_m=x_0-sum_(r<m)h_(i_r)g_(i_r)=x_n.
These are polynomial identities in independent original formal vectors:
the omitted coefficients h_t are ZERO. They do not use any equality
between gradients sampled at identical actual positions.

Put lambda_(iota(s),iota(t))=lambdahat_(s,t) on mapped pairs, and
put every other original multiplier equal to zero.
At each mapped finite node the original flow equals its compressed
flow; at each unmapped node both in-flow and out-flow vanish.
The mapped terminal is n, so the original finite flow is exactly
out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n). No multiplier points into original n.
Every mapped Q expression equals the corresponding compressed
Q expression after identifying the mapped values, whose coefficients
cancel by flow.

Let T_pick be the linear map selecting
(x_0,g_(i_0),...,g_(i_(m-1)),g_n)
from (x_0,g_0,...,g_n). The ORIGINAL full certificate matrix is precisely
T_pick^T Mhat_cert T_pick and is PSD because Mhat_cert is PSD.
Unmapped gradient columns are zero. No duplicate-gradient constraint
or auxiliary squared penalty is imposed or needed. This proves the
claimed original-index, full-formal-space lift, including leading,
interior, and trailing zero steps.

If m=0, including the empty schedule n=0, every actual iterate is x_0.
Smoothness at the minimizer gives 2f(x_0)<=||x_0||^2, and the scalar
quadratic x^2/2 attains equality. Thus W_n(h)=1.
Set lambda_(n,star)=2 and every other multiplier to zero.
It has exactly the required flow and no incoming terminal edges.
Since x_n=x_0 as a formal identity, its full quadratic is
||x_0||^2-2f_n+2Q_(n,star)=||x_0-g_n||^2.
This treats all-zero schedules and n=0 without any Slater assertion
about duplicated points. Parts(a) and the maximal extension remain
valid independently for these schedules by paragraphs2--3.

6. Exact terminal-square and full prefix-PSD parameterization.

Take ANY certificate whose incoming terminal multipliers vanish.
The finite terminal flow gives sum_(j in J_pre)lambda_(n,j)=2.
Consequently alpha_j=lambda_(n,j)/2 is a nonnegative probability
vector. All other multipliers lie inside the prefix, and their finite
flow is out_i^pre-in_i^pre=2alpha_i for i<n.
For any such alpha and prefix multipliers define y_j,delta_j,V_alpha,
R_alpha as in the statement, taking the star quantities equal to zero.

Expanding the outgoing terminal sum gives
2sum_j alpha_j Q_(n,j)
 =2f_n-2sum_j alpha_j f_j
       +2<g_n,sum_j alpha_j x_j-x_n>
       +sum_j alpha_j||g_n-g_j||^2
 =2f_n-2sum_j alpha_j delta_j
       +||g_n-V_alpha||^2-||V_alpha||^2.
Substituting into the full certificate proves
M_cert=||g_n-V_alpha||^2+R_alpha.
In R_alpha the coefficient of each finite f_i is
(out_i^pre-in_i^pre)-2alpha_i=0; it contains no f_n.
It is the complete quadratic form on ALL original prefix vectors.
The vector V_alpha is independent of the formal terminal gradient g_n.
Hence full PSD is equivalent to prefix PSD: prefix PSD implies full
PSD from the square identity, and full PSD implies prefix PSD by
setting the free formal vector g_n equal to V_alpha.
This is true even with zero-step duplicates; it is not a test only
on legal transcripts.

Conversely any alpha and nonnegative prefix multipliers with the
stated prefix flow define a full multiplier family by
lambda_(n,j)=2alpha_j and incoming terminal multipliers zero.
Their full finite flow is the required objective flow. If R_alpha
is PSD the full square identity certifies it. This proves both
directions of the parameterization for any real q_cert.

For n>=1, expand positions in
V_alpha=x_n-sum_(j in J_pre)alpha_j(x_j-g_j).
The coefficient of x_0 is
1-sum_(j=0)^(n-1)alpha_j=alpha_star.
For t<n, the coefficient of g_t is
-h_t+alpha_t+h_t sum_(j=t+1)^(n-1)alpha_j
 =-h_t[alpha_star+sum_(j=0)^t alpha_j]+alpha_t.
With w_t as in the statement, alpha_t=w_t-w_(t-1), giving
the claimed chronological formula and the nondecreasing w sequence.
For n=0 the probability simplex contains only alpha_star=1,
there are no prefix pairs, V_alpha=x_0, and
R_alpha=(q_cert-1)||x_0||^2.

Finally take an actual normalized trajectory attaining a feasible
certificate level q_cert. The certificate identity evaluated there is
M_cert=sum lambda_ij Q_ij<=0, while full PSD makes it nonnegative.
Every term lambda_ij Q_ij is nonpositive, so each is zero.
The square identity and prefix PSD then imply
g_n=V_alpha and R_alpha=0. This is an assertion about matching
actual trajectories, not a presumed rank condition on a primal.

7. Scope and proof dependencies.

The proof uses elementary smoothness, the displayed explicit convex
conjugate construction, finite-dimensional linear algebra, compactness
of the bounded finite Gram system, and a fully specified separating
hyperplane argument. No historical project KKT claim or unverified
finding is a premise. No external paper theorem is used as a black
box. In particular the separation argument is used only for genuinely
positive compressed steps, and the zero-step conclusion is obtained
by an explicit embedding into the original formal space.

Only incoming constraints at n, which is unqueried by the recursion,
are removed. Every ordered interpolation constraint inside J_pre
remains. The statement implies neither Huber tightness nor a removal
of earlier remote edges or a transfer of earlier-cut stationarity
under arbitrary re-selection; it does not decide the asymptotic
exact-profile problem.
