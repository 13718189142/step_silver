## 0. Mathematical verdict

**Not solved.** The exact all-schedule profile converse and a full-class
asymptotically improved schedule are both missing.

The fixed goal is unchanged: for every epsilon>0 there must be N_epsilon
independent of the finite nonnegative schedule h such that every n>=N_epsilon
has W_n(h)>=(1-epsilon)/B_n, or there must be a rigorous fixed relative
improvement along unbounded horizons. Here W_n(h)=sup 2(f_n-f_star)/
(L||x0-x_star||^2), over all finite-dimensional convex L-smooth functions
with a minimizer, and B_n is the exact recurrence below. Keep W=2R.

Closed components include universal Huber and selected legal adversarial
constructions, and exact unqueried-terminal completion/re-selection
(facts 0be8b54e0a33381c, 0e7e78056dc6258c, b98bb807d514e80d,
dfde2fa326c908bf, 1fca3e5dd555cb4a, 1252b8823bb5a290).
They do not close the target. The current conditional negative architecture
is a full objective certificate on a precisely defined critical dyadic word,
followed by a new scalar comparison. The main blocker is actual nonnegative
full-array feasibility and PSD at growing depths, not scalar asymptotics.
The affirmative architecture still lacks a lossless quantitative all-schedule
construction or necessary dual inequality.

The new evidence establishes method failure in several stated classes, not
proposition failure. Neither a finite Huber match nor a full-metric exclusion
within a fixed temporal span gives the unrestricted conclusion. SUBSTANTIAL
describes a conditional package with unmatched hypotheses, not proximity to
completion.

Current best proof skeleton:
1. Fix lambda=1+sqrt2 and the exact P/B recurrences below.
2. At depth m fix c_m=lambda(1+1/m), a_m=(1-1/m)/c_m for the whole word.
3. Let H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a_m c_m^j,H_j), n_m=2^m-1, M_m=1+sum H_m.
4. C54 draft gives B_(n_m)<=lambda^(m+1) and M_m/lambda^m->e.
5. Construct an actual full smooth-convex objective certificate with q_m M_m eventually bounded strictly below e/lambda. This is MISSING.
6. Its full value cancellation, multiplier signs and PSD must hold on x0,g0,...,g_n, giving W_(n_m)(H_m)<=q_m.
7. Combine the strict comparison to get a fixed relative gap below U_(n_m)^ZJ=1/B_(n_m).
8. Subject the entire package, including the comparator, to independent attack and verification.

Central missing lemma: for the words in steps 2-3, there exist m0 and a
constant C<e/lambda such that every m>=m0 has q_m<=C/M_m and nonnegative
coefficients Lambda_ij whose smooth-convex interpolation combination cancels
all values in q_m||x0||^2-2f_n+sum Lambda_ij Q_ij and leaves a PSD quadratic
form on the entire independent formal space. Sign convention for Q must be
specified; optimum edges and all queried-prefix constraints are retained.
This lemma is a sufficient negative-resolution target, not a known theorem
or a replacement of the original affirmative goal. The exact finite n=7
certificate and several failed scalable arrays leave this full feasibility
problem open. Even q_m=1/M_m is sufficient; Huber equality is unnecessary.

## 2. Interface contract table

**Input required.** P0=B0=1,

G(x,d)=2x+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8xd)/2,

**Available facts.** b98bb807d514e80d supplies the benchmark definitions.

still a draft; it gives no objective upper bound.

performance guarantee.

## 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- "The critical word is Huber-tight because H_3 is." Status: DANGEROUS.
  The finite certificate does not construct all depths. Larger q remains
  sufficient and may need a different optimum-edge and kernel structure.
- "A balanced pair fixes the old bad vector, therefore the certificate is fixed."
  Status: FALSE AS STATED. C55's pair increment is indefinite on the original
  space; the complete Schur/range conditions remain necessary.
- "Small modes remain harmless under every metric." Status: FALSE AS STATED.
  c5a096d73740bf81 is an actual legal counterexample. With sufficiently many
  modes, an unrestricted metric can encode the original problem.
- "Exact terminal elimination can be repeated at positive queried vertices."
  Status: FALSE AS STATED. Positive-query incidence and the fixed GD update
  prevent that deletion; the accepted terminal fact explicitly retains them.
- "An endpoint-fan no-go refutes the schedule." Status: DANGEROUS.
  The negative formal template itself violates a queried pair. It refutes
  the stated certificate array, not the existence of all arrays or a function.

Strategies not to pursue:
- Do not reopen the closed two-mode span by another covariance amplitude.
- Do not rename an unrestricted Gram formulation as a new finite-field method.
- Do not infer full PSD from the returned numerical primal span.
- Do not promote C54 or C51 to fact status without actual acceptance.
- Do not trade an unspecified constant or exponent-only theorem for the exact B_n target.
- Do not discard zero steps, optimum edges, or singular range conditions.
