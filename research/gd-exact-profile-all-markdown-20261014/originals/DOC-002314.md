## 1. Full formal interface and the gradient-difference matrix

Let r>=1, h_0,...,h_(r-1)>=0, P_i=sum_(t<i)h_t for0<=i<=r,
s=P_r, and C=1+s. Set h_r=0 only for formulas. Indices of the formal
gradients range over0,...,r. Let e be the all-ones column, and define

    T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, T_it=0 for t>i,
    omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(r-1),2s)^T,
    D=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(r-1)(2-h_(r-1)),-s(s+2)),
    L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,  M=D+(LT+T^T L^T)/2.

Assume Lambda_ii=0, all off-diagonal Lambda_ij>=0, L^T e=omega,
and M>=0 on the entire original space R^(r+1). Thus this is precisely
the full finite certificate in fact04219554e41d9750, with arbitrary r.
No multiplier support, rank, or equality between formal gradients is imposed.

For x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, define

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    V_h=2sum_(i<r)h_i(f_i-f_r)+sum_(i<r)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                               -s(s+1)||g_r||^2.

The full coefficient identity from fact04219554e41d9750 is

    V_h+sum_(i!=j)Lambda_ij Q_ij = <M,Gram(g_0,...,g_r)>.       (1)

The residuals Q_ij are nonpositive on every actual convex differentiable
1-smooth trajectory by fact1252b8823bb5a290. Hence this interface implies
V_h>=0 on all such trajectories with a minimizer.

Here is an algebraic proof of the constant-gradient kernel, needed below.
Since Te=e+(P_0,...,P_r)^T and
2sum_(i<r)h_i P_i=s^2-sum_(i<r)h_i^2, we have

    e^T D e=-s^2-sum h_i^2,
    e^T LT e=omega^T Te=s^2+sum h_i^2.

Thus e^T M e=0. Positive semidefiniteness implies Me=0: otherwise for
some y the linear term in (e+t y)^T M(e+t y) would be negative for one
sign of sufficiently small t.

For0<=j<r let u^j be the indicator of indices0,...,j. Let U have these
r columns and put K_jump=U^T M U. For any scalar formal vector g,

    g=e*g_r+sum_(j<r)u^j(g_j-g_(j+1)),
    g^T M g=sum_(i,j<r)(K_jump)_ij(g_i-g_(i+1))(g_j-g_(j+1)). (2)

In particular K_jump>=0. Conversely, under Me=0, its PSD property is
equivalent to full M>=0 by(2). This is a congruence identity retaining
all formal gradients, not a deletion of zero-step coordinates.

## 2. A legal cut trajectory for every positive step

Fix j with b=h_j>0. In this section define

    P=P_j, R=s-P_(j+1), A=1+P, B=1+R, d=b-1,
    v=1-b                 if 0<b<=1,
    v=(1-b)/B             if b>=1,
    w=1-v>0.

The two formulas agree at b=1. There is one legal scalar convex1-smooth
function with a minimizer whose GD trajectory has gradients1 at all
indices i<=j and v at all indices k>j, starting from x_0=A. Its positions
are

    x_i=1+P_j-P_i                         (i<=j),
    x_k=1-b-v(P_k-P_(j+1))                (k>j).             (3)

All sampled values can be chosen as f_i=x_i g_i-g_i^2/2.

For b>1, use the derivative clip(x,[v,1]) and f(0)=0. The bridge lands
at -d=vB, the suffix remains at or below v, and its terminal point is v.
The derivative is continuous, nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz, and0 is a
minimizer. On each plateau the stated values follow by integration.

For0<b<=1, v=1-b>=0. Put zeta=v(1-R)-1, strictly below every suffix
sample and below v. Define a derivative on the whole real line by

    f'(x)=0                  for x<=zeta-v,
          x-zeta+v           for zeta-v<=x<=zeta,
          v                  for zeta<=x<=v,
          x                  for v<=x<=1,
          1                  for x>=1.

Choose the integration constant by f(v)=v^2/2. This derivative is again
continuous, nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz, and its zero region contains
minimizers. The bridge lands at v. The suffix stays in[zeta,v], so its
gradient is v. Integration gives the stated sampled values on both
plateaus. Degenerate intervals when v=0 cause no difficulty. The minimum
value need not be zero; V_h and every finite Q are invariant under adding
a constant to f.

For any scalar data satisfying f_i=x_i g_i-g_i^2/2, telescoping the GD
squares gives

    V_h=x_0^2-(x_r+s g_r)^2.

On(3), x_r=-d-vR, hence the exact energy is

    E_j=A^2-[(A+d)v-d]^2.                                (4)

The same sampled-value identity gives Q_ik=(g_i-g_k)(x_k-g_k).
Define, for k>j,

    t_jk=d+v(1+P_k-P_(j+1)).

These numbers are nonnegative, with the more transparent forms

    t_jk=v(P_k-P_(j+1))          if0<b<=1,
    t_jk=(-v)(s-P_k)             ifb>=1.

Every within-block residual is zero. Every cross residual is exactly

    -Q_(i,k)=w*t_jk,
    -Q_(k,i)=w*(P_j-P_i),              i<=j<k.             (5)

Since g=v e+w u^j and Me=0, substitution into the complete identity(1)
gives the exact slack budget

    E_j=w^2(K_jump)_jj
       +w sum_(i<=j<k)[t_jk Lambda_(i,k)
                                      +(P_j-P_i)Lambda_(k,i)]. (6)

Every term on the right is nonnegative. Equation(6) keeps every original
cross multiplier, including both directions and both endpoints.

## 3. Step-only matrix bounds and a quadratic compatibility test

For each positive h_j define beta_j=E_j/w^2 using the preceding formulas.
A feasible full certificate necessarily has beta_j>=0 and

    0<=(K_jump)_jj<=beta_j.                              (7)

When b>=1, the expression simplifies to

    beta_j=[A^2 B^2-C^2 d^2]/(B+d)^2,
    C=A+B+d.                                             (8)

For0<b<=1 it is

    beta_j=[A^2-(1-b)^2(A+b)^2]/b^2.                     (9)

No negative-v trajectory is used in deriving(9). In particular the
small-step case is not obtained by applying a wrong-sign Huber residual.

For this section suppose all steps are positive. Set beta_(-1)=beta_r=0
and Drow_i=sqrt(beta_(i-1))+sqrt(beta_i) for0<=i<=r.
For a PSD matrix, its2-by-2 principal minors give
|(K_jump)_ik|<=sqrt(beta_i beta_k). Equation(2), applied to a standard
coordinate vector, therefore gives

    M_ii<=Drow_i^2,
    |(M u^j)_i|<=Drow_i sqrt(beta_j).                    (10)

The second inequality is the PSD Cauchy--Schwarz inequality; it also
follows by requiring (e_i+t u^j)^T M(e_i+t u^j)>=0 for every real t.
For every real vector z of length r,

    0<=z^T K_jump z<=(sum_(i<r)|z_i|sqrt(beta_i))^2.      (11)

An additional necessary condition involves no unknown multipliers.
Put p_0=1, p_(i+1)=(1-h_i)p_i. The actual quadratic f(x)=x^2/2,
started at1, has x_i=g_i=p_i and every finite Q_ik=0. Consequently

    V_h=1-C^2 p_r^2,
    0<=1-C^2 p_r^2
          <=(sum_(i<r)|h_i p_i|sqrt(beta_i))^2.           (12)

The upper inequality follows from(2),(11), since p_i-p_(i+1)=h_i p_i.
Equation(12) is only a necessary compatibility test. Neither it nor the
scalar inequalities beta_j>=0 are asserted sufficient.

## 4. Explicit stability at a cut with b>1

Assume all h_i>0 and fix a cut j with b=h_j>1. Continue using the local
notation P,R,A,B,d above, and set

    a=d/B, w=1+a, delta=beta_j,
    m=min_(i<r)h_i>0, Delta_cut=AB-Cd.

Equation(8) implies Delta_cut>=0 and d<B: if d>=B, then Cd>=CB>AB.
Write u=Lambda_(j,r), v_k=Lambda_(k,j) for k>j, and set
c_k=h_k for j<k<r, c_r=1; thus sum_(k>j)c_k=B.

The ideal cross array has only the entries

    Lambda^0_(j,r)=d q, Lambda^0_(k,j)=q c_k (k>j),
    q=2(A+d)/(B-d),                                     (13)

with every other cross entry zero. At exact cut equality this is the
usual primitive-join array. Away from equality it is just an explicitly
defined comparison array; it is not claimed to be a certificate.

Here are step-only error bounds, with empty sums interpreted as zero:

    Fbd=w delta/(a m) if j<r-1, and Fbd=0 otherwise,
    Bbd=w delta/m     if j>0,   and Bbd=0 otherwise,
    Ubd=[2 sqrt(delta)sum_(i<j)Drow_i+P Bbd]/B,
    eta=Fbd+Bbd+Ubd,
    Ebd=2 sqrt(delta)sum_(k>j)Drow_k+2C(r-j)eta,
    Lbd=[2B/(B-d)] [eta+Ebd/d].                         (14)

Then the sum of the absolute differences of ALL crossing coefficients
in the supplied full array and(13) is at most Lbd:

    sum_(i<=j<k)(|Lambda_(i,k)-Lambda^0_(i,k)|
                      +|Lambda_(k,i)-Lambda^0_(k,i)|)<=Lbd. (15)

Here is a derivation, keeping track of the off-support terms.
First (6) with t_jk=a(s-P_k) yields

    Factual=sum_(i<=j,j<k<r)Lambda_(i,k)<=Fbd,
    Bactual=sum_(k>j,i<j)Lambda_(k,i)<=Bbd.              (16)

The weights s-P_k and P_j-P_i in these sums are at least m. The
remaining potentially unwanted cross terms are u_i=Lambda_(i,r),i<j.
For i<j, summing the complete mixed matrix entries gives

    2(M 1_right)_i
      =-B u_i-sum_(j<l<r)(1+P_l-P_(j+1))Lambda_(i,l)
       -sum_(k>j)Lambda_(k,i)
       +h_i sum_(k>j,l<=i)Lambda_(k,l).                 (17)

In(17), 1_right=e-u^j. By Me=0 and(10), the absolute value of its left
side is at most2Drow_i sqrt(delta). The two subtracted sums are
nonnegative. Thus

    B u_i<=2Drow_i sqrt(delta)+h_i Bactual.

Summing gives Uactual=sum_(i<j)u_i<=Ubd. Hence the total mass of all
cross entries absent from(13) is at most eta.

For completeness, the full off-diagonal formula for i<k is

    2M_ik=-(Lambda_ik+Lambda_ki)
          -h_k sum_(l>k)Lambda_il+h_i sum_(l<=i)Lambda_kl. (18)

It follows directly by multiplying L and T; set h_r=0 in this formula.
For fixed k>j, summing absolute values in(18) over i<j gives

    sum_(i<j)|2M_ik|
       <=(1+h_k)(Factual+Uactual)+(1+P)Bactual<=C eta.

Since (M u^j)_k=M_kj+sum_(i<j)M_ki, equation(10) gives
|2M_jk|<=2Drow_k sqrt(delta)+C eta.
Formula(18) at i=j gives, for k<r,

    2M_jk=d v_k-h_k u
        -Lambda_jk-h_k sum_(k<l<r)Lambda_jl
                                  +b sum_(l<j)Lambda_kl,

and at k=r gives 2M_jr=d v_r-u+b sum_(l<j)Lambda_rl.
The terms after d v_k-c_k u have total absolute value at most C eta.
Therefore, for every k>j,

    |d v_k-c_k u|<=e_k,
    e_k=2Drow_k sqrt(delta)+2C eta,
    sum_(k>j)e_k=Ebd.                                  (19)

Sum the original value-flow equations over the left block. Its outward
flow is u+Uactual+Factual and its inward flow is sum v_k+Bactual. Thus

    |sum_(k>j)v_k-u-2(A+d)|<=eta.

Together with(19) this implies

    |u-d q|<=[d eta+Ebd]/(B-d)=:Uerr,
    |v_k-q c_k|<=[c_k Uerr+e_k]/d.

Adding these bounds and the off-support mass eta gives

    eta+(1+B/d)Uerr+Ebd/d
        =[2B/(B-d)] [eta+Ebd/d]=Lbd,

which proves(15). In particular this controls the entire cross array,
not a selected sparse ansatz. No assertion about the internal arrays'
signs after tail restriction has entered the calculation.

## 5. Quantitative primitive-energy bound for the actual prefix

Let h_left=(h_0,...,h_(j-1)), of sum P and capacity A. Under the
hypotheses of Section4, EVERY actual convex1-smooth trajectory of this
prefix obeys

    V_(h_left)>=-[Rbd+(2P+b+1/2)Lbd] Gmax^2,             (20)
    Rbd=(A+d)Delta_cut/(B-d),
    Gmax=max_(0<=i<=j)||g_i||.

The energy of an empty prefix is0. For a nonempty prefix, no initial
distance normalization or bounded minimizer distance is assumed in(20).

To prove the claim, take any actual prefix data. Translate positions
and shift values so x_j=0 and f_j=0. Append formal tail data by setting
every g_k=f_k=0 for k>j and x_k=-b g_j for k>j. These tail data are NOT
asserted to arise from an actual function. They are permitted for
evaluating the full formal PSD identity(1). All tail-internal Q vanish.

Using the ideal cross array(13), direct substitution yields

    V_h+sum_cross Lambda^0_ik Q_ik
                      =V_(h_left)+Rbd||g_j||^2.         (21)

For an explicit coefficient check before the f_j=0 shift, V_h-V_left
has value coefficient2(P+b)f_j and gradient coefficient
b(1-b)+P(P+1) on ||g_j||^2. The ideal cross sum contributes
q(d-B)f_j+q[B/2-d(b-1/2)]||g_j||^2. The value coefficients cancel
because q(B-d)=2(A+d)=2(P+b). The remaining coefficient is

    A^2-(1+q)d^2
      =[(A+d)(AB-d(A+B+d))]/(B-d)=Rbd.

The actual prefix inequalities Q_ik<=0 and Lambda_ik>=0 make their
contribution in(1) nonpositive. Every cross residual on these formal
data has absolute value at most(2P+b+1/2)Gmax^2: actual convexity gives
|f_i-f_j|<=Gmax||x_i-x_j||<=P Gmax^2, GD gives
||x_i-x_j||<=P Gmax, and the formulas are

    Q_(i,k)=f_i+<g_i,-b g_j-x_i>+||g_i||^2/2,
    Q_(k,i)=-f_i+||g_i||^2/2,                    i<=j<k.

Equations(1),(15),(21) and full M>=0 now give(20).
This proof uses the unobserved formal extension only inside a PSD
identity; it never claims those data constitute a legal GD witness.

The error Rbd is linear in the scalar cut gap:

    delta=Delta_cut(AB+Cd)/(B+d)^2,
    Rbd=delta (A+d)(B+d)^2/[(B-d)(AB+Cd)].              (22)

Consequently, along a bounded positive-step family with m and d bounded
away from0, B-d bounded away from0, and all beta_i bounded, the right
side's coefficient in(20) is O(sqrt(delta)). This asymptotic notation
is only a consequence of the explicit bounds(14),(22).

An actual prefix witness with V_left=-nu Gmax^2, nu>0, therefore forces

    nu<=Rbd+(2P+b+1/2)Lbd.                              (23)

This is an explicit necessary condition on the parent steps. It supplies
a quantitative route from a prefix obstruction to a positive gap at the
parent cut, rather than assuming an exactly saturated cut.

For a concrete symbolic instance, take a parent whose first step is3/2
and select j=1, assuming its selected bridge h_1>1 and all steps positive.
For its one-step prefix, f(x)=x^2/2 started at1 has gradients1,-1/2,
Gmax=1 and V_left=1-(5/2)^2(1/2)^2=-9/16. Therefore every such parent
full certificate must satisfy

    Rbd+(b+7/2)Lbd>=9/16.                              (24)

In any bounded family meeting the positivity conditions after(22),
this prohibits approaching delta=0 with that fixed first step. It does
not exclude a parent with a positive cut gap or any unrelated seed.

## 6. Equality, zero steps, and exact symbolic checks

At delta=0, every quantity in(14) vanishes and Delta_cut=0. Thus all
cross entries are exactly(13). Their mixed gradient block is zero by
(18),(19). The left internal multipliers are nonnegative and give a
full certificate for V_left, since(21) now has Rbd=0 and the full left
matrix is the corresponding principal PSD block. This recovers the
old saturated-prefix restriction by a new limiting-compatible estimate.

Writing kappa=(C/B)^2 at equality gives q=kappa-1. The accepted full
primitive-join identity then shows that the tail has a certificate for

    V_tail-(1-1/kappa)sum_(k<r)h_k Q_(k,r),

where this sum is over nonterminal TAIL indices. Removing this signed
credit would subtract q h_k from its actual coefficient Lambda_(k,r).
Nothing in the preceding estimates asserts that those differences are
nonnegative. Thus no automatic ordinary primitive-tail restriction is
inferred; the old counterexample remains relevant.

Zero steps require care. The formulas(1),(2),(6) at a POSITIVE cut hold
even if other steps vanish. But(7)--(15) for every coordinate are not
claimed for an original word containing zeros. A full certificate can
have arbitrarily large curvature in an unused zero-step gradient direction.
For example h=(0), Lambda_01=Lambda_10=t>=0 satisfies the complete
flow equations and has M=t[[1,-1],[-1,1]]. Its jump matrix is[t], with
no finite step-only bound on t. This is an exact counterexample to
extending beta=2-b^2 continuously to a formal coordinate at b=0.

For necessary schedule tests one may instead compress zero steps in a
justified way. Retain the original index of each positive step and the
original terminal index. Identify formal jets within each repeated-query
group only to RESTRICT a given full certificate. The GD formulas and
V_h become exactly those of the positive-step compressed word. An edge
within one group vanishes after restriction; every other edge becomes
the corresponding compressed Q with its nonnegative coefficients summed.
Thus a full original certificate implies a full compressed certificate.

Conversely, a full compressed primitive certificate embeds into the
original formal space by assigning its edges only to the retained indices
and assigning zero to other edges. Zero steps contribute nothing to V_h
or to any queried displacement, and the original terminal is retained.
Therefore the embedded identity is the pullback of the compressed PSD
matrix and is PSD on ALL original independent gradients. No equality
between zero-step gradients is needed for this converse. The all-zero
word has the zero certificate. This proves feasibility equivalence while
also explaining why the original matrix entries need not be bounded.

In particular the tests above may be applied to the compressed word,
and the exact saturated-prefix conclusion may be lifted to the original
prefix by this embedding. The quantitative constants for a compressed
word refer to its compressed coefficient array, not an unjustified bound
on every possible original zero-step coefficient.

For one positive step b, the only value-flow and constant-kernel solution
is Lambda_01=2, Lambda_10=2b+2. Direct expansion gives

    V_(b)+2Q_01+(2b+2)Q_10=(2-b^2)||g_0-g_1||^2.

Both versions(8),(9) reduce exactly to beta_0=2-b^2. Thus they recover
the complete one-step interface and its endpoint b=sqrt(2), including
the b<1 branch, without a numerical evaluation.
