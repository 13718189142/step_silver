    2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L ||x_0-x_star||^2)

## 4. Exact backward construction, including the cap

    t_j>=0.

the capped test is y_j<=kappa[u_j+(alpha-1)c]. If it holds, set
mu=u_j+(alpha-1)c. Then mu>=y_j/kappa>0 and

    r_j+alpha v(0)-v(0)

alpha'>=0 this implies y_(j-1)<=kappa[u_(j-1)+(alpha'-1)c]. Hence

If the cap test fails, a previously capped state is impossible. Define

    F_j(theta)=<r_j,e(theta)-kappa^(-1)e_t(theta)>/R_sp(theta).

The root equation therefore says that

It proves the projection of r_(j-1)=r_j+alpha v(theta), and gives its

## 13. Actual finite equal-angle words and their total cost

Fix the permitted parameters and any integer M>=1. Put
Delta=Theta/M and theta_l=Theta-l Delta for0<=l<=M. Define

    A_D=exp(-kappa Delta)(cos Delta+sin Delta/kappa),
    B_D=exp(-kappa Delta)sin Delta/kappa,
    t_0=0, t_l=B_D+A_D t_(l-1),
    r_l=R_sp(theta_l)[(1+t_l)e(theta_l)+kappa t_l e_t(theta_l)],
    a_D=1-exp(-kappa Delta)cos Delta+B_D,
    alpha_l=a_D+(1+kappa^2)B_D t_(l-1).

Every coefficient A_D,B_D is nonnegative and every alpha_l is
positive: 1-exp(-kappa Delta)cos Delta>=0 and B_D>0.
Expanding r_(l-1)/R_sp(theta_l) in the orthonormal basis at theta_l gives tangent
component
exp(-kappa Delta)[(1+t_(l-1))sin Delta+kappa t_(l-1)cos Delta],
which equals kappa t_l. Its radial component is
exp(-kappa Delta)[(1+t_(l-1))cos Delta-kappa t_(l-1)sin Delta].
Subtracting it from1+t_l gives exactly alpha_l. Thus

    r_l-r_(l-1)=alpha_l v(theta_l).

Also r_l-v(theta_l)=R_sp(theta_l)t_l n_sp(theta_l) is a valid
supporting normal. Therefore v(theta_l)=Pi_K(r_l). The finite
chronological word (alpha_M,...,alpha_1), starting at r_M, follows
these actual states to r_0=(epsilon,-1). At theta_M=0 its last
backward step is precisely the allowed cap-boundary equality.

Let S_M=sum_(l=1)^M alpha_l,
P_M=(r_M)_1-epsilon, Q_M=-(r_M)_2-1. Then

    P_M=c(1+t_M)-epsilon, 1+Q_M=c kappa t_M.

For large enough M, A_D>1 because A_D'(0)=d/kappa>0. Discrete
telescoping t_l-t_(l-1)=B_D+(A_D-1)t_(l-1) yields

    t_M=B_D(A_D^M-1)/(A_D-1),
    S_M=M a_D+(1+kappa^2)B_D(t_M-M B_D)/(A_D-1).

The required limits are
A_D^M->exp(d Theta/kappa), B_D/(A_D-1)->1/d,
M B_D->Theta/kappa, and M a_D->(kappa+1/kappa)Theta.
Consequently

    t_M->T,
    S_M->(1+kappa^2)T/d-kappa(1+kappa^2)Theta/d.

These limits are taken only after establishing each exact finite word
and its cost. No continuous-time proxy or arithmetic sweep is used.
