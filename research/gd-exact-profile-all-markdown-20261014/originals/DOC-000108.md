## 2. One explicit construction for any symmetric concatenation

Let `h` be any finite strictly positive word of mass `S`, put `A=S+1`, and choose any `d>0`. Consider the literal concatenation `(h,1+d,h)`. Set

\[
r=\frac dA>0.
\]

Define a single global function on the real line by

\[
f_r(x)=
\begin{cases}
-rx-\tfrac12r^2,&x\le-r,\\
\tfrac12x^2,&-r\le x\le1,\\
x-\tfrac12,&x\ge1.
\end{cases} \tag{1}
\]

The function and its derivative agree at both junctions. Its derivative is

\[
f_r'(x)=\max\{-r,\min\{x,1\}\}.
\]

This derivative is nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz. Consequently `f_r` is globally convex, differentiable and 1-smooth. Its unique minimizer is `x_star=0`, with minimum zero. This is an actual function, not proposed interpolation data.

Start at `x_0=A`. In the first copy of `h`, let `u` denote the sum of all steps already taken. Every iterate is

\[
x=A-u\ge1,\qquad g=1,
\qquad 0\le u\le S.
\]

This follows directly by induction using the prescribed GD recursion. The first copy ends at `x=1`. The bridge is then

\[
1-(1+d)f_r'(1)=-d=-Ar.
\]

In the second copy of `h`, the corresponding iterates are

\[
x=-Ar+ur=-r(A-u)\le-r,\qquad g=-r,
\qquad 0\le u\le S.
\]

They again follow by the exact GD recursion. The actual terminal data are therefore

\[
x_N=-r,\qquad g_N=-r,\qquad f_N=\tfrac12r^2,
\qquad \|x_0-x_\star\|^2=A^2. \tag{2}
\]

Every physical step in both copies is retained. No property of the order within `h` was needed except positivity, which ensures every partial sum lies between zero and `S`.

Let the full mass be `T=2S+1+d`, and put `A_+=T+1=2A+d=A(2+r)`. For an arbitrary coefficient `q`, the ratio between the left and right sides of the joint terminal inequality with coefficients `2T` and `qT(T+1)` is exactly

\[
\frac{r^2[1+T+qT(T+1)]}{A^2}
=q\,[r(2+r)]^2
+(1-q)\frac{r^2(2+r)}A. \tag{3}
\]

In particular, if `0<q<1` and `q[r(2+r)]^2>1`, this actual trajectory strictly violates the proposed terminal inequality. This is a necessary test for the universal inequality itself, independent of a chosen dual multiplier support or PSD ansatz.

## 3. Exact inherited-parameter estimate at child level six

\[

Here `c>lambda>2`, `lambda(lambda-2)=1`, and `0<1-epsilon<1`, so `0<v<1`. Substitution gives

\[

where

\[
R(\epsilon)=

For completeness, the coefficient in (5) follows by expanding

\[

\[
