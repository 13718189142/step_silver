# Whole-tail residual transport: exact rule and balanced-doubling failure

Status: self-contained unverified certificate algebra, a finite valid test, and a quantitative limitation of one explicitly specified recursion. The conclusion concerns the bound produced by this certificate rule, not the true worst case of the resulting schedules. No new numerical evaluation or verifier call.

Work at L=1. For any block h with nonnegative proof weights u on its iterates define D=sum u_i, d=x_start-x_end, U=sum u_i g_i, e=U-d, and

  E_h(u)=2sum_i u_i(f_i-f_end)+sum_i u_i||g_i||^2
         +2sum_i u_i<g_i,x_start-x_i>-||U||^2.

For every minimizer x_star, expansion gives

  ||x_start-x_star||^2+2sum_i u_i Q_(i,star)-2D(f_end-f_star)
     =||x_end-x_star-e||^2+E_h(u),

where Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0. Therefore E_h(u)>=0 implies W(h)<=1/D.

## 1. An aggregate interpolation identity across the full tail

Let a prefix finish at y, with g=grad f(y), function value f_y, and certificate state(D,e,E_a). Take a bridge step tau>=0 to y'=y-tau g. Run a tail block from y' to z. Write its nonnegative proof weights as v, its mass as D_b, its gradient aggregate as U_b, and its intrinsic energy as E_b. Summing all Q_(i,y) over the tail, with weights v_i, gives exactly

  2D_b(f_y-f_z)
   =E_b+||U_b||^2+2(tau-1)<U_b,g>+D_b||g||^2
       +2sum_i v_i(-Q_(i,y)).                        (1)

To check this, substitute y-x_i=tau g+(y'-x_i) into the Q expression. The tail terms 2sum v_i(f_i-f_z)+sum v_i||g_i||^2+2sum v_i<g_i,y'-x_i> combine to E_b+||U_b||^2; the remaining cross terms are2(tau-1)<U_b,g> and D_b||g||^2. All interpolation terms on the right of(1) are nonnegative.

Unlike the adjacent-only update, this identity uses every tail iterate and retains the actual aggregate U_b.

## 2. Exact matrix transport with a primitive tail

Assume the prefix has the residual certificate

  E_a>=nu||e-K g||^2, nu>0.                         (2)

Let b be primitive with shifted physical capacity A=1+sum(b). Use its standard proof weights v=(b_0,...,b_(ell-1),A). Then

  D_b=2A-1,
  U_b=(y'-z)+Aq, q=grad f(z),
  E_b=V_b>=0,

where V_b=2sum b_i(f_i-f_z)+sum b_i(1-b_i)||g_i||^2-(A-1)A||q||^2 is precisely its primitive potential.

Increase the old terminal proof weight by eta and scale all tail weights by beta. Require old_terminal_weight+eta>=0 and beta>=0. The new weights have mass

  D'=D+eta+beta D_b,

and their residual satisfies

  e'-Aq=e+(eta-tau)g+(beta-1)U_b.                   (3)

Direct expansion of E for the joined block gives

  E'=E_a+beta E_b+2(D+eta)(f_y-f_z)
      +eta(1-eta)||g||^2-2eta<e,g>
      +beta(1-beta)||U_b||^2
      +2beta<U_b,(tau-eta)g-e>.                     (4)

Choose c=(D+eta)/D_b>=0 and nu'>=0. Substituting(1) into(4), and subtracting nu' times the squared residual in(3), gives the exact identity

  E'-nu'||e'-Aq||^2
  =E_a-nu||e-Kg||^2+(beta+c)E_b
     +2c sum_i v_i(-Q_(i,y))+(e,g,U_b)^T M(e,g,U_b),

where the symmetric3-by3 matrix is

  M_ee=nu-nu',
  M_eg=-nu K-eta-nu'(eta-tau),
  M_eU=-beta-nu'(beta-1),
  M_gg=nu K^2+D+2eta-eta^2-nu'(eta-tau)^2,
  M_gU=c(tau-1)+beta(tau-eta)-nu'(eta-tau)(beta-1),
  M_UU=c+beta(1-beta)-nu'(beta-1)^2.

Thus M>=0 suffices for another residual certificate, with new K'=A and mass D'. In particular it gives an actual objective bound1/D'. This algebra does not assume that D=2sum(a)+1.

## 3. A closed scalar subrule and an exact finite test

Keep nu'=nu fixed and set

  k=1+1/nu, beta=1/k, eta=(tau-K)/k.

The e row and column of M then vanish. The remaining matrix is

  [ D+((K-1)^2-(tau-1)^2)/k    c(tau-1) ]
  [ c(tau-1)                  c         ].

Put X=kD, r=tau-1, and X'=kD'=X+r+1-K+D_b. Subject to c>=0, this matrix is positive semidefinite exactly when

  r^2 X'<=D_b[X+(K-1)^2].                           (5)

For c>0 this is its Schur-complement condition after multiplication by kD_b. When c=0, X'=D_b and(5) is exactly the remaining diagonal condition. The state update is K'=A.

For a genuine finite example, use the prefix a=(5/3), weights u=(3/2,3/4), D=9/4, K=1, nu=27. Its identity is

  E_a=3(-Q_10)+27||e-g_1||^2.

Choose the primitive tail b=(sqrt(2)), whose capacity is A=1+sqrt(2), and use tau=1. The primitive tail certificate is explicitly

  V_b=2(1+sqrt(2))(-Q_10)+2(-Q_01),

with local tail indices. Then eta=0, beta=27/28 and the remaining transport matrix is diagonal with entries9/4 and(9/4)/(1+2sqrt(2)), both positive. Consequently the actual three-step schedule

  h=(5/3,1,sqrt(2))

has nonnegative proof weights (3/2,3/4,27sqrt(2)/28,27(1+sqrt(2))/28), and the certified bound

  W_3(h)<=14/(45+27sqrt(2)).

This is weaker than the exact ZJ benchmark. It tests the block rule but provides no improvement.

## 4. Balanced primitive-tail doubling cannot be competitive under this subrule

Let lambda=1+sqrt(2), and start with any finite valid state of length1. At round j append a bridge and the standard silver primitive tail of the same length n_j=2^(j+1)-1, so n_(j+1)=2n_j+1. Its shifted capacity is A_j=lambda^(j+1), obtained by repeated ConPP using F(x,x)=lambda x. Use the fixed-nu subrule of part3 with any parameters satisfying its actual weight and PSD conditions.

For j>=1 the reset K_(j+1)=A_j gives K_j=lambda^j=A_j/lambda. Write X_j=kD_j. The recurrence and(5) become

  X_(j+1)=X_j+2A_j+r_j-K_j,
  r_j^2 X_(j+1)<=(2A_j-1)[X_j+(K_j-1)^2],
  X_(j+1)>=2A_j-1.                                 (6)

The last inequality follows from c>=0. Hence r_j<=sqrt(X_j+(K_j-1)^2), and since K_j>=1,

  X_(j+1)<=X_j+2A_j+sqrt(X_j).

Choose M sufficiently large that X_1<=M A_1 and(lambda-1)M>=2+sqrt(M). Induction gives X_j<=M A_j, so x_j=X_j/A_j is bounded. Let L=limsup_j x_j. If L=0 there is nothing to prove. Otherwise take a subsequence with x_(j+1)->L. Put t_j=r_j/A_j. From(6),

  lambda x_(j+1)=x_j+2-1/lambda+t_j,
  t_j^2 lambda x_(j+1)
    <=(2-1/A_j)[x_j/A_j+(1/lambda-1/A_j)^2].         (7)

Along a further subsequence x_j converges to a number at most L; thus t_j has a limit at least(lambda-1)L-2+1/lambda. Passing to the limit in(7) yields

  L*[(lambda-1)L-2+1/lambda]_+^2<=2/lambda^3.        (8)

Let L_0 be the unique solution, above(2-1/lambda)/(lambda-1), of the corresponding equality. The left side is strictly increasing on that interval, so L<=L_0. At L=sqrt(2), its value times lambda^3 equals lambda sqrt(2)=2+sqrt(2)>2; hence

  limsup_j D_j/lambda^(j+1)<=L_0/k<sqrt(2)/k.        (9)

The exact benchmark along these same dyadic horizons has liminf B_(n_j)/lambda^(j+1)>=2. A short scalar proof avoids importing any phase theorem. A complete balanced primitive tree with2^K unit leaves has capacity lambda^K. Replace one leaf by zero and collapse its parent using F(x,0)=x; the resulting legal tree has2^K-1 leaves. The map F is2-Lipschitz in each coordinate (differentiate its explicit formula, whose partial derivatives lie in[1,2]), so this single leaf replacement decreases the root by at most2^K. Thus P_(2^K-2)>=lambda^K-2^K. The admissible dominant split with that prefix and an empty tail gives

  B_(2^K-1)>=G(P_(2^K-2),1)>=2lambda^K-2^(K+1).

Since2/lambda<1, the claimed normalized lower limit follows. Combining with(9), the value1/D_j certified by this transport satisfies

  liminf_j (1/D_j)/U_(n_j)>=2k/L_0>sqrt(2)k>1.

Therefore this specific whole-tail rule, under the displayed constant-nu and equal-horizon recursion, cannot certify an improvement even over the basic primitive rate. This does not lower-bound W(h_j), does not exclude other parameter recursions, and does not show that all residual certificates fail.

## 5. What the next block interface must retain

The aggregate-only expression depends on(e,g,U_b) but not independently on q. Therefore a positive new residual coefficient forces K'=A unless another q-dependent certificate is retained: subtracting nu'||e'-K'q||^2 would otherwise give a negative q^2 coefficient in a variable absent from the original matrix. This explains the forced reset in the tested rule.

A source-supported extra piece is the primitive dual residual

  2(f_start-f_end)-2(A-1)||q||^2
     >=||g_start-(d_b+q)/A||^2.                     (10)

The proof in Grimmer–Shu–Wang arXiv:2410.16249, AppendixA.1 Lemma17, was read in full; before adding the optimum interpolation inequality it gives exactly(10), using the primitive potential plus nonnegative interpolation sums. That derivation uses the primitive potential itself and A=1+sum(b), not an assumed equality of primitive and dominant ambient classes. A future proof may rederive this combination explicitly rather than appeal to the final gradient-norm statement. No verifier promotion is claimed here. The next test is to retain both this dual residual and the objective residual through a join, with the bridge gradient and tail initial gradient distinct. A coarse sum of the bridge descent estimate and(10) has not recovered the basic large silver bridge, so it is not yet a replacement recursion.
