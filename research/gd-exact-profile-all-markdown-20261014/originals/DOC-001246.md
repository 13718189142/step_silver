## 2. Full certificate and the actual primitive energy

Indices i,j run from 0 to 4. Put e=(1,1,1,1,1)^T,
P_i=sum_{t<i} h_t, P_0=0, P_4=s. Let T have diagonal 1,
T_it=h_t for t<i, and zero above the diagonal. Define

    omega=(-2h_0,-2h_1,-2h_2,-2h_3,2s)^T,
    D=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_3(2-h_3),-s(s+2)),
    L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    M=D+sym(LT),          sym(A)=(A+A^T)/2.

The required full certificate has Lambda_ii=0,

    Lambda_ij>=0 (i!=j),       L^T e=omega,       M>=0.     (5)

Here M>=0 means positive semidefinite on all of R^5. For actual
smooth convex data with smoothness 1 and x_{i+1}=x_i-h_i g_i, put

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    V_h=2 sum_{i<4} h_i(f_i-f_4)
        +sum_{i<4} h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_4||^2.

The accepted full-space identity is

    V_h+sum_{i!=j} Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g_0,...,g_4)>.

Every actual smooth convex trajectory has Q_ij<=0. Thus (5) implies
V_h>=0 on every such trajectory. We use only this sufficient-certificate
implication; no unproved dual attainment or certificate necessity enters.

For data satisfying f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2, expansion of the GD
update gives

    sum_{i<4}[2h_i<x_i,g_i>-h_i^2||g_i||^2]
       =||x_0||^2-||x_4||^2.

Substitution in V_h then gives

    V_h=||x_0||^2-||x_4+s g_4||^2.                        (6)

When x_4=g_4, this is ||x_0||^2-C^2||g_4||^2.

## 3. Every needed cut inequality, including boundary cases

Fix j in {0,1,2,3}. Let v=h_j, let p=sum_{i<j} h_i and
r=sum_{i>j} h_i. If v>1, set alpha=(v-1)/(1+r)>0 and define

    f_alpha(x)=x-1/2                    for x>=1,
               x^2/2                   for -alpha<=x<=1,
               -alpha*x-alpha^2/2      for x<=-alpha.

Its derivative clamp(x,[-alpha,1]) is continuous, nondecreasing,
and 1-Lipschitz. This is one convex differentiable 1-smooth function,
with unique minimizer 0. Start at x_0=1+p. The gradients through
query j are all 1 and x_j=1. After its step,
x_{j+1}=-(1+r)alpha. Every remaining step has gradient -alpha,
and the terminal point is x_4=g_4=-alpha. Nonnegative step lengths
ensure all intermediate points stay on the stated pieces, including
zero-step repetitions. Every sampled value has the form used in (6).

Consequently

    V_h=(1+p)^2-C^2*(v-1)^2/(1+r)^2.

Any full certificate must therefore obey

    C*(h_j-1)_+ <= (1+sum_{i<j}h_i)(1+sum_{i>j}h_i).       (7)

For h_j<=1 the inequality holds automatically; no division by
h_j-1 was used in that case. Strict failure of (7) is an explicit
negative-energy function, with every original interpolation pair legal.

The actual quadratic f(x)=x^2/2, started at 1, has
x_i=g_i=product_{t<i}(1-h_t). Formula (6) gives

    V_h=1-C^2*product_{i=0}^3(1-h_i)^2.

Hence every full certificate also requires

    C*|product_{i=0}^3(1-h_i)| <= 1.                      (8)

Both (7) and (8) were proved for the original nonnegative-step domain.
Neither uses a second formal kernel or assumes equality in (8).

## 4. A coupled endpoint bound from the two middle cuts

Assume (1). Use a,b,c,d as in Section 1, so

    2/5<=a,d<=1/2,    C=5+a+b+c+d.

At this point b and c have not been assumed positive. Define

    A=2+a,   D_end=2+d,   t=a+d,   S=b+c=C-5-t.

The middle cuts (7) imply Cb<=A(3+c+d) and
Cc<=D_end(3+a+b), since replacing a positive part by the underlying
real number only weakens their left sides. As
3+c+d=C-A-b and 3+a+b=C-D_end-c, these are equivalent to

    b <= A(C-A)/(C+A),
    c <= D_end(C-D_end)/(C+D_end).                        (9)

All denominators are positive. For fixed C>=193/25, the function
z(C-z)/(C+z) has derivative

    (C^2-2Cz-z^2)/(C+z)^2.

On 12/5<=z<=5/2, we have z/C<1/3 because C>15/2; the derivative
is positive since 1-2(z/C)-(z/C)^2>2/9. Thus (9) yields

    b,c <= U(C),      U(C)=(5/2)*(C-5/2)/(C+5/2).          (10)

Put K(C)=C-5-U(C). Both U and K increase strictly on this capacity
range: U'(C)=(25/2)/(C+5/2)^2 is positive and less than 1/8,
so K'(C)>7/8. At C_0=193/25 their exact values are

    U(C_0)=1305/1022 > 51/40,
    K(C_0)=36871/25550 > 36/25.                           (11)

For the first strict comparison the cross-product difference is
1305*40-1022*51=78. For the second it is
36871-36*1022=79. Therefore, at every C>=C_0,

    U(C)>51/40,       K(C)>36/25.

Since 4/5<=t<=1, the sum constraint and (10) imply

    b=S-c>=S-U=K-t>11/25>0,
    c=S-b>=S-U=K-t>11/25>0.                              (12)

In particular the two middle steps are strictly greater than 1 as a
consequence, not a hypothesis. Every zero-step boundary has been handled.

Multiplying the nonnegative factors U-b and U-c gives

    0 <= (U-b)(U-c)=U^2-U*S+bc,
    bc >= U(S-U)=U(K-t).                                (13)

To keep the endpoint product coupled to their sum, use both elementary
inequalities

    (a-2/5)(d-2/5)>=0,      (1/2-a)(1/2-d)>=0.

They imply

    ad >= (2/5)t-4/25,
    ad >= t/2-1/4.                                       (14)

The first lower bound is larger for 4/5<=t<=9/10; the second is
larger for 9/10<=t<=1. Each is positive on its stated interval.
On the first interval the product

    ((2/5)t-4/25)*(36/25-t)

is a concave quadratic, so its minimum is attained at one of the two
ends. The two values are 64/625 and 27/250. On the second interval
the same argument for

    (t/2-1/4)*(36/25-t)

gives endpoint values 27/250 and 11/100. Both latter values exceed
64/625. Equations (11), (14), and positivity therefore give

    ad*(K-t) > 64/625.                                  (15)

Now use (12)--(15), keeping all multiplication factors positive:

    C*a*b*c*d >= C*U(C)*ad*(K(C)-t)
               > (193/25)*(51/40)*(64/625)
               = 78744/78125 > 1.                       (16)

All four shifts are positive, so (16) contradicts (8). If either
middle cut did not hold, Section 3 had already supplied a legal negative
energy witness. This proves the full statement, including (2)--(3).

The capacity was arbitrary throughout C>=193/25. Monotonicity in
(11) justifies comparison to its lower endpoint; no fixed-capacity

## 6. An additional necessary inequality from the full PSD endpoint block

Here h is any nonnegative four-step word with
u=h_0>1, v=h_3>0, s>v, and a full certificate (5). Set

    delta_u=2C-(C+1)u,
    delta_v=2C-(C+1)v,
    B_end=s-v.

Then

    2u(u-1)v B_end
       <= u*delta_u*B_end^2+(u-1)^2*s^2*v*delta_v.         (18)

All signs and PSD directions are retained. In particular, simultaneous
saturation delta_u=delta_v=0 is impossible under the stated positive
denominators. No claim of independence from all earlier witness tests
is made for (18).

To prove it, first obtain the constant kernel without an assumption.
The identity sum h_i P_i=(s^2-sum h_i^2)/2 and Te=e+P give
e^T M e=0 whenever the flow holds. PSD then forces Me=0: inserting
e+tau z into the quadratic form for both signs of small tau forces
z^T Me=0 for every z. Its row equations are

    sum_j Lambda_ij(P_j-P_i)=2h_i(1+P_i), i<4,
    sum_{j<4}Lambda_4j(s-P_j)=2sC.                        (19)

Let z_j=Lambda_0j for 1<=j<=4, y_j=Lambda_4j for 0<=j<=3,
m_0=M_00, and m_4=M_44. Put Z=sum_{j=1}^3 z_j. The initial
moment and its diagonal yield exactly

    s*m_0=u*delta_u-(u-1)*sum_{j=1}^3(s-P_j)z_j.

Because s-P_j>=v and all z_j>=0,

    Z <= (u*delta_u-s*m_0)/((u-1)v),
    z_4=(2u-sum_{j=1}^3 P_j z_j)/s
       >=2u/s-B_end*(u*delta_u-s*m_0)/(s*(u-1)v).        (20)

The terminal diagonal is m_4=sum_{j<4}y_j-s(s+2).
Its moment in (19) gives

    v*m_4+sum_{j=0}^2(P_3-P_j)y_j=s*delta_v.

Since P_3=B_end and all y_j are nonnegative,

    y_0 <= (s*delta_v-v*m_4)/B_end.                      (21)

Directly from the full matrix definition,
2M_04=(u-1)y_0-z_4. Its principal 2-by-2 PSD condition therefore
implies z_4<=(u-1)y_0+2sqrt(m_0*m_4). Combine (20)--(21):

    L_0+A_end*m_0+m_4/A_end <=2sqrt(m_0*m_4),
    A_end=B_end/((u-1)v)>0,
    L_0=2u/s-B_end*u*delta_u/(s*(u-1)v)
                   -(u-1)*s*delta_v/B_end.

The difference A_end*m_0+m_4/A_end-2sqrt(m_0*m_4) is
(sqrt(A_end*m_0)-sqrt(m_4/A_end))^2>=0. Hence L_0<=0.
Multiplication by the positive s*(u-1)*v*B_end proves (18).
This proof includes m_0=0 or m_4=0 without dividing by either.

## 7. Audit of the full-support PSD/skew interface used for construction

The purpose of this section is to check the fragile identities in
443f4b4325a9901d, not to claim a second coordinate reformulation as
the outcome. The substantive regional exclusion is Sections 1--5.

Assume s>0. Choose mu strictly between the last distinct nonterminal
prefix P_k with h_k>0 and s. Put phi_i=1+P_i,

    w_i=2h_i/(mu-P_i) when h_i>0, w_i=0 when h_i=0 (i<4),
    w_4=2s/(s-mu), W=sum_i w_i,
    Lambda^c_ij=phi_i*w_i*w_j/W for i!=j.

All nonzero weights are positive. Summing w_i(P_i-mu) gives zero,
so sum w_i P_i=mu W and sum phi_i w_i=(1+mu)W. Therefore
out_i-in_i=w_i(P_i-mu)=omega_i, with this exact flow sign.
Also sum_j Lambda^c_ij(P_j-P_i)=phi_i w_i(mu-P_i), which is
the row moment in (19). Thus M_c e=0; no PSD conclusion follows.
When all h_i>0 the twenty coefficients are strictly positive. When
some h_i=0, their formal coordinates still remain in the matrices.

Let e_i be the standard columns of R^5. Let E have columns e_i-e_4,
i=0,...,3, and let F have columns

    e_j-(1-P_j/s)e_0-(P_j/s)e_4, j=1,2,3.

E is a basis of e-perp. F has three independent middle coordinate
columns and is a basis of span(e,P)-perp, including at repeated
prefixes. An arbitrary homogeneous flow/kernel perturbation Delta L
must satisfy Delta L e=0, Delta L^T e=0, Delta L P=0. Conversely
these equations preserve flow and the kernel. They are equivalent to

    Delta L=E X F^T, X an arbitrary real 4-by-3 matrix.

The signs are precisely

    (E X F^T)_ij <= Lambda^c_ij, every i!=j.               (22)

Let B be the 5-by-4 matrix with columns e_0,...,e_3. Put
R=F^T T B, K_c=B^T M_c B. Since B^T E=I,

    K=K_c+sym(XR).

The constant kernel determines the full matrix as

    M=[K,-K*1; -1^T*K,1^T*K*1].                          (23)

Its quadratic form is (g_0-g_4,...,g_3-g_4)^T K times that same
column. Hence full M>=0 is equivalent to K>=0. This is a full-space
identity, without imposing any equality between formal gradients.

Define p_0=1, p_{i+1}=(1-h_i)p_i and
q=(p_0-p_4,...,p_3-p_4)^T. Direct substitution gives Tp=e and
T^{-1}P=e-p. Since s>0, p and e are independent. Therefore
ker(F^T T)=span(e,p); intersecting with the subspace having last
coordinate zero shows ker R=span(q), q!=0, and rank R=3.
This proof remains valid at zero steps and at h_i=1.

The telescope h_i(2-h_i)p_i^2=p_i^2-p_{i+1}^2 yields

    q^T K_c q=p^T M_c p=1-C^2 p_4^2=:c_quad.

For a proposed symmetric K with q^T Kq=c_quad, let H=K-K_c and

    S_0=[q(Hq)^T-(Hq)q^T]/(q^T q).

The sign check is S_0 q=-Hq, because q^T Hq=0. Every real completion
of the symmetric equation, with no signs imposed yet, is

    X=(H+S_0+Z)R^T(RR^T)^{-1},
    Z^T=-Z, Zq=0.                                       (24)

Indeed A=H+S_0+Z annihilates q, and R^T(RR^T)^{-1}R is the
orthogonal projection onto q-perp, so XR=A. Conversely XR-H is
skew and has value -Hq on q, leaving exactly such a Z. The skew
operators on the three-dimensional q-perp have three independent
coordinates. No rank assumption on K entered.

At c_quad>0 no second kernel is forced. At c_quad=0, and only with
K>=0, the identity q^T Kq=0 forces Kq=0 by the same quadratic-form
argument used for e. Choosing a calibrated PSD K does not establish
