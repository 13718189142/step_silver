# Joint suffix deformation with exact PSD preservation

## Full certificate and the additional triangular hypothesis

Normalize L=1 and x_star=g_star=f_star=0. Let h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)) be
nonnegative and finite. For distinct indices in I={star,0,...,n}, set

Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2.

Assume a complete upper certificate (q,lambda,h): q>0, lambda_ij>=0,
out_i-in_i=2 1_(i=n) at finite indices, and the quadratic form

M=q||x_0||^2-2f_n+sum_(i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ij

after x_j=x_0-sum_(t<j)h_t g_t is positive semidefinite. It is a
quadratic form in the independent formal vectors (x_0,g_0,...,g_n);
flow cancels every function-value coefficient. Thus 2f_n<=q||x_0||^2
on every legal trajectory.

For this candidate ONLY, additionally assume

lambda_ij=0 whenever 0<=i<j<=n.                         (T)

All finite-index multiplier flow goes backward. Star edges may go either
way. Write mu_j=lambda_(j,star), nu_j=lambda_(star,j), and, for t<j,

c_(t,j)=mu_j+sum_(l=0)^t lambda_(j,l).

Every c is nonnegative. The complete matrix entries are

M_(x_0,x_0)=q,   M_(x_0,g_j)=-mu_j/2,
M_(g_j,g_j)=out_j-1_(j=n),
2 M_(g_t,g_j)=h_t c_(t,j)-lambda_(j,t),   t<j.          (1)

To check (1), the squared-gradient terms contribute
(out_j+in_j)/2=out_j-1_(j=n) on the diagonal. A backward
edge j->t contributes -lambda_(j,t)<g_j,g_t> from the squared
difference, and h_t times the total flow from j to star or an index at
most t contributes h_t c_(t,j)<g_j,g_t>. The star pair contributes
-mu_j<x_0,g_j>. This accounts for every term; no principal submatrix
replaces M.

## Deformation

Fix k<n with nu_k>0. Define C=sum_(j>k)c_(k,j). By summing flow over
{k+1,...,n}, hypothesis (T) gives C=2+sum_(j>k)nu_j>0.
Put p_j=c_(k,j)/C for j>k, so p_j>=0 and sum p_j=1.

Assume the following explicit proportionality condition on later cuts:

for every k<t<n there is r_t>=0 such that
p_j=r_t c_(t,j) for all j>t.                           (P)

Zero p entries are allowed. If all p_j vanish beyond t, one may take
r_t=0. Set r_k=1/C.

Choose any 0<eta<=nu_k. Change only these multipliers:

nu'_k=nu_k-eta,
nu'_j=nu_j+eta p_j             (j>k),
lambda'_(j,k)=lambda_(j,k)+eta p_j   (j>k).             (2)

Keep every mu_j and every other finite edge unchanged. Change the schedule by

h'_t=h_t                                      (t<k),
h'_k=(h_k+eta r_k)/(1+eta r_k),
h'_t=h_t/(1+eta r_t)                           (k<t<n). (3)

All new steps and multipliers are nonnegative. At k the increased
incoming backward flow eta cancels the removed incoming star flow eta;
at j>k the new outgoing edge eta p_j cancels the new incoming star
edge eta p_j. Thus every flow equation stays exact.

The NEW full matrix, on the same formal vector coordinates, satisfies

M(q,lambda',h') =
M(q,lambda,h)+eta sum_(j>k)p_j ||g_j||^2.               (4)

Proof: diagonal j>k increases by eta p_j, while all x_0 entries and
all other diagonals stay fixed. For t<k, c_(t,j), h_t and lambda_(j,t)
are unchanged. At t=k,

h'_k(c_(k,j)+eta p_j)-(lambda_(j,k)+eta p_j)
=h_k c_(k,j)-lambda_(j,k),

using p_j=r_k c_(k,j). At t>k the edge lambda_(j,t) is unchanged and

h'_t(c_(t,j)+eta p_j)=h_t c_(t,j),

using (P). Therefore EVERY mixed entry is unchanged, proving (4).
In particular the whole new matrix remains PSD, including every old
kernel direction. No forward edge is created and no preexisting edge
is decreased except the explicitly available star multiplier nu_k.

## Strict worst-case improvement

Identity (4) gives, on every trajectory of the NEW schedule,

q R^2-2f_n >= eta sum_(j>k)p_j ||g_j||^2,               (5)

where R=||x_0||>0. Here the old M on the right of (4) is PSD on arbitrary
formal vectors; the new transcript need not be a trajectory of the old
schedule.

Set B=product_(t=0)^(n-1)(1+h'_t) and
D_j=product_(t=j)^(n-1)(1+h'_t), with D_n=1.
These finite positive quantities are deliberately crude.

Since g_star=0 and the gradient is 1-Lipschitz, ||g_t||<=||x_t||, hence
||x_n||<=B R. Convexity gives f_n<=<g_n,x_n><=B R||g_n||.
The same Lipschitz property gives
||g_(t+1)||<=||g_t||+||x_(t+1)-x_t||=(1+h'_t)||g_t||,
so ||g_n||<=D_j||g_j||.

Writing E=2f_n/R^2, these inequalities and (5) yield

q-E >= gamma E^2,
gamma=eta/(4B^2) sum_(j>k) p_j/D_j^2 >0.              (6)

Consequently the actual fixed-length new schedule obeys

W_n(h') <= 2q/[1+sqrt(1+4 gamma q)] < q.               (7)

This is a uniform bound over every dimension and every legal function,
not a calculation on the old active instance. Neither stationarity nor
q=1/(2sum h+1) was used. In particular, if the old certificate is tight
at a globally minimizing schedule, hypotheses (T),(P),nu_k>0 cannot
hold simultaneously.

## Two remaining steps: condition (P) is automatic

Take k=n-2. Define

c_1=mu_(n-1)+sum_(l<=n-2)lambda_(n-1,l),
c_2=mu_n+sum_(l<=n-2)lambda_(n,l),
c_3=mu_n+sum_(l<=n-1)lambda_(n,l)=2+nu_n>0.

Then C=c_1+c_2, p_(n-1)=c_1/C, p_n=c_2/C, and
r_(n-1)=p_n/c_3 satisfies (P), including c_2=0. Formula (3) changes
BOTH of the final two steps:

h'_(n-2)=(C h_(n-2)+eta)/(C+eta),
h'_(n-1)=h_(n-1)c_3/(c_3+eta p_n).

The complete slack gains exactly
eta p_(n-1)||g_(n-1)||^2+eta p_n||g_n||^2.
Thus for a triangular tight certificate at a global minimizer,
nu_(n-2) must vanish. At horizon n=2 this excludes nu_0>0 under (T).
This is a genuine earlier-step joint deformation, not the already
recorded final-step convex blend.

## Precise limitation

For k<t<n, if positive ratios p_j/c_(t,j) differ as j varies, no single
h'_t can satisfy h'_t(c_(t,j)+eta p_j)=h_t c_(t,j) for all j>t when
h_t>0. Thus this one-column transport cannot simply be declared to
work on arbitrary longer suffixes. Additional multiplier changes,
a non-diagonal PSD residual, or a different mechanism would be needed.

Nor is (T) established for arbitrary optimal certificates. Forward
edges produce additional mixed terms when suffix steps change.
The candidate does NOT establish global Huber tightness or the
Zhang--Jiang exact-profile lower bound.

## Hostile checks within the derivation

- No division by h_t, h_t-1, p_j, or nu_j for j>k is used.
  Zero steps, zero weights, and steps on either side of1 are covered.
- If h_k=1, that coordinate stays fixed; positive later r_t can still
  change the suffix. If every affected coordinate were unchanged,
  (5)--(7) would show that q was not a tight certificate.
- Even p_n=0 leaves gamma>0, since some earlier p_j is positive and
  D_j is finite.
- The old matching primal is never reused as if it satisfied new GD
  recursions. Uniform legality enters only in (5)--(7).
