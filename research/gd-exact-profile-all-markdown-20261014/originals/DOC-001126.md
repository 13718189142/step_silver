Let n>=1, h>=0, and let lambda>=0 cancel every value in q||x0||^2-2fn+sum lambda_ik Q_ik for a finite real q, with all original gradients independent and xi=x0-sum_(t<i)h_t gt. The necessary finite value flow is out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n). Suppose h_j>0 and every interpolation coefficient incident to j is0. Put T_cut={j+1,...,n}, and let C_out,C_in be the actual multiplier sums crossing this cut in the two directions, optimum included in its complement. Summing the value flow over T_cut gives C_out-C_in=2, hence C_out>=2. Set scalar formal x0=0, g_i=0 for i<j, g_j=t, g_i=1 for i>j. No incident edge uses the gradient g_j, so every norm-difference term is independent of t. All positions inside T_cut acquire the common term -h_j t, while earlier/optimum positions do not. Thus the only t terms are from edges sourced inside T_cut and targeting its complement, each contributing +lambda_ik h_j t. Every other edge either has equal position shifts or source gradient0. The FULL polynomial is M(t)=M(0)+h_j C_out t. Choosing t=-(|M(0)|+1)/(h_j C_out) gives M(t)<=-1. Therefore no such full PSD certificate exists. This is a formal certificate-support obstruction, not a legal GD lower witness. It permits arbitrary q>Huber and arbitrary optimum-source multipliers. It does not require tightness or a positive-definite remainder, and it gives no obstruction to preserving some incident edges during a bulk re-selection.
