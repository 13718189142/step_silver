**Not solved.** Neither the exact uniform all-schedule lower bound nor a
certified asymptotic improving family has been established. The proposed
critical word now has finite numerical behavior beyond the Huber witness,
so an all-depth Huber extrapolation is especially unjustified.

The fixed target is W_n^*/U_n^ZJ ->1, or a rigorous disproof. Here

Central missing lemma: for some explicit predetermined schedule family and

**Failure mode if ignored.** Numerical feasibility or a small dual gap is
mistaken for an exact certificate; finite behavior is extrapolated to infinity.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

## 4. Missing bridge lemmas

### Bridge 1 — An exact scalable non-Huber witness
**Target statement.** Give an explicit finite-dimensional legal trajectory
and one smooth convex function for each horizon of a concrete unbounded
family, with a quantitatively useful payoff against exact B_n or the tested
critical upper candidate. The evidence suggests signed/correlated gradients;
no particular limiting rank or sign pattern is assumed.
**Prerequisites.** All ordered pairs, a true minimizer, initial distance,
predetermined steps and a fully specified common value array.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290 supplies realization/completion.
**Missing checks.** Exact reconstruction of a generating pattern and uniform
payoff. A finite reconstructed witness is only a structural starting point.
**Closure criterion.** A full exact family and proved constants; to resolve
the original affirmative target it must further cover EVERY h and horizon.
Independent of Bridge2.

### Bridge 2 — Uniform full-array continuation
**Target statement.** Construct nonnegative complete arrays on an explicit
deformed schedule with q_m B_(n_m)<=1-delta eventually, for one fixed delta>0.
**Prerequisites.** A valid all-depth baseline, full PSD and true objective scale.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290 and the exact benchmark definitions;
include the unverified C54 scalar proof if that comparison is used.
**Missing checks.** Actual feasible coefficient path and horizon-uniform
control, including singular and newly activated edges.
**Closure criterion.** An explicit complete certificate family, not a tangent
direction or finite solver output. Independent alternative to Bridge1.

### Bridge 3 — Quantitative correlated increment/height policy
**Target statement.** On the proper increment cone or joint positive-height
quotient, supply one explicit correlated generating policy, complete legality
and exact target-scale payoff, or a scoped exact cost obstruction showing
why that policy cannot deliver it.
**Prerequisites.** One joint object rather than independent pair/modal solutions.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290, b98bb807d514e80d and the scoped
orthogonal obstruction0e7e78056dc6258c as a calibration warning.
**Missing checks.** The quantitative policy and its constant-preserving
iteration; a generic covariance parameterization is not sufficient.
**Closure criterion.** A genuine explicit construction or definite obstruction
that changes the next mechanism. The original all-h comparison stays open
until separately proved. This can support Bridge1 in parallel with Bridge2.
