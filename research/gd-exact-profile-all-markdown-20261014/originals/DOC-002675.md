In R^(m+2) take an orthonormal basis e_0,...,e_(m+1). Fix r=-2/5 and set

Then ||u_i||=1 and <u_i,u_j>=r^|i-j|, directly by induction. These are the block-start points, not independent sampled curvature matrices.

w_i=(u_i-u_(i+1))/M_i,0<=i<=m.
Choose eta=1/50 and define
J(z)=max{0, max_(0<=i<=m)(<w_i,z>-eta)},
f(x)=min_z {J(z)+||x-z||^2/2}.                           (2)

This follows directly from the subgradient optimality condition for the strongly convex minimization; monotonicity of the two subgradients gives the global1-Lipschitz gradient bound.

Start from x_0=u_0, so the initial squared distance from minimizer0 is1.
The intended two queries in block i are u_i and u_i-w_i, both with gradient w_i. Their two steps then end exactly at

## 4. Growing dimension, temporal correlations and nonseparability
The stage gradient energies are2(1-r)/M_i^2, so they vary with the b_i. For j=i+k>i,
<w_i,w_j>=-(1-r)^2 r^(k-1)/(M_i M_j).                    (10)
They are nonzero at every separation and alternate in sign. The two equal gradients inside each block add positive temporal correlations. The Gram matrix is explicitly PSD because(1) constructs real vectors, not because of a guessed covariance condition.

The normals w_0,...,w_m are linearly independent: the coefficient of the new basis vector e_(i+1) in w_i is nonzero, while all earlier normals lack that coordinate. The number of independent sampled gradient directions therefore grows with m.

The Moreau envelope is genuinely nonseparable in any fixed orthonormal coordinates. For each i, linear independence of the normals allows a point z satisfying <w_i,z>=eta and <w_j,z><eta for j!=i. Around x=z+theta w_i,0<theta<1, the local envelope is that of the single hinge max{0,<w_i,z>-eta}, and its Hessian is the projector w_i w_i^T/||w_i||^2. Neighboring normals are neither orthogonal nor parallel by(1),(10), so these two projectors do not commute. A function separable in one orthonormal basis has commuting Hessians at all points where they exist. This proves the claim.

This family is thus outside both the scalar constructions and the exact orthogonality hypotheses of fact0e7e78056dc6258c.
