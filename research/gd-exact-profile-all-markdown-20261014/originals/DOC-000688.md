# Chronological kernel: an explicit local square decomposition

Status: **unverified proof draft**, fifth launch, first continuation round.
There is a complete algebraic factorization below, including the constant
gradient column and the original initial-position variable. Its coefficient
signs have passed an outward-interval evaluation at the actual depth128.
This is not a uniform proof for all m>=128, a verifier-accepted fact, or a
solution of the original minimax problem. No fact_submit was allocated or used.

The new theoretical question is the sign of explicitly displayed scalar
coefficients in the birth coordinates. This is a sufficient condition for
the old covariance inequality; equivalence of their positivity is NOT claimed.

## 1. Original word, shared data, and exact certificate identity

For an integer m>=128 set epsilon=1/m, lambda=1+sqrt(2),
c=lambda(1+epsilon), a=(1-epsilon)/c, d_j=1+a*c^j, and
H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,d_j,H_j). Let q=2^m-2 and let h_0,...,h_(q-1)
be H_m with its final d_0 removed. Define s_i=sum_(r<i)h_r and S=s_q.
For real vectors v,w_0,...,w_(m-1), all using one arbitrary common Gram,
put p_ij=product_(r<i)(1-h_r/d_j), g_i=v+sum_j p_ij*w_j, and
x_i=x_0-sum_(r<i)h_r*g_r. The first d_j kills its column at query2^j.
Thus g_q=v and x_q=x_0-S*v-sum_j d_j*w_j.

For one common array of sampled values and the zero optimum jet, use
Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2. Necessity and sufficiency
of the entire interpolation array are in accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290.
Define, purely algebraically,

 J=sum_(i<q) [2s_(i+1)h_i<g_(i+1),g_i>
             +s_(i+1)||g_(i+1)-g_i||^2+h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2].

The GD norm identity and summation by parts give

 ||x_0||^2-2S*f_q+2sum_i h_i Q_(i,star)
                  +2sum_i s_(i+1)Q_(i+1,i)=||x_q||^2+J.       (1)

Indeed the optimum terms equal
2sum h_i f_i-||x_0||^2+||x_q||^2+sum h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2;
the chain terms equal 2S f_q-2sum h_i f_i plus the first two terms of J.
All selected multipliers are nonnegative. No unused original interpolation
constraint is deleted from the definition of legal data.

Let T be the symmetric matrix of J in (w_0,...,w_(m-1),v). For a word W,
put P_W(z)=product_(h in W)(1-hz) and S_W=sum W. The zero-clock kernels
K,T satisfy, for concatenated words U,V,

 K_UV=K_U+P_U(z)P_U(w)K_V,
 T_UV=T_U+P_U(z)P_U(w)[T_V+2S_U K_V],                         (2)
 K_[h]=h-h^2(z+w-zw)/2,
 T_[h]=h(h+1)-h^3(z+w-zw).

These follow by separating the defining time sums, retaining the offset
S_U in every second-block clock. At z=1/d_j and z=0 these compute the
actual matrix with O(m^3) scalar operations, without enumerating2^m steps.
Deletion of the last d_0 changes only T_(v,v), to S(S+1), because all
residual columns are already zero. This final diagonal is retained below.

For comparison with the earlier covariance formulation, D_i=g_i-g_(i+1),
A_ij=h_i*p_ij/d_j, beta_i=s_(i+1)(s_i+1), beta_q=S(S+1), and
c_i=(h_i-1)s_(i+1). Expansion of J and completion of the constant square give

 J=beta_q||v+sum_i beta_i D_i/beta_q||^2
       +sum_(i,l)[beta_min(i,l)-beta_i beta_l/beta_q]<D_i,D_l>
       -sum_i c_i||D_i||^2.                                    (3)

For example the coefficient of ||g_i||^2 after collecting adjacent terms
is beta_i-beta_(i-1); this proves (3) by writing g_i=v+sum_(r>=i)D_r.
Consequently positivity of T is exactly the old covariance domination,
including its constant-mode charge. This is only an equivalence of formal
quadratic forms, not a test of whether a negative formal direction is legal.

## 2. Lossless birth coordinates and exact sparsity

Define phi_k(z)=product_(r<k)(1-d_r*z),
V_k=v+sum_j phi_k(1/d_j)w_j for0<=k<=m, and U_k=V_k-v.
In particular V_m=v and U_m=0. The triangular node-evaluation matrix has
positive diagonal, so these coordinates lose no modal direction.

The identity
(1-hz)phi_k=(1-h/d_k)phi_k+(h/d_k)phi_(k+1)
gives g_i=sum_k B_ik V_k, where B starts at the point mass at0 and
at step h moves mass from state k to k+1 with fraction h/d_k.
An occupied state has d_k>=h. Lower states have already been killed by
their first physical level. Thus B_ik>=0, sum_k B_ik=1, and

 B_ik=0 if k>i or i>=2^k, for k<m.                           (4)

The first zero follows because one step advances at most one state; the
second follows by induction on first physical occurrences.

Set xi_j=d_j*w_j, z=(S+1)v, and y_k=d_k U_k. Then y=R*xi, with

 R_kj=(d_k/d_j)phi_k(1/d_j) for j>=k; R_kj=0 for j<k.

Let M=diag(1/d_0,...,1/d_(m-1),1/(S+1)) T diag(same).
Define the symmetric matrix

 H=diag(R^(-T),1) M diag(R^(-1),1)
       = [ E, ell ; ell^T, D ],       D=S/(S+1).               (5)

Thus J=sum_kl E_kl<y_k,y_l>+2sum_k ell_k<y_k,z>+D||z||^2.
All scalings are positive and R is invertible. The vectors remain in one
real Euclidean space, with their original cross terms.

There are useful EXACT zeros:

                 E_kl=0 if l>2^k and k<l<m.                  (6)

Each term in J uses only g_i and g_(i+1). If its state-k coefficient is
nonzero, (4) forces i+1<=2^k. All states at either query are then <=2^k,
so state l>2^k cannot enter that term. In particular rows0,1 have no
off-diagonal entries beyond columns1,2 respectively. Numerical arithmetic
may be replaced by zero at precisely these positions because of this proof;
no other small entries are discarded.

## 3. Exact square-factor lemma, with the complete constant load

The following is an algebraic identity for ANY symmetric real m-by-m matrix
E and real column ell, with m>=3. Define

 e_i=E_(i,i+2),                    0<=i<=m-3,
 b_i=E_(i,i+1)-2(e_(i-1)+e_i),    0<=i<=m-2,
 r_i=E_ii-E_(i-1,i)-E_(i,i+1)+e_(i-2)+e_i
                   -sum_(|k-i|>=3)|E_ik|, 0<=i<m.             (7)

An out-of-range e or adjacent entry is zero. Put alpha_(-1)=0 and

 alpha_i=ell_i-alpha_(i-1), 0<=i<m,
 C=sum_(i<m-1)alpha_i^2/b_i + alpha_(m-1)^2/r_(m-1),
 Delta=D-C.                                                   (8)

If b_i>0 and r_(m-1)>0, with no other sign assumption yet, then

 J=sum_(i<m-1)r_i||y_i||^2
     +r_(m-1)||y_(m-1)+(alpha_(m-1)/r_(m-1))*z||^2
     +sum_(i<m-1)b_i||y_i+y_(i+1)+(alpha_i/b_i)*z||^2
     +sum_(i<m-2)e_i||y_i+2y_(i+1)+y_(i+2)||^2
     +sum_(k<l, l-k>=3)|E_kl| ||y_k+sgn(E_kl)y_l||^2
     +Delta||z||^2.                                          (9)

For a zero long-range coefficient use sgn(0)=0; its whole term is zero.

Proof. The triple squares supply off-diagonal entry e_i at distance2 and
entries2e_i at each of its two neighboring pairs. Subtracting them leaves
the adjacent coefficient b_i in (7). Their diagonal contributions, together
with those of the pair squares, are
E_(i-1,i)+E_(i,i+1)-e_(i-2)-e_i. Each long-range square contributes
|E_ik| to the corresponding diagonal. The remaining diagonal is exactly r_i.
This proves the residual identity. Finally

 sum_i ell_i y_i=sum_(i<m-1)alpha_i(y_i+y_(i+1))
                                      +alpha_(m-1)y_(m-1)

by ell_i=alpha_i+alpha_(i-1). Completing precisely those displayed squares
spends C and leaves Delta. This proves (9), including all cross terms.

Therefore the explicit scalar conditions

 e_i>=0, b_i>0, r_i>=0 (all i), r_(m-1)>0, Delta>=0            (10)

are SUFFICIENT for the full T to be positive semidefinite. This supplies an
actual square-factor formula, not an LDL recurrence with unknown pivots.
The constant column is paid by the exact alternating loads (8), without a
matrix inverse or pseudoinverse. No individual adjacent-swap sign is used.

The original certificate in (1) has the additional square

 ||x_0-S*z/(S+1)-sum_j xi_j||^2.                              (11)

The change from x_0 to that square's vector is an invertible unit triangular
change of coordinates. Thus (9) plus (11) is a factorization on the FULL
original formal space, including x_0. There is no hidden range condition.

## 4. Uniform exact boundary calculation: a coefficient vanishes as 1/m

Write t=d_0, u=d_1, d=d_2. The first three gradients in birth coordinates
are V_0,V_1,V_2. Direct expansion of the first two physical steps gives

 E_00=(2-t)/t, E_01=(t-1)/u,
 E_11=[2t+u(2-u)]/u^2,
 E_12=(t+u)(u-1)/(u*d).                                     (12)

By (6), e_0=e_1=0 and both rows have no longer tails. Consequently
r_0=E_00-E_01 and r_1=E_11-E_01-E_12. Also b_0=E_01,b_1=E_12.
The actual parameters are

For 0<epsilon<=1/128, one has 7/5<t<283/200, 199/100<u<2, and d<7/2.

For b_1, use (t+u)(u-1)> (339/100)(99/100) and
u*d<683/100, since sqrt(2)<283/200 gives d<683/200.

More significantly,

 r_1>3epsilon/2, and r_1/epsilon -> (3sqrt(2)-1)/2.            (14)

Here is a direct sign proof. Put delta=d-t-2. Then
delta/epsilon=2/[lambda(1+epsilon)]-lambda*epsilon>3/4,

In particular the finite observed minimum around .01267 cannot be promoted

### 4.2 Why plain diagonal dominance cannot replace the triple squares

The limiting actual parameters as m tends to infinity are
t=sqrt(2), u=2, d=sqrt(2)+2, e=2d, f=8+5sqrt(2). Substitution into
(12),(16), with tau=sqrt(2), gives

 E_22=1, E_12=1/2, E_23=(9-5tau)/4, E_24=(4tau-5)/14.

Every one of these entries is positive. Since row2 has no other entries,
its ordinary diagonal-dominance margin tends to

 E_22-E_12-E_23-E_24=(27tau-39)/28<0.                         (18)

The strict inequality follows by tau<13/9, which follows by squaring
2<169/81. The entries are continuous rational functions of epsilon near0,
with positive denominators, so this margin is negative for all sufficiently
large ACTUAL integer horizons. Thus ordinary diagonal dominance in the
same lossless birth basis does not prove the desired whole family.

In contrast the local-square remainder tends to
r_2=(43tau-59)/28>0. The positive second-neighbor interaction helps through
its triple square; replacing every off-diagonal entry by an absolute
diagonal cost would discard exactly that contribution. Equation (18) is
not a negative direction of T and does not refute its PSD claim.
