## Directed degree-preserving elimination

Cohen, Kelner, Kyng, Peebles, Peng, Rao and Sidford,
Solving Directed Laplacian Systems in Nearly-Linear Time through Sparse
LU Factorizations, arXiv:1811.10722v1.
Primary full paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10722 .

Read the introduction's elimination discussion, Section3 Algorithm4,
Section4 Definition4.1 and Lemmas4.2--4.4. For Eulerian directed
Laplacians, eliminating a vertex creates an incoming-to-outgoing biclique.
Their nonnegative equal-mass coupling preserves weighted degrees while
matching that dense object in expectation. Their analysis also identifies
Schur-complement growth; arbitrary elimination is not protected by a
uniform constant, and controlled sets/norms are needed. The main output
is an approximate LU/linear-system guarantee, not an exact PSD
certificate for an arbitrary signed symmetric matrix.

## Chordal positive/semidefinite completion

Grone, Johnson, Sa and Wolkowicz, Positive Definite Completions of Partial
Hermitian Matrices, Linear Algebra and its Applications58 (1984),109--124.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(84)90207-6 .
Author-hosted original paper:
https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~hwolkowi/henry/reports/pdcomplGJSW84.pdf .

Read Section2 definitions, Proposition2, Section6 Theorem7 and Section7.
The theorem concerns partially specified Hermitian matrices: compatible
positive data on specified cliques admit completion for chordal patterns.
The paper also treats the semidefinite case. Unspecified entries are
free completion variables. This does not assert positivity of an already
fully specified matrix, or realizability of a completion by a prescribed
nonnegative multiplier/flow map.
