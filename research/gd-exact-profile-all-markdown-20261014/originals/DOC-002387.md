## 6. Why the absorption comparison fails, and the exact surviving gap

For an arbitrary actual state y write r=y_+, n=(-y)_+, and U=P_h r.
If y_i<=0, then U_i=(h/4)(r_(i-1)+r_(i+1))>=0. Define

    a_i=min(n_i,U_i) if y_i<=0, and a_i=0 otherwise;
    b_i=(-U_i)_+ if y_i>0, and b_i=0 otherwise.

Directly taking the positive part of y'=U-n proves

    r_next=P_h r-a+b,      a>=0, b>=0.                (25)

At an old tie n_i=0, so a_i=0 and the same formula applies. A zero step
also satisfies it. The vector b records the repair needed when an old
positive coordinate becomes strictly negative; strict sign nesting
implies b=0. The converse may have ties and is not asserted.

For the four-step actual prefix above b=0 throughout. At its third
update the new absorption includes nu_3(e_3+e_(-3)). Propagating this
deviation through P_(t_2) multiplies its entry at3 by1-2eta<0.
Equations (17)--(19) show that the earlier absorption does not offset
this sign change. That is the precise reason the larger free profile
ceases to majorize the smaller actual incoming profile.

For a GENERAL terminal index ell, define P_(ell,k+1) as the ordered
product P_(h_(ell-1))...P_(h_(k+1)), with the empty product I. Iterating
(25), with the actual a_k,b_k, gives

    r_ell=R_ell-sum_(k=0)^(ell-1)P_(ell,k+1)a_k
                   +sum_(k=0)^(ell-1)P_(ell,k+1)b_k.  (26)

All these linear factors are on the same finite cycle; the nonlinear
sources still depend on the actual chronological states. Equation (26)
is a diagnosis of the missing source estimate, not a terminal result.

If every remaining-suffix product P_(ell,k+1) were entrywise nonnegative
and b_k=0 for all physical queries, then (26) would yield terminal-only
domination r_ell<=R_ell. This conditional implication is elementary and
is NOT refuted by (21): a later complete block might change the sign of
an earlier absorption contribution again. Nonnegativity of the full
prefix kernel K_k alone is insufficient, as (20)--(21) demonstrate.

The all-query profile induction selected here is refuted. The weaker
