# Exact six/eight-anchor multistage completions and two special word families

All norms and inner products are Euclidean. For an integer n>=0, a finite
nonnegative physical schedule h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), and L>0, let W_n(h)
be the supremum of

    2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L||x_0-x_star||^2)

over every finite-dimensional convex differentiable f with L-Lipschitz
gradient, every minimizer x_star, and x_0!=x_star, using the actual updates
x_(i+1)=x_i-(h_i/L)grad f(x_i). Write R_n(h)=W_n(h)/2. The witnesses
below use smoothness1 and the true closest initial distance to the complete
minimum set. Multiplication of their objective by any L>0 gives the same
points with steps h_i/L and the same normalized payoff.

## Definitions of the two completed functions

Fix real parameters M=M_mc>=2, B=B_mc>=2, A=A_mc>=4B+4, and
epsilon in [0,1]. These are local construction parameters, distinct from
the benchmark sequences C_n and B_n below. Put a=a_mc=1-1/M,
C=C_mc=2B+1, and let e_1,e_2,e_3 be the standard basis of R^3. Define

    nu=sqrt(2A-4),
    p=p_mc=e_1/M,
    q=q_mc=(-2e_1+nu e_2)/M,
    Q=Qnorm_mc=||q||^2=2A/M^2,
    r=r_mc=-q/2+sqrt((B/2-1/4)Q)e_3,
    R=Rnorm_mc=||r||^2=BQ/2,       <q,r>=-Q/2,
    D_mc=conv{0,p,q,r},
    F_old(x)=max_(g in D_mc){<x,g>-||g||^2/2},
    y=y_mc=-Ae_1-Mq,
    z=z_mc=y-BMq-Mr,
    w=w_mc=z-CMr-Mq,
    t_P=a e_1,              delta_P=a/M,
    t_Q=y+(M-1)q,           delta_Q=(M-1)Q,
    t_R=z+(M-1)r,           delta_R=(M-1)R,
    t_D=w+(M-1)q,           delta_D=(M-1)Q.

Here conv denotes convex hull. For each ordinary list L_i, choose any
finite nonnegative list of total mass M-1 and retain every entry, including
zeros. With concatenation in the displayed physical order, define

    h_three=(L_0,AM+1,L_1,BM+1,L_2,1+M epsilon),
    h_return=(L_0,AM+1,L_1,BM+1,L_2,CM+1,L_3,1+M epsilon).

Their physical lengths are sum_(i=0)^2 length(L_i)+3 and
sum_(i=0)^3 length(L_i)+4. Assertions about h_return additionally assume
M epsilon>=2B^2. Define, for v,x in R^3,

    H_three(v)=||v||^2/2+max{0,<t_P,v>-delta_P,
               <y,v>,<t_Q,v>-delta_Q,<z,v>,<t_R,v>-delta_R},
    H_return(v)=||v||^2/2+max{0,<t_P,v>-delta_P,
               <y,v>,<t_Q,v>-delta_Q,<z,v>,<t_R,v>-delta_R,
               <w,v>,<t_D,v>-delta_D},
    F_three(x)=sup_v{<x,v>-H_three(v)},
    F_return(x)=sup_v{<x,v>-H_return(v)}.

These are one fixed globally defined function per full physical run,
specified before that run. To define its complete old prefix, run F_old
from x_0=e_1 on the appropriate schedule and retain every triple
(x_i,grad F_old(x_i),F_old(x_i)) with 0<=i<n, together with the
zero optimum triple. The terminal index n is unqueried by the updates.
For any of the resulting actual functions, its signed ordered residual is
Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 on
{star,0,...,n}, where f_i=F(x_i), g_i=grad F(x_i), and
x_star=g_star=f_star=0.

## The specifically defined collapsed comparison

For the SAME h_three or h_return, retain the first list L_0 and bridge
AM+1, and treat all subsequent entries before the last 1+M epsilon as
one ordinary list, retaining their physical indices. Its mass is M_2-1,
where

    M_2=M_col_three=(B+2)M,
    or M_2=M_col_return=(B+C+3)M=(3B+4)M.

Set q_col=(-2e_1+nu e_2)/M_2 and define the static old collapsed function

    F_col_old(x)=max_(g in conv{0,p,q_col}){<x,g>-||g||^2/2}.

Run F_col_old from e_1 on that same full physical word and retain all
its old queried triples at indices 0<=i<n.
The collapsed completion class consists exactly of all everywhere defined
convex differentiable 1-smooth functions on R^3 interpolating its ENTIRE
old queried prefix, including values and gradients, and the optimum triple
(0,0,0). It is nonempty. For any such completion F_col, the same actual word
from e_1 has normalized true-distance payoff V_col(F_col). Define the positive
or zero upper comparison number

    U_col_three=2A epsilon^2/(B+2)^2,
    U_col_return=2A epsilon^2/(3B+4)^2,
    rho_col_three=[B(B+2)^2/2](1-1/(2B^3)),
    rho_col_return=(3/4)(3B+4)^2.

## Exact named reference channels and original benchmark

For any h of length n, put s(h)=sum_(i<n)h_i, P_j=sum_(i<j)h_i,
T_j=sum_(i>j)h_i, and (u)_+=max(u,0). The accepted single-cut channel is

    K_n(h)=max{1/(1+2s(h)),
          max_(0<=j<n)(h_j-1)_+^2/[(1+P_j)^2(1+2T_j)]},

with the inner maximum omitted at n=0. Define the entire convex-quadratic
channel by

    Q_n(h)=max_(0<=t<=1) t product_(i<n)(1-h_i t)^2.

This is the supremum of the normalized payoff over all finite-dimensional
convex quadratics with gradient Lipschitz constant at most L, with arbitrary
initial points and chosen minimizers, in the original normalization.

For n>=1 and a complex variable zeta=alpha+i beta, put
Poly_0(zeta)=1, Poly_i(zeta)=product_(t<i)(1-h_t zeta),
xi_i=Poly_i(zeta), gamma_i=zeta Poly_i(zeta), and xi_star=gamma_star=0.
Identify the complex plane with the real plane using <u,v>=Re(conj(u)v). On the index set
I_n={star,0,...,n}, the weight of the directed edge j->i is

    q_ij=<gamma_j,xi_i-xi_j>+||gamma_i-gamma_j||^2/2.

Let ell_n be the maximum sum of these weights on directed simple paths
n->star. Define Gap_h(zeta)=-2ell_n for zeta in the exact universal lens

    Lens_n={alpha+i beta:
       alpha-alpha^2-beta^2>=|beta|cot(pi/(n+2))},
    L_lens_n(h)=sup_(zeta in Lens_n) Gap_h(zeta),
    V_lens(h)=max{L_lens_n(h),1/(1+2s(h))}.

The meaning and legality of this transcript are those of accepted fact_id
dfde2fa326c908bf; the reference is restricted to Lens_n. No upper
comparison with the full schedule-dependent complex legal region is made.

Use the original all-split sequences, exactly as in accepted fact_id

For each integer N>=2^60, set

## Conclusions

(i) F_three and, on its additional parameter domain, F_return are finite
everywhere, convex, continuously differentiable, and have least global
gradient Lipschitz constant EXACTLY1. Each is the pointwise maximal legal
completion of its complete specified old prefix. The exact maximum of
all physical prefix conjugate quadratics is H_three or H_return, with
six or eight distinct affine anchors, respectively; the first r-query
anchor t_R is retained in BOTH laws. Every old prefix point, value and
gradient is preserved. The actual queried gradients are p,q,r for the
three-stage law and p,q,r,q for the return law, one constant gradient on
each ordinary list and its following bridge query. Every signed ordered
interpolation residual, including every remote, optimum and terminal pair,
in both orientations, is nonpositive for the resulting actual function.
The complete minimum sets and minimum values are

    argmin F_three=conv{0,y,z},   min F_three=0,
    argmin F_return=conv{0,y,z,w},   min F_return=0.

Origin is the unique closest minimizer to e_1, at distance1 in both cases.

(ii) For the three-stage law set

    L_stage=B+a, E_3=M(B+a)q+Mr,
    D_3=||E_3||^2=2A[(B+a)^2-(B+a)+B/2],
    b_3=Aa(epsilon-2/M), theta_3=(b_3)_+/D_3.

The actual terminal point, gradient and normalized payoff are exactly

    x_n=z-M epsilon r,
    grad F_three(x_n)=-M epsilon r-theta_3 E_3,
    V_three=2F_three(x_n)
       =AB epsilon^2-[Aa(epsilon-2/M)_+]^2/D_3,
    AB epsilon^2(1-1/(2B^3))<=V_three<=AB epsilon^2.

This includes epsilon=0, epsilon=2/M when in range, M=2, and epsilon=1.
For the return law set

    d_R=C+a, E_R=Mq+M d_R r,
    Dhat_R=1-d_R+(B/2)d_R^2, D_R=||E_R||^2=2A Dhat_R,
    b_R=2A[(B-1/(2M))epsilon-Ba/(2M)], theta_R=b_R/D_R.

The actual terminal quantities are exactly

    x_n=w-M epsilon q,
    grad F_return(x_n)=-M epsilon q-theta_R E_R,
    V_return=2F_return(x_n)=2A epsilon^2-b_R^2/D_R,
    2A epsilon^2(1-1/(2B))<=V_return<=2A epsilon^2.

They include the feasible boundary M epsilon=2B^2. The denominator
bounds are 2B^3<=Dhat_R<=(9/2)B^3; the lower-point identity is exactly
1-(2B+1)+(B/2)(2B+1)^2=2B^3+2B^2-3B/2.
In particular W_n(h_three)>=V_three and W_n(h_return)>=V_return.

(iii) Every member of the narrowly defined collapsed completion class
has true closest initial radius1 and 0<=V_col(F_col)<=U_col for its word.
On the entire respective parameter domains the WEAK comparisons are

    V_three>=rho_col_three U_col_three>=rho_col_three V_col(F_col),
    V_return>=rho_col_return U_col_return>=rho_col_return V_col(F_col).

At epsilon=0 in the three-stage domain, V_three=U_col_three=V_col(F_col)=0:
the displayed weak comparison has equality and no payoff ratio is asserted.
When epsilon>0 the comparison upper U_col is positive and the ratios
V_three/U_col_three>=rho_col_three>1 and
V_return/U_col_return>=rho_col_return>1 are defined and give strict
domination of every such collapsed completion. Epsilon>0 is automatic
in the return parameter domain. Both factors diverge on their specified
growing families. The comparison covers this exact retained prefix and
second slope, with no claim about other partitions, slopes, or witnesses.
