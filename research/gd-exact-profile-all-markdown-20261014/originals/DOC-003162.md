retains the same enclosing-m parameters. No chronological index, step,

Here 1_(condition) equals one or zero according to the displayed condition.

    [Q_(before,after)+Q_(after,before)]/Gamma_before^2

## 6. Finite full support: explicit perturbation bound

This follows by writing the probability as its displayed reference

mass. It is a direct finite probability bound, including high binary
