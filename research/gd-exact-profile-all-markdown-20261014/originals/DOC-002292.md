# Primary-source tools for the current structural routes

Checked September27,2026. Main-authored source triage, not a proof, imported
fact or external consult. These optional tools do not change the assignments,
launch limits or closed numerical/verification allowances.

## Symmetry and matrix algebra

Primary source: de Klerk, Pasechnik and Schrijver, *Reduction of symmetric
semidefinite programs using the regular star-representation* (2007),
[author-hosted paper](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~lex/files/symm.pdf).
Read Section2, Theorem1 and equations17-19. The regular representation of the
commutant is a star-isomorphism, preserving positive semidefiniteness. In the
displayed SDP application, a permutation group commutes with the objective
and constraint matrices; averaging therefore preserves feasibility and cost.

Possible use for max: test whether a proposed bulk tensor coefficient family
has an actual closed matrix algebra with a smaller faithful representation.
This is a research suggestion, not a verified match. Repeated step values or
a dyadic indexing set alone do not establish the source's invariance
hypotheses for chronological GD, its optimum edges, or its last objective.
A Walsh/Haar coordinate change alone supplies no new feasible array. Keep
actual coefficient nonnegativity separate from PSD of a transformed matrix.

## Chordal PSD versus polynomial certificates

Primary source: Zheng and Fantuzzi, *Sum-of-squares chordal decomposition of
polynomial matrix inequalities*,
[full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.11410).
Theorem2.1 states the classical clique-supported PSD-sum characterization for
a constant matrix with chordal sparsity. Proposition2.1 shows that even an
everywhere positive definite polynomial matrix need not have a polynomial
clique decomposition of the naive same form. Theorem2.4 obtains weighted SOS
clique decompositions on an Archimedean compact semialgebraic domain under
strict positive definiteness.

Possible use for max/max3: an explicitly structured full remainder might
admit clique-sized storage/Schur certificates. First identify its ACTUAL
matrix sparsity and retained separator variables. A graph of interpolation
edges is not automatically the sparsity graph of the full quadratic matrix.
Neither pointwise constant-matrix decomposition nor an existence theorem
supplies a uniform-degree all-depth formula. Singular PSD boundaries and a
parameter tending to zero require their own proof; strict positivity cannot
be silently supplied by a numerical check. This is conditional source triage.

## Spectral sparsification has a load-bearing PSD-atom hypothesis

Primary source: de Carli Silva, Harvey and Sato, *Sparse Sums of Positive
Semidefinite Matrices*,
[paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.0088), Theorem3 and AppendixD.
For PSD summands B_i of size d, their sum B admits a nonnegative weighted
O(d/epsilon^2)-term approximation lying between B and (1+epsilon)B. The paper
explicitly says the assumption that the summands are PSD cannot be removed.

Possible use only after an actual positive-block decomposition exists.
C55's individual added-pair update is indefinite, so this theorem does not
justify sparsifying those updates as if they were PSD atoms. Exact value-flow
and interpolation-coefficient constraints also require an explicitly matched
argument; approximate spectral preservation alone does not preserve them or
the exact target constant. No randomized or deterministic solver is allocated.

## Arbitrary spectra do not substitute for gradient legality

Primary source: Greenbaum, Ptak and Strakos, *Any Nonincreasing Convergence
Curve is Possible for GMRES* (1996),
[publisher abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/S0895479894275030).
The abstract permits a prescribed positive nonincreasing residual history
with arbitrarily specified eigenvalues for a constructed linear system.
Only the publisher statement was used here; no full proof was retrieved.

This is an analogy/caution for max2's temporal-span work, not a theorem about
GD. GMRES residual minimization and a general linear system do not establish
cyclic smooth-convex interpolation, gradient integrability, or the correct
objective/initial-distance normalization. The current legal finite example
c5a096d73740bf81 already demonstrates the relevant radius-only failure within
this project. No additional GMRES detour is requested absent a concrete
legality-preserving construction.
