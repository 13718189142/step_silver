## 1. The certificate family, including all its available parameters

Use smoothness one and optimum jet x_star=g_star=f_star=0. For every ordered
pair of distinct samples put

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    C_ij=-2Q_ji
        =2(f_i-f_j)+2<g_j,x_j-x_i>-||g_i-g_j||^2.

The accepted fact implies C_ij>=0 on every legal trajectory. All identities
below, however, are identities in independent formal vectors after the GD
substitution; no interpolation constraints are imposed on test vectors.

Fix a>0,c>0 and define

    d_j=a c^j,
    H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+d_j,H_j),
    n_j=2^j-1, S_j=sum H_j, M_j=1+S_j.

The initial block in the certificate recursion is H_1=(1+a), not H_0.
For each block choose real u_j,v_j, with final v_m>0, and a multiplier array
lambda^(j) on its samples and star. Require the following star pattern:

    lambda_(star,i)=h_i             for every queried i<n_j,
    lambda_(star,n_j)=u_j,
    lambda_(i,star)=0               for i<n_j,
    lambda_(n_j,star)=s_j,
    s_j=S_j+u_j-v_j.                                      (1)

Here s_j is only an incoming optimum multiplier, not the stepsize sum S_j.
All final actual multipliers must be nonnegative if the array is to certify
an objective bound. We will prove a necessary condition even without using
those signs. Assume exact cancellation of every function-value coefficient
in the identity defining the gradient-only remainder E_j:

    ||x_start||^2-||x_end-u_j g_end||^2-2v_j f_end
            =sum lambda^(j)_ik C_ik+E_j.                  (2)

Put

    Z_j=(u_j-1)^2+v_j,
    z_j=sum_(i<n_j) h_i g_i+g_end.                         (3)

The seed may be ANY one-step array satisfying (1)--(2). Its remainder need
not be PSD. In particular this includes the primitive one-step seed in the
shared construction, with u_1=M_1,v_1=2M_1-1 and
E_1=(2-(1+a)^2)||g_0-g_1||^2.

Here is the permitted join. Write n=n_j,p=n,q=2n+1,S=S_j,
d=d_j,b=1+d,M=M_j. Choose parameters A,B,P,Q with

    r=1+A+B,
    P-Q=b+(1-A)S.                                       (4)

Embed one copy of the old array on the left block and r times the same
array on the right block. For every right queried index i=p+1,...,q-1 add

    A h_i to (p,i), B h_i to (q,i),
    -(A+B)h_i to (star,i).                               (5)

Add the following six sparse corrections:

    (p,q): P,                    (q,p): Q,
    (p,star): -s_j,              (q,star): s_(j+1)-r s_j,
    (star,p): b-u_j,             (star,q): u_(j+1)-r u_j.  (6)

No restriction to H=ell=0, greedy choices of Z, PSD local terms, rank-one
child remainders, or PSD child remainders is imposed. The final objective
matrix must still be PSD on the full formal space. Nonnegative actual
coefficients, when they hold, include all the star coefficients in (1);
requiring A,B,P,Q>=0 is a sufficient way to preserve the other signs.

The desired result is a necessary condition for ANY such recursion. It is
therefore valid for every choice of its parameters that passes all actual
sign tests, including choices depending on the target horizon.

## 2. The complete local identity

Define

    t=A-B,
    H=M+d-B(M-d)-t M,
    ell=P-A,
    V=x_p-x_q-g_p+g_q.

The exact recursion for the entire gradient remainder is

    E_(j+1)=E_j(left)+r E_j(right)
       +t||V||^2+2<V,(H-ell)g_p+ell g_q>
       +(Z_j-rd^2)||g_p||^2+(rZ_j-Z_(j+1))||g_q||^2.    (7)

We verify both the value and quadratic coefficients. Write x=x_p,g=g_p,
y=x_q,e=g_q, and w=sum_(p<i<q)h_i g_i=x-bg-y. Summing (5) gives

    2AS f_p+2BS f_q-AS||g||^2-BS||e||^2
                      -2<w,A(x-g)+B(y-e)>.             (8)

This follows by expanding a summand: the coefficient of each f_i and of
each ||g_i||^2 cancels, leaving the displayed linear and bilinear terms.
The value coefficient at p in the two children plus (5)--(6) is

    -2v_j+2AS+2(P-Q)-2s_j-2(b-u_j)
       =2[(A-1)S+P-Q-b]=0.

The coefficient at q is

    -2r v_j+2BS-2(P-Q)+2(s_(j+1)-r s_j)
                         -2(u_(j+1)-r u_j)=-2v_(j+1).

This uses (1),(4) and r=1+A+B. All other values have already canceled.

For completeness, the quadratic parts of the six expressions used in (6)
are respectively

    C_pq: 2<e,y-x>-||g-e||^2,
    C_qp: 2<g,x-y>-||g-e||^2,
    C_pstar: -||g||^2,       C_qstar: -||e||^2,
    C_starp: 2<g,x>-||g||^2,
    C_starq: 2<e,y>-||e||^2.

Subtract their weighted sum and (8) from the new potential minus the two
child potentials. With D=x-y, all dependence on the separate x,y cancels.
The resulting coefficients, using the convention that cross coefficients
multiply twice the stated inner product, are

    ||D||^2: t,
    <D,g>: H-ell-t,               <D,e>: ell+t,
    ||g||^2: Z_j-rd^2+t-2H+2ell,
    ||e||^2: rZ_j-Z_(j+1)+t+2ell,
    <g,e>: H-2ell-t.

Substituting V=D-g+e gives (7). Thus (7) concerns the full original
gradient space, not only a condensed boundary form.

## 3. A formal template on which all mixed terms vanish

Define nonzero signed scalars

    k_1=2+a=M_1,
    k_(j+1)=-k_j^2/d_j,       j>=1.                       (9)

For each block H_j and each vector e, there is an explicit formal gradient
assignment, linear in e, with terminal gradient e such that

    z_j=k_j e,
    E_j=(k_j^2-Z_j)||e||^2.                              (10)

Crucially this is true for EVERY parameter choice in (4)--(6). The same
template (9) works after a change of the storage variables, multiplier
weights, or mixed boundary terms.

First check the seed carefully. From (1)--(2), subtraction of the star
terms gives the intrinsic identity

    E_j=2sum_(i<n_j)h_i(f_i-f_end)
          +sum_(i<n_j)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
          +(M_j-Z_j)||g_end||^2
          -sum_(i!=k finite)lambda^(j)_ik C_ik.          (11)

To obtain (11), use
||x_start||^2-||x_end||^2
 =2sum h_i<g_i,x_i>-sum h_i^2||g_i||^2
and s_j=S_j+u_j-v_j. This also verifies that no hypothesis s_j=0 is needed.

Assign both seed gradients equal to e, and set the formal values
f_i=<e,x_i>-||e||^2/2. Every finite C_ik is then zero. More generally,
for a constant-gradient block with prefix sums P_i,
f_i-f_end=(S_j-P_i)||e||^2 and

    2sum h_i(S_j-P_i)+sum h_i(1-h_i)=S_j(S_j+1).

The latter equality follows from
sum_i h_i sum_(t>=i)h_t=(S_j^2+sum_i h_i^2)/2.
Equation (11) therefore evaluates to (M_j^2-Z_j)||e||^2.
For H_1, z_1=(2+a)e=k_1 e, proving (10). The auxiliary values merely
evaluate a polynomial identity; they are not claimed to be feasible jets.

Suppose (10) holds at level j. In the right child use its template with
terminal vector e. In the left child use its template with terminal vector

    g=-k_j e/d_j.

The block displacement gives exactly

    V=d_j g+z_right=0,
    z_parent=z_left+d_j g+z_right=z_left=k_j g=k_(j+1)e.  (12)

Evaluating the ENTIRE right side of (7), rather than discarding any child
part, now gives

    E_(j+1)
      =(k_j^2-Z_j)||g||^2+r(k_j^2-Z_j)||e||^2
           +(Z_j-rd_j^2)||g||^2+(rZ_j-Z_(j+1))||e||^2
      =[(k_j^2-rd_j^2)k_j^2/d_j^2+r k_j^2-Z_(j+1)]||e||^2
      =(k_j^4/d_j^2-Z_(j+1))||e||^2.

This is (10) at the parent. Neither E_j>=0 nor Z_j=k_j^2 was used.
In particular reducing Z at an earlier join does not remove the test:
its resulting positive template energy is carried exactly into the next
calculation and cancels with that join's storage terms.

## 4. Even changing the final initial-distance coefficient cannot repair it

Write u=u_m,v=v_m>0,k=k_m,Z=(u-1)^2+v. The full objective certificate
generated by the final array, at any proposed level q_cert, is

    M_cert=q_cert||x_0||^2-2f_n
                         +sum_(i!=j) (2lambda^(m)_ji/v) Q_ij
       =(q_cert-1/v)||x_0||^2
                         +[||x_n-u g_n||^2+E_m]/v.       (13)

The whole formula follows from (2) and C_ij=-2Q_ji. Its actual
interpolation multipliers are nonnegative when the lambda array is.

Apply (10), keep the initial formal vector x_0 independent, and write e
for the terminal template vector. Since z_m=k e,

    x_n-u g_n=x_0-(k+u-1)e.

Set beta=k+u-1. The restriction of v M_cert to the two independent
scalar coordinates in x_0,e has matrix

                    [ q_cert v      -beta       ]
                    [   -beta       2k beta-v   ].       (14)

Indeed its second diagonal is
beta^2+k^2-Z=2k(k+u-1)-v. Full PSD requires (14) PSD.
Its lower diagonal cannot be zero: PSD would then force beta=0, making
that diagonal -v<0. Consequently 2k beta-v>0, and its determinant gives

    q_cert >= beta^2/[v(2k beta-v)] >= 1/k^2.             (15)

For the last inequality use the exact identity

    k^2 beta^2-v(2k beta-v)=(v-k beta)^2>=0.

Both denominators are positive. Thus (15) holds whether E_m is PSD or
indefinite, whether q_cert equals 1/v or is increased, and whether any
child storage was selected greedily. All nonlinear mixing parameters in
(7) have vanished from the necessary bound.

This is a full-space certificate obstruction. Passing the test on legal
sampled trajectories alone would not suffice, since the formal gradient
assignment used in (10) is generally not a legal transcript.

## 5. Quantitative growing-horizon consequence for the assigned words

Fix an integer m>=128, set epsilon=1/m, lambda=1+sqrt(2), and use the
actual assignment

    c=lambda(1+epsilon), a=(1-epsilon)/c,
    d_j=(1-epsilon)c^(j-1), 1<=j<=m.

The symbol d_m is used only to express the recurrence solution; H_m uses
the bridges d_0,...,d_(m-1). Put

    R=(2+a)/[c(1-epsilon)].

Solving (9) in absolute value gives

    |k_j|=(1-epsilon)c^j R^(2^(j-1)),   1<=j<=m.          (16)

To check (16), divide |k_j| by c d_j. This ratio is R at j=1 and
is squared at every next step, since d_(j+1)=c d_j.

There is a strict gap below one in R, with an explicit uniform constant:

    1-R = epsilon[2-lambda^2 epsilon(1+epsilon)]
                  /[lambda^2(1+epsilon)^2(1-epsilon)]
          > epsilon/4.                                  (17)

For the equality multiply by c^2(1-epsilon). Its numerator is
lambda^2(1+epsilon)^2(1-epsilon)
 -[lambda^2+(2lambda-1)epsilon]
 =2epsilon-lambda^2 epsilon^2(1+epsilon),
using lambda^2=2lambda+1. For the inequality, lambda^2<6 and
epsilon<=1/128 imply

    2-lambda^2 epsilon(1+epsilon)>7/4,
    lambda^2(1+epsilon)^2(1-epsilon)<7.

Also R>0 from its definition. The bound 1-x<=exp(-x), proved for
0<=x<1 by integrating the derivative of log(1-x), now yields

    |k_m| < c^m exp[-2^(m-3)/m].                         (18)

Here c<5/2: sqrt(2)<10/7 gives
c<(17/7)(129/128)=2193/896<5/2. Combining (15),(18) gives

    q_cert > (2/5)^(2m) exp[2^(m-2)/m] -> infinity.       (19)

The limit follows because 2^m/m^2 tends to infinity: its successive ratio
is 2m^2/(m+1)^2>3/2 for all m>=7. In particular the exponential term in
the logarithm of (19) dominates the linear term 2m log(5/2).

Already for every m>=128, (18) gives |k_m|<1 and hence q_cert>1.
For an explicit elementary comparison, exp(1)>1+1+1/2+1/6>5/2, so
log(5/2)<1, while 2^(m-3)>m^2 for every m>=16 by checking m=16
and noting that 2m^2/(m+1)^2>1 thereafter. Thus the exponent in (18),
after bounding c^m by exp(m), is negative.

No upper coefficient from this family can therefore approach the small
objective levels needed for a comparison with the exact Zhang--Jiang
profile. This is stronger than failure of the previously chosen H=ell=0
rule: all the mixed terms, all retained child matrices, all earlier
storage choices, and arbitrary q_cert were included in the derivation.

## 6. Scope and the surviving repair directions

The obstruction covers exactly the repeated-copy array (4)--(6), started
from any H_1 seed with the star pattern (1). The identity requires neither
PSD child remainders nor local PSD checks, and the final test includes x_0
and every original gradient coordinate. It also permits nonzero incoming
terminal optimum weights s_j and arbitrary u_j,v_j consistent with (1).

It does NOT exclude another complete interpolation array for H_m. In
particular C51's finite H_3 certificate uses a different unrestricted
array, and is consistent with this result. The obstruction also does not
automatically cover independent internal coefficient changes at a parent,
additional interpolation edges or corrections outside (5)--(6), a
different star pattern, or a different seed size allowed to grow with m.

The concrete repair requirement is now an algebraic one: a useful changed
array must act nontrivially on the template (10)--(12). Altering only the
local V-dependent terms cannot do that. Nor can changing Z alone, since
the precise earlier template energy is propagated in (10).

For literature context, the gluing-plus-rank-one-plus-sparse architecture
is present in Altschuler--Parrilo, arXiv:2309.16530v1, Theorem 3.4 and its
proof in Sections 3.1--3.2. The exact paper was downloaded and read. No
statement from that paper is imported into this proof, and its certificate
for the undeformed silver schedule is not claimed to apply after the
present parameter deformation.

All new claims here remain candidates until verifier acceptance. The
original unrestricted target remains unresolved.

## 7. Changing only a fixed or moderately growing seed also fails

The one-step starting point is not essential. Permit an arbitrary seed
certificate for the entire block H_r, 1<=r<=m, with the same star pattern
(1), all its finite interpolation entries free, and any gradient remainder.
Above that seed use the repeated-copy join (4)--(6).

By (11), the constant-gradient assignment on the seed gives

    z_r=M_r e, E_r=(M_r^2-Z_r)||e||^2.

Start the template with k_r=M_r and repeat (12). Every subsequent identity
and the full-matrix lower bound (15) remain valid. The exact solution is

    |k_m|=(1-epsilon)c^m R_r^(2^(m-r)),
    R_r=M_r/(c d_r).

In fact R_r<=R_1 for EVERY r>=1 at the assigned parameters. To see this,
the mass recurrence M_(j+1)=2M_j+a c^j gives

    M_j=A c^j+(1-A)2^j, A=a/(c-2),
    R_j=[A+(1-A)(2/c)^j]/(1-epsilon).

One has 0<A<1: a>0,c>2, and

    c(c-2)-(1-epsilon)
       =(2lambda+3)epsilon+lambda^2 epsilon^2>0.

Thus the displayed R_j is decreasing. Combining with (17) gives the uniform
seed-size estimate

    |k_m|<c^m exp[-2^(m-r)/(4m)],
    q_cert>(2/5)^(2m) exp[2^(m-r-1)/m].                   (20)

Consequently no such certificate sequence can provide a bounded useful
upper level whenever 2^(m-r_m)/m^2 tends to infinity. This includes every
fixed seed depth and, more strongly, every choice

    r_m<=m-3 ceil(log_2 m)

for all sufficiently large m. The conclusion permits a different completely
free seed array at each horizon; it is not a finite-library assumption.

A correction confined to a fixed H_3 or H_4 seed therefore cannot repair
the growing certificate. A useful modification must continue to act at
growing recursion levels, or leave this repeated-copy architecture.

## 8. An explicit violated pair and an actual array change outside the family

This section independently checks that the template is not a legal
adversary, and exhibits a concrete multiplier direction that the previous
obstruction does not cover. It does NOT assert full PSD after that change.

For the original one-step template define b0=1+a and M1=2+a. At every
one-step leaf its two gradients coincide. Moreover all leaf endpoints have
the same proximal point Y=x_end-g_end: (12) says the left and right child
endpoints have equal proximal points, so induction propagates the common
value to every leaf. A leaf with gradient w thus has endpoint Y+w and
start Y+M1 w.

Look at the rightmost H_3 copy, which has terminal template vector e. Put

    T=M1^2/(d_1 d_2)>0.

Its left H_2 endpoint gradient is T e, while its last one-step leaf has
gradient e. In the full word these samples are j=n_m-4 (the earlier
endpoint) and i=n_m-1 (the last queried point). Hence

    g_j=T e, x_j=Y+T e,
    g_i=e,   x_i=Y+M1 e.

Adding the two interpolation expressions eliminates values exactly:

    C_ij+C_ji
       =2<g_i-g_j,x_i-x_j>-2||g_i-g_j||^2
       =2b0(1-T)||e||^2.                                (21)

For m>=128 the actual parameters have T>8/5. Indeed M1>2,
d_1 d_2=lambda(1-epsilon)(1-epsilon^2)<lambda<5/2, so
T>4/(5/2)=8/5. Thus (21) is strictly negative for e!=0.
Every legal smooth-convex transcript has both C_ij and C_ji nonnegative,
independently of the choice of function values. This gives an explicit
violated pair; no choice of values or optimum can turn this template into
an actual lower witness.

There is also a concrete, sign-preserving change of the multiplier array:
add gamma>=0 to both lambda_ij and lambda_ji. It preserves every value
flow and every star coefficient. The gradient remainder changes by

    -gamma(C_ij+C_ji)
      =2gamma[||g_i-g_j||^2-<g_i-g_j,x_i-x_j>].           (22)

On the template, the lower-right entry in (14) increases by
2gamma b0(T-1). For ANY prescribed q_cert>0 and v>0, set beta=k+u-1
as before and choose

    gamma=max(0,
       [beta^2/(q_cert v)-(2k beta-v)]/[2b0(T-1)]).        (23)

This makes that particular two-variable restriction PSD, by its Schur
complement. The increment is a real nonnegative interpolation multiplier
change, not a projection of a negative eigenvalue or a free PSD addition.

The complete matrix remains to be controlled. In fact the update (22)
itself is indefinite: setting only g_i nonzero gives a positive value,
whereas setting only g_j nonzero gives
2gamma(1-h_j)||g_j||^2<0 when gamma>0, since h_j=1+d_2>1.
This explicitly prevents treating (23) as a full certificate repair.

The indices i,j are queried samples, so the change introduces no incoming
terminal edge. The pair is already present in the unrestricted model of
accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290; it was the recursive parameterization that
failed to allow this independent change. Equation (20) also shows why a
useful implementation cannot stop at correcting only a bounded seed.

An exact extension for further work is to add, at each growing join, both
edges between p and q-1. Write w=g_(q-1), so x_(q-1)=x_q+b0 w. Its local
remainder update is

    2gamma<g_p-w,g_q+a w-V>,                             (24)

obtained by substituting x_p-x_(q-1)=V+g_p-g_q-b0 w in (22).
This term depends on the last queried gradient separately from z_block and
g_end. A boundary state that retains (z_block,g_end,g_last_query) can
represent (24); the two-variable state alone does not retain that data.
Choosing gamma and the old join parameters with full PSD at every growing
level remains an OPEN construction problem, not a conclusion of this file.

## 9. A concrete three-variable starting state for the surviving construction

The added pair can be represented with an exact, attained boundary state;
there is no need to assume an unspecified positive child remainder. The
following formulas initialize such an analytic attempt at H_2 for the
actual parameters m>=128.

Write b0=1+a,M=1+b0,d=d_1,x=d/M,eta=x^2+2x-1, and

    a0=(2-b0^2)/b0^2.

Then 1<b0<sqrt(2), 3/8<x<sqrt(2)-1, and eta<0. For the upper bound on x,
put s=sqrt(2)-1 and compute
d-sM=-epsilon[1-2s^2/(1+epsilon)]<0. For the lower bound use
d>=127/128 and M<5/2. Thus a0>0.

Start from the seed in Section 1. At the H_1-to-H_2 join set

    A=1/x, B=2+x, r=3+x+1/x, t=1/x-2-x,
    P=(1+M eta)/x, Q=M,
    u_2=M2=2M+d, v_2=2M2-1.

All actual multipliers are nonnegative: t>0, A,B,Q>0, and
1+M eta>=1-35/128>0 since eta>=-7/64 and M<5/2. Both incoming
optimum weights s_1,s_2 are zero, and the corrections (5)--(6) preserve
the star pattern as already checked in Section 2.

For H_2 gradients g0,g1,g2,g3 put

    U=b0(g0-g1), V0=b0(g2-g3), D0=g1-g3.

Substitution in (7) gives the complete remainder

    E_2=a0||U||^2+(r a0+t)||V0||^2+M^2(-eta)||D0||^2.   (25)

To check the substitution, these parameter choices give
H=-t(M+d), ell=-tM, Z_1=M^2,Z_2=M2^2. In the variables
V=d g1+z_right and g1,g3, the cross coefficient between V0 and D0
is A d-M=0, and the D0 diagonal is M^2(1-2x-x^2)=M^2(-eta).
Together with the exact two seed remainders this gives (25).

Retain the three boundary vectors (z,e,w)=(z_2,g3,g2). They are independent
coordinates because b0>0 allows g0 to vary z while holding g2,g3 fixed.
The relation is

    z-M2 e=U+V0+(M+d)D0.

The SHARP attained boundary form obtained by minimizing (25) over g0,g1 is

    K_2(z,e,w)
       =A2||z-M2 e-b0(w-e)||^2+B2||w-e||^2,
    A2=[1/a0+(1+x)^2/(-eta)]^(-1),
    B2=(r a0+t)b0^2.                                    (26)

For exact attainment and the remaining PSD part, put c0=M^2(-eta),l=M+d.
The scalar/vector square identity

    a0||U||^2+c0||D0||^2
      =[a0 c0/(c0+a0 l^2)]||U+lD0||^2
          +||a0 l U-c0 D0||^2/(c0+a0 l^2)

proves (26), and the second square can be set to zero for any supplied
boundary data. In particular (26) is not a test confined to a primal span.

At a subsequent modified join let a sharp child form be K(z,e,w). The
complete six-boundary-variable form before eliminating internal variables is

    K(L,g,u)+r K(R,e,w)
      +t||d g+R||^2+2<d g+R,(H-ell)g+ell e>
      +(Z-rd^2)||g||^2+(rZ-Znext)||e||^2
      +2gamma<g-w,e+a w-d g-R>.                         (27)

Here u is the left last-query gradient; w is the right last-query gradient,
and the parent boundary is (z,e,w), z=L+d g+R. All earlier internal PSD
squares are retained, with the right ones multiplied by r>0. Replace
L by z-d g-R and eliminate (g,u,R). Full PSD requires the eliminated
3x3 block to be PSD, its cross columns to lie in its range, and its Schur
complement in (z,e,w) to be PSD. If the eliminated block is positive
definite, this is the explicit update K_next=C-B^T A_elim^(-1)B, where
A_elim,B,C are the coefficient blocks of (27) in the indicated order.
If it is singular the range condition is essential and cannot be omitted.

Equations (26)--(27), gamma>=0, the actual coefficient signs from (4)--(6),
and the new balanced pair give a definite growing-horizon construction
