## 5. Entire equal-tail family: statement and reductions

### 5.1. The whole d>=1/2 piece

On the rectangle a in[a0,rC], d in[1/2,rC], define

    F_C(a,d)=a d^2(C-5-a-2d).

For C>=C0, the last factor is positive throughout this rectangle:
its minimum is C-5-3rC, positive at C0 and increasing thereafter.
For fixed d, F_C is a concave quadratic in a, so its minimum is
at an endpoint of the a interval. At either endpoint a, its derivative
in d is 2a d(C-5-a-3d), which can change sign only from positive
to negative. Hence the minimum in d is also at an endpoint.

The four possible minimum values are

    a0(C-6-a0)/4,
    rC(C-6-rC)/4,
    a0 rC^2(C-5-a0-2rC),
    rC^3(C-5-3rC).                                      (23)

Each is positive and increasing for C>=C0: rC'=2/(C+1)^2,
so each bracket in (23) has positive derivative, and its positive
nonconstant prefactors also increase. At C0 use the rational bounds

    41/100<a0<21/50, 3/4<rC0=84/109<4/5.

They give the following respective lower bounds for (23):

    533/4000, 69/400, 2583/16000, 27/200.

For the third bracket, for example,
C0-5-a0-2rC0>68/25-21/50-8/5=7/10.
For the fourth it is >68/25-12/5=8/25.
Every displayed bound is at least 533/4000. It follows that

    C F_C(a,d) >= (193/25)(533/4000)
                 =102869/100000>1,

contradicting (22). This closes the entire d>=1/2 piece.
