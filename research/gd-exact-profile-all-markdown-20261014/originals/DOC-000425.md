## 3. An exact deletion lemma, retaining the next checkpoint cost

Here suppose2<c<=5/2, and suppose C>=1,A>=C are fixed for the whole word.
Keep Q(b)=max{epsilon*b,A}; for this lemma epsilon may be any nonnegative
number. The proof does not change any remaining checkpoint or its parameter.

For a selected minimum-level checkpoint b=b_j=1+d, put

    M=M_j, S=M-1, u=d/M.

By(7),0<u<=c-2<=1/2. The costs Q(b), and Q of any next checkpoint,
are at least A.

First suppose there IS a next selected checkpoint b', with R=Q(b').
All earlier and later factors in(1) cancel when b is deleted. The exact
local comparison of the positive, unsquared factors is

       d/(C*s+Q(b)) * (b'-1)/(C*t+R)
    <= (b'-1)/(C*(s+b+t)+R)                          (9)

if and only if

    d*[C*(s+b+t)+R] <= (C*s+Q(b))*(C*t+R).           (10)

This pays for the entire deleted physical step b, including its added1.
It also keeps the ACTUAL next checkpoint cost R, even if that step is huge.

To prove(10), subtract its left side from its right side. The difference
is increasing in Q(b). Its derivative with respect to R is C*s+Q(b)-d.
Its derivatives with respect to s and t are respectively
C*(C*t+R-d) and C*(C*s+Q(b)-d). All are positive on the required region:
by(8), C*s+Q(b)>=C*(M-1)+A>=C*M>=M>=2*d, and the same holds for C*t+R.
Therefore the smallest difference occurs at

    s=t=S, Q(b)=R=A.

Define z=(A-C)/M>=0. At these values the difference, divided by M^2, is

    E(C,u,z)=(C+z)^2-u*[C*(2+u)+z]
            =C^2-C*u*(2+u)+z*(2*C-u)+z^2.            (11)

This proves that either of the following WHOLE-WORD conditions suffices
for deleting every selected minimum-level interior checkpoint:

    (I)  C>=c(c-2);
    (II) A>=2*C*M_max.                               (12)

For(I), u*(2+u)<=c(c-2)<=C; the first two terms of(11) have nonnegative
sum, and the other terms are nonnegative because2*C-u>0.

For(II), M<=M_max and M_max>=1 imply

    z=(A-C)/M>=C*(2*M_max-1)/M>=C.

Using u<=1/2, C>=1, z>=C, and2*C-u>0 in(11) gives

    E(C,u,z)>=C^2-(5/4)*C+C*(2*C-1/2)+C^2
              =4*C^2-(7/4)*C>0.                     (13)

Thus(9) holds in either case. Squaring preserves the comparison, all
unchanged factors are nonnegative, and deleting b cannot lower Phi.

    d^2*[1+2*(s+b+t)]/[(s+1)^2*(1+2*t)]<=1.          (15)

    [1+2*(s+b)/(1+2*t)]/(s+1)^2,
