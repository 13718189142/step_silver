# 0. Mathematical verdict

**Not solved.** Exact terminal completion is available, but neither a uniform sharp lower witness nor an asymptotically improving full-class upper certificate has been obtained.

The fixed goal is unchanged: for predetermined nonnegative GD steps, with
W_n(h)=sup 2(f(x_n)-f_*)/(L||x_0-x_*||^2), decide whether W_n^*/U_n^ZJ tends to one. The affirmative statement requires every epsilon>0 to admit N_epsilon independent of h such that W_n(h)>=(1-epsilon)U_n^ZJ for every n>=N_epsilon and every h in [0,infinity)^n. A negative resolution requires a fixed delta>0 and certified schedules on unbounded horizons with W_n(h)<=(1-delta)U_n^ZJ.

For the exact comparator, P_0=B_0=1,
F(x,y)=(x+y+sqrt(x^2+6xy+y^2))/2,
G(x,d)=2x+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8xd)/2,
P_n=max_(k+l=n-1)F(P_k,P_l), B_n=max_(k+l=n-1)G(P_k,B_l), and U_n^ZJ=1/B_n. No decimal constant or assumed normalized limit replaces this sequence.

Closed components are the six scoped facts listed below. The viable architectures are a full legal correlated adversary with quantitative normalization, or a genuinely stronger upper-certificate composition. The main blocker is a target-scale consequence of the exact representations: a sign-preserving primitive reduction has no automatic objective-scale guarantee. The highest-priority unresolved bridge in the constructive upper route is an actual useful enlargement by signed terminal credit, or a theorem excluding that enlargement. Current evidence is **method failure**, not proposition failure: several entire construction classes fail on specified schedules, but unrestricted W remains undetermined.

Calibration caveat: PARTIAL and SUBSTANTIAL do not mean almost solved; the quantitative interfaces remain unmatched.

The newest scoped evidence makes the constant-scale obstruction sharper. On the exact near-critical word defined in Bridge2 below, draftbf591e52671f6d33 bounds every retained zero-height two-block prefix followed by any legal constant-gradient tail, over its entire angle range, by (5949/6088)K, where K=1/(2 sum H_m+1). Together with the listed ray, lens and full quadratic baselines, the resulting benchmark ratio has limit superior at most2/e. This is an obstruction to those mechanisms, never an upper bound on unrestricted W. Draft8010ea721f41d0d3 shows that positive checkpoint height without its location can still lead to zero terminal error. Actual varying-tail terminal correlation, or an attained positive-height/position continuation, is therefore a concrete missing interface.

Draft295385c862b91a07 excludes every common horizon-dependent SPD metric in the compact parabolic regime z_m=1-u_m/m+i v_m/sqrt(m), with bounded u_m and v_m in a fixed positive compact interval, even allowing legal values and free completion. Other frequency regimes and more general correlations remain open. Draft4aaee6131a92da0e excludes signed-certificate repair that holds the supplied full PSD remainder fixed; a different ordinary certificate exists for its example. Draft347ec0d726695de2 excludes one simultaneous energy-preserving core shear because it creates a positive old interpolation residual. Neither proves strict cone enlargement nor rules out variable-matrix re-selection.

2. Supply a nonnegative complete interpolation/PSD certificate for its intrinsic primitive energy; passing necessary cuts or all quadratics is insufficient.

5. Prove the unbounded-horizon comparison to the exact B_n profile, including all constants in the amplification argument.

This existential claim may be false. It is not presented as a necessary condition for every possible negative answer, and its absence does not support the affirmative answer.

# 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- **DANGEROUS:** "Primitive failure gives a uniform objective improvement." Its negative energy can survive a minimizer change while the initial-distance denominator grows. Draft20c6d21ee60a4cb0 provides a defect conversion with explicit instance-dependent losses, not the uniform profile theorem.
- **DANGEROUS:** "Every quadratic and every cut passing implies primitive validity." Draftc58273e20409955e shows those necessary tests admit arbitrarily excessive capacity. Nonlinear correlated geometry remains required.
- **DANGEROUS:** "The last-sample deletion can be repeated backwards." Fact1252b8823bb5a290 retains all queried-prefix constraints.
- **DANGEROUS:** "The four local silver witnesses globalize." Draft66067b85d1228261 passes all four at silver capacity but is defeated by an endpoint cut.
- **DANGEROUS:** "Failure of normal mixtures means all polynomial methods fail." Nonnormal correlations, higher-order clocks and more general structured kernels are outside that exclusion.
- **DANGEROUS:** "A fixed-prefix last-step minimax exchange optimizes every step." The fixed-prefix mixture result and the joint nonconvexity counterexample do not authorize earlier exchanges.

Do not discard old planes, old query heights or the true minimizer cost. Do not optimize an already dominated scalar transport without changing its mechanism. Do not replace the exact B_n profile by a decimal. Do not infer unrestricted upper bounds from failed lower-witness families. Do not promote a complete-looking draft to a fact. Do not equate finite-horizon capacity calibration with asymptotic minimax resolution.
