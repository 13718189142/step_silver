# 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- A lower witness above U for one schedule is neither a minimax disproof
  nor an all-word affirmative theorem.
- A complete audit is not verifier acceptance. General component drafts
  are not promoted wholesale by an accepted specialization.
- A bound on a reduced mass channel is not a bound on its actual function;
  the inequality direction and closest-minimizer normalization matter.
- The single-source-stage radius cannot be copied into a longer chain.
  Nor can actual backward P,Q be preserved by replacing a block by its sum.
- Small turning angle reduces a LOCAL allowance, but remote old-plane
  constraints, metric conditioning and any sum smoothness remain payable.
- Averaging illegal transcripts, resetting old values, or repairing only
  adjacent interpolation inequalities cannot define the needed function.
- A positive tangent/Gram direction is not full nonlinear feasibility;
  a local capacity bound is not a global or objective-minimax bound.
- Finite-field patterns or floating-point positive eigenvalues cannot
  replace ordered-real sign and PSD proofs in the actual certificate.
- Do not delete independent formal coordinates at zero physical steps,
  assume arbitrary-template inflation, or conflate enclosing-m families.

# 4. Missing bridge lemmas

**Bridge1, actual sufficient nonzero-PSD seed.** Produce one complete
original feasible array at a sufficient capacity, outside the locally
excluded region. A symbolic real-algebraic or SOS construction must retain
every sign, exact flow and the actual full remainder. Existing accepted
physical lifts then apply. A restricted parameter-family obstruction must
state only that restricted scope.

**Bridge2, mixed correlated stages with paid history.** Extend the actual
finite calibration to an unbounded stage count using one static function,
keeping every mixture, positive proximal height and original query. Prove
all remote constraints and true initial distance and compute the complete
accumulated loss. A local arc or pairwise transfer formula is insufficient.

**Bridge3, true-radius arbitrary-count comparison.** On the SAME small-tau
H_(m,tau), control actual ordered P_i,Q_i, all transfer products and the
full source minimum polyhedron for every selection; alternatively produce
a genuine normalized escape with actual trajectory and unbounded depth.
The zero/one-checkpoint theorem and wide scalar collapse are inputs for
orientation, not the missing all-count inequality.

**Bridge4, uniform profile matching.** Given a genuinely legal all-word
family, establish a single word-independent vanishing loss at the exact
all-split B_n scale for every sufficiently large n. This bridge remains
separate even if any diagnostic family or geometric sublemma is closed.
