# 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- "Random gradients are approximately independent." Conditional only: the accepted orthogonal-moment theorem shows that this exact special case is too weak; controlled temporal correlations matter.
- "Full-history envelopes characterize the process." Conditional only: characterization is not a quantitative minimax comparison.
- "A polynomial basis spans every transcript Gram matrix." Method shortcut, not a proof step: an equivalent parameterization does not prove the missing uniform inequality.
- "A better one-step sum gives a better asymptotic schedule." Not automatic: the accepted terminal obstruction and unverified scalar-deficit calculation identify distinct propagation failures.
- Do not strengthen an accepted restricted obstruction to all finite-dimensional state or probability methods.
- Do not treat small values of a lower-bound channel as an upper bound on W.
- Do not repeat verifier submissions for elementary support or minor variants.
- Do not replace U_n^ZJ by the decimal 0.4239.
