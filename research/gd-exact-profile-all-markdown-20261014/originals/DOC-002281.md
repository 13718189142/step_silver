# Directed graph tools: exact matching still required

Main-authored optional source triage, September27,2026. No theorem import,
new assignment, solver allowance or accepted fact.

Yibin Zhao, *Fully Dynamic Spectral and Cut Sparsifiers for Directed Graphs*,
[ICALP2026 paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol374-icalp2026/html/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.157/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.157.html):
Definition2 controls the directed bilinear error relative to the associated
undirected Laplacian. Lemma10 identifies preservation of each vertex's degree
imbalance. Theorem3 allows auxiliary vertices, with guarantees after their
Schur elimination, and assumes polynomially bounded weights. The balanced-cut
results apply to cuts satisfying their specified balance condition.

Potential connection for max/max4, still UNMATCHED: actual interpolation
coefficients form a directed array and value cancellation imposes flows.
Test whether any exact flow-preserving operation also controls the physical
chronological moment terms in the FULL objective remainder. Degree balance
alone is not that conclusion. Auxiliary graph vertices do not automatically
represent legal extra queries; arbitrary multiplier weights do not meet a
polynomial-bound hypothesis automatically. An approximation theorem needs
an explicit error budget below the original constant threshold, including
all repeated levels and singular kernels. Do not remove queried constraints
under the label of graph elimination.

Cohen et al., *Solving Directed Laplacian Systems in Nearly-Linear Time
through Sparse LU Factorizations*,
[full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.10722): Section4 explicitly notes that
the symmetric part of a directed Schur complement can grow relative to the
original. Lemma4.3 gives a conditional general-matrix bound; Lemma4.2 supplies
a bounded-elimination statement for the paper's alpha-RCDD subsets of an
Eulerian Laplacian. This is not a general monotonicity principle for arbitrary
certificate matrices or arbitrary eliminated blocks.

Peng and Song, *Sparsified Block Elimination for Directed Laplacians*,
[full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.10257): Theorem3.3 concerns strongly
connected Eulerian Laplacians with an alpha-RCDD block. Section4 uses augmented
matrices to encode and bound errors of repeated block elimination. A possible
method analogy is explicit storage of errors across recursion, but neither
its Laplacian hypotheses nor its algorithmic approximation guarantee has
been matched to the present GD remainder. No claim of constant preservation
or full-certificate positivity follows merely from the analogy.

Disposition: keep as a source lead if a concrete coefficient/cut identity
exposes the required structure. Continue the existing exact calculations
first; a generic Laplacian reformulation with the hard PSD inequality still
unproved is a blocked route, not a solved bridge.
