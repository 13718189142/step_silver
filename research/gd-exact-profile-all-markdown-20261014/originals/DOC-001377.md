## 1. Original recurrence, normalization and all-split comparison

Verdict: SURVIVES ANALYTICAL ATTACK, including both zero arguments and
the larger-root condition. C107 preserves the accepted W=2R convention

When a=d=0, G(a,lambda d)=lambda Omega(a,d)=0. Otherwise set
w=Omega(a,d)>0 and t=a/w. The positive-root identity gives

    0<=t<=1,
    d/w=(1-t)/(1+t).

The polynomial P(v)=v^2-(4a+lambda d)v+4a^2 has larger root
G(a,lambda d), since its discriminant is lambda^2 d^2+8lambda ad.
Using lambda^2=2lambda+1, the substitution is exactly

    P(lambda w)/w^2
      =[t/(1+t)]*[2lambda^2-4lambda+(4-4lambda)t+4t^2]

      =4t(t-1/sqrt(2))^2/(1+t)>=0.

The branch check is essential and valid: w>=a+d, and

    lambda(a+d)-(2a+lambda d/2)

places it at or beyond the larger root, not below the smaller one.
Consequently G(a,lambda d)<=lambda Omega(a,d).

for d=0 they are 2a and lambda a. The nonzero equality case also survives:

sqrt((2-sqrt(2))^2+8(2-sqrt(2)))=3sqrt(2)-2>0. Therefore both

the signs and orientation explicitly, normalize L=1, put

    x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t,
    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    V_h=2sum_(i<r)h_i(f_i-f_r)
         +sum_(i<r)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_r||^2.

Here f_i=f(x_i), g_i=grad f(x_i), I is the identity matrix,
out_i=sum_j Lambda_ij and in_i=sum_j Lambda_ji.
The coefficient of f_i in sum Lambda_ij Q_ij is out_i-in_i,
which equals (L_flow^T e)_i. Thus the displayed omega cancels the
function-value coefficients of V_h. The position terms contribute
sym(L_flow(T-I)), where sym(K)=(K+K^T)/2. The squared differences
contribute sym(L_flow)-diag(omega)/2. Their sum with V_h's diagonal
is precisely M_prim: the nonterminal entries become h_i(2-h_i),
and the terminal entry becomes -s(s+2). Hence

    V_h+sum_(i!=j)Lambda_ij Q_ij
       =<M_prim,Gram(g_0,...,g_r)>.

Here Gram is the matrix of all gradient inner products and the matrix
pairing is the entrywise inner product. Accepted Section10 gives
Q_ij<=0 on actual trajectories, so full PSD and all nonnegative
multiplier signs imply V_h>=0. All r+1 formal gradients remain, even
when h_i=0. No duplicated-position quotient, rank assumption, support
restriction or zero-remainder assumption enters this implication.

For a primitive child its capacity is one plus its step sum; for a
dominant child its mass is one plus twice its step sum. A_left,A_right
denote the primitive child capacities, and D_right the dominant right
child mass. Accepted Sections11--12 give the actual physical joins:

* A primitive join uses bridge1+d with
  d(A_left+A_right+d)=A_left*A_right; its capacity is Omega(A_left,A_right).
* A dominant join uses bridge1+A_left/zeta, with
  zeta=(1+sqrt(1+8A_left/D_right))/2; its mass is G(A_left,D_right).

All capacities and masses here are positive. Those sections retain
the original interpolation coordinates and arbitrary child PSD

In a fully expanded B_n tree there are n bridge nodes, n primitive
