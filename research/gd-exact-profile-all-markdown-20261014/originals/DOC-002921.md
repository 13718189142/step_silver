### 7a. The common cycle and its exact recurrence

For any mode z=alpha+i beta, put sigma=|z|^2, kappa=alpha-sigma, and let

    L_r(z)=sum_(i in H_r) h_i |P_i(z)|^2,
    E_r(z)=sum_(i in H_r) h_i^2 sigma |P_i(z)|^2.

    D_r(z)=sum_(i in H_r)
                 h_i sigma(1-h_i alpha+h_i sigma/2)|P_i(z)|^2
                  +(sigma/2)|p_r(z)|^2-alpha+sigma/2.        (30)

The cycle goes from the optimum to the end of H_r, backwards through its

the algebraic weights in(10). Its area and energy alternatively give

    D_r(z)=beta^2 L_r(z)
                 -(kappa/2)[1+|p_r(z)|^2+E_r(z)].            (31)

sign, and the two optimum edges contribute zero area. The cycle energy is

    1-|p_r|^2=2alpha L_r-E_r.

into real scalar blocks and planar complex-multiplication blocks. For a
normalized initial vector, the squared block-coordinate norms give exactly
the weights in(27); initial phases are harmless planar rotations. Therefore
(29) applies equivalently to EVERY jointly legal prefix of the form

    x_i=P_i(A)x_0,  g_i=A x_i,

with one real normal operator A, arbitrary real Gram dimension, arbitrary
legal values, and arbitrary free terminal completion. No spectral restriction
on A is imposed. The finite direct-sum formulation(27) also states the
whole result without using operator terminology.

The horizon after the unit step is2^m. Multiplying(29) and the canonical
Huber bound(20) by B_(2^m)<=1+4*2^(4m/3), proved in Section5, makes both
ratios tend to zero, since c^3>16. This is a full rigorous candidate
obstruction for the chosen NORMAL spectral-mixture ansatz. It does not
upper-bound unrestricted W or exclude a genuinely NONNORMAL kernel.

## Remaining possible repairs

1. Jointly repairing illegal normal modes works in the finite3D calibration,
   but Sections6--7 show it cannot restore the benchmark uniformly on H_m.
   More such normal modes or altered legal values do not avoid(29).
2. A definite nonnormal polynomial kernel can have correlations absent from
   every normal spectral mixture. Replacing it by an arbitrary Gram form
   would merely restate the original problem and is not a quantitative repair.
3. The legal growing Chebyshev constructions remain valid scoped candidates.
   They do not supply the missing uniform comparison on this recursive family.

Multiplying every interpolant by an arbitrary prescribed L>0 restores the
project's smoothness normalization without changing these normalized bounds.
