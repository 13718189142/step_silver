## 3. The off-root bridge identity and its actual correction

This section derives the identity needed for(4). Take two physical words
alpha,beta, each of capacity C, concatenate (alpha,1+d,beta), and suppose
0<=d<C. Let P denote the bridge query, Z the terminal query, and j range
over the nonterminal queries in beta. Write beta_j for the corresponding
physical steps. Define

  c=(C+d)/(C-d), k=1+2c=(3C+d)/(C-d),
  Delta=d(2C+d)-C^2,
  E=sum_j beta_j Q_(j,P)+Q_(Z,P)+d Q_(P,Z)
                                      +sum_j beta_j Q_(j,Z).

Direct coefficient matching yields

  V_(alpha,1+d,beta)
    =V_alpha+k V_beta-2c E
                     +c Delta(||g_Z||^2-||g_P||^2).     (10)

Here is the complete coefficient audit. The value part of E is

  2sum_j beta_j f_j+(d-C)f_P+(2-C-d)f_Z.

Its gradient part, after the physical GD substitution, is

  sum_j beta_j(1-beta_j)||g_j||^2
   +[(C-d)/2-d^2]||g_P||^2+(C+d)||g_Z||^2/2.           (11)

For (11), internal mixed products cancel between the two sums over j.
The bridge-to-tail products cancel against d Q_(P,Z); terminal-to-tail
products cancel against Q_(Z,P); and the bridge-terminal product also
cancels. Thus no mixed entry is omitted.

Subtracting V_alpha+k V_beta from the parent gives the value part

  2(C+d)(f_P-f_Z)-4c sum_j beta_j(f_j-f_Z),

which is the value part of -2c E because c(C-d)=C+d. The bridge-gradient
coefficient before subtracting -2c E is C^2-C-d-d^2; the difference is
C^2-k d^2=-c Delta. The tail internal coefficients are
-2c beta_j(1-beta_j). The terminal-gradient coefficient is
-(2C+d-1)(2C+d)+k(C-1)C; subtracting -c(C+d) leaves c Delta.
The identity c Delta=k d^2-C^2 verifies the two endpoint comparisons.
These checks prove(10) as a polynomial identity in all original gradients
and values.

Away from Delta=0, the final diagonal difference in(10) alone is not a
PSD correction. Define instead

  tau=-c Delta/(C+d)=-Delta/(C-d), eta=2tau.             (12)

Add eta to BOTH actual multipliers on (P,Z) and (Z,P). This is a
zero-divergence coefficient change, performed jointly with the changing
physical word. Put

  zeta=g_P-g_Z,
  J=sum_j beta_j(g_j-g_Z).

The exact resulting remainder is

  M_alpha+k M_beta
     +tau[(C-d)||zeta||^2-2<zeta,J>]                   (13)

or, equivalently,

  M_alpha+k M_beta-tau||J||^2/(C-d)
     +tau(C-d)||zeta-J/(C-d)||^2.                     (14)

To check the correction, x_P-x_Z=(1+d)g_P+sum_j beta_j g_j gives

  Q_(P,Z)+Q_(Z,P)
       =-d||zeta||^2-(C+d)<zeta,g_Z>-<zeta,J>.

Adding twice tau times this expression to
c Delta(||g_Z||^2-||g_P||^2) gives exactly(13).
Thus the full mixed term in(13) is retained and must be paid by real
child slack. No diagonal-only or terminal-only PSD claim is used.
