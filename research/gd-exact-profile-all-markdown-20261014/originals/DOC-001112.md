## 1. Original formal space and the forced kernel

Let h=(h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3) have nonnegative entries, let s=sum_i h_i>0,
and set C=1+s. Indices of formal gradients run from 0 through 4.
Put P_i=sum_(t<i) h_t, so P_0=0 and P_4=s. Let e be the column of
five ones, phi_i=1+P_i, and let T have diagonal 1, entry h_t at (i,t)
when t<i, and zero above its diagonal. Define

    omega=(-2h_0,-2h_1,-2h_2,-2h_3,2s)^T,
    D=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_3(2-h_3),-s(s+2)).

For an array Lambda with zero diagonal let

    L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    M=D+sym(LT),                 sym(A)=(A+A^T)/2.

The required full certificate is precisely

    Lambda_ij>=0 for all i!=j,  L^T e=omega,  M>=0 on R^5.       (1)

It certifies the primitive energy

    V_h=2 sum_(i<4) h_i(f_i-f_4)
        +sum_(i<4)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_4||^2

through V_h+sum Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g_0,...,g_4)>, where
Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 and
x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t. This is the accepted identity, including
all original independent formal gradients at zero steps.

Since Te=e+P and sum_(i<4) h_i P_i=(s^2-sum h_i^2)/2,

    e^T D e=-s^2-sum h_i^2,
    omega^T Te=s^2+sum h_i^2.

Thus every array with the stated flow has e^T M e=0. If M is PSD,
nonnegativity of (e+t z)^T M(e+t z) for both signs of t implies
Me=0. Its individual equations are

    sum_j Lambda_ij(P_j-P_i)=2h_i(1+P_i),  i<4,
    sum_(j<4)Lambda_4j(s-P_j)=2s(s+1).                         (2)

For example, (T^T omega)_i=-2h_i+2h_i(P_i+h_i) at i<4, and direct
substitution gives (Me)_i=h_i(1+P_i)+(LP)_i/2. The terminal component
is -s(s+1)+(LP)_4/2. Consequently (2) is equivalent to Me=0 when
flow is already imposed.

## 2. An explicit nonnegative full-support flow/kernel center

Let k be the last index with h_k>0. Choose any mu strictly between
P_k and s; mu=(P_k+s)/2 is an exact choice. No P_i lies strictly
between P_k and s. Define

    w_i=2h_i/(mu-P_i) if h_i>0, and w_i=0 if h_i=0, i<4,
    w_4=2s/(s-mu),       W=sum_(i=0)^4 w_i,
    Lambda^c_ij=phi_i w_i w_j/W for i!=j, Lambda^c_ii=0.        (3)

All denominators in a nonzero w_i are positive. Thus this array is
nonnegative; if all four steps are positive, every one of its twenty
off-diagonal entries is strictly positive. It has initial-to-interior,
interior-to-initial, and both terminal incidences. Its positive row
factors are not proportional to step length: phi_i w_i/h_i equals
2(1+P_i)/(mu-P_i), which strictly increases with P_i.

To prove its identities, sum w_i(P_i-mu). Nonterminal positive indices
contribute -2h_i, and index 4 contributes 2s. The sum is zero, so

    sum_i w_i P_i=mu W,
    sum_i phi_i w_i=(1+mu)W.                                (4)

The outgoing mass minus incoming mass at node i equals

    phi_i w_i - w_i sum_j(phi_j w_j)/W
      =w_i(P_i-mu)=omega_i.

Its row moment is

    sum_j Lambda^c_ij(P_j-P_i)
      =phi_i w_i(mu-P_i),

which is 2h_i phi_i at i<4 and -2sC at i=4. Therefore this array
satisfies every flow and every constant-kernel equation. Write its
Laplacian and remainder as L_c and M_c.

This is deliberately NOT a PSD claim. A symbolic stress test is the
whole family h=(b,0,0,0), b>0. Formula (3) gives only
Lambda^c_04=2 and Lambda^c_40=2(1+b), independently of mu. Its full
five-dimensional remainder is

    M_c=(2-b^2)(e_0-e_4)(e_0-e_4)^T.                         (5)

Here e_i denotes a standard coordinate column. For b>sqrt(2) this is
negative on a formal direction, despite nonnegative coefficients,
correct flow, and the constant kernel. Zero-step gradients remain in
the matrix; they have zero rows in this particular array. The failure
also occurs with all twenty coefficients positive: for the symbolic
family h_i=b>2 for all i<4, (3) has that support, but
M_00=b(2-b)+(1-b)sum_(j>0)Lambda^c_0j<0. Thus neither positive
support nor the row-moment equations can replace PSD.

The canonical center has the special ratio
Lambda^c_ij/Lambda^c_ji=phi_i/phi_j when both weights are nonzero.
This is only a property of the center, not a restriction in the complete
family in the next section.

## 3. Every full-support completion: twelve unrestricted directions

Let E be the 5-by-4 matrix with columns e_i-e_4, i=0,1,2,3. Let F
be the 5-by-3 matrix with columns

    F_j=e_j-(1-P_j/s)e_0-(P_j/s)e_4, j=1,2,3.                 (6)

The columns of E form a basis of e-perp. The columns of F are
independent, annihilate both e and P under transpose, and form a basis
of span(e,P)-perp. Independence follows from their three middle
coordinates; e and P are independent because P_0=0<P_4=s.

Let X be ANY real 4-by-3 matrix and set

    L(X)=L_c+E X F^T,
    Lambda_ij(X)=Lambda^c_ij-(E X F^T)_ij for i!=j,
    Lambda_ii(X)=0.                                        (7)

Then L(X)e=0, L(X)^T e=omega and M(X)e=0. In particular, L(X)
is the row Laplacian of the displayed extracted array. The proof is
E^T e=0, F^T e=F^T P=0, followed by Te=e+P.

Conversely, let L be ANY real array Laplacian with the required flow
and Me=0, with no multiplier sign restriction yet. Put Delta=L-L_c.
Then Delta e=0, Delta^T e=0 and Delta P=0: the last follows from
0=(M-M_c)e=Delta P/2. Thus its columns lie in e-perp and its rows
lie in span(e,P)-perp. Expanding in these two bases gives uniquely
Delta=E X F^T. This proves exhaustiveness of (7), including arrays
with arbitrary initial remote edges, arbitrary reverse edges, and
arbitrary tail/bridge proportions. In particular, the special ratio
of the center is completely released.

The twenty sign constraints are the explicit affine inequalities

    (E X F^T)_ij <= phi_i w_i w_j/W, i!=j.                    (8)

When h_i>0 for every queried index, X=0 is a strict interior point
of this sign region in all twelve coordinate directions. It need
not satisfy PSD. For zero steps some signs can be on their boundary;
the representation still includes all formal coordinates and all arrays.

## 4. Exact full PSD test and the remaining three skew variables

Let B be the 5-by-4 matrix with columns e_0,...,e_3, and let

    K_c=B^T M_c B,      R=F^T T B.                            (9)

The leading four-dimensional block of M(X) is

    K(X)=K_c+sym(XR),                                       (10)

because B^T E=I. Since M(X)e=0, its entire matrix is

    M(X)=[K(X), -K(X)1; -1^T K(X), 1^T K(X)1].              (11)

Consequently M(X) is PSD on ALL of R^5 if and only if K(X) is PSD.
This is a congruence identity in the differences g_i-g_4, i<4, and
does not set any original formal gradient equal to another.

For explicit entries of K_c, put u_i=phi_i w_i, v_i=w_i/W and
t_i=(T^T v)_i=v_i+h_i sum_(j>i)v_j for i<4. Then L_c=diag(u)-u v^T,
and

    (K_c)_ii=h_i(2-h_i)+u_i(1-t_i),
    2(K_c)_ij=u_j h_i-u_i t_j-u_j t_i, i<j<4.                (12)

These give exact rational functions for rational h and rational mu.

Define p_0=1 and p_(i+1)=(1-h_i)p_i. Then Tp=e. Let

    q=(p_0-p_4,p_1-p_4,p_2-p_4,p_3-p_4)^T,
    c_quad=1-C^2 p_4^2.                                    (13)

The vector q is nonzero: otherwise all p_i=1, forcing every h_i=0.
The kernel of F^T T is span(e,p), since F^T annihilates span(e,P)
and T^-1 P=e-p. Restriction to the subspace with final coordinate
zero shows that R has rank 3 and ker R=span(q).

Also, p^T M_c p=p^T Dp because Tp=e and L_c e=0. The identity
h_i(2-h_i)p_i^2=p_i^2-p_(i+1)^2 telescopes and gives

    q^T K_c q=1-C^2 p_4^2=c_quad.                           (14)

Thus any proposed PSD K in (10) must obey q^T Kq=c_quad.
This condition is also sufficient for SOME real X, without signs.
Here are all such completions. Set H=K-K_c, so q^T Hq=0, and put

    S_0=[q(Hq)^T-(Hq)q^T]/(q^T q).

For an arbitrary skew-symmetric 4-by-4 matrix Z satisfying Zq=0,
let

    X=(H+S_0+Z)R^T(RR^T)^(-1).                             (15)

Indeed S_0 q=-Hq, so A=H+S_0+Z annihilates q. The matrix
R^T(RR^T)^(-1)R is the orthogonal projection onto q-perp;
therefore XR=A, and sym(XR)=H. Conversely any X with (10) has
XR-H skew-symmetric and its value on q is -Hq, giving exactly
S_0+Z. The possible Z form the three-dimensional skew space on
q-perp. No other freedom was omitted.

The COMPLETE feasibility problem at a fixed h is therefore: choose
K>=0 with q^T Kq=c_quad, choose the three skew variables of Z, use
(15), and satisfy ALL twenty inequalities (8). No second kernel is
imposed when c_quad>0. If c_quad=0, PSD forces Kq=0, and only then
the corresponding additional full-space kernel is legitimate.

Choosing a PSD K with the right scalar calibration alone does not solve
the twenty signs. For instance, when c_quad>=0 the formal choice
K=c_quad q q^T/(q^T q)^2 obeys the calibration, but (8) remains an
independent obligation. No universal nonnegative completion of that
rank-one choice is asserted. This is the precise remaining completion
problem, not a claimed certificate.

## 5. The released initial remote edges in Schur coordinates

This section assumes a=h_0>0 only to divide by a. The preceding
lossless coordinates do not make this assumption. Write
z_j=Lambda_0j and y_j=Lambda_j0 for j=1,...,4, and set h_4=0 here

    a z_1+sum_(j=2)^4 P_j z_j=2a,
    sum_(j=1)^4 y_j=2a+sum_(j=1)^4 z_j.                     (16)

The initial diagonal m=M_00 is exactly

    m=2-a^2+(a-1)/a sum_(j=2)^4 (P_j-a)z_j.                 (17)

To check it, first use M_00=a(2-a)+(1-a)sum_j z_j, then
sum_j z_j=2-a^(-1)sum_(j=2)^4(P_j-a)z_j from (16).
For j=2,3,4, the mixed entries are

    2 beta_j=2 M_0j=(a-1)y_j-z_j-h_j sum_(k>j)z_k.          (18)

In gradient differences u=g_0-g_1 and
v=(g_2-g_1,g_3-g_1,g_4-g_1), the full quadratic matrix is

    [m, beta^T; beta, K_tail],  K_tail=M_{[2,3,4],[2,3,4]}.

For m>0, its PSD condition is exactly
K_tail-beta beta^T/m>=0, by completing the square in u.
For m=0 it is exactly beta=0 and K_tail>=0; m<0 is impossible.

Formula (17) displays why keeping only Lambda_04 was a restriction:
Lambda_02 and Lambda_03 have distinct positive displacement weights
when a>1. Formula (18) displays the independent reverse-edge and
mixed-remainder freedoms. The one-chord cap 44fb89e3e8aa8c34 cannot
be extended to these variables without an additional argument. Even

## 6. Two necessary inequalities rederived on the full cone

Every certificate (1) with s>0 satisfies

    h_0,h_3 <= 2(s+1)/(s+2)=2C/(C+1)<2,                   (19)
    C |product_(i=0)^3(1-h_i)| <= 1.                       (20)

For the first endpoint, h_0<=1 is below the bound already. If
h_0>1, its row moment gives 2h_0<=s sum_(j>0)Lambda_0j,
whereas its nonnegative diagonal gives
h_0(2-h_0)>=(h_0-1)sum_(j>0)Lambda_0j. Combining and dividing by
h_0 yields s(2-h_0)>=2(h_0-1), which is (19).

For the last endpoint h_3=0 is immediate. If h_3>0, the terminal
row moment and terminal diagonal give

    2s(s+1)=sum_(j<4)Lambda_4j(s-P_j)
             >=h_3 sum_(j<4)Lambda_4j
             >=h_3 s(s+2),

which proves its bound. No prefix or tail is assumed primitive.

Finally the telescope in (14) gives p^T M p=1-C^2 p_4^2 for every
array Laplacian, since Tp=e and Le=0. PSD makes it nonnegative.
Equivalently one may use the actual scalar function f(x)=x^2/2,
whose gradients are p_i and whose finite interpolation residuals all
vanish. This proves (20) without a saturation assumption.

## 7. Global obstruction to copying the endpoints into the interior

Define the endpoint-pairing distance of a schedule by

    d_pair(h)=min{
       max(|h_1-h_0|,|h_2-h_3|),
       max(|h_2-h_0|,|h_1-h_3|)}.                          (21)

This is an explicitly declared restriction on STEP DATA, not on the
twenty multipliers, their support, their skew part, or the rank of M.
The family d_pair=0 consists of the two arrangements (u,u,v,v) and
(u,v,u,v); nearby words need not have any exact symmetry or ordering.

Claim: there is no certificate (1) with C>=193/25 and
d_pair(h)<=1/20. This excludes the whole closed neighborhood,
including curved or distant changes to every coefficient.

Proof. Put delta=1/20. By (21) the two interior steps, in one of
the two orders, are h_0+epsilon and h_3+eta with
|epsilon|,|eta|<=delta. Put a=h_0-1, d=h_3-1, S=a+d. Then

    C=5+2S+epsilon+eta,
    S >= (C-5-2delta)/2 >=131/100.                         (22)

By (19) each endpoint is below 2. Thus C<9+2delta=91/10, and
another application of (19) gives

    a,d <=(C-1)/(C+1)<81/101<81/100=:r_bar.                (23)

It follows that a,d>S-r_bar>=1/2. In particular every step is
strictly greater than 1, so the absolute value in (20) equals the
positive product of all four step excesses. Also S<=2r_bar=81/50.
Writing p=ad and expanding (r_bar-a)(r_bar-d)>=0 yields

    p>=r_bar(S-r_bar)>=(81/100)(1/2)=81/200.               (24)

The four excesses have product

    a d (a+epsilon)(d+eta)
       >=p(a-delta)(d-delta)
       =p(p-delta S+delta^2)
       >=(81/200)(81/200-81/1000+1/400)
       =(81/200)(653/2000)
       =52893/400000 >13/100.                            (25)

Both factors in the penultimate lower bound are positive, so all
multiplications of bounds preserve their directions. Equations (20)
and (25) contradict each other because

    C product_i(h_i-1) > (193/25)(13/100)=2509/2500>1.

This proves the claim for the entire stated family, including all
nonnegative step boundary cases: (22)--(23) themselves force those
boundary cases out before (20) is used.

Consequently any actual threshold seed must have d_pair(h)>1/20.
Changing only full support or PSD rank while staying within that step
neighborhood cannot work. The exact result is a necessary quantitative
asymmetry, not a claim that asymmetry suffices. The remaining family
d_pair>1/20 is not excluded here.

## 8. Incorporating the missing clipping constraint with matched hypotheses

The exact interface above must still obey the legal-function constraints
identified in drafts 399341aaa6b09bdb and 7cd0bb4a795ee1df. Here is a
fresh derivation, rather than an import of either draft as a premise.

Suppose h=(1+a,1+b,1+c,1+d) with a,b,c,d>0, so
C=5+a+b+c+d. For alpha>0 let

    f_alpha(x)=x-1/2 for x>=1,
               x^2/2 for -alpha<=x<=1,
               -alpha x-alpha^2/2 for x<=-alpha.

Its derivative is clamp(x,[-alpha,1]), which is continuous,
nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz, and 0 is its unique minimizer. At every
point f_alpha(x)=x g-g^2/2. Telescoping the actual GD update gives

    V_h=x_0^2-(x_4+s g_4)^2.                              (26)

Indeed sum_i[2h_i x_i g_i-h_i^2 g_i^2]=x_0^2-x_4^2;
inserting the saturated values in V_h leaves -2s x_4g_4-s^2g_4^2.

Take x_0=P=2+a and alpha=b/(2+c). The actual points are

    x_1=1, x_2=-b, x_3=-alpha, x_4=d alpha,
    g_0=g_1=1, g_2=g_3=-alpha, g_4=min(d alpha,1).

If d alpha<=1, (26) is P^2-C^2 d^2 alpha^2. If d alpha>1,

    C b d <= (2+a)(2+c).                                 (27)

This matches the hypotheses of 399341aaa6b09bdb exactly and uses

For 0<alpha<=b set A_cap=b/H and B_cap=b/(2+c); both lie in (0,b)

## 9. Outcome, failed shortcuts, and exact next freedom

The original requested threshold certificate has not been constructed.
The concrete outcome is a whole-family obstruction (21)--(25), plus a
lossless full-support construction interface for schedules outside it.
The smallest parameter restriction removed by the obstruction is explicit:
at least one interior-to-endpoint discrepancy must exceed 1/20 under
each possible matching. The coefficient problem after that release is
exactly (8)--(15), or its full initial-row Schur form (16)--(18).

Two tempting shortcuts were checked symbolically and rejected. Taking
the dense positive center already solves flow and the constant kernel,
but (5) proves that it does not solve PSD in general. Choosing a PSD
remainder with only the quadratic calibration solves the real linear
representation problem, but it leaves all twenty affine signs in (8),
and all actual clipping witnesses still apply. No argument here excludes
curved or distant gains outside the explicitly defined paired family.

The short literature check found no directly applicable theorem for the
new positive center. The exact local Zhang--Jiang source (arXiv:2410.12395,
Definition 7, Proposition 8, Theorem 11 and its proof) was read to check
the primitive convention. No new external theorem is used above; the
graph-weight analogy is elementary algebra inside the already accepted
finite certificate interface.

Self-audit: justification is explicit coefficient algebra and exact
inequalities, with special attention to the off-diagonal sign convention,
zero steps, the rank of R, and absence of an unjustified second kernel.
All mathematical conclusions remain unverified drafts. No fact_id is
claimed for them, and the original minimax target remains open.
