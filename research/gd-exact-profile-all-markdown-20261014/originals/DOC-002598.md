## 3. One controlled auxiliary coordinate repairs all pairs

Choose

    kappa=1/512, t0=1/8,
    xi_0=1, xi_(i+1)=(1-kappa h_i)xi_i.

Every factor is positive since h_i<=S<256. For any initial segment,
the elementary product inequality

    product_t(1-a_t)>=1-sum_t a_t  (a_t>=0, sum a_t<1)

follows inductively from (1-a)(1-b)>=1-a-b. It gives

    1>=xi_i>=1-kappa S>1/2.

For i<j, positivity and h_i>1 imply

    xi_i-xi_j>=xi_i-xi_(i+1)=kappa h_i xi_i>kappa/2.

The distance to the optimum sample xi_star=0 is also greater than
kappa/2. Thus these bounds cover every distinct pair, including star.

Define the66-dimensional jets and values

    X_i=(z_i,t0 xi_i),
    G_i=(q_i,t0 kappa xi_i),
    f_i=u_i+t0^2 kappa xi_i^2/2
       =u_i+xi_i^2/65536, 0<=i<=63,
    X_star=G_star=0, f_star=0.                      (6)

Their GD recursion is EXACT by (2) and the recursion for xi. The full
interpolation residual separates by coordinates into

    Q_ij=Q^core_ij
          -t0^2 kappa(1-kappa)(xi_i-xi_j)^2/2.      (7)

This is a polynomial identity, not a claimed separability of the final
function. The second term is the residual for the single quadratic

## 5. A concrete feature of the surviving geometry

The rational core gradients have the exact inner product

    <q_35,q_36>
      =-14715168270572693173715883215/10^30
      <-14/1000.

The extra coordinate changes this by at most t0^2 kappa^2=2^(-24),
since0<xi_i<=1. Thus

    <G_35,G_36><-1/100.

Both indices are queried. The actual object retains substantial signed
gradient correlation; it is not a Huber common-gradient line, a mutually
orthogonal gradient transcript, or an all-gradients acute-cone trajectory.
The auxiliary coordinate repairs small interpolation errors in an already
strong correlated core, in contrast to attempting to repair the grossly
illegal fixed scalar C55 template. No claim is made that this description
is a scalable generating rule.

There is also a basis-independent distinction from a bounded-jump
coordinate construction. Let d_i=G_i-G_(i-1),1<=i<=63, and let J be their
Gram matrix. If an orthonormal coordinate system makes every coordinate
of the gradient list change at most k times, then

    2 sum_(i<j)|J_ij| <= (k-1) trace(J).             (10)

Indeed each scalar coordinate of the jump list has at most k nonzero
entries. Cauchy--Schwarz gives (sum|a_i|)^2<=k sum a_i^2, hence
2sum_(i<j)|a_i a_j|<=(k-1)sum a_i^2. Sum over coordinates and apply the
triangle inequality to the resulting off-diagonal entries. Since J is
unchanged by orthogonal changes of physical coordinates, (10) applies to
every such representation.

For the exact rational core, the integer increment Gram is saved PUBLICLY
in `research/max3_c59_exact_increment_gram.json`. Its entries follow
directly from the rational vectors already defined, with denominator D^2.
Exact integer summation gives

    trace(J_core) approximately 0.44249400386221016,
    2sum_(i<j)|J_core_ij| approximately 2.306374594722243,
    2sum_(i<j)|J_core_ij|-5trace(J_core)>9/100.       (11)

Only the last, rational inequality is used. The auxiliary jump vector is
a_i=t0 kappa(xi_i-xi_(i-1)); all entries are negative and
(sum|a_i|)^2<t0^2 kappa^2=2^(-24). Its contribution is aa^T. By the
triangle inequality, adding aa^T can lower the left side of (11) by at
most

    2sum_(i<j)|a_i a_j|+5sum_i a_i^2
           <=5(sum_i|a_i|)^2<5*2^(-24).

Thus the full repaired J still satisfies
2sum_(i<j)|J_ij|>5trace(J). Equation (10) rules out every representation
in which all coordinate gradients have at most SIX chronological jumps.
In particular this actual finite adversary is outside the common two-jump
primal cone. The large core margin in (11) ensures this distinction is
not caused only by the tiny auxiliary quadratic coordinate.

## 6. Scope and next open interface

This candidate is finite, exact in its definitions, and includes all
function values, gradients, positions, actual GD equations, and the true
distance. It does not rely on a numerically feasible Gram or a nearly
positive semidefinite dual. The source solver arrays are preserved
unchanged; the rational data and added coordinate are separately labeled
new objects.

The estimate rules out1/M6 for this one actual word. It does NOT refute an
upper bound valid only at sufficiently large horizons, establish a common
pattern for the changing parameters epsilon=1/m, or prove the all-schedule
Zhang--Jiang profile. In particular the length63 block changes physically
as the enclosing horizon changes. The missing scalable interface is an
analytic rule for the correlated core that controls ALL interpolation
pairs and distance under those parameter changes. Repeating this finite
data set, extrapolating its numerical eigenvectors, or fixing only one
turning pair would not provide that rule.

No routine verification allowance was used. This remains a complete
finite proof candidate, not an accepted fact.
