# Two distinct exact projection faces at the five-step source word

Status: **joint unverified proof draft**, cumulative round17. No optimization
or verifier call. This is a finite structural result, not a local/global
optimality claim and not the original asymptotic minimax theorem.

The normalized performance W_n and B_n are as in accepted facts
1252b8823bb5a290 and b98bb807d514e80d. The proof below supplies its own
functions and all ordered-pair checks, rather than relying on an unverified
earlier finding.

## 1. Source word and one common construction convention

Write s=sqrt(2), theta=s-1, nu=theta^2=3-2s,
d0=sqrt(7+4s)-1, gamma=d0/4, and B0=(d0+2)^2. The source word is

    h0=(s,2,s,1+d0,3/2).

The exact recurrence permits this primitive-three-step/dominant-one-step
join, so B_5>=B0. Equality of those two finite recurrence values is not
needed below and is not inferred from a floating-point maximizing split.

For any compact convex K containing0 use

    F_K(x)=max_(g in K)(<x,g>-||g||^2/2).

The maximizer is the unique projection P_K(x). Its defining normal is
<v-P_K(x),x-P_K(x)><=0 for every vertex v. Two such normals imply
||P_K(x')-P_K(x)||^2<=<x'-x,P_K(x')-P_K(x)>, proving 1-Lipschitzness.
The inequalities obtained by evaluating the two maxima at each other's
maximizers bound the differentiability remainder by ||x'-x||^2. Thus F_K
is convex differentiable and 1-smooth. Its minimizer set is
K^polar={z:<z,g><=0 for every g in K}: the reverse implication follows by
testing epsilon*g and letting epsilon decrease to zero. If X0 belongs to
the positive cone of K, then0 is a closest minimizer, since
<X0,z><=0 implies ||X0-z||^2>=||X0||^2 for z in K^polar.

## 2. A continuum with x1=g1 and x3=g3

Near the source word, write the five steps as

    h=(a,b,c,1+d,e),   z=b-1,   D=2e+1.

For a parameter r>0 define

    U=1/(a+1)^2,
    kappa=-[z^2+(c+1)r]/[z(c+2)],
    u=(1/(a+1),0),
    v=(kappa,sqrt(r-kappa^2))/(a+1),
    w=-z*u-c*v,
    k=-(d/D)*w,
    J=[z^2(2-c)+c(c^2-2)r]/(c+2).                            (1)

Whenever these vectors satisfy the normal conditions below, use
K=conv(0,u,v,w,k) and the exact trajectory

    x0=(a+1)u, g0=u;
    x1=u,     g1=u;
    x2=-z*u,  g2=v;
    x3=w,     g3=w;
    x4=D*k,   g4=k;
    x5=(e+1)k,g5=k.                                           (2)

Every update follows from (1). The choice of kappa imposes the exact identity
<w-v,-z*u-v>=0. Also ||w||^2=J*U by expansion. The attained payoff is

    E_r(h)=d^2*J/[D*(a+1)^2].                                (3)

At the source a=c=s,b=2,d=d0,e=3/2, (1) reduces to

    kappa=-(r+theta)/s,  J=nu,
    <u,w>/U=r+s-2,  <v,w>/U=(theta-r)/s.                     (4)

The Gram condition r>=kappa^2 is exactly nu<=r<=1. At sample0 the normal
against u is zero. Against v and w it is nonpositive because
kappa<0 and r+s-2<1; against k it follows from
-gamma*(r+s-2)<=gamma*theta<1. Against0 it is negative.
Samples1 and3 have zero residual. At sample2, residual y=-u-v, the normals
against0 andu in units of U are respectively theta*(r-1)/s and r-1.
The w normal is zero by construction. If T=<v,y>=theta*(1-r)*U/s>=0,
the k normal is -(1+gamma)*T. This covers every vertex at sample2.

For samples4 and5 the residual is a nonnegative multiple of k. The normals
against0,w,k hold directly. Against u andv they are equivalent to

    r+s-2 >= -gamma*nu,
    (theta-r)/s >= -gamma*nu.

It follows that the EXACT admissible r interval within this construction is

    r_minus=2-s-gamma*nu <= r <= theta+s*gamma*nu=r_plus.       (5)

Indeed (5) lies strictly inside [nu,1]. Its width is
nu*((s+1)*gamma-1)>0. For instance the rational square bounds
1.4142<s<1.4143 and 3.557<d0+1<3.558 give
gamma>theta and gamma*theta<1, proving these assertions.

Thus the source has a continuum of legal full-model witnesses all giving
E_r(h0)=1/B0: use 2(3+2s)=d0(d0+2) in (3). All old/new normals were retained. This is a continuum inside
the complete feasible set, not a claimed description of every tight primal.

For every fixed r strictly inside (5), all non-identical constraints just
checked are strict. Zero residuals and the w-v equality persist identically
in (1)-(2). Therefore (1)-(3) remain legal for every sufficiently small
perturbation of all five steps, with r held fixed. The square root also
remains real by the strict Gram condition. Since u,v are independent and
z*u+c*v+w=0 with positive coefficients,0 lies inside conv(u,v,w); the
minimizer is then unique and x0=(1,0) has true distance1.

## 3. The associated necessary first-order cone

Let h(t)=h0+t*v+o(t), t decreasing to0 through positive values, where
v=(v0,v1,v2,v3,v4) is the five-coordinate step velocity. Differentiate (3)
at h0. Direct differentiation of J in (1) gives

    L_r(v):=(log E_r(h(t)))'_(t=0)
      =-2*v0/(s+1)+2*v1+[2(s+2)r-2]*v2
         +2*v3/d0-v4/2.                                     (6)

Hence

    liminf_(t down to0) [B0*W_5(h(t))-1]/t
         >= max{L_(r_minus)(v),L_(r_plus)(v)}.                (7)

To justify the endpoints, first apply the lower witness to each fixed
interior r, then take the supremum of the affine function L_r over that
interval. No exchange of an optimized SDP and differentiation is used.

Any direction whose performance decreases to first order below1/B0 must
have both endpoint expressions nonpositive. The balanced dense direction
tested in the closed experiment is

    v=(-p0,q0,-p0,-m0/4,0),
    p0=(16-11s)/3, q0=(8s-10)/3, m0=10s-14.

Here EVERY L_r in (5) is positive. In fact r_plus<.57, so
2(s+2)r-2<1.9. The bounds
.147<p0<.149, .437<q0<.439, .140<m0<.145 and d0>2.55 give

    L_r > .294/2.415+.874-1.9*.149-.145/5.10 > 2/3.           (8)

This explains that local failure by exact common-function witnesses,
independently of the reselected dual array.

## 4. An independent face with a nonzero residual at sample1

The preceding cone is incomplete. A different exact construction works for
ALL a,c,e>=0, b>1 and d>0. Put

    z=b-1,  r=z/(c+1),  D=2e+1,
    U_vec=(1,0), V_vec=(0,sqrt(r)), k=-(d/D)*V_vec,
    K=conv(0,U_vec,V_vec,k),
    X0=(a+b)*U_vec+(c+1)*V_vec.

The full trajectory is

    X1=b*U_vec+(c+1)*V_vec,
    X2=(c+1)*V_vec,
    X3=V_vec,
    X4=D*k,
    X5=(e+1)*k,

with gradients U_vec,U_vec,V_vec,V_vec,k,k in chronological order.
At X0 the normal against V_vec is
r(c+1)-(a+b-1)=-a<=0. Against0 andk it is also nonpositive, since
a+b-1=a+z>0. At X1 the V_vec normal is zero because r(c+1)=z,
while0 andk have nonpositive normals. At X2 the residual is c*V_vec,
so the normals against the orthogonal U_vec,0, and the opposite k all
hold. X3 has zero residual. At X4 andX5 the residual is a nonnegative
multiple of k; U_vec is orthogonal to k and V_vec is opposite to k,
so all normals hold. These are every original projection condition.

Also X0 belongs to the positive cone of U_vec,V_vec, so0 is a closest
minimizer. The exact attained normalized payoff is

    E_orth(h)=d^2*r/[D*((a+b)^2+r(c+1)^2)]
            =d^2*z/[D*(c+1)*((a+b)^2+z(c+1))].               (9)

At h0, (3+2s)^2*theta=7+5s=(s+2)^2+(s+1), proving
E_orth(h0)=1/B0. Its logarithmic derivative is

    L_orth(v)=(2nu-2theta)*(v0+v2)+2nu*v1
                 +2*v3/d0-v4/2.                            (10)

For example the coefficient of v0 in log(9) is
-2(s+2)/(7+5s)=2nu-2theta; the coefficient of v1 is
1-[2(s+2)+(s+1)]/(7+5s)=2nu. The c coefficient equals the a coefficient.
The last two coefficients follow directly from d^2/D.

This family retains a nonzero proximal residual X1-U_vec. It is therefore
not contained in the x1=g1 construction in section2. No ambient-rank or
dual-support argument licenses discarding it.

## 5. A concrete direction missed by the first cone and scalar screens

Take the exact velocity

    v_escape=(1,1/2,-1,-1/20,-3/10).                           (11)

For sufficiently small positive t every actual step of h0+t*v_escape
is positive. The three standard legal lower tests all decrease at t=0:

* The global Huber test has logarithmic derivative
  -2*sum(v_escape)/B0=-3/(10B0)<0.
* The unit-curvature quadratic test has logarithmic derivative
  2*(1/2-1/(20d0)-3/5)=-1/5-1/(10d0)<0.
* The cut at the fourth step has logarithmic derivative
  -1/(10d0)-1/(3+2s)+3/20=-1/(10d0)-nu+3/20<0.

All witnesses in the continuum (5) also decrease. Substitution in (6) gives

    L_r(v_escape)=63/20-2theta-2(s+2)r-1/(10d0)<0.

For an entirely rational bound, (5) gives r_minus>.475; then
63/20-2*.4142-2*3.4142*.475<0, even after dropping the final negative term.

Nevertheless (10) gives

    L_orth(v_escape)=nu+3/20-1/(10d0)>1/4,                   (12)

because nu>.17 and d0>2.5. Thus for all sufficiently small positive t,
the exact legal function in section4 has normalized payoff strictly above
1/B0, and hence W_5(h0+t*v_escape)>1/B0>=1/B_5.

This is a counterexample to the sufficiency of the stated first-order
screen, not a counterexample to the minimax conjecture. No additional
concrete numerical case was evaluated or optimization proposed. The exact
direction (11) is an analytic stress test with its own explicit defeating
function.

The outcome explains a real limitation of the previously selected active
geometry: fixing the first post-step point to equal its gradient deletes
another source-tight face. It does not prove that the enlarged collection
is complete or that the source word is locally optimal. A proof using the
entire active set across growing horizons is still missing.
