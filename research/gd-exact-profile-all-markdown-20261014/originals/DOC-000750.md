# A fixed quadratic core with an exact terminal affine cap

Status: complete new candidate derivation, unverified. Worker: max3.
This repairs one specific deficiency of the verified outward-ray construction:
the prefix may return inside a previously visited region arbitrarily many
times, because one fixed quadratic governs all those returns.

## Legal function and exact trajectory

Fix any n>=1 and nonnegative finite schedule h, any lambda in (0,1], a sign
sigma in {-1,+1}, and an index j in {0,...,n-1}. Define prefix polynomials

\[
p_0(\lambda)=1,\qquad p_t(\lambda)=\prod_{r<t}(1-h_r\lambda).
\]

Define

\[
R=\max\{0,\sigma p_t(\lambda):0\leq t\leq j\},\qquad
A=\sigma p_{j+1}(\lambda),\qquad T=\sum_{r>j}h_r.
\]

Assume A>0 and A>=(1+lambda T)R. Choose a cap radius

\[
r\in[R,A/(1+\lambda T)].
\]

The explicit function is

\[
f(x)=\begin{cases}
\lambda x^2/2,&\sigma x\leq r,\\
\lambda r\sigma x-\lambda r^2/2,&\sigma x\geq r.
\end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]

The two branches and their derivatives agree at sigma x=r. Its derivative
is continuous, nondecreasing, and lambda-Lipschitz, hence at most1-Lipschitz;
it is convex and differentiable and has minimum0 at0. These properties also
hold when r=0, in which case one side is flat. The entire function is fixed
before the GD run, after observing the predetermined schedule.

Start x_0=1. For every query through j, sigma p_t(lambda)<=r. An induction
therefore gives x_t=p_t(lambda) and f'(x_t)=lambda p_t(lambda) through that
prefix. All sign changes and inner returns in the prefix are legal without
resetting the function or dropping older values. The update at j gives
sigma x_{j+1}=A. On the suffix the derivative is the fixed value
sigma lambda r. Nonnegative steps and A>=(1+lambda T)r imply that every
suffix iterate stays in the capped region, since its signed coordinate is at
least A-lambda rT>=r. Thus

\[
\sigma x_n=A-\lambda rT,\qquad
2f(x_n)=2\lambda rA-\lambda(1+2\lambda T)r^2.
\tag{2}
\]

The initial squared distance to the minimizer is1. Multiplying (1) by L
gives the same normalized error for the original h_t/L steps at arbitrary
L>0. The best radius in the displayed interval is

\[
r_{\rm opt}=\max\{R,A/(1+2\lambda T)\}.
\tag{3}
\]

The right endpoint is never exceeded by (3), because T>=0. This gives a
fully explicit lower-bound functional after maximizing over feasible
(lambda,sigma,j). The record R is essential: choosing a smaller radius would
change the already used prefix function and invalidate the polynomial formula.

## Remaining target obligation

No claim is made that the supremum of (2), even together with F_ray_n and Q_n,
matches the exact ZJ profile for every schedule. The specific new mechanism
is a legal quadratic prefix, including inner returns, followed by a nonlinear
continuation constrained by an exact remote-excursion record. A next extension
could allow a fixed two-curvature core or repeated cap exits and returns, but
must preserve the old record and values instead of independently resampling
curvature. Any uniform profile comparison still needs an actual proof.
