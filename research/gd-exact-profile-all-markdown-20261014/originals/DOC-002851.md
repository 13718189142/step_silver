c=lambda(1+1/m), a=beta/c, r=2/c, w_j=r^j/sum_(k<m)r^k.

There is an orthonormal coordinate e_v for each binary word v of length
less than m. For a leaf s of length m define
v_s=sum_(j<m)(-1)^(s_(j+1))*sqrt(w_j)*e_(s_1...s_j).
The full body K_m is the convex hull of these 2^m vectors, in dimension
2^m-1. Each leaf has squared norm sum w_j=1. The feature matrix rows
have zero sum and are pairwise orthogonal: disjoint subtrees have zero
product; a descendant's two signs cancel on an ancestor's constant
sign region. Every row is nonzero. Thus the matrix has rank 2^m-1 and
kernel the constant leaf vector. The leaves are affinely independent,
their average is zero, and zero is interior to K_m. In particular this
is the CENTERED simplex without a carrier or an extra dimension.

Let K_scaled=delta*K_m and
F_scaled(x)=max_(g in K_scaled)(<x,g>-||g||^2/2).
Strict convexity of squared distance gives a unique maximizer g, and
completing the square identifies

The projection characterization is <x-g,v-g><=0 for every vertex v.
Necessity follows by a derivative along the segment from g to v;
sufficiency follows by expanding ||x-v||^2-||x-g||^2. Adding the two
normal inequalities at x,x' gives ||g-g'||^2<=<g-g',x-x'>, proving
1-Lipschitz continuity. The maximizing formulas sandwich
F_scaled(x+d)-F_scaled(x)-<g,d> between zero and
<P_(delta*K_m)(x+d)-g,d>, bounded by ||d||^2. Hence F_scaled is convex
and differentiable with the stated gradient and global smoothness L=1.
Inside the body the gradient equals x; an open ball is contained there,
so no smaller global Lipschitz constant is possible. Every gradient has
norm at most delta by convexity and the unit leaf norms. Along t*v_left
with t>=delta its norm equals delta, so this cap is sharp.
