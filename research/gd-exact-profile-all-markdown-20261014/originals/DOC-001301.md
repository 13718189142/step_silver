## 1. An actual legal counterexample to a radius-only metric extension

Take the schedule (10,1). Its retained queried prefix is the single step10,
so its retained mass is S=10, with final free step b=1 in [0,2]. Use three
positive real modal parameters

        z_1=1/100, z_2=2/100, z_3=3/100.

Every S z_j is at most3/10<1/3. Let

        alpha=(1128,-2303,1176).

Direct integer arithmetic gives

 sum alpha_j=1,  sum alpha_j z_j=1/2,
                         sum alpha_j z_j^2=1/4.        (1)

Define an injective real linear map A:R^3->R^4 by

 A(v_1,v_2,v_3)=(alpha_1 v_1+alpha_2 v_2+alpha_3 v_3,
                                  v_1,v_2,v_3)/2.     (2)

Its metric is the strictly positive definite matrix
A^T A=(alpha alpha^T+I_3)/4; it includes nonzero initial correlations.
Also ||A(1,1,1)||=1 exactly. Define, on R^4,

 F(X)=X_0^2/4+X_1^2/200+X_2^2/100+3X_3^2/200.          (3)

This function is convex and differentiable, has gradient Lipschitz
constant1/2<=1, and has its unique minimizer at0. Thus it is admissible
for normalized smoothness1. Start at x_0=(1,1,1,1)/2. Its gradient is
g_0=(1/2,1/100,2/100,3/100)/2. The actual first step gives

 x_1=(-4,9/10,8/10,7/10)/2,
 g_1=(-2,9/1000,16/1000,21/1000)/2.                     (4)

For P_0(z)=1 and P_1(z)=1-10z, identities (1) show exactly that

 x_i=A(P_i(z_1),P_i(z_2),P_i(z_3)),
 g_i=A(z_1 P_i(z_1),z_2 P_i(z_2),z_3 P_i(z_3)), i=0,1. (5)

The last gradient g_2 is unrestricted by the modal ansatz. The actual
last step x_2=x_1-g_1 has first coordinate -1, so

                        2F(x_2) > 1/2.                (6)

Every prefix/optimum interpolation inequality holds, since these are
actual jets of the explicit global function (3). The initial distance
is exactly one and the minimizer is genuinely0. Thus this is an actual
legal polynomial-image trajectory, with a FULL SPD common metric,
three small positive REAL modes, and an allowed final step.

In particular it refutes the proposed upgrade that arbitrary common
metrics at S|z|<=1/3 must obey the normal-mixture Huber cap
2F(x_1-b g_1)<=1/[2S+1+b(2-b)]. That right side is1/22 at b=1, whereas
(6) is larger than1/2. The earlier normal and bounded-coupling theorems
did not assert such an upgrade, and this example does not contradict
them. It also does not contradict the specific two-complex-mode
near-critical obstruction in finding cebf601250c82dc2: the spectra,
chronology, and metric assumptions are different.

## 2. Why the scope changes when the number of modes grows

This is a warning about extrapolation, not a proposed arbitrary-Gram
research program. For a positive schedule of length n>=1, select ANY
n+1 distinct positive real nodes theta_j, even inside an arbitrarily
small interval. Let P_i(z)=product_(t<i)(1-h_t z). The polynomials

                  1, zP_0(z), ..., zP_(n-1)(z)          (7)

have successive degrees0,1,...,n and nonzero leading coefficients.
They form a basis of polynomials of degree at most n. Their evaluations
at the n+1 distinct nodes are an invertible square matrix: a linear
combination vanishing at all nodes would be a polynomial of degree at
most n with n+1 roots, and hence zero; its basis coefficients must vanish.
This is the same polynomial-degree argument used in the strict-primal
proof of accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290.

Given ANY actual normalized legal prefix vectors x_0,g_0,...,g_(n-1),
define a real linear map A on the basis evaluation columns in (7) by

 A(1,...,1)=x_0,
 A((theta_j P_i(theta_j))_j)=g_i.

Invertibility defines A uniquely. The polynomial identity
P_i(z)=1-sum_(t<i)h_t zP_t(z) then gives

 A((P_i(theta_j))_j)=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t=x_i

for all0<=i<=n, including the unqueried final position. Thus every legal
prefix has this modal representation at arbitrarily small positive nodes
when the full PSD metric A^T A is unrestricted. The prefix values are
unchanged, the initial norm is unchanged, and the terminal gradient stays
free, exactly as required in the reduced formulation of fact1252.

Zero steps cause identical queried polynomials and identical actual
jets at those positions. Retain one representative of each distinct
queried polynomial. Their degrees are distinct. They can be extended
to a basis of all polynomials of degree at most n, and the unused basis
vectors can be assigned arbitrary images, giving the same representation
with n+1 nodes. This also directly covers a last queried zero step,
without assuming an unqueried endpoint gradient identity.

Consequently the unrestricted full-metric, n+1-node modal formulation
with every joint interpolation constraint and a free terminal gradient
is exactly as strong as the full original fixed-schedule problem. It
does not furnish a new lower bound by itself. A radius argument alone
cannot make this enlarged formulation simpler. A scalable proof must
identify additional genuine structure, or solve the full interpolation
problem it encodes.

## 3. Implication for the next model

The completed two-mode bound controls a fixed four-dimensional temporal
polynomial span, not merely the ambient Euclidean dimension or spectral
radius. Changing only the metric within that precise span is now closed
by the candidate proof. A new direction must change the temporal span or
the trajectory mechanism and still prove all joint legality and the
correct benchmark payoff. Neither the finite example (6) nor the exact
representation (7) is an asymptotic answer to the original problem.
