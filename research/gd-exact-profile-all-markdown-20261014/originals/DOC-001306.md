# Joint analytic prerequisites (UNVERIFIED, max54)

These are rederived here as one draft. No unverified earlier finding is
used as an accepted premise, and no formal gate is authorized this round.
All m used below are integers >=2, so beta,c,a,w are positive.

For each internal node v of the full binary tree of depth m let e_v be
an orthonormal vector. The leaf vector a_s is the sum over its ancestors
at depths j of (-1)^(next bit)*sqrt(w_j)e_v. Its norm squared is sum w_j=1.
Every coordinate row of the leaf matrix has zero sum. Distinct rows are
orthogonal: disjoint supports have zero product; on a nested pair the
descendant's two signs cancel where the ancestor is constant. Each row
has positive norm. The matrix consequently has rank2^m-1 and kernel the
constant leaf vector. Thus the leaves are affinely independent, average0,
and their convex hull K has0 in its full Euclidean interior.

For any x the unique maximizer of <x,g>-||g||^2/2 over compact K is the
minimizer of ||x-g||^2. Its characterization is
g in K and <x-g,v-g><=0 for every vertex v. Necessity follows by the
one-sided derivative along g+t(v-g); sufficiency by expansion of distance.
Adding these inequalities for two projections yields
||g-g'||^2<=<g-g',x-x'>, hence the projection is1-Lipschitz.
The two maximizing formulas sandwich the first-order error of F between
0 and <P_K(x+d)-P_K(x),d>, bounded by||d||^2. This proves convexity,
differentiability and grad F=P_K. On the interior of K, grad F(x)=x,
so smoothness1 is sharp. F>=0. If x!=0, a sufficiently small positive
multiple t*x belongs to K and <x,t*x>-||t*x||^2/2>0. Thus the unique
minimizer is0. At x0=a_(0^m) the true squared minimum distance is1.

Permuting the two children of off-spine tree nodes is an orthogonal signed
coordinate transformation preserving K and fixing every spine vector.
Uniqueness makes projection commute with these transformations. Their
fixed subspace consists exactly of the m coordinates e_(0^j): swapping
the children at any off-spine node negates that coordinate. GD from x0
therefore stays in this subspace at every physical query.

Group leaves by their first right turn k<m, plus the final all-left group
k=m. Averaging a group cancels every off-spine coordinate and gives b_k,
whose j coordinate is sqrt(w_j) for j<k, -sqrt(w_k) for j=k<m,
and0 for j>k; b_m has all coordinates sqrt(w_j). Averaging any convex
representation shows K intersect spine=conv{b_0,...,b_m}. Conversely each
b_k is such an average, so lies in K. They are affinely independent.
For a spine residual y, <y,a_s> depends only on the first-right group and
equals <y,b_k>. Therefore checking all m+1 reduced normals checks every
one of the2^m original full-leaf normals, without assuming sparsity.
A group mass p_k is expanded uniformly over2^(m-k-1) leaves for k<m;
p_m is the last leaf mass. This gives full feasibility from reduced masses.

Write x_j=sqrt(w_j)z_j and let q_j=sum_(k>=j)p_k, with q_0=1.
Then g_j=sqrt(w_j)(2q_(j+1)-q_j), and its exact projection uniquely
minimizes sum_j w_j[z_j-(2q_(j+1)-q_j)]^2 subject to
1=q_0>=q_1>=...>=q_m>=0. This is the true feature metric; ordinary
Euclidean probability-simplex thresholding changes it.

For fresh core membership solve q_(j+1)=(q_j+z_j)/2 and take
p_j=q_j-q_(j+1), p_m=q_m. An independent equivalent formula is
p_k=2^(-k-1)[1+sum_(j<k)2^j z_j-2^k z_k] for k<m,
p_m=2^(-m)[1+sum_(j<m)2^j z_j]. Affine independence proves membership
iff every p is nonnegative. Only at that input does this imply g=x;
it says nothing by itself about the next input or any later face.

For any nonempty candidate face I with base vertex v0 and edge matrix E,
the affine closest point is v0+E t with (E^T E)t=E^T(x-v0).
E has independent columns, so its Gram is positive definite. Alternatively
the barycentric affine solution solves [V^T V,1;1^T,0](p,nu)=(V^T x,1).
This augmented system is nonsingular: a homogeneous solution has1^T p=0
and p^T V^T V p=0, which forces p=0 by affine independence, then nu=0.
An affine solution with nonnegative masses and all vertex normals<=0
is the true projection by the distance expansion. An active-set search
starts feasible, descends to an affine face minimizer, stops at a first
zero weight if needed, and adds a vertex with violated normal. We do
not rely on a complexity/termination theorem: a cap invalidates the case,
and only checked feasible/KKT outputs can be used. The small full-body
calibration instead enumerates its entire finite face list independently.

For y=x-g, F=||g||^2/2+<y,g>, with <y,g>>=0 from the vertex-normal
condition at0. The equality also gives2F=||x||^2-||y||^2. For sampled
jets of this SAME function, the original interpolation convention of
accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290 reduces exactly to
Q_ij=F_i-F_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2
    =<y_j,g_i-g_j><=0.
Full normals therefore control every past/future directed pair, without
fitting values. Floating-point checks are evidence, not exact certification.

The physical step is h_i=1+a*c^(v2(i+1)), where v2 is the exponent of2.
This follows by induction from H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a*c^j,H_j).
Every child uses the same enclosing m. Set A_i=||x_i||^2 and cex_i=<y_i,g_i>.
The directly expanded GD identity is
A_(i+1)=(1-h_i)^2 A_i
        +h_i(2-h_i)||y_i||^2-2h_i(h_i-1)cex_i.
