## 1. Notation, normalization, and deterministic step words

Normalize smoothness to one and the minimizer to zero. Thus

    x_i = x_0 - sum_{t<i} h_t g_t,
    Q_ij = f_i-f_j + <g_i,x_j-x_i> + ||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    Q_i* = f_i-<g_i,x_i>+||g_i||^2/2.

An actual trajectory has every Q_ij <= 0. A full upper certificate at level q
is the identity

    q||x_0||^2 - 2f_n + sum lambda_ij Q_ij = R,

with all lambda_ij >= 0 and R PSD in the ORIGINAL vectors
(x_0,g_0,...,g_n). An upper bound derived this way uses the true initial
distance; translating/scaling back gives the project's normalization
2(f_n-f_star)/(L||x_0-x_star||^2).

Write P_n = C_n = 1+S_circle(n), D_n = B_n = 1+2S_bullet(n). These aliases
are used only in this note. Their exact recurrences are

    P_0=D_0=1,
    P_n=max_{0<=k<n} Omega(P_k,P_{n-k-1}),
    D_n=max_{0<=k<n} G(P_k,D_{n-k-1}),
    Omega(A,C)=(A+C+sqrt((A+C)^2+4AC))/2,
    G(A,D)=2A+D/2+sqrt(D^2+8AD)/2.

Let k_P(n), respectively k_D(n), be the SMALLEST index attaining the
corresponding maximum. These are exact real comparisons among a finite list
of nested radicals, with a specified tie rule, independent of the objective.
Set p^(0)=d^(0)=(). Recursively define the complete words

    p^(n) = (p^(k), phi(P_k-1,P_l-1), p^(l)),
        k=k_P(n), l=n-k-1,
    d^(n) = (p^(k), psi(P_k-1,(D_l-1)/2), d^(l)),
        k=k_D(n), l=n-k-1.

Here phi and psi are exactly those in accepted b98bb807d514e80d. Induction
gives length n, sums P_n-1 and (D_n-1)/2, respectively. All entries exceed
one: at a primitive join b-1 is the positive root of
r^2+(A+C)r-AC=0; at a dominant join b=1+A/r with r>1 below.
Thus these are actual predetermined legal words at EVERY horizon, with no
assumption on maximizing split patterns. Sections 2--4 specify every one of
their certificate coefficients, not just their scalar values.

## 2. Primitive finite-array identity

For a segment from sample a to sample z, with steps h_a,...,h_(z-1) and sum s,
define

    E_P = (||x_a||^2-||x_z||^2)/2
          + sum_{a<=i<z} h_i Q_i* - s f_z
          - s(s+1)||g_z||^2/2.

We construct a nonnegative finite-node array L_P with zero diagonal such that

    E_P = -sum_{a<=i,j<=z; i!=j} L_P(i,j) Q_ij.          (P)

The empty segment has L_P=0 and E_P=0. All arrays below are embedded at their
actual chronological indices and added if supports overlap.

For a join, let p be the last left sample, p+1 the first right sample, and z
the final sample. Let the left/right step sums be x,y, and b=phi(x,y). Put

    t=(x+b)/(y+2-b)>0.

The denominator is positive: substituting the defining radical gives
1<b<y+2. Let L_L,L_R be the child arrays. The complete recursion is

    L_P = L_L + (1+2t)L_R
          + t sum_{p<i<z} h_i (E_(i,p)+E_(i,z))
          + t(b-1)E_(p,z) + t E_(z,p),                 (P-array)

where E_(i,j) denotes one unit at the indicated ordered pair. Empty sums are
zero. Every displayed coefficient is nonnegative.

Here is the polynomial identity behind the recursion. Put C_R=y(y+1) and
F_j=sum_{p<i<z} h_i Q_(i,j), for j=p,z. Direct substitution of the definition
of Q gives, for ANY bridge b,

    (y+2-b)(f_p-f_z)
      -(y+3b-2b^2)||g_p||^2/2
      -(2y^2+3y+b)||g_z||^2/2
    = 2 E_R - F_p-F_z-(b-1)Q_(p,z)-Q_(z,p).          (P-decrease)

To check it without a black-box inequality, substitute twice the identity

    sum h_i Q_i* = F_j+y(f_j-||g_j||^2/2)
                  + <x_(p+1)-x_z,g_j-x_j>

into 2E_R, then use x_(p+1)=x_p-bg_p. Adding the two last Q terms cancels
the mixed product involving x_(p+1)-x_z and leaves exactly the displayed
two squared-gradient coefficients.

With s=x+b+y and C_L=x(x+1), C=s(s+1), expansion also gives

    E_P = E_L+E_R+(x+b)(f_p-f_z)
          +(C_L+b-b^2)||g_p||^2/2+(C_R-C)||g_z||^2/2.

The defining quadratic b^2+(x+y)b-(xy+2x+2y+2)=0 yields

    (x+b)(y+3b-2b^2)/(y+2-b)=b^2-b-C_L,
    (x+b)(2y^2+3y+b)/(y+2-b)=C-C_R.

Inserting (P-decrease) proves (P-array) and (P) by induction. This proves
the primitive certificate on every formal vector, not merely on a compressed
endpoint matrix.

## 3. Dominant finite-array identity

For a segment with sum y, let its mass be D=2y+1. We construct positive
weights v_i on ALL its samples, summing to D, and a nonnegative finite array
L_D such that

    E_D := ||x_a||^2/2 + sum v_i Q_i* - D f_z
           - ||x_a-sum v_i g_i||^2/2
         = -sum L_D(i,j) Q_ij.                        (D)

For the empty segment take v_a=1 and L_D=0. The identity then follows by
expanding Q_a*.

Join a primitive left segment of sum x to a dominant right segment of mass
D=2y+1, with its weights v and array L_R. Set A=x+1 and

    mu=2A/D,
    r=(1+sqrt(1+8A/D))/2,
    lambda=r^2=r+mu,
    gamma=A+1=x+2,
    b=1+A/r=psi(x,y).

The new weights u and finite array are

    u_i=h_i                  (i<p),
    u_p=gamma,
    u_i=r v_i                (p<i<=z),
    L_D=L_L+lambda L_R+mu sum_{p<i<=z} v_i E_(i,p).   (D-array)

They have the exact mass

    M=sum u_i=x+gamma+rD=2A+rD=Dr^2
      =G(A,D)=2(x+b+y)+1.

For completeness, the whole algebra follows from the more general identity
in section 6. Setting these parameters makes all three entries of its mixed
matrix K zero: gamma=A+1 gives K_11=0, lambda=r^2 gives K_22=0, and
lambda b=mu+gamma r gives K_12=0. The value-flow equality is mu D=x+gamma.
Thus (D) follows from (P), the right child identity, and the displayed fan.
There is no discarded nonnegative square or omitted mixed term.

## 4. The actual full certificate at exactly 1/B_n

Apply section 3 recursively to the deterministic word d^(n). Its mass is
M=B_n. On I_n={*,0,...,n}, set

    lambda_(i,*)=2u_i/B_n,
    lambda_(*,i)=0,
    lambda_(i,j)=2L_D(i,j)/B_n  (finite i!=j).

Then the exact full identity is

    ||x_0||^2/B_n - 2f_n + sum lambda_ij Q_ij
       = ||x_0-sum u_i g_i||^2/B_n.                  (Full)

Every coefficient and every formal-vector term has now been specified.
The function-value flow is out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n), by equating the independent
f_i coefficients in (D). The remainder is a full rank-one PSD quadratic.
Its kernel is x_0=sum u_i g_i. It is not a PSD assertion on a quotient.

The actual source array has no incoming terminal edge. This follows
inductively from (D-array): its left subtree is strictly before the final
sample, and every new edge involving that sample leaves it. Also
u_n=sqrt(B_n), by the dominant recursion u_n=r v_n and B_n=r^2 D.
Consequently, in the exact terminal framework of accepted1252b8823bb5a290,

    alpha_star=1/sqrt(B_n), alpha_j=L_D(n,j)/B_n (j<n),
    V_alpha=(x_0-sum_{i<n}u_i g_i)/sqrt(B_n),
    R_alpha=0 IDENTICALLY on the entire original prefix space.       (Terminal)

The probabilities sum to one by the terminal value flow. Indeed (Full) is
exactly ||g_n-V_alpha||^2, and the terminal identity of that accepted fact
then gives the displayed zero prefix remainder. This records the full
baseline kernel and its mixed-block normalization explicitly; the claim is
not just that R_alpha vanishes on one extremal trajectory.

The legal canonical Huber instance in accepted0be8b54e0a33381c, with
x_0=1, x_star=0, threshold 1/B_n, gives 2f_n=1/B_n for this physical word.
Consequently the constructed upper bound is attained: W_n(d^(n))=1/B_n.

Some explicit horizons also check the orientation and normalization. Put
rho=1+sqrt(2).

* n=1: d=(3/2), B_1=4, u=(2,2), L_(1,0)=2.
  Thus lambda_(1,0)=lambda_(0,*)=lambda_(1,*)=1 and the remainder is
  ||x_0-2g_0-2g_1||^2/4.
* n=2: put z=sqrt(9+8sqrt(2)). The maximizing split is k=1 and
  d=(sqrt(2),(3+z)/4), B_2=(5+4sqrt(2)+z)/2.
  The weights are u=(sqrt(2),2+sqrt(2),(1+z)/2).
  The ONLY finite entries are L_(0,1)=1, L_(1,0)=rho,
  L_(2,1)=2rho. The full multipliers are 2L/B_2 and 2u/B_2.
* n=3: the maximizing split is k=1,
  d=(sqrt(2),1+sqrt(2),3/2), B_3=6+4sqrt(2),
  u=(sqrt(2),2+sqrt(2),2+sqrt(2),2+sqrt(2)).
  The ONLY finite entries are
  L_(0,1)=1, L_(1,0)=rho, L_(2,1)=rho,
  L_(3,1)=rho, L_(3,2)=rho^2.

Here the small split comparisons can themselves be checked exactly. At n=2,
G(1,4)=4+2sqrt(3)<15/2, whereas G(rho,1)>15/2: use
sqrt(2)>45/32 and sqrt(2)>7/5 to obtain

## 5. A second exact tight instance and full-array implications

For a primitive source word of mass A,

    product_i |1-h_i|=1/A.                            (Q-P)

Indeed it is 1 for the empty word. At a primitive join, put C=Omega(A,E)
and d=b-1. Then C=A+E+d and dC=AE, so the joined product is
d/(AE)=1/C. Similarly the dominant source word satisfies

    product_i |1-h_i|=1/sqrt(B_n).                    (Q-D)

The dominant induction is d/(A sqrt(D)) with d=A/r and B_n=Dr^2.
The actual scalar function f(x)=x^2/2, x_0=1, therefore gives exactly
2f_n=product_i(1-h_i)^2=1/B_n. It is a second normalized tight instance.

Any full certificate, however its multipliers and rank are reselected, obeys
for a changed nonnegative word h'=h+e

    q B_n >= max{B_n/(B_n+2sum e_i),
                 product_i(1+e_i/(h_i-1))^2}.        (Two tests)

The second expression is valid without assuming h'_i>1 because it is
squared; when logs are used below require h'_i>1. The first is the canonical
Huber bound with the TRUE new step sum, the second the actual unit quadratic.
In particular qB_n<=1-delta, delta in (0,1), requires

    sum e_i >= delta B_n/[2(1-delta)],
    sum log(1+e_i/(h_i-1)) <= log(1-delta)/2           (if h'_i>1).

For a differentiable one-sided continuation h(t),q(t) at t=0 this gives

    q'(0) >= -2 B_n^(-2) sum h_i'(0),
    q'(0) >= (2/B_n) sum h_i'(0)/(h_i-1).            (Tangent tests)

These are NECESSARY only. At the exact baseline, complementarity on the
Huber instance forces every optimum-source multiplier lambda_(*,i) to be
zero, for n>=1: Q_(*,i)=tau(tau-x_i)<0, since s(h)>0 and
x_i>tau. This conclusion applies to every tight baseline certificate,
not merely the particular source array. It does NOT force those entries
to remain zero after a perturbation with non-Huber worst-case behavior.

## 6. Exact obstruction for a relaxed one-fan dominant join

This class changes the source coupling and bridge, including signed
perturbations of every positive parameter within their final sign constraints.
It is strictly more general than fixing b=psi or fixing the rank-one remainder.
It still retains a specified interface and a single cross fan; it is not the
full cone of arbitrary cross-copy interpolation edges.

Take ANY primitive left certificate of the form

    E_L+sum L_L Q = R_L,

and ANY sharp dominant right certificate of the form

    E_R+sum L_R Q = R_R,

where E_L and E_R have the definitions above, D=2y+1, sum v_i=D,
the child finite arrays are nonnegative, and R_L,R_R are full PSD quadratics.
The steps and arrays inside either child need not be their source selections.
Allow an arbitrary bridge b>=0 and parameters

    gamma>=0, lambda>=mu>=0, r=lambda-mu,
    mu D=x+gamma.                                   (Flow)

The new optimum-sink weights and finite array are

    u=(h_left,gamma,r v),
    L=L_L+lambda L_R+mu sum v_i E_(i,p),
    M=x+gamma+rD>0.

Positive child weights and multipliers may be independently reselected so
long as their displayed child identities remain true. No sign restriction
is placed on their DERIVATIVES; only the final nonnegative arrays are required.
Let V=sum_{right} v_i g_i and A=x+1. Direct expansion on the original space
gives the exact identity

    ||x_0||^2/2 + sum u_i Q_i* - M f_z + sum L_ij Q_ij
    = ||x_p-gamma g_p-rV||^2/2
      + R_L+lambda R_R
      + (K_11||g_p||^2+2K_12<g_p,V>+K_22||V||^2)/2, (Join)

where the ENTIRE mixed block is

    K_11=A^2-(gamma-1)^2,
    K_22=lambda-r^2,
    K_12=lambda b-mu-gamma r.                        (K)

To verify the identity, use

    sum v_i Q_i* = sum v_i Q_(i,p)
                  +D(f_p-||g_p||^2/2)+<V,g_p-x_p>,

multiply the right certificate by lambda, subtract mu times this identity,
add the left certificate and gamma(Q_p*-f_z), and substitute
x_(p+1)=x_p-bg_p. The coefficient of f_p-f_z is
x+gamma-mu D and hence is zero. Completing the indicated square leaves
x(x+1)+mu D+gamma-gamma^2=A^2-(gamma-1)^2,
lambda-r^2, and lambda b-mu-gamma r as claimed.

The condition K PSD is necessary for the FULL parent quadratic to be PSD,
even if both child remainders have arbitrarily large ranks. Here is why this
is stronger than an unsupported endpoint compression. Put all left formal
gradients, INCLUDING g_p, equal to an arbitrary vector a, and all right
gradients equal to an independent arbitrary vector c. Choose affine function
values separately on each child: f_i=f_child_end+(remaining child step sum)
times its constant gradient norm squared. All within-child Q vanish. Direct
substitution gives E_L=0 and E_R=D(2y+1-D)||c||^2/2=0. Thus
R_L=R_R=0 on this restriction for EVERY such child certificate. Moreover
V=Dc, and choosing x_0=(x+gamma)a+rDc makes the first square zero.
All choices of a,c are still available, so the restriction is precisely K.
This is a legitimate test of a full formal PSD matrix; it does not claim these
independent constants are one legal primal trajectory.

It follows that

    gamma<=A+1,
    r^2-r-(x+gamma)/D<=0.

Let w=x+gamma. Then w<=2A and

    M=w+rD <= w+(D+sqrt(D^2+4Dw))/2 <= G(A,D).        (Mass cap)

Both scalar expressions are strictly increasing in w>=0. Equality forces

    gamma=A+1, mu=2A/D,
    r=(1+sqrt(1+8A/D))/2, lambda=r^2.

At equality both diagonals of K are zero. PSD then forces the mixed entry
to vanish too, giving b=(mu+gamma r)/lambda=1+A/r=psi(x,y).
Thus the exact source mass is the UNIQUE maximizing local parameter choice
within this relaxed join, including its bridge. A positive extra remainder
does not buy a larger mass because the independently constant child modes
annihilate every sharp child remainder.

This is a finite inequality, not an infinitesimal one. Apply it at any number
of dominant nodes with primitive prefix sums at most S_circle of their
lengths and sharp dominant children. At a node of total length n, the exact
DP monotonicity gives M<=G(P_k,B_l)<=B_n inductively. The root need only have
the displayed valid certificate of level 1/M; it need not itself be sharp.
Consequently this entire recursive one-fan family cannot certify
q B_n<1 at ANY horizon. It allows changes at unboundedly many scales and a
changed bridge rule, so its obstruction is not just the fixed finite
terminal-library result. Its retained hypotheses are the sharp child
interfaces, the physical primitive prefix weights, and the single cross fan.
Non-Huber children, independent history weights, extra interior cross edges,
and couplings outside this construction remain open.

## 7. A separate global schedule class, with unrestricted full re-selection

Let n>=1, h=d^(n), and a_i=h_i-1>0. Let T be ANY n-by-n doubly stochastic
matrix (nonnegative entries; every row and column sums to one), and c>=0.
Define a new actual word in its new chronological order by

    h'_i=1+c(Ta)_i.                                  (Mix)

Then

    W_n(h')>=1/B_n.                                  (Mix obstruction)

No hypothesis whatsoever is made here on its certificate array, its rank,
or its worst-case function. This class includes every permutation, arbitrary
mixing of different scales, and any common dilation or contraction of the
positive step gaps; most such words are not ConPD words.

Proof. Since column sums of T equal one,
s(h')=n+c sum a_i. If 0<=c<=1, the actual Huber lower bound is at least
1/(1+2s(h))=1/B_n. If c>=1, log concavity gives

    sum_i log((Ta)_i)
      >=sum_(i,j) T_ij log(a_j)=sum_j log(a_j).

The unit quadratic therefore gives

    W_n(h') >= c^(2n) product_i((Ta)_i)^2
             >= c^(2n) product_i a_i^2=c^(2n)/B_n.

This proves the finite, uniform claim. It does not exclude making the
normalized gap vector more dispersed, or incomparable in the majorization
order, nor any schedule with some entries below one.

## 8. What is still blocked

The original target asks for either a uniform matching all-schedule lower
bound, or one fixed delta>0 and a certified finite continuation with
q_n B_n<=1-delta on an explicit unbounded sequence. Neither follows here.

The all-horizon source word and full array are now explicit. The one-fan
closure and global gap mixing classes have exact finite obstructions.
The remaining useful variation must leave those classes. Passing (Two tests)
or (Tangent tests) alone is not a PSD continuation. In particular positive
optimum-source multipliers and a higher-rank remainder after deformation
are allowed by the original problem and must not be excluded by inference
from sharp baseline complementarity.
