# Bounded nonbasic primitive-seed discovery: completed at the authorized cap

Status: numerical discovery only, no improved seed found, no nonexistence theorem. The exact reversed-seed counterexample in Section3 is self-contained but unverified.

1. Model and calibration.
The code max4_primitive_pep.py minimizes the saturated intrinsic primitive energy
V_h=2 sum_i h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_i h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g_n||^2
with s=sum h. It keeps the full gradient Gram matrix G positive semidefinite, trace(G)=1, f_0=0, and every ordered smooth-convex sample inequality Q_ij<=0. Thus it tests the full intrinsic primitive condition, not a quadratic-only or Huber-only relaxation. It records all primal data, all nonnegative interpolation multipliers, the dual PSD slack, and residuals.

The runtime is isolated in workers/max4/.venv-pep. Installed versions: cvxpy1.9.3, clarabel0.11.1, numpy2.5.3, scipy1.18.1. Each solve used one thread, max_iter150, time_limit10 seconds, and requested gap/feasibility tolerances1e-9. No core or verifier code was changed.

Six initial evaluations included known primitive blocks [sqrt(2)], [3/2,3/2], exact DP length3 and length7, and the two orderings of (17/10,2,6/5). The known primitive objectives were between -2.3e-9 and -1.7e-10, consistent with their exact zero optimum. The forward seed returned a negative value and the reversed seed returned a negative value. These calibration signs match the symbolic witnesses, rather than being counted as new mathematical conclusions.

2. Scope and complete budget.
The experiment then ran two local continuous SLSQP probes, of lengths3 and7, with all steps independently variable and constrained only to be nonnegative and to have shifted sum 1.002 times the corresponding exact-DP shifted mass (evaluated in floating point for discovery). Neither symmetry nor a recursive grammar was imposed. The starting points were small nonsymmetric perturbations of the DP blocks. The outer objective was minus the minimum primitive energy; its sensitivity used the computed primal and dual solutions. This is a local, nonsmooth numerical search, not a global optimization proof.

Total evaluations:60, including six calibration/candidate evaluations,24 local evaluations at length3 and30 at length7. The persistent counter in max4_pep_budget.json blocks further calls. Accumulated measured solve/build time was1.890137771 seconds. There were50 solver statuses `optimal` and10 `optimal_inaccurate`; no inaccurate output was promoted to a certificate.

Best sampled minimum energy at length3 was about -0.03623216044 (evaluation28), with h approximately
(1.4184351724,2.0071236092,1.4145251974).
Best sampled minimum energy at length7 was about -0.06114950110 (evaluation59), with h approximately
(1.4221824349,2.0110133066,1.4173278397,3.4184527329,1.4143778864,2.0014068997,1.4144488473).
Both were negative by amounts far larger than the recorded numerical residuals, and no credible improved primitive certificate appeared. The search stopped at the exact authorized evaluation cap. It did not test all nonbasic seeds, other initializations, lengths4 or5, or other target gains.

Across all60 evaluations, the largest positive interpolation residual was6.85e-8 and the most negative computed dual slack eigenvalue was-6.54e-7 (including inaccurate outputs). At the two best local samples, interpolation residuals were below4.3e-11; their slack eigenvalue minima were about-7.46e-11 and-1.36e-8. All full data and solver statuses are in max4_pep_evaluations.jsonl; the two local summaries are in max4_pep_local_summary.json. No exact feasibility or infeasibility assertion is inferred from these floating-point residuals.

3. Exact reconstruction for the reversed rational seed.
The rank-one numerical witness for h=(6/5,2,17/10) suggested gradients proportional to (1,1,-1,7/10). This was reconstructed without solver tolerances as follows.
Define f(y)=y^2/2 for y<=1 and f(y)=y-1/2 for y>=1. Its derivative min(y,1) is continuous, monotone and1-Lipschitz, so f is convex and1-smooth, with minimizer0. Starting at y_0=11/5, the three steps give exactly
y_1=1, y_2=-1, y_3=7/10,
with gradients1,1,-1,7/10. At all four points Q_(i,star)=0. With s=49/10 and R=59/10, the intrinsic primitive energy is therefore
V_h=(11/5)^2-(59/10)^2(7/10)^2=-122169/10000<0.
Its trace-normalized value is -122169/34900, explaining the numerical value near-3.5005444. The exact function, not a numerical rank determination, proves the seed's failure.
This also yields a useful specific test for schedules (H,2,C), with H>=0 and1<=C<=2: the same function starting at H+1 gives endpoints1,-1,C-1, so any primitive certificate must satisfy
(H+C+3)(C-1)<=H+1.
This is an additional witness condition, not a complete characterization of such schedules.

4. What remains.
The experiment found no nonbasic saturated seed with a proved positive gain and hence supplied no asymptotic negative construction. It does not establish that all primitive or all GD schedules are basic. The next analytic upper-bound attempt must change the invariant, rather than enlarge this scan or claim that numerical failure settles the seed question. In particular, a start/end gradient potential can retain cross terms omitted by a scalar primitive capacity; its one-step and two-step identities should be derived before any recursive closure is asserted.
