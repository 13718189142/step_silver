## 4. Mechanism1b: a legal fan of actual rows, with arbitrary probabilities

for i in I, with sum omega_i=1. Retain alpha,beta,gamma,ell,eta,R_flow

    mu_(0,star)=alpha, mu_(R,star)=gamma,
    mu_(i,star)=beta*omega_i,
    mu_(i,0)=(ell+eta)*omega_i,
    mu_(0,i)=eta*omega_i,
    mu_(R,i)=(beta+ell)*omega_i          (i in I),
    mu_(R,0)=R_flow,
    mu_(n,R)=1.                                  (4.1)

All are explicit nonnegative original-row multipliers. There are no
middle-to-middle rows in this particular fan. The balance at EVERY
middle index is

    beta*omega_i+(ell+eta)*omega_i
                         -eta*omega_i-(beta+ell)*omega_i=0.

Thus all original sampled values cancel separately. No averaged value
or fictitious jet is introduced. Define algebraic moments

    P_i=sum_(l=1)^(i-1)h_l*g_l,  P_1=0,
    dvec=sum_(i in I)h_i*g_i,
    q_omega=sum_(i in I)omega_i*g_i,
    N_omega=sum_(i in I)omega_i*||g_i||^2,
    P_omega=sum_(i in I)omega_i*P_i,
    T_omega=sum_(i in I)omega_i*<g_i,P_i>,
    gbar_omega=alpha*p+beta*q_omega+gamma*r.         (4.2)

The term T_omega is chronologically ordered. It is not the square of a
mean. Expanding every row in (4.1), using x_i=x_0-Ap-P_i and
x_R=x_0-Ap-dvec, gives exactly

    chi||x_0||^2-2f_n+2sum mu Q
      =chi||x_0||^2-2<gbar_omega,x_0>+2K_omega
                                        +||g_n+(A-1)r||^2,       (4.3)

where

    K_omega=(alpha-eta*d)||p||^2+kappa*N_omega+w||r||^2
       +[beta*A+ell*d+eta*(d-1)]<p,q_omega>
       +[gamma*A+R_flow*d]<p,r>
       +<r,dvec>-eta*<p,P_omega>
       -(beta+ell)*<r,P_omega+q_omega>+kappa*T_omega.             (4.4)

One can check every coefficient directly as follows. The combined
coefficient of ||g_i||^2 is
[beta+(ell+eta)+eta+(beta+ell)]omega_i/2=kappa*omega_i.
The coefficient of <g_i,P_i> is
[beta+(ell+eta)]omega_i=kappa*omega_i.
The reverse rows Q_(0,i) contribute -eta<p,P_omega>; the Q_(R,i)

x_R contribute exactly <r,dvec>, because
gamma+beta+ell+R_flow=1. These exhaust internal occurrences.

When omega is the point mass at K, q_omega=g_K and (4.4) reduces to
Section3 including its displacement remainder. This independently

## 6. Exact full-space obstruction to displacement-weighted row averaging

Set x_0=p=r=g_n=0. In (4.3), before any mean substitution, the complete
internal principal form is

    2*kappa*[sum_i omega_i||g_i||^2
                         +sum_(l<i)omega_i*h_l<g_i,g_l>].       (6.1)

For omega_i=h_i/B, this is

    (kappa/B)*[||sum_i h_i*g_i||^2
                              +sum_i h_i(2-h_i)||g_i||^2].      (6.2)

Both formulas follow directly from (4.4) and do not assume any common
gradient contact equations. In particular, the following obstruction
also applies to any outer coefficients of the same fan architecture
with kappa>0, whenever the displayed node balances hold.

There are two actual interior steps with h_i,h_j>2: choose the whole
root i=2^(m-1)-1 and the right-child root j=3*2^(m-2)-1. Both are in I,
and h_i=1+a*c^(m-1), h_j=1+a*c^(m-2). Bounds (1.2), c>2 and m>=1536
give the strict inequalities h_i,h_j>2 without any numeric evaluation.

For a nonzero vector z set g_i=z/h_i, g_j=-z/h_j and all other middle
gradients zero. Then sum h_l*g_l=0, and (6.2) equals

    (2*kappa/B)*(1/h_i+1/h_j-1)*||z||^2 <0.        (6.3)

This is an exact full-space negative direction at every diagnostic m.
No x_0 allowance, including 17/(16Z), changes it. A merely positive
variance based on sum h_i||nu_i||^2 is not the actual row remainder.
The chronological part contributes the negative h_i^2 weights.

For arbitrary positive omega, a two-coordinate principal minor of
(6.1), at l<i, also yields the necessary condition

    omega_l >= (h_l^2/4)*omega_i.                 (6.4)

Indeed its diagonal entries, after removing the common positive factor,
are omega_l,omega_i and its off-diagonal is h_l*omega_i/2. At the
displacement probabilities this condition becomes h_l*h_i<=4, which
fails for the two selected long steps. Non-displacement probabilities
are NOT ruled out by this argument, and (6.4) is not sufficient for
their full-space PSD. No adaptive probability family is asserted.

## 7. Mechanism2: a concrete four-sector discriminator on the same word

    V_L=sum_(i in I_L)h_i(2-h_i),
    V_R=sum_(i in I_R)h_i(2-h_i).                 (7.1)

Its three genuine change locations are0,J,K. This discriminator is

their total positive linear contribution is at most2S<2c^m. I_L

    p=r=x_0=g_n=0,
    s=z/B_L, t=-z/B_R.                          (7.3)

    (kappa/B)*[V_L/B_L^2+V_R/B_R^2]*||z||^2.      (7.4)
