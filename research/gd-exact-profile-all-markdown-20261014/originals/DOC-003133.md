# C35. Every ordered fresh-child tree relay is too weak on the near-critical ruler

Status: UNVERIFIED joint candidate, no verifier or solver. All ingredients used
below are proved internally. The result covers the exact incidence-flow class
defined here, not arbitrary tree-indexed vectors or arbitrary simplex walks.

## 1. A genuine real tree family with all normal inequalities

Partition a schedule into N ordinary blocks of masses S_i and checkpoints
b_i>1 after each block except the last. Set M_i=S_i+1, L_i=b_i-1 and
T_i=M_i+L_i for i<N-1. Choose T_(N-1)>=M_(N-1).

Choose ANY rooted tree on vertices 0,...,N-1 with root0 and with each parent
strictly earlier than its children. Write Ch_i for the children of i, and
W_i={j>i:parent(j)<i} for the waiting vertices at phase i. Define positive
energies E_i and actual vectors in the standard basis of R^N:

    alpha_i=sqrt(E_i)/T_i,
    beta_ij=sqrt(E_j)/T_i  (j in Ch_i),
    p_i=alpha_i e_i-sum_(j in Ch_i) beta_ij e_j.                (1)

The vector matrix is triangular with positive diagonal, hence these are
linearly independent vectors with an explicit positive definite real Gram
matrix. Distinct normals have negative inner product exactly along a tree
edge, and zero inner product otherwise. Let C=conv(0,p_i) and

    F(x)=max_(p in C)(<x,p>-||p||^2/2).

Its maximizer is the Euclidean projection P_C(x). The projection variational
inequalities give firm nonexpansiveness, hence Lipschitz continuity, of P_C.
Bounding maximization differences with the two maximizing linear forms gives
grad F=P_C. Thus F is one fixed everywhere-defined convex 1-smooth function.

Start at x_init=sqrt(E_0)e_0. At the beginning of phase i the nonzero
coordinates are sqrt(E_j) for the current vertex i and for j in W_i.
During ordinary partial mass u the current coordinate is
(T_i-u)alpha_i, each waiting coordinate stays sqrt(E_j), and each child
coordinate is u beta_ij. After the checkpoint, the current coordinate
vanishes and every child coordinate becomes sqrt(E_j). This is the
prescribed GD recursion whenever its projection normals hold.

For y=x-p_i, all its coordinates are nonnegative. Its own normal value is

    delta_i(u)=(T_i-u-1)alpha_i^2
                         -(u+1)sum_(j in Ch_i)beta_ij^2.      (2)

A past vertex has nonpositive dot product with y: its positive coordinate
has been exhausted, and all its remaining coordinates are nonpositive.
A future vertex j has no mass yet in its children, since the tree has a
unique parent. Thus its dot product with y is exactly

    E_j/T_j                      if j in W_i,
    (u+1)sqrt(E_j)alpha_j/T_i     if j in Ch_i,
    0                            otherwise.

The comparison with zero is also required. The own value (2) decreases in u;
the child comparisons increase; the waiting comparisons are constant.
Therefore ALL comparisons against ALL past/future vertices and zero are
equivalent to the following endpoint conditions for i<N-1:

    L_i E_i-M_i sum_(j in Ch_i)E_j >=
       T_i^2 max(0,
           max_(j in W_i) E_j/T_j,
           (M_i/T_i) max_(j in Ch_i) E_j/T_j).                 (3)

Empty maxima are zero. The terminal phase has no children or waiting
vertices, and its condition is just T_(N-1)>=M_(N-1).
These checks include every actual ordinary query and checkpoint query.
They construct a fixed legal smooth-convex function, without oracle resets.

The minimizer set is C^polar: a violated dot-product inequality yields a
positive maximand at a small multiple of the corresponding vertex.
Its inequalities force every coordinate of a minimizer nonpositive, by
backward induction through the tree. Thus 0 is a closest minimizer to
sqrt(E_0)e_0, with true squared initial distance E_0.

Writing T=T_(N-1) and D=2S_(N-1)+1, the exact normalized objective is

    score=(2T-D) E_(N-1)/(T^2 E_0).                            (4)

For fixed tree, phase masses, T and terminal energy E_(N-1)=1, setting (3)
to equality in decreasing order of i constructs the componentwise least
positive feasible E_i. Indeed every right hand side is a nondecreasing
function of already-set future energies. This proves, without numerical
optimization, that the recursion attains the best score in this exact class.
There is always a positive future waiting or child energy until the last
phase, so the constructed E_i are positive.

The primitive defect is also exact on the SAME normalized trajectory.
At each projection query F_t=<x_t,g_t>-||g_t||^2/2. Consequently

    2h_t F_t+h_t(1-h_t)||g_t||^2
                         =||x_t||^2-||x_(t+1)||^2.

If s is the entire schedule mass and
V=2sum_t h_t(F_t-F_end)+sum_t h_t(1-h_t)||g_t||^2
  -s(s+1)||g_end||^2, then

    V/E_0=1-[(T-S_(N-1)+s)/T]^2 E_(N-1)/E_0.                 (5)

No raw negative sign is substituted for objective or distance control.

## 2. A chronology bound valid for EVERY tree shape

Put H_i=E_i/T_i. The next chronological vertex i+1 is either a child of i
or already waiting. If it is waiting, (3), even after dropping all child
costs, implies

    H_(i+1)/H_i <= L_i/T_i.

If it is a child, retaining its own term in the child sum and its own
normal comparison gives

    H_(i+1)/H_i <= L_i T_i/[M_i(T_i+T_(i+1))].

Hence every legal tree relay satisfies

    H_(i+1)/H_i <=
        max(L_i/T_i, L_i T_i/[M_i(T_i+T_(i+1))]).             (6)

This is only a necessary bound; the constructive recursion (3) still keeps
every other comparison. The upper bound (6) is used only to exclude payoff.

## 3. Uniform quantitative exclusion on H_m

Fix m>=128, lambda=1+sqrt(2), c=lambda(1+1/m),
a=(1-1/m)/c, H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a c^j,H_j).
Let S_j be the mass of H_j and M_j=1+S_j. Geometric summation gives

    M_j=A c^j+(1-A)2^j,  A=a/(c-2) in (0,1).

Choose ANY dyadic blocking depth r<m. Put k=m-r, N=2^k, M=M_r and
q=a c^r/M. The N ordinary blocks are all copies of H_r and

    L_i=M q c^(nu_2(i+1)),
    T_i=M(1+q c^(nu_2(i+1)))  (i<N-1).                       (7)

The following simple uniform bounds will suffice:

    12/5<c<39/16,      2/5<q<=c-2<7/16.                    (8)

For the upper bound, sqrt(2)<99/70 and
(169/70)(129/128)<39/16. Also a<c-2 because ac<1<c(c-2).
This gives 0<A<1 and q<=c-2. Since M_r<=c^r, q>=a.
Finally a=(m-1)/[lambda(m+1)] >=127/(129lambda)
>2540/6321>2/5, using lambda<49/20. This proves (8).

Every even index i<N-1 has the low checkpoint q_i=q. In (6), its
waiting option is at least its child option. For a nonterminal next phase
this is q+q^2<=1+q_(i+1), which follows from q<1 and q_(i+1)>=q.
For the terminal next phase it follows from q+q^2<1<=T/M.
Thus every such low transition obeys

    H_(i+1)/H_i <= q/(1+q).                                  (9)

Every odd index has q_i=q c^ell for some ell>=1, and is followed by
a low checkpoint. Its child option dominates the waiting option:
q_i^2+q_i>1+q, because q_i>24/25 while q<7/16.
Therefore

    H_(i+1)/H_i <= q c^ell / D_ell,
    D_ell=(2+q+q c^ell)/(1+q c^ell)>1.                       (10)

There are N/2 low transitions and 2^(k-ell-1) transitions of level ell>=1.
Counting binary valuations gives

    product_(i<N-1)q c^(nu_2(i+1))=(qc)^(N-1)c^(-k).

Since (2T-D)/T<2, (4), (9), and (10) yield

    score < [2/(M(1+q)c^k)]
             (qc)^(N-1) /
             [(1+q)^(N/2) product_(ell=1)^(k-1)
                           D_ell^(2^(k-ell-1))].             (11)

Let K=1/(2M_m-1), the global Huber score. The exact mass relation is

    M_m=M[(1-q/(c-2))2^k+(q/(c-2))c^k] <=M c^k.

It follows that score/K<B_k, where

    B_k=4(qc)^(2^k-1) /
       [(1+q)^(2^(k-1)+1)
          product_(ell=1)^(k-1)D_ell^(2^(k-ell-1))].          (12)

For k=2,

    B_2=4(qc)^3(1+qc)/[(1+q)^3(2+q+qc)].

This increases in both q and c. Its logarithmic q derivative is
3/[q(1+q)]+(c-1)/[(1+cq)(2+(1+c)q)]>0. Its logarithmic c
derivative is 3/c+q/(1+cq)-q/(2+(1+c)q)>0. Consequently (8) gives

    B_2 <
      273^3*529/[1024*23^3*897]
      =10763254593/11175730176 =: eta <1.                    (13)

The recurrence is exact:

    B_(k+1)/B_k^2 = (qc)(1+q)/(4D_k)
                  <6279/16384 =: zeta <1.                  (14)

Thus, for every k>=2,

    score/K < B_k
             <=zeta^(2^(k-2)-1) eta^(2^(k-2)) <1.            (15)

This excludes EVERY ordered rooted tree and EVERY legal positive energy
choice in the specified family. If k grows, the ratio tends to zero
exponentially in 2^k. In particular no choice of tree shape avoids the loss.

When k=1 the sole tree is the two-vertex path. The exact endpoint condition
gives, with T_0=M(1+q) and D=2M-1,

    score=q(2T-D)/[T(T+T_0)].

The maximum is attained at T=(D+sqrt(D^2+2D T_0))/2. Writing epsilon=1/M
its ratio to K is

    2q[2(2+q)-epsilon] /
       [3+q-epsilon+sqrt((2-epsilon)(4+2q-epsilon))]
      <=4q(2+q)/[2+q+sqrt(3+2q)].

The last expression increases in q>=0 because
sqrt(3+2q)/(2+q) decreases. As q<9/20 it is less than
441/[245+10sqrt(390)]<1; the latter inequality is 196^2<39000.

Allowing r=m leaves just one vertex and one ordinary block. The choice
T=2S_m+1 attains K. Therefore the union over ALL blocking depths, ALL
ordered rooted trees and ALL legal parameters has exact best score K.

## 4. The obstruction and the next genuinely different mechanism

Tree branching reduces the number of edges from root to terminal, but it
creates waiting coordinate masses. Their supporting planes must be below
the active plane at every intervening query. Inequality (9) is the
quantitative price at each low checkpoint, independent of the chosen tree.

The unique-parent condition is load-bearing. With two parents contributing
to one child coordinate, two earlier gradient vectors can have positive
correlation. A future plane can then have a negative term from a child
already preloaded by another parent. The waiting comparison E_j/T_j used
above is no longer correct, so (6) and (15) do not cover directed acyclic
split-and-merge constructions. Nor do they cover signed coordinate mass,
revisiting exhausted coordinates, other simplex geometries, or general
smooth-convex functions. The original all-schedule minimax target is open.
