## 0. Mathematical verdict

**Not solved.** The exact all-horizon/all-schedule profile comparison remains
open. A finite diagnostic is now accepted, while the newer universal
projection/simplex descriptions are reductions without a sharp minimax bound.

Fixed goal: W_n^*/U_n^ZJ ->1, or a rigorous asymptotic disproof, where
W_n(h)=sup 2(f_n-f_star)/(L||x0-x_star||^2), h is predetermined and
nonnegative, and U_n^ZJ=1/B_n. Every dimension is finite but may grow.
An affirmative proof needs one N_epsilon independent of h for ALL n>=N_epsilon;
a negative proof needs a fixed relative gap along unbounded horizons. W=2R.
P0=B0=1, F(x,y)=(x+y+sqrt(x^2+6xy+y^2))/2,
G(x,d)=2x+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8xd)/2,
P_n=max_(k+l=n-1)F(P_k,P_l), B_n=max_(k+l=n-1)G(P_k,B_l).

Seven scoped facts are accepted, including the genuine finite H6 adversary
e9a55f57461f253c. It does not decide eventual critical-word behavior or the
original minimax ratio. The projection-simplex and two-state constructions
are concrete potential languages for a proof, but their necessary quantitative
estimates remain missing. Current failures are method failure in named
families, not proposition failure. PARTIAL/SUBSTANTIAL do not mean almost solved.

## 2. Interface contract table

every chronological/optimum normal inequality, and true polar-cone distance.
**Output claimed.** C62 draft016467bc5c3eb666 gives an anchored conversion

chronological normals nor objective normalization by themselves.
**Failure mode if ignored.** PSD or a basis change is called a legal hard
function; distance to the chosen origin is substituted for closest distance.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

**Input required.** All ordinary Q inequalities, shared arbitrary heights and

**Failure mode if ignored.** Conditional covariance is discarded or a

**Status.** SUBSTANTIAL.

### Coupled parabolic block map

**Failure mode if ignored.** A convenient weak non-turning regime is

**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Exact benchmark variation
