## 1. Statement and notation

Write a positive-excess four-step word as

    h=(1+a,1+b,1+c,1+d),    a,b,c,d>0,
    C=1+sum h=5+a+b+c+d.

Suppose it admits the full primitive certificate of accepted fact
04219554e41d9750. Suppose additionally that a>sqrt(2)-1. Define

    u=1+a,             delta=u^2-2,
    H=3+c+d,           eta=b/H,
    R=a+3+1/a,         k=u*delta/a,
    nu=(1+eta)*(1-a*eta)/a,
    Erow=R-k*eta-C^2*eta^2,
    Qenergy=1-C^2*a^2*b^2*c^2*d^2,
    Wrow=max(1+b,1+b*c*d),
    Krow=max(u,a*Wrow).

Full feasibility forces a<1, a*eta<1, nu>0, and
0<=Qenergy<1. The following quantitative necessary condition holds:

    u*delta <= Krow*Erow/nu + 2*sqrt(Qenergy*Erow/nu).          (A)

In particular,

    Erow >= nu * [u*delta /
          (sqrt(Qenergy+Krow*u*delta)+sqrt(Qenergy))]^2 > 0.  (B)

Thus equality in the previously used lifted middle-cut condition Erow>=0
is impossible throughout this initial-step regime. A version avoiding
square roots is

    Erow>=0,
    [max(u*delta*nu-Krow*Erow,0)]^2 <= 4*Qenergy*Erow*nu.     (C)

If a*b<=1 and c*d<=1, the simpler strict necessary inequality is

    Erow > nu*delta^2/u^2,                                 (D)

equivalently

    b < (a+3)*(C-a-2) / ((a+1)*(C+a+2)).                (E)

The last two product assumptions are explicit hypotheses of this
simplification. They are not silently declared lossless for the
original problem. Conditions(A)--(C) do not require them. The separate
assumption a,b,c,d>0 is also retained explicitly; no assertion about a
zero step or a step at most one is being imported from an unverified
compactness draft.

## 5. The original first row couples the two energies

Define the weighted incoming combination

    Bweight=(1+b)*Lambda20+(1-b*c)*Lambda30
                                    +(1+b*c*d)*Lambda40.

The exact first-row identity is

    2*e0^T M p = u*(m-delta)+a^2*Bweight.             (13)

Here e0 denotes the original first coordinate column of R5. A direct
derivation avoids any coordinate reduction. Since Tp=e and Le=0,
Mp=Dp+T^T L^T p/2. Also Te0=u*e-a*e0, giving

    2*e0^T M p=2(1-a^2)-a*p^T L e0.

The flow at node0 gives Lambda10=Aout+2u-Bin. Hence

    p^T L e0
      =Aout+a*Lambda10-a*b*Lambda20
                        +a*b*c*Lambda30-a*b*c*d*Lambda40
      =u*Aout+2a*u-a*Bweight.

Finally substitute a*Aout=1-a^2-m from(4). The constant term becomes

    (2-u)(1-a^2)-2a^2*u
      =u*((1-a)^2-2a^2)=-u*delta,

which proves(13), with all row and flow contributions retained.

All three incoming multipliers are nonnegative. Since b,c,d>0,
each coefficient in Bweight is at most Wrow. This remains true even
if 1-b*c is negative. Thus Bweight<=Wrow*Bin. Applying(5) with e0,p,
and using(8), gives from(13)

    u*delta <= u*m+a^2*Wrow*Bin+2*sqrt(m*Qenergy)
             <= Krow*(m+a*Bin)+2*sqrt(m*Qenergy)
             <= Krow*Erow/nu+2*sqrt(Qenergy*Erow/nu).        (14)

This is(A). Put t=sqrt(Erow/nu). Because Krow,u,delta are positive,
(14) says

    Krow*t^2+2*sqrt(Qenergy)*t-u*delta>=0.

Solving this increasing quadratic for t>=0 and rationalizing its
positive root gives(B). Its numerator is strictly positive, so Erow>0.
Multiplying(A) by nu and squaring only when its left side after
subtraction is positive gives exactly(C). There is no sign-losing
squaring in this formulation.

## 6. A rational strengthened middle-cut boundary

If c*d<=1, then Wrow=1+b. If also a*b<=1, then
a*Wrow=a+a*b<=1+a=u, so Krow=u. Since Qenergy<1 and Erow>0,
(14) implies the strict inequality

    u*delta < u*t^2+2t,       t=sqrt(Erow/nu)>0.

The right side is strictly increasing for t>=0. Its value at
t=delta/u is

    delta^2/u+2delta/u=delta*(delta+2)/u=u*delta,

because delta+2=u^2. It follows that t>delta/u, which is(D).
The exact algebraic identity

coefficients in eta. Those coefficients in the numerator are
(a+3)^2, -2(a+3)*delta, and delta^2-u^2*C^2.

The first factor a+3-delta*eta is positive: by a*eta<1,

    a+3-delta*eta > a+3-delta/a = 1+1/a >0.

Thus(D) and(15) give

    a+3 > (u*C+delta)*eta.

Substitute eta=b/(C-2-a-b), whose denominator is H>0. Since
delta+a+3=(a+1)(a+2), the result is exactly(E). Every denominator
