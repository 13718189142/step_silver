# An exact SDP for optimizing only the final predetermined step

Status: complete candidate proof, NOT verifier-accepted. No new submission is
allocated. The only accepted mathematical predecessor is 1252b8823bb5a290.
In particular the earlier hull/minimax candidate is not a proof premise here.
This proof obtains the exchange through explicit finite-dimensional separation,
with reconstruction and all zero-step cases, rather than postulating minimax.

## 1. The complete prefix and the new terminal constraints

Fix k>=0 and a finite predetermined nonnegative prefix
h_pre=(h_0,...,h_(k-1)). The prefix is empty when k=0. Let

    rho_last(h_pre)=inf_(b>=0) W_(k+1)(h_pre,b),

with W in its original arbitrary-finite-dimensional and L>0 normalization
from fact1252b8823bb5a290. Normalize the finite formulations to L=1,
x_star=g_star=f_star=0, and ||x_0||^2<=1.

Use formal vectors (x_0,g_0,...,g_k,w_term), their full Gram matrix G_term,
real prefix values f_0,...,f_k, and a real objective variable gamma_term.
Define the positions, proximal points, and offsets by

    x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t,       0<=i<=k,
    J={star,0,...,k},
    y_j=x_j-g_j,  delta_j=f_j-||g_j||^2/2,
    y_star=delta_star=0.

Keep EVERY distinct ordered prefix interpolation inequality Q_ij<=0 on J,
with Q as in the accepted predecessor. For every j in J put

    Bterm_j=gamma_term-2delta_j
                  -2<x_k-y_j,w_term>+||w_term||^2.

Consider the following program, denoted SDP_last(h_pre):

    maximize gamma_term
    subject to G_term PSD, G_term_(x0,x0)<=1,
               Q_ij<=0 for all distinct i,j in J,
               Bterm_j<=0 for all j in J,
               <g_k,w_term>=0.                         (1)

Every expression is affine in the Gram entries, values, and gamma_term.
Thus (1) is an SDP with k+3 formal vectors. It has no final-position
variable, no stepsize variable, and no rank constraint. Its exact value is

    value SDP_last(h_pre)=rho_last(h_pre)>0.             (2)

Both this primal maximum and its dual minimum are attained. Every optimal
dual produces a finite optimal final step b>1. Conversely every optimal
final step has an optimal dual of (1). The reconstruction is explicit below.

No earlier stepsize is optimized in (1). Its prefix interpolation still
depends on every supplied earlier step in its actual chronological position.

## 2. A direct lower certificate against every last step

Take ANY feasible point of (1) and realize its Gram matrix by vectors.
Fact1252b8823bb5a290 supplies the maximal legal prefix interpolant F=H*,
where

    H(v)=||v||^2/2+max_(j in J)(<y_j,v>-delta_j).

The terminal inequalities and orthogonality give, for every real b,

    gamma_term
      <=2<x_k,w_term>-2H(w_term)
       =2<x_k-b g_k,w_term>-2H(w_term)
      <=2F(x_k-b g_k).                                 (3)

For b>=0 this is a legal final GD step after precisely the supplied prefix.
If ||x_0||>0, the last expression is at most
W_(k+1)(h_pre,b)||x_0||^2<=W_(k+1)(h_pre,b). If ||x_0||=0,
all prefix iterates equal zero and F(x_k-b g_k)=0. Therefore, writing v_line
for the supremum in (1),

    v_line<=rho_last(h_pre).                            (4)

More generally, a particular feasible prefix and orthogonal vector give the
quantitative lower certificate

    rho_last(h_pre) >= 2min_j[delta_j+<x_k-y_j,w_term>]
                                      -||w_term||^2,    (5)

when its initial norm is one. Every prefix anchor appears in this minimum.
There is no presumed two-anchor support and no adaptive stepsize on its RHS.

## 3. Primal attainment and positivity

The complete feasible prefix is compact in Gram/value coordinates. Indeed,
the predecessor interpolates each feasible prefix by a legal function, and,
with D_i=product_(t<i)(1+h_t), gives

    ||x_i||,||g_i||<=D_i,       0<=f_i<=D_i^2/2.

These bounds and Cauchy--Schwarz bound every prefix Gram entry. The set is
closed and nonempty. For purposes of maximizing (1), restrict gamma_term>=0:
gamma_term=0,w_term=0 is feasible because delta_j>=0 for every prefix jet.
The star constraint implies

    ||w_term-x_k||^2<=||x_k||^2-gamma_term,

so ||w_term||<=2D_k and 0<=gamma_term<=D_k^2. All additional Gram entries
are bounded. This restricted feasible set is compact, proving primal attainment.

Its value is positive. Choose distinct theta_1,theta_2 in
(0,1/[2(1+sum h_t)]) and the actual quadratic

    f(z_1,z_2)=(theta_1 z_1^2+theta_2 z_2^2)/2,
    x_0=(1/sqrt(2),1/sqrt(2)).

Each prefix polynomial P_l=product_(t<k)(1-h_t theta_l) is positive.
At x_k, both coordinates are nonzero and x_k is not parallel to g_k.
The quadratic along x_k-b g_k has a positive attained minimum: in the
positive definite quadratic metric, the line misses the unique minimizer 0.
Equivalently its minimum is the positive expression proved in Section8 below.
At that minimizing step, let w_term be its actual terminal gradient and
gamma_term its positive normalized objective. Exact scalar differentiation
gives <g_k,w_term>=0. The actual outgoing interpolation inequalities,
expanded using that orthogonality, are exactly Bterm_j<=0. This is a positive
feasible point of (1), so v_line>0.

## 4. A strict primal on a positive compressed prefix

First suppose all k prefix steps are positive, allowing k=0. Choose k+2
distinct eigenvalues theta_l in (0,1), a diagonal matrix A with those
eigenvalues, and an x_0 with every coordinate nonzero and ||x_0||<1.
Use the quadratic f(x)=<x,Ax>/2 for the actual prefix. The k+2 formal
columns (x_0,g_0,...,g_k) evaluate the polynomials

    1, z, z(1-h_0 z),...,z product_(t<k)(1-h_t z)

at the distinct eigenvalues, with nonzero coordinate factors. Their degrees
are 0,...,k+1 and their leading coefficients are nonzero, so the evaluation
matrix is invertible. All distinct prefix positions differ, including from
zero: their polynomial differences are nonzero and cannot vanish at all
k+2 nodes. For every distinct prefix pair,

    Q_ij=-<x_i-x_j,A(I-A)(x_i-x_j)>/2<0.

Adjoin a nonzero w_term in one fresh orthogonal coordinate. The enlarged
Gram matrix is positive definite and <g_k,w_term>=0. Choose gamma_term
sufficiently negative to make every Bterm_j strictly negative. This is
a strict point for every inequality, inside the orthogonality equality.

## 5. Separation and an attained full-space dual

Here are explicit dual-attainment details; Slater is not used as an unexplained
black box. Collect all the Q and Bterm residuals into a vector R(z), with
z=(G_term,f,gamma_term), and set E(G_term)=2<g_k,w_term>.
Consider the set O of (s,t,c,e) for which there exists G_term positive
definite and real f,gamma_term with

    s>R(z) coordinatewise,
    t>G_term_(x0,x0)-1,
    c<gamma_term,
    e=E(G_term).

It is convex. It is open: a positive definite witness and its finitely many
strict margins persist under sufficiently small changes; any small requested
change in e is realized by a small change in the (g_k,w_term) Gram entry.
It is nonempty, and (0,0,v_line,0) is outside it by definition of v_line.
Finite-dimensional separation gives a nonzero functional with coefficients
(lambda,alpha,q_cert,-eta,b), where lambda are the Q coefficients and alpha
are the Bterm coefficients. Independent upward changes of s,t and downward
changes of c force lambda,alpha,q_cert,eta>=0; b is unrestricted.

One has eta>0. If eta=0 and any of lambda,alpha,q_cert is positive, substitute
the strict equality-feasible point of Section4 into the separating inequality
and take limits to its residuals. Its left side is strictly negative and
E=0, a contradiction. If all those coefficients vanish, a nonzero functional
could only be b e, but O contains e of both signs, also a contradiction.
Divide all coefficients by eta to set eta=1.

Taking limits gives, for EVERY G_term PSD and EVERY real f,gamma_term,

    sum lambda_ij Q_ij+sum alpha_j Bterm_j
      +q_cert(G_term_(x0,x0)-1)-gamma_term+b E(G_term)
                          >=-v_line.                  (6)

The extension from positive definite to PSD uses G_term+epsilon I followed
by epsilon down to zero; there is no equality restriction in (6), because e
is itself an output coordinate of O. Free gamma_term in (6) forces
sum_j alpha_j=1. Free f_i force the COMPLETE prefix flow

    out_i^pre-in_i^pre=2alpha_i,          0<=i<=k.

After these cancellations, (6) contains the full quadratic

    M_line=q_cert||x_0||^2-2sum_j alpha_j delta_j
       +||w_term||^2
       -2<w_term,x_k-sum_j alpha_j y_j-b g_k>
       +sum_(i!=j in J)lambda_ij Q_ij.                  (7)

This is PSD on ALL formal vectors, without imposing E=0: otherwise a scaled
rank-one Gram matrix along a negative direction contradicts (6). Taking zero
Gram in (6) yields q_cert<=v_line. Conversely, evaluate (7) on a feasible
point of (1). Its unreduced form is

    M_line=q_cert||x_0||^2-gamma_term
                 +sum lambda Q+sum alpha Bterm+bE.

All added residual terms are nonpositive and E=0; PSD gives
gamma_term<=q_cert||x_0||^2<=q_cert. Therefore v_line<=q_cert, so equality
holds and this dual is attained.

Define

    V_alpha(b)=x_k-b g_k-sum_j alpha_j y_j,
    R_line=q_cert||x_0||^2-2sum_j alpha_j delta_j
                         -||V_alpha(b)||^2+sum lambda Q.

The precise certificate identity is

    M_line=||w_term-V_alpha(b)||^2+R_line.               (8)

As w_term is a free formal vector, full PSD is equivalent to PSD of the
entire prefix quadratic R_line. This is exactly the full certificate
parameterization of accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290 for an appended step b,
except that separation has not yet proved b nonnegative.

## 6. Positivity of the recovered step, equality, and reconstruction

Let M_pre=W_k(h_pre)>0 and D=D_k. For every legal normalized prefix with
last function value t=f_k, convexity and Cauchy--Schwarz give
t<=<g_k,x_k><=D||g_k||. A step of length1 therefore satisfies

    2f_(k+1)<=2t-||g_k||^2<=2t-t^2/D^2.

Here 0<=t<=M_pre/2<=D^2/2, and the last expression is increasing in this
interval. Consequently

    W_(k+1)(h_pre,1)
        <=M_pre-M_pre^2/(4D^2)<M_pre.                   (9)

By (4), v_line is at most the left side. If the attained dual in (8) had
b<=0, then its full PSD identity would still bound every actual trajectory
with that real final step: interpolation necessity is independent of the
sign of a chosen step. Convexity gives

    f(x_k-b g_k)>=f(x_k)-b||g_k||^2>=f(x_k)

for each instance. Taking the supremum over prefixes contradicts
q_cert=v_line<M_pre. Hence b>0. Now (8) is a legal full upper certificate
for the nonnegative schedule (h_pre,b), so

    W_(k+1)(h_pre,b)<=v_line<=rho_last(h_pre)
                              <=W_(k+1)(h_pre,b).

All quantities are equal. This proves (2), finite final-step attainment,
and the claimed exact optimization order without any minimax assumption.

Take ANY primal maximizer of (1), and form its maximal interpolant F.
Pair it with ANY optimal final step b. At this step, (3) and normalization
give equal endpoints:

    v_line<=2<x_k-b g_k,w_term>-2H(w_term)
       <=2F(x_k-b g_k)<=v_line||x_0||^2<=v_line.

Because v_line>0, ||x_0||=1. Uniqueness of the conjugate maximizer gives

    grad F(x_k-b g_k)=w_term,
    2F(x_k-b g_k)=v_line.                              (10)

Thus the auxiliary vector is a TRUE terminal gradient at a point on the
original gradient ray. Every omitted incoming terminal inequality holds
for this actual F. No collinearity constraint has been silently dropped.

Every optimal final b has a selected tight full certificate by the accepted
predecessor. Substituting its alpha and prefix multipliers into (7)-(8)
gives an optimal dual of (1) with equality multiplier b. This proves the
converse assertion as well.

Here g_k and w_term are nonzero: if g_k=0 the actual prefix point is a
minimizer, contradicting (10), and w_term=0 contradicts the positive value
and the star inequality. Cocoercivity between the last query and (10),
using <g_k,w_term>=0, yields

    b||g_k||^2 >= ||g_k-w_term||^2
                 =||g_k||^2+||w_term||^2.

Hence EVERY optimal final step satisfies

    b>=1+||w_term||^2/||g_k||^2>1.                      (11)

The same primal maximizer and its one function F work for every such b,
and (3) gives 2F(x_k-b' g_k)>=v_line for ALL b'>=0. This also proves
the final-stage minimax identity in the original class:

    inf_(b>=0) sup_(legal instances) terminal_error
       =sup_(legal instances) inf_(b>=0) terminal_error.

The supremum on the right is attained by this one F. It lives in the span
of the k+2 prefix formal vectors: at equality (8) gives w_term=V_alpha(b).

## 7. Zero steps and full original-coordinate lifting

For a prefix with zero steps, compress its positive steps, retaining as
representatives the original indices of those steps and original index k
for the last queried gradient. If two queried positions coincide, their
two ordered Q inequalities add to ||g_i-g_j||^2<=0; then their individual
inequalities give f_i=f_j. Thus feasible original prefixes, including their
Gram matrices, reduce to exactly the corresponding compressed prefixes.
Their terminal constraints Bterm duplicate those at identical jets.
This proves equality of the primal SDP values in both directions, and
the same equality of actual W values follows from the trajectory recursion.

Apply Sections4-6 to the positive compressed prefix, including length zero.
Lift its dual by setting omitted alpha and prefix multipliers to zero and
using the coordinate selection of the accepted fact. The equality multiplier
still multiplies 2<g_k,w_term>, with original last index k. The full lifted
matrix is T_pick^T Mhat_line T_pick; unused formal-gradient columns are zero.
It is PSD on the ENTIRE original formal space without imposing any duplicate
gradient equality there. The same b and q_cert are therefore valid, attained,
and optimal. This treats leading, interior, trailing, and all-zero prefixes.

## 8. A quantitative two-curvature prefix-polynomial guard

Here is an explicit feasible family, including a closed optimized value.
Choose distinct theta_1,theta_2 in (0,1] for which

    P_l=product_(t<k)(1-h_t theta_l)!=0,    l=1,2.

Put c_l=P_l^2>0, d_l=c_l theta_l^3, and

    omega=sqrt(d_2)/(sqrt(d_1)+sqrt(d_2)).

For the FIXED legal quadratic f(z_1,z_2)=(theta_1 z_1^2+theta_2 z_2^2)/2
and initial point (sqrt(omega),sqrt(1-omega)), its normalized error curve
after the supplied prefix and an arbitrary final b is

    E(b)=omega c_1 theta_1(1-b theta_1)^2
                  +(1-omega)c_2 theta_2(1-b theta_2)^2.

The quadratic coefficient is D_spec=omega d_1+(1-omega)d_2>0, and its
minimizing b is

    b_spec=[omega c_1 theta_1^2+(1-omega)c_2 theta_2^2]/D_spec>0.

Expanding the discriminant gives, for every b>=0,

    E(b)=q_spec+D_spec(b-b_spec)^2,

    q_spec=theta_1 theta_2(theta_1-theta_2)^2 c_1 c_2
                  / (sqrt(d_1)+sqrt(d_2))^2 >0.          (12)

For an arbitrary weight w in [0,1], the minimum is the same numerator
times w(1-w)/(w d_1+(1-w)d_2). Its maximum over w is attained at omega,
with the factor 1/(sqrt(d_1)+sqrt(d_2))^2. This follows from

    w d_1+(1-w)d_2
      -w(1-w)(sqrt(d_1)+sqrt(d_2))^2
        =(w sqrt(d_1)-(1-w)sqrt(d_2))^2>=0.

Thus the explicitly optimized two-coordinate family gives the quantitative
full-prefix-polynomial bound

    rho_last(h_pre)>=q_spec.                            (13)

All data are from one deterministic function, and its stationary terminal
gradient is a feasible w_term in (1). This illustrates (5) without assuming
any reduction of the complete SDP to quadratics. Its P_l retain the complete
prefix product but discard order; no constant-preserving comparison with
the Zhang--Jiang profile is asserted for this restricted spectral family.

## 9. Scope and literature audit

The equality (2) is a one-stage result. Earlier GD steps remain inside every
Q inequality, so there is no exchange over them. The probabilities alpha_j
are certificate weights, while b is one fixed algorithmic step recovered from
an equality multiplier. The full prefix PSD condition in (8) is indispensable.

The primary paper de Klerk, Glineur, Taylor, arXiv1606.09365v2, was downloaded
as PDF and HTML, its HTML text extracted, and Sections2.1,3.2,6 read. It treats
strongly convex functions and an initial FUNCTION-gap normalization. Its
multi-step orthogonality SDP is explicitly a relaxation of the collinearity
constraints; tightness there uses a matching quadratic. It is not an applicable
black box for (2). Its discussion motivated checking reconstruction carefully.
No external theorem from it is a proof premise here, and no literature result
is being promoted without the project's verifier gate.
