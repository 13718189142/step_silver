# Exact terminal re-selection through the fixed-prefix maximal extension

Status: The expanded self-contained package, including arbitrary zero steps and the empty horizon, is ACCEPTED as fact 1252b8823bb5a290. See max2_terminal_reselection_selected.md for the exact submitted package and max2_terminal_reselection_errata.md for the notation note. This earlier positive-step research draft is retained for history.
This is a precise positive support result for the full optimal certificate set.
It removes only incoming edges at the UNQUERIED final sample, not arbitrary
remote forward edges at queried samples. No descent or profile theorem is claimed.

## 1. The reduced primal retains the complete prefix

Normalize L=1, x_star=g_star=f_star=0. Let h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)) be a
finite positive schedule, n>=1, and let P={star,0,...,n-1}.
As usual x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t, and

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2.

Consider the full Gram primal on (x_0,g_0,...,g_n), with G>=0,
||x_0||^2<=1, and objective maximize 2f_n. Keep

    Q_ij<=0 for every distinct i,j in P,
    Q_(n,j)<=0 for every j in P.                    (1)

Delete every Q_(j,n)<=0, j in P, including Q_(star,n).
No other constraint is removed. In particular all remote forward and
backward interpolation constraints inside the prefix remain.

The value of this reduced primal equals the true unrestricted W_n(h).

## 2. Exactness by an explicit globally legal extension

Take any feasible reduced transcript and realize its Gram matrix by
finite-dimensional Euclidean vectors. For j in P put

    a_j=<g_j,x_j>-f_j,
    H_j(v)=a_j+<x_j,v-g_j>+||v-g_j||^2/2,
    H(v)=max_(j in P) H_j(v),
    F(x)=sup_v {<x,v>-H(v)}.                       (2)

Each H_j is a quadratic with Hessian I, so H is finite, continuous and
1-strongly convex. Its quadratic growth makes the supremum defining F
finite and attained at a unique vector v(x). The optimality relation is
x in partial H(v(x)). Strong convexity gives

    <x-x',v(x)-v(x')> >= ||v(x)-v(x')||^2,

and hence ||v(x)-v(x')||<=||x-x'||. The maximizing affine functions in (2)
then show grad F(x)=v(x): the lower bound from testing v(x) and upper
bound from testing v(x') squeeze the first-order difference, with an
O(||x-x'||^2) error. Thus F is globally convex, differentiable, and
1-smooth.

For any i,j in P,

    H_j(g_i)-a_i=Q_ij<=0.

Consequently H(g_i)=a_i, and x_i is a subgradient of H at g_i, since the
i-th quadratic is active there and has gradient x_i. It follows that
grad F(x_i)=g_i and F(x_i)=f_i. The star sample gives F(0)=0 and
grad F(0)=0, so zero is a global minimizer. This proves legality of the
complete prefix without invoking an external interpolation black box.

Because GD through x_n uses only g_0,...,g_(n-1), the SAME predetermined
schedule on F follows all the supplied positions through x_n.
The retained terminal inequalities say

    H_j(g_n)<=<x_n,g_n>-f_n for every j in P.

Therefore

    f_n<=<x_n,g_n>-H(g_n)<=F(x_n).                  (3)

The new actual terminal value is at least the reduced transcript's
objective. Thus every reduced feasible value is at most W_n(h).
Conversely any legal trajectory is reduced feasible, proving exactness.

The constructed F is also the pointwise maximal smooth-convex extension
of the fixed prefix. Indeed, for any legal extension K, the elementary
smooth-convex interpolation inequality between each fixed sample j and
an arbitrary sample x with gradient grad K(x) gives
K(x)<=<x,grad K(x)>-H(grad K(x))<=F(x).
Maximality is useful motivation but (3) alone proves reduced-PEP exactness.

At an optimal reduced transcript, 2f_n=W_n(h), both inequalities in (3)
are equalities. Uniqueness of the conjugate maximizer implies
g_n=grad F(x_n) and f_n=F(x_n). Hence an optimal reduced primal is itself
fully interpolable: the deleted incoming terminal inequalities hold at
optimality. This statement is about optimal transcripts, not all reduced
feasible transcripts.

## 3. The reduced dual attains the SAME value

Here are the necessary finite-dimensional details, so re-selection is
not an appeal to an unattained dual infimum.

A strict reduced primal exists when every h_t>0. Choose n+2 distinct
numbers theta_l in (0,1), take a quadratic with diagonal Hessian
diag(theta_l), and an initial vector with every coordinate nonzero and
norm less than one. The columns x_0,g_0,...,g_n are evaluations of

    1, z, z product_(t<1)(1-h_t z), ...,
                       z product_(t<n)(1-h_t z)

at the distinct nodes, times nonzero initial coordinates. These n+2
polynomials have degrees 0,1,...,n+1 with nonzero leading coefficients,
so their evaluation matrix is invertible. Thus G is positive definite.

No two positions among star and 0,...,n are equal: their distinct
prefix polynomials, each of degree at most n, cannot agree at all n+2
distinct nodes; none of those polynomials vanishes at all nodes either.
For the strictly convex quadratic with Hessian strictly below I,
direct substitution gives

    Q_ij=-1/2 (x_i-x_j)^T Hquad(I-Hquad)(x_i-x_j)<0

for every distinct pair. In particular every retained inequality is strict.

The reduced optimal value is finite by Section2 and the elementary GD
bound W_n(h)<=product_t(1+h_t)^2. Its dual attainment now follows directly
from separating an open convex set, as follows. Let E_red be the retained
ordered pairs. The open convex set of (a,b,c) satisfying, for some G>=0,f,

    a_ij>Q_ij(f,G) (ij in E_red),
    b>G_00-1,
    c<2f_n

does not contain (0,0,q), where q=W_n(h). A separating hyperplane has
nonnegative coefficients lambda_ij,r,eta, not all zero, and yields

    sum_(E_red) lambda_ij Q_ij(f,G)
             +r(G_00-1)-eta*2f_n >= -eta*q

for all G>=0,f. The strict primal point rules out eta=0. Normalize eta=1.
Arbitrary values f then force out_i-in_i=2*1_(i=n), and arbitrary PSD G
forces the full matrix M(r,lambda,h)>=0. Testing G=f=0 gives r<=q;
weak duality and reduced exactness give r>=q. Thus r=q and the dual
attains the exact objective value.

Extend this reduced certificate to the original full format by setting
all omitted multipliers to zero. We have proved the precise re-selection:

    At every positive h there EXISTS a tight full certificate with
        lambda_(j,n)=0 for ALL j in P.             (4)

In particular nu_n=0 can be imposed, and the remaining outgoing
terminal weights sum to exactly two. This does not say that every
optimal certificate already has this support. It does not assert that
a source vector can be held fixed during the re-selection.

This argument is the ordinary finite-dimensional separation proof only
for the reduced system just justified. It does not repeat or claim as
new the historical global minimizer/KKT interface.

## 4. Exact terminal square and monotone probability coordinates

For a certificate satisfying (4), write

    alpha_j=lambda_(n,j)/2 >=0, j in P,
    sum_(j in P)alpha_j=1,
    y_j=x_j-g_j,  delta_j=f_j-||g_j||^2/2,
    V_alpha=x_n-sum_(j in P)alpha_j y_j.            (5)

Let lambda^P denote the unchanged multipliers on ordered pairs inside P.
Direct expansion, with delta_star=y_star=0, gives the FULL identity

    M(q,lambda,h)
      =||g_n-V_alpha||^2+R_alpha,                  (6)

where

    R_alpha=q||x_0||^2
                  -2sum_(j in P)alpha_j delta_j-||V_alpha||^2
                  +sum_(i,j in P)lambda^P_ij Q_ij. (7)

The prefix flow cancels all function values in R_alpha. In detail,
for each finite i<n it is

    out_i^P-in_i^P=2alpha_i,

because the removed terminal outgoing edge n->i supplied incoming flow
2alpha_i in the full certificate. R_alpha is thus a quadratic form
in (x_0,g_0,...,g_(n-1)).

Full PSD is EQUIVALENT to R_alpha>=0: one direction follows from (6),
and the other follows by minimizing the full quadratic over the
independent formal vector g_n. This is an exact Schur complement,
not the omission of an unobserved kernel direction.

The same identity is a necessary-and-sufficient certificate
parameterization of this selected subface: nonnegative prefix
multipliers with the displayed prefix flow, alpha in the probability
simplex, and the complete PSD remainder R_alpha.

For explicit chronological coefficients define

    w_(-1)=alpha_star,
    w_t=alpha_star+sum_(j=0)^t alpha_j, 0<=t<n.

Then 0<=w_(-1)<=w_0<=...<=w_(n-1)=1, and

    V_alpha=alpha_star x_0
            -sum_(t<n)[h_t w_t-(w_t-w_(t-1))]g_t. (8)

This keeps all earlier gradient coordinates. In particular
lambda_(n,star)=2alpha_star<=2. The formula is not a scalar-mass
replacement for the prefix PSD condition (7).

On ANY actual matching worst case for this selected certificate,

    g_n=V_alpha,  R_alpha=0,
    alpha_j Q_(n,j)=0, lambda^P_ij Q_ij=0.          (9)

Thus re-selection gives an actual terminal gradient formula for the
matching primal. It does not impose such a formula on every legal
trajectory; away from equality the square in (6) measures its defect.

## 5. Relation to the remote-edge obstruction and source variation cost

For a two-step suffix k=n-2, the selected support eliminates remote
forward edges i->n and the local edge (n-1)->n. It does NOT eliminate
remote i->(n-1) for i<k. Those edges are within the complete prefix
and remain in (7). In particular the old three-step counterexample
at h=(2,9/4,3) already has no incoming edges at its final sample3:
its hostile edge0->2 survives this reduction unchanged.

At a full stationary matching tuple with nu_k>0, the common strict
barrier derived directly from interpolation still applies:
lambda_(k,k+1)=0 across the full optimal set, with positive old slack.
The terminal re-selection may alter earlier source coefficients and
may alter which actual matching primal supplies earlier-cut stationarity.
One cannot transport all stationarity equations to an arbitrarily
chosen reselected certificate.

For any joint change from a fixed stationary base, the exact old-primal
identity remains

    q'-q >= sum lambda'_ij(-Q_ij(old))
                  -sum_t (h'_t-h_t)<g_t,z_t(lambda'-lambda)>.

Neither the terminal square (6) nor dropping the redundant terminal
incoming inequalities cancels this inactive cost or bilinear term.

## 6. Literature check and scope

The explicit maximum-of-quadratics conjugate construction is consistent
with Taylor, Hendrickx and Glineur, Smooth strongly convex interpolation
and exact worst-case performance of first-order methods,
arXiv:1502.05666, Theorem4 and Remark2. Their Theorem6 uses a full-rank
quadratic transcript to establish fixed-step PEP duality. Those sections
were read in the downloaded paper. All construction, reduced-system
exactness, separation, and full square identities needed here are
written out, rather than imported as unexplained external theorems.
A targeted Matlas query returned unrelated results; no such result was used.

This is a concrete, validly scoped route to re-selection and a complete
matrix parameterization of the resulting subface. It is not a descent
theorem at a global minimizer, does not remove the surviving remote
prefix edges, and does not resolve Huber tightness or the exact DP mass
restriction.
