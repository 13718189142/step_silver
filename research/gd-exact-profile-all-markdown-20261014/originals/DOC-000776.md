# Two-sided residual join with aggregate interpolation cancellation

Status: unverified exact certificate identity and successful recovery of the ordinary ConPP join on its structural subspace. No improved recursive family has been found. This continues the failed aggregate-only rule4be2737d7e8764a6 without silently reusing its insufficient positivity restrictions.

For a GD block and nonnegative proof weights u, write D=sum u_i, d=x_start-x_end, U=sum u_i g_i, e=U-d, and

  E=2sum_i u_i(f_i-f_end)+sum_i u_i||g_i||^2
      +2sum_i u_i<g_i,x_start-x_i>-||U||^2.

Then E>=0 implies the objective guarantee W<=1/D by the squared-distance identity reproduced in research/max4_nonlocal_tail_transport.md. All formulas below use Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0, at smoothness1.

## 1. Two arbitrary blocks and the exact new state

Let block a end at y with gradient g. A bridge of physical length tau>=1 moves to y'=y-tau g, and block b starts there and ends at z with gradient q. Let the blocks have states(D_a,e_a,E_a) and(D_b,e_b,E_b); denote the tail gradient aggregate by U_b and its displacement by d_b, so e_b=U_b-d_b. The tail's proof-weight vector is v and its terminal entry is v_end. Assume certificates

  E_a>=nu_a||e_a-K_a g||^2,
  E_b>=nu_b||e_b-K_b q||^2,

where the nu coefficients are nonnegative. Exact structural relations such as e_a=K_a g may instead be retained as relations, without replacing them by a finite quadratic penalty.

Form new proof weights by keeping the prefix weights, adding eta to the prefix terminal entry, scaling all tail weights by beta, and adding gamma to the final entry. Require

  beta>=0,
  prefix_terminal_weight+eta>=0,
  beta*v_end+gamma>=0,
  D_c=D_a+eta+beta D_b+gamma>0.

The new residual is exactly

  e_c=e_a+(eta-tau)g+(beta-1)U_b+e_b+gamma q.         (1)

The change in energy, before eliminating the function difference Delta=f_y-f_z, is

  E_c=E_a+beta E_b+2(D_a+eta)Delta+R_0,

where

  R_0=eta(1-eta)||g||^2-2eta<e_a,g>
      +beta(1-beta)||U_b||^2
      +2beta<U_b,(tau-eta)g-e_a>
      +gamma(1-gamma)||q||^2
      -2gamma<q,e_a+(eta-tau)g+(beta-1)U_b+e_b>.      (2)

Equation(2) follows by expanding the definition of E under the displayed changes of proof weights; it involves no smoothness inequality yet.

## 2. Four aggregate interpolation identities

Define

  A_y=-2sum_(i in tail) v_i Q_(i,y),
  A_z=-2sum_(i in tail) v_i Q_(i,z),
  P=-2Q_(z,y), N=-2Q_(y,z).

Substitution of y-x_i=tau g+(y'-x_i) and d_b=U_b-e_b gives

  A_y=2D_b Delta-E_b-||U_b||^2
         -2(tau-1)<U_b,g>-D_b||g||^2,

  A_z=-E_b+||U_b||^2-2<U_b,e_b-q>-D_b||q||^2,

  P=2Delta-||g||^2-||q||^2+2(1-tau)<g,q>
          -2<q,U_b>+2<q,e_b>,

  N=-2Delta+(2tau-1)||g||^2-||q||^2
           +2<g,U_b>-2<g,e_b>+2<g,q>.              (3)

Choose scalars c,m,t_y,t_z satisfying

  c>=0, m>=0, t_z>=0, t_y+c*v_end>=0,
  c D_b+t_y-t_z=D_a+eta.                            (4)

Although t_y can be negative, the actual interpolation multipliers in c A_y+m A_z+t_y P+t_z N are all nonnegative. At the edge(z,y), A_y already contributes terminal coefficient c*v_end, so the total coefficient is c*v_end+t_y. At each other tail-to-y edge the coefficient is c*v_i; at tail-to-z edges it is m*v_i; and the edge(y,z) has coefficient t_z. Self-edges vanish. These are the signs that matter, rather than the signs of all four aggregate coordinates separately.

The function-coefficient equality in(4) eliminates Delta from(2).

## 3. A finite-dimensional sufficient certificate test

Define the following quadratic form in(e_a,e_b,g,U_b,q):

  R=R_0
    +c[||U_b||^2+2(tau-1)<U_b,g>+D_b||g||^2]
    +m[-||U_b||^2+2<U_b,e_b-q>+D_b||q||^2]
    +t_y[||g||^2+||q||^2+2(tau-1)<g,q>
                       +2<q,U_b>-2<q,e_b>]
    +t_z[-(2tau-1)||g||^2+||q||^2-2<g,U_b>
                       +2<g,e_b>-2<g,q>].          (5)

Equations(2)–(4) give the exact identity

  E_c=E_a+(beta+c+m)E_b+R
                  +c A_y+m A_z+t_y P+t_z N.        (6)

Consequently, for any nu_c>=0 and real K_c, it suffices that the quadratic form

  R+nu_a||e_a-K_a g||^2
      +(beta+c+m)nu_b||e_b-K_b q||^2
      -nu_c||e_a+(eta-tau)g+(beta-1)U_b
                          +e_b+(gamma-K_c)q||^2    (7)

be positive semidefinite. Any exact structural relations from the input blocks may first be substituted into(7); imposing positivity only on that known subspace is then legitimate. For free residual variables, positivity means the full symmetric5-by5 coefficient matrix is PSD, in every Euclidean dimension. This implies E_c>=nu_c||e_c-K_c q||^2 and W<=1/D_c.

This is a certificate construction, not a new algorithm with auxiliary state: all u,v and residuals are proof quantities. The actual algorithm is precisely the predetermined concatenation[a,tau,b].

## 4. Exact ordinary-ConPP calibration

Take genuinely primitive input blocks of capacities K=1+sum(a) and A=1+sum(b), with their standard proof weights(a,K) and(b,A). Then

  D_a=2K-1, D_b=2A-1,
  e_a=K g, e_b=Aq,
  E_a=V_a>=0, E_b=V_b>=0.

Let r>0 be the unique root

  r^2+(A+K)r-AK=0,

so the usual bridge is tau=1+r. Since r<A, set

  J=K+r,
  beta=1, eta=tau-K, gamma=J,
  K_c=K+A+r,
  c=m=J/(A-r),
  t_y=c(1-A), t_z=c r.                             (8)

All actual weight conditions hold: the new bridge weight is K+eta=tau; the final weight is A+gamma=K_c. The interpolation conditions(4) also hold, because

  t_y+c*v_end=c(1-A)+c A=c>0,
  c D_b+t_y-t_z=c(A-r)=J=D_a+eta.

After substitution of e_a=Kg and e_b=Aq, the quadratic remainder(5) vanishes identically. One can check this by the equivalent two scalar identities

  r(K+A+r)=KA,
  K+r=2Ar/(A-r).

Indeed the resulting energy identity is exactly

  V_c=V_a+(1+2c)V_b
          -2c sum_(i in tail, i!=end) h_i(Q_(i,y)+Q_(i,z))
          -2c Q_(z,y)-2cr Q_(y,z),                 (9)

where V_c is the saturated primitive energy for the combined physical sum K+A+r-1. Every multiplier on -Q in(9) is nonnegative. Thus the rule recovers the successful ordinary ConPP certificate on its actual structural identities, with capacity F(K,A)=K+A+r.

The negative aggregate coefficient t_y in(8) is essential when A>1, even though the final multiplier on its interpolation edge is positive. Earlier screens that required every aggregate coefficient nonnegative excluded this calibration. This is the same distinction exposed by the signed interval-coordinate test cd0f93f08a50828f.

## Remaining task

The calibration does not prove closure for arbitrary finite residual penalties. At the exact primitive point e_a=Kg and e_b=Aq are identities, not estimates with a finite nu. A candidate involving nonprimitive blocks must either retain its actual additional boundary relations or satisfy the full matrix(7). No parameters giving a competitive recursive nonprimitive family have been found, and no claim of an improved denominator is made. The endpoint-only weight template is separately refuted by12ea2963a6e53e67; it cannot be substituted for this full internal-weight accounting.
