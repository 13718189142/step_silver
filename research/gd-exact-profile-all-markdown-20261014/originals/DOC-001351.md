## 1. Statements and scope

For a finite nonnegative word h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), put s=sum h_i and
C=1+s. On formal GD data x_(i+1)=x_i-h_i g_i define

 Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                              -sC||g_n||^2.

A full ordinary primitive certificate is an array lambda_ij>=0 on ALL
ordered distinct finite query pairs such that V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij
cancels every independent f_i and is PSD on ALL n+1 independent gradients.
Entries are allowed to be zero; no support is imposed on the input.
This is exactly the interface in fact04219554e41d9750. A signed-terminal
certificate permits arbitrary signs only on lambda_(i,n), i<n.

The joint claims below are:

(A) For every nonnegative word with at most two positive steps, existence
of a full ordinary primitive certificate is equivalent to existence for
its reversal. The proof gives an explicit certificate transformation,
including a possible change of the PSD remainder and full zero-step lift.

(B) Suppose h=(a,1+r,b), A=1+sum a, D=1+sum b,
T=A+D+r, r>0, and rT=AD. Suppose each child a,b has at most two positive
steps. A full ordinary certificate for h yields full ordinary certificates
for BOTH children after re-selection. Consequently h and rev(h) have
ordinary certificates simultaneously, by an explicit construction.

(C) In particular, for four physical steps and a saturated middle cut
(at index1 or2), ordinary full-certificate feasibility is reversal
invariant. Any such feasible word has capacity T<77/10<193/25.
Thus a four-step threshold seed from fact04219554e41d9750 cannot have
either middle cut saturated.

These assertions do NOT prove arbitrary four-step reversal. They cannot
transport the threshold C82/C83 prism exclusions: the class in(C) does
not meet the threshold region. Endpoint saturated cuts can leave a
three-positive-step child, which is outside(A)--(B). Fully unsaturated
four-step words are also outside(B). The original exact-profile goal
remains open. No new fact is asserted.

## 2. Zero-step compression and full-space restoration

This preliminary statement applies to ordinary certificates. It also
applies to signed-terminal certificates when all nonterminal-targeting
coefficients are nonnegative.

Given a nonnegative word, partition its original query indices into
classes that represent the same point after deleting zero updates.
Restrict a supplied certificate to formal data where the values and
gradients in each class are equal. Every original position becomes the
corresponding compressed formal position. A Q between two different
classes is the corresponding compressed Q; a Q within a class vanishes.
Aggregate multipliers over each ordered pair of different classes.
Value cancellation is preserved. The matrix restriction is a congruence,
so it preserves PSD. Nonnegative signs are preserved. In the signed
case only edges entering the final compressed class can be signed.
The original energy restricts to the compressed energy because zero
steps have coefficient zero, and the original terminal becomes the
compressed terminal.

Conversely, for an ORDINARY compressed certificate, choose the original
index of each positive step as its query representative, and the original
index n as the terminal representative. Put its multiplier on that
representative ordered pair and set every other original multiplier to
zero. The represented positions are exactly the compressed GD positions,
as formal identities, since every omitted update has zero coefficient.
The original energy equals the compressed one on those selected formal
gradients; its unused gradients have coefficient zero. Therefore the
lifted identity is PSD on the entire original space, with zero rows and
columns at unused gradients. No equality of duplicated formal gradients
is imposed in the FINAL certificate.

All-zero and empty words have V_h=0 and the zero certificate. Reversal
commutes with positive-step compression up to this representative choice.
It therefore suffices to prove(A) for one or two strictly positive steps.

## 3. Complete two-step signed-terminal repair

This section proves the repair used after inversion and after a cut.
For one positive step u, flow gives lambda_10=lambda_01+2u. Evaluation
at equal gradients gives zero energy and zero Q, so PSD implies that the
all-one vector is in the matrix kernel. Direct expansion then gives
lambda_01=2 and the full identity

 V_(u)+2Q_01+(2+2u)Q_10=(2-u^2)||g_0-g_1||^2.          (1)

Thus u<=sqrt(2), and the coefficients are already ordinary.

For two positive steps (u,v), s=u+v, introduce coefficient names

 a=lambda_01, b=lambda_12, c=lambda_02, d=lambda_20.

Only b,c are allowed to be signed. The other four entries are
nonnegative. Value cancellation gives

 lambda_10=a+c-d+2u,
 lambda_21=b+c-d+2s.

Equal gradients, with values following an affine trajectory, again give
V=Q=0; PSD implies the all-one vector is in the kernel. Expanding its
first and last rows gives

 u*a+s*c=2u,
 v*(b+c)+u*d=2s(u+1).

Consequently

 a=2-(s/u)c,
 b=2s(u+1)/v-c-(u/v)d,
 lambda_10=2(u+1)-(v/u)c-d,
 b=2(u+1)+(u/v)lambda_10>0.                           (2)

To audit both the kernel equations and the entire matrix, use U=g_0-g_1,
W=g_1-g_2. Substitution of the six Q terms gives

 M=[2-u^2+v(u-1)c/u]||U||^2
       +[c-(u-1)d]<U,W>+[Cpair-(u/v)d]||W||^2,
 Cpair=s[2(u+1)/v-s].                                (3)

For example, the forward adjacent Q_01 has quadratic part
(1/2-u)||g_0||^2-<g_0,g_1>+||g_1||^2/2; its reverse has
(||g_0||^2+||g_1||^2)/2+(u-1)<g_0,g_1>.
For Q_02 use x_2-x_0=-u g_0-v g_1; for Q_20 use its negative.
These expansions, the value equations, and(2) give(3) without a
restriction on the independent gradients.

If c>=0, every supplied coefficient is ordinary already, by(2).
If c<0, nonnegativity of the W diagonal in(3), with d>=0, gives
Cpair>=0. If u>=1, nonnegativity of the U diagonal and its nonpositive
c term give u<=sqrt(2). If u<1 that bound holds directly.
Replace the array by

 lambda'_01=2, lambda'_10=2+2u,
 lambda'_12=2s(u+1)/v, lambda'_21=2s(u+1)/v+2s,
 lambda'_02=lambda'_20=0.

These are nonnegative and satisfy exact flow. Formula(3) now gives

 M'=(2-u^2)||U||^2+Cpair||W||^2>=0.                    (4)

This is a change of the full PSD matrix, not a claim about preserving
its rank, its range, or its difference in PSD order. Section2 extends
this repair to words with at most two positive steps and any zero steps.

## 4. Unsigned reversal identity, rederived on the full space

Let the two positive steps be h=(u,v), C=1+u+v. Let e=(1,1,1)^T,
e_2=(0,0,1)^T, hbar=(u,v,0)^T, w=(u,v,1)^T, and

 T_h=[[1,0,0],[u,1,0],[u,v,1]], p=T_h e=(1,1+u,C)^T.

Let L be the row Laplacian of the supplied ordinary multipliers:
L_ij=-lambda_ij for i!=j, L_ii=sum_(j!=i)lambda_ij. Its flow is
L^T e=(-2u,-2v,2(u+v))^T. Define

 K=L+diag(hbar)-hbar e_2^T+diag(w).

Direct expansion of the full interface in fact04219554e41d9750 gives

 M=sym(K T_h)-C^2 e_2 e_2^T,                         (5)
 K e=w, K^T e=C e_2.

For an explicit check of(5), sym((K-L)T_h)=diag(u(2-u),v(2-v),1).
Moreover e^T M e=0, hence M e=0, and therefore

 K p=-C w+2C^2 e_2.

All off-diagonal entries of K are nonpositive. Its row-sum surplus is
w_i>0 at every row. If Kz=0, a coordinate attaining max_i|z_i| and
strict row diagonal dominance force this maximum to be zero. Thus K
is invertible. Put G=K^(-1). The displayed equations imply

 G w=e, G e_2=(p+C e)/(2C^2), e_2^T G=e^T/C.           (6)

Define

 Delta=[[0,-1,1],[-1,1,0],[1,0,0]].

For hrev=(v,u), write wrev=(v,u,1)^T and T_rev=T_(v,u).
Direct multiplication gives

 Delta e=e_2, Delta p=wrev, Delta T_rev=T_h^T Delta.

Set F=Delta G Delta and

 K_rev=4C^2 F-(wrev+2C e_2)e_2^T,
 lambda_rev,ij=-4C^2 F_ij  (i!=j).                    (7)

For now these coefficients are allowed to be signed. Equations(6) give
K_rev e=wrev and K_rev^T e=C e_2. The off-diagonals in(7), including
its last-column correction, are exactly the grounding of lambda_rev;
the row sums fix its diagonals. Thus every reversed function-value
coefficient cancels with the required flow (-2v,-2u,2(u+v)).

The entire reversed quadratic remainder obeys

 M_rev=4C^2 Delta G M G^T Delta.                       (8)

Here is a full algebra check. Inserting K_rev into sym(K_rev T_rev)
produces the non-boundary term 4C^2 sym(Delta G T_h^T Delta).
After subtracting C^2 e_2 e_2^T, its boundary term is

 -(wrev+C e_2)(wrev+C e_2)^T.

Inserting(5) in the right side of(8) produces the same non-boundary
term and the same boundary term, since Delta G e_2=(wrev+C e_2)/(2C^2).
This proves(8) on all three independent reversed gradients. No
positivity of the output multipliers has yet been inferred.

## 5. The four nonterminal-directed signs are automatic in dimension three

This is the additional sign argument specific to two physical steps.
Write k00=K_00 and k11=K_11, and put

 det2=k00*k11-lambda_01*lambda_10>0.

Strict row diagonal dominance gives k00>lambda_01 and
k11>lambda_10, so det2>0. The original value flows also give

 k00=lambda_01+lambda_02+2u=lambda_10+lambda_20,
 k11=lambda_10+lambda_12+2v=lambda_01+lambda_21.         (9)

Every column difference G(e_i-e_j) has last coordinate zero by(6).
Its first two coordinates are obtained by inverting

 [[k00,-lambda_01],[-lambda_10,k11]].

In particular, for columns0-1 the first two coordinates are
((k11-lambda_01)/det2,(lambda_10-k00)/det2), and for
columns1-2 they are(lambda_01/det2,k00/det2).
Using the displayed Delta and(9) gives ALL FOUR signs

 F_01=-lambda_20/det2,
 F_10=-(lambda_02+2u)/det2,
 F_20=-lambda_01/det2,
 F_21=-lambda_21/det2.                                (10)

Therefore the output lambda_rev is nonnegative on every pair whose
target is not the terminal2. Only lambda_rev,02 and lambda_rev,12
could have failed signs. It is precisely a signed-terminal certificate,
with full PSD by(8). Apply Section3, with reversed steps(v,u), to turn
it into an ordinary certificate. In fact Section3 automatically makes
lambda_rev,12 positive, so only the single entry02 can require repair.
If it does, (4) gives the replacement explicitly.

This proves the forward implication in(A). Applying the same result
to the reversed word proves the backward implication. This does not
claim that the unmodified inverse map preserves every ordinary array:
a negative incoming-terminal output can occur and is repaired here.

## 6. Exact extraction at a saturated cut

First compress zero steps as in Section2. Retain a strictly positive
bridge1+r, positive entries in the two children, and allow either child
to be empty. Let their capacities be A,D, with r(A+D+r)=AD,
T=A+D+r, kappa=(T/D)^2. The prefix query set P ends at the bridge query
L; the tail query set B begins just after it and ends at z.

Suppose a full ordinary parent certificate is supplied. An affine Huber
trajectory with constant gradients1 has V=Q=0. A second legal scalar
function has derivative clip(x,[-eta,1]), eta=r/D=A/T, and starts at A.
The prefix gradients are1; the bridge takes x_L=1 to-r; the tail
gradients are-eta and end at x_z=-eta. Integration from0 defines a
convex differentiable1-smooth function with minimizer0. At every query
f_i=x_i g_i-g_i^2/2. Telescoping gives V=A^2-T^2 eta^2=0.

For i in P and j in B, let R_i^a and R_j^b be their remaining child-step
sums, so R_L^a=R_z^b=0. Direct substitution gives every cross residual:

 Q_ij=-(1+eta)eta R_j^b,
 Q_ji=-(1+eta)R_i^a.                                 (11)

Same-block residuals are zero. Since lambda>=0 and M>=0, equality of V
forces complementarity. Thus the only possible cross edges are i->z
for i in P and j->L for j in B. The two equality gradient vectors span
the block indicators; hence both indicators are in the full PSD kernel.
Writing nu_i=lambda_i,z and mu_j=lambda_j,L, the row i<L of M e_B is
-D nu_i/2, so nu_i=0. The row j<z of M e_P is
(r mu_j-nu_L b_j)/2; its row z is(r mu_z-nu_L)/2.
Therefore mu_j=(nu_L/r)b_j and mu_z=nu_L/r. Summing the prefix flow
now yields D nu_L/r-nu_L=2(A+r). Since r<D, this gives

 lambda_L,z=r(kappa-1),
 lambda_j,L=(kappa-1)b_j (j<z in B),
 lambda_z,L=kappa-1,                                  (12)

with all other cross entries zero. The identity
(kappa-1)(D-r)=2(A+r) follows by rT=AD and verifies(12).

The accepted full join identity can now be applied as an algebraic
identity, without assuming that the children are primitive. Subtracting
the forced cross terms(12) gives the block-diagonal remainder

 M_parent=M_a+kappa M_b,
 M_a=V_a+sum_(i,j in P)lambda_ij Q_ij,
 M_b=V_b+sum_(i,j in B)lambda_hat_ij Q_ij,
 lambda_hat_ij=lambda_ij/kappa
                 -(1-1/kappa)b_i*1_(i<z,j=z).         (13)

Each block cancels its own function values. The formal gradient sets
are disjoint, so each block is PSD. The prefix coefficients are ordinary.
The tail coefficients are nonnegative except possibly those entering z.
One cannot discard that possible sign defect in the supplied array.
When each child has at most two positive steps, Section3 repairs the
right child and leaves ordinary full certificates for both children.
Section5 reverses them. The full saturated join from the accepted fact,
with capacities interchanged, constructs a nonnegative full certificate
for (rev(b),1+r,rev(a)). Section2 restores every original zero-step
gradient. This proves(B), and hence the reversal part of(C).

## 7. The saturated-middle class cannot reach the threshold

A singleton full primitive certificate has step<=sqrt(2), by(1), and
capacity<=1+sqrt(2)<483/200. We give a short independent two-step
capacity bound, sufficient for the four-step corollary.

For any full primitive word of total capacity C, the legal clipped
quadratic at a selected step h_j>1, with prefix sum P and suffix sum R,
is obtained from derivative clip(x,[-(h_j-1)/(R+1),1]) starting at P+1.
Its prefix gradient is1, its tail gradient is the negative threshold,
and its terminal point equals that gradient. Since all its values
satisfy f_i=x_i g_i-g_i^2/2, its energy is

 (P+1)^2-C^2 (h_j-1)^2/(R+1)^2.

Nonnegativity gives C(h_j-1)_+<=(P+1)(R+1). For a two-step word, both
endpoint steps are therefore bounded by2C/(C+1), including the case
h_j<=1. The actual unit quadratic, started at1, also gives

 C |(h_0-1)(h_1-1)|<=1.                               (14)

Assume a two-step certificate had C>=201/50. Set alpha=h_0-1,
beta=h_1-1 and rC=(C-1)/(C+1). Then
alpha+beta=C-3 and alpha,beta<=rC. Their lower bound
lC=C-3-rC=C-4+2/(C+1) is positive. If lC>rC the endpoint bounds are
already inconsistent. Otherwise alpha beta>=rC*lC, since both factors
lie in[lC,rC] with fixed sum. The functions C,rC,lC are positive and
strictly increasing for C>=201/50. At that endpoint,

 C*rC*lC=159373101/157502500>1,

contradicting(14). Thus every two-step primitive certificate has
capacity strictly below201/50. This also covers its zero-step limits
by compression or the same witnesses.

At either middle cut in a four-step word, one child has at most one
physical step and the other at most two. Section6 extracts ordinary
certificates for those children. Saturation gives

 T=Omega(A,D),
 Omega(A,D)=(A+D+sqrt((A+D)^2+4AD))/2.

This function is increasing in both positive arguments, directly from
the displayed expression. Consequently

 T<Omega(483/200,201/50)<77/10<193/25.

For the strict penultimate inequality, at t=77/10 the increasing-root
polynomial t^2-(483/200+201/50)t-(483/200)(201/50) equals161/5000>0,
and t is above the nonnegative root's vertex. This proves(C).
