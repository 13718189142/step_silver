## 4. An exact value-independent three-query cycle identity

We derive the needed identity for general local data. Suppose three
consecutive gradients are v, v'=v+d, v''=v'+e, and the two steps
are a,b. Their positions differ by -a*v and -b*v'. Put

 d=-p*a*alpha*u,  e=-q*b*beta*v_dir,
 alpha=<u,v>, beta=<v_dir,v'>,
 ||u||=||v_dir||=1, c=<u,v_dir>.

Here p,q are the local dampings and v_dir denotes a direction,
distinct from the starting gradient v. The original cycle using
the first-to-third row and the two reverse adjacent rows is

 C=Q_(first,third)+Q_(third,second)+Q_(second,first).

All function values cancel. Its linear terms are

 a<d,v>+b<d+e,v'>,

and its half-squared-norm terms sum to

 ||d||^2+||e||^2+<d,e>.

Regrouping gives

 C=[a<d,v>+||d||^2]+[b<e,v'>+||e||^2]
                                      +b<d,v'>+<d,e>
  =p*a*b*alpha*[(p*a-1)*alpha+q*c*beta]
       -p*(1-p)*a^2*alpha^2-q*(1-q)*b^2*beta^2.      (19)

This is a literal sum of original Q rows. It assumes neither the
midpoint values nor any optimum. It is unchanged by translating all
positions or by their initial constant component. In particular it
does not assign a fictitious smooth convex function to the recurrence.
