# Terminal sum-guard equality is equivalent to intrinsic primitivity

Status: complete UNVERIFIED candidate. No additional fact_submit is allocated.
The accepted predecessors are1252b8823bb5a290 and b98bb807d514e80d. The
known primitive-energy functional is already used in max4's shared research;

No unverified terminal minimax or guard candidate is a proof premise.

## 1. Definitions and the equivalence

Fix k>=0 and a finite nonnegative prefix h_pre=(h_0,...,h_(k-1)). Put
S=sum h_pre and define

    t=(-1+sqrt(8S+9))/4,
    b_S=1+t,
    r=1/(2t+1),
    q_S=r^2=1/[1+2(S+b_S)].                            (1)

Thus t(2t+1)=S+1, t=r(S+1), and 1-r=2r^2(S+1).
Let rho_last=inf_(b>=0) W_(k+1)(h_pre,b), in the original project
normalization. It is always at least q_S.

For a smoothness-one trajectory of the prefix, with arbitrary initial
point and any minimizer, define the translation-invariant intrinsic energy

    V_prim(h_pre)=2sum_(i<k) h_i(f_i-f_k)
       +sum_(i<k)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
       -S(S+1)||g_k||^2.                              (2)

Here f_i are function values, g_i are gradients, and the GD recursion is
x_(i+1)=x_i-h_i g_i. In arbitrary smoothness L, use f_i/L and g_i=grad f(x_i)/L.
The value in(2) is unchanged by a common additive shift of all function values.

The following four statements are equivalent:

(A) rho_last(h_pre)=q_S.

(B) W_(k+1)(h_pre,b_S)=q_S.

(C) V_prim(h_pre)>=0 on EVERY finite-dimensional legal convex 1-smooth
    trajectory with a minimizer.

(D) There are nonnegative coefficients eta_ij on distinct finite-prefix
    pairs i,j in {0,...,k} such that

      V_prim(h_pre)+sum_(i!=j)eta_ij Q_ij                (3)

    is PSD on the FULL formal space of (g_0,...,g_k), after substituting
    the prefix GD positions. All value coefficients cancel. Equivalently,
    the finite-node flow of eta is

      out_i-in_i=-2h_i,       i<k,
      out_k-in_k=2S.

When (A) holds, b_S is the unique optimal final step. Leading, interior,
trailing and all-zero prefixes are included in the equivalence.

## 2. The universal lower guard and its equality location

Accepted fact b98bb807d514e80d gives, for every b>=0,

    W_(k+1)(h_pre,b)
       >=max{1/[1+2(S+b)], (b-1)_+^2/(S+1)^2}.         (4)

The first term decreases strictly; the second increases strictly for b>1.
They meet at b=b_S and value q_S, by(1). Thus rho_last>=q_S, and (B)
implies (A) with the asserted unique step.

To justify (A) implies (B) without using the unverified minimax result,
take a sequence b_j with W(h_pre,b_j)->q_S. The continuous lower envelope
in(4) has unique minimizer b_S and tends to infinity as b tends to infinity,
so b_j->b_S. The function W(h_pre,b) is lower semicontinuous: for every
fixed actual prefix instance its last objective is a continuous function
of b, and W is the supremum of these functions in the same normalization.
Therefore W(h_pre,b_S)<=liminf_j W(h_pre,b_j)=q_S. The reverse follows
from(4), proving (B). This argument needs no prior existence theorem for
an optimal final step.

## 3. Sufficiency of the intrinsic energy: an explicit identity

Normalize the minimizer to zero. Append b_S, write n=k+1, and put

    V=r[x_k-(S+2)g_k].

The following algebraic identity holds on every formal trajectory:

    q_S||x_0||^2-2f_n
      +2q_S sum_(i<k)h_i Q_(i,star)
      +2q_S(S+2) Q_(k,star)
      +2r Q_(n,star)+2(1-r)Q_(n,k)
          =||g_n-V||^2+q_S V_prim(h_pre).             (5)

One derivation is to apply the terminal-square expansion in accepted
fact1252b8823bb5a290 with alpha_star=r, alpha_k=1-r and every other
terminal weight zero. It gives V=x_n-(1-r)(x_k-g_k), which equals the
displayed V because b_S-1+r=t+r=r(S+2). After expanding its remaining
prefix terms and using

    ||x_0||^2-||x_k||^2
       =sum_(i<k)[2h_i<x_i,g_i>-h_i^2||g_i||^2],

the star terms cancel every <x_i,g_i>, and the remaining expression is
exactly q_S times(2). This checks (5) without any assumption that its
prefix remainder is PSD on unrestricted data.

If (C) holds, the RHS of(5) is nonnegative on every actual trajectory.
All Q terms on the LHS are nonpositive with nonnegative coefficients.
Thus 2f_n<=q_S||x_0||^2 and W(h_pre,b_S)<=q_S. Equation(4) gives equality.
This proves (C)=>(B) directly, without needing a finite multiplier
representation in advance. The implication (D)=>(C) follows from(3)
because all finite Q are nonpositive on actual trajectories.

## 4. Necessity: two explicit equality instances force the terminal support

First assume all k prefix steps are positive, allowing k=0, and assume (B).
By accepted fact1252b8823bb5a290, select a tight full nonnegative-flow PSD
certificate at level q_S with no incoming terminal multipliers. Write its
outgoing probabilities as alpha_j=lambda_(n,j)/2 for j in {star,0,...,k}.

Consider the scalar Huber function with derivative clamp(x,[-q_S,q_S]),
started at x_0=1. If P_i=sum_(u<i)h_u, its prefix points and gradients are

    x_i=1-q_S P_i,    g_i=q_S.

The final point is 1-q_S(S+b_S), still strictly in its positive linear
region, and its normalized error is q_S. Moreover, at EVERY prefix sample,

    delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2
            =q_S[1-q_S(P_i+1)]>0.                    (6)

The inequality uses P_i<=S and q_S(S+1)=r t=(1-r)/2<1.
Every star-outgoing prefix residual Q_(star,i)=-delta_i is strictly negative.
Complementarity in the accepted full certificate therefore forces

    lambda_(star,i)=0,       0<=i<=k.                  (7)

Next use the scalar capped quadratic with derivative min(c,x), c=1/(S+1),
also started at1. Its whole prefix remains on the right affine branch:

    x_i=1-cP_i,   g_i=c,   x_k=c.

The appended step sends it to x_n=-ct and gradient g_n=-ct. Its normalized
error is c^2t^2=q_S. For i<k the outgoing terminal residual is exactly

    Q_(n,i)=-b_S c^2(S-P_i)<0,                        (8)

while Q_(n,k)=Q_(n,star)=0. To check(8), start from Q_(n,k)=0, then increase
the old point from c to x_i=c+c(S-P_i). Its old value increases by c times
that displacement while its pairing with g_n changes by -ct times that
displacement. The resulting difference is -(1+t)c^2(S-P_i).
Strict positivity of the prefix steps makes S-P_i>0 for every i<k.
Complementarity now forces alpha_i=0 for every i<k.

Thus only alpha_star and alpha_k=1-alpha_star remain. Evaluate the terminal
square on the Huber equality trajectory. The accepted fact gives g_n=V_alpha,
so

    q_S=alpha_star(1-Sq_S)-(t+alpha_star)q_S.

Equivalently,

    alpha_star[1-(S+1)q_S]=(t+1)q_S.

The bracket is positive, and(1) gives its unique solution

    alpha_star=r,   alpha_k=1-r.                      (9)

All these restrictions hold for every selected optimal certificate of
the stated no-incoming-terminal type. They were deduced from actual scalar
equality functions, not from a rank guess or an assumed sparse prefix.

## 5. Full PSD forces the remaining optimum multipliers

The accepted terminal remainder is

    R_alpha=q_S||x_0||^2-2(1-r)delta_k
             -r^2||x_k-(S+2)g_k||^2
             +sum_(i!=j in {star,0,...,k})lambda_ij Q_ij.

It is PSD on the FULL original prefix formal space. By(7), no multiplier
points out of star. Let mu_i=lambda_(i,star). Its x_0-square coefficient
is q_S-r^2=0. A PSD matrix with a zero diagonal entry has a zero row:
on any two-coordinate restriction, a nonzero off-diagonal entry would make
the quadratic negative for one sufficiently small signed second coordinate.
Therefore every x_0--gradient cross coefficient in R_alpha vanishes.

Finite-pair Q_ij have no x_0 cross term after the GD substitution, because
x_j-x_i does not contain x_0. The star terms contribute -mu_i<x_0,g_i>.
Expansion of the negative square then forces

    mu_i=2q_S h_i,       i<k,
    mu_k=2q_S(S+2).                                  (10)

No interpolation multiplier on a finite queried-prefix pair is discarded.
Substitute(10), divide by q_S, and use the telescoping calculation in(5).
The result is exactly

    R_alpha/q_S=V_prim(h_pre)
           +sum_(i!=j in {0,...,k})(lambda_ij/q_S)Q_ij.

This is PSD on the full gradient formal space, and each eta_ij=lambda_ij/q_S
is nonnegative. All value coefficients cancel because they already cancel
in R_alpha. Their coefficients give precisely the finite flows in(D).
Thus (B)=>(D), completing the equivalence for positive prefixes.

## 6. Zero-step lifting and the empty prefix

Delete zero prefix steps and select as representatives each original
positive-step query index, with original index k representing the last
prefix query. Actual coincident queries have identical gradients and values:
adding their two Q inequalities gives ||g_i-g_j||^2<=0, and their values
then agree. The original and compressed schedules therefore have identical
S, b_S, q_S, W and rho_last.

The energy(2) is also unchanged by this compression. Terms at zero steps
have coefficient zero, while the final gradient is the selected original
g_k. Hence condition(C) is equivalent on the two schedules.

If (B) holds, apply the positive-prefix proof to the compressed schedule
and lift its finite eta multipliers by the representative-index map.
All other eta vanish. Every mapped finite Q is the corresponding compressed
Q as an identity in independent original formal vectors, because the
omitted h coefficients are zero. The lifted quadratic(3) is the congruence
of the compressed PSD matrix with the coordinate-selection map. Unused
gradient columns are zero; no equality between duplicated formal gradients
is needed for PSD. This proves(D) in the full ORIGINAL formal space.

For an empty or all-zero prefix, S=0, V_prim=0, b_S=3/2, q_S=1/4, and
eta=0 is a certificate in(D). The sufficiency identity(5) proves the upper
value, and(4) proves the lower value and uniqueness. These cases therefore
require no strictness or Slater assumption.

## 7. What this does and does not close

The result characterizes exactly WHEN the sum-based terminal lower guard
is sharp: the complete prefix must satisfy the full intrinsic primitive
energy inequality, equivalently its full finite-pair PSD certificate.
It supplies a necessity statement for the single terminal ConPD bridge
psi(S,0), not merely the familiar sufficiency of composing a primitive
prefix with an empty terminal block.

The primitive energy itself is not new; it is already present in shared
findings504a69a1e000d6aa and2973c2322cdee545. Their numerical probes and
unverified composition statements are not premises here. The accepted
terminal reduction is what forces(7)-(10) and the complete finite certificate.

An arbitrary globally optimal longer schedule need not attain this particular
sum-only guard. A dominant terminal block can have several steps, and its
earlier prefix is not thereby primitive. Nor does this equivalence bound
the largest sum of a primitive prefix of a fixed length. Those are distinct
unresolved bridges to the original asymptotic target.
