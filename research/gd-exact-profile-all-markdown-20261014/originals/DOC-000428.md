## 9. Why an affine transport also loses exact Huber contact

This additional identity diagnoses the repair, without being a premise
of Sections 5-8. Suppose the reference child really has Huber contact
and mu_k>0. A Huber-tight full parent must also have contact. Indeed
take Hub(x)=x^2/2 for |x|<=1 and Hub(x)=|x|-1/2 otherwise, and its
actual trajectory from x_0=D. Every x_i=D-P_i>=S+1>1, so all finite
g_i=1 and f_i=x_i-1/2. Its terminal numerator 2f_n is D and its initial
square is D^2;
every finite Q_ij and every Q_(i,star) is zero, and
Q_(star,i)=1-x_i<0. Nonnegative multipliers and PSD at q=1/D force
optimum-outgoing multipliers to vanish and M(D,1,...,1)=0. A PSD
quadratic with zero value at a vector annihilates that vector: apply
it to that vector plus an arbitrary scalar multiple of another vector.

Writing P_i for original chronological mass, expansion of (3) gives
the finite-coordinate contact equations

 (2S-P_i)*nu_i=2h_i+sum_(v finite)lambda_iv*(P_i-P_v).  (27)

More explicitly, if e is the all-ones gradient vector, differentiating
(3) at equal gradients gives
2(Ce)_i=2h_i+(1+P_i)*nu_i+sum_v lambda_iv*(P_i-P_v).
In the position terms, full finite value flow makes the sum of the
position-derivative contributions 2h_i; the remaining derivative based
at i is its displayed outgoing drift. The norm terms contribute nu_i.
The gradient part of M(D,e)=0 is 2Ce=D*nu, which yields (27).

For the right finite copy (7), the child version of (27) subtracts out.
Let backward coefficients more generally have the form
lambda_(R_i,L_l)=mu_i*(b_l+c_l*p_i), with every actual coefficient
nonnegative. Permit arbitrary nonnegative forward coefficients, and
require their terminal column to be zero. Put
B0=sum b_l, B1=sum c_l, M0=sum p_l*b_l, M1=sum p_l*c_l.
Right flow gives F_i/mu_i=B0+B1*p_i-s at every mu_i>0.
The right equations (27) require

 (t+p_i)*(B0+B1*p_i)-(M0+M1*p_i)
       =theta*t-s*(2A-p_i).                            (28)

If there are at least three distinct positions with mu_i>0, equality
of the two degree-at-most-two polynomials at those positions forces
B1=0. At the terminal, zero forward inflow and mu_k>0 then give
B0=s. Thus every F_i=0. At mu_i=0, flow and the backward formula
also force F_i=0. Nonnegativity makes EVERY forward coefficient zero.

Consequently an affine backward source profile cannot retain genuine
forward mass while simultaneously preserving this exact finite copy,
a single right attachment scale, and Huber contact. This is scoped to
the stated law; it does not follow from the approximate numerical fits.

For the explicit repair (9), the backward first moment is
kappa*t*(A-p_i). At the endpoints p_i=0,A the intended right equation
is matched. At an interior position its drift exceeds the required drift
by exactly kappa*mu_i*p_i*(A-p_i). This provides the concrete interior
contact defect. The result of Sections 5-8 is stronger for the selected
theta,kappa: raising q within a small whole-word budget cannot repair
the full PSD failure, even if the contact requirement is abandoned.
