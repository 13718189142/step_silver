# A finite exact projection lift of arbitrary primitive defects

Status: complete candidate proof, NOT verifier-accepted. No solver or
fact_submit. The argument below is elementary and self-contained. It does
not use another unverified finding as a premise. It gives an alternative
finite-data implementation of the projection reduction in1afc93b8e38d3cfc.

## 1. Input, notation and conclusion

Let h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)) be any finite nonnegative schedule. Put
s=sum h_i and D=1+s. Take any actual convex differentiable1-smooth
GD data(x_i,g_i,f_i), 0<=i<=n, with a minimizer normalized to0 of value0.
Write

  Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
  I_i=<g_i,x_i>-f_i-||g_i||^2/2>=0,
  delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2>=0,
  J_i=sum_(t<i)h_t I_t,
  V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)
       +sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-sD||g_n||^2.

Assume V_h=-v_def<0. We construct explicit finite data in dimension d+3,
on a fixed projection function associated with a compact polytope K
containing0, such that its primitive energy is still negative and all its
incoming optimum slacks are exactly0.

The constructed K has at most n+2 listed vertices:0 and the n+1 sampled
gradients. After an optional orthogonal compression, its ambient dimension
is at most n+1. Every quantity is obtained by finite arithmetic, square roots,
a fixed two-dimensional recurrence, and finite maxima. No limiting
interpolant or numerical PEP evaluation is required.

As a consequence, primitive validity of h is equivalent to

  ||X_n+s P_K(X_n)||<=||X_0||                             (1)

for every finite-dimensional compact polytope K containing0 and every
GD run X_(i+1)=X_i-h_i P_K(X_i). It suffices in testing failure to use
polytopes with at most n+2 listed vertices.

## 2. One slack coordinate, with its interpolation errors retained exactly

For Lambda>0 define auxiliary finite data

  X_i^a=(x_i,-Lambda-J_i/Lambda),
  G_i^a=(g_i,I_i/Lambda),
  F_i^a=<G_i^a,X_i^a>-||G_i^a||^2/2
       =f_i-[I_i J_i+I_i^2/2]/Lambda^2.                  (2)

The GD equations hold exactly because J_(i+1)-J_i=h_i I_i.
Every incoming optimum residual of this auxiliary array is0 by its
assigned value. We do NOT assume its other interpolation constraints
are legal. They are exactly

  Q_ij^a=Q_ij+A_ij/Lambda^2,
  A_ij=-(I_i-I_j)(J_j+I_j),                              (3)
  Q_(star,i)^a=-delta_i+B_i/Lambda^2,
  B_i=I_i(J_i+I_i)>=0.                                  (4)

To verify(3), when incoming slacks are0 the finite residual equals
<G_i^a-G_j^a,X_j^a-G_j^a>. Its original-coordinate part is
Q_ij+I_i-I_j. The added-coordinate part is
-(I_i-I_j)-(I_i-I_j)(J_j+I_j)/Lambda^2.
The constant terms cancel, leaving(3). Equation(4) follows directly
from(2).

If the sum of h between indices i and j is0, every step between them
is zero. The original queries, gradients and values are identical;
also I_i=I_j and J_i=J_j. Thus A_ij=0 for those duplicate queries.

## 3. One explicit strict projection trajectory repairs every other edge

Let e=(1,0) in R^2 and take the closed Euclidean ball

  K_ball={z:||z-e||<=2}.

It contains0 in its interior. Define the fixed projection function

  F_ball(x)=max_(g in K_ball){<x,g>-||g||^2/2}.

Run the SAME schedule h from x_0^ball=e+(0,L), where L=6s+4.
Its gradients always have norm at most3. Hence

  ||x_i^ball-e||>=L-3s=3s+4>2.

Every query stays strictly outside the ball, so, setting
z_i=x_i^ball-e, u_i=z_i/||z_i||,

  g_i^ball=e+2u_i,
  y_i^ball=x_i^ball-g_i^ball=(||z_i||-2)u_i.              (5)

This is an exact finite recurrence of arithmetic and square roots.
All incoming optimum slacks are0. The outgoing optimum slack is

  delta_i^ball=<g_i^ball,y_i^ball>
       =(||z_i||-2)(2+<e,u_i>)>0.                       (6)

Different positive cumulative times have different unit directions u_i.
Here is an explicit proof, including arbitrary large finite steps.
The recursion for z_i is

  z_(i+1)=mu_i z_i-h_i e,
  mu_i=1-2h_i/||z_i||>0,

because h_i<=s and ||z_i||>=3s+4>2s.
The second coordinate z_(i,2) stays strictly positive.
For the ratio of first to second coordinates,

  z_(i+1,1)/z_(i+1,2)
    =z_(i,1)/z_(i,2)-h_i/(mu_i z_(i,2)).

It decreases strictly at every positive step and is unchanged at a
zero step. Thus u_i!=u_j whenever the cumulative step between i and j
is positive.

The finite projection residuals are therefore

  Q_ij^ball=2(||z_j||-2)(<u_i,u_j>-1)<0                 (7)

for every such pair, and they are0 at zero-step duplicates. This
checks BOTH ordered directions of every pair.

For completeness, projection functions are legal without invoking a
new external theorem. The nearest-point normal inequalities imply
<P_K(x)-P_K(x'),x-x'>>=||P_K(x)-P_K(x')||^2, hence
P_K is1-Lipschitz. The maximizing expression for F_K implies

  0<=F_K(x+d)-F_K(x)-<P_K(x),d>
     <=<P_K(x+d)-P_K(x),d><=||d||^2.

Thus grad F_K=P_K, so F_K is convex differentiable1-smooth. Since0
belongs to K, its minimum is F_K(0)=0. At every query
F_K(x)=<P_K(x),x>-||P_K(x)||^2/2, giving the asserted zero incoming
slack and the formula for finite Q.

## 4. A finite repair coefficient gives exact full interpolation

Let the finite coefficient K_rep be

  max{0,
      max_(0<=i<=n) B_i/delta_i^ball,
      max_(i!=j, positive cumulative step)
                  (A_ij)_+/(-Q_ij^ball)}.               (8)

Every denominator is strictly positive by(6),(7). The last maximum is
omitted if its index set is empty. All quantities are explicitly
determined by the original finite data and the fixed ball run.

For any Lambda>0 combine the auxiliary array(2) and the ball array in
orthogonal coordinates with ball-coordinate scale sqrt(K_rep)/Lambda:

  X_i=(X_i^a,(sqrt(K_rep)/Lambda)x_i^ball),
  G_i=(G_i^a,(sqrt(K_rep)/Lambda)g_i^ball),
  F_i=F_i^a+(K_rep/Lambda^2)f_i^ball.                     (9)

When K_rep=0 these extra coordinates are simply zero. Every GD
equation remains exact, and all incoming optimum residuals remain0.
For distinct finite samples,

  Q_ij_new=Q_ij+[A_ij+K_rep Q_ij^ball]/Lambda^2<=0,

using(8); the duplicate-query case follows from A_ij=Q_ij^ball=0.
For outgoing optimum residuals,

  Q_(star,i)_new=-delta_i+
              [B_i-K_rep delta_i^ball]/Lambda^2<=0.

These checks account for EVERY original-index interpolation constraint.
No positivity has been assumed merely from a small-parameter argument.

Define the compact polytope

  K=conv{0,G_0,...,G_n}.

Because all incoming optimum residuals are0, the checked finite
inequalities state

  <X_j-G_j,G_i-G_j><=0,

and the outgoing optimum inequalities give the same condition at vertex0.
Convex combinations give the condition for every point of K. It is
precisely the nearest-point normal condition, so P_K(X_j)=G_j.
Consequently the fixed function

  F_K(X)=max_(G in K){<X,G>-||G||^2/2}

interpolates all the constructed points, gradients and values. It is
a fixed globally legal function, with no adaptive resetting.

## 5. Exact primitive energy and a finite negative choice

For arbitrary original legal data, telescoping the GD squared distances
and using the definitions of I_i gives

  V_h=||x_0||^2-||x_n+s g_n||^2+2(sI_n-J_n).             (10)

For the auxiliary array all incoming slacks are0, so the same algebra,
which does not require prior legality, gives

  V_aux=||X_0^a||^2-||X_n^a+s G_n^a||^2
       =V_h-(sI_n-J_n)^2/Lambda^2.                      (11)

Indeed X_0^a has added coordinate-Lambda, while X_n^a+sG_n^a has added
coordinate-Lambda+(sI_n-J_n)/Lambda. Expanding these squares and then
using(10) proves(11).

Let V_ball be the intrinsic primitive energy of the fixed ball
trajectory, calculated by(1)'s energy definition, or equivalently by

  V_ball=||x_0^ball||^2
                  -||x_n^ball+s g_n^ball||^2.

Energy is additive in orthogonal coordinates. Therefore the fully legal
data(9) have EXACTLY

  V_new=V_h+
        [K_rep V_ball-(sI_n-J_n)^2]/Lambda^2.             (12)

Choose, for example,

  Lambda^2=1+2K_rep max(V_ball,0)/v_def.                 (13)

This is positive and finite. If K_rep max(V_ball,0)=0, (12) is at most
-v_def. Otherwise the positive contribution in(12) is strictly below
v_def/2. In all cases

  V_new<-v_def/2<0.

The output is thus a concrete negative witness on a compact projection
function, produced by finite exact operations.

The final dimension can be reduced to at most n+1. Let W be the linear
span of the n+1 output gradients, hence of K. Project every query onto W.
The GD equations, gradient pairings and function values are unchanged;
projection onto K ignores the component orthogonal to W. The orthogonal
component of every query is the same, so it also cancels from the two
squared norms in the energy identity. This preserves V_new exactly.

## 6. The lossless primitive operator criterion and its limitation

For a projection function, every I_i is0, so(10) is exactly

  V_h=||X_0||^2-||X_n+s P_K(X_n)||^2.

If h is primitive, it is nonnegative on every such trajectory, proving(1).
Conversely, if h is not primitive, sections2-5 produce a compact polytope
with at most n+2 listed vertices and a negative energy, so(1) fails.
This proves the asserted equivalence with the original intrinsic
primitive inequality.

All old function values remain encoded: they determine I_i and hence
the additional coordinates, the repair coefficient and the final
polytope. The reduction is not a permission to delete queried-history
constraints or optimum terms from an arbitrary full certificate.

The initial norm is changed by the added -Lambda coordinate and the
ball coordinates. The construction does NOT preserve normalized
objective error and does not by itself settle the asymptotic
Zhang-Jiang target. It supplies an exact finite projection realization
for intrinsic primitive defects and a precise geometry in which to
pursue that target's remaining primitive-capacity questions.
