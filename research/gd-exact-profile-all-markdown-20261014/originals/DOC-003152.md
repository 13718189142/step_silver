# General removal of terminal credit with an explicit physical contraction

Status: complete candidate proof, NOT verifier-accepted. This result changes
the physical steps and does not prove same-schedule credit re-selection.
It uses all actual interpolation coefficients and a full PSD identity.
No solver or verifier call, and no external theorem used as a premise.

## 1. Statement, including zero steps

Fix n>=0 and a nonnegative physical schedule h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)). Write
s=sum h_i, C=1+s. On independent formal gradients g_0,...,g_n and values
f_0,...,f_n, substitute x_i=x_0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t and put

 Q_ij^h=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
 V_h=2sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                                            -s(s+1)||g_n||^2,
 J_h=sum_(i<n)h_i Q_(i,n)^h,
 E_theta=V_h-theta J_h.

Suppose 0<=theta<2 and actual nonnegative multipliers Lambda_ij for all
distinct finite pairs give an exact certificate

              E_theta+sum_(i!=j)Lambda_ij Q_ij^h=M,       (1)

where all value coefficients vanish and M is PSD on the FULL independent
gradient space. This includes the project range 0<=theta<1.

Define positive parameters and the contracted schedule by

 a=1-theta/2,
 kappa=theta C/(2-theta),
 t=1/(1+kappa)=(2-theta)/(2+theta s),
 c=2/(2+theta s),
 h'_i=t h_i,     Lambda'_ij=c Lambda_ij.                  (2)

Then Lambda' is an ordinary nonnegative certificate for V_(h') with the
complete PSD remainder

 V_(h')+sum Lambda'_ij Q_ij^(h')
       =(t^2/a)[M+kappa(S_Lambda+a Var_h)],               (3)

where

 S_Lambda=(1/2)sum_(i!=j)Lambda_ij||g_i-g_j||^2,
 Var_h=sum_(i<n)h_i||g_i||^2+||g_n||^2
                     -(1/C)||sum_(i<n)h_i g_i+g_n||^2.   (4)

Both added forms are PSD, with no missing gradient directions. Every
actual output multiplier is nonnegative because c>0. Zero steps remain
zero, and the proof works on the original formal space without identifying
duplicate gradients or invoking compression. Empty and all-zero schedules
are included.

The actual physical capacity after this same-length conversion is exactly

             C'=1+sum h'_i=2C/[2+theta(C-1)].             (5)

No prefix or bridge is appended. The loss in (5) must be charged before
claiming an improving ordinary seed.

## 2. Two identities before changing the schedule

Put H_g=sum_(i<n)h_i g_i and define the rank-at-most-two quadratic

                    D_h=||H_g+g_n||^2-C^2||g_n||^2.

Direct expansion gives

                    V_h-2J_h=D_h,                       (6)
                    E_theta=a V_h+(1-a)D_h.

Here is an explicit verification of (6). The value terms cancel. Write
x_n-x_i=-sum_(j=i)^(n-1)h_j g_j in J_h. Expanding 2J_h and canceling the
diagonal norm terms in V_h leaves

 2sum_(i<=j<n)h_i h_j<g_i,g_j>-sum_(i<n)h_i^2||g_i||^2
     +2<H_g,g_n>-[s(s+1)+s]||g_n||^2.

The first two terms equal ||H_g||^2, and s(s+1)+s=C^2-1. The displayed
expression is therefore D_h, proving (6).

Also define the formal scalar value combination

                    F_h=sum_(i<n)h_i(f_i-f_n).

The value part of E_theta is 2a F_h. Exact cancellation in (1) forces

              sum Lambda_ij(f_i-f_j)=-2a F_h.            (7)

This checks the complete finite-node flow, not only its total sum.

## 3. Complete coefficient identity under contraction

All query differences scale by t, so, as an identity in independent
values and gradients,

 Q_ij^(h')=t Q_ij^h
                +(1-t)[f_i-f_j+||g_i-g_j||^2/2].         (8)

Let K_h=sum_(i<n)h_i||g_i||^2-s||g_n||^2. The value and gradient terms of
V_(h') give exactly

                 V_(h')=t^2 V_h+t(1-t)(2F_h+K_h).       (9)

Using Lambda'=c Lambda, equations (1), (6), (7), and (8) now yield

 V_(h')+sum Lambda'_ij Q_ij^(h')
  =V_(h')+ct[M-aV_h-(1-a)D_h]
                         +c(1-t)[-2aF_h+S_Lambda].      (10)

The parameter identities t=ac, ct=t^2/a, 1-t=kappa t and
1-a=a kappa/C are exact consequences of (2). Substitute (9) into (10):
the V_h coefficients cancel since cta=t^2, and the F_h coefficients cancel
since ca=t. The remaining expression is

 ct M+t(1-t)K_h-ct(1-a)D_h+c(1-t)S_Lambda
   =ct[M+kappa S_Lambda+a kappa(K_h-D_h/C)].              (11)

Finally K_h-D_h/C is precisely Var_h, because C-s=1. Equations (11) and
ct=t^2/a prove the entire formal identity (3).

## 4. Positivity on the full formal space

S_Lambda is a nonnegative sum of squared gradient differences. For Var_h,
assign weights w_i=h_i for i<n and w_n=1, whose sum is C>0. Expanding
the squared differences gives

 Var_h=(1/C)sum_(0<=i<j<=n)w_i w_j||g_i-g_j||^2>=0.     (12)

This remains valid when any h_i vanishes. Since a,t>0 and kappa>=0, (3)
and the assumed full PSD of M prove full PSD of the repaired matrix.
All output interpolation coefficients are positive scalar multiples of
actual nonnegative coefficients, so none of their signs is omitted.
The cancellation of the independent F_h terms in Section 3 explicitly
checks the new value flow. This establishes the statement in Section 1.

For an all-zero schedule, t=a, c=1, Var_h=0, and the input value flow is
zero. Its remainder is M=S_Lambda, so (3) reduces to the same matrix M.
For n=0 every sum of distinct finite pairs is empty and M=0. Thus these
boundary cases satisfy the formula directly.

## 5. Explicit target-relevance cost

Summing the contracted physical steps gives (5). Let K>1 be any desired
capacity threshold for an ordinary seed of this same length. Algebra shows

 C'>K
   iff (2-theta K)C>K(2-theta).                         (13)

Consequently a necessary condition is theta<2/K. When theta<2/K,
condition (13) is equivalent to the explicit input requirement

                    C>K(2-theta)/(2-theta K).            (14)

If theta>=2/K and theta<2, no finite C>=1 can satisfy C'>K: the left side
of (13) is nonpositive and its right side is strictly positive. In
particular, for the previously proposed useful primitive-seed threshold
K=(n+1)^rho, rho=log_2(1+sqrt2), this same-length conversion requires
theta<2/(n+1)^rho and then (14). This is only an exact cost comparison;
the conditional asymptotic amplification argument is not invoked here as
a verified premise, and no seed meeting the threshold has been found.

When theta>0 is fixed and C tends to infinity, C' tends to2/theta. Thus
fixed positive credit cannot yield unbounded ordinary capacity by THIS
conversion. An improving sequence by this mechanism must control theta
at the corresponding inverse-capacity scale. Other conversions with
prefixes or bridges may behave differently and are not excluded.

## 6. A useful corollary about uniform contraction

Given an ordinary nonnegative certificate for h, adding theta h_i to
every incoming-terminal multiplier makes (1) hold with the SAME M for
any 0<=theta<2: those additions cancel -theta J_h. For any prescribed
0<t<=1, choose

                    theta=2(1-t)/(1+t s).

It lies in [0,2), and (2) gives exactly that t. Therefore every uniform
contraction t h of a certified ordinary schedule has the explicit ordinary
certificate (3). This statement concerns certificates on the full formal
space, including zero steps, and does not assert that enlarging steps is
safe or that arbitrary independent contractions preserve the property.

## Remaining issue

This general transformation removes credit by paying an exact physical
sum loss. It neither fixes h nor proves that E_theta and V_h have the same
certificate cone. The same-schedule problem remains open outside the
saturated-cut and weighted-cut repair classes. Any target-level use must
produce a concrete E_theta family surviving (5), an exact ordinary
certificate as above, and the full infinite-horizon benchmark comparison.
