Finite multiplicative phase covers and conditional full-certificate amplification for actual primitive seeds.

All vector spaces and inner products are finite-dimensional real Euclidean ones. Fix L>0. For every nonnegative integer n and every nonnegative schedule v=(v_0,...,v_(n-1)), let W_n(v) be the supremum of
    2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L ||x_0-x_star||^2)
over all convex differentiable f with L-Lipschitz gradient, all minimizers x_star of f, and all x_0!=x_star, where x_(i+1)=x_i-(v_i/L)grad f(x_i) for 0<=i<n. The supremum ranges over every finite dimension. The empty schedule is included. Write R_n(v) for the same supremum without the factor 2, so W_n=2R_n.

For nonnegative real x,y define
    Omega(x,y)=(x+y+sqrt((x+y)^2+4xy))/2,
    G(x,y)=2x+y/2+sqrt(y^2+8xy)/2.
Define the original ALL-split benchmark by C_0=B_0=1 and, for every integer n>=1,
    C_n=max_(0<=k<n) Omega(C_k,C_(n-k-1)),
    B_n=max_(0<=k<n) G(C_k,B_(n-k-1)),
where every split k is an integer. Put U_n^ZJ=1/B_n, lambda=1+sqrt(2), and rho=log_2(lambda). For every positive integer b, let Z_b be the finite positive limit lim_(j->infinity)lambda^(-j)B_(b*2^j-1), whose existence is part of predecessor 04219554e41d9750. All logarithms denoted log are natural logarithms.

Fix an integer r>=1 and an ACTUAL nonnegative seed word h=(h_0,...,h_(r-1)). Put s=sum_(i=0)^(r-1)h_i, A=1+s, and m=r+1. Suppose there is an entire real (r+1)-by-(r+1) matrix Lambda satisfying Lambda_ii=0 and Lambda_ij>=0 for EVERY ordered distinct i,j in {0,...,r}. Let e be the all-ones column of length r+1. Define T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, and T_it=0 for t>i, and set
    L_flow=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(r-1),2s)^T,
    D_prim=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(r-1)(2-h_(r-1)),-s(s+2)),
    M_prim=D_prim+(L_flow T+T^T L_flow^T)/2.
Here diag of a column is the diagonal matrix with that column; a superscript T means transpose. Require the EXACT flow equation L_flow^T e=omega and positive semidefiniteness of M_prim on the ENTIRE R^(r+1). Thus all r(r+1) ordered signs and all r+1 formal gradients are retained, including gradients at zero physical steps, with no coordinate deletion or imposed gradient equality. No symmetry, support, rank, zero-remainder, product-balance, or h_i>=1 condition is imposed. Nonzero and singular positive semidefinite remainders are allowed.

For each integer n>=0 choose any fully expanded maximizing tree of the original B_n recurrence, expanding every primitive C child by the original C recurrence. It has n internal nodes, n primitive empty leaves, and one dominant empty leaf. Replace each primitive empty leaf by the ACTUAL word h, leave the dominant empty leaf EMPTY with mass 1, and keep all internal nodes.
