# Selected C107 theorem package

## Statement

Exact fixed-b=3 benchmark thresholds and conditional full-certificate amplification for five- and six-step seeds.

For a nonnegative integer n and a finite nonnegative schedule h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)), let W_n(h) be the supremum of

    2(f(x_n)-f(x_star))/(L ||x_0-x_star||^2)

over all finite-dimensional Euclidean spaces, all convex differentiable functions f with L-Lipschitz gradient and at least one minimizer x_star, and all x_0!=x_star, under x_(i+1)=x_i-(h_i/L)grad f(x_i). Here L>0 is arbitrary and fixed; the empty schedule is allowed. This is the W=2R normalization, where R is the same supremum without the factor2.

For nonnegative real a,d define

    Omega(a,d)=(a+d+sqrt((a+d)^2+4ad))/2,
    G(a,d)=2a+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8ad)/2.

Retain the ORIGINAL ALL-split benchmark recurrences

    C_0=B_0=1,
    C_n=max_(0<=k<n) Omega(C_k,C_(n-k-1)),
    B_n=max_(0<=k<n) G(C_k,B_(n-k-1)),  integer n>=1,
    U_n^ZJ=1/B_n,
    lambda=1+sqrt(2).

Every split index k is an integer. For every positive integer b let Z_b be the positive finite limit lim_(j->infinity)lambda^(-j)B_(b*2^j-1), established in predecessor04219554e41d9750. Define

    Gamma5_c107=7977607/806000,
    Gamma6_c107=387153999/32240000,

and let Gamma_r_c107 denote Gamma5_c107 for r=5 and Gamma6_c107 for r=6. With the denominator ray FIXED at b=3, the strict comparisons are

    Z_18/Z_3 < Gamma5_c107 < 99/10 < 292666/29275,
    Z_21/Z_3 < Gamma6_c107 < 1201/100 < 90/7.

The following assertions are conditional. Fix r in {5,6}. Suppose h=(h_0,...,h_(r-1)) is an ACTUAL nonnegative seed word with s=sum_(i=0)^(r-1)h_i and capacity A=1+s>Gamma_r_c107. Suppose there is a full real (r+1)-by-(r+1) array Lambda with Lambda_ii=0 and Lambda_ij>=0 for EVERY ordered distinct i,j in {0,...,r}. Let e be the all-ones column of length r+1; let the real matrix T have T_ii=1, T_it=h_t for t<i, and T_it=0 for t>i. Set

    L_flow=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    omega=(-2h_0,...,-2h_(r-1),2s)^T,
    D_prim=diag(h_0(2-h_0),...,h_(r-1)(2-h_(r-1)),-s(s+2)).

Require

    L_flow^T e=omega,
    M_prim=D_prim+(L_flow T+T^T L_flow^T)/2
        positive semidefinite on the entire R^(r+1).

Here diag of a column is the diagonal matrix with that column, and a superscript T on a matrix or column means transpose. Thus r=5 requires all30 ordered multipliers and all6 formal gradients, and r=6 requires all42 multipliers and all7 formal gradients. A zero physical step permits neither deletion nor equality of formal gradient coordinates. No rank, support, symmetry, or zero-remainder restriction is imposed.

For each nonnegative integer n choose ANY fully expanded maximizing tree of the original B_n recurrence, expanding every C child by the original C recurrence. Replace each of its n primitive empty leaves by the actual word h, retain every internal node, and leave its one terminal dominant empty leaf EMPTY with mass1. Recompute each physical bridge as follows. A primitive join, with actual child capacities A_left=1+sum(left) and A_right=1+sum(right), uses the unique positive d_pp solving d_pp(A_left+A_right+d_pp)=A_left*A_right and concatenates (left,1+d_pp,right). A dominant join, with primitive left capacity A_left and actual dominant right mass D_right=1+2sum(right), uses zeta=(1+sqrt(1+8A_left/D_right))/2 and concatenates (left,1+A_left/zeta,right). Write Hsub_n for this actual physical word and Dsub_n=1+2sum Hsub_n for its mass.

For every n>=0 the accepted full substitution identities give

    length(Hsub_n)=(r+1)n,
    W_((r+1)n)(Hsub_n)=1/Dsub_n,
    Dsub_n>=A B_n(1-ell_n)^2,
    ell_n=(1-A^(-1/2))/sqrt(B_n).

The equality is an all-function objective upper certificate with a matching one-dimensional Huber lower witness. For every nonnegative integer j put

    n_j=3*2^j-1,
    N_j=(r+1)n_j=3(r+1)2^j-(r+1),
    q107_j=(2/3)^j,
    epsilon_c107=1-Gamma_r_c107/A in (0,1).

At these TRUE physical horizons,

    lim_(j->infinity) B_(N_j)W_(N_j)(Hsub_(n_j))
        =Z_(3(r+1))/(A Z_3)<1.

For every j with q107_j<1/3 one has the finite bound

    B_(N_j)W_(N_j)(Hsub_(n_j))
        < (Gamma_r_c107/A)/(1-3q107_j).

In particular, for EVERY integer

    j>=ceil(log(6/epsilon_c107)/log(3/2)),

where log is the natural logarithm,

    W_(N_j)(Hsub_(n_j)) < (1-epsilon_c107/2) U_(N_j)^ZJ.

Each of the following separate sufficient hypotheses gives the uniform strict bound W_(N_j)(Hsub_(n_j)) < (1-1/16000)U_(N_j)^ZJ for EVERY integer j>=27:

    r=5 and A>=99/10, with N_j=18*2^j-6;
    r=6 and A>=1201/100, with N_j=21*2^j-7.

This theorem supplies sufficient upper enclosures for this fixed ray and is conditional on an actual full seed. It supplies no such seed, proves no seed nonexistence, and asserts no global phase optimum, no ordering between the TRUE b=1 and b=3 ratios, and no unrestricted all-n/all-nonnegative-word exact-profile theorem. The last two comparisons to292666/29275 and90/7 are numerical comparisons of displayed constants, not an invocation or acceptance of another seed-threshold draft.

## 2. All-split comparison with primitive capacities

We prove jointly that

  G(a,lambda d)<=lambda Omega(a,d)   for all a,d>=0.       (6)

When a=d=0 both sides are zero. Otherwise put w=Omega(a,d)>0 and t=a/w.
The equation w^2-(a+d)w-ad=0 gives

  0<=t<=1,    d/w=(1-t)/(1+t).

The larger root of P(v)=v^2-(4a+lambda d)v+4a^2 is G(a,lambda d).
Since lambda^2=2lambda+1, substitution and expansion give

  P(lambda w)/w^2
    =lambda^2-4lambda t-lambda^2*(1-t)/(1+t)+4t^2
    =4t*(t-1/sqrt(2))^2/(1+t) >=0.

Also w>=a+d, so lambda w>=lambda(a+d)>=2a+lambda d/2,
the vertex of P. A quadratic of positive leading coefficient is
nonnegative to the right of its vertex only at or beyond its larger
root. This proves (6), including either zero argument.

Both kernels are increasing in each nonnegative argument directly from
their displayed formulas. Since B_0=1<=lambda C_0, induction over every
ORIGINAL split k+l=n-1 gives

  G(C_k,B_l)<=G(C_k,lambda C_l)
             <=lambda Omega(C_k,C_l)<=lambda C_n.

Taking the maximum over all splits proves B_n<=lambda C_n. The accepted
symmetric identity C_(b*2^j-1)=lambda^j C_(b-1) therefore implies

  Z_b<=lambda C_(b-1),  for every fixed integer b>=1.      (7)

No optimizing split for B_n has been selected or discarded.

## 7. Scope

No seed existence, all-word lower bound, global ray optimum, or ordering
of the true b=1 and b=3 ratios is inferred. Dyadic rescaling alone is
not a new phase: the definition gives Z_(2b)=lambda Z_b, hence preserves
Z_((r+1)b)/Z_b. Nothing here asserts density or continuity of phases.

The conditional consequence requires an actual seed satisfying Section6.
No pointwise domination claim about another seed family is a premise.
This theorem does not supply such a seed, prove its nonexistence, or
settle the original all-n/all-nonnegative-word exact-profile problem.
