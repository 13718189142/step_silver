# Huber contact for the weighted-prefix objective

SELF-AUTHORED analytical lead; JOINT UNVERIFIED. No mathematical program. Original target OPEN. This note does not identify any numerical PEP optimum as exact.

Fix a nonnegative prefix k=(k_0,...,k_(p-1)), total s, and 0<=a<1. The weighted terminal criterion is V=2f_p-(1-a^2)||g_p||^2 under original radius one and advertised smoothness one. Put K=2+2s-a^2>0. If this prefix is obtained by deleting the last step b=1+a from a full word of total mass S, then K=1+2S-b^2.

## A universal exact lower witness for this criterion

Take the one-dimensional Huber function F_t(x)=x^2/2 for |x|<=t and t|x|-t^2/2 otherwise, with t=1/K and x0=1. Its clipped gradient is continuous, nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz; zero is its unique minimizer. Every actual prefix point is x_i=1-t sum_(j<i) k_j. Since K-(1+s)=1+s-a^2>0, x_i>t for all i. Thus every queried gradient equals t and

V=2t-(2+2s-a^2)t^2=1/K.

Consequently the exact full PEP value is at least 1/K for EVERY nonnegative prefix. If the proposed sufficient target V<=17/(16D), D=1+2S, is to hold, then necessarily D>=17b^2. This is not an upper bound or a full-word lower counterexample. Smoothness only gives 2f_after<=V.

## Contact equations for a hypothetical sharp dual

Retain all original rows Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0, including both minimizer directions and incoming final-query rows. Let lambda>=0 be their multipliers and let q be the coefficient on the original squared radius. Write mu_i=lambda_(i,star), nu_i=lambda_(star,i). Exact value cancellation gives

sum_i mu_i=2+sum_i nu_i.

Let L be lower triangular, L_ii=1 and L_ij=k_j for j<i. Set N_ij=-lambda_ij off diagonal and N_ii=mu_i+sum_(j!=i)lambda_ij. Then N e=mu and N^T e=2 e_p+nu. With B=sym(NL), the full quadratic after value cancellation is

P=q||x0||^2-<x0,sum_i mu_i g_i>+sum_(i,j)B_ij<g_i,g_j>-a^2||g_p||^2.

For a genuine sharp dual at q=1/K, Huber attainment makes P vanish on x0=K v, g_i=v. All actual Q_i,star and chronological Q_ij vanish there, whereas Q_star,i=-t x_i+t^2<0. Since the dual is PSD and all multipliers nonnegative, this forces every nu_i=0, hence sum mu_i=2. Equivalently, the common Huber direction must be a null vector of the PSD matrix, which yields

N tclock+L^T mu-2a^2 e_p=K mu, where tclock=L e.

These are exact contact restrictions at q=1/K ONLY. At the desired relaxed q=17/(16D), Huber contact need not be tight, nu may be nonzero, and none of these additional null identities may be imposed as necessary. A law deliberately choosing nu=0 is still a legitimate restricted construction, but its failure does not refute the full target.
