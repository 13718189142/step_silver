# Explicit forward transfer of optimum-outgoing multipliers

Status: unverified new certificate operation. It preserves the standard full matrix and nonnegative interpolation multipliers under the stated capacity condition.

## 1. One adjacent transfer

Use the signed smooth-convex quantities

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 <=0,

with the optimum sample * equal to zero. Fix a positive step h_i=a>0, so x_{i+1}=x_i-a g_i. A direct expansion gives the polynomial identity

    Q_{*,i+1}+(1/a)Q_{i+1,i}+((1-a)/a)Q_{i,i+1}
       -Q_{*,i}
       =(1/a)||g_{i+1}-(1-a)g_i||^2.                       (I)

For clarity, write delta_j=f_j-||g_j||^2/2. Then
Q_{i+1,i}=delta_{i+1}-delta_i+||g_{i+1}||^2+(a-1)<g_i,g_{i+1}>,
and
Q_{i,i+1}+Q_{i+1,i}=(1-a)||g_i||^2+||g_{i+1}||^2+(a-2)<g_i,g_{i+1}>.
Substitution yields (I), including all coefficients and signs.

Take any full feasible upper certificate with multipliers lambda and PSD slack M. Choose t>0 satisfying

    t<=lambda_{*,i},
    t<=a lambda_{i,i+1}/(a-1)       when a>1.                (C)

When 0<a<=1 the second condition is absent. Change only these four multipliers:

    lambda'_{*,i}=lambda_{*,i}-t,
    lambda'_{*,i+1}=lambda_{*,i+1}+t,
    lambda'_{i+1,i}=lambda_{i+1,i}+t/a,
    lambda'_{i,i+1}=lambda_{i,i+1}+t(1-a)/a.                (T)

All multipliers remain nonnegative by (C), and each node's flow divergence is unchanged. The schedule h and certificate level q are unchanged. The FULL slack changes by

    M'=M+(t/a)||g_{i+1}-(1-a)g_i||^2 >=0.                  (P)

Thus (T) is an explicit feasible motion in the original certificate variables, not merely a reduced row-sum operation. It neither assumes stationarity nor removes any PSD constraint.

For a<=1, the transfer adds or preserves the forward-edge multiplier. For a>1 it consumes exactly t(a-1)/a of the existing forward edge lambda_{i,i+1}. At a=1 the added square is t||g_{i+1}||^2 and no forward capacity is consumed.

## 2. Transport across a whole interval

Suppose lambda_{*,i}>0 for some i<n-1. Assume that for every j=i,...,n-2, either

    0<h_j<=1,

or

    h_j>1 and lambda_{j,j+1}>0.                             (A)

Choose t>0 at most lambda_{*,i} and at most every finite capacity

    h_j lambda_{j,j+1}/(h_j-1),  j in [i,n-2] with h_j>1.

There are finitely many strictly positive capacities, so such a t exists. Apply (T) successively at j=i,...,n-2, always transferring the same amount t. Intermediate optimum-outgoing weights receive t and then pass it on, while the consumed adjacent forward edges are distinct. The resulting standard-format certificate has

    lambda'_{*,i}=lambda_{*,i}-t,
    lambda'_{*,n-1}=lambda_{*,n-1}+t>0,

and unchanged other optimum-outgoing weights, schedule, objective level and flow divergence. Its full matrix is

    M'=M+sum_{j=i}^{n-2}(t/h_j)
             ||g_{j+1}-(1-h_j)g_j||^2 >=0.                 (B)

No arbitrary chronological crossing edge has been discarded; the operation modifies only the stated adjacent multipliers and retains the rest of the original matrix exactly.

## 3. A resulting actual descent of the algorithm

Write nu=lambda'_{*,n-1}>0 and b=h_{n-1}. Isolating Q_{*,n-1}=-(f_{n-1}-||g_{n-1}||^2/2) in the transferred certificate gives

    2f_n+nu delta_{n-1}<=q||x_0-x_*||^2.

At x=x_{n-1}, the unit-step point y=x-g_{n-1} satisfies f(y)<=delta_{n-1}. The old endpoint is z=x-b g_{n-1}. For epsilon=nu/(nu+2), c=1-epsilon, the new final step b_new=c b+epsilon gives z_new=c z+epsilon y. Convexity yields

    2f(z_new)<=2c f(z)+2epsilon delta_{n-1}
                =c[2f(z)+nu delta_{n-1}]
                <=c q||x_0-x_*||^2.

Thus the concrete predetermined schedule with final step

    b_new=(2b+nu)/(2+nu)

has the strict uniform bound

    W_n(h_0,...,h_{n-2},b_new)<=2q/(2+nu)<q.

This is a genuine fixed-length improvement. The transfer phase stayed within the minimal full interpolation certificate, and the final convexity argument is valid for the same function in every instance. If desired, its explicit enlarged PSD certificate is obtained by adding the old endpoint and unit-step point as auxiliary analysis samples; its identity is given in research/max2_penultimate_flow_descent.md.

## 4. A forced blocking pattern at global minimizers

For a globally minimizing positive schedule and ANY tight full certificate, lambda_{*,n-1}=0. Moreover, if lambda_{*,i}>0 with i<n-1, then there must exist at least one j in [i,n-2] such that

    h_j>1 and lambda_{j,j+1}=0.                             (Block)

Otherwise (A) would transport positive weight to the penultimate point and the preceding explicit schedule change would strictly improve the global value.

This is a necessary pattern in the actual full certificate. It does not say that every star-outgoing weight vanishes: an overrelaxed step with a zero forward edge can block this particular transfer. The final weight lambda_{*,n} is also outside the fixed-length transport argument.

## 5. Tightness and matching primal data

If the original q is tight at the fixed h, every matching legal primal remains matching after any feasible transfer: h and q have not changed and the new certificate is globally valid. Because M' is the sum of M and the nonnegative squares in (B), each transferred square must vanish on that matching primal:

    g_{j+1}=(1-h_j)g_j

at every transfer edge. The new positive star weights also require delta_j=0 wherever they are placed. These are consequences of the full certificate and primal complementarity, not assumptions used to discard other inequalities.

This explains the restrictive nature of the transport. In particular, the earlier nonlinear-suffix counterexample does not invalidate (I): its relevant tight-certificate capacity conditions are not supplied. Conversely, it remains invalid to bypass a zero forward edge merely by claiming that the composed objective is convex.

## 6. Scope of the new mechanism

The operation supplies a concrete way to exploit some earlier optimum-outgoing weights while preserving PSD and every multiplier sign. It isolates exact barriers h_j>1, lambda_{j,j+1}=0 rather than an unspecified missing separator.

It still does not show that a q>tau global minimizer must have a transferable weight, or that the full Schur defect can always remove a blocking edge. Those are distinct open structural questions. No implication to the exact profile U_n^ZJ is asserted.
