# A flat interval defeats the next short primitive candidate

Status: explicit unverified counterexample package. This addresses a new four-copy/three-bridge candidate after the three-copy obstruction. It is not a fact and is not a statement about the unrestricted target.

1. The candidate escapes the preceding simple screens.
A tempting four-copy rule is
J_4(a)=[a,1+(7/10)A,a,1+A,a,1+(1/5)A,a], A=1+sum a.
Its shifted mass is (59/10)A, exceeding the ordinary balanced four-copy factor (1+sqrt(2))^2. For a quadratic-balanced seed, the macro cut tests are satisfied:
(59/10)(7/10)=413/100 <=21/5,
59/10 <=(27/10)(11/5)=297/50,
(59/10)(1/5)=59/50 <=47/10.
The normalized full-curvature product is (59/10)(7/10)(1)(1/5)=413/500<1.
Thus the three-copy cut-plus-quadratic obstruction does not extend automatically to four copies.

The rule is nevertheless invalid already for the empty primitive seed A=1. Its proposed schedule is h=(17/10,2,6/5), with sum s=49/10 and shifted mass R=59/10. These three steps individually satisfy all the primitive cut tests. Its full-curvature endpoint multiplier is -7/50, so R times its magnitude is413/500<1.

2. Exact function and trajectory.
Put w=7/34 and ell=21/85, and define a continuous derivative
f'(y)=y for y<=w,
f'(y)=w for w<=y<=w+ell,
f'(y)=y-ell for y>=w+ell.
Define f(0)=0 and integrate. This derivative is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz. Thus f is convex, differentiable,1-smooth, and has minimizer0. Its formula is
f(y)=y^2/2 for y<=w;
f(y)=w y-w^2/2 for w<=y<=w+ell;
f(y)=(y-ell)^2/2+w ell for y>=w+ell.

Start at y_0=106/85. The GD data are exactly
(y_0,y_1,y_2,y_3)=(106/85,-77/170,77/170,7/34),
(g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3)=(1,-77/170,7/34,7/34).
Indeed the first step reaches -77/170; the step2 reflects it to77/170=w+ell; and the final6/5-step traverses the plateau of length ell=(6/5)w and reaches w. All specified gradients follow from the displayed derivative.

For the saturated intrinsic primitive energy
V_h=2 sum_(i<3)h_i(f_i-f_3)+sum_(i<3)h_i(1-h_i)g_i^2-sR g_3^2,
the function values are
f_0=1/2+w ell, f_1=(77/170)^2/2,
f_2=w^2/2+w ell, f_3=w^2/2.
Direct rational substitution gives
V_h=-13581/23120<0.
The proposed primitive interface is therefore false. The actual objective error on this particular instance is only (35/212)^2, so this is specifically a primitive-certificate counterexample, not an objective minimax lower bound matching the target.

3. A reusable two-parameter test.
For h=(H,2,C) with H>1,C>0, define
w=(H-1)/(2C+1), v=(C+1)w, ell=Cw.
Assume w<=1 so that y_0=1+ell lies in the right quadratic region of the same derivative shape (plateau [w,v]). Then the actual trajectory is
(y_0,y_1,y_2,y_3)=(1+Cw,-v,v,w),
(g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3)=(1,-v,w,w).
The saturated primitive energy is exactly
V_h=H(2-H)-(H^2+6H+4C+8)((H-1)/(2C+1))^2.
To check it, use f_0=1/2+Cw^2, f_1=v^2/2, f_2=(C+1/2)w^2, f_3=w^2/2 and s=H+C+2. The constant term is H(2-H); the w^2 coefficient simplifies to -(H^2+6H+4C+8). The specific H=17/10,C=6/5 gives the negative rational value above.

4. Certificate attempts and remaining direction.
Before finding the function, two direct conic interpolation attempts were screened by a tiny exact rational coefficient calculation (four sampled gradients and twelve ordered Q_ij terms). A remainder proportional to ||g_0-g_1||^2 and one proportional to ||g_1-g_2||^2 both forced inconsistent nonnegative multiplier bounds. Adding either of two chosen squares vanishing on the constant-gradient and full-curvature trajectories did not repair those restricted ansatzes. These are only failed certificate forms; the explicit function is what refutes the candidate. The exact arithmetic used negligible memory/time and was not an exhaustive search or a numerical optimization.

The reversed schedule (6/5,2,17/10), with the same sum and quadratic multiplier, is a distinct unresolved candidate. The displayed flat-interval test does not refute it. Selected rank-one certificate attempts have also failed, which is not an impossibility proof. No universal reversal theorem for arbitrary primitive schedules is being assumed: the downloaded GSW paper gives reversal results for basic schedules in the relevant section. A useful next step is either an actual nonnegative interpolation certificate (including its PSD remainder) for this reversed seed or an explicit legal function with negative V. Only after such a certificate passes verification could it be used as an improved primitive seed, and a separate infinite-family comparison to the exact objective DP would remain.
