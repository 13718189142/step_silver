## 2. Exact equivalence of the implemented original PSD model

L^T e=omega, and M>=0 on all of R^(r+1).

   =-2sum_i h_i(1+sum_(t<i)h_t)+2s(1+s)

nonnegativity of (e+t v)^T M(e+t v) for both signs of sufficiently
