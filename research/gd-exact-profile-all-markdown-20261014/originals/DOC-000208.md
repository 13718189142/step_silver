# 0. Mathematical verdict

**Not solved.** No accepted fact supplies the uniform all-schedule lower
bound or an asymptotic full-class upper separation required by the original
problem.

The fixed goal is to prove W_n^*/U_n^ZJ ->1, or disprove it rigorously.
Here W_n(h)=sup 2(f(x_n)-f_star)/(L||x0-x_star||^2), over all finite-dimensional
convex L-smooth functions with a minimizer, for predetermined h>=0 and GD
x_(t+1)=x_t-h_t grad f(x_t)/L. W_n^*=inf_h W_n(h), W=2R.
P0=B0=1; F(x,y)=(x+y+sqrt(x^2+6xy+y^2))/2;
G(x,d)=2x+d/2+sqrt(d^2+8xd)/2;
P_n=max_(k+l=n-1)F(P_k,P_l); B_n=max_(k+l=n-1)G(P_k,B_l);
U_n^ZJ=1/B_n. Every horizon, zero steps and unbounded steps remain in scope.

Closed components include universal Huber testing (0be8b54e0a33381c),
exact nonnegative-schedule terminal completion (1252b8823bb5a290), and the
finite actual-H6 adversary (e9a55f57461f253c). None closes the asymptotic target.
A current conditional affirmative architecture couples actual child
adversaries while paying every cross constraint and original distance.
The most developed modal-kernel draft is a restricted-family exclusion,
not a full-class negative proof. Both outcomes remain logically open.

The main blocker is a target-scale legal construction for every schedule,
or a full original-space upper certificate with a fixed relative gain on
unbounded horizons. The immediate independent missing interfaces are
two-child legal defect aggregation, a useful construction outside the
screened scalar power/character classes, and uniform chronological kernel
signs with their correct restricted scope. The exclusions are method failure;
there is no evidence here of proposition failure.
Status labels do not measure distance to a complete proof.

Current best conditional proof skeleton:
1. Preserve the exact F/G dynamic program and W=2R normalization.
2. For a given arbitrary finite nonnegative schedule, test the accepted Huber
   witness and other applicable accepted finite tools.
3. When these miss the target scale, identify actual compatible child
   transcripts rather than assign hypothetical scalar defects.
4. Construct one parent using both children, preserving all old, cross and
   optimum interpolation constraints and closest-distance normalization.
5. Establish a quantitative aggregation rule and a uniform all-schedule
   decomposition with total loss tending to zero. Both are currently missing.
6. Use accepted terminal completion only within its matched hypotheses;
   conclude the all-h lower bound and independently attack the entire proof.
The negative alternative requires actual full-class certificates; a bound on
any proper family cannot substitute for step5 or for those upper certificates.

Central missing lemma: for every epsilon>0 there exists an N_epsilon,
independent of h, such that for every n>=N_epsilon and every h in[0,infinity)^n
there exists a finite-dimensional convex L-smooth function with a minimizer
and a nontrivial initial point, whose actual GD payoff is at least
(1-epsilon)/B_n. A full-class upper separation along an unbounded subsequence
would instead disprove this statement. This is the original minimax-strength
gap, not a routine lemma or a new reduction counted as progress.

# 2. Interface contract table

### Two-child legal composition — affirmative construction
**Input required.** Two complete actual smooth-convex child transcripts,
their endpoints, normals, objective values and closest-distance data.
**Output claimed.** The existing draft transfers one actual core defect
across a tail; it does not combine two independent defects without dilution.
**Available facts.** 1252b8823bb5a290 supplies terminal completion only after
legality, b98bb807d514e80d supplies the exact comparator.
**Missing verification on the actual model.** A common parent embedding,
every cross-child Q, exact initial-radius cost, empty-child boundaries,
an iterative gain law and application to all h.
**Failure mode if ignored.** Favorable abstract Gram cross terms may not
admit one legal function, and normalization may consume the claimed gain.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Chronological kernel — restricted temporal upper bound
**Input required.** Actual H_m with enclosing c=(1+sqrt2)(1+1/m),
a=(1-1/m)/c, steps d_j=1+a c^j and queried prefix H_m without its final d0.
For p_ij=product_(r<i)(1-h_r/d_j), gradients have the special form
g_i=v+sum_j p_ij w_j in one arbitrary real Gram space. Keep original x0,
constant mode v, full terminal clock and all legal sampled values.
**Output claimed.** A complete formal square identity for its chronological
kernel J, and uniform signs for the first three birth-coordinate rows.
**Available facts.** 1252b8823bb5a290 addresses exact original terminal treatment.
**Missing verification on the actual model.** Uniform e_i>=0,b_i>0,r_i>=0,
r_(m-1)>0 and Delta>=0 for all original m>=128, using the explicitly defined
coefficients in3d57065c9de5e34b. Complete formal-space accounting is retained.
**Failure mode if ignored.** Finite positivity, total positivity or individual
swap heuristics do not establish the coefficient signs. Even a completed
proof covers this temporal family, not unrestricted trajectories.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Actual nonlinear projection geometry — scalable lower witnesses
**Input required.** One fixed convex projection body, actual gradients during
the entire prescribed schedule and true initial distance to its polar minimizer set.
**Output claimed.** C69 supplies a full proper-family exclusion for scalar
power epigraphs with2<p<=m/256; C62 offers a draft finite representation.
**Available facts.** e9a55f57461f253c demonstrates one legal finite high-payoff
geometry; b98bb807d514e80d fixes the benchmark.
**Missing verification on the actual model.** A useful mechanism outside the
excluded domain, with horizon-dependent constants and normalization paid.
**Failure mode if ignored.** A nonlinear clock example or a generic simplex
representation can be mistaken for a quantitative adversary.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

### Character weights to one legal function — finite-field/probability construction
**Input required.** An explicit nonnegative finite character measure,
original chronological labels, real vectors, an amplitude rule and common anchor.
**Output claimed.** The old moment chart fails at draft level for all positive
amplitudes. The proposed low-pair-correlated law leaves that chart.
**Available facts.** 1252b8823bb5a290 and b98bb807d514e80d.
**Missing verification on the actual model.** ALL remote interpolation pairs,
nonsaturation costs, actual minimizer, true polar distance and target-scale payoff.
**Failure mode if ignored.** Positive semidefinite Grams and escape from one
necessary-condition obstruction are not legal useful GD trajectories.
**Status.** PARTIAL.

# 3. Dangerous heuristic lines and strategies not to pursue

- “The local defect can be propagated indefinitely.” DANGEROUS: the tail
  transfer and repeated balanced composition are different claims; only one
  child's surplus can be diluted even when a tail preserves it.
- “The kernel has positive pivots up to128.” DANGEROUS: this is finite
  evidence. A known boundary coefficient vanishes as the horizon grows.
- “A generic Hardy estimate closes the covariance comparison.” DANGEROUS:
  primary-source estimates have quantified factor loss and require matched
  weights/boundaries. Their applicability has not been established here.
- “Power epigraphs now have a full upper bound.” DANGEROUS: C69 is a draft
  bound for the displayed family only, not an upper bound on W_n.
- “The new character law evades the moment condition.” PARTIAL: it does so
  algebraically, but no useful all-pair legal trajectory follows automatically.
- “A projection/simplex representation repairs inconsistent data.” DANGEROUS:
  the representation assumes the required original legality.

Do not erase the terminal constant column or original initial-position square.
Do not impose a fixed positive lower bound on all birth-coordinate residuals.
Do not spend remaining numerical capacity simply repeating stable positive tests.
Do not reset enclosing-m parameters inside a child word.
Do not reuse old fixed-m H6 geometry as an automatically legal large-m subword.
Do not verify each proper-family exclusion merely to increase the fact count.
Do not infer either original answer from failure of a restricted construction.

# 4. Missing bridge lemmas

### Bridge1 — simultaneous legal child aggregation
**Target statement.** An explicit parent-construction theorem for a stated
class of ACTUAL child transcripts, with all cross interpolation, true radius
and final value derived jointly, gives a quantitative gain from BOTH child
defects and remains iterable on the actual schedule decompositions used.
**Prerequisites.** Concrete child contracts, physical joining step and one
common real Gram/function; empty children explicitly included or scoped.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290 and b98bb807d514e80d.
**Missing checks.** Cross normals, normalization, defect accumulation and
all-schedule applicability.
**Closure criterion.** A proved constructive quantitative law or a legal
counterexample to that specified law, not an unevaluated block SDP.
Independent of Bridges2 and3.

### Bridge2 — uniform full modal-kernel factor signs
**Target statement.** For every integer m>=128, the exact coefficients in
the displayed lossless birth-coordinate factor identity satisfy the sign
and constant-load conditions recorded in the kernel interface.
**Prerequisites.** Original chronological word, physical nodes, complete
constant-column charge and all signed far-entry costs.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290 for matched terminal treatment.
**Missing checks.** Bulk, right boundary and alternating constant load;
first-row proofs and finite intervals do not supply these.
**Closure criterion.** A uniform analytic proof, or a different explicit
factorization if one sufficient coefficient sign fails. The resulting scope
remains restricted. Independent of Bridges1 and3.

### Bridge3 — low-pair-dependent character realization
**Target statement.** On the actual unchanged H_m, construct positive
amplitudes and one anchor/function for the explicit measure637fa4d19362db29,
retaining every pair and giving a quantified original-B_n payoff, or prove
an all-amplitude obstruction for this new measure.
**Prerequisites.** Explicit finite nonnegative weights, original labels and
horizon-dependent mixture; singular limits cannot be taken for free.
**Existing facts to use.** 1252b8823bb5a290 and b98bb807d514e80d.
**Missing checks.** Feasibility of amplitudes, remote pairs, common anchor,
true polar distance and asymptotic comparison.
**Closure criterion.** Actual legal quantitative witness or a scoped exact
obstruction that identifies what freedom still survives.
Independent of Bridge1; it must not borrow the missing conclusion of Bridge2.
