# C55: the prescribed three-boundary queried-pair recurrence is insufficient

Status: **complete scoped proof candidate, not verifier-accepted**. No
optimization or verifier allocation is used. The predecessor
`1252b8823bb5a290` supplies the interpolation convention and the requirement
that an objective certificate be positive semidefinite on all the original
formal vectors. Everything specific to this recursive family is derived
below. Prior C53/C55 findings are motivation, not mathematical premises.

The result covers precisely the H2 seed and the repeated-copy join with the
added balanced pair described below, including arbitrary horizon-dependent
join parameters and storage choices. It does not cover arbitrary seeds,
independent internal array corrections, or unrestricted certificates for the
same schedule. The test vectors are formal vectors, not a legal function.

## 1. Statement and notation

Fix an integer m>=128 and define

    epsilon=1/m, lambda=1+sqrt(2),
    c=lambda(1+epsilon), a=(1-epsilon)/c,
    d_j=a c^j,
    H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+d_j,H_j),
    n_j=2^j-1, S_j=sum H_j, M_j=1+S_j.

All steps are predetermined and strictly positive; the bridges grow with
the recursion level. No upper bound on the steps is assumed. The claims
below concern this explicit family, not arbitrary nonnegative schedules.

For formal gradients substitute the actual GD recursion with smoothness
one and optimum jet zero. For distinct samples define

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    C_ij=-2 Q_ji
        =2(f_i-f_j)+2<g_j,x_j-x_i>-||g_i-g_j||^2.

The accepted predecessor proves C_ij>=0 for legal smooth convex data.
Formal vectors used to test a quadratic certificate need not be legal.

An array at level j has storage parameters u_j,v_j and star pattern

    lambda_(star,i)=h_i              (i<n_j),
    lambda_(star,n_j)=u_j,
    lambda_(i,star)=0                (i<n_j),
    lambda_(n_j,star)=s_j=S_j+u_j-v_j.                 (1)

Here lambda_ik denotes a multiplier array on C_ik; the scalar lambda without
indices denotes 1+sqrt(2). The full identity defines its remainder E_j:

    ||x_start||^2-||x_end-u_j g_end||^2-2v_j f_end
                   =sum lambda_ik C_ik+E_j,
    Z_j=(u_j-1)^2+v_j,
    z_j=sum_(i<n_j) h_i g_i+g_end.                    (2)

Start from the exact H2 seed in Section 2, and for j=2,...,m-1 apply any
join in Section 3, with a balanced multiplier gamma_j>=0 on the queried
pair (p,q-1). The same child array is embedded on the left and multiplied
by r_j>=0 on the right. All actual final multipliers must be nonnegative
to give a certificate. One may use any real u_j,v_j, with v_m>0, and need
not require positive semidefinite child or local remainders.

Then, if the complete objective matrix at level q_cert is PSD, necessarily

    q_cert >= 1/k_m^2
           > (2/5)^(2m) exp[2^(m-3)/m],              (3)

where the signed numbers k_j are defined in Section 5. The last expression
tends to infinity. In particular q_cert>1 for m>=128, so this family cannot
supply v_m>=M_m with E_m PSD, nor any useful bounded objective coefficient.
This is an obstruction for the enlarged C55 recurrence, not an upper or
lower bound on the actual schedule's worst-case objective.

The proof retains every internal child square and every new pair term. Its
key change from C53 is that the last-query gradient is c times the terminal
gradient, instead of equal to it.

## 2. The exact seed, its signs, and its attained boundary form

Put

    b0=1+a, M=M_1=2+a, d=d_1=1-epsilon,
    x=d/M, eta=x^2+2x-1, a0=(2-b0^2)/b0^2.

The one-step seed H1=(b0) has finite multipliers

    lambda_01=M, lambda_10=1,

star multipliers lambda_(star,0)=b0, lambda_(star,1)=M, and no incoming
star multipliers. Set u_1=M, v_1=2M-1, hence Z_1=M^2. Direct expansion in
(2) gives

    E_1=(2-b0^2)||g_0-g_1||^2.

To obtain H2, use the join of Section 3 with gamma=0 and

    A=1/x, B=2+x, r=3+x+1/x, t=1/x-2-x,
    P=(1+M eta)/x, Q=M,
    u_2=M_2=2M+d, v_2=2M_2-1.                        (4)

Here 1<b0<sqrt(2), 3/8<x<sqrt(2)-1, and eta<0. For the upper bound on x,
with s=sqrt(2)-1=1/lambda, expansion gives

    d-sM=-epsilon[1-2s^2/(1+epsilon)]<0.

The lower bound follows from d>=127/128 and M<5/2. Thus a0,t>0.
Moreover eta>=-7/64, so 1+M eta>1-35/128>0. Consequently A,B,P,Q,r are
positive. The physical star pattern has s_1=s_2=0, and the update described
in Section 3 yields nonnegative actual star multipliers as well.

For the four gradients of H2 define

    U=b0(g_0-g_1), V0=b0(g_2-g_3), D0=g_1-g_3.

Expanding the complete join gives

    E_2=a0||U||^2+(r a0+t)||V0||^2
                         +M^2(-eta)||D0||^2.          (5)

For an explicit coefficient check, (4) gives H=-t(M+d), ell=-tM in (8)
below. Substitute V=d g_1+b0 g_2+g_3, Z_1=M^2, Z_2=(2M+d)^2 into (8).
The mixed V0,D0 coefficient is A d-M=0; the D0 square coefficient is
M^2(1-2x-x^2). The two seed remainders provide a0||U||^2+r a0||V0||^2.
The remaining term is t||V0||^2, establishing (5).

Changing u_2,v_2 and only the two terminal star coefficients in (1) adds
(M_2^2-Z_2)||g_3||^2 to (5). Thus the obstruction also permits these
storage changes at the seed. The finite seed coefficients remain fixed.

With (z,e,w)=(z_2,g_3,g_2), the boundary relation is

    z-M_2 e=U+V0+(M+d)D0.

All three boundary vectors are independent. Set c0=M^2(-eta), l0=M+d.
The exact identity

    a0||U||^2+c0||D0||^2
      =a0 c0/(c0+a0 l0^2) ||U+l0 D0||^2
           +||a0 l0 U-c0 D0||^2/(c0+a0 l0^2)

has its second square equal to zero for every prescribed value of
U+l0 D0. Therefore the sharp, attained boundary form is

    K_2(z,e,w)=A2||z-M_2 e-b0(w-e)||^2
                         +B2||w-e||^2
                         +(M_2^2-Z_2)||e||^2,
    A2=[1/a0+(1+x)^2/(-eta)]^(-1),
    B2=(r a0+t)b0^2.                                 (6)

No unattainability of boundary minimizers is assumed in what follows.

## 3. The actual enlarged array and the full local identity

At a join write n=n_j, p=n, q=2n+1, S=S_j, M=M_j, d=d_j, b=1+d.
Choose A,B,P,Q with

    r=1+A+B, P-Q=b+(1-A)S.

Embed the old array on the left and r times that array on the right.
For each right queried index p<i<q add

    A h_i to (p,i), B h_i to (q,i),
    -(A+B)h_i to (star,i).

Add the six corrections

    (p,q):P, (q,p):Q,
    (p,star):-s_j, (q,star):s_(j+1)-r s_j,
    (star,p):b-u_j, (star,q):u_(j+1)-r u_j.            (7)

Finally add gamma>=0 to both (p,q-1) and (q-1,p). These are actual queried
samples. This balanced change preserves every function-value flow and
every star multiplier. It adds no incoming unqueried-terminal constraint.

For example A,B,P,Q>=0, u_j>=0 and s_j>=0 at the relevant levels suffice
for nonnegative actual arrays when the seed is nonnegative: right queried
star coefficients become (r-A-B)h_i=h_i; the two terminal star coefficients
become b and u_(j+1); the two incoming terminal star coefficients become
zero and s_(j+1). All other entries are copied or receive nonnegative
additions. The obstruction allows all real A,B,P,Q satisfying the flow
identity and r>=0, so it also covers choices whose signs are certified by
a more detailed inspection of the actual array.

Write g=g_p, e=g_q, w=g_(q-1), and define

    t=A-B,
    H=M+d-B(M-d)-tM,
    ell=P-A,
    V=x_p-x_q-g+e=d g+z_right.

The complete remainder identity is

    E_next=E_left+r E_right
       +t||V||^2+2<V,(H-ell)g+ell e>
       +(Z_j-rd^2)||g||^2+(rZ_j-Z_next)||e||^2
       +2gamma<g-w,e+a w-V>.                          (8)

Here a is the fixed parameter in b0=1+a, since every block ends with that
same step b0. To verify the new term, x_(q-1)=x_q+b0 w gives

    -gamma(C_(p,q-1)+C_(q-1,p))
       =2gamma[||g-w||^2-<g-w,x_p-x_(q-1)>]
       =2gamma<g-w,e+a w-V>.

For completeness the old part of (8) can be checked before the pair is
added. Put D=x_p-x_q and W=sum_(p<i<q)h_i g_i=x_p-b g-x_q. The rank
correction sums to

    2AS f_p+2BS f_q-AS||g||^2-BS||e||^2
                      -2<W,A(x_p-g)+B(x_q-e)>.

The value coefficient at p becomes

    -2v_j+2AS+2(P-Q)-2s_j-2(b-u_j)
            =2[(A-1)S+P-Q-b]=0.

The value coefficient at q becomes -2v_next using r=1+A+B and (1).
After subtracting the correction from the new potential minus the two
child potentials, the quadratic coefficients are

    D^2:t, (D,g):H-ell-t, (D,e):ell+t,
    g^2:Z_j-rd^2+t-2H+2ell,
    e^2:rZ_j-Z_next+t+2ell, (g,e):H-2ell-t,

where a mixed coefficient multiplies twice the indicated inner product.
Substituting V=D-g+e proves the old part of (8). Thus (8) is an identity
on the full formal space, with every inherited remainder retained.

## 4. Explicit eliminated matrix and every singular range condition

This section records the exact recurrence being obstructed, including
its singular cases. Write an attained child boundary form as

    K(z,e,w)=alpha||z||^2+2beta<z,e>+2chi<z,w>
                        +delta||e||^2+2etaK<e,w>+phi||w||^2.

In (8) substitute E_left=K(L,g,u)+its internal remainder and
E_right=K(R,e,w)+its internal remainder. The six-variable form has
parent boundary z=L+d g+R. Eliminate X=(g,u,R) and retain Y=(z,e,w).
After L=z-d g-R, its symmetric matrix is

                         [ D_el  B_el ]
                         [ B_el^T C_el].

Its entries are exactly

    D_el(1,1)=(alpha+t-r)d^2-2beta d+delta
                                      +2d(H-ell-gamma)+Z_j,
    D_el(1,2)=etaK-d chi,
    D_el(1,3)=d(alpha+t)-beta+H-ell-gamma,
    D_el(2,2)=phi,
    D_el(2,3)=-chi,
    D_el(3,3)=(1+r)alpha+t;

    B_el=[ -d alpha+beta,  d ell+gamma, gamma(a+d);
                 chi,          0,              0;
               -alpha,     r beta+ell,       r chi+gamma ];

    C_el=[ alpha,          0,                    0;
              0, r delta+rZ_j-Z_next,  r etaK-gamma;
              0,       r etaK-gamma,    r phi-2a gamma ].  (9)

The full six-variable matrix is PSD if and only if all of the following
hold:

    D_el is PSD;
    B_el^T xi=0 for EVERY xi in ker(D_el);
    C_el-B_el^T D_el^dagger B_el is PSD.                (10)

The middle condition is equivalently that every column of B_el belongs to
the range of D_el. The dagger is the inverse on its positive eigenspaces
and zero on its kernel. To prove (10), first restrict to Y=0. A nonzero
cross coefficient against a null vector of D_el makes the form unbounded
below by scaling that null direction. Once these coefficients vanish,
complete the square on the positive eigenspaces. This proves both
necessity and sufficiency, including every rank of D_el.

When the children have exact attained decompositions with PSD internal
remainders and r>=0, (10) is also equivalent to PSD of the entire parent
remainder: the independent boundary data can be lifted while setting all
internal child squares to zero. The new sharp boundary form is the last
Schur complement in (10). No such interpretation is needed for the
obstruction below: it directly evaluates the original E_j and does not
assume that any earlier elimination passed.

## 5. A different formal seed survives every new queried pair

Define

    k_2=M_2+6epsilon, theta=c.

At the H2 seed choose the following explicit four gradients, all multiples
of an arbitrary vector e:

    g_0=(2-c+6epsilon/b0)e,
    g_1=e, g_2=c e, g_3=e.                            (11)

Then the exact boundary is

    z_2=k_2 e, g_end=e, g_last_query=c e.

Indeed z_2=b0 g_0+(1+d_1)g_1+b0 g_2+g_3=(M_2+6epsilon)e.
In (5), D0=0, V0=b0(c-1)e, and U=[6epsilon-b0(c-1)]e.
We next prove the strict energy inequality

    E_2 < (k_2^2-Z_2)||e||^2                         (12)

for e!=0, for every m>=128. This includes all internal seed squares.

First, a>2/5. In fact sqrt(2)<10/7 gives lambda<17/7, so

    a >= (127/129)(7/17)=889/2193 >2/5.

Also a<s=sqrt(2)-1, b0<sqrt(2), M>12/5 and c<5/2. To bound a0, use

    b0=sqrt(2)-2s epsilon/(1+epsilon),
    2-b0^2<4sqrt(2)s epsilon=4(2-sqrt(2))epsilon
                                          <(12/5)epsilon,
    b0^2>49/25.

Therefore a0<(60/49)epsilon<(5/4)epsilon. Since 3/8<x<s<1/2,

    r=3+x+1/x<37/6<25/4.

Furthermore

    s-x=epsilon[1-2s^2/(1+epsilon)]/M<(5/12)epsilon,
    t=(s-x)(1+1/(sx))<(5/12)(23/3)epsilon<4epsilon.

The estimate on 1/(sx) uses s>2/5 and x>3/8. We have
0<6epsilon<b0(c-1), because 6epsilon<=3/64 and b0,c-1>7/5. Hence

    ||U||^2 <= b0^2(c-1)^2||e||^2,
    b0^2(c-1)^2<2(3/2)^2=9/2.

Substitution into the FULL seed formula (5) gives

    E_2-(M_2^2-Z_2)||e||^2
       <=[(r+1)a0+t] b0^2(c-1)^2||e||^2
       <[(29/4)(5/4)+4](9/2)epsilon||e||^2
       =(1881/32)epsilon||e||^2
       <60epsilon||e||^2.

Finally M_2=5-epsilon+2a>5, so

    k_2^2-M_2^2=12M_2 epsilon+36epsilon^2>60epsilon.

This proves (12). In particular the argument has not replaced the full
seed by an unattainable boundary minimum or dropped a positive square.

## 6. A uniform subcritical gap and its exact propagation

Set

    R0=k_2/(c d_2)=(M_2+6epsilon)/[(1-epsilon)c^2].

We have the explicit bound

    0<R0<1-epsilon/4.                                (13)

For its proof expand the available gap using lambda^2=2lambda+1:

    (c d_2-M_2)/epsilon
      =[6sqrt(2)+epsilon-2lambda^2 epsilon^2
                                  -lambda^2 epsilon^3]/(1+epsilon).

This is greater than 8. Indeed after subtracting 8 and multiplying by
1+epsilon, the numerator is

    6sqrt(2)-8-7epsilon-2lambda^2 epsilon^2-lambda^2 epsilon^3
      >2/5-7/128-12/128^2-6/128^3>0.

Consequently c d_2-k_2>2epsilon. Since c d_2=(1-epsilon)c^2<25/4,

    1-R0>8epsilon/25>epsilon/4,

as claimed.

Now define signed numbers recursively by

    k_(j+1)=-k_j^2/d_j, j>=2.                         (14)

For every block H_j there is a formal gradient assignment linear in its
terminal vector e such that

    z_j=k_j e, g_end=e, g_last_query=c e,
    E_j <= (k_j^2-Z_j)||e||^2.                        (15)

This holds simultaneously for every permitted choice of the join and
storage parameters, including choices depending on the target horizon.
The seed is (11)-(12). For the induction step put the right child on its
template with terminal e, and the left child on its template with terminal

    g=-k_j e/d_j.

Then V=d_j g+z_right=0, and the parent moment is

    z_next=z_left+d_j g+z_right=k_j g=k_(j+1)e.

Its last-query gradient remains c e. The crucial NEW pair term in (8) is

    2gamma(-k_j/d_j-c)(1+a c)||e||^2.                 (16)

It is nonpositive at every level. At j=2, k_2>0 proves this directly. For
j>=3, k_j<0; solving (14) gives

    |k_j|=(1-epsilon)c^j R0^(2^(j-2)),
    |k_j|/d_j=c R0^(2^(j-2))<c.                     (17)

Thus (16) is negative for gamma>0 and e!=0, since 1+a c=2-epsilon>0.

The other mixed local terms vanish because V=0. Since r>=0, the two
complete child estimates in (15) retain their direction under addition.
Keeping their full values and the nonpositive new pair term gives

    E_next <= (k_j^2-Z_j)||g||^2
                 +r(k_j^2-Z_j)||e||^2
                 +(Z_j-rd_j^2)||g||^2+(rZ_j-Z_next)||e||^2
             = (k_j^4/d_j^2-Z_next)||e||^2,

which is exactly (15) at the parent. No local PSD, nonsingularity, greedy
storage, or rank assumption was used.

In co-coercivity language the pair has

    C_(p,q-1)+C_(q-1,p)
          =2(2-epsilon)(c+k_j/d_j)||e||^2>=0.

This explains why a nonnegative weight on this actual pair cannot repair
this new formal template. The old C53 template had the opposite sign on
some such pairs; those are different formal vectors.

## 7. The original initial-vector objective conversion

Let u=u_m, v=v_m>0, Z=(u-1)^2+v and k=k_m. For the final array the full
objective certificate at any level q_cert is

    M_cert=q_cert||x_0||^2-2f_n
                     +sum_(i!=j) (2lambda_ji/v)Q_ij
           =(q_cert-1/v)||x_0||^2
                     +[||x_n-u g_n||^2+E_m]/v.        (18)

This follows directly from (2) and C_ij=-2Q_ji. In particular E_m PSD
and nonnegative actual multipliers yield the bound q_cert=1/v. The
normalization is the project's 2(f_n-f_star)/(L||x_0-x_star||^2), by the
scaling in predecessor `1252b8823bb5a290`.

Keep x_0 independent of the formal template in (15), and set
beta0=k+u-1. Since z_m=k e,

    x_n-u g_n=x_0-beta0 e.

The restriction of v M_cert is bounded ABOVE by the two-variable form
with matrix

                  [ q_cert v    -beta0       ]
                  [  -beta0    2k beta0-v    ].        (19)

The difference is a nonnegative multiple of ||e||^2, by (15). If the
original M_cert is PSD, this upper matrix must therefore also be PSD.
Its second diagonal is positive: if it were zero, PSD would force
beta0=0, making that diagonal -v<0. Its determinant now implies

    q_cert >= beta0^2/[v(2k beta0-v)] >=1/k^2,

where the last step is exactly

    k^2 beta0^2-v(2k beta0-v)=(v-k beta0)^2>=0.

This includes arbitrary increases of q_cert and indefinite child or final
remainders. It tests the original x_0 variable, not only the condensed
gradient state.

From (13),(17) and 1-x<=exp(-x),

    |k_m|<c^m exp[-2^(m-4)/m].                        (20)

Thus (3) follows from c<5/2. The logarithm of its right side is
2^(m-3)/m-2m log(5/2), which tends to infinity because 2^m/m^2 tends to
infinity. Its successive ratio is 2m^2/(m+1)^2>3/2 for m>=7.
For m>=128, (20) also gives |k_m|<1: log(5/2)<1 follows from
exp(1)>1+1+1/2+1/6>5/2, and 2^(m-4)>m^2 for m>=128. The latter inequality
holds at 128 and is preserved since 2m^2/(m+1)^2>1. Hence q_cert>1.

## 8. Undeformed calibration and scope checks

Set epsilon=0, so a=1/lambda, c=lambda. Then b0=sqrt(2), M_j=lambda^j,
d_j=lambda^(j-1), a0=t=eta=0, and the physical seed E_2 is identically
zero. At each higher join choose

    A=B=lambda, r=lambda^2, t=H=ell=0,
    P=lambda, Q=M_j,
    u_j=(1+sqrt(4M_j^2-3))/2, v_j=u_j, Z_j=M_j^2.

The same storage choice can be made at H2 by its permitted terminal-star
change, preserving E_2=0. It has s_j=S_j>=0. The flow equation follows
from M_j=lambda d_j, and (8) with gamma=0 gives E_next=0. Every actual
multiplier is nonnegative by Section 3. This is an exact valid undeformed
calibration of the full array and the objective conversion (18).

If one retains the physical H2 storage u_2=M_2,v_2=2M_2-1 instead, the
seed still has Z_2=M_2^2; switching to the displayed u_j,v_j at the next
level likewise changes only the two terminal star coefficients and is
allowed. No calibration identity is being carried from a differently
normalized performance measure.

At epsilon=0 the obstruction template has k_2=M_2 and R0=1, so it yields
only q_cert>=lambda^(-2m), entirely compatible with the valid bound
1/u_m of order lambda^(-m). The strict horizon-dependent gap in (13)
is essential.

The hypothesis r>=0 is exactly the scaling restriction in the C55 state
recurrence, not an extra PSD assumption on its children. It is also
forced by nonnegative final arrays in this prescribed construction. The
seed has the positive coefficient lambda_01=M. At every larger level,
the first two queried samples retain this coefficient unchanged, because
they lie in the left child and none of the join corrections touches their
ordered pair. In a right child the corresponding ordered pair has
coefficient rM and is untouched by the parent's fan, sparse, and new-pair
corrections. In the final leftmost occurrence of any such join it remains
untouched by all higher joins. Thus a negative r at any level would leave
a negative actual coefficient in the final array. This also shows that
the proof covers arrays required to be nonnegative only at the final
level. The case r=0 is already included in the energy induction.

The seed restriction matters: the result does not assert that every H2
array has (5), or that every three-boundary seed is covered. A different
seed has to be tested against the seed-energy and last-query conditions
(12),(15). Arbitrary additional queried pairs can change (16); no claim
about those corrections is made here. Likewise the formal template need
not satisfy omitted interpolation constraints, so it is not an actual
lower witness for H_m. Accepted terminal completion removes no queried
prefix constraints and cannot justify dropping them.

This analysis makes no lower-bound claim for schedules with zero steps.
The accepted predecessor handles those schedules without identifying
duplicated formal gradients. Any future unrestricted conclusion would
still have to retain both zero-step and arbitrarily-large-step cases.

All new mathematics in this file remains a proof candidate. The exact
minimax/profile target remains unresolved, and the fact graph is unchanged.

## 9. Reproducibility of the algebraic audit

One fixed rational substitution in the six-variable identity (9) was
checked by polarization of its 21 coefficients, and the four rational
constant margins in Sections 5-6 were checked exactly. These were tiny
arithmetic checks, not optimization or a numerical search. The displayed
expansions and inequalities are the proof; the checks are not premises.

For architecture context, the exact local text of Altschuler--Parrilo,
arXiv:2309.16530, Theorem 3.4 and its helper-lemma proof was reread. No
theorem from that paper is imported; its undeformed recursion motivated
the array, whose modified full identity is proved in Section 3.
