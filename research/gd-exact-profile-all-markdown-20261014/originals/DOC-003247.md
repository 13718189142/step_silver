# Saturated-cut certificates retain a signed terminal-interpolation credit

Status: self-contained candidate, NOT verifier-accepted. No solver or
fact_submit was used. The full initial-distance objective theorem is not
being restricted here: the statement concerns the explicitly specified
intrinsic primitive energy. The only accepted background used is the
necessity of the interpolation inequalities in fact 1252b8823bb5a290.

## 1. Definitions and an exact identity

Work at smoothness one. On a GD trajectory for a list h of sum s and
capacity C=1+s, write g_i=grad f(x_i), f_i=f(x_i), and

  Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2 <= 0,
  V_h=2 sum_(i<n) h_i(f_i-f_n)
          +sum_(i<n) h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-sC||g_n||^2.

Take two lists a,b of capacities A,D, respectively, and set

  T=A+D+r,  r>0,  rT=AD,
  kappa=(T/D)^2=(A/r)^2,
  theta=1-1/kappa.

In particular 0<r<min(A,D), kappa>1 and 0<theta<1. The joined
schedule is h=(a,1+r,b). Denote its bridge query by L and the tail
queries by j=0,...,m; z=m is the final query. Tail indices in Q always
mean the corresponding original joined indices. Define

  J_b=sum_(j<m) b_j Q_(j,z),
  E_theta(b)=V_b-theta J_b,
  C_cross=sum_(j<m) b_j Q_(j,L)+Q_(z,L)+r Q_(L,z).

The exact identity, including every value term, is

  V_h = V_a + kappa E_theta(b) - (kappa-1) C_cross.       (1)

All coefficients in -C_cross are nonnegative on actual inequalities
-Q. Thus universal nonnegativity of V_a and E_theta(b) is a sufficient
condition for universal nonnegativity of V_h. It is not necessary here
to assume V_b>=0. Whether this genuinely enlarges the allowable tail
class is a separate question.

Here is a direct audit of (1). Put p=g_L, q=g_z, and
H=sum_(j<m)b_j g_j. Then x_tail,0=x_L-(1+r)p and x_z=x_tail,0-H.
For C=C_cross+J_b the value part is

  C_f=2sum_(j<m)b_j f_j+(r-D)f_L+(2-D-r)f_z.

Expand each Q and use
2sum_j b_j<g_j,sum_(i<j)b_i g_i>=||H||^2-sum_j b_j^2||g_j||^2.
The gradient part reduces exactly to

  C_g=sum_(j<m)b_j(1-b_j)||g_j||^2
            +[(D-r)/2-r^2]||p||^2+(D+r)||q||^2/2.

All mixed p,H,q products cancel. The identities

  (kappa-1)(D-r)=2(A+r),
  kappa r^2=A^2,  kappa D^2=T^2

then show V_h=V_a+kappa V_b-(kappa-1)C coefficient by coefficient:
the f_L coefficient is 2(1+r), the f_z coefficient is -2(T-1),
the p-square coefficient is -r(1+r), and the q-square coefficient
is -T(T-1). Substituting kappa theta=kappa-1 proves (1).

## 2. Full intrinsic certificates force the bridge coefficients

For this part assume the entries of a and b are strictly positive;
either list may be empty. Suppose an explicit intrinsic certificate is
given:

  M_h=V_h+sum_(i!=j)lambda_ij Q_ij,
  lambda_ij>=0,

where values cancel and M_h is PSD in the full independent formal
gradient vector. Allowing the optimum index and x_0 initially does not
enlarge this class. Indeed V_h has no x_0-square coefficient, so PSD
forces the x_0 row to vanish. Its cross coefficients are -lambda_i,star,
which forces every lambda_i,star=0. Summing the value-flow equalities
then gives sum_i lambda_star,i=0, so these multipliers also vanish.
This argument relies on the absence of an initial-distance term and
does NOT apply to a full objective certificate.

There are two explicit equality trajectories for V_h. A scalar Huber
trajectory staying on one affine ray has every gradient equal to 1,
all finite Q zero, and V_h=0. A second trajectory uses the derivative

  f'(x)=min(1,max(x,-eta)),  eta=r/D,

starting at A. The prefix gradient is 1, its last query is 1, the
bridge lands at -r, and the tail gradient is -eta until its endpoint
-eta. Every incoming optimum residual is zero. Distance telescoping
gives V_h=A^2-T^2 eta^2=0.

For completeness, if R_i is a prefix query's remaining prefix sum
and R_j is a tail query's remaining tail sum, this second trajectory
has

  Q_(prefix i,tail j)=-(1+eta)eta R_j,
  Q_(tail j,prefix i)=-(1+eta)R_i.                       (2)

Every within-block Q is zero. Since M_h>=0 and all lambda Q<=0,
equality of V_h forces multiplier complementarity and M_h to vanish
on each equality gradient vector. Those vectors span the two block
indicators e_A,e_B, so both indicators lie in the kernel of M_h.

By (2), the only potentially nonzero cross multipliers are

  nu_i=lambda_(prefix i,z),  mu_j=lambda_(tail j,L).

For a prefix index i<L, the corresponding row of M_h e_B is
-D nu_i/2. Thus nu_i=0 for every i<L. Write nu=nu_L.
For a nonterminal tail index j, the corresponding row of M_h e_A
is [r mu_j-nu b_j]/2; at z it is [r mu_z-nu]/2.
Consequently

  mu_j=(nu/r)b_j  (j<m),  mu_z=nu/r.

The sum of the prefix value-flow equations is
sum_j mu_j-nu=2(A+r). Therefore all cross multipliers are fixed:

  lambda_(j,L)=(kappa-1)b_j (j<m),
  lambda_(z,L)=kappa-1,
  lambda_(L,z)=r(kappa-1),                              (3)

and every other cross multiplier is zero. The mixed gradient block
of the full matrix vanishes, not merely its value on equality data.

## 3. Exact cone interface and the sign that cannot be discarded

Let lambda^A,lambda^B be the two within-block multiplier arrays of
the given full certificate. Define a signed tail array by

  lambda_hat_ij=lambda^B_ij/kappa
                 -theta b_i * 1_(i<m,j=z).             (4)

Combining (1) with (3) yields the full block-diagonal identity

  M_h = M_a + kappa M_b,
  M_a=V_a+sum lambda^A_ij Q_ij,
  M_b=V_b+sum lambda_hat_ij Q_ij.                       (5)

Each summand has its own value cancellation, since its values are in
a disjoint block. Full PSD is equivalent to PSD of both summands.
The prefix multipliers are nonnegative. The exact tail constraints are

  lambda_hat_ij>=0 except possibly i<m,j=z,
  lambda_hat_(i,z)>=-theta b_i.                        (6)

Conversely, any prefix intrinsic certificate and any PSD tail identity
with the value cancellation and bounds (6) give a nonnegative full
certificate by (3),(4). Equivalently, the tail has an ordinary
nonnegative certificate for E_theta(b), not necessarily for V_b.

Thus direct restriction to an ordinary primitive tail certificate would
require the stronger bounds

  lambda^B_(i,z)>=(kappa-1)b_i.                        (7)

They are not implied for each full certificate. The next exact example
proves this, including full nonnegativity and PSD.

## 4. Exact full certificate violating the proposed coefficient floor

Use the empty prefix A=1, tail b=(1,1) of capacity D=3, and

  r=sqrt(7)-2, T=sqrt(7)+2, kappa=(11+4sqrt(7))/9.

The full schedule is h=(sqrt(7)-1,1,1). Use original indices 0,1,2,3,
where 0 is the bridge query and 1,2,3 are the tail queries. Set

  lambda_10=lambda_20=lambda_30=kappa-1,
  lambda_03=r(kappa-1),
  lambda_12=5kappa/2, lambda_21=17kappa/4,
  lambda_23=37kappa/4-1, lambda_32=12kappa,
  lambda_13=3kappa/4-1,

with every unlisted multiplier zero. All are nonnegative, since
kappa>4/3. Direct substitution, or (1) and the audited tail identity
below, gives

  V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij
   =kappa { ||(g_1-g_2)-(g_2-g_3)/8||^2
                         +(255/64)||g_2-g_3||^2 } >=0. (8)

For the tail alone, write u=g_0-g_1 and v=g_1-g_2 using local indices.
Its signed multiplier list is

  lambda_hat_01=5/2, lambda_hat_10=17/4,
  lambda_hat_12=33/4, lambda_hat_21=12,
  lambda_hat_02=-1/4, lambda_hat_20=0.

The value coefficients cancel, and expansion gives

  V_(1,1)+sum lambda_hat_ij Q_ij
      =||u||^2-<u,v>/4+4||v||^2
      =||u-v/8||^2+(255/64)||v||^2.

Nevertheless lambda_13=(kappa-1)-kappa/4 is strictly below the
proposed floor kappa-1. The restricted terminal coefficient is -1/4.
This refutes the inference of (7) from full nonnegativity and PSD.

There IS another ordinary primitive certificate for this tail:
lambda_01=2,lambda_10=4,lambda_12=8,lambda_21=12, all other
multipliers zero, gives ||u||^2+4||v||^2. Accordingly this example
does not disprove existence of a suitable re-selection, arbitrary-tail
heredity, or the original asymptotic minimax problem. It identifies
the exact missing sign assertion rather than replacing it with PSD.

## 5. A particular nonprimitive tail cannot exploit this credit

The previously considered b=(17/10,2,6/5) fails even E_theta for
every 0<=theta<=1. Here is a self-contained witness.
Put m=7/34, ell=21/85. Define the continuous derivative

  f'(x)=x                  for x<=m,
         m                 for m<=x<=m+ell,
         x-ell             for x>=m+ell,

and integrate with f(0)=0. Its derivative is nondecreasing and
1-Lipschitz, so f is convex differentiable 1-smooth with minimizer 0.
Starting at x_0=106/85 gives

  (x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3)=(106/85,-77/170,77/170,7/34),
  (g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3)=(1,-77/170,7/34,7/34).

The endpoint is core: x_3=g_3=m, f_3=m^2/2. Define
I_i=<g_i,x_i>-f_i-||g_i||^2/2. Direct scalar evaluation gives

  I_0=ell(1-m)=567/2890, I_1=I_2=I_3=0,
  sum_(i<3)b_i I_i=567/1700,
  V_b=-13581/23120.

At a core endpoint, expansion gives Q_(i,3)=-I_i. Thus

  E_theta(b)=-13581/23120+theta*567/1700
           <= -29349/115600 <0.                       (9)

The value of V_b can also be checked without integrating every term:
distance telescoping gives
V_b=x_0^2-D^2g_3^2-2sum b_i I_i, with D=59/10.
This proves (9) exactly.

Consequently this specific tail cannot acquire a valid E_theta
certificate at any saturated join parameter. A rank-one coefficient
matching attempt on this same fixed tail also failed, but (9) is the
stronger actual-function obstruction. Neither statement excludes other
nonprimitive tails. No numerical schedule screen was reopened.

## 6. Complete two-bridge identity with retained earlier anchors

The identity also provides a closed, explicitly signed value state
through two bridges. Let a_1,a_2 be any nonnegative physical prefix
lists of capacities A_1,A_2. Starting with the physical tail b of
capacity D_0, define

  H_1=(a_1,1+r_1,b), D_1=A_1+D_0+r_1, r_1 D_1=A_1 D_0,
  H_2=(a_2,1+r_2,H_1), D_2=A_2+D_1+r_2, r_2 D_2=A_2 D_1,
  kappa_1=(D_1/D_0)^2, kappa_2=(D_2/D_1)^2,
  K=kappa_1 kappa_2=(D_2/D_0)^2, theta_2=1-1/K.

Let L_1,L_2 be the respective bridge query indices in the final
original indexing and z the final query. For each physical step in
a_1 or a_2, its gradient/value index is also its original index in H_2.
Define the following sums of actual inequalities, without collapsing
any old query or replacing it by a new anchor:

  C_1=sum_(j in b steps)b_j Q_(j,L_1)+Q_(z,L_1)+r_1 Q_(L_1,z),
  C_2=sum_(i in H_1 steps)(H_1)_i Q_(i,L_2)
                                      +Q_(z,L_2)+r_2 Q_(L_2,z),
  J_left1=sum_(i in a_1 steps)(a_1)_i Q_(i,z)
                                      +(1+r_1)Q_(L_1,z).

Then the complete identity is

  V_(H_2)=V_(a_2)+kappa_2 V_(a_1)+K E_theta2(b)
       -kappa_2(kappa_1-1)C_1-(kappa_2-1)C_2
       -(kappa_2-1)J_left1.                            (10)

Every coefficient displayed on -Q is nonnegative. In particular the
earlier a_1 queries still have their actual outgoing edges to z, and
every query inside H_1 has its actual edge to L_2. These are not removed
by the unqueried-terminal theorem.

To verify (10) algebraically, apply the audited expansion in Section 1
at each cut. The weighted incoming-terminal sum for H_1 is exactly
J_b+J_left1. Substitution gives a J_b coefficient
kappa_2(kappa_1-1)+(kappa_2-1)=K-1=K theta_2; all other terms are
those displayed in (10). This derivation is part of this same candidate
proof, not an invocation of an unverified finding as a theorem.

Consequently, if explicit nonnegative intrinsic certificates are given
for a_1 and a_2, and an explicit nonnegative certificate is given for
E_theta2(b), (10) assembles an intrinsic certificate for H_2. Its PSD
remainder is the sum of the three input PSD remainders with scales
1,kappa_2,K. All original actual multipliers are sums of nonnegative
numbers. No positive remainder is presumed for a benchmark block.

## 7. Final objective certificate, including initial distance

Here is the exact final conversion for any intrinsic certificate
produced by (10), and in particular for the example below. Write H=H_2,
let its terminal query be n, its capacity be D=1+sum_(i<n)H_i, and let

  M_H=V_H+sum_(i!=j<=n)lambda_ij Q_ij >=0

be the assembled PSD identity, with all lambda_ij>=0. Define

  t=(-1+sqrt(8D+1))/4, beta=1+t,
  gamma=1/(2t+1), q=gamma^2,
  x_(n+1)=x_n-beta g_n.

The arithmetic identities are t=gamma D, 1-gamma=2qD, and
q=1/[1+2(sum H_i+beta)]. The accepted terminal-square parameterization
in fact 1252b8823bb5a290(c) applies with terminal probabilities
alpha_star=gamma, alpha_n=1-gamma, and all other alpha_i=0.
Give the queried prefix the following additional optimum multipliers:

  lambda_pre_(i,star)=2q H_i  (i<n),
  lambda_pre_(n,star)=2q(D+1),

and multiply every intrinsic lambda_ij by q. All these coefficients
are nonnegative and their flows are exactly the required 2alpha_i.
There are no optimum-outgoing multipliers in this particular
constructed certificate; none is dropped from an arbitrary certificate.
The full identity supplied by the accepted parameterization is

  q||x_0||^2-2f_(n+1)
    +q sum_(i!=j<=n)lambda_ij Q_ij
    +2q sum_(i<n)H_i Q_(i,star)+2q(D+1)Q_(n,star)
    +2(1-gamma)Q_(n+1,n)+2gamma Q_(n+1,star)
  =||g_(n+1)-[gamma x_n-(t+gamma)g_n]||^2+q M_H.       (11)

The optimum is translated to x_star=0,f_star=0 in this display.
It follows that W_(n+1)(H,beta)<=q. The factor on ||x_0||^2 is retained,
so this is a certificate in the original objective normalization.

To audit its application rather than re-prove the accepted component,
the prefix remainder of that component is

  R=q||x_0||^2-2(1-gamma)(f_n-||g_n||^2/2)
       -||gamma x_n-(t+gamma)g_n||^2
       +2q sum_(i<n)H_i Q_(i,star)+2q(D+1)Q_(n,star)
       +q sum lambda_ij Q_ij.

Using t=gamma D and 1-gamma=2qD, its mixed <x_n,g_n> coefficient
cancels. Its f_n and ||g_n||^2 coefficients are respectively
-2q(D-1) and -qD(D-1). Distance telescoping then gives R=q M_H,
which is exactly (11). Every queried-prefix inequality is retained.

## 8. A full two-bridge calibration and its exact scope

Take b=(1,1), and take both a_1,a_2 empty. Then

  D_0=3, D_1=2+sqrt(7), r_1=sqrt(7)-2,
  D_2=[1+D_1+sqrt((1+D_1)^2+4D_1)]/2, r_2=D_2-1-D_1.

Thus H_2=(1+r_2,sqrt(7)-1,1,1). For E_theta2(b), use the signed
tail coefficients in Section 4 and add theta_2 b_i to each incoming
terminal coefficient. The only initially negative coefficient becomes
theta_2-1/4>0: already kappa_1>4/3 and K>kappa_1. All other
coefficients are nonnegative. The PSD remainder is
||u-v/8||^2+(255/64)||v||^2, because the added interpolation terms
exactly cancel the -theta_2 J_b term in E_theta2.

Equations (10),(11) therefore give a completely explicit certificate
for this five-step objective schedule after appending beta. This is a
calibration of signed coordinates with nonnegative actual multipliers,
not an improved asymptotic schedule. In particular, the physical tail
capacity 3 is already smaller than that of the legal two-step primitive
schedule (3/2,3/2), whose capacity is 4. The old tail also has an ordinary
primitive certificate, so this example does not establish strict
enlargement by the E_theta state.

The comparator's primitive property has a direct identity, so no
unverified external premise is needed: at h=(3/2,3/2), take
lambda_01=1,lambda_10=7/2,lambda_12=17/2,lambda_21=14,
lambda_02=1/2,lambda_20=1. Expanding gives V_h+sum lambda_ij Q_ij=0.
All six multipliers are positive. Replacing the capacity-3 tail by this
capacity-4 tail in the same two saturated joins strictly increases both
successive capacities, since the positive root
Omega(A,D)=[A+D+sqrt((A+D)^2+4AD)]/2 is increasing in D for A,D>0.
The terminal q in (11) strictly decreases. The displayed calibration
is therefore strictly dominated already at its own finite horizon.

The unresolved bridge toward an improvement is concrete: find an actual
tail with a nonnegative certificate for E_theta at some theta<1 and with
a capacity gain large enough to pay for the physical prefix lengths.
Neither the existence of that tail nor the required infinite-horizon
comparison with U_n^ZJ is asserted here.
