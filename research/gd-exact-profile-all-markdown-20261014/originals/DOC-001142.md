# Exact one-bridge defect lifting and the retained slack penalty

Status: complete candidate interface, NOT verified. No fact_submit or numerical
search. The proof uses only accepted interpolation fact1252b8823bb5a290 and
the direct calculations below, not earlier unverified findings as premises.

## Definitions

Let h have capacity K=1+sum h_i. On a legal normalized GD trajectory with
minimizer0 define

    I_i_star=-Q_(i,star)=<g_i,x_i>-f_i-||g_i||^2/2>=0,
    delta_end=f_n-||g_n||^2/2>=0,
    y_end=x_n-g_n,
    V_h=2sum h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2
                           -K(K-1)||g_n||^2.

The squared-distance telescope gives

    V_h=||x0||^2-||xn||^2-2sum h_i I_i_star
                         -2(K-1)f_n-K(K-1)||g_n||^2.             (1)

Call the endpoint core when x_n=g_n. In that case interpolation to/from0
forces f_n=||g_n||^2/2, and (1) reduces to

    V_h=||x0||^2-K^2||g_n||^2-2sum h_i I_i_star.                (2)

All sample data and the initial-distance term are retained.

## A. Appending at a saturated outer cut preserves a core-ended defect

Let b be any nonnegative schedule of capacity D with an actual legal
trajectory having endpoint x_m=g_m. Its dimension and all earlier values,
points, gradients and optimum interpolation slacks are arbitrary.
Let c be any nonnegative block of capacity C, and r_ext>0 satisfy

    T_tot=D+C+r_ext, r_ext T_tot=DC.                            (3)

If V_b<0 on that trajectory, then (b,1+r_ext,c) is not primitive.

Proof. Use a scalar base trajectory whose derivative is clip(x,-r_ext/C,1),
starting at D. The entire b block stays on its positive plateau and ends
at1; the bridge lands at-r_ext; c remains on its negative plateau and
ends at-r_ext/C. The base energy is D^2-T_tot^2(r_ext/C)^2=0 by(3).

For the auxiliary vector array retain EVERY supplied b sample. Write
e=g_m=x_m and append the bridge, which lands at-r_ext e. On c set the
constant gradient v_end=-(r_ext/C)e, so its initial point is C v_end
and its final point v_end. Assign each appended value
f_i=<v_end,x_i>-||v_end||^2/2. This auxiliary array may violate old/new
cross interpolation and is not claimed to be legal by itself.

Combine the base and auxiliary arrays in orthogonal coordinates with a
positive multiplier epsilon_mix on every auxiliary squared norm and value.
All base-zero constraints remain valid under this mixture:

* Within the old b samples, the auxiliary data are the original legal data.
* Within the new c samples, the auxiliary gradient is constant.
* A new-to-old base constraint can be zero only at an old query with
  zero remaining b steps, hence at its endpoint x_m=e. The auxiliary
  Q_(new,m) is zero by direct expansion using x_m=g_m=e and f_m=||e||^2/2.
* An old-to-new base constraint can be zero only at a new query with zero
  remaining c steps, hence at x=v_end=g. Direct expansion gives
  Q_(old,new)=Q_(old,star)<=0.
* Every auxiliary optimum constraint is valid: old ones by hypothesis;
  appended incoming ones are zero and outgoing ones are
  -R_i||v_end||^2<=0, where R_i is the remaining c stepsize sum.

Zero steps create only the endpoint duplicates already covered above.
All other base cross constraints are strictly negative. If E_bad is the
finite set of pairs with Q_aux>0, choose

    epsilon_mix=min(1, min_(ij in E_bad) [-Q_base,ij/(2Q_aux,ij)]),

interpreting the inner minimum as1 for an empty set. Every pair in E_bad
has Q_base<0 by the preceding audit, so epsilon_mix>0. The combined data
obey all original-index interpolation inequalities. The explicit conjugate
construction in fact1252b8823bb5a290 interpolates them by one fixed legal
convex differentiable 1-smooth function with minimizer0.

The auxiliary core endpoint permits (2). No new I_i_star is added, and
T_tot^2||v_end||^2=D^2||e||^2 by(3). Therefore

    V_aux=V_b, V_mix=epsilon_mix V_b<0.                         (4)

This is a true defect inheritance rule for the displayed core-ended
witness class. It does not assert that every failure of primitive validity
admits such a witness.

## B. Prepending has an explicit nonnegative retained-slack penalty

Let b now be any nonnegative schedule of capacity D and any actual legal
trajectory (z_j,g_j,f_j) with minimizer0. Let a have capacity A and let
r_ext>0 satisfy

    T_tot=A+D+r_ext, r_ext T_tot=AD,
    kappa_lift=(T_tot/D)^2>1.

Put p_aux=-z_0/r_ext. Before the supplied b trajectory insert constant
gradient p_aux on a, starting at A p_aux and ending at p_aux, then the
bridge1+r_ext. Its next point is -r_ext p_aux=z_0, exactly the supplied
tail start. On the inserted points use f=<p_aux,x>-||p_aux||^2/2.

The scalar base is clip(x,-r_ext/D,1), starting at A; its primitive energy
is zero by the saturated-cut identity. The auxiliary old/new data can be
mixed with this base exactly as in partA, PROVIDED

    <p_aux,y_end><=delta_end.                                  (5)

For completeness, the active cross checks differ from partA as follows.
Tail-to-inserted-end constraints have Q_aux=Q_(tail,star)<=0 because the
inserted endpoint has x=g=p_aux and f=||p_aux||^2/2. Inserted-to-tail-end
constraints have the common value <p_aux,y_end>-delta_end, which is
nonpositive exactly by(5). Every other possibly positive cross constraint
has strictly negative base slack. The same finite positive minimum
therefore gives a legal mixture, including all zero-step duplicates and
all optimum constraints. This proves interpolation, not negativity yet.

Let Sigma_I=sum_(j<m) b_j I_j_star be the weighted ACTUAL tail optimum
interpolation slack. Applying (1) separately to the tail and auxiliary
full array, and using A^2/r_ext^2=kappa_lift, gives the exact identity

    V_aux=kappa_lift V_b+Penalty,

    Penalty=(kappa_lift-1)
                  [||y_end||^2+2 I_m_star+2 Sigma_I]
              +[2T_tot(T_tot-D)/D] delta_end.                  (6)

Every term of Penalty is nonnegative. Here
I_m_star=<g_m,y_end>-delta_end; substitution of
z_m=g_m+y_end into (1) verifies (6) term by term. In particular, a negative
tail V_b alone does NOT imply V_aux<0. A valid defect-lifting certificate
must keep this complete penalty and require kappa_lift V_b+Penalty<0.

At a core endpoint, (5) is automatic and delta_end=I_m_star=0, so

    V_aux=kappa_lift V_b+2(kappa_lift-1)Sigma_I.                (7)

When additionally every weighted incoming optimum slack vanishes,
Sigma_I=0, the negative energy scales exactly by kappa_lift. The new
two-cut Huber construction is in this special class. Discarding Sigma_I
outside it would repeat the same wrong-sign loss of value information
that obstructed the earlier mixed-moment state.

## C. The exact criterion for moving the minimum to the final proximal point

There is a precise normalization test; a reset cannot be presumed. For any
finite fully legal transcript without fixing a particular optimum, define
delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2 for every sample, including the terminal one. Add
the proposed optimum jet

    x_star_new=x_n-g_n, g_star_new=0, f_star_new=delta_n.

Keeping ALL original samples, a convex 1-smooth interpolant with this new
optimum exists if and only if

    delta_n<=delta_i for every i.                              (8)

Necessity and sufficiency are both explicit. The new interpolation edges
are Q_(i,star_new)=Q_(i,n)<=0 and
Q_(star_new,i)=delta_n-delta_i. The former already hold in the full
transcript; the latter are exactly(8). When they all hold, translate the
new optimum to(0,0,0) and use the finite interpolation construction of
fact1252b8823bb5a290. Translation changes no GD displacement or primitive
energy and makes x_n=g_n. This supplies a core-ended witness with exactly
the original V_h. When (8) fails, such a preserved-data normalization is
impossible.

For a concrete failure, f(x)=x^2/4, h=(5), x0=1 has
x1=-3/2, g0=1/2, g1=-3/4, f0=1/4, f1=9/16. Thus
delta0=1/8 and delta1=9/32. The proposed new optimum is at-3/4 with
value9/32, and its edge to sample0 violates interpolation by
delta1-delta0=5/32>0. The transcript has V_h=-25<0, so even a genuinely
negative primitive-energy example need not admit this particular
normalization. This does NOT exclude a different core-ended counterexample
for the same schedule; indeed this one-step schedule has others.

## Scope and useful next test

These interfaces give necessary inheritance rules at saturated primitive
cuts, and an exact penalty away from the special all-tight tail class.
They retain all actual prefix interpolation and an explicit fixed-function
realization. They neither provide an improved objective certificate nor
prove that arbitrary primitive failures have core-ended witnesses. The
preserved-data normalization is now exactly characterized by(8), and can
fail on a negative-energy instance. Existence of some OTHER core-ended
witness for every nonprimitive schedule is still a separate closure claim;
no all-schedule induction may assume it.
