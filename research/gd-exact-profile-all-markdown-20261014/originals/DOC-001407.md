# Fixed-two-plane return debt and inherited-hierarchy failure

This is an exact scoped failure of a proposed THIRD repair, not a third
attained construction and not a general impossibility theorem.

Suppose ANY fixed max-affine function has two DISTINCT slopes q,r with
independently fixed offsets o_q,o_r. Other planes may be present.
Assume actual queries realize: a q-stage ending at proximal point y;
a bridge with excess D>=0; an r-stage of ordinary mass M2-1, ending
at z=y-Dq-M2r; a bridge with excess C>=0; and a RETURNED q-stage
of ordinary mass M3-1, ending at w=z-Cr-M3q. Here M2,M3>=1.
These are actual physical updates, not reset states. Heights at y,z,w
may be arbitrary positive values, and no endpoint is assumed minimal.
The named q-plane and r-plane must actually be active at their respective
queries; representing q as a mixture of unrelated active slopes is outside
this specific fixed-two-plane face policy.

Put s=<q,r>, alpha=||q||^2-s, beta=||r||^2-s, and
ell(u)=<q-r,u>+o_q-o_r. The q/r active-plane conditions demand

    ell(y)>=0,      ell(z)<=0,       ell(w)>=0,
    ell(z)=ell(y)-D*alpha+M2*beta,
    ell(w)=ell(z)+C*beta-M3*alpha.                       (15)

In any nontrivial feasible q-r-q path alpha,beta MUST be positive.
If alpha<=0, then beta>0 because alpha+beta=||q-r||^2>0;
ell(z)>=ell(y)+M2*beta>0 contradicts its r guard. If beta<=0,
alpha>0 and ell(w)<=ell(z)-M3*alpha<0 contradicts its q guard.
Thus the first two guards in(15) give beta/alpha<=D/M2, and the last
two give beta/alpha>=M3/C. If C=0 they directly contradict one another.
Consequently every such fixed-two-plane return NECESSARILY obeys

    D*C >= M2*M3.                                      (16)

Offsets, positive affine heights, minimum-level translations or subsequent
terminal completions cannot repair a violation: they cancel from these
ORIGINAL preterminal active-plane comparisons. Other planes only add
guards. The failure already precedes any smoothness or radius issue.

Apply this to the declared first-occurrence physical prefix

    (H_j,b_j,H_j,b_(j+1),H_(j+1),b_(j+2),
        H_(j+2),b_(j+3)),                               (17)

using ONE enclosing m>=7, j+3<m, and tau=1/4 throughout. Define
lambda=1+sqrt2, beta_h=1-1/(4m), c_h=lambda*(1+1/(4m)),
a_h=beta_h/c_h, H_0=(), H_(l+1)=(H_l,1+a_h*c_h^l,H_l),
S_l=sum H_l, b_l=1+a_h*c_h^l. Induction on the mass identity gives

    1+S_l=v_h*c_h^l+(1-v_h)*2^l,  v_h=a_h/(c_h-2) in(0,1).

Indeed c_h-2>1/lambda>a_h, c_h<3. For the q-r-q assignment in(17),
D=a_h*c_h^(j+1), C=a_h*c_h^(j+2),
M2=1+S_(j+1)>v_h*c_h^(j+1),
M3=1+S_(j+2)>v_h*c_h^(j+2). Therefore

    D*C/(M2*M3)<(a_h/v_h)^2=(c_h-2)^2<1.

This contradicts(16), even with independent affine offsets and POSITIVE
endpoint heights. Thus this specific first-occurrence two-slope-return
repair cannot be instantiated on the actual inherited hierarchy. The
explicit return Law2 is legal because its declared physical data instead
give D*C/(M2*M3)=4. These two statements concern different physical
words; no ratio is silently substituted or reset.

This does not exclude interleaved visits with changing gradients within
a face/edge, more than two returning slopes, nonlinear curved regions,
different physical checkpoint selections, or other full smooth functions.
It does not give an upper bound on W_n(H_m). It is useful because it
identifies a debt unaffected by the newly proposed affine-height freedom.
