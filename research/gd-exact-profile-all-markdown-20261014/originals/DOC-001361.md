## 1. Scope, accepted inputs, and the changed law

The former T1/T2 laws held the gradient constant inside each original H2
half. The law here replaces that assumption by affine projection motion
on an exposed segment: the tangent component changes at every physical
query. A remote support condition obstructs an orthogonal-face version.
An actual triangle then realizes the correct face transition, with every
subsequent physical query given explicitly. The latter construction is
legal for every enclosing integer m>=100, but its entire specified
family is quantitatively weaker than the complete same-word references.

The accepted mathematical inputs are:

  interpolation inequality, including the optimum; its proof also gives
  the normalization used here.

  benchmark estimate B_n<=1+4n^(log_2 3).
* fact1fca3e5dd555cb4a: the complete alternating-ray maximum F_ray_n,
  with K_n<=F_ray_n.
* factdfde2fa326c908bf: the FULL schedule-dependent complex cycle

    (ell,1+beta,ell,1+b,ell,1+beta,ell),
    ell=1+a, b=beta*c.

The following are local scalars, denoted A48,eta48,b48,k48,r48,chi48,

    (p0,p1,p2,p3)=(0,ell,ell+1+beta,A-1),

For any integer i>=0, s2(i) is its number of binary1 digits, with

## 4. Exact moving-face H2 block and its remote obstruction

Suppose q is a face normal, w perpendicular to q, and the four proposed
projections q+t_i w all lie in that face. Put

    x_i=(A-p_i)q+t_i w,
    g_i=q+t_i w,
    y_i=x_i-g_i=(A-1-p_i)q,       0<=i<=3.                 (6)

Since t_(i+1)=(1-h_i)t_i for the three actual H2 steps, subtracting
h_i*g_i in(6) gives x_(i+1). The gradient changes at every one of these
steps whenever w!=0. Every normal reserve A-1-p_i is nonnegative, and
y3=0. Thus, if the full support conditions hold, these are actual
projections throughout the block, with the final core reset

    x3=g3=q-eta*w.                                        (7)

This replaces constant gradients by tangent reflection and contraction;
it does not replace H2 by one fictitious large step.

Now consider TWO such consecutive original H2 blocks, with respective
normal/tangent pairs (q0,w0) and(q1,w1), each ending in a core reset.
The intervening original step is1+b, so exact GD requires

    -b(q0-eta*w0)=A*q1+w1,  <qj,wj>=0.                   (8)

The putative gradients at the original indices0 and4 are q0+w0 and
q1+w1. The residual at4 is (A-1)q1. Dotting(8) with q1 gives

    <q1,w0>=A||q1||^2/(b*eta)+<q1,q0>/eta.

Hence the ORIGINAL directed interpolation residual for the natural
projection values f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2 is

 Q_(0,4)=(A-1)[(1+1/eta)<q0,q1>
                         +(A/(b*eta)-1)||q1||^2].        (9)

To check the convention, direct expansion of these values gives
Q_ij=<g_i-g_j,y_j>, where Q_ij is exactly the signed residual in
accepted1252b8823bb5a290.

If q0 and q1 are orthogonal and q1!=0, then

    Q_(0,4)=(A-1)(k-1)||q1||^2>0.                        (10)

Thus this full-dimensional orthogonal-normal family fails a retained
original row. Tangent directions and additional dimensions are arbitrary.
No bound on their norms repairs(10). The zero q1 exception is a quadratic
core block, rather than a nonzero exposed-face normal.

More generally a necessary condition is

    <q0,q1><=-(A-b*eta)/(b(1+eta))*||q1||^2
             <-(7/4)||q1||^2,       q1!=0.               (11)

Indeed A-b*eta>23/4-5/12=16/3 and
b(1+eta)<(5/2)(1+25/144)=845/288. Their ratio exceeds
1536/845>7/4. This is a necessary condition on two particular
core-reset blocks separated by the low bridge; it is not a general
angle obstruction for arbitrary projection trajectories.

Arbitrary function values do not repair this particular geometry when
both core resets and the stated optimum are retained. At a core sample
x=g, the two optimum inequalities in1252b8823bb5a290 force
f=||g||^2/2. For any two samples within one block, (6) gives

    <g_i-g_j,x_i-x_j>=||g_i-g_j||^2.

Thus Q_ij+Q_ji=0 independently of the values. If both inequalities are
nonpositive, both are equalities. Their differences, together with the
value forced at the block's core sample, uniquely determine
f_i=<x_i,g_i>-||g_i||^2/2 at every sample of that block. Apply this to
the blocks ending at3 and7. Formula(9) is therefore forced for EVERY
value assignment compatible with all interpolation inequalities.

The obstruction is not an adjacent-row failure. With these forced values,
every ordered pair within either individual block has Q=0. Across the
bridge, Q_(4,3)=0 and

    Q_(3,4)=-(A-1)(A/b+1)||q1||^2<0.

All optimum rows have Q_(i,star)=0 and
Q_(star,i)=-(A-1-p_i)||qj||^2<=0 in block j. Thus adjacent and optimum
rows all pass even though(10) is positive.

## 5. One actual triangle, with its actual first face transition

The illegal orthogonal second face suggests the following single static
convex set, for which we follow the TRUE projection instead. In the plane
let

    U=(1,k*r), V=(1,-a*k*r), L=(-b,r),
    C48=conv{U,V,L},
    F48(x)=max_(v in C48)[<x,v>-||v||^2/2],
    X0=(A,k*r).                                          (12)

All three vertices and the starting point are finite explicit vectors
depending only on m. Here L is a vertex label; the smoothness constant
is1 until the final scaling paragraph. The defining maximum is one
fixed function, not a Gram feasibility oracle.

The triangle is the intersection of the three halfspaces

    x <=1,
    -(k-1)r*x+(b+1)y <=(b*k+1)r,
    -(a*k+1)r*x-(b+1)y <=(a*b*k-1)r.                    (13)

The first line is tight at U,V, the second at U,L and the third at V,L.
The third right side is positive: a*b*k=A/(a*beta)>69/5>1 by(3).
All three inequalities are strict at0, so0 is an interior point of C48.

For completeness, a maximizer defining F48 exists by compactness and
is unique by strict concavity. It is the Euclidean projection P_C48(x),
since the objective is ||x||^2/2-||x-v||^2/2. The projection condition is

    <x-g,v-g><=0 for every v in C48.                     (14)

Necessity follows by differentiating squared distance along each segment
from g to v; sufficiency follows by expanding ||x-v||^2. Applying(14)
at x and x' and adding gives

    <x-x',P_C48(x)-P_C48(x')>>=||P_C48(x)-P_C48(x')||^2.

Cauchy--Schwarz proves the projection is1-Lipschitz. Comparing the two
maximizing objectives gives

    0<=F48(x+z)-F48(x)-<P_C48(x),z><=||z||^2.

This proves differentiability with gradient P_C48, as well as convexity
and global1-smoothness. Since0 belongs to the triangle, F48>=0 and
F48(0)=0. If x!=0, an adequately small positive multiple of x belongs
to the triangle's interior and makes <x,v>-||v||^2/2 positive. Therefore

    argmin F48={0}, min F48=0,
    dist(X0,argmin F48)^2=A^2+k^2*r^2.                   (15)

For i=0,1,2,3 define

    Xi=(A-p_i,t_i*k*r), Gi=(1,t_i*k*r).                 (16)

Each Gi lies in the vertical segment UV: the four t_i lie between
-a and1, since eta=a^2*beta<a and 0<a*beta<1. Also

    Xi-Gi=(A-1-p_i)(1,0).

The first halfspace in(13) proves(14) for every vertex and hence every
point of C48. Thus all four Gi are the ACTUAL projections. The three
individual GD updates follow from(2), and the true values are

    F48(Xi)=||Gi||^2/2+(A-1-p_i), 0<=i<=3.              (17)

In particular X3=G3=(1,-A*r/b). The original fourth step1+b gives

    X4=-b*X3=(-b,A*r).                                  (18)

The orthogonal-face guess would prescribe G4=L=(-b,r), with residual
(0,(A-1)r). The old gradient U invalidates its normal condition by

    <U-L,X4-L>=(A-1)(k-1)r^2>0.                        (19)

This is the concrete instance of(10), at the actual physical indices0,4.
We do not use L as the true gradient at4.

Let n_plus=(-(k-1)r,b+1), and D_plus=(b*k+1)r. The aspect ratio in(1)
makes X4 a positive multiple of n_plus. Moreover

    <n_plus,X4>/D_plus
      =[b(k-1)+A(b+1)]/(b*k+1)=chi.

The perpendicular foot on the upper supporting line is therefore

    q=X4/chi.                                           (20)

It lies strictly inside segment LU. Indeed the orthogonal projection
parameter of X4 on its line, measured from L toward U, is

    theta=(A-1)(k-1)r^2/[(b+1)^2+(k-1)^2*r^2],
    0<theta<(A-1)/(k-1)<1.                              (21)

Its residual is a positive multiple of n_plus, so the second halfspace
in(13) proves(14) against the WHOLE triangle, including the remote V.
Thus G4=q is the actual gradient, and

    Y4=X4-G4=(chi-1)q,
    F48(X4)=(chi-1/2)||q||^2.                            (22)

This is a real transition from the vertical exposed segment to the
sloping exposed segment. The two first gradients U,V are noncollinear;
the law has not become a constant-H2 transcript in different notation.

## 6. The exact full trajectory after the transition

The next physical step is h4=ell, and(5),(20) give

    X5=X4-ell*G4=d*q, 0<d<1/8.                         (23)

We prove a sufficiently large segment of the line through q lies inside
the triangle. The point q itself belongs to C48. The point -q/2 also
belongs to C48, by all three original halfspaces(13). For the right
halfspace its horizontal coordinate is

    b/(2chi)<(5/2)/(14/5)=25/28<1.

For the upper halfspace its inner product with n_plus is -D_plus/2.
For the lower halfspace its left side is

    [A(b+1)-b(a*k+1)]r/(2chi)
        <=A(b+1)r/(2chi)<15r/2,

while the right side is (a*b*k-1)r>64r/5. This proves

    [-q/2,q] subset C48.                                 (24)

We retain every physical step in the remaining word. The defining ruler
recursion gives, for 0<=t<n,

    h_t=1+beta*c^(nu2(t+1)-1).

One way to verify the identity is induction on H_j: the center position
t+1=2^j has valuation j, and adding2^j to each right-child position
does not change its smaller valuation. For i>=0,

    sum_(j=1)^i nu2(j)=sum_(ell>=1)floor(i/2^ell)=i-s2(i).

The second equality follows by writing i=sum_l b_l*2^l, with b_l in
{0,1}, and summing b_l*(2^l-1). Therefore the exact physical prefix
product is

    P_i:=product_(t<i)(1-h_t)=(-1)^i beta^i c^(-s2(i)).    (25)

In particular P5=-beta^5*c^(-2), and all these products are nonzero.
For every 5<=i<=n set

    Ti=(-1)^(i-5)*d*beta^(i-5)*c^(2-s2(i)),
    Xi=Gi=Ti*q, F48(Xi)=Ti^2||q||^2/2.                   (26)

For i>=5, s2(i)>=1 and beta^(i-5)<=1, so

    |Ti|<=d*c<5/16<1/2.

Equation(24) proves Xi lies in C48. Its projection is consequently
itself, giving exactly the gradient and value in(26). Equation(25)
then proves Xi+1=(1-h_i)Xi for every i>=5. This establishes(26) by
induction starting from(23); it is not a hypothetical quadratic tail.

All physical queries inside every later H2 block have changing nonzero
gradients: h_i>1 makes the scalar in(26) change sign at each step, and
no factor vanishes. In the first H2, changes arise from tangent motion
on the face; in the later blocks, they arise inside the triangle's
quadratic core. Both coordinate gradients continue changing. Dimension
is exactly2 and is not used as a substitute for the actual radius(15).

## 7. All remote and optimum interpolation rows

Set X_star=G_star=0 and f_star=0, and at every finite index use the
single actual value f_i=<Xi,Gi>-||Gi||^2/2 established in(17),(22),(26).
Direct expansion yields

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<Gi,Xj-Xi>+||Gi-Gj||^2/2
        =<Gi-Gj,Yj>,       Yj=Xj-Gj.                    (27)

Every finite Gi is in the fixed triangle: (16) handles0 through3,
(20)--(21) handle4, and(24)--(26) handle the full remaining word.
For j<=3, Yj is a nonnegative horizontal normal, so the first
halfspace(13) makes(27) nonpositive for EVERY i. For j=4, Y4 is a
positive multiple of n_plus, so the second halfspace does the same.
For j>=5, Yj=0 and every residual with this target index is zero. The optimum gradient0
also lies in the triangle; Y_star=0. Thus both optimum directions and
every remote pair, including both terminal directions, are covered
simultaneously by the same data. No directed row has been dropped.

## 8. Exact terminal gap, true cost, and complete comparisons

Since n=2^m-1, s2(n)=m and n-5 is even. Equations(15),(26) give the
exact true-distance payoff of this actual function:

 V48(m):=2(F48(Xn)-min F48)/dist(X0,argmin F48)^2
       =d^2 beta^(2(n-5)) c^(4-2m)
          *(b^2+A^2*r^2)/[chi^2(A^2+k^2*r^2)].          (28)

Let Qunit(m)=P_n^2=beta^(2n)c^(-2m), the exact payoff of the scalar
unit quadratic on the SAME original word with initial distance1.
Then

    V48(m)/Qunit(m)=d^2*c^4*beta^(-10)*||q||^2/||X0||^2.

Here

    ||q||^2/||X0||^2
      =(b^2+A^2*r^2)/[chi^2(A^2+k^2*r^2)]<1/8.          (29)

To prove(29), its numerator-to-denominator ratio before chi^2 is a
weighted average of (b/A)^2 and(A/k)^2. Both are less than1/4:
b/A<10/23<1/2, and A/k=b*eta<5/12<1/2. Also chi^2>2 by(5).
Bernoulli's inequality, proved by induction from(1-u)^(j+1)
>=(1-ju)(1-u)>=1-(j+1)u whenever its induction lower bound is used,
gives beta^10>=(1-10/m)>=9/10. Consequently

    V48(m)/Qunit(m)
       <(1/64)(625/16)(10/9)(1/8)
       =6250/73728<1/10.                                (30)

All quantities in(28) are positive. This closes the specified balanced
triangle family as a source of improvement, with an absolute margin.

For comparison with the complete K and ray references, put
S=sum H_m and M=1+S. Summing the actual multiplicities gives

    M=2^m+(beta/c)(c^m-2^m)/(c-2)
     =(1-zeta)2^m+zeta*c^m,
    zeta=beta/[c(c-2)] in(0,1).                          (31)

The last inclusion follows from c>lambda and lambda(lambda-2)=1.
Thus M<c^m. It follows that

    (2S+1)*Qunit(m)<2c^m*c^(-2m)=2c^(-m)<1.

The full reference of factb98bb807d514e80d is

 K_n(h)=max{1/(1+2sum h),
            max_j (h_j-1)_+^2/[(1+sum_(t<j)h_t)^2
                                      *(1+2sum_(t>j)h_t)]}.

It includes the canonical Huber term. Fact1fca3e5dd555cb4a takes the
maximum over ALL feasible alternating-ray checkpoint sets, including
zero and one cut, so its complete F_ray_n is at least this full K_n.
Therefore, on exactly this original h and physical n,

    0<V48(m)<Qunit(m)/10
             <1/[10(2S+1)]<=K_n(h)/10<=F_ray_n(h)/10.   (32)

This comparison does not replace either full reference by a chosen cut;
using a lower bound on each complete reference is enough to prove the
displayed domination.

For the full complex channel of factdfde2fa326c908bf, take z=1. Its
cycle quantities satisfy alpha-|z|^2=0 and Im(z)=0, so EVERY cycle
inequality defining the actual D_h holds. Both accepted payoff bounds

    Gap_h(1)=|P_n|^2=Qunit(m),
    0<V48(m)<(1/10)*sup_(z in D_h)Gap_h(z).              (33)

This is the FULL unaugmented complex reference, not just the universal
lens or a short-cycle restriction. The Huber-augmented lens is also
larger than V48(m), by(32), without needing any lens optimization.

Finally use the original all-split benchmark, without substituting a
balanced approximation. Accepted b98bb807d514e80d gives, for p=log_2 3,

    B_n<=1+4n^p<5*3^m.

Combining with(30), beta<1 and c>12/5 proves

                       <(1/2)(25/48)^m ->0.            (34)

The equality of actual and chosen minimizer distances is already proved

## 9. What the balanced choice closes, and what it leaves open

There are two distinct scoped conclusions in this joint draft.

First, ANY two fixed-face H2 blocks with core resets, the intervening
low original bridge and orthogonal nonzero normals fail the original
row(0,4). All adjacent and optimum inequalities passing does not fix
that defect. Equation(11) gives the stronger necessary correlation.
No failure is asserted for arbitrary oblique normals, intermediate
face changes, noncore endpoints, or other conjugate height laws.

Second, the explicit balanced-aspect triangle(12) is globally legal
but its true normalized payoff is less than one tenth of each complete
same-word reference in(32)--(33), and its original-B ratio tends to0.
This conclusion covers exactly the aspect ratio r in(1), arbitrary
positive spatial scaling and orthogonal embeddings of that instance,
and the objective scaling just described. It is not a claim about ALL
triangles, polytopes, or functions with changing gradients.

One useful exact interface survives if the aspect r>0 in(12) is released.
Keep A,b,eta,k fixed, and define the same triangle and X0. All first-block
formulas(16)--(19) remain valid. At4 the true projection is

    g4=L+theta(U-L),
    theta=(A-1)(k-1)r^2/[(b+1)^2+(k-1)^2*r^2].           (35)

This holds for every r>0: 0<theta<1; the residual is perpendicular
to UL, and its dot product with n_plus has the positive sign because
<n_plus,X4>-D_plus=(A-1)(b+1)r>0. Thus the residual is outward and
the remote V also passes. If q is the perpendicular foot of0 on the
upper supporting line and w=g4-q, then

    q perpendicular w, X4=chi*q+w,
    X5=d*q-a*w.                                         (36)

In(36), chi and d are EXACTLY those of(1), independent of the aspect.
The projection at5 cannot lie in the RELATIVE INTERIOR of the old upper
edge: its affine projection would have normal residual(d-1)q, pointing
inward, since d<1/8. A projection in the relative interior of that edge
requires a nonnegative multiple of its outward normal. Equivalently the
first original low step already uses more normal reserve than is
available on this face. This is an explicit normal-cone failure, not
just an inequality on the desired terminal gap. Endpoint projections,
which have larger normal cones, are not excluded by this argument.

For the balanced aspect w=0, (24)--(26) prove persistent core entry.
For a free aspect w need not vanish; the next point may instead hit
another face. No full-word estimate for those true further transitions
is asserted. Such a law must follow the actual projections at queries
inside H2, not hold the current edge for a whole block. This is a
materially different remaining interface from the closed T1/T2 carriers.

The frozen internal-H2 observation has a further qualitative warning:
motion on one fixed face makes BOTH directed Q residuals within that
face zero, as proved in Section4. The saved H6 diagnostics show a
nearly tight forward row and a substantially slack reverse row. Raw
diagnostics are not exact identities, but there is no evidential basis
for imposing a single fixed face throughout each original H2.

An eventual stronger construction on H_m would still leave the original
all-n/all-nonnegative-word lower comparison BLOCKED. That comparison is
a theorem-level requirement, not a routine induction. No such uniform
bridge or unrestricted upper separation is supplied by this draft.
