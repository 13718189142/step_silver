Exact local bottleneck in minimum-reset arc relays. Let one two-dimensional future cell start at a prescribed point p+D a, where p is a GLOBAL minimizer of the final one-envelope function, D>0, and a,b are orthonormal input/output axes. Its intended arc data have initial unscaled state r_0=(epsilon+P,-1-Q) and endpoint normal(epsilon,-1). Scaling by D rho and centering at
z_circ=p+D[1-rho(epsilon+P)]a+D rho(1+Q)b
makes the cell start at p+D a. Suppose its nonsmooth local piece is the nonnegative support maximum
Phi(z)=D rho max_(v in V)<v_a a+v_b b,z-z_circ>,
where V includes0 and(epsilon,-1). A maximum of such pieces can vanish at p only if THIS endpoint plane is nonpositive there. Its value before the positive D rho factor is
D[-epsilon+rho epsilon(epsilon+P)+rho(1+Q)].
Thus necessarily
rho <= 1/[epsilon+P+(1+Q)/epsilon].
This is precisely the large A^arc budget in b70f11135ec8688e. It is invariant under rotating a,b or embedding in higher dimension. Passing from the origin to the true closest minimizer cancels this scale only at the first cell; it does not remove the subsequent requirement that an already visited proximal point be a minimizer.

Concrete failed mutation: align the next incoming axis with minus the preceding endpoint gradient and choose rho=1/(epsilon+P), so each center would advance only in a fresh orthogonal direction. At the preceding minimum point p, the new endpoint plane then has strictly positive value D^2 rho^2(1+Q)>0. Consequently the preceding proximal point ceases to be globally minimizing and its prescribed gradient is no longer certified. This is an ADJACENT future-plane failure, independent of all remote Markov comparisons. Adding rotations alone does not repair it.

To avoid this specific bottleneck, a new model must change at least one hypothesis: the checkpoint's proximal value may be positive (carry genuine function-value levels), the local support geometry may change, or the endpoint normal may change. Replacing the required reciprocal-scale bound by an unproved covariance choice would hide the same missing condition. No claim is made that positive-value constructions are impossible or that this obstructs the unrestricted target.
