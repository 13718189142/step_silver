# Correlated-character repair still needs strict short-step margins

MAIN-AUTHORED UNVERIFIED derivation. Original target OPEN. No program was used.
This is not part of the completed native two-law allocation.

Use the SAME m>=1536 hierarchy, N=2^m, K=N-1-m,
beta=1-1/(4m), a=beta/[(1+sqrt2)(1+1/(4m))].
Let dominant character labels be tau_k=N-2^k,0<=k<m, with weights
b_(tau_k)^2=2^k/(N-1). Give every remaining nonzero character weight
zeta/(N^2 K),0<zeta<=1. All characters remain present. Set
U_s=(b_t chi_t(s))_(t!=0), M=sum b_t^2=1+zeta/N^2,
T=M+R^(-2), w_s=(U_s,1/R), R>0.

If Gamma(i)=i xor floor(i/2), then
chi_(tau_k)(Gamma(i))=(-1)^(bit_k(i)), because the xor sum of Gray bits
k through m-1 telescopes to bit_k(i). No chronology is permuted.
For a flip of bit l the exact marginal energy is

 E_l=(2^(l+1)-1)/(N-1)
       +zeta*(N/2-l-1)/(N^2 K).

The joint energy for bits0 and1 is
 J=1/(N-1)+zeta*(N/4-1)/(N^2 K),
 E_1-2J=1/(N-1).                                    (1)

The improved overlap may fix the old first triple, but it does not repair
the undamped short-step root law. Consider ORIGINAL queries1,2,3, whose
two physical steps are1+beta and1+a and whose Gray bit flips are1 then0.
Let u be the positive adjacent firm-equality radius root at the first
step and v the corresponding root at the second; both lie in(0,1).
Write e=E_1,f=E_0, and retain the positive overall initial radius scale s.
Both adjacent equality conditions force both corresponding projection
normal rows to be0. The remote query-3 normal against the query-1 vertex
is then exactly

 B=s^2*u*[(a+v)A+2v f(1-u)-4vJ],
 A=T(u-1)+2e=2e*beta*(1+u)/(u+beta)>2e*beta.         (2)

To rederive(2), use three unscaled frame vectors W0,W1,W2 with inner
products T-2e,T-2f,T-2e-2f+4J. Set g0=s W0,g1=s u W1,g2=s u v W2.
At the middle point the residual is orthogonal to both differences.
The last residual differs by -a*g1-g2. Its normal against g0 is
(a*g1+g2) dot(g2-g0), which expands to(2), using the second root
equality. The first root equation rearranges as
(1-u)(u+beta)=2(1-beta)e*u/T, yielding the expression for A.

Every omitted factor is positive, so B>s^2*u*v*(2beta E_1-4J).
Equation(1) gives

 2beta E_1-4J=2/(N-1)-2(1-beta)E_1>0.

Indeed K>=4 and zeta<=1 imply E_1<4/(N-1), while1-beta<=1/6144.
Thus B>0 for every declared R,zeta,m. This is a uniform original-row
contradiction, independent of every later radius choice and the initial
point. It applies only when BOTH low-level radius ratios retain their
adjacent equality roots. It does not exclude strict short-step damping,
different overlaps or a different actual itinerary.

The next proposed law must therefore alter at least one of these low-level
equalities, rather than claim that correlated characters alone fixed the
global constraints. Feasibility and payoff of a damped law remain OPEN.
