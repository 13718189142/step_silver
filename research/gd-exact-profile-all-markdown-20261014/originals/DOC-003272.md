## 5. Even length and total sum together do not determine the final-stage value

This is a separate, quantitative obstruction to a sum-only state. For a fixed
prefix h_pre of length k define directly

    rho_last(h_pre)=inf_(b>=0) W_(k+1)(h_pre,b).

For every integer k>=1 let S=2k, and compare the two legal prefixes

    h_even=(2,...,2),             k entries,
    h_burst=(S,0,...,0),          k entries.

They have the SAME length and the SAME total sum. Nevertheless

    rho_last(h_even)<=1/4,
    rho_last(h_burst)>=S^(2/3)/12.                       (12)

In particular their values are different for k>=3 and have an unbounded
separation as k increases. This refutes an exact value closure depending
only on (k,S), not merely a claim about one selected function.

For the first upper bound, the norm calculation in Section3 with h=2 gives
||x_k-x_star||<=||x_0-x_star|| for every legal function. Append b=3/2.
At that final stage write x=x_k-x_star, z=grad f(x_k)/L and w for the next
normalized gradient, and use values divided by L and shifted by f_star.
The normalized one-step identity, with the signed Q on samples 0,1,star,
is

    (1/4)||x||^2-2f_1+Q_(0,star)+Q_(1,star)+Q_(1,0)
                      =||(1/2)x-z-w||^2.

Expanding Q and the recursion x_1=x-(3/2)z verifies every term. Since all
Q<=0, it bounds the last objective by (1/4)||x||^2 in the W normalization.
The initial norm bound proves rho_last(h_even)<=1/4 in all dimensions.

For the second lower bound use the verified single-cut fact b98bb807d514e80d,
whose full statement and proof have been read. Its cut at the first step S
and its cut at the appended final step b yield, for EVERY b>=0,

    W_(k+1)(h_burst,b)
       >=max{(S-1)^2/(1+2b), (b-1)_+^2/(S+1)^2}.        (13)

The internal zero steps change neither prefix nor suffix sums in that fact.
If b<=S^(4/3), then S>=2 and the first term in (13) is at least

    (S^2/4)/(1+2S^(4/3)) >= S^(2/3)/12.

If b>S^(4/3), then b>2, b-1>=b/2 and S+1<=3S/2. The second term is
therefore at least

    (b^2/4)/[(3S/2)^2] > S^(2/3)/9.

Taking the infimum over b proves the second part of (12). No compactness
or attainment assertion for this infimum is needed for either bound.

The explicit prefix-sum guard in research/prefix_sum_terminal_guard.md
accordingly supplies a universal LOWER boundary condition q_S, not an exact
schedule-level state variable. The proof of (12) does not use that new,
unverified guard or the unverified minimax result.
