## 2. An explicit nested-normal geometry and all its reverse constraints

First take an arbitrary word with h_i>1 and eta>1. Write

    tau=eta-1,

    d_i=h_i-1.

    Ldual_0>=...>=Ldual_n=1.

x_i-h_i*g_i=y_i-d_i*g_i=x_(i+1). The dependence of g_i includes
the full future position and the independent accumulated dual normal;
it is not a scalar multiple of g_(i+1) plus a fresh innovation.

    <rdual_i,g_j>=1                 for every j>=i.          (3)
