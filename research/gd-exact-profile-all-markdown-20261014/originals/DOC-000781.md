# P5 retains inner returns that the outward-ray family omits
Status: new analytic candidate; not verifier-accepted. The only cited correctness bricks are facts 1fca3e5dd555cb4a and b98bb807d514e80d. This document gives a self-contained legal-function calculation, without relying on another unverified construction as a brick.

For n>=8 let h_t=(t+1)/2 for 0<=t<n. Set c=2/5 and define the global derivative
q(x)=x for x<=2/5,
q(x)=2/5 for 2/5<=x<=1,
q(x)=x-3/5 for x>=1.
Let f(x)=integral_0^x q(z)dz. The derivative is continuous, nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz, with q(0)=0. Thus f is convex differentiable, 1-smooth, minimized at0 with value0. The initial point is1, so the normalized error is2f(x_n).

The exact first eight steps are
x_0=1, x_1=4/5, x_2=2/5, x_3=-1/5,
x_4=1/5, x_5=-3/10, x_6=3/5, x_7=-4/5, x_8=12/5.
Direct substitution into q and x_{t+1}=x_t-h_t q(x_t) verifies all these values.
In particular x_4 and x_6 return inside the previous positive-ray record1. An adversary restricted to successive outward ray records cannot follow this chronology.

For every t>=8 the signs alternate and |x_t|>=12/5. If x_t<0 then
|x_{t+1}|=((t-1)/2)|x_t| >= (t/4)|x_t|.
If x_t>0 then
x_{t+1}= -((t-1)/2)x_t+3(t+1)/10.
Using x_t>=12/5 gives
|x_{t+1}| >= [(t-1)/2-(t+1)/8]|x_t|
 =((3t-5)/8)|x_t| >= (t/4)|x_t|.
The last bound holds for t>=5; with t>=8 it also preserves the magnitude lower bound and proves the claimed alternating signs. Hence
|x_n| >= (12/5) product_{t=8}^{n-1}(t/4).

For x<0, 2f(x)=x^2. For x>=1,
2f(x)=(x-3/5)^2+12/25.
This is at least x^2/4: the difference is
(3/4)x^2-(6/5)x+21/25
=(3/4)(x-4/5)^2+9/25 >0.
Therefore the legal one-dimensional objective satisfies
W_n(h)>=2f(x_n)>=(36/25)[product_{t=8}^{n-1}(t/4)]^2.
The product is (n-1)!/[7! 4^(n-8)] for n>=8. This grows faster than any fixed power. No computation or exhaustive search is part of the proof.

## Membership in the concrete P5 action tree
The seed j=2 in P5 forces c=1/(1+h_0+h_1)=2/5, agreeing with the displayed first three queried gradients. Thereafter taking the signed steep envelope at every step reproduces q. Here is the direct envelope check, so this assertion is not a reliance on an unverified earlier finding.

The stored knots at(0,0),(2/5,2/5),(1,2/5) force the upper gradient envelope on the positive half-line to be exactly q there: the origin bounds it by x, the knot at2/5 bounds it by2/5 for smaller x and by x for larger x, and the knot at1 bounds it by2/5 below1 and by x-3/5 above1. Taking the minimum gives the displayed q. Every other positive queried knot lies on q, so cannot tighten this envelope below q. On the negative half-line the lower envelope supplied by the origin is x. All positive knots have v<=y, so v-(y-x)<=x and cannot raise it. Every negative knot on q(x)=x likewise preserves that envelope. Thus every subsequent signed steep query equals q(x_t), including the interior returns.

Consequently this single leaf is present in P5. Under the original fair law its probability is2^{-(n-3)}, which already leaves a diverging lower bound after multiplication by the factorial-square payoff. Under the separately derived power tilt its expected payoff is within2^{-1/n} of the maximum leaf; that probability repair is an unverified companion candidate, not needed for the present legal-function lower bound.
