**Not solved.** The exact uniform lower comparison and a certified asymptotic improvement are both absent.

The fixed target is unchanged. For nonnegative predetermined GD steps h of length n, let W_n(h) be the supremum of 2(f(x_n)-f_*)/(L||x_0-x_*||^2) over all finite-dimensional smooth convex instances with a minimizer, and let W_n^*=inf_h W_n(h). P_n=max_(k+l=n-1) F(P_k,P_l),
B_n=max_(k+l=n-1) G(P_k,B_l),
F(x,y)=(x+y+sqrt(x^2+6xy+y^2))/2,

Thus P_n=1+S_circle(n), B_n=1+2S_bullet(n), and U_n^ZJ=1/B_n.
