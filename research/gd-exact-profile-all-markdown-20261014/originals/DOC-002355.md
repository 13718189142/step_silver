## 1. Frozen object and actual function

For the enclosing integer m set beta=1-1/m,
c=(1+sqrt(2))(1+1/m), a=beta/c, r_tree=2/c, and
w_j=r_tree^j/sum_(k<m)r_tree^k. Set H_0=() and
H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a*c^j,H_j); every child keeps the same m.
The physical length is n=2^m-1. Each full leaf feature is the signed
sum of sqrt(w_j) times its orthonormal internal-node coordinates.
K is the centered convex hull of all2^m leaf features, and
F_K(x)=max_(g in K)(<x,g>-||g||^2/2).

The actual initial point is the all-left leaf. The joint derivation
proves that the full feature matrix has rank2^m-1, the leaves form a
simplex with0 in its interior, grad F_K=P_K, smoothness1 is sharp,
the unique minimizer is0, and the true initial squared distance is1.
Thus the reported normalized value is V=2F_K(x_n)/(L R^2)=2F_K(x_n).
The actual GD step is x_(i+1)=x_i-h_i P_K(x_i), with L=1.

Off-spine automorphisms leave the trajectory invariant. Their fixed
section is exactly the m-dimensional simplex with vertices b_k: positive
sqrt(w_j) coordinates before the first-right exit k, negative sqrt(w_k)
at k, zero later; b_m is the all-left leaf. A residual in this spine has
the same normal on every full leaf in an exit group as on its b_k.
Every group weight lifts to a uniform nonnegative mixture of that group's
leaves. Thus reduced barycentric feasibility and all m+1 reduced normals
are precisely the original full-body conditions, not a substituted metric.

DERIVATION.md gives the weighted q-coordinate objective and two independent
inverse barycentric formulas. Ordinary probability-simplex thresholding
is not used. Every query receives a fresh core test. A core identity is
used only at that tested input; no suffix is skipped and no permanent
core claim is inferred from an entry time or the two-sided diagnostics.
