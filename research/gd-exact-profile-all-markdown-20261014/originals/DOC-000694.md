# A globally minimizing stationary schedule with an unavoidable forward edge

Status: UNVERIFIED exact counterexample package. No additional verifier call
is allocated. The two-step optimum is used here as a fully certified calibration
example for a FACE-LEVEL support claim, not as a solution to the asymptotic target.

## 1. The precise property being refuted

The proposed property is:

    At a globally minimizing positive schedule, one can reselect a tight
    full interpolation certificate with lambda_(i,j)=0 for every
    finite chronological forward pair i<j.

This property is false. The example below is BOTH globally minimizing
among all nonnegative two-step schedules AND a full matching stationary
primal-dual tuple. Its ENTIRE optimal certificate set is a singleton,
and its multiplier lambda_01 is strictly positive.

The forced edge0->1 is ADJACENT. This does NOT refute the narrower
unproved property that only nonadjacent remote forward edges can be removed.

## 2. Exact constants and a complete full certificate

Define

    u=sqrt(2), a=u-1,
    b=(-1+sqrt(9+8u))/4,
    r=a b, q=r^2,
    h=(u,1+b).                                      (1)

The useful exact identities are

    a^2+2a=1,  u=1+a,  1/a=a+2,
    2b^2+b=a+2,
    r(2b+1)=1,
    q=1/[1+2(u+1+b)].                               (2)

For example, r(2b+1)=a(2b^2+b)=a(a+2)=1, and
1+2(u+1+b)=2a+2b+5=(2b+1)^2.
Also 2/5<a<1/2, 3/4<b<1, 0<r<1/2.

Normalize L=1, x_star=g_star=f_star=0. Use the complete inequalities

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0.

Set all unlisted multipliers to zero and take

    mu_0=lambda_(0,star)=2u r^2,
    mu_1=lambda_(1,star)=2u b r,
    mu_2=lambda_(2,star)=2r,

    lambda_01=2r^2,
    lambda_10=2r^2/a,
    lambda_21=2(1-r).                               (3)

All are positive. There are no star-outgoing multipliers.
The finite flow equations are

    mu_0+lambda_01=lambda_10,
    mu_1+lambda_10=lambda_01+lambda_21,
    mu_2+lambda_21=2.

They follow from (2); for example 2r^2/a+r=1 and
u r+u b+1=2b+1=1/r.

After x_1=x_0-u g_0 and x_2=x_1-(1+b)g_1, the FULL quadratic identity is

    q||x_0||^2-2f_2+sum lambda_ij Q_ij
         =||r x_0-u r g_0-u b g_1-g_2||^2.          (4)

One way to check all coefficients is to write its gradient block as

    A=(u r,u b,1)^T(u r,u b,1),

whose diagonals are (2r^2,2b^2,1) and off-diagonal entries are
A_01=2rb, A_02=u r, A_12=u b.
Its x_0 cross coefficients are minus the three mu_i from (3).
Thus every multiplier sign, flow, and full PSD entry is explicit.

This proves W_2(h)<=q.

## 3. Three exact matching legal functions

Each scalar function starts at x_0=1 and is minimized at zero.

H: the even Huber function of threshold q, with derivative
max(-q,min(q,x)). Its vector (x_0,g_0,g_1,g_2) is

    v_H=(1,q,q,q),

and its final value is f_2=q/2. At every sampled point its
star-outgoing slack is strictly positive:

    f_i-||g_i||^2/2=q[1-q(P_i+1)]>0,               (5)

where P_i=sum_(t<i)h_t, including P_2=u+1+b.
The strict inequality follows from P_i<=P_2 and
q=1/(2P_2+1), with P_2>0.

Q: the quadratic function x^2/2. Its transcript vector is

    v_Q=(1,1,-a,r),

and f_2=r^2/2=q/2.

C: the one-sided capped quadratic

    f_C(x)=x^2/2 for x<=a,
           a x-a^2/2 for x>=a.

Its derivative min(x,a) is continuous, nondecreasing, and 1-Lipschitz.
The trajectory is x_0=1, x_1=a, x_2=-r, with

    v_C=(1,a,a,-r),  f_2=q/2.                       (6)

All are actual globally legal smooth-convex functions. They establish
W_2(h)>=q and hence equality with the full certificate (4).

## 4. Global optimality among ALL two-step schedules

The only verified predecessor used here is fact b98bb807d514e80d:
its exact Huber and both single-cut lower bounds apply to arbitrary
nonnegative schedules. The elementary quadratic witness adds its own
explicit lower bound. Thus for every X,Y>=0,

    W_2(X,Y)>=max{
       1/(1+2X+2Y),
       (X-1)_+^2/(1+2Y),
       (Y-1)_+^2/(1+X)^2,
       (X-1)^2(Y-1)^2
    }.                                             (7)

The last term is the error on x^2/2; the other three are the verified
single-cut/Huber bounds. Suppose W_2(X,Y)<=q=r^2. The Huber term gives

    X+Y>=u+1+b=2+a+b>3.                            (8)

If X<=1, then Y>1 and the third term in (7) gives
Y-1<=r(1+X), whence X+Y<=2+2r<3, contradicting (8).
If Y<=1, then X>1 and the second term gives
X-1<=r sqrt(1+2Y)<=r sqrt(3), whence X+Y<3, again impossible.

We may therefore set U=X-1>0 and V=Y-1>0. Equation (7) implies

    U V<=r,
    V<=r(U+2),
    U^2<=r^2(2V+3).                                (9)

For U<=a, use the second inequality:

    U+V<=U+r(U+2)<=a+r(a+2)=a+b,

with equality only at U=a,V=b.
For U>=a, the first and third inequalities imply

    U^3-3r^2 U-2r^3<=0,
    (U-2r)(U+r)^2<=0,

so a<=U<=2r. Also V<=r/U. The strictly convex function
U+r/U attains its maximum on [a,2r] at an endpoint. Its values are

    a+r/a=a+b,
    2r+1/2.

The first is strictly greater, since

    (a+b)-(2r+1/2)=a^2(b-1/2)>0.

Thus U+V<=a+b, again with equality only at U=a,V=b.
Combining with (8), every schedule with W_2(X,Y)<=q is exactly
(X,Y)=(u,1+b). In particular (1) is the UNIQUE global minimizer, and

    W_2^*=q.                                       (10)

All nonnegative schedules, including zero steps and arbitrary large
steps, were included. This is an exact two-step argument, not an
asymptotic profile claim.

## 5. The ENTIRE optimal certificate set is a singleton

Take ANY full nonnegative-flow PSD certificate at this fixed h,q.
Every actual instance in Section3 attains q. Therefore PSD and the
interpolation signs force the certificate matrix M to annihilate
v_H,v_Q,v_C, and every multiplier to vanish on a strict interpolation
slack of any one of these instances.

The three transcript vectors are independent. Indeed, v_H-v_C has
equal, nonzero g_0 and g_1 coordinates, whereas v_Q-v_C has those
coordinates 1-a and -2a, which are unequal. Both differences have
x_0 coordinate zero, while v_C has x_0 coordinate one. The nonzero
claim uses q<a, which follows from a b^2=(1-r)/2<1/2.

Their common orthogonal complement is generated by

    N=(r,-u r,-u b,-1).

It annihilates v_Q and v_C by (2), and N dot v_H=0 follows from
u r+u b+1=1/r. Since M is PSD and its x_0,x_0 coefficient is q=r^2,

    M=N N^T.                                       (11)

This is the WHOLE matrix for every optimal certificate. No assumption
about a numerical maximal rank or a chosen observed kernel is used;
three explicit legal equality instances force it.

Equation (5) forces every star-outgoing multiplier nu_i to be zero.
The x_0 row of (11) fixes mu uniquely at the values in (3).
The final gradient diagonal satisfies

    A_22=out_2-1=1+in_2

by the full flow equation. In (11), A_22=1, so every incoming
terminal multiplier is zero: lambda_02=lambda_12=nu_2=0.

For function C a direct substitution gives

    Q_20=(a-1)(a+r)<0.

Therefore lambda_20=0 for every optimal certificate as well.
The first gradient diagonal now reads

    A_00=mu_0-(u-1)lambda_01
         =2u r^2-a lambda_01.

In (11) it equals 2r^2, hence

    lambda_01=2r^2=2q>0.                            (12)

Flow then forces lambda_10=2r^2/a and lambda_21=2(1-r), leaving
exactly (3). All directed pairs have now been accounted for.

Thus the optimal certificate set is a singleton, and re-selection,
relative-interior selection, and maximal-rank selection all leave
the positive adjacent forward edge unchanged.

## 6. An ACTUAL matching stationary primal, with no assumed regularity

To check stationarity directly, set

    omega_H=1/(1+a^2 b^3),
    omega_C=omega_H*a^2 b^2*(b-a)/(1+a^2),
    omega_Q=omega_H*a^3 b^2*(1+a b)/(1+a^2).          (13)

They are positive and sum to one:
omega_C+omega_Q=omega_H a^2 b^3.
Construct the actual three-dimensional function

    F(z_H,z_Q,z_C)
       =sum_(J in {H,Q,C}) omega_J f_J(z_J/sqrt(omega_J))

and start at (sqrt(omega_H),sqrt(omega_Q),sqrt(omega_C)).
It is convex and 1-smooth, the initial squared distance is one, and
its full trajectory Gram matrix is
G=sum_J omega_J v_J v_J^T. Its final normalized value is q.

For the certificate (3), the two formal matrix derivatives evaluated
on any scalar/vector realization are

    D_0=2b^2<g_0,g_1>+2r<g_0,g_2>-2r^2||g_0||^2,
    D_1=2<g_1,g_2>.                                (14)

Their values on the three equality instances are

              H              Q                C
    D_0       2r^4           -2a b^2           2a r^2
    D_1       2r^4           -2a r             -2a r.

These values are obtained from (14), not by differentiating a
nonsmooth family of extremal functions. In particular the fixed
capped function C has a gradient kink at x_1=a, so that shortcut
would be unsafe.

The identities

    omega_Q-a^2 omega_C=omega_H a^3 b^2,
    omega_Q+omega_C=omega_H a^2 b^3

make both weighted derivatives zero. Therefore

    <G,partial M/partial h_0>=<G,partial M/partial h_1>=0.

All primal interpolation inequalities, PSD, normalization, full
matrix complementarity, multiplier complementarity, and both
stepsize stationarity equations hold for this actual instance.
Moreover rank(G)=3, rank(M)=1, and the formal matrix size is4.
This example even has strict complementarity; a hidden nonphysical
common kernel is not responsible for the obstruction.

## 7. Consequences and precise limits

This is a complete counterexample to removing ALL forward finite
multipliers by re-selection, even at a positive globally minimizing
schedule with a full stationary matching instance and no extra
common kernel. It closes a possible attempt to turn the triangular
sufficient hypothesis in the earlier suffix-deformation candidate
into a universal property of optimal schedules.

It does not refute removal of ONLY nonadjacent remote forward
edges: lambda_02=0 in the example. It also has q equal to the
Huber value, every nu_i=0, and Z=0; consequently it is not a
counterexample to global Huber tightness. The asymptotic exact-profile
question and the mass-cap bridge remain unresolved.

No literature result beyond the verified predecessor b98bb807d514e80d
was used as a proof brick. The targeted Matlas query returned no
applicable statement. The global two-step calculation is included
solely to make the counterexample's global and stationary status
reviewable without relying on a numerical optimum.
