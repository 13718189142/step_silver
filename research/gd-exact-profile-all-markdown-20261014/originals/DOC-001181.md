## 4. Exact signed step images and a monotone-block bound

    p'=p-t*min(p,v), q'=q-t*min(q,u).                 (4.1)

    p'=max(e*min(q,u),p-t*v),
    q'=max(e*min(p,v),q-t*u).                        (4.2)

its extrema are at a threshold or an endpoint. The first terms in the

all endpoint/threshold equalities. At t=2 the core slope is-1, and for
t>2 it is less than-1; neither case is omitted.

Write Phi_j(p,q) for the TWO nonnegative endpoint magnitudes of the
exact image Psi_j([-p,q]). Continuity makes both endpoints attained.

with(4.1) or(4.2) is exact for the whole interval. Bounds below therefore
apply to compatible actual child histories, not independently chosen
child maximizers. This interface is used only symbolically.

Consider any block all of whose effective steps lie in[0,1], of total

nondecreasing on r>=0, fixes0, and has slopes1-t,1. The complete map is

    B(theta*r)<=theta*B(r), 0<=theta<=1.             (4.3)

    B(r)<=V_M(r;w),
