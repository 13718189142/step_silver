# Endpoint cross-term fusion: concrete tests

Status: unverified candidate derivations; not a target solution and not a fact. Initial-wave verification allowance already used.

Cross-term fusion screen, beyond the earlier scalar-deficit rule: (i) the rational motif [3/2,3/2] has an exact saturated primitive certificate with nonlocal interpolation multipliers, proving that cross terms can repair scalar deficits; (ii) nevertheless pairing any known ZJ objective block of length n with its reverse cannot improve the primitive DP capacity at length 2n, because S_circle(2n)+1>2S_bullet(n)+1 for n>=1; (iii) an explicit asymmetric two-bridge rule promising a larger three-block capacity fails already on the primitive block [sqrt(2)] by a legal one-dimensional Huber example. These are unverified route-screening findings, not target results.

Notation. Put P_n=S_circle(n)+1 and B_n=2S_bullet(n)+1, with exact DP definitions and normalized worst-case W_n as in fact 0be8b54e0a33381c. Define homogeneous scalar joins
F(A,B)=(A+B+sqrt(A^2+6AB+B^2))/2,
G(A,D)=2A+D/2+sqrt(D^2+8AD)/2
for positive A,B,D. The exact recurrences are
P_0=B_0=1,
P_n=max_{k+l+1=n} F(P_k,P_l),
B_n=max_{k+l+1=n} G(P_k,B_l).

1. A real cross-term repair, with no bridge.
For a nonnegative schedule h of length n and sum s, define the intrinsic potential
V_h(C)=2 sum_{i<n} h_i(f_i-f_n)+sum_{i<n}h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-C||g_n||^2.
This is the same potential as in candidate 79da55e26c4a7406: the cross terms from the GD displacement cancel exactly.
Let Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2<=0 for convex 1-smooth functions, with x_{i+1}=x_i-h_i g_i.
For h=[3/2,3/2], s=3 and C=s(s+1)=12, the following formal identity holds:
V_h(12)=(7/2)(-Q_10)+(-Q_01)+14(-Q_21)+(17/2)(-Q_12)+(-Q_20)+(1/2)(-Q_02).
All six multipliers are nonnegative. To check it, put u=g_0,v=g_1,w=g_2, x_1=x_0-(3/2)u and x_2=x_1-(3/2)v. Function coefficients become 3(f_0-f_1)+6(f_1-f_2); squared-gradient coefficients become -3||u||^2/4-3||v||^2/4-12||w||^2; and all three cross coefficients cancel. This is precisely V_h(12).
Thus a bridge-free two-block proof with retained history inequalities repairs the deficit of the one-step [3/2] seed. It is a concrete demonstration that the scalar-deficit obstruction does not forbid richer fusion. However its mass is 4, below P_2=F(1,1+sqrt(2)), so this test seed is not an improvement.

2. Exact capacity screen for pairing ZJ objective schedules with their reversals.
We prove the scalar inequality
F(A,F(D,A))>G(A,D) for A,D>0.
By homogeneity set A=1 and d=D/A. Let q=F(1,d), z=F(1,q), g=G(1,d). The defining quadratic identities give
d=q(q-1)/(q+1), q=z(z-1)/(z+1),
so d=z(z-1)(z^2-2z-1)/[(z+1)(z^2+1)] and z>1+sqrt(2)>2.
The larger root g>2 satisfies d=g-4+4/g. Direct expansion yields
(z-4+4/z)-d=4/[z(z+1)(z^2+1)]>0.
The function t maps to t-4+4/t is strictly increasing for t>2, since its derivative is 1-4/t^2>0. Hence z>g and the displayed inequality follows after restoring A.

We now prove P_{2n}>=B_n for every n>=0, strictly for n>=1. At n=0 both equal 1. For n>=1 choose a maximizing split k+l+1=n for B_n. By induction P_{2l}>=B_l. The primitive DP permits the two joins
P_{2n}>=F(P_k,F(P_{2l},P_k))
>=F(P_k,F(B_l,P_k))
>G(P_k,B_l)=B_n.
All indices are valid: the inner length is 2l+k+1 and the outer length is 2l+2k+2=2n. This is a pure algebraic property of the actual DP, not an assertion of unrestricted schedule optimality.

If h is a ZJ objective schedule of length n and sum S_bullet(n), the concatenated schedule [h,reverse(h)] has length 2n and shifted sum 2S_bullet(n)+1=B_n. Even if a new richer proof established that this concatenation is primitive, its capacity would still be strictly below P_{2n}, so every subsequent ordinary ConPP/ConPD construction using it can replace it by the existing same-length primitive DP block with a greater scalar capacity. Thus this fusion route using only existing ZJ objective blocks cannot furnish the requested strict improvement. General f/g-to-primitive closure was not assumed or claimed.
A nonbasic f/g seed of length n would need shifted pair mass strictly larger than P_{2n} even to escape this exact capacity screen, and then would still need a valid primitive certificate.

3. A hostile test of a different, direct three-block join.
Choose exact constants c=1/4 and d=(-5+5sqrt(17))/8-1=(5sqrt(17)-13)/8. Then 0<d<1 and
c d (3+c+d)=1.
Let M=3+c+d. A tempting homogeneous triple-primitive rule, on a primitive block a of shifted sum A=1+s(a), is
J(a)=[a, 1+cA, a, 1+dA, a].
Its shifted sum is M A. Because c d M=1, it balances the full-curvature quadratic endpoint product when the seed block has product 1/A; M exceeds the ordinary two-join factor F(1,F(1,1)) (numerically M is about 4.20194, versus about 4.01545). Quadratic/Huber balance alone therefore makes it an attractive but insufficient candidate.

It is not a universally primitive join. Take the exact primitive one-step block a=[sqrt(2)], write A=1+sqrt(2), and let
P=2sqrt(2)+1+cA
be the sum of all steps before the larger bridge b=1+dA. The tail is the one step sqrt(2). Define the convex 1-smooth function
f(x)=x^2/2 for x<=1,
f(x)=x-1/2 for x>=1,
with minimizer 0. Its derivative is min(x,1), continuous, nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz.
Start at x_0=P+1=(2+c)A. Every step before b occurs with derivative 1 and the point immediately before b is 1. The bridge sends it to -dA. The final sqrt(2) step is taken in the quadratic region and yields
x_final=(sqrt(2)-1)dA=d,
which lies in (0,1). At every trajectory point, Q_{i,star}=f_i-<g_i,x_i>+||g_i||^2/2=0: the identity holds in both pieces of this explicit function.
For a saturated primitive schedule of shifted sum R=1+s, its potential would require x_0^2>=R^2 x_final^2 whenever the final point is in the quadratic piece and all Q_{i,star}=0. Here R=M A, so it would require
(2+c)^2 A^2 >= M^2 A^2 d^2.
But Md=1/c=4, whereas 2+c=9/4; the inequality is strictly false. Hence the proposed primitive interface is refuted exactly, in dimension one.
This hostile test rules out that uniform join rule; it does not exclude a horizon-dependent rule valid only for a different restricted block family. Any such reopening must supply a new certificate and re-test the full smooth-convex class.
