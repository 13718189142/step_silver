## 5. The quantitative signed-state law and the precise next gap

The transverse repair does not alter the endpoint norm multiplication,
but it changes the signed boundary pairings. The exact update can be
stated for any legal endpoint-saturated child with initial P, endpoint w,
actual vectors G_i,Y_i, and E_i=<G_i,Y_i>. Choose a real transverse
contraction C in the specified frames and scale alpha=r*||w||/R, using
(11),(14). For the parent P'=L(P), w'=alpha*Rmap(w):

    <P',G'_i>=<P,G_i>,       <P',Y'_i>=<P,Y_i>,
    <G'_i,w'>=B_C(G_i,w),   <Y'_i,w'>=B_C(Y_i,w),        (17L)

    <P',G'_(M+i)>=B_C(P,G_i),
    <P',Y'_(M+i)>=B_C(P,Y_i),
    <G'_(M+i),w'>=alpha^2*<G_i,w>,
    <Y'_(M+i),w'>=alpha^2*<Y_i,w>.                      (17R)

Here M is the number of child samples in this section. The energy list
updates to(E,alpha^2*E), the initial
radius stays R by the retained cone representation, and the endpoint
norm becomes alpha^2*||w||^2. For r=theta*A and A'=lambda*A,

    kappa'=kappa^2.                                     (18)

Every sign and transverse term in(17) is retained. The exact legality
test remains the full inequalities(15). The four scalar lists alone do
not determine the transverse vectors, so discarding them would lose the
mechanism that makes the present repair possible.

The construction gives C=0 in the first2-by2 transverse space and the
explicit4-by4 C in(10) for the next join. The resulting actual child now
has15 steps and lives in R^9. Its next balanced parent would have31 steps,
bridge1+theta*A_2, and an8-by8 transverse block. Producing such a block
with all original inequalities, and then a uniform all-depth rule, remain
open. No n>15 computation was run or authorized.

Two naive continuations do not constitute a proof. Setting all future
C=0 discards exactly the transverse terms that repaired the earlier
queried row(2,9). Padding(10) with zero rows and columns preserves the
contraction bound but does not establish the new cross-normal inequalities
because the frames and signed vectors have changed. Neither a verified
31-step parent nor an exact obstruction to all its transverse choices is
claimed. Equations(17)-(18) state the interface that a further construction
has to preserve, rather than treating a finite coupling as an asymptotic
iteration theorem.
