# Exact Huber padding gives a core-ended realization of every primitive defect

## 1. Statement and definitions

Fix a finite nonnegative stepsize schedule h=(h_0,...,h_(n-1)). Put
s=sum_i h_i, T=1+s, and R_i=sum_(t=i)^(n-1) h_t for 0<=i<=n.
Thus R_n=0. Consider any actual finite convex differentiable 1-smooth
trajectory x_(i+1)=x_i-h_i g_i, with g_i=grad f(x_i), f_i=f(x_i).
Let

  delta_i=f_i-||g_i||^2/2,
  Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
  V_h=2 sum_(i<n) h_i(f_i-f_n)
       +sum_(i<n)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-T(T-1)||g_n||^2.

Then there exists another actual trajectory for the SAME h, in at most
one additional dimension, on an everywhere finite convex differentiable
1-smooth function with minimizer0, such that

  x_n_new=g_n_new, f_n_new=||g_n_new||^2/2,
  V_h_new=V_h.

In particular, h has a negative primitive-energy witness if and only if it
has a witness whose endpoint equals its terminal gradient relative to a
minimizer. No preservation of the normalized objective error is asserted.

Here and below a core endpoint means precisely x_n=g_n with optimum0.

## 2. Choose one finite Huber companion

If R_i=0, nonnegativity of h forces every later step to be zero.
Consequently x_i=x_n, g_i=g_n, f_i=f_n and delta_i=delta_n.
Choose any c>0 such that

  c^2 >= max_(i:R_i>0) (delta_n-delta_i)_+/R_i,                 (1)

with empty maximum0 and (a)_+=max(a,0). This is a finite maximum.

Define the fixed scalar Huber function

  H_c(z)=z^2/2                         if |z|<=c,
         c|z|-c^2/2                   if |z|>=c.

Start its GD run at z_0=T c. Every step of the given schedule stays on
its positive affine ray:

  z_i=(1+R_i)c, H_c'(z_i)=c,
  H_c(z_i)=(R_i+1/2)c^2.                                    (2)

These formulas include its final point z_n=c. The derivative is
continuous, nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz, so H_c is a fixed legal
convex differentiable 1-smooth function.

Run the direct sum f(x)+H_c(z). Its exact sampled data are

  X_i=(x_i,(1+R_i)c),
  G_i=(g_i,c),
  F_i=f_i+(R_i+1/2)c^2.                                    (3)

The original-index GD equations hold, including zero steps.

The companion's finite interpolation residual is zero for every ordered
pair: its two gradients are equal and its sampled values are affine.
Therefore

  Q_ij_new=Q_ij_original<=0.                                (4)

Its adjusted values obey

  Delta_i=F_i-||G_i||^2/2=delta_i+R_i c^2>=delta_n=Delta_n.    (5)

For R_i>0 this follows from(1); for R_i=0 it follows from the duplicate
sample observation above. No strictness or limiting argument is needed.

## 3. Add the new optimum, retaining every query jet

Replace the old optimum by the new jet

  X_star_new=X_n-G_n=(x_n-g_n,0),
  G_star_new=0,
  F_star_new=Delta_n=delta_n.                               (6)

The previous optimum, if one was specified, is not retained. All query
jets(3) are retained exactly.

For every query i, direct expansion gives

  Q_(i,star_new)=Q_(i,n)_new=Q_(i,n)_original<=0,
  Q_(star_new,i)=Delta_n-Delta_i<=0.                         (7)

At i=n both quantities are zero. Equations(4),(7) check every ordered
interpolation pair in the enlarged finite data set.

By the finite interpolation construction in fact1252b8823bb5a290, these
data are interpolated by one everywhere finite convex differentiable
1-smooth function with the new optimum jet. Concretely, after translating
the new optimum to0 and its value to0, form

  H(v)=max_(j in {star,0,...,n})
       {<G_j,X_j>-F_j+<X_j,v-G_j>+||v-G_j||^2/2},
  F_new(x)=sup_v {<x,v>-H(v)}.

Here X_j,F_j in the displayed formula denote the translated data.
This is the accepted fact's explicit construction, with the whole
finite set treated as interpolation input. It interpolates every
original-index query and the zero optimum. Translation preserves all
GD differences. At the last query,

  x_n_new=G_n=(g_n,c),
  f_n_new=F_n-delta_n=||G_n||^2/2.                           (8)

The initial displacement is explicitly

  x_0_new=(x_0-x_n+g_n,Tc),

so it is nonzero since c>0.

## 4. Exact preservation of primitive energy

The energy is unchanged by a common translation of positions and a
common subtraction from all function values. It is additive under the
orthogonal direct sum(3). The Huber companion has exactly zero energy:
from(2),

  V_companion/c^2
    =2 sum_i h_i R_i+sum_i h_i(1-h_i)-s(s+1)=0,

because

  2 sum_i h_i R_i=s^2+sum_i h_i^2.

Thus V_h_new=V_h, without an approximation or an unproved inequality.

This supplies a core-ended realization of every negative witness.
The converse follows because a core-ended witness is itself legal.
For n=0 or an all-zero schedule the construction still works, and V_h=0.

## 5. What happens to the retained optimum slacks

After the normalization, the incoming optimum slack is exactly

  I_i_star_new=-Q_(i,n)_original>=0.                         (9)

Thus the construction DOES NOT remove queried-to-terminal interpolation
slack. Define d=x_0-x_n and Sigma_n=-sum_(i<n)h_i Q_(i,n).
Expansion of the definitions gives

  V_h+2 Sigma_n=||d+g_n||^2-T^2||g_n||^2.                    (10)

Equivalently, the core realization has

  ||x_0_new||^2-T^2||g_n_new||^2
     =||d+g_n||^2-T^2||g_n||^2,

independent of c. Padding removes the terminal-adjusted-value obstruction
but leaves exactly the old query-to-terminal slack ledger. This is why
the result does not, by itself, prove tail heredity at a prepended
saturated bridge.

## 6. Exact hostile check: the old direct-reset counterexample

Take f(x)=x^2/4, h=(5), x_0=1. The original data are

  (x_0,x_1)=(1,-3/2),
  (g_0,g_1)=(1/2,-3/4),
  (f_0,f_1)=(1/4,9/16),
  (delta_0,delta_1)=(1/8,9/32),
  V_h=-25.

A direct preserved-data core reset fails because delta_1>delta_0.
Choose c=1/4. Then c^2=1/16>=(delta_1-delta_0)/5=1/32.
After padding and normalizing, the data are

  x_0_new=(7/4,3/2),  g_0_new=(1/2,1/4), f_0_new=5/16,
  x_1_new=(-3/4,1/4), g_1_new=(-3/4,1/4), f_1_new=5/16.

They satisfy x_1_new=x_0_new-5g_0_new and
x_1_new=g_1_new. Their finite Q residuals are unchanged from the legal
original pair; their optimum residuals follow(7). Direct substitution
in the energy gives

  V_h_new=5(1-5)(5/16)-30(5/8)=-25.

Thus the very example that defeats an unpadded reset is repaired
exactly by one extra equality-mode coordinate.

## 7. Saturated append heredity, with the entire proof included

Let b and c_block be any nonnegative physical blocks, with capacities
D=1+sum b_i and C=1+sum(c_block)_i. Let r>0 solve

  T_join=D+C+r, r T_join=DC.                               (11)

If the joined schedule (b,1+r,c_block) is primitive, then b is primitive.
This is a necessary prefix heredity theorem at an exactly saturated
primitive bridge. No primitive assumption about c_block is needed.

Proof by contrapositive. If b is not primitive, sections1-4 turn an
actual negative witness into a legal core-ended witness for b, with
endpoint e=x_m=g_m and value||e||^2/2. Denote its energy V_b<0.

Construct a scalar base Huber run with gradient clip(z,-r/C,1), initial
point D. During b it has gradient1 and ends at1; the bridge lands at-r;
during c_block its gradient is v_base=-r/C and it ends atv_base.
All incoming optimum slacks are zero and the final point equals its
gradient. Squared-distance telescoping therefore gives

  V_base=D^2-T_join^2(r/C)^2=0                             (12)

by(11).

For an auxiliary vector array, retain ALL supplied core-ended b samples.
The new bridge lands at-r e. During c_block use constant gradient

  v_aux=-(r/C)e.

Its starting point is C v_aux, and its terminal point is v_aux.
Assign each new value <v_aux,x>-||v_aux||^2/2. This array is not presumed
legal across the join; the following audit supplies that legality after
mixing.

For epsilon>0, combine base and auxiliary points and gradients in
orthogonal coordinates with auxiliary scale sqrt(epsilon), and combine
their values with scale epsilon. All GD equations are exact.

Every active base interpolation constraint has nonpositive auxiliary
residual:

* Old-old constraints: the auxiliary data are the original legal b run.
* New-new constraints: constant auxiliary gradient makes them zero.
* New-to-old constraints: the base residual can be zero only at an old
  target whose remaining b sum is0. That target is the old core endpoint,
  including any zero-step duplicates. At this target, direct expansion
  gives Q_aux(new,m)=0.
* Old-to-new constraints: the base residual can be zero only at a new
  target whose remaining c_block sum is0. That target has x=g=v_aux,
  so Q_aux(old,new)=Q_aux(old,star)<=0.
* Incoming optimum constraints: old ones are legal; new ones are0.
  Outgoing optimum constraints: old ones are legal; new ones are
  minus the remaining c_block sum times||v_aux||^2, hence nonpositive.

For every auxiliary pair with Q_aux>0 the corresponding base residual
is therefore strictly negative. The finite choice

  epsilon=min(1, min_(Q_aux>0) [-Q_base/(2Q_aux)])

is positive, interpreting the inner minimum as1 when there are no such
pairs. The combined data obey all interpolation constraints, including
both optimum directions and every original zero-step index. The
construction in fact1252b8823bb5a290 realizes one fixed legal function.

For any core-ended legal trajectory of capacityK, distance telescoping
gives

  V=||x_0||^2-K^2||g_end||^2-2sum h_i I_i_star.              (13)

The auxiliary full trajectory is core-ended, adds no new incoming
optimum slack, and its bridge query was already a core endpoint.
Equation(11) gives T_join^2||v_aux||^2=D^2||e||^2.
Using(13) for the old b run and the full auxiliary array yields

  V_aux=V_b.

Together with(12), the fully legal combined trajectory has energy
epsilon V_b<0. This proves the stated contrapositive.

## 8. Scope and the next unmatched condition

The missing existence of SOME core-ended negative witness is settled
by this candidate construction; the old preserved-data reset criterion
remains correct but was not a universal obstruction after dimensional
padding. The saturated-append converse now covers arbitrary primitive
failures in its prefix.

This does not establish a corresponding arbitrary-tail converse for a
prepended saturated bridge. Its complete value/optimum-slack penalty
remains, and(9) identifies the retained quantity exactly. Neither the
original normalized objective ratio nor the unrestricted asymptotic
Zhang-Jiang target follows from this primitive-energy reduction.
