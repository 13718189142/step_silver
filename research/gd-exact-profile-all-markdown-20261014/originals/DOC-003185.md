# C67: an exact moving-normal clock and a failed scalar-clock transfer

Status: COMPLETE UNVERIFIED statements and proofs below; the proposed
full-horizon power-epigraph program remains PARTIAL. No fact_submit,

C65 and C66 are not mathematical premises. All power-epigraph claims

## 1. Statements and scope

For any real p>2 put phi_p(r)=|r|^p/p and define the single global function

    K_p={(r,s):s>=phi_p(r)},
    F_p(X)=max_(g in K_p)(<X,g>-||g||^2/2).

For r real and v>=0 use the states

    eta=v+phi_p(r),
    u=eta*|r|^(p-2),
    X_p(r,v)=(r(1+u),-v),
    G_p(r)=(r,phi_p(r)).

F_p is globally convex and differentiable with 1-Lipschitz gradient.
Its minimizer set is {(0,y):y<=0}, its minimum value is zero, and

    grad F_p(X_p(r,v))=G_p(r),
    2F_p(X_p(r,v))
      =r^2+2(p-1)v|r|^p/p+(2p-1)|r|^(2p)/p^2.             (1)

For every nonzero initial r_0 and v_0>=0 the true initial squared
closest-minimizer distance is

    R_0^2=r_0^2[1+(v_0+|r_0|^p/p)|r_0|^(p-2)]^2=x_0^2.  (2)

Here x is the horizontal coordinate of X_p. All p,r,v,u,eta and clock
symbols in this document are local variables, not redefinitions of the
accepted project glossary.

For an actual consecutive run with nonnegative steps of total mass T,
whose nonzero horizontal gradients all have the same sign, the normalized
payoff with its TRUE entry horizontal distance obeys, if T>=1/(2p-2),

    2F_p(X_end)/x_entry^2 <= E_p(T),
    E_p(T)=[(2p-2)T+2p-1]^2
             /[4p(p-1)(T+1)^2(2T+1)].                    (3)

No v-coordinate is reset when applying this to a later block. Its ratio
to the ORIGINAL initial payoff includes x_entry^2/x_0^2 explicitly.

Define, for r>0 and v>=0,

    C_p(r,v)=log r+(p-1)v*r^(p-2)/(p-2)
                    +(2p-1)r^(2p-2)/[p(2p-2)].           (4)

At a single sign-preserving step h>0 the exact moving-state identity
in Section4 implies

    C_p(|r_plus|,v_plus)-C_p(|r|,v)
       <=-h+|r|^(p-2)(v_plus-v)/(p-2)
       =-h+h|r|^(2p-2)/[p(p-2)].                         (5)

For a whole one-sign interval [A,B] this gives the inherited-state budget

    C_p(|r_B|,v_B)-C_p(|r_A|,v_A)
       <=-T+J_(A,B)
       <=-T+|r_A|^(p-2)(v_B-v_A)/(p-2),                 (6)
    J_(A,B)=sum_(i=A)^(B-1)
                 |r_i|^(p-2)(v_(i+1)-v_i)/(p-2).

The tempting stronger statement with J omitted is FALSE, even at the
first actual small step of H_m. For p=4, r_0=2 and v_0=0, the first
step of every actual H_m, m>=128, preserves the positive sign and has

    C_4(r_1,v_1)-C_4(2,0)>3.                             (7)

Thus the fixed-profile scalar rule that every noncrossing step decreases
its clock by at least h does not transfer to this changing normal state.
This does not refute a full-horizon power-epigraph payoff bound, nor C65.

## 2. Global legality, values, and every ordered interpolation pair

The epigraph K_p is nonempty, closed and convex. Completing the square
in the definition of F_p gives a coercive, strictly convex projection
problem, so there is a unique maximizing gradient Pi_(K_p)(X).
Adding the two projection normal inequalities gives

    ||Pi_(K_p)(X)-Pi_(K_p)(Y)||^2
       <=<Pi_(K_p)(X)-Pi_(K_p)(Y),X-Y>.

Thus the projection is 1-Lipschitz. Comparison of the two defining
maxima bounds the first-order remainder of F_p between zero and
||Y-X||^2. This proves differentiability and the stated gradient.
Convexity follows from the supremum-of-affine-functions definition.

The difference X_p-G_p equals eta*(phi_p'(r),-1). For any (t,s) in K_p,

    <X_p-G_p,(t,s)-G_p>
       <=eta*[phi_p'(r)(t-r)-phi_p(t)+phi_p(r)]<=0.

The final inequality is the defining tangent inequality for the convex
differentiable function phi_p. This proves the gradient formula. Direct
substitution into <X_p,G_p>-||G_p||^2/2 proves (1).

For arbitrary two such states, write f_i=F_p(X_i). The complete ordered
interpolation residual, with no restriction on chronological distance, is

    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<G_i,X_j-X_i>+||G_i-G_j||^2/2
       =<X_j-G_j,G_i-G_j>
       =eta_j*[phi_p'(r_j)(r_i-r_j)-phi_p(r_i)+phi_p(r_j)]
       <=0.                                               (8)

Every point (0,y), y<=0, has F_p=0 by testing zero and bounding all other
feasible inner products. For x!=0, testing (t,phi_p(t)) for sufficiently
small t with the sign of x gives a positive value: the linear term xt
dominates the terms of orders two and higher. For x=0 and y>0, a small
positive vertical gradient gives positive value. These tests determine
the minimizer set exactly and prove (2).

At a physical step h>=0 the exact GD recurrence is

    v_plus=v+h|r|^p/p,
    x_plus=x-hr,
    x_plus=r_plus+v_plus*r_plus*|r_plus|^(p-2)
                         +r_plus*|r_plus|^(2p-2)/p.        (9)

The last right-hand side is odd, continuous, strictly increasing and
surjective. Its derivative off zero is
1+(p-1)v_plus|r_plus|^(p-2)+(2p-1)|r_plus|^(2p-2)/p>=1;
the derivative at zero is 1. Thus (9) defines the unique actual next
state of the SAME function. Its normal coefficient satisfies

    eta_plus-eta=(h-1)phi_p(r)+phi_p(r_plus)>=0 for h>=1.   (10)

The effective turning variable u=eta|r|^(p-2) need not increase.

## 3. The no-turn payoff retains accumulated v and the entry radius

By symmetry suppose the whole block is on the positive ray. For fixed
v define T_v(r)=r+v r^(p-1)+r^(2p-1)/p on r>=0. At each noncrossing
positive step, x_plus<=x and v_plus>=v, so its inverse in (9) gives
0<r_plus<=r. Consequently, with terminal r>0 and u=v*r^(p-2)+r^(2p-2)/p,

    x_entry=x_end+sum h_i r_i >=r(T+1+u),
    v_end=v_entry+sum h_i r_i^p/p >=T r^p/p.               (11)

Set z=r^(2p-2)/p and w=v_end*r^(p-2), so u=w+z and w>=Tz.
Equation (1), divided by r^2, is

    2F_p(X_end)/r^2=1+(2-2/p)u+z/p.

Since z<=u/(T+1), the ratio in (3) is at most

    [1+k_T u]/[T+1+u]^2,
    k_T=2-2/p+1/[p(T+1)].                                (12)

For u>=0 the derivative in (12) has the sign of
k_T(T+1)-2-k_T u. When T>=1/(2p-2), its maximum is attained at
u=T+1-2/k_T>=0 and equals k_T^2/[4(k_T(T+1)-1)]. Simplification is
exactly (3). Negative-ray blocks give the same result by reflection.
An absorbing endpoint has zero payoff and requires no logarithmic clock.

For p=2 the rational expression E_p(T) formally reduces to the parabolic
expression, but this argument and the clock statements here are stated
for p>2. For fixed p>2,

    lim_(T->infinity) T E_p(T)=(p-1)/(2p).                (13)

This is a no-turn result, not a full-family bound in the presence of
repeated turns. It does not assume a fresh v=0 at a later block.

## 4. Exact horizontal clock and the positive vertical-state cost

Let g_v be the inverse of T_v on the positive half-line and set
Psi_p(x,v)=C_p(g_v(x),v), for x>0. Direct differentiation gives

    d C_p(r,v)/dr=T_v'(r)/r,
    partial Psi_p(x,v)/partial x=1/g_v(x),
    partial Psi_p(x,v)/partial v=g_v(x)^(p-2)/(p-2).       (14)

For the last identity, differentiating T_v(g_v(x))=x at fixed x gives
partial g_v/partial v=-g_v(x)^(p-1)/T_v'(g_v(x)). The chain rule then
subtracts r^(p-2) from (p-1)r^(p-2)/(p-2), leaving the last expression
in (14). It is POSITIVE.

For one positive noncrossing step, write x_plus=x-hr>0. The exact split
into horizontal motion at the old v followed by the actual v increment is

    C_p(r_plus,v_plus)-C_p(r,v)
       =-integral_(x_plus)^x [dy/g_v(y)]
        +1/(p-2)*integral_v^(v_plus) g_s(x_plus)^(p-2) ds. (15)

The first integral is at least (x-x_plus)/r=h, since g_v(y)<=r.
For the second integral, x_plus<=x and s>=v imply g_s(x_plus)<=r.
This proves (5); summing it over the same-sign interval proves (6),
because its gradient magnitudes decrease. By evenness the same
calculation applies to a negative interval using absolute radii.

The cost J is an actual weighted vertical increment. It is not a
freely chosen auxiliary value or a discarded initial height. A proposed
block argument for power epigraphs must bound it, or replace this clock
with a potential that pays for it. Merely knowing that eta grows does
not justify deleting the positive second integral in (15).

## 5. An exact actual-H_m counterexample to omitting the cost

For m>=128 keep the enclosing-horizon physical parameters

    lambda=1+sqrt(2), c=lambda(1+1/m), a=(1-1/m)/c,
    H_0=(), H_(j+1)=(H_j,1+a c^j,H_j).

The elementary bounds 2/5<a<5/12 give
7/5<h=1+a<17/12<3/2 for the first actual step. For completeness,
sqrt(2)<99/70 gives c<(169/70)(129/128)<73/30 and hence
a>(127/128)(30/73)>2/5. Also a<sqrt(2)-1<5/12.

Fix p=4,r_0=2,v_0=0. Then

    X_0=(34,0), R_0^2=1156,
    x_1=34-2h>31,
    v_1=4h>28/5.

The unique positive r_1 is determined by T_(v_1)(r_1)=x_1, where
T_v(r)=r+v r^3+r^7/4. At r=3/2,

    T_(v_1)(3/2)=3/2+(27/2)h+2187/512,
    x_1-T_(v_1)(3/2)=65/2-(31/2)h-2187/512
                     >37/4-2187/512>0.

Strict monotonicity implies r_1>3/2. Also r_1<2 follows from x_1<x_0
and v_1>v_0. Thus the step is genuinely positive and sign-preserving.
By (4),

    C_4(r_1,v_1)-C_4(2,0)
       >log(3/4)+(3/2)(28/5)(9/4)
                    +(7/24)[729/64-64]
       =log(3/4)+189/10-23569/1536
       >-1/3+189/10-23569/1536>3.                        (16)

Here log(4/3)<1/3 follows by integrating 1/t<1 on (1,4/3).
The final rational difference above 3 is 1707/7680=569/2560>0.
No scalar root was approximated or trajectory numerically evaluated.
Equations (1),(2),(8),(9) give the global function, true denominator,
every ordered interpolation pair, and actual prescribed step.

This refutes any universal claim C_plus-C<=-kappa h with kappa>=0
for this moving-state clock. It is not a counterexample to payoff
convergence or to the separate parabolic argument.

## 6. Literature grounding and the open quantitative interface

The exact power projection equation agrees with Propositions3.2--3.3
of Chierchia, Pustelnik, Pesquet and Pesquet-Popescu, "Epigraphical
splitting for solving constrained convex formulations of inverse
problems with proximal tools", arXiv:1210.5844v3. Its exact downloaded
paper and extracted text are in downloads/c66_epigraph_projection_1210.5844.
The Hilbert product is Euclidean, the generator has full domain and is
proper closed convex, and their parameters beta=p,tau=1/p,zeta=-v give
T_v(r)=x. The definitions and both projection proofs were re-read.
No theorem from that paper is a premise of this self-contained proof.

The precise failure is a proof migration from a FIXED scalar gradient
profile to an epigraph whose vertical coordinate changes along the
same legal trajectory. Equation (15) supplies the omitted term.
There is still no estimate of the sum of the J costs and crossing
gains over every chronological block on H_m, in terms of R_0 and the
exact benchmark. Consequently the full p>2 family, including quartic
multiple-turn trajectories, remains open. No new original-target
claim or verifier allocation follows from this result.
