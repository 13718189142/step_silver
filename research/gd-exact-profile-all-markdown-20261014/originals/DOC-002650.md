## 1. Original five-gradient space and its two exact balances

Let h=(h0,h1,h2,h3) have nonnegative entries. Put

    P_i=sum_(t<i) h_t, 0<=i<=4; s=P_4; p_i=1+P_i.

Let e be the five-entry all-ones column, and let T have diagonal1,
T_it=h_t for t<i, and entries0 above the diagonal. Every formal gradient
g0,...,g4 is retained, even when a step vanishes. A full primitive certificate
has twenty nonnegative coefficients Lambda_ij, i!=j, zero diagonal, and

    L=diag(Lambda e)-Lambda,
    omega=(-2h0,-2h1,-2h2,-2h3,2s)^T,
    D=diag(h0(2-h0),...,h3(2-h3),-s(s+2)),
    L^T e=omega, M=D+(LT+T^T L^T)/2 >=0.                 (1)

This is the exact interface in accepted04219554e41d9750. Its identity is

    V_h+sum Lambda_ij Q_ij=<M,Gram(g0,...,g4)>,
    V_h=2sum_(i<4)h_i(f_i-f4)
          +sum_(i<4)h_i(1-h_i)||g_i||^2-s(s+1)||g4||^2,
    Q_ij=f_i-f_j+<g_i,x_j-x_i>+||g_i-g_j||^2/2,
    x_i=x0-sum_(t<i)h_t g_t.

The matrix inequality in (1) is on ALL of R5. Since Te=p and
sum_(i<4)h_i P_i=(s^2-sum h_i^2)/2, direct expansion gives

    e^T D e=-s^2-sum h_i^2,
    omega^T Te=s^2+sum h_i^2,
    e^T M e=0.

For any real column v, nonnegativity of (e+t v)^T M(e+t v) for both signs
of sufficiently small t forces v^T Me=0. Therefore Me=0. Substituting
in (1) gives

    LP=diag(p)omega.                                    (2)

For an explicit component check at i<4,
(T^T omega)_i=-2h_i+2h_i(P_i+h_i), so

Define mu_ij=Lambda_ij p_j. Equation(2), together with the value flow,

    sum_j mu_ij=sum_j mu_ji.                             (3)

## 2. Weighted cuts, with all original edges present

For j in{0,1,2,3}, let z^(j) be1 on indices0,...,j and0 thereafter.
Write A_j=P_(j+1), and define nonnegative weighted crossing sums

    F_j=sum_(i<=j<k) Lambda_ik (p_k-p_(j+1)),
    B_j=sum_(k<=j<i) Lambda_ik (p_j-p_k).                 (4)

These are sums over every original crossing pair. Nonnegative h makes
every weight nonnegative; zero displacement simply has weight0.
The adjacent forward and backward edges have weight0.

Let O_j and I_j be the unweighted forward and backward crossing masses.
Summing the original value flows gives

    O_j-I_j=-2A_j.

Summing(3) over the prefix gives

    p_(j+1) O_j+F_j=p_j I_j-B_j,
    h_j O_j=2p_j A_j-F_j-B_j.                            (5)

We also have the full formal-direction identity

    (z^(j))^T M z^(j)=-A_j^2+O_j+F_j.                   (6)

To check it directly, Tz has entry1+P_i on i<=j and A_j otherwise.
The diagonal contribution is sum_(i<=j)h_i(2-h_i). The internal part
of z^T LTz is minus the internal row moment; its forward-crossing
part is sum_(i<=j<k)Lambda_ik(1+P_i-A_j). Sum the row moments(2)
over i<=j to replace the internal part by

    -2sum_(i<=j)h_i(1+P_i)
                  +sum_(i<=j<k)Lambda_ik(P_k-P_i).

The combined forward coefficient is1+P_k-A_j. The remaining scalar is

    sum_(i<=j)h_i(2-h_i)-2sum_(i<=j)h_i(1+P_i)=-A_j^2.

This proves(6) without deleting a gradient coordinate or an interpolation
pair. Define the step deficit polynomial

    Dstep_j=h_j(P_j+h_j)-2(P_j+1).                       (7)

Combining(5) and(6), for h_j>0, yields

    h_j (z^(j))^T M z^(j)
       =-A_j Dstep_j+(h_j-1)F_j-B_j.                    (8)

Consequently full PSD requires

    (h_j-1)F_j-B_j >= A_j Dstep_j.                      (9)

Formula(8) is the weighted-cut identity already present in
draft5bd01e2b19002a59, rederived here. The new point below is that a
