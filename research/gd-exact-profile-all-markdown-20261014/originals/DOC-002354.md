Primary projection method: Euclidean affine-face active-set method on
the explicit spine vertices, solving positive-definite edge systems by
Cholesky. At each non-core query start from the nearest vertex, add a
maximally violated original normal, and move to an affine minimizer or
the first blocking boundary. No mass thresholding, face freezing,
regularization, or clipping into feasibility. Every result independently
passes all original normal and barycentric reconstruction checks. For m2
the core shortcut is disabled to exercise the reduced active-set solver.
The independent full m2 solver enumerates all 15 nonempty vertex faces
at EVERY query, solves augmented Gram/equality systems by pivoted Gaussian
elimination, and checks ALL full vertices. It evolves its own full state.

are counted, not silently reported as mathematical zero. No data clipping.
