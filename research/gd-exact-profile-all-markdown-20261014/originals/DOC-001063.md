## 1. Static model, physical word, and parameters

    H_(k+1)=(H_k,1+a*c^k,H_k), 0<=k<m.

    r=a*c^(m-1), b=1+r, d=1/(P+1), sigma=r*d/(2P+1),
    Kcut=r^2*d^2/(2P+1),
    p=12/13, q=5/13, rho=q^2/p^2=25/144,

it is not the entire worst-case functional W_n and need not equal the Huber

    0<epsilon<=1/4, |eta|<=1/4.

For positive Rminus,Rplus define phi(0)=0 and
phi'(s)=clip(s,[-Rminus,Rplus]). Explicitly phi(s)=s^2/2 in the core,
phi(s)=Rplus*s-Rplus^2/2 on the positive outer interval, and
phi(s)=-Rminus*s-Rminus^2/2 on the negative outer interval.

Use u_+=(p,q), u_-=(p,-q), positive radii

    Rplus_+=p*d*(1+epsilon), Rplus_-=p*d*(1-epsilon),
    Rminus_+=p*sigma*(1+eta), Rminus_-=p*sigma*(1-eta),

and the corresponding two functions phi_+,phi_-. Define ONE static function

    F(x,y)=kappa*y^2/2+w*phi_+(p*x+q*y)+w*phi_-(p*x-q*y).

no objective switch, stepsize adaptation, or local curvature rescaling.

## 3. Exact prefix and its central queried gate

Write X_i=(x_i,y_i). Put

    delta=epsilon*p*d, zeta=eta*p*sigma,
    y_plus=-epsilon*(q/p)*d*ell,
    y_minus=eta*(q/p)*sigma*ell,
    Lcut=epsilon*d*J/2.

As long as both ridges are in the positive outer intervals, the gradient is

    grad F=(d,kappa*y+delta*q/p^2).

The FIRST physical step is ell=1/kappa. It sends y_0=0 exactly to y_plus,
the stationary value of this affine scalar recursion. Every subsequent
step preserves y_plus until a ridge changes gate, irrespective of its size.
The proposed prefix is therefore

    x_i=1-d*sum_(t<i) h_t,
    y_0=0, y_i=y_plus for 1<=i<=j.                 (C96.2)

Here is an induction validating every proposed gate, rather than assuming
the affine formula remains applicable. At i=0 both projections are p, while
Rplus_+<=5p/(4(P+1))<p. For 1<=i<j, the remaining prefix mass includes its
last step ell, so x_i-d>=ell*d. Hence

    p*x_i+q*y_plus-Rplus_+
       >=p*d*(ell-epsilon*J)>0.

The other projection is even larger than p*x_i, and Rplus_-<p*d. This
proves that both ridges remain strictly positive outer before index j.
Thus (C96.2) follows from the ACTUAL recursion, and x_j=d.

At index j the first ridge projection is

    p*d+q*y_plus=p*d*(1-epsilon*(J-1)).

It is positive because epsilon*(J-1)<3/40, and strictly less than
Rplus_+. It therefore lies strictly in that ridge's core. The second
projection is p*d*(1+epsilon*(J-1))>Rplus_-, so the second ridge is still
positive outer. Direct differentiation now gives

    grad F(X_j)=(d-Lcut,-(q/p)*Lcut),
    x_(j+1)=-r*d+b*Lcut,
    y_(j+1)=epsilon*(q/p)*d*(b*J/2-ell).          (C96.3)

The last y-coordinate is positive by b>10 and ell<3/2. In particular the
central gradient is noncollinear with the stationary precentral gradient
(d,0). The actual gate Hessians diag(0,kappa) and
diag(0,kappa)+w*u_+*u_+^T do not commute: their off-diagonal commutator
has nonzero magnitude kappa*w*p*q.

    x_(j+1)+(q/p)*abs(y_(j+1))

    grad F=(-sigma,kappa*y-q*zeta/p^2).

    x_n=-sigma*(P+1)+b*Lcut, y_n=y_minus.         (C96.4)

    x_n+(q/p)*abs(y_minus)<-sigma*(1+|eta|)

    P-|eta|*J > (b/r)*(2P+1)*epsilon*J/2.        (C96.5)

The left side exceeds P-13/40. The right side is less than
13*(2P+1)/72. Their difference exceeds
