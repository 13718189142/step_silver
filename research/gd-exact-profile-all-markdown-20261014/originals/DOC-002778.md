## Exact analytical interface completed before freeze

DERIVATION.md gives the source-bound analytical derivation. Its algebra has not
been submitted to Danus verification. Physical coordinates are g0,...,gn with
auxiliary p; t_i=1+sum_(k<i)h_k, t_p=M=1+2sum h. Edges use the original
orientation Q_ij=<X_j-G_j,G_i-G_j><=0. With mu=a_j lambda, where a_j=1 on
physical destinations and a_p=1/M, the recovered original coefficients are
lambda_ij=mu_ij for physical j and lambda_(i,p)=M mu_(i,p).

The x0 coefficient cancels exactly under out_i-in_i=2(1_(i=n)-1_(i=p)). The
full gradient quadratic is

    R=2<g_n,sum_(k<n)h_k g_k>+||g_n||^2-M||p||^2
      +sum_ij mu_ij <B_jG,G_j-G_i>,
    B_iG=g_i+sum_(k<i)h_k g_k, B_pG=Mp.

For its coefficient matrix P, the common-gradient contact equations
sum_j mu_ij(t_j-t_i)=2h_i for i<n, and zero at n,p, imply P1=0. Subtracting
g_n from every gradient therefore gives P=A^T Rred A, where Rred is the
principal matrix obtained by setting g_n=0 and retaining p. Full PSD and
quotient PSD are equivalent under these exact equations. The program maximizes
gamma with Rred-gamma diag(1,...,1,0) PSD. This gamma is a matrix margin;
it is not an objective gap, a W value, or a normalized convergence rate.

All physical-to-p absorptions are independent nonnegative variables. Their
sum is two by divergence. No canonical absorption condition is imposed.
The physical tree retains both directions of i--(i+2^nu2(i+1)); the complete
topology retains every physical ordered pair of distinct nodes. These are
finite sufficient certificate classes. Their matrix coordinates do not
construct an actual GD or projection trajectory.

For n=1 and h>0, direct solution of the divergence and contact equations gives
mu01=2-2w, mu10=w1=2-w and 0<=w=w0<=1. After g1=0,

    R=M(p-w g0/2)^2+
      [2(1-h)+(2h-1)w-Mw^2/4] g0^2.

For h=1 the bracket is 1/3-(3/4)(w-2/3)^2; for h=2 it is
-2+3w-5w^2/4, increasing on [0,1]. Hence the exact optima are respectively
