## 3. Submitted route: an actual repair with an exact cost

Let prototype data satisfy the actual GD updates, both star constraints, and all interpolation rows inside the left and right blocks. Only cross-block rows may fail. Choose a root index \(r\) with \(H=h_r>1\) and \(h_t<H\) for all \(t<r\). Such a root exists whenever the word has a step above one, by choosing its earliest global maximum.

Let
\[
p_i=\prod_{t<i}(1-h_t/H)>0,\qquad
\Delta_i=\max_{j>r}\{0,-R^0_{ij},-R^0_{ji}\}.
\]
The exact required amplitude is
\[
\boxed{M=\max_{i\le r}\frac{2H^2\Delta_i}{(H-1)p_i^2}.}
\]
Add finite data from a quadratic coordinate of curvature \(1/H\), starting at \(\sqrt M\). The root annihilates the coordinate. Every cross row receives at least its deficit; internal and star rows remain nonnegative. Full smooth-convex interpolation then supplies a single actual function. No globally defined prototype function is assumed.

The terminal objective is unchanged, while the original squared radius becomes
\[
\|x_0^0\|^2+M.
\]
Thus the actual lower witness has payoff
\[
\boxed{\frac{2f_n^0}{\|x_0^0\|^2+M}.}
\]
This is the corrected version of the draft's uncharged repair.

If every preceding step is at most \(qH\), \(q<1\), and the prefix mass is \(P\), then
\[
M\le\frac{2H^2}{H-1}
\exp\!\left(\frac{2P}{H(1-q)}\right)\max_i\Delta_i.
\]
The target lower bound would follow from suitable near-benchmark prototypes and a uniform total repair cost \(o(B_n\,2f_n^0)\). No such all-word defect bound or summable recursive cost estimate is established.

Two restrictions prevent reuse of the original draft's shortcut:

- An auxiliary trace that leaves every child residual unchanged and keeps diagonal root ports cannot repair one of the two cross-row orientations at all. A real repair must change those data.

A translated quadratic-data refinement trades conjugate height against radius, but the original diagonal root still forces more than \(M/4\) in added squared radius and generally destroys the terminal-core condition.
